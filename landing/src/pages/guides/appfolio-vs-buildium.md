## How much does AppFolio cost compared to Buildium?

AppFolio prices per unit, and Buildium prices per month from a base. Both pages show "starting at" prices only. Here are the plans as each company shows them on 2026-10-19.

| Plan | Starting price | Minimum | What the plan adds |
|---|---|---|---|
| AppFolio Core | $1.49 per unit per month | 50 units and a minimum spend | Accounting, leasing, work orders, inspections, Realm-X Assistant |
| AppFolio Plus | $3.20 per unit per month | Units and spend (not shown) | Affordable and student housing tools, advanced budgeting, read-only API |
| AppFolio Max | $5.00 per unit per month | Units and spend (not shown) | Leasing CRM, read/write API, a customer success manager |
| Buildium Essential | $62 per month | None shown | Basic accounting, maintenance and leasing, ticket support |
| Buildium Growth | $192 per month | None shown, onboarding required | Live phone support, analytics, lower eSignature and EFT fees |
| Buildium Premium | $400 per month | None shown, onboarding required | Open API, full automation suite, free inspections and eSignatures |

Source: the AppFolio and Buildium pricing pages. Buildium asks customers with more than 5,000 units to call for a price.

## What does AppFolio cost for 50, 100 and 200 doors?

AppFolio Core costs at least $1.49 times your unit count each month. That is the list floor, before the minimum spend that AppFolio does not publish.

| Doors | AppFolio Core floor | AppFolio Plus floor | Buildium |
|---|---|---|---|
| 20 | Not sold (50-unit minimum) | Not shown | From $62 |
| 50 | $74.50 | $160 | From $62 (Growth from $192) |
| 100 | $149 | $320 | Quote |
| 200 | $298 | $640 | Quote |
| 500 | $745 | $1,600 | Quote |

We did the math from the per-unit prices. AppFolio Plus also has a unit minimum that the page does not show. Buildium does not show how its price grows with units. So for 100 doors or more, the only true Buildium number is a quote. Use the table to plan a budget, not to sign a contract.

## Which is better for under 50 doors?

Buildium is the better fit for under 50 doors, because AppFolio Core has a 50-unit minimum. Buildium Essential starts at $62 per month. The Buildium sign-up form also lets you start a 14-day free trial with sample data and no credit card.

Watch for: Buildium Essential lists $2.35 per incoming EFT payment and $5 per eSignature document. On 30 doors, rent alone is 30 EFT payments a month. Count those fees before you compare plans.

## Which is better for 50 to 200 doors?

Both tools fit 50 to 200 doors, so the choice comes down to quotes and fees. AppFolio Core at 100 units starts at $149 per month at list price. Buildium Growth starts at $192 per month and includes live phone support.

What to do:

1. **Ask for a written quote** from each company for your exact unit count.
2. **Ask about the minimum spend** at AppFolio. The pricing page says one applies but does not give the number.
3. **Ask about onboarding** at Buildium. Growth and Premium require it, and the pricing page does not show its price.
4. **Price your payment volume.** Buildium Growth charges $1.35 per incoming EFT payment. AppFolio does not list payment fees on its pricing page, so ask.
5. **Count eSignatures and screenings.** Buildium Growth charges $1 per eSignature document and $20 per enhanced screening.

## Which is better for 500 doors or more?

AppFolio Plus and Max target larger and mixed portfolios. Plus adds affordable housing and student housing tools, advanced budgeting and custom fields. Max adds a leasing CRM, a read/write API and a dedicated customer success manager.

Buildium Premium also adds an Open API and the full automation suite. Premium includes up to 100 free business bank accounts and free incoming EFT payments for the first 12 months. Above 5,000 units, Buildium sets the price by phone.

## What fees come on top of the base price?

Buildium lists its add-on fees on the pricing page. AppFolio says to "contact us for details". Here are the Buildium fees as shown on 2026-10-19.

| Buildium fee | Essential | Growth | Premium |
|---|---|---|---|
| Incoming EFT payment | $2.35 | $1.35 | Free 12 months, then $0.60 |
| Outgoing EFT payment | $0.99 | $0.99 | $0.99 |
| Credit card payment | 2.99% | 2.99% | 2.99% |
| eSignature | $5 per document | $1 per document | Included |
| Business bank account setup | $99 each | 10 free, then $99 | 100 free, then $99 |
| Property inspections | $99 setup, $40–$95 per month | Included | Included |

Buildium also charges $17 per tenant screening, or $20 for enhanced screening on Growth and Premium. Applicant-paid screening costs the applicant $35. 1099 e-filing costs $50 per batch plus $4.50 per form.

## How do the AI features compare?

Both companies now sell AI inside the software. AppFolio puts Realm-X Assistant and Messages in Core and Realm-X Flows in Plus. Buildium Essential has the Buildium AI Assistant, Growth adds AI-enhanced communications, and Premium has the full AI and automation suite. Buildium prices its Lumina AI Workforce per account, on a separate page.

These AI tools help your team work faster inside the software. They do not make ChatGPT or Google name your company to owners. That is a separate job: see [how rental owners use AI to choose a property manager](/guides/how-owners-choose-a-property-manager-with-ai).

## Does either tool help you get new owner clients?

Buildium owns All Property Management, a service that sells leads from owners who look for a manager. Buildium Premium customers get up to $500 in free leads, after they buy their first lead. AppFolio does not list a lead service on its pricing page.

Paid leads are one channel. Your own site and AI answers are another, and you own them. Read [why owner leads are dropping](/guides/why-owner-leads-are-dropping) for the full picture.

## How to choose between AppFolio and Buildium

Use your door count first, then your fees.

- **Under 50 doors:** Buildium. AppFolio Core does not sell below 50 units.
- **50 to 200 doors:** get both quotes. Compare the total with payment and eSignature fees, not the base price.
- **Affordable or student housing:** look at AppFolio Plus first. It lists tools for both.
- **More than 5,000 units:** both sell custom plans. Call each company.

Before you sign, set your own management fee with the [property management fee calculator](/tools/property-management-fee-calculator). Software is a cost per door. Your fee must cover it.
