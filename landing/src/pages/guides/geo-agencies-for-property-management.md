## Which agencies do GEO for property management companies?

A GEO agency is a marketing firm that works to get its clients named and linked in AI answers. This list compares 11 companies that sell GEO or AEO work. Ten serve PM companies; FirstIndex serves software companies and is our model. GEO (getting cited by AI answers) and AEO (answer engine optimization) mean the same job here. The job is to make ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude and Google AI Overviews name and link your company.

We picked the list from our own test. On 2026-10-18 we asked ChatGPT, Claude and Gemini 20 prompts, 2 runs each. The answers named ClearLead Digital and Goodjuju Marketing 9 times each, and Upkeep Media 8 times. ApartmentSEO and Resident360 got 6 names each, and Digible 5 (our data). The answers cited clearleaddigital.com 22 times and propertymanagerwebsites.com 11 times. We ran the test again on 2026-11-02: see [who AI answers name now](#which-geo-agencies-do-ai-answers-name).

We run Citata.ai, so weigh our entry with that in mind. We sort the list by audience: single-family managers first, then multifamily, then general. Inside each group, we sort by how central AI search is to the offer: a named GEO service first, AI work inside an SEO plan next, a report or copy add-on last. Each fact comes from the company's own site, checked 2026-10-18. We checked the public prices again on 2026-11-03. "Not stated" means we did not find it on their pages.

## What does GEO for property management include?

GEO for property management is mostly good SEO, aimed at the questions landlords ask AI. Goodjuju Marketing says so plainly on its GEO page. A full GEO service usually includes 6 parts:

1. **Baseline.** Ask ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude and Google AI Overviews a fixed set of landlord prompts. Record who they name and which sites they cite.
2. **Landlord answer pages.** Fees, contracts, screening, Section 8, and one page per city you serve.
3. **Facts AI can read.** Schema, a clear About page, the same name, address and phone on every listing.
4. **Third-party sources.** Reviews on Google, Yelp and Thumbtack, plus directories that AI cites. Upkeep Media names NARPM, AppFolio and Buildium as citation targets.
5. **Fixes.** Wrong fees, old addresses or missing cities in AI answers get fixed at the source.
6. **Weekly or monthly report.** The same prompts again, with changes in citations and landlord leads.

## How do you pick a GEO agency for property management?

Pick a GEO agency by its method and its reports, not by its claims. Ask each agency these 6 questions before you sign:

1. **Do you take a baseline first?** Without a "before" number, nobody can show a change.
2. **Which prompts do you track?** Ask for the list. It should match how landlords ask, like "best property manager in Tampa for a single-family home".
3. **Which engines do you check?** ChatGPT, Gemini, Google AI Overviews, Claude, Perplexity and Copilot give different answers.
4. **What is in the report, and how often?** Look for citations by prompt and engine, and landlord leads from AI.
5. **Who checks the content?** A person must read each page before it goes live.
6. **What proof can you show for AI results?** Many case studies show door growth or Google rankings, not AI citations. Both are useful, but they are not the same.

To track AI leads yourself, see [how to track AI traffic and AI leads](/guides/track-ai-traffic-property-management).

## What are the red flags when you hire a GEO agency?

The biggest red flag is a guarantee of a #1 spot in ChatGPT. AI answers change by engine, by run and by week. No agency controls them.

Other red flags:

- **Bulk AI content.** Hundreds of thin city pages can break Google's "scaled content abuse" policy (Google Search Central).
- **A score with no prompts.** An "AI visibility score" means little if you cannot see the prompts and answers behind it.
- **Citation counts with no method.** Ask which prompts, which engines and which dates.
- **No baseline.** Without a start point, the agency can claim any result.
- **Long lock-in with no report.** ClearLead Digital and Goodjuju Marketing sell month to month. FirstIndex asks for 3 months first, then goes month to month.
