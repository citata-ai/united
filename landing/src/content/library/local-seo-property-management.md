## What are the main local ranking factors?

Google ranks local results by relevance, distance and prominence. Its own help page says so in one line: "Local results are mainly based on relevance, distance, and popularity."

| Factor | What Google means | What you control |
|---|---|---|
| Relevance | How well your profile matches the search | Primary category, services, description, city pages |
| Distance | How far you are from the searcher | Your real address or service area. Little else. |
| Prominence | How well-known you are | Reviews, links, articles, directory listings |

The same page says: "There's no way to request or pay for a better local ranking on Google." Ads sit in a separate slot with a label.

Experts add detail. The Whitespark 2026 Local Search Ranking Factors survey asked 47 local search experts. Their top 5 map pack factors were:

1. Primary category.
2. Distance to the searcher.
3. Keywords in the business name.
4. A physical address in the city of search.
5. Open at the time of search.

Review and behavior signals rose in 2026. Citations (directory listings) fell by about half a point.

Watch for: a keyword in the business name helps rank, but Google bans it unless it is your real, legal brand. Google can suspend a profile for "unnecessary information" in the name. Do not stuff the name.

## Why does local SEO matter for owner leads?

Local search is still the largest owner lead channel. Goodjuju studied 35 property management companies and 2,525 owner leads in 2026. In the fall window (July 8 to October 5, 2026), the split was:

| Channel | Share of owner leads |
|---|---|
| Google Search and map pack | 50.6% |
| Direct and Maps | 35.6% |
| Other search | 4.3% |
| Referral | 3.7% |
| AI tools | 3.4% |
| Paid search | 1.2% |
| Social media | 0.8% |

Source: Goodjuju lead source report, fall 2026 update. Leads are form fills, not phone calls. All 35 companies work with an SEO agency, so the sample is better at SEO than most.

AI tools are small but convert well: 3.9% of AI visitors filled a form, against 1.2% from Google. AI answers often pull from the same local facts. See [how owners choose a property manager with AI](/guides/how-owners-choose-a-property-manager-with-ai).

## How do Google Business Profile and your website work together?

Google Business Profile wins the map pack, and your website wins the blue links below it. Each one feeds the other. The profile links to your site. Your site confirms the facts on the profile.

Google says it can detect category information "from your website and from mentions about your business throughout the web." So the site and the profile must say the same thing.

Match these 6 facts on both:

1. **Name.** The real-world name on your sign and contracts. No city or service words added.
2. **Address or service area.** A property manager that meets owners at an office shows the address. One that works only at the properties hides it and sets a service area.
3. **Phone.** A local number under your control, not a call center line.
4. **Hours.** The hours a person answers the phone.
5. **Services.** The same list on the profile and on your services page.
6. **Cities.** The service area on the profile matches the city pages on the site.

Google allows up to 20 service areas. The whole area should not reach past about 2 hours of driving time from your base. Google also allows one profile per business, unless each location has its own staff and its own service area.

## How do reviews affect local rank?

Reviews raise prominence. Google's help page says "More reviews and positive ratings can help your business's local ranking." It also asks you to reply to reviews.

Owners read reviews before they call. The BrightLocal Local Consumer Review Survey 2026 asked 1,002 US adults. 68% said they only use a business with 4 stars or more. 74% look for reviews from the last 3 months. 80% are likely to use a business that replies to all of its reviews.

So the goal is a steady flow, not a one-time push. A profile with 200 reviews and none in a year looks stale to an owner. Ask at the right moments: after a lease signing, a fast repair or a first owner statement. Never pay or give a gift for a review. Google bans any incentive for a review.

## Do city pages help you rank in more towns?

A city page helps the blue-link results in each town you serve. The map pack still leans on distance. So a manager based in one town rarely wins the map pack 30 miles away. A strong city page can still rank in the organic results there.

A good city page for owners has:

- Local rent ranges and your fee for that city.
- The local rules an owner must follow, with links to the city or state source.
- Reviews from owners in that city.
- A link to your services page and to a rental analysis form.

Do not clone one page and swap the city name. A thin page with the same text in 20 towns helps no one. Write each page from local facts.

## What local SEO tasks repeat each month?

Local SEO is a monthly habit. Use this list.

| Task | Time | Done when |
|---|---|---|
| Check name, phone, hours, service area on the profile | 5 min | Matches the site |
| Check "suggested edits" and Google updates | 5 min | No wrong edits live |
| Add 2–4 new photos (team, office, a managed property with owner consent) | 10 min | Photos dated this month |
| Publish 1 profile post | 10 min | Post live |
| Send review requests to this month's lease signings and finished repairs | 15 min | Requests sent |
| Reply to every new review | 15 min | 0 unanswered |
| Check top 3 directory listings for wrong facts | 10 min | Facts match |
| Update one city page with a new fact or review | 30 min | Page saved |
| Read Search Console clicks for city queries | 10 min | Note the trend |

Google archives profile posts older than 6 months unless you set a date range. A monthly post keeps the profile current.

## How long does local SEO take?

Profile fixes show fast. Google says service area edits can take up to 48 hours to show. Rank changes take longer and depend on your competitors. We do not promise a time or a rank, and no honest agency should.

Measure what counts: owner leads by source, not only rank. Mark profile links with UTM tags (source labels in the link) so GA4 can separate profile clicks from other Google traffic. ## Local SEO checklist

- Profile verified, with the right primary category.
- Name matches the real brand, with no keywords added.
- Service area set by city, 20 or fewer, within about 2 hours of driving.
- Services list matches the site.
- One page per real service city, with local facts.
- Review requests at 3 or more moments in the owner and tenant journey.
- Every review answered.
- Top directories checked each quarter.
- Owner leads counted by source each month.
