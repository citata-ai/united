## How much did HUD's fair market rent change in Austin for FY2027?

HUD's fair market rent for a 2-bedroom in Austin fell 1.9% for FY2027, from $1,852 to $1,817. The FY2027 figures took effect on 2026-10-01 (Federal Register notice FR-6553-N-03). They cover Travis, Williamson, Hays, Bastrop and Caldwell counties.

| Unit size | FY2026 FMR | FY2027 FMR | Change |
|---|---|---|---|
| Studio | $1,474 | $1,447 | -1.8% |
| 1 bedroom | $1,562 | $1,530 | -2.0% |
| 2 bedrooms | $1,852 | $1,817 | -1.9% |
| 3 bedrooms | $2,347 | $2,292 | -2.3% |
| 4 bedrooms | $2,760 | $2,689 | -2.6% |

Source: HUD FY2027 and FY2026 FMR files. Every size fell.

What this means for you: if you rent to a voucher holder, the housing authority's payment standard may fall too. Check the full table and the local housing authority on the [Austin fair market rent 2027 page](/guides/section-8-rent-limits/austin-fair-market-rent-2027).

## Are Austin rents rising or falling?

Austin rents stopped falling in 2026, but they did not recover. Zillow's Observed Rent Index (ZORI, the typical asking rent for all home types) shows these points for the Austin metro.

| Point in time | Zillow rent index | Change |
|---|---|---|
| September 2019 | $1,386 | Before the boom |
| August 2022 (peak) | $1,798 | +29.7% from 2019 |
| September 2025 | $1,592 | -11.5% from peak |
| September 2026 | $1,603 | +0.7% in 1 year |

We did the math from the Zillow file. September 2026 rent is 10.8% below the 2022 peak. The US index rose 2.7% in the same year, to $1,932. So Austin lags the country.

Yardi Matrix tells the same story with a different measure. Its July 2026 report put Austin asking rents down 3.7% year over year, at $1,508 on a trailing 3-month basis through May. It also said asking rents rose 0.4% in that 3-month span. The two sources measure different things (all homes vs. apartments), so the exact numbers differ.

## What vacancy rate does Austin have?

Austin has a high vacancy rate for a large metro. Yardi Matrix reported that occupancy in stabilized properties fell 0.9 points in a year, to 91.8% in April 2026. That leaves about 8.2% of those units empty.

The cause is supply. Yardi Matrix counted 21,224 units under construction and 4,459 units delivered in 2026 through May. Each new building competes for the same renters with concessions and new finishes.

Watch for: in a high-vacancy market, a week of vacancy costs more than a small rent cut. At $1,603 a month, each empty week costs about $370. A $25 cut on a 12-month lease costs $300.

## Which local rules affect Austin rental returns?

Texas state law sets most of the rules for Austin owners, and it leaves rent levels to the market. These rules matter most.

| Rule | What it says | Effect on your return |
|---|---|---|
| Texas Local Gov't Code 214.902 | A city can set rent control only after a disaster housing emergency, with the governor's approval | No rent cap in normal times |
| Texas Property Code 92.019 | Late fee only after rent is 2 full days late; up to 12% of rent (1–4 units) or 10% (5+ units) is presumed reasonable | Caps what late fees add |
| Texas Occupations Code 1101.002 | A person who leases or collects rent for others for pay is a broker | A paid manager needs a TREC license |

What to do:

1. **Write the late fee into the lease.** Section 92.019 requires notice in a written lease.
2. **Price to the market each renewal.** With no cap, you can raise rent. With 8% vacancy, a large raise can cost you the tenant.
3. **Budget for concessions.** New buildings offer free weeks. Match them with a fair price, not with a long vacancy.

This is general information, not legal advice. Check the current law or ask a local attorney.

## Should I rent or sell my Austin home now?

Rent or sell depends on your loan, your plans and your cash flow, but the Austin data points to a slow market for both. Zillow's home value index (ZHVI) for the Austin metro is $414,273 for September 2026. That is 4.3% less than a year ago and 26.7% below the June 2022 peak.

A quick test is the gross rent yield: 12 months of rent divided by the home value.

| Metro | Typical rent (ZORI) | Typical value (ZHVI) | Gross yield |
|---|---|---|---|
| Austin | $1,603 | $414,273 | 4.64% |
| United States | $1,932 | $366,157 | 6.33% |

We did the math from the Zillow September 2026 files. A gross yield is before tax, insurance, repairs, vacancy and management. At 8% management, Austin's typical rent leaves $1,474.76 a month before those other costs.

Questions to answer before you decide:

- [ ] Does the rent cover your mortgage, tax, insurance and HOA, with a reserve for 1 to 2 months of vacancy?
- [ ] Would you sell below your purchase price? Home values fell 26.7% from the peak.
- [ ] Do you plan to move back? Renting keeps that choice open.
- [ ] Can you handle tenant calls, or will you pay a manager a share of the rent?

If you rent, run your numbers in the [property management fee calculator](/tools/property-management-fee-calculator) before you call managers. If the rent does not cover the costs with a vacancy reserve, a sale may be the calmer choice.
