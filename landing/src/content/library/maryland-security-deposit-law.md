Maryland puts its deposit rules in one long section: Real Property § 8-203, subsections (a) to (n). I read the current text on the Maryland General Assembly site on 2026-10-19. Below are the rules that matter most to a landlord.

| Rule | Limit or deadline | Statute |
|---|---|---|
| Maximum deposit | 1 month's rent per unit | § 8-203(b)(1) |
| Exception | Up to 2 months with utility assistance and written agreement | § 8-203(b)(2) |
| Overcharge | Up to 3x the extra amount + fees; sue within 2 years | § 8-203(b)(3), (4) |
| Receipt | Required, inside a written lease | § 8-203(c) |
| Account | Interest-bearing, deposits only, Maryland branch | § 8-203(d)(1) |
| Deposit the money | Within 30 days of receipt | § 8-203(d)(1)(iii) |
| Interest | Greater of 1-year Treasury rate or 1.5%; simple | § 8-203(e)(1) |
| Return deadline | 45 days after tenancy ends | § 8-203(e)(1) |
| Itemized list | First-class mail, within 45 days, with documentation | § 8-203(g), (j) |
| Move-out inspection | Tenant may attend if notice sent 15 days ahead | § 8-203(f)(1) |
| Wrongful retention | Up to 3x withheld amount + attorney fees | § 8-203(e)(4) |
| Waiver | Not allowed in any lease | § 8-203(l) |

## What is the maximum security deposit a landlord can charge in Maryland?

The maximum Maryland deposit is 1 month's rent. A landlord "may not impose a security deposit in excess of the equivalent of 1 month's rent per dwelling unit, regardless of the number of tenants" (§ 8-203(b)(1)).

The only exception allows up to 2 months' rent if all 3 apply (§ 8-203(b)(2)):

1. the tenant qualified for utility assistance through the Department of Human Services,
2. the lease has the tenant pay for utilities directly to the landlord, and
3. tenant and landlord agree in writing on the amount.

Last month's rent paid in advance counts as part of the deposit (§ 8-203(a)(3)). Section 8-208(d)(11) also bars a lease that requires more than the deposit plus the first month's rent to move in.

If a tenant buys a surety bond instead, the bond and any deposit together may not pass 2 months' rent (§ 8-203(i)(3)). A landlord may not require a bond.

## How many days does a Maryland landlord have to return a security deposit?

A Maryland landlord has 45 days. "Within 45 days after the end of the tenancy, the landlord shall return the security deposit to the tenant together with simple interest" less damages rightfully withheld (§ 8-203(e)(1)).

Different rules apply if the tenant was evicted for breach or abandoned the unit. That tenant may demand the deposit by first-class mail within 45 days and give a new address. The landlord then has 45 days from receipt to send the list and the balance (§ 8-203(h)).

## What can a landlord deduct from a security deposit in Maryland?

A Maryland landlord may withhold the deposit "for unpaid rent, damage due to breach of lease or for damage by the tenant" or the tenant's family, guests or invitees "in excess of ordinary wear and tear" (§ 8-203(f)(1)(i)). Damage covers the unit, common areas, major appliances and the landlord's furnishings.

Two limits:

- The deposit "is not liquidated damages." It may cover only the landlord's actual damage from a breach (§ 8-203(f)(2)).
- For lost future rent, any rent from a new tenant for the rest of the term reduces the claim (§ 8-203(f)(3)).

A court may let the landlord deduct the eviction filing surcharge, up to $43, if the landlord won possession and the lease allows it (Cts. & Jud. Proc. § 7-301(c)(5)).

## Does a Maryland landlord have to give an itemized list of deductions?

Yes. If any part is withheld, the landlord sends, "by first-class mail directed to the last known address of the tenant, within 45 days after the termination of the tenancy, a written list of the damages claimed" with "an itemized statement of the cost incurred" (§ 8-203(g)(1)).

The statement must include "supporting documentation that identifies the materials or services provided" (§ 8-203(j)(1)). An estimate is allowed. If you use one, send the final invoice when repairs are done, and refund any excess within 30 days (§ 8-203(k)).

If you skip the list, you forfeit the right to withhold any part for damages (§ 8-203(g)(2)).

**Move-out inspection.** A tenant who mails notice by certified mail at least 15 days before moving may attend the inspection. The landlord answers by certified mail with a time within 5 days before or after the move date. Tell the tenant of this right in writing when you take the deposit. If you do not follow the rule, you forfeit the right to withhold for damages (§ 8-203(f)(1)).

## Must a Maryland landlord pay interest or keep the deposit in a separate account?

Yes to both.

**Account (§ 8-203(d)).** Hold deposits in federally insured financial institutions that do business in Maryland. The accounts must be at branches in the State, "devoted exclusively to security deposits and bear interest." Deposit each payment within 30 days. Insured CDs or U.S. or Maryland securities are also allowed. Creditors of the landlord or tenant may not attach the deposits.

**Interest (§ 8-203(e)).** Pay simple interest at the daily U.S. Treasury yield curve rate for 1 year, as of the first business day of each year, or 1.5% a year, whichever is greater. Interest accrues monthly from the day of payment and is not compounded. No interest is due on deposits under $50, if you held the deposit under 6 months, or for a part month. The Department of Housing and Community Development keeps the rates or a calculator online, and you may rely on it (§ 8-203(m), (n)).

Worked example at the 1.5% floor: a $1,500 deposit held 24 full months earns $1,500 x 1.5% x 2 = $45. If the Treasury rate for a year is higher, use it for that year.

## What is the penalty for a Maryland landlord who keeps a deposit wrongly?

If the landlord, "without a reasonable basis," fails to return any part with interest within 45 days, "the tenant has an action of up to threefold of the withheld amount, plus reasonable attorney's fees" (§ 8-203(e)(4)).

| Item | Example |
|---|---|
| Amount withheld without basis | $900 |
| Up to 3x | $2,700 |
| Plus | Reasonable attorney fees |

Charging over the cap carries the same up to 3x remedy on the extra amount (§ 8-203(b)(3)). The tenant may sue during the tenancy or within 2 years after it ends (§ 8-203(b)(4)). No lease may waive § 8-203 (§ 8-203(l)).

## Maryland deposit checklist

- Cap the deposit at 1 month's rent, including any prepaid last month.
- Put the deposit receipt and inspection rights in the written lease.
- Deposit the money within 30 days in a Maryland-branch, interest-bearing account.
- Return the deposit with interest within 45 days.
- Mail an itemized list with invoices or estimates within 45 days.
- Track the interest rate each year from DHCD.
