Check first if the law covers you. RSA 540-A:5, I excludes 2 groups from the deposit rules. One is a person who rents a single-family house and owns no other rental. The other is an owner who lives in a building of 5 units or less. But any unit in that building with a tenant aged 60 or older stays covered.

## What is the maximum security deposit a landlord can charge in New Hampshire?

The maximum security deposit in New Hampshire is one month's rent or $100, whichever is greater. RSA 540-A:6, I(a) sets this cap. For a $1,600 monthly rent, the cap is $1,600. For a $90 rent, the cap is $100.

The definition is wide. RSA 540-A:5, II says a security deposit is "all funds in excess of the monthly rent" that the tenant pays "for any purpose". So a fee you call a pet deposit or a cleaning deposit counts toward the cap.

A lease with rent paid each quarter or less often is a special case. The deposit on top of the first rent payment still may not exceed one month's rent.

At move-in, follow these steps:

1. **Give a signed receipt.** It states the amount and where you hold the deposit. You can skip the receipt when the tenant pays by personal check, bank check or an agency check.
2. **Tell the tenant in writing** to list any repair needs within 5 days of move-in. This notice is required even with no receipt.
3. **Deposit the money in trust.** Use a separate account, not your own.

## How many days does a New Hampshire landlord have to return a security deposit?

A New Hampshire landlord has 30 days from the end of the tenancy to return the deposit. RSA 540-A:7, I adds that you also pay any interest due in that time.

The deadline has one escape. RSA 540-A:8, II says you are not liable if the tenant failed to give a new address. A deposit unclaimed for 6 months after the tenancy ends becomes yours, absent fraud.

If you sell the building, the deposit moves with it. RSA 540-A:6, III says you turn it over to the buyer within 5 days of the deed. Then notify the tenant by registered or certified mail, with the buyer's name and address. After that, the buyer is responsible for the return.

## What can a landlord deduct from a security deposit in New Hampshire?

A New Hampshire landlord may deduct 4 kinds of costs, under RSA 540-A:7:

- **Repair of damage**, but not reasonable wear and tear.
- **Unpaid rent.**
- **The tenant's share of a real estate tax increase**, if the lease requires it.
- **Other lawful charges** under the lease that remain unpaid.

A worked example. The tenant paid a $1,600 deposit. The tenant held the unit for 10 months, so no interest is due. At move-out, a door needs a $250 repair, and $400 rent is unpaid.

| Line | Amount |
|---|---|
| Deposit held | $1,600 |
| Door repair, with invoice | -$250 |
| Unpaid rent | -$400 |
| Refund due within 30 days | $950 |

## Does a New Hampshire landlord have to give an itemized list of deductions?

Yes, a New Hampshire landlord must give a written, itemized list for every deduction. RSA 540-A:7 sets 2 kinds of lists.

For damage, the list must:

- [ ] Name each damage the tenant is liable for.
- [ ] Describe "with particularity" the repair each one needs.
- [ ] Include satisfactory evidence that the repair is done or will be done.

Satisfactory evidence may include receipts for materials, labor estimates, bills or invoices.

For unpaid rent, taxes or other charges, the list must state the period for each claim. Send the list with the refund, inside the 30 days.

## Must a New Hampshire landlord pay interest or keep the deposit in a separate account?

A New Hampshire landlord must keep the deposit apart from personal money and pay interest after one year. RSA 540-A:6, II says the deposit stays the tenant's money and you hold it in trust.

You have 2 ways to comply:

1. **A trust account.** You may pool all deposits in one trust account at a New Hampshire bank, savings and loan or credit union.
2. **A bond.** A New Hampshire bond company writes a bond for the total deposits in that town. You post it with the city or town clerk. The bond exempts you from the trust account rule.

Interest applies when you hold a deposit for one year or longer (RSA 540-A:6, IV). The rate is the regular savings rate at the institution that holds it. In a pooled account, you pay each tenant a share of the actual interest earned. On request, you must tell the tenant the bank, account number, amount and rate. A tenant may also ask for the accrued interest every 3 years.

## What is the penalty for a New Hampshire landlord who keeps a deposit wrongly?

A New Hampshire landlord who breaks the return or interest rules can owe twice the deposit plus interest. RSA 540-A:8, I(b) sets damages at "twice the sum of the amount of the security deposit plus any interest due". The statute then takes off payments made and charges owing for damage, rent or taxes.

Example: you held a $1,600 deposit, owed no interest and had no valid charges. You returned nothing. The claim could reach 2 × $1,600 = $3,200.

Breaking the cap, receipt, trust or sale-transfer rules is a different violation. RSA 540-A:8, I(a) treats it as an unfair practice under RSA 358-A:2. Under RSA 358-A:10, the tenant gets actual damages or $1,000, whichever is greater. A willful violation brings 2 to 3 times that amount, plus attorney's fees.

A lease clause that waives these rights is void (RSA 540-A:8, III).
