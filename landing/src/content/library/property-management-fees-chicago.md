## What is the average monthly management fee in Chicago?

The middle monthly management fee on 10 Chicago-area price pages is 8% of collected rent. We read each page on 2026-10-19. We did not use survey averages from past years: a published price is the number an owner can check today.

| City Roots Properties | 6–8% | 1 month's rent | $200 | No fee when vacant |
| Sync Properties | 7–9% single-family, 6–8% condo, 4–10% multifamily | 1 month's rent | Not listed | $0 repair markup, $150/hour legal work |
| Kettle & Oak | Flat $129 per unit (under 4 units), $109 (4+), $85 (20+) | Not listed | Not listed | No contracts |

Source: each company's price page, linked below. Related party: Fridman Properties is our client, and its owner is CEO of MyRemotely, our partner.

How we got 8%: we took one number per percent-based firm (the stated rate, or the middle of a range) and sorted them: 7, 7, 8, 8, 8, 8, 8.5, 9, 10. The middle value is 8%. Keyrenter Chicago North's page shows a "$275 minimum" next to the 9% fee. Ask the firm what that minimum covers.

## What leasing and renewal fees do Chicago managers charge?

Chicago managers most often charge one full month's rent to place a new tenant. Seven of the 10 pages say so. ALPS charges half a month. Fridman Properties lists no placement fee and no screening fee. Kettle & Oak does not list one on its price page.

Renewal fees come in 2 forms:

- **A flat fee:** $175 (MF Cashflow), $200 (City Roots), $250 (ALPS, Keyrenter Chicago Metro), $299 (Chicago's Property Management).
- **A percent of one month's rent:** GC Realty charges 10% to 25%, based on the rent level. At $1,250 to $2,000 rent, the rate is 25%, so $500 on a $2,000 rent.

Watch for: a renewal fee in percent grows each year your rent grows. MF Cashflow makes this point on its own page, and the math agrees.

## Which local rules add cost for Chicago owners?

The Chicago Residential Landlord and Tenant Ordinance (RLTO) adds work that a good manager does for you. Under Municipal Code 5-12-080, deposits must sit in a separate, interest-bearing account at an Illinois bank. Interest is paid each year. A deposit mistake can cost 2 times the deposit plus interest.

Other cost drivers to price in:

- **Notice tracking.** Chicago requires 30, 60 or 120 days' written notice before a lease ends or the rent rises (5-12-130(j)). A missed date can delay a rent increase.
- **Voucher units.** Chicago Housing Authority inspections and rent increase requests take staff time. HUD's FY2027 fair market rent for a Chicago-area 2-bedroom rose 12.9% to $2,011. That raises the rent ceiling for many voucher units, and so the fee in dollars. See [Chicago fair market rent 2027](/guides/section-8-rent-limits/chicago-fair-market-rent-2027).
- **Repair markups.** Sync Properties lists a $0 markup. Fridman Properties charges $50 per repair request. Ask each manager in writing.

This is general information, not legal advice. Check the current law or ask a local attorney.

## Must a Chicago property manager hold an Illinois license?

Real estate work for others in Illinois falls under the Real Estate License Act of 2000 (225 ILCS 454). The Illinois Department of Financial and Professional Regulation (IDFPR) issues broker ($150), managing broker ($175), residential leasing agent ($100) and firm ($250) licenses (IDFPR brochure). The Illinois General Assembly site did not answer when we checked, so we could not quote the section on who must hold which license.

What to do: search the firm and its managing broker on the IDFPR License Lookup. Write both license numbers in the management agreement.

## Flat fee or percent: which model is common in Chicago?

The percent model is common in Chicago: 9 of the 10 price pages use it. Kettle & Oak is the one flat-fee firm in our set, at $129 per unit per month for owners with fewer than 4 units.

Break-even check:

| Rent | 8% fee | Flat $129 | Cheaper |
|---|---|---|---|
| $1,250 | $100 | $129 | 8% |
| $1,612 | $129 | $129 | Same |
| $2,000 | $160 | $129 | Flat |
| $2,600 | $208 | $129 | Flat |

Two points to weigh. A flat fee does not drop when the unit is vacant unless the contract says so. A percent fee on collected rent drops to $0 in a vacant month at ALPS, City Roots and Sync Properties, as their pages state. Compare the full contract, not the headline number.
