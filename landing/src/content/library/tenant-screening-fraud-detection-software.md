## How common is application fraud?

Application fraud is a risk to test for, but vendor samples do not establish a universal landlord fraud rate. A rate can change with the properties, documents, and applicants included.

Do not use a vendor's customer sample as the rate for your city. Ask how the study defined fraud and who reviewed the flags. Separate suspected edits from confirmed false income.

Your own baseline can be more useful. Track applications reviewed, documents flagged, flags cleared, and cases still open. Use the same definitions across staff.

Do not record an unclear file as confirmed fraud just to close the task. A document may fail a check because of its format or source coverage. Keep the original file, the reason for the flag, and the result of follow-up.

## How does Snappt check documents?

Snappt says its document tool analyzes metadata and document features to check authenticity. Its platform also offers source-connected income verification and identity checks. Those are related checks with different purposes.

Ask for a sample result that explains what the software found. A useful result identifies the file and issue. A red label without detail makes a fair review harder.

Snappt's pricing page lists payroll and bank linking, document analysis, and review by a fraud team within its bundles. Confirm which routes are available for your applicant income types.

Test a genuine file with an unusual layout alongside a known altered training file. Use files you have permission to test. Ask how staff can challenge a finding and request further review.

## Can tools detect AI-made documents?

Document tools may flag AI-made records, but the creation method does not guarantee detection. A claim about finding altered files is not proof that every synthetic record will fail.

Ask the vendor to show how it handles a fully invented document. That differs from a genuine statement with edited figures. Find out whether the system recognizes the issuer or checks connected data.

Prefer corroboration over visual judgment. Compare the claimed income with an authorized source connection or independently verified issuer. A convincing logo and clean type do not establish origin.

Do not upload applicant files to a public chatbot for a second opinion. Use an approved review system with suitable access controls. Record the evidence staff relied on, rather than an unsupported AI accusation.

## What manual checks still work?

Manual checks still help when staff verify facts through a route independent of the uploaded file. Start with the issuer, dates, pay period, and the applicant's explanation.

Use a published employer contact rather than only a number printed on a pay stub. Seek permission where needed, and request only information relevant to your written criteria.

Compare the income periods consistently. A deposit date, pay period, and statement month may not match neatly. Ask for a plain explanation before treating the difference as deception.

Build a review checklist:

- **Confirm the file source.** Ask for the original issuer download through an approved route.
- **Check the period.** Align the claimed income with the period your policy measures.
- **Review separate evidence.** Compare authorized source records without double-counting transfers.
- **Document the response.** Give staff a place to record clarification and the result.
- **Escalate uncertainty.** Let a trained reviewer decide what evidence is sufficient.

Avoid broad rules that assume unfamiliar employers or non-payroll income are false. Apply the same evidence standards to each applicant.

## What does fraud detection cost?

Snappt is quote only on the pricing page read 2026-10-19. Request a quote for your application volume, needed checks, and staff review process.

Ask whether the fee applies per application, file, unit, or subscription. Include rechecks, manual reviews, identity services, and integrations. A bundle price can conceal a limit that matters during a busy leasing period.

Keep decision rules separate from vendor flags. The FTC says an adverse action based partly on a consumer report requires notice. Ask counsel how each vendor report fits your process.

Do not assume a fraud product removes notice or dispute duties. This is general information, not legal advice. Check the current law or ask a local attorney.

## How should you test the choice before you pay?

Test the full workflow with sample records before you move live data. Use the same case in each product. Ask the person who will do the daily work to run the test. A sales demo alone does not show where your team will need help.

Keep a decision sheet with the task, result, missing step, and person who checked it. Mark each result as shown, promised, or still open. Ask for written terms when a feature needs an upgrade or another service. Include staff setup time in your cost review.

Check the exit path while you still control the choice. Export the sample records and open them outside the platform. Confirm that notes, files, dates, and links remain useful. Ask how you will get records after you close the account.
