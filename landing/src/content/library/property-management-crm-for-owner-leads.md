## Which CRMs do property managers use for owner leads?

Property managers use 3 main kinds of CRM for owner leads. LeadSimple is built for property management. HubSpot is a general sales CRM. HighLevel is an all-in-one marketing platform. Here are the prices from each vendor's own page, read on 2026-10-19.

| CRM | Built for | Starting price | What the price includes | Watch for |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| LeadSimple One | Property management | $199/month | 50 doors, 2 CRM sales pipelines, unlimited teammates, 500 calling minutes, 1,000 messaging credits | $2.49 per extra door up to 250 doors; extra pipelines $30/month each |
| LeadSimple One Max | Property management | $499/month | 100 doors, unlimited pipelines, calling and texting within fair use | Same per-door price above 100 doors |
| HubSpot Free | Any business | $0 | Free for up to 2 users | Few automation tools |
| HubSpot Sales Hub Starter | Any business | $7 to $20 per seat/month | Pipeline management | The page shows two prices by billing term |
| HubSpot Sales Hub Professional | Any business | $90 to $100 per seat/month | More automation and reports | Required $1,500 one-time onboarding |
| HighLevel Starter | Agencies and small businesses | $97/month | CRM and pipelines, forms, calendars, unlimited contacts and users | Calls, texts, email and AI use cost extra |
| HighLevel Unlimited | Agencies | $297/month | Unlimited sub-accounts, basic API | Built for agencies with many clients |

LeadSimple charges per door, so its price grows with your portfolio. LeadSimple lists the per-door price as $2.49 up to 250 doors, $1.99 from 251 to 1,000 doors, and $1.79 above 1,000 doors.

## Does my property management software have a lead CRM?

Your property management software may have a CRM, but it is often for renters, not owners. AppFolio lists "Leasing CRM" as part of its Max plan, which starts at $5.00 per unit per month with a minimum spend. A leasing CRM tracks people who want to rent a unit. An owner sales pipeline tracks people who may hire you to manage their property.

Ask your vendor 3 questions in the demo:

1. Can I track owner leads separately from renter prospects?
2. Can I set stages, owners (staff) and follow-up tasks for each owner lead?
3. Can I see the lead source and the doors for each signed owner in a report?

If the answer to any question is no, use a separate CRM for owner sales. Keep the property management software for accounting and operations. For a software price comparison, see [AppFolio vs Buildium](/guides/appfolio-vs-buildium).

## Do LeadSimple, HubSpot or HighLevel fit property management sales?

Each tool fits a different team. The right pick depends on your door count, your team size and who will set it up.

- **LeadSimple** fits a team that wants a property management tool with sales and operations workflows in one place. You pay per door, so check the cost at your door count. At 150 doors, the list math is $199 + (100 × $2.49) = $448 per month, before extra usage.
- **HubSpot** fits a small team that wants a clean pipeline at low cost. The free plan covers 2 users. Advanced automation starts at Professional, with a $1,500 onboarding fee.
- **HighLevel** fits a team that wants forms, texting, email, calendars and review requests in one tool. It is built for agencies, so many property managers get it through their marketing agency. Ask who owns the account and the data if you leave that agency.

Watch for: a CRM that your agency owns. What to do: ask in writing that you can export all contacts, notes and history at any time.

## What should an owner lead pipeline look like?

An owner lead pipeline needs few stages, and each stage needs a clear exit rule. Here is a simple 6-stage pipeline.

| Stage | Exit rule | Target time |
|---|---|---|
| 1. New lead | First reply sent (call, text or email) | Minutes, not hours |
| 2. Contacted | Two-way talk happened | Same day |
| 3. Qualified | Property, doors, goals and timing fit your service | 1 to 3 days |
| 4. Proposal sent | Fee sheet and agreement sent | Within 24 hours of the call |
| 5. Signed | Agreement signed, doors added | Owner's timeline |
| 6. Lost | Reason recorded from a fixed list | Any stage |

Add these fields to each lead: lead source, address, doors, property type, status and lost reason. Status values: vacant, tenant in place, self-managed, with another manager.

The lead source field matters most. The Goodjuju 2026 lead source report found that GA4 missed about 4 in 10 of the AI leads Goodjuju could trace on its own site. Goodjuju recommends a required "How did you first hear about us?" field on the form. Ask it again in the first reply, and store the answer in the CRM. That gives you a source for each signed owner, not only for each website visit.

## What do these tools cost for 100 doors?

At 100 doors, the list prices on 2026-10-19 work out as follows. These are floor prices before usage and taxes.

| Tool | Monthly math | Monthly floor |
|---|---|---|
| HubSpot Free, 2 users | $0 | $0 |
| HubSpot Starter, 2 seats | 2 × $20 | $40 |
| HighLevel Starter | Flat | $97 |
| LeadSimple One | $199 + (50 × $2.49) | $323.50 |
| LeadSimple One Max | Flat (100 doors included) | $499 |

The cheapest tool is not always the cheapest choice. A CRM pays for itself if it helps you sign one more owner a year. Count the setup time too: a tool nobody updates is worth $0.
