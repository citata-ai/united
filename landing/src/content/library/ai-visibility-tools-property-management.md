## What does each AI visibility tool track?

Each AI visibility tool runs a list of prompts (questions you type into an AI) on a set of engines (ChatGPT, Gemini and others). It then records which brands the answers name and which sites they cite. The tools differ in engines, prompt limits and price.

| Tool | Engines named on the page | Entry plan | Prompts on entry plan | Free trial |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| OtterlyAI | ChatGPT, Perplexity, Google AI Overviews, Copilot; AI Mode, Gemini, Claude as add-ons | Lite, $29/mo | 15 | Yes, length not stated |
| Peec AI | ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini named; choose 3 models per plan | Starter, price not in page text | 50, daily | Not stated |
| Profound | ChatGPT, Gemini, AI Overviews on trial; up to 9 engines on Enterprise | Trial free; Enterprise custom | 50 for 7 days on trial | Yes, 7 days |
| Ahrefs Brand Radar | AI Overviews, AI Mode, ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini, Copilot; Claude | Custom prompts from $50/mo | Not stated | Custom prompts free with a paid Ahrefs Lite plan or above |
| Semrush AI Visibility Toolkit | ChatGPT and Google AI Mode named | $99/mo | Semrush One plans: 50, 100 or 200 | Not stated |
| Trakkr | ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Perplexity, AI Overviews, Grok, DeepSeek, Meta AI | Growth, £79/mo | 50 per brand, daily | 14 days |

Source: each vendor's own pricing or product page, read 2026-10-19. Prices change often. Check the page before you buy.

Watch for: two pages showed unclear prices. OtterlyAI shows two price sets ($29 and $25 for Lite) without a clear label for monthly or annual. Trakkr shows prices in GBP but says it bills in USD, and its FAQ says Growth is "$100/mo after 14 days".

Ahrefs Brand Radar is different from the others. It also has a large prompt database (the page says 492M+ monthly prompts), so you can look up prompts you did not set up yourself. Its AI Visibility Index starts at $199 a month.

## What is the price per prompt?

The price per prompt is the monthly price divided by the number of prompts the plan tracks. Only some vendors publish both numbers, so we can compute it for 5 plans.

| Plan | Monthly price | Prompts | Price per prompt per month |
|---|---|---|---|
| OtterlyAI Lite | $29 | 15 | $1.93 |
| OtterlyAI Standard | $189 | 100 | $1.89 |
| OtterlyAI Premium | $489 | 400 | $1.22 |
| Trakkr Growth | £79 | 50 | £1.58 |
| Trakkr Scale (10 brands) | £395 | 500 | £0.79 |

We did the math from the list prices on 2026-10-19 (higher OtterlyAI price set). Engine count is not equal across plans. OtterlyAI base plans cover 4 engines. Trakkr lists 8 models on each plan. Add-ons raise the cost: Google AI Mode and Gemini cost $9 a month more on OtterlyAI Lite, and Claude costs $29 a month more.

Peec AI, Profound and Semrush did not show both numbers in the page text we read, so we left them out of this table.

## Can you track AI visibility by hand?

Yes, you can track AI visibility by hand, and for one city it is the best way to start. You learn what owners see before you pay for a dashboard.

This is how we do it for our own site:

1. **Write 20 prompts.** Use the questions owners ask, such as "best property manager in <city> for a single-family home".
2. **Pick 3 engines.** We use ChatGPT, Gemini and Claude.
3. **Run each prompt twice.** AI answers change from run to run. Two runs show what is stable.
4. **Log 4 fields per answer:** engine, prompt, companies named, sources cited.
5. **Repeat monthly** on the same prompts, so you can compare.

That gives 20 × 3 × 2 = 120 answers per run. In our 2026-10-19 run, it showed us a hard fact: AI named citata.ai in 0 of 108 non-brand answers (our data, 2026-10-19). A paid tool would have told us the same. The hand run cost only time.

Hand tracking has limits. Logged-in accounts, location and chat history change answers. A tool runs the same setup each time. When you pass about 20 to 30 prompts, or you add a second city, a tool saves hours.

## Which tool fits a 100-door company?

A 100-door company with one or two service cities needs few prompts and few engines. Here is a simple fit.

| Your situation | Good fit | Why |
|---|---|---|
| One city, under 20 prompts, no budget | By hand, monthly | Free; you see the real answers |
| One city, wants a dashboard | OtterlyAI Lite | Lowest listed entry price, 15 prompts |
| Two or three cities, 30 to 50 prompts | Trakkr Growth or Peec AI Starter | 50 prompts tracked daily |
| Already pays for Ahrefs | Ahrefs Brand Radar custom prompts | Free with a paid Ahrefs Lite plan or above |
| Already pays for Semrush | Semrush AI Visibility Toolkit | $99/mo, adds AI to tools you use |
| Large portfolio, many markets | Profound Enterprise or Peec AI Enterprise | Custom prompts, more engines |

Whatever you pick, measure the same 3 numbers each month. Count the answers that name you and the answers that cite your site. Then list the sources the answers cite most. The source list tells you where to work next. In our 50-city run, expertise.com was a source in 93 of 150 answers, and yelp.com and ipropertymanagement.com in 50 each (our data, 2026-10-19).

## What should you check before you buy?

Check five things before you buy any AI visibility tool:

1. **Engines.** Does the base plan include ChatGPT? It gave 37 of 43 AI owner leads in a 35-company study (Goodjuju Marketing, fall 2026).
2. **Location.** Can you set the city? Local answers change by place.
3. **Sources.** Does the tool show the cited URLs, not only brand mentions?
4. **Run count.** Does it run each prompt more than once? One run can mislead.
5. **Export.** Can you get the raw answers as CSV? You need them for your own report.
