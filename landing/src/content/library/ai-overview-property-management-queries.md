## Which property management searches show a Google AI Overview?

Question searches show AI Overviews most. Local "find a business" searches show them least. Two studies of local business searches agree on this split, with 15 months between them.

| Study | Date | Sample | Local-intent searches with an AI Overview | Informational searches with an AI Overview |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Whitespark | May 2025 | 540 searches, 3 cities, 6 industries | 15% | 92% |
| Steady Demand (review by Bill Hartzer) | August 2026 | 7,990 searches, 50 US metros, 10 verticals | 21.8% | 75.8% |

Whitespark also tested "hybrid" searches, which mix a local need with a question. Those showed AI Overviews 97% of the time.

Neither study tested property management. Whitespark studied plumbers, personal injury lawyers, dentists, optometrists, medical clinics and real estate agents. The real estate agent group is the closest to property management. Whitespark calls real estate an outlier: up to 50% of its local-intent searches showed an AI Overview.

So read the numbers as a pattern, not a measure of your market. The pattern is strong across both studies. Your own test (below) gives the real number for your city.

## Do "near me" searches show AI Overviews?

"Near me" searches are local-intent searches, and local-intent searches show AI Overviews the least. In Whitespark's study, local-intent searches showed the map pack 93% of the time and an AI Overview 15% of the time. Neither study broke out the words "near me" alone.

For a property manager, this means "property management near me" and "property manager in Tampa" still lean on the map pack. Your Google Business Profile, reviews and categories carry that result. A local-intent search with a question in it is different. "Best property management company in Tampa for single-family homes" is closer to a hybrid search. In the Whitespark study, hybrid searches showed AI Overviews 97% of the time.

## Do fee questions show AI Overviews?

Fee questions are informational searches, the type that shows AI Overviews most: 92% in Whitespark's study and 75.8% in Steady Demand's. ClearLead Digital, a property management marketing agency, names these searches as ones that show AI Overviews:

- "what does a property manager do"
- "average property management fees"
- "is a property manager worth it"

ClearLead also suggests a self-test: search "average property management fees in [your city]". We did not run that test for this page, so we do not report a result.

Pew Research Center found that longer searches show AI summaries more often. In March 2025 data from 900 US adults, searches of 1 or 2 words showed a summary 8% of the time. Searches of 10 or more words showed one 53% of the time. Owner fee questions are often long: "how much does a property manager charge for a single-family home in Phoenix".

Our [property management fee calculator](/tools/property-management-fee-calculator) gives owners a number for their own rent. A fee page with clear numbers is the kind of page an AI Overview can quote.

## Do landlord law questions show AI Overviews?

No public study we found measured landlord law searches. Two facts from Pew point the same way as the fee data:

- Searches that started with a question word showed an AI summary 60% of the time.
- .gov sites made up 6% of AI summary sources, against 2% of normal results.

A search such as "how long does a landlord have to return a security deposit in Illinois" is a long question. By the Pew pattern, it is likely to show a summary. The .gov share suggests that state sites compete for the citation. A law page on your site needs the statute number and a link to the official text to have a chance.

## How often does the list of AI Overview searches change?

AI Overviews change often. Ahrefs tracked 43,000 keywords for one month in 2025. Each keyword had at least 16 recorded AI Overviews. The results:

- A 70% chance that the AI Overview changed from one check to the next.
- 45.5% of cited sources were new between checks.
- An average AI Overview lasted 2.15 days before it changed.
- The meaning stayed close: an average similarity of 0.95 out of 1.0.

So the text and the sources move every few days, but the message stays about the same. The two local studies also show the share of local searches with an AI Overview at 15% in 2025 and 21.8% in 2026. The methods differ, so do not read that gap as exact growth.

Being cited still links to rank. In an earlier Ahrefs study, 76% of URLs cited in AI Overviews came from pages in the top 10. A page that does not rank well is rarely the source.

## How to test your own property management searches

Run a 4-week test with 20 searches. It takes about 20 minutes a week.

1. **Pick 20 searches in 4 groups.** 5 local ("property manager near me"), 5 fee ("property management fees [city]"), 5 law ("security deposit return [state]"), 5 how-to ("how to choose a property manager").
2. **Use a private window.** Sign out of Google. Search from your service city.
3. **Record 3 facts per search.** AI Overview: yes or no. Your site cited: yes or no. Top 3 cited sites.
4. **Repeat each week for 4 weeks.** Use the same day and the same device.
5. **Count the share per group.** For example: AI Overview in 14 of 20 fee checks = 70%.

Use a sheet with these columns:

| Week | Search | Group | AI Overview shown | Your site cited | Top 3 cited sites |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | property management fees Tampa | Fee | | | |
| 1 | property manager near me | Local | | | |

After 4 weeks, you have 80 checks. Put your effort where AI Overviews show and your site is not yet cited. For local searches, the map pack and your listings come first.
