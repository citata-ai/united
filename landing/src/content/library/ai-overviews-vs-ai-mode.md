## What is the difference between AI Overviews and AI Mode?

AI Overviews is a short summary on top of normal Google results. AI Mode is a separate chat tab for long questions and follow-ups. Both come from Google Search, and both can cite your property management website.

Google describes them this way on its Search Central page about AI features. AI Overviews help people grasp a topic fast. AI Mode is "particularly helpful for queries where further exploration, reasoning, or complex comparisons are needed." Google also says AI Overviews show "only when our systems determine that it is additive to classic Search."

AI Mode is now large. Google wrote in May 2026 that AI Mode passed 1 billion monthly users worldwide, one year after its US launch. Google also says the average AI Mode search is 3 times as long as a normal search.

Here is the side-by-side view for a property manager:

| Point | AI Overviews | AI Mode |
|---|---|---|
| Where it shows | On top of normal results, on some searches | In its own tab |
| Follow-up questions | No; it offers a link to "Dive deeper in AI Mode" (Whitespark) | Yes, with context kept |
| Answer length | Short | About 4 times longer (Ahrefs) |
| People and brands named per answer | 1.3 on average (Ahrefs) | 3.3 on average (Ahrefs) |
| Answers with no citation | 11% (Ahrefs) | 3% (Ahrefs) |
| Wikipedia share of citations | 18.1% (Ahrefs) | 28.9% (Ahrefs) |
| Setup to appear | Indexed page, eligible for a snippet | The same |
| Where traffic shows in GA4 | Organic search | Organic search |

The Ahrefs numbers come from a study by Despina Gavoyannis, published December 15, 2025. It used US data from September 2025: 730,000 query pairs for content and 540,000 for citations.

## Do AI Overviews and AI Mode cite the same sources?

AI Overviews and AI Mode cite different sources most of the time. Ahrefs found 13.7% citation overlap across 540,000 query pairs. In the top 3 citations of each answer, the overlap was 16.3%.

The meaning is close, though. The same study found an average semantic similarity of 86%. Word overlap was only 16%. The two answers started with the same first sentence 2.51% of the time.

Google's own page agrees in plain words. It says the two features "may use different models and techniques, so the set of responses and links they show will vary."

For a property manager, this means one check is not enough. Your fee page can be cited in the AI Overview for "property management fees Dallas". The AI Mode answer to the same search can skip it. Check both.

## Which one shows local business lists?

AI Mode is the one that shows lists of local businesses with details. Whitespark's guide to AI Mode for local businesses (May 2026) says AI Mode gives "AI-generated summaries about businesses". The summaries include star ratings, review counts, pricing and hours. Whitespark also says "reviews have emerged as one of the key sources of information" in AI Mode.

The Ahrefs entity numbers point the same way. In Ahrefs' data, 59.41% of AI Overviews named no person or brand at all. For AI Mode, the share was 34.66%. And AI Mode included every entity the AI Overview named in 61% of cases.

Whitespark warns about 2 more things:

- "Each user may see different AI Mode results for the same query."
- "Your local pack rankings are not a guarantee of equal visibility in AI Mode."

So an owner who types "best property manager in Dallas for duplexes" into AI Mode can get a list of named companies. In an AI Overview, the same search more often gets a general answer with no names. Your Google Business Profile and your reviews feed the AI Mode list.

## Which one takes more clicks?

Both features can take clicks from your site, and the best public data covers AI Overviews. Pew Research Center tracked 900 US adults in March 2025. When a Google search showed an AI summary, people clicked a normal result in 8% of visits. Without a summary, they clicked in 15%. Links inside the summary got about 1% of visits.

We did not find a public click study for AI Mode that we could check. Two facts suggest the effect is different. AI Mode answers are about 4 times longer, by Ahrefs. And AI Mode keeps the person in a chat with follow-ups. A longer answer with more names may send fewer clicks but better ones: an owner who clicks has already read about you.

Treat that as a hypothesis. Measure it on your own site. Google says traffic from AI features is part of the "Web" search type in the Search Console Performance report. In Google Analytics, the AI Assistant channel excludes AI Overviews and AI Mode, so both count as organic search. ## Do you need different pages for each?

No. Use one set of pages for both. Google says: "There are no additional requirements to appear in AI Overviews or AI Mode, nor other special optimizations necessary." A page must be indexed and eligible to show with a snippet.

What differs is which part of your presence each feature reads most. Use this checklist:

- [ ] **For AI Overviews:** a fee page and how-to pages that answer one question in the first sentence.
- [ ] **For AI Mode:** a complete Google Business Profile, recent reviews and real hours.
- [ ] **For both:** one page per service city that names the city, the home types and your company.
- [ ] **For both:** the same name, address and phone on your site and every listing.
- [ ] **For both:** a check of 10 owner searches in each feature, once a month.

The last point matters most. With 13.7% overlap, a win in one feature tells you little about the other. Log each search, each feature and each cited site in one sheet. After 3 months, you will see which feature names you and which one does not.
