## How much should a property manager spend on a marketing agency?

A property manager should spend what the new doors can pay back. A useful test is the door math that Goodjuju Marketing prints on its price page: one door × $180 a month × 36 months, or $6,480 of fees per door. We use that method below. Your own fee per door may be higher or lower, so put in your own number.

## Which plan fits your door count?

Fit by door count is our view, based on the door math above. It is not an agency rule.

1. **Under 100 doors: spend under about $800/mo.** Fix your Google Business Profile, your owner pages and your reviews first. Fourandhalf Launch, PMW (if you use Rentvine) and Citata.ai Starter sit here.
2. **100 to 300 doors: $1,000 to $2,300/mo.** You can carry a full SEO plan. Goodjuju Emerge or Takeover, ClearLead Digital Growth Engine and Citata.ai Growth sit here. Pick one main channel before you add ads.
3. **Over 300 doors or several cities: ask for a written quote.** Upkeep Media, Goodjuju Dominate or Empire, and ClearLead Enterprise sell to larger firms. Ask for a report that ties leads to signed doors in each city.

Two more checks matter at every size. First, how much of your owner leads come from search now. Goodjuju's fall 2026 study of 35 PM companies found Google Search and the Map Pack brought 50.6% of owner leads, and AI tools 3.4%. Second, how fast the agency can show a first result in writing.

## How do you check a marketing agency before you sign?

Check ownership, reports, terms and proof, in that order. Our checklist of [20 questions to ask a property management marketing agency](/guides/questions-to-ask-property-management-marketing-agency) gives the answer you want for each one. Here are the 5 that decide most deals:

1. **Who owns the domain and accounts?** Your company must own the domain, the website files, Google Business Profile, GA4 and Google Ads.
2. **How do you count a lead and a signed door?** Get the rule in writing.
3. **What is the notice period to cancel?** Goodjuju asks for 15 days. Ask each agency for its number.
4. **Which results can you prove, with dates?** Ask to call 2 PM clients of your size.
5. **Who checks each page before it goes live?** Bulk pages can break Google's "scaled content abuse" policy (Google Search Central).
