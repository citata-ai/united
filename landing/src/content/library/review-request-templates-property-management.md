A maintenance tech closes a leak at 4 p.m. The tenant is happy, and nobody asks for a review. Three weeks later, a different tenant posts 1 star about a slow reply. The profile now shows the one bad day and none of the good ones.

Reviews need a habit, not a campaign. BrightLocal's Local Consumer Review Survey 2026 found that 74% of consumers care only about reviews from the last 3 months, and 47% will not use a business with fewer than 20 reviews. This guide gives you the messages, the timing and the rules.

## What are the rules for a review request?

A review request is fair when you ask everyone, offer nothing and do not shape the content. Two sets of rules apply.

| Rule | Google Maps policy | FTC Consumer Reviews and Testimonials Rule |
|---|---|---|
| Rewards | No payment, discounts or free services for any review | Incentives are not banned by the rule itself, but not if tied to a positive or negative sentiment |
| Who you ask | No selective requests to happy customers only | Generalized requests to all customers are allowed |
| Content | No requests for specific content, such as a staff name | Paying for a 5-star review is banned, even with a disclosure |
| Pressure | No pressure to review while on site; no staff quotas | No threats or intimidation to stop negative reviews |
| Staff reviews | Conflict-of-interest reviews break policy | Insiders must clearly disclose the relationship |

Sources: Google Maps Help, Prohibited and restricted content; FTC rule Q&A (both read 2026-10-20). The FTC rule took effect on October 21, 2024.

Google's rule is stricter on rewards. So for Google reviews, use no rewards at all, not even a raffle.

## What is a good review request email to an owner?

A good owner review request names the event, asks one question and gives one link. Send it after a moment the owner can judge: the first statement, a lease signed, a turn finished.

```text
Subject: How did we do on [property address]?

Hi [Owner first name],

[Event: The new lease for 123 Oak St. starts on May 1 / Your first owner
statement is in your portal.]

Would you share how working with us has gone so far? An honest review
on Google helps other owners decide if we fit them. It takes about
2 minutes:

[Google review link]

If something is not right, reply to this email. I read every reply.

[Your name]
[Role], [Company]
[Phone]
```

Why it works:

- **One event.** The owner knows what to write about.
- **"Honest review".** It asks for any opinion, not a good one.
- **A reply path.** Problems come to you, but the review link is open to all. You do not filter who gets the link.

## What is a short text request to a tenant?

A tenant text should be under 300 characters, name the event and hold one link. Text only tenants who gave you their mobile number and agreed to texts.

After a closed work order:

```text
Hi [First name], this is [Name] at [Company]. We closed your repair
request for [item] today. If you have a minute, please leave an honest
Google review of how we handled it: [link]. Reply STOP to opt out.
```

After move-in:

```text
Hi [First name], welcome to [address]. How was your move-in with
[Company]? An honest Google review helps other renters: [link].
Questions? Call [phone]. Reply STOP to opt out.
```

After a renewal:

```text
Hi [First name], thanks for renewing at [address]. If you want to share
how the last year went, here is our Google review page: [link].
Reply STOP to opt out.
```

Send the same text to every tenant after the same event. Do not skip tenants who complained last month.

## When should I send a reminder?

Send one reminder, a few days after the first message, and then stop. One reminder catches people who meant to write. A third message is pressure.

A simple schedule:

| Day | Owner (email) | Tenant (text) |
|---|---|---|
| Day 0 | Request, same day as the event | Request, within hours of the event |
| Day 5 | One short reminder | One short reminder |
| Day 6 and after | Nothing | Nothing |

Reminder text:

```text
Hi [First name], a quick follow-up on my note from [day]. If you
have 2 minutes for an honest review, here is the link: [link].
If not, no problem. Thanks, [Name]
```

"If not, no problem" matters. It tells the reader there is no pressure.

## Which words break Google review rules?

Words that offer a reward, ask for a rating or filter who reviews break the rules. Check every template against this list.

| Do not write | Why | Write this |
|---|---|---|
| "Leave us 5 stars" | Asks for a rating | "Leave an honest review" |
| "Get $10 off rent for a review" | Incentive | (no reward) |
| "Enter our raffle when you review" | Incentive | (no reward) |
| "If you are happy, review us. If not, call us" | Selective request | Give the link to all, plus a reply path to all |
| "Please mention Maria by name" | Asks for specific content | "Tell others how it went" |
| "Review us before you leave the office" | Pressure on site | Send a link later |
| "Each agent needs 10 reviews this month" | Staff quota | Track requests sent, not reviews won |

The last row is about your team. Measure how many requests went out after real events. Do not set a review target per person.

## How do I reply when a review comes in?

Reply to every review, short and personal. In the BrightLocal 2026 survey, 89% of consumers expect owners to respond, and 50% say templated or generic replies make them unlikely to choose the business. Google's help page also tells owners to keep replies short and to sign with a name or initials.

Positive review:

```text
Thanks, [first name]. I am glad the [specific event] went smoothly.
[One fact or next step.] — [Your name]
```

Negative review:

```text
[First name], I am sorry about [issue in their words]. We want to fix
this. Please call me at [phone] or email [address]. — [Your name], [Role]
```

Never share tenant details in a public reply.

## Checklist

- [ ] One request per real event, sent the same day
- [ ] Same message to every customer after that event
- [ ] One link, no reward, no star request
- [ ] One reminder, then stop
- [ ] Texts only to people who agreed to texts, with an opt-out
- [ ] No staff review quotas
- [ ] A reply to every review within a week (81% of consumers expect one by then, BrightLocal 2026)
