## Why might a manager leave Propertyware?

A manager should leave when a tested improvement justifies the cost and disruption. Do not assume every older system needs replacement. A process change or a better report may address the actual problem.

Write down the tasks that fail today. Examples include unclear owner statements, duplicate entry and manual renewal tracking. Label these as your team's observations, rather than general claims about Propertyware.

Separate software limits from setup problems. Ask whether training or a configuration change could remove the extra work. Include that option in your comparison. A move has its own cost in staff attention, owner questions and accounting checks.

A vendor's migration story can help you ask questions. It cannot tell you how your own records will move. Ask for a written list of included fields and excluded history. If the new vendor cannot define the work, delay the decision until the scope is clear.

## Which alternatives fit single-family portfolios?

Use scattered homes with different owners as the demo case. A single apartment building may hide the accounting and communication work your team needs to handle.

Rentvine, Buildium, Rent Manager and DoorLoop appear in the table because their own pages describe property operations and accounting. That is a shortlist, not an endorsement. Require proof for your own owner structure and fee rules.

Ask each vendor to show an owner with several properties, a home with shared ownership and a vacant property with expenses. Then ask for owner statements. Look for separate balances and clear explanations of held funds.

Maintenance matters as much as accounting. Test a request that needs owner approval, a vendor bill and a tenant update. Check where each message stays. A system that stores only the final invoice may leave your team searching email for the reason behind a charge.

## How should you compare prices?

Compare a written full quote with the cost of staying. Propertyware's pricing page makes its minimum and implementation formula explicit. Other offers may need a sales quote for the same portfolio and service scope.

Ask what counts as a billable unit. Include vacant homes, inactive records and future growth in that question. Request a clear billing schedule rather than relying on a headline rate.

Make separate rows for subscription, setup, payment processing, screening, signatures, support and connected services. Show which charges repeat and which occur only during the move. Ask how long the quote remains valid.

Use actual recent payment and leasing activity to estimate transaction costs. Mark your calculations as planning examples. Do not assume every tenant will use the cheapest payment method. Review the result with the person who pays vendor invoices. The [management fee calculator](/tools/property-management-fee-calculator) can help you check the effect on your service margin.

## Which alternative has better reviews?

No review winner is established here. A verified feature and a recent reference are more useful than an old score copied across comparison pages.

Ask references about their current version and plan. Find out which system they left, what improved and what they still do manually. A glowing comment from a different property type may not address your needs.

Bring specific questions. Ask how a reference handled a returned payment after an owner payout. Ask what happened when a report did not match the ledger. Ask whether support explained the cause and recorded the fix.

Keep the same questions for each reference. Record concrete examples instead of labels such as easy or powerful. Your team can then compare the work itself. Treat unresolved complaints as demo tests, not as proof that a vendor fails every customer.

## How do you migrate off Propertyware?

Start with an export and a map of where each record belongs. Do this while the old account remains available. A list of tenants alone is not a complete migration.

Use this handoff checklist:

- Save owner, property, unit, tenant and vendor records.
- Export the chart of accounts and open balances.
- Preserve deposits, reserves, unpaid bills and owner contributions.
- Save signed agreements, leases, invoices and inspection records.
- Identify messages and attachments that need a separate archive.
- Confirm who handles payment authorizations and portal invitations.
- Assign a reviewer for every opening balance.

Run a sample import before the final transfer. Check names, dates and links between records. Trace a statement back to the ledger and then to the source documents. Keep a written list of missing data and who will fix it.

Choose the collection cutover only after the accounting checks pass. Give residents clear instructions about the new payment route. Explain what happens to existing automatic payments. Confirm the old route cannot create duplicate collections.

## What should the final decision record show?

The decision record should name the task improved, the total cost and the remaining work. Keep it brief enough for the team to use during onboarding.

Attach the signed scope, sample reports and unresolved questions. Record who owns each part of the move. Keep old reports in a secure archive with clear access rules.

After the first close, compare the new process with the reason you switched. If a report still needs manual repair, fix it before adding more automation.
