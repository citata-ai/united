## What is rent guarantee insurance?

Rent guarantee insurance is a policy or program that pays the owner when a tenant does not pay rent. It replaces lost income for a set number of months or up to a dollar limit. Many policies also pay the legal costs of an eviction.

Rent guarantee insurance is a different product from landlord insurance. The Insurance Information Institute says most landlord policies cover loss of rental income. But they pay only while repairs after a covered event, such as a fire, keep the unit unrentable. A tenant who simply stops paying is not a covered event in a normal landlord policy.

| Product | Pays when | Typical payer of the premium |
|---------|-----------|-----------------------------|
| Landlord insurance, loss of rent | A covered event, like a fire, makes the unit unrentable | Owner |
| Rent guarantee insurance | The tenant does not pay | Owner |
| Lease guarantor service | The tenant does not pay, under the guaranty | Tenant |
| Property manager's rent guarantee | The tenant does not pay, under the management agreement | Owner, through a fee |

## What does rent guarantee insurance pay?

Rent guarantee insurance pays unpaid rent up to the policy limit, and often eviction legal costs. Azibo, a landlord software company, lists 4 types of coverage you may find:

- **Unpaid rent.** The policy pays the missed amount.
- **Legal costs.** Azibo notes "most rent guarantee insurance policies cover legal expenses associated with the eviction process."
- **Vacancy loss.** Only "more comprehensive policies" cover rent lost after the tenant leaves.
- **Tenant damage.** Some policies cover property damage the tenant caused.

Real program terms vary a lot. Here are 3 examples we read on 2026-10-19:

| Program | What it pays | Limit | Cost |
|---------|-------------|-------|------|
| Nomad Guaranteed Rent | 100% of rent, paid on the 5th of each month | Up to 3 months of delinquency; up to $5,000 toward eviction legal fees | 4% of monthly rent paid up front for the lease, or 6% paid monthly; $75 monthly minimum; $99 onboarding fee |
| Sample PM clause A (Law Insider) | 1 month's rent after a court-ordered eviction is carried out | 1 month | $9 per month |
| Sample PM clause B (Law Insider) | Rent when the tenant does not pay | Up to 1 month | 0.8% of collected rent |

The limits are short. Most programs cover a few months, not a full lease.

## How is it different from a rent guarantee from a property manager?

An insurance company backs rent guarantee insurance under a policy. A property manager's rent guarantee is a promise in the management agreement, backed by the manager's own money. The difference matters when many tenants stop paying at once.

| Point | Rent guarantee insurance | Property manager's guarantee |
|-------|-------------------------|-----------------------------|
| Who pays you | An insurer or program provider | The manager |
| Backed by | A policy and its insurer | The manager's own funds |
| Terms set by | The policy wording | The management agreement |
| If you change managers | Policy may stay in place, if you own it | Guarantee usually ends |
| Typical limit | A few months, or a dollar cap | Often 1 month in the samples we read |

Questions to ask a manager who offers a guarantee:

1. Is this insurance, or your own promise?
2. How many months does it pay, and when does payment start?
3. What tenant screening must the tenant pass?
4. What events end the guarantee: early move-out, abandonment, a lease you signed yourself?
5. Do I give up the right to collect the debt from the tenant? One Law Insider sample clause takes that right from the owner.

## What are the common exclusions?

Common exclusions limit when a rent guarantee pays. Azibo lists these:

- **Old arrears.** Most policies "won't cover arrears that existed before you took out the policy."
- **Rent increases.** The insurer "will only cover the original rent amount agreed upon."
- **Time limits.** Coverage usually lasts a set period, often 6 or 12 months.
- **Deliberate damage** by the tenant.

Exclusions we saw in the sample clauses:

- **Early terminations and abandonment.** The first Law Insider sample excludes them, along with military and court-order move-outs.
- **Tenants not screened by the manager.** The same sample requires the manager's own screening and lease.

Common claim rules to check:

- **Waiting period** after the first missed payment.
- **Deadlines** to report the default and start an eviction.
- **Proof** the insurer needs: lease, ledger, notices and screening file.

Missing a deadline can void the claim. Ask your manager who files and by when.

## Is rent guarantee insurance worth it for an owner?

Rent guarantee insurance is worth it when its cost is less than your expected loss, or when one missed month would put your loan at risk. Use your own history to decide.

Break-even example, with made-up numbers and Nomad's published fee rate:

| Line | Amount |
|------|--------|
| Monthly rent | $2,000 |
| Yearly cost at 4% of monthly rent | $80 x 12 = $960 |
| Policy pays at most | 3 months of rent ($6,000) plus up to $5,000 legal |
| Your history: one non-paying tenant in 8 years, lost $6,000 rent plus $2,000 legal | $8,000 / 8 years = $1,000 per year |

In this example, the expected loss ($1,000 a year) is close to the cost ($960 a year). The policy is close to break-even. It still helps if a single $8,000 loss would strain your cash flow.

When it is more likely worth it:

- You own 1 to 3 units, so one bad tenant is a large share of your income.
- Your loan payment needs every month of rent.
- Evictions in your area take a long time.

When it is less likely worth it:

- You have many units and cash reserves, so losses average out.
- Your screening and collections already keep losses low.

Fair Housing: a rent guarantee program must not change your screening rules for any protected class. Apply the same criteria to every applicant.

This is general information, not legal or insurance advice. Read the policy and check the current law.
