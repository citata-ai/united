Iowa sets late fee caps in one subsection: Iowa Code § 562A.9(4). I read the Iowa Code 2026 text on the Iowa Legislature site on 2026-10-19. The section history lists 2013 Acts, chapter 97, as its last change. The cap changes at $700 of monthly rent.

| Rule | Limit or deadline | Statute |
|---|---|---|
| Rent $700 a month or less | Up to $12 a day, $60 a month total | § 562A.9(4) |
| Rent above $700 a month | Up to $20 a day, $100 a month total | § 562A.9(4) |
| Grace period | None set by statute | § 562A.9(3) |
| When rent is due | At the agreed time, without demand or notice | § 562A.9(3) |
| Where the fee comes from | The rental agreement | § 562A.9(1), (4) |
| Notice for unpaid rent | 3 days to pay after written notice | § 562A.27(2) |
| Deposit use | Rent or other funds due under the agreement | § 562A.12(3) |

## What is the maximum late fee a landlord can charge in Iowa?

The maximum Iowa late fee depends on the monthly rent. Section 562A.9(4) sets two tiers:

- **Rent of $700 or less:** a late fee may not exceed $12 per day or $60 per month in total.
- **Rent above $700:** a late fee may not exceed $20 per day or $100 per month in total.

Both limits apply at the same time. A daily fee must stay under the daily cap, and the month's total must stay under the monthly cap.

| Monthly rent | Daily cap | Monthly cap | Monthly cap as % of rent |
|---|---|---|---|
| $600 | $12 | $60 | 10.0% |
| $700 | $12 | $60 | 8.6% |
| $701 | $20 | $100 | 14.3% |
| $1,200 | $20 | $100 | 8.3% |
| $2,000 | $20 | $100 | 5.0% |

We did the math from the statute. Note the jump at $701. A unit at $700 caps at $60. A unit at $701 caps at $100.

## Does Iowa law require a grace period before a late fee?

No. Iowa Code § 562A.9 does not set a grace period. Section 562A.9(3) says "rent shall be payable without demand or notice at the time and place agreed upon by the parties." Unless the parties agree otherwise, rent is payable at the unit, at the start of each month.

So the lease controls when a fee starts. A short grace period, such as 3 to 5 days, is a business choice, not a legal duty. It can cut disputes about mail and bank holds.

## Must the late fee be written in the lease in Iowa?

Yes, in practice. Section 562A.9(4) limits what "a rental agreement shall not provide for." The chapter lets the parties put rent and other terms in the rental agreement (§ 562A.9(1)). A late fee with no agreed term has no basis.

The rental agreement can be written or oral (§ 562A.6). Put it in writing so you can prove it. Write these items:

- **Due date** and the place or method of payment.
- **Start day** for the late fee.
- **Amount**, flat or daily, under the caps for that unit's rent.
- **Monthly total cap**, $60 or $100.

## Can an Iowa landlord charge a daily late fee?

Yes. The Iowa statute names a daily fee directly. The daily fee may not exceed $12 or $20, and the month's total may not exceed $60 or $100.

Worked example for $1,100 rent (cap: $20 a day, $100 a month):

| Fee design | Total after 10 late days | Legal? |
|---|---|---|
| $20 per day, no cap | $200 | No, above $100 |
| $10 per day, capped at $100 | $100 | Yes |
| Flat $75 on day 4 | $75 | Yes |
| $25 per day | $250 | No, above $20 a day |

The easiest safe design is a flat fee under the monthly cap, or a daily fee with the cap written into the lease.

## Can an Iowa landlord evict a tenant for unpaid late fees?

Iowa's notice for nonpayment is about rent. Section 562A.27(2) lets the landlord end the agreement "if rent is unpaid when due" and the tenant does not pay within 3 days after written notice.

Section 562A.6 defines rent as "a payment to be made to the landlord under the rental agreement." That wording is broad. But no Iowa statute we read says in plain words that a late fee is rent. A judge may see the case differently.

What the Code does make clear:

- **Deposit use.** At move-out, the landlord may withhold from the deposit to cover a default in rent "or of other funds due to the landlord pursuant to the rental agreement" (§ 562A.12(3)(a)(1)). Late fees in the lease fit that wording.
- **Damages.** The landlord may recover damages for noncompliance with the rental agreement (§ 562A.27(3)).

A case built only on late fees is weak. Collect the fee, credit payments in a clear order, and ask an Iowa attorney before you file on fees alone.

## Iowa late fee checklist

- Check the unit's rent: $700 or less, or above $700.
- Write the fee in the rental agreement, with the start day.
- Keep any daily fee at or under $12 or $20.
- Cap the month's total at $60 or $100.
- Keep a ledger that shows rent and fees apart.
- Use the 3-day notice for unpaid rent, not for fees alone.
