## Why does a property manager need these terms?

A property manager needs these terms to read an agency proposal or a report. Agency blogs use words like "share of voice" and "entity" without a definition. This glossary gives each term in plain words, with a property management example.

We use these words the same way in every Citata.ai report. One word has one meaning.

## The glossary at a glance

| Term | Short meaning | Property management example |
|---|---|---|
| AI answer | The text an AI tool writes for a prompt | ChatGPT lists 5 managers in Tampa |
| Prompt | The question a person types into an AI tool | "Best property manager in Tampa for one house" |
| Engine | The AI product that writes the answer | ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude, Perplexity, Copilot |
| Mention | The answer names your company | "Acme Property Management charges 8%" |
| Citation | The answer links or credits your page as a source | A link to your fees page under the answer |
| Source | Any page an answer links or credits | Expertise.com, Yelp, your city page |
| Share of voice | Your part of all mentions for a prompt list | 8 of 100 mentions = 8% |
| Entity | Your company as one known thing with facts | Name, office, cities, phone, services |
| Zero-click search | A search that ends with no click | The owner reads the AI summary and stops |
| AI crawler | A bot that reads pages for an AI company | OAI-SearchBot, Claude-SearchBot |
| Index | The store of pages an engine can show | Bing's or Google's index |
| AI Overview | Google's AI summary above search results | A summary for "property management fees" |
| AI Mode | Google's chat-style search tab | A follow-up question about leasing fees |
| Query fan-out | The engine runs many related searches for one prompt | Fees, reviews and Section 8 searches at once |
| GEO | Work to get named and cited in AI answers | Fixing directory profiles, writing fee pages |
| llms.txt / IndexNow | A site map file for AI agents / a change ping to engines | /llms.txt; a ping to Bing after a fee change |

## What is a citation vs a mention?

A mention is when an AI answer names your company. A citation is when the answer links or credits one of your pages as a source. The two often come apart.

Here is a common case. A landlord asks ChatGPT for a manager in Phoenix. ChatGPT names your company but links Expertise.com as the source. You got a mention, and Expertise.com got the citation.

In our test of 150 AI answers for the 50 largest US cities, Expertise.com was a source in 93 (our data, 2026-10-19). So many managers get mentioned through a directory, not through their own site. Both count. But only a citation of your page sends a click to you.

Watch for: some reports call any mention a "citation". Ask the agency which one it counts.

## What is share of voice?

Share of voice is your part of all company mentions across a fixed list of prompts. You need a fixed list, or the number means nothing from month to month.

Worked example:

1. **Pick 20 prompts** a landlord in your city would ask.
2. **Run each prompt** in ChatGPT, Gemini and Claude. That gives 60 answers.
3. **Count every company name** in all 60 answers. Say the total is 300.
4. **Count your own name.** Say it appears 15 times.
5. **Divide:** 15 / 300 = 5% share of voice.

A second measure is simpler: the share of answers that name you at all. In the example, you might appear in 12 of 60 answers, or 20%. Report both, and keep the prompt list the same.

## What is an entity?

An entity is a person, place or thing that a system knows as one item with facts. Google calls its Knowledge Graph a database of "billions of facts about people, places, and things". Google builds knowledge panels from it when enough facts exist on the open web.

For a property manager, the entity is your company: legal name, brand name, office, phone, service cities, services and owners. AI tools join facts from your site, your Google Business Profile, Yelp, the BBB and directories. When those facts disagree, the tool may merge you with another company or skip you.

This happened to us. In our 2026-10-19 test, Claude answered about Citate.ai or Citant.ai in 4 of 4 answers about Citata.ai (our data). The fix is the same name and facts everywhere.

## What is a zero-click search?

A zero-click search ends without a click to any website. The person reads the answer on the results page or in the chat and stops.

Pew Research tracked 900 US adults in March 2025. When a Google AI summary appeared, users clicked a regular result on 8% of visits. Without a summary, they clicked on 15%. They clicked a link inside the summary on only 1% of visits (Pew, 2025-07-22).

For a property manager, zero-click means a landlord may pick you, or reject you, from the answer text alone. Your name and your facts in that text matter more than your traffic report shows.

## What is an AI crawler?

An AI crawler is a bot that an AI company runs to read web pages. Each company runs more than one, with different jobs.

| Company | Search bot (keep allowed) | Training bot (your choice) | User-request bot |
|---|---|---|---|
| OpenAI | OAI-SearchBot | GPTBot | ChatGPT-User |
| Anthropic | Claude-SearchBot | ClaudeBot | Claude-User |
| Perplexity | PerplexityBot | none listed | Perplexity-User |

Source: each company's crawler page, read 2026-10-19. OpenAI says that if you block OAI-SearchBot, your site stays out of ChatGPT search answers. Anthropic says that blocking Claude-SearchBot may cut your visibility in its search results. If you block a training bot, you still keep your search visibility.

## What do index, AI Overview, AI Mode and fan-out mean?

These 4 terms describe how Google and Bing find and show pages.

- **Index:** the store of pages an engine can show. If a page is not in the index, no AI answer from that engine can cite it.
- **AI Overview:** Google's AI summary at the top of results. Google says it helps people "get to the gist" of a question fast.
- **AI Mode:** Google's chat-style search for questions that need more reasoning or comparison.
- **Query fan-out:** Google says both features may run "multiple related searches across subtopics". One owner question can pull in pages about fees, reviews and local law.

Google says AI features need no extra technical work: the page must be indexed and able to show a snippet.

## What do GEO, llms.txt and IndexNow mean?

GEO (generative engine optimization) is the work to get your company named and cited in AI answers. It covers directory profiles, reviews, owner pages and tracking.

llms.txt is a Markdown file that lists a site's key pages for AI agents. It is a proposal from 2024 (llmstxt.org), not a standard.

IndexNow is a free protocol that tells Bing and other engines when a page changes. Google does not take part (indexnow.org).
