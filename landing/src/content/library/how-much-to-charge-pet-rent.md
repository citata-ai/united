## What is a normal pet rent?

A normal pet rent in the US is about $35 a month. RentCafe analyzed listings for pet-friendly apartments and put the 2025 average at $35.65 a month. The page was last updated in October 2025.

| Charge (RentCafe, 2025) | Small cities (under 100,000) | Large cities (over 300,000) | National |
|---|---|---|---|
| Monthly pet rent | $34.88 | $37.14 | $35.65 |
| Refundable pet deposit | $289.52 | $311.20 | "slightly over $300" |
| One-time pet fee | $312.54 | not given | $315 |

RentCafe also reports that around 80% of rentals in its data allow at least one common pet, a dog or a cat. Large cities were higher, at 84.05%. So a no-pets rule cuts you off from many renters who own pets.

Watch for: these are averages from listings. They are not a rule, and local markets differ. Look at 5 to 10 pet-friendly listings near your unit before you pick a number.

## Do state laws limit pet rent?

Some states cap pet charges. Most do not have a pet-specific cap, but a general deposit cap can still apply. Here are 2 examples.

| State | Rule | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Colorado | Pet rent: no more than the greater of $35/month or 1.5% of monthly rent. Pet deposit: no more than $300, and it must be refundable. In effect since January 1, 2024. | HB23-1068 |
| New York | No deposit or advance may exceed 1 month's rent, with narrow exceptions. The law does not name pets, so a pet deposit counts toward the same limit. | Gen. Oblig. Law 7-108 |

Under the Colorado rule, a $2,000 unit can carry pet rent of up to $35, because 1.5% of $2,000 is $30. A $3,000 unit can carry up to $45, because 1.5% of $3,000 is $45.

Some states treat any refundable pet charge as part of the security deposit. Then the total of both deposits must stay under the state cap.

This is general information, not legal advice. Check the current law or ask a local attorney.

## Should pet rent vary by size?

Many owners use tiers by pet type, size or count. A big dog and a cat cause different wear. Tiers are a business choice, not a legal must. If you use them, put them in writing and apply them to every tenant.

Here is a sample tier table. The prices are an example, near the RentCafe averages.

| Pet | Example monthly pet rent |
|---|---|
| Cat | $25 |
| Dog under 30 lb | $35 |
| Dog 30 lb or more | $45 |
| Each extra pet | +$15 |

Check any state cap against your top tier. In Colorado, a $45 tier is only legal when 1.5% of the rent is $45 or more.

Assistance animals are not pets. HUD's fact sheet on Notice FHEO-2020-01 says housing providers may not exclude or charge a fee or deposit for assistance animals. That covers trained service animals and support animals for a person with a disability. Your tier table does not apply to them.

## Does pet rent cover pet damage?

Pet rent does not cover damage. Pet rent pays for normal wear: more cleaning, more carpet wear, more yard care. Damage is a separate cost that you take from the security or pet deposit, under your state's deposit rules.

| Charge | Refundable? | What it pays for |
|---|---|---|
| Pet rent | No | Ongoing wear from the pet |
| Pet deposit | Yes, minus damage | Pet damage found at move-out |
| One-time pet fee | No | Extra cleaning or admin at move-in or move-out. Some states limit or ban non-refundable fees. |

What to do: write in the lease that pet rent is not a cap on the tenant's liability for damage. The tenant still pays for damage beyond normal wear. For an assistance animal, the tenant pays for actual damage too, as any tenant would, but you charge no pet rent or deposit.

## Worked math: pet rent over a 12-month lease

Pet rent adds up over a full lease. This example uses the RentCafe national averages. Your numbers will differ.

| Line | Amount |
|---|---|
| Monthly pet rent | $35.65 |
| Months in lease | 12 |
| Pet rent for the year | $35.65 × 12 = $427.80 |
| One-time pet fee (if your state allows it) | $315 |
| Total non-refundable pet income | $742.80 |
| Refundable pet deposit (held, not income) | about $300 |

Now compare it to a common pet cost at turnover. Say you pay $250 for an extra deep clean and odor treatment. That cost is an example, not market data. The $427.80 in pet rent covers it with $177.80 left over.

There is a second benefit. RentCafe's data shows around 80% of rentals accept pets. When you allow pets, more renters can apply, which can cut vacant days. One vacant week on a $1,800 unit costs about $415 ($1,800 × 12 ÷ 52). That is close to a full year of average pet rent.
