## What does EliseAI add on top of a PMS?

EliseAI presents leasing, resident-service and customer record tools. Its value as an extra layer depends on a gap in your current property management system, or PMS.

Start with repeat work. That might involve availability questions, tour booking or resident handoffs. Record how staff complete the task today.

Ask EliseAI to show the whole task through your proposed connection. An answer is not the same as a completed booking or correct work order. Inspect the final record and notification.

Run the same case in your existing platform. Keep source records and requests consistent. If both finish safely, compare cost and supervision. If neither does, fix the data or process first. Do not buy an extra layer because its demo sounds more natural. Require a useful result that remains visible to your staff.

## What does AppFolio Realm-X cover?

AppFolio lists Realm-X Assistant and Messages in Core, with Flows in Plus. Use those plan names to start checking scope.

Names alone do not establish what the tool may change without approval. Ask the vendor to separate reading, drafting and acting. Test permissions in the proposed plan.

Bring a resident question that needs lease details. Then use a case with missing information. Inspect whether the tool asks for help or supplies an unsupported answer. Check the conversation record.

Ask how staff pause automation, correct a response and inspect actions. A tool that drafts well can still fit while staff control sending. Define the boundary before measuring time saved. Keep payment decisions and policy exceptions within your chosen approval rules. The presenter should show the control rather than merely describe it.

## What does a second vendor cost?

An extra vendor costs more than its subscription. EliseAI's reviewed pages offer a demo route instead of a public rate, so pricing is quote only.

Request setup, connection, training and usage terms in writing. Ask whether the PMS needs an upgraded plan. Include overlapping tools that you cannot yet cancel.

Budget remaining staff work. Someone maintains property facts, checks errors and handles escalations. Use your own time records, not a general vendor savings claim.

Separate completed tasks from total conversations. An assistant can answer many easy questions without removing much work. Track corrections and difficult handoffs too. Compare cost with work the team actually stops doing. Keep savings labelled as a hypothesis until the pilot provides evidence. A lower bill has little value if staff must repeat every answer afterward.

## How do the tools share data?

EliseAI publishes an integration directory, but a listing does not prove your full connection. Confirm the exact system and record types in the proposed scope.

Ask where prices, availability, fees and lease details come from. Name the final source for each fact. Confirm what happens when a record changes or an update fails.

Use this data test:

- Change a sample unit's availability.
- Update a property policy.
- Ask about the changed fact.
- Inspect the source and response.
- Trigger a failed update.
- Check the alert and recovery.
- Remove access and confirm work stops.

Test reads and writes separately. A tool may read a lease without permission to change the expected workflow. Restrict credentials to required records. Ask how resident information is stored and removed after service ends. Document any delay so staff know when the answer may be stale.

## Which portfolio size justifies a standalone tool?

No verified door-count threshold appears in the reviewed sources. Workload and process fit need a direct test.

A team with repeated contacts may have a useful pilot case. A quiet portfolio with poor data may gain little immediately. These are planning judgments, not claims about specific customers.

Measure the baseline first. Record task type, staff time, delays and errors. Keep definitions stable during the pilot. Exclude cases where staff still repeat the full task.

Review resident experience too. Confirm people can reach a human and keep useful context in the handoff. Fewer staff contacts do not prove success if residents stop getting answers. Keep unresolved cases visible. Ask staff to review failures before expanding the service. A clear recovery path matters as much as a correct ordinary response.

## How should you run a buying test?

Run a bounded pilot with a named owner and written pass rules. Choose a task with reliable facts and a safe review route.

Require correct records, useful handoffs and visible errors. Inspect completed work and every failure. Ask staff to explain how they stop or reverse an action.

Compare built-in and separate tools on the same work. Keep full quotes and manual steps beside the result. Do not count a future feature as a pass.

AI in a PMS helps operations. For the separate task of how owners discover your company, read [how owners choose a manager with AI](/guides/how-owners-choose-a-property-manager-with-ai). Take a real unresolved workflow to both vendors and require the final record.
