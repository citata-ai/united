## What is a special or complaint inspection?

A special inspection, or complaint inspection, is a PHA visit triggered by a report of a problem. 24 CFR 982.405(d) calls it an interim inspection. It starts "when a participant family or government official notifies the PHA of a potential deficiency". DeKalb Housing Authority says owners may also request a special inspection.

The PHA must act fast, and the clock depends on the problem:

| Reported problem | PHA must inspect and notify you within | You must repair within |
|---|---|---|
| Life-threatening | 24 hours of the report | 24 hours of PHA notice |
| Not life-threatening | 15 days of the report | 30 days of PHA notice, or an approved extension |

Source: 24 CFR 982.405(d)(1)–(2) and 982.404(a)(3). HUD may approve an exception to the 24-hour or 15-day PHA deadlines in extraordinary cases, such as a presidentially declared disaster area.

A complaint inspection is not the only path. The PHA must also consider complaints when it schedules its regular inspections (982.405(e)).

## How fast must I fix it?

You must fix a confirmed life-threatening item within 24 hours of the PHA's notice. For other items, you have 30 calendar days, or a reasonable PHA-approved extension (24 CFR 982.404(a)(3)).

Which items count as life-threatening comes from your PHA's inspection standard. Ask your PHA for it, because voucher programs are moving to the NSPIRE standard.

What to do the day the PHA calls:

1. **Get the item list in writing.** Note the date and time of the notice.
2. **Sort by clock.** Life-threatening first, same day.
3. **Book the vendor.** Get a written appointment time.
4. **Ask for an extension before day 30** if parts or weather delay the repair. Put the reason in writing.
5. **Send proof.** A PHA may accept photos or other reliable evidence that you fixed an item (982.405(i)).
6. **Book the reinspection** if the PHA needs one, and make sure someone gives access.

## Can the PHA abate for a complaint item?

Yes, the PHA can abate for a complaint item if you miss the deadline. 24 CFR 982.404(d) sets the steps for any inspection after the first one, including complaint inspections:

- **Withhold.** After it tells you of the deficiency, the PHA may hold the HAP. If you fix it within the cure period, the PHA pays the held amount.
- **Abate.** If you miss the cure period, the PHA must abate (stop) the HAP, including held amounts.
- **Terminate.** If the unit still fails 60 days after the abatement notice, or a longer PHA period, the PHA must end the HAP contract.

The PHA does not pay you for abated time once you fix the item (982.404(d)(4)). And you may not evict the family because of the abatement (982.404(d)(3)).

Worked example: HAP is $1,100 per month. A non-life-threatening item is confirmed on March 1. You fix it on April 15, 45 days later.

| Date | Status | HAP effect |
|---|---|---|
| March 1 | PHA notice | Clock starts; PHA may withhold HAP |
| March 31 | Day 30, cure period ends | Abatement starts, including any withheld HAP |
| April 15 | Repair verified | HAP resumes |
| Lost HAP, PHA did not withhold | About 15 days | About $550, not repaid |
| Lost HAP, PHA withheld from March 1 | About 45 days | About $1,650, not repaid |

### What if the tenant caused it?

If the tenant caused the damage, the rules shift. Under 24 CFR 982.404(a)(4), if the PHA finds the tenant, a household member or a guest caused damage beyond ordinary use, it may waive your duty to repair. Then it "may not" withhold or abate your HAP. The family must fix it within the same 24-hour or 30-day clock (982.404(b)). Send the PHA your proof: move-in photos, the work order and any vendor note on the cause.

## How do I log repair requests?

Log every repair request the same way, whether it comes from the tenant or the PHA. A clean log is your evidence if the PHA abates or the tenant disputes a repair.

Repair log columns:

| Field | Example |
|---|---|
| Request ID | R-2026-0412 |
| Unit and tenant | 18 Elm St, #3 |
| Received from | Tenant call / PHA notice |
| Date and time received | 2026-10-19 09:40 |
| Description | No hot water |
| Life-threatening per PHA? | No |
| Deadline | 2026-11-18 (30 days from PHA notice) |
| Vendor and appointment | ABC Plumbing, 2026-10-20 10:00 |
| Completed | 2026-10-20 |
| Proof | 2 photos, invoice #5512 |
| Sent to PHA | 2026-10-20, portal upload |

Watch for: a tenant often calls the PHA after a request to you went unanswered. A fast first response, even "we booked a plumber for tomorrow", can stop a complaint before it starts.

## Can I raise rent after a complaint?

You can change rent only under the voucher rules, and never as payback. During the initial lease term, you may not raise the rent to owner (24 CFR 982.309(a)(3)). After that, you must tell the PHA at least 60 days before any rent change, and the new rent must pass rent reasonableness (24 CFR 982.308(g)(4)).

State law adds limits. Florida is one example. Florida Statutes 83.64 makes it unlawful to "discriminatorily increase a tenant's rent or decrease services" primarily to retaliate. Its examples include a tenant who "complained to a governmental agency" about a building, housing or health code issue. A tenant can raise retaliation as a defense in an eviction.

So separate the 2 events. Fix the complaint item, document it, and wait. If you plan a rent change, base it on market comps, apply it the same way to like units, and send the 60-day PHA notice.
