In our 2026-10-18 test, we ran 20 prompts about AI search for property managers through ChatGPT, Gemini and Claude, 2 runs each. YouTube.com was a cited source in 7 answers. AI tools read video pages and transcripts. That is a reason to make videos. But it is not proof that videos bring owners, and the lead data says to keep expectations low.

## Can a YouTube channel bring owner leads?

A YouTube channel brings few owner leads directly. In the Goodjuju Marketing fall 2026 study of 35 property management companies, social media gave 0.8% of owner leads from 4.3% of traffic. YouTube sits inside that social number.

| Channel (Goodjuju, fall 2026) | Share of owner leads |
|---|---|
| Google Search and Map Pack | 50.6% |
| Direct / Maps | 35.6% |
| Referral | 3.7% |
| AI tools | 3.4% |
| Social media (incl. YouTube) | 0.8% |

So do not start YouTube to replace Google work. Start it to make your site and your sales calls stronger. An owner who watched you explain your fees for 4 minutes arrives at the call half sold. Videos also give AI tools and Google a clear, quotable answer with your name on it.

## Which video topics do owners watch?

Owners watch videos that answer a question with money or risk attached. Pick one question per video and say the answer in the first 20 seconds.

| Topic | Example title | Format |
|---|---|---|
| Fees | "What a property manager in [city] really costs" | 4–6 min explainer with a fee table on screen |
| Rent price | "How we price a 3-bed rental in [city]" | 3–5 min, show comps |
| Vacancy | "Why your rental sits empty: 5 causes" | 5–7 min |
| Local rules | "[State] deposit rules in 3 minutes" | Short, cite the statute on screen |
| Rent or sell | "Should you rent or sell your [city] home?" | 6–8 min with numbers |
| Behind the scenes | "What a move-in inspection looks like" | 2–3 min, no tenant faces |
| FAQ | "Can I fire my property manager?" | 1–3 min Short |

Your channel list should come from real calls. Write down every question owners ask for 2 weeks. Each repeated question is a video.

Rules for content: show homes only with the owner's consent. Never show a tenant, a unit number or a tenant's belongings without consent. Do not describe the "ideal tenant" by family status, age or any protected class.

## How long should videos be?

Most owner videos work at 3 to 8 minutes, with quick answers as Shorts. YouTube's own limits shape the choice:

- **Shorts:** YouTube says square or vertical videos up to 3 minutes, uploaded on or after 2024-10-15, count as Shorts.
- **Standard uploads:** up to 15 minutes by default. Verified accounts can upload longer videos.
- **Chapters:** add timestamps in the description. The first must be 00:00, you need at least 3 in ascending order, and each chapter must be at least 10 seconds.

Chapters help an owner jump to the part they need. Google can also show them as key moments in search.

### Worked example: one recording, four videos

You record one 25-minute session on "owner fees." From it you cut:

1. A 7-minute explainer with 5 chapters (management, leasing, renewal, maintenance markup, cancellation).
2. Three Shorts of 45 to 90 seconds, one per fee.

That is 4 videos for about 2 hours of work, recording plus editing.

## What gear do I need?

You need a phone, a microphone, light and a tripod. Sound quality matters more than picture quality: owners forgive a plain picture but not echo.

Starter checklist:

- [ ] A recent phone, recording in 1080p or 4K
- [ ] A clip-on microphone (wired or wireless)
- [ ] A window in front of you, or one soft light
- [ ] A tripod or phone stand at eye level
- [ ] A quiet room with soft surfaces to cut echo
- [ ] A simple editing app for cuts and captions
- [ ] Captions on every video; many people watch with sound off

Record the same day each week. A steady pace of 2 videos a month beats 10 videos in one month and then silence.

## How do I link videos to my website?

Embed each video on a page on your own site, and give each video its own page. Google's video SEO guide says to "create a dedicated watch page for each video" to be eligible for video results, key moments and other features.

Google's guide also lists these needs:

- The watch page must be indexed and performing well in Search.
- The video must be embedded on the watch page.
- The video must have a valid thumbnail at a stable URL.

Step list for each video:

1. **Upload to YouTube** with a clear title, chapters and a link to your matching page.
2. **Make a watch page** on your site: the embedded video, a 150–300 word summary and the transcript.
3. **Add the facts in text.** Put your fee table or the statute in plain text under the video. AI tools and Google read text more reliably than speech.
4. **Link from related pages.** Put the fee video on your fee page.
5. **Tag links.** Add UTM tags (tracking labels) to links in the YouTube description, so Google Analytics counts visits from YouTube.

After 6 months, check three numbers: YouTube visits to your site, owner leads who watched a video (ask on the call), and any AI answers that cite your videos. If all three are near zero, cut back to Shorts made from calls you already record.
