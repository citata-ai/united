## Do YouTube videos help a property management company show up in AI answers?

YouTube videos help a little, mostly for how-to questions. They do not replace your site or your directory listings. Our own tests show the gap.

We ran two sets of prompts in October 2026 in ChatGPT, Claude and Gemini (our data, 2026-10).

| Prompt set | Answers | Answers that cited youtube.com |
|---|---|---|
| Prompts on AI search and marketing for managers, 2026-10-18 | 120 | 7 |
| Prompts on AI search and marketing for managers, 2026-10-19 | 120 | 7 |
| "Best property management company in [city]", 50 cities, 2026-10-19 | 150 | 3 (all Gemini) |

For the local question, the same 150 answers cited Expertise.com 93 times, Yelp 50 times and iPropertyManagement 50 times. So for "who should manage my rental", YouTube is a small source. For "how do I do X", it shows up more.

## Does Google AI cite YouTube?

Google AI can show videos, but Google gives no special path for them. Google says AI Overviews and AI Mode have "no additional requirements" beyond normal Search (Google Search Central). A page must be indexed and able to show a snippet.

Google's best practices for AI features list 3 points:

1. **Text first.** Put important content in text form.
2. **Support with media.** Use high-quality images and videos, when they apply.
3. **Match your data.** Structured data must match the visible text.

The page does not name YouTube. So treat video as support for a text answer, not as the answer.

## Which video topics do owners search?

Owners search for money, risk and process. Pick topics that match the questions owners ask AI. Goodjuju Marketing's own video series covers SEO timelines and Google versus AI search, which are topics for managers, not owners (Goodjuju). Make owner videos instead.

Good owner topics:

- **What your fee covers.** Walk through your fee table, line by line.
- **How you price a rental.** Show the steps of your rental analysis.
- **What happens when a tenant stops paying.** Explain your steps and the local timeline, with the law cited on the page.
- **How the first month works.** Onboarding, keys, inspection, first owner statement.
- **A real owner story,** with consent and a disclosure.

Watch for: do not promise rent levels or results in a video. A video stays online for years. Your numbers change.

## Do transcripts matter?

Transcripts matter because they put the spoken answer in text, and Google asks for important content in text form. YouTube makes automatic captions with speech recognition. Google warns that automatic captions "might misrepresent the spoken content" because of accents, noise or poor pronunciation (YouTube Help).

What to do:

1. **Open YouTube Studio,** then Subtitles, then the video.
2. **Read the automatic captions** and fix names, numbers and city names.
3. **Copy the clean text** to a page on your site, under the video.
4. **Add chapters.** Google says you can set key moments with timestamps and labels in the YouTube description, and Google gives those priority (Google Search Central).

A wrong caption can say "$15 fee" when you said "15%". Fix every number.

## How short can a video be?

A video can be as short as the answer needs. YouTube Shorts can now run up to 3 minutes (YouTube Help). That is enough for one clear answer.

| Format | Length | Use it for |
|---|---|---|
| Short | Up to 3 minutes | One fact: "Do you charge a leasing fee?" |
| Standard video | About 3 to 10 minutes | A process: "How we take over a rental" |
| Long video | 10 minutes or more | A walkthrough or an owner webinar |

These lengths are our rule of thumb, not a Google rule. One question per video makes the title, the chapter and the transcript match.

## How do you put a video on your site so search engines index it?

Put each video on its own page, with text around it. Google says a video must be embedded on a watch page to be indexed. It also says an indexed page does not mean an indexed video (Google Search Central).

Checklist for each video page:

- [ ] One video is the main content of the page.
- [ ] The page title and description are unique to that video.
- [ ] The clean transcript sits under the video.
- [ ] `VideoObject` structured data has a unique `name`, `description` and `thumbnailUrl`.
- [ ] The text answer is in the first paragraph, not only in the video.
- [ ] The page links to your related owner page, such as your fee page.

Google also warns that markup does not guarantee a video feature in Search.

## What should a 50 to 500 door manager do first?

Do the cheap, high-return work first. Video comes after it. In our local test, listings and manager sites carried most citations.

1. **Fix your listings** on Expertise.com, Yelp and Google.
2. **Write the owner pages** that answer fees, process and cities.
3. **Then record** one short video per owner page, and embed it there.
4. **Track** which prompts name you each month.
