## What does GEO mean?

GEO means generative engine optimization: the work that makes AI answers name or cite your business. A generative engine is an AI tool that writes an answer from many sources, such as ChatGPT or Google AI Overviews.

The term comes from research. Pranjal Aggarwal and five co-authors published "GEO: Generative Engine Optimization" on arXiv in November 2023. The paper was accepted at KDD 2024. It calls GEO a way to help content creators improve "visibility in generative engine responses". It reports that the methods can raise visibility by up to 40%.

Three words come up in every GEO talk. Here is what each means in this article.

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Prompt | The question a person types into an AI tool |
| Mention | The AI answer names your company |
| Citation | The AI answer links or lists your site as a source |

For a property manager, the GEO goal is simple. A landlord asks, "Who is the best property manager in Tampa for a single-family home?"

The AI answer names your company, and it cites a page that tells the truth about you.

## How is GEO different from SEO?

GEO differs from SEO in where you want to appear. SEO aims for a rank in a list of 10 links. GEO aims for a place inside the answer itself, which often names only 5 or so companies.

On 2026-10-19 we asked ChatGPT for the best property manager in 50 US cities. It named 5.3 companies per answer on average (our data, 2026-10-19). A company in place 8 on Google still gets seen. A 5-name answer has no place 8.

The two share most of the work. Google says there are no extra requirements and no special schema to appear in AI Overviews or AI Mode (Google Search Central, updated 2025-12-10). A page must be indexed and eligible for a snippet. So good SEO is the base of GEO. The full side-by-side is in a separate article in this library.

## Which AI assistants does GEO cover?

GEO covers every AI assistant that answers a question with names and sources. For property managers, the main ones are:

| Assistant | Who runs it | What we checked |
|---|---|---|
| ChatGPT search | OpenAI | Uses the OAI-SearchBot crawler; a block keeps your pages out of its search answers |
| Google AI Overviews and AI Mode | Google | Use the Google Search index and "query fan-out" searches; no special schema needed |
| Gemini | Google | Tested in our 50-city run |
| Claude | Anthropic | Tested in our 50-city run |
| Perplexity | Perplexity AI | Gave 1 AI owner lead in the Goodjuju fall 2026 data |
| Microsoft Copilot | Microsoft | Counted as an AI source in the Goodjuju study |

Sources: OpenAI crawler docs, Google Search Central, Goodjuju Marketing and our data, read 2026-10-19.

Which one matters most depends on your owners. One study tracked 35 companies from July to October 2026. ChatGPT gave 37 of 43 AI owner leads, Gemini 5 and Perplexity 1 (Goodjuju Marketing, fall 2026).

The engines also read different sources. In our 50-city run, ChatGPT cited a company website in 30% of answers. Gemini did so in 96% (our data, 2026-10-19). One plan has to serve both.

## What does GEO work include?

GEO work for a property manager falls into 6 jobs. Most are not new. The order matters.

1. **Open the doors.** Check that robots.txt lets Googlebot and OAI-SearchBot in. OpenAI says a site that blocks OAI-SearchBot does not show in ChatGPT search answers.
2. **Fix your facts everywhere.** Same name, address, phone and services on your site, Google Business Profile, Yelp, BBB and the directories.
3. **Get on the lists AI cites.** In our 50-city run, expertise.com was a source in 93 of 150 answers, and yelp.com and ipropertymanagement.com in 50 each (our data, 2026-10-19).
4. **Write quotable owner pages.** A fee table, contract terms and one page per real service city. The GEO paper found that adding statistics, quotes and source citations raised visibility. Keyword stuffing did not help.
5. **Earn honest reviews.** Ask real owners, and reply to each review.
6. **Track prompts each month.** Ask the same owner questions in ChatGPT, Gemini and Google. Log who gets named and which sources get cited.

Our [AI search checklist](/guides/ai-search-checklist-property-management-website) covers the website part of jobs 1, 2 and 4 step by step.

Watch for: no one controls AI answers. A GEO provider who promises a rank in ChatGPT promises something they cannot deliver. Ask for a baseline and a monthly report instead.

## Who needs GEO?

Any property manager that wants owner leads needs basic GEO, because owners now ask AI for names. How much you need depends on where you stand.

| Your situation | How much GEO |
|---|---|
| You get most owners from referrals | Basics only: fix listings, check AI twice a year |
| Google leads are falling | Basics plus a monthly prompt check |
| A competitor shows up in ChatGPT and you do not | Full GEO: listings, city pages, fee table, tracking |
| You serve several cities | Full GEO, one city at a time |

The share of leads from AI is still small but growing. In the same Goodjuju study, AI tools went from 2.1% to 3.4% of owner leads between spring and fall 2026. AI visitors converted at 3.9%, against 1.2% for Google.

Our own test shows the cost of doing nothing. AI named citata.ai in 0 of 108 non-brand answers on 2026-10-19 (our data, 2026-10-19). A business that AI does not know is not on the list.
