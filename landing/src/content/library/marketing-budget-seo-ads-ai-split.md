## What does each channel cost?

Each channel has a different price unit, so compare them by the cost of one signed owner. Here are the public numbers we could check on 2026-10-19.

| Channel | Public price | Source | What you get |
|---|---|---|---|
| SEO retainer | $1,500 to $5,000 a month; most growth-stage firms $2,500 to $3,500 | ClearLead Digital, 2026-06-08 | Pages, local SEO, links; leads build over months |
| Cheap SEO package | $300 to $750 a month | ClearLead Digital, 2026-06-08 | The post advises against it |
| Google Ads, property management | $3.94 per click, $76.71 per lead, 10.24% conversion rate | LocaliQ 2026 benchmarks, quoted by Web Tonic | Leads from the first week; they stop when spend stops |
| Google Business Profile | Your time | Google rules apply | A local listing, if you meet the rules |
| AI search work | Mostly inside SEO; extra cost is answer tracking | Our view | Citations in AI answers; no public price benchmark |

Notes on the numbers:

- **The ad numbers are benchmarks.** Web Tonic says LocaliQ used nearly 900 real estate search campaigns from April 2025 to March 2026. Your city and your keywords can cost much more or less.
- **A "lead" is not a signed owner.** In ad benchmarks, a conversion is a form or a call. Your close rate turns it into a cost per signed owner.
- **ClearLead sells SEO.** Its ranges come from a vendor. They are still useful, because few agencies publish prices.

## What share of leads does each channel bring?

We found no public, checkable study of lead share by channel for property managers, so you must measure your own. Agency case studies quote lead counts, but they come from the agency that sold the work.

Two facts shape the share today. First, AI summaries take clicks from normal results. Pew Research found that users clicked a normal result on 8% of Google visits with an AI summary, against 15% without one (2025-07-22). They clicked a link inside the summary on 1% of visits.

Second, AI answers lean on listings. On 2026-10-19 we asked ChatGPT, Claude and Gemini to name the best property manager in 50 US cities. 88% of the 150 answers cited a listing site, and Expertise.com was a source in 93 of them (our data, 2026-10). So listing work now feeds both local SEO and AI answers.

For the full picture of why owner leads from Google are falling, see [why landlord leads are dropping](/guides/why-owner-leads-are-dropping).

## How do you work out a split? A worked example

Work backward from what one signed owner is worth to you. Here is an example with made-up inputs. Replace each input with your own number.

Example inputs (not benchmarks):

- Average rent: $1,800 a month.
- Management fee: 8% of rent, so $144 a month per door.
- An owner signs 2 doors and stays 3 years.
- Value of one signed owner: $144 × 2 doors × 36 months = $10,368 in fees.

Now price the ad channel:

1. **Budget:** $1,000 a month on Google Ads.
2. **Leads:** $1,000 ÷ $76.71 per lead = about 13 leads (LocaliQ benchmark).
3. **Close rate:** say 1 in 10 leads signs (your number). That is about 1.3 signed owners.
4. **Cost per signed owner:** $1,000 ÷ 1.3 = about $770.

ClearLead gives a rule of thumb for SEO. SEO pays off "if the monthly SEO fee is less than the value of one new management agreement". With $10,368 per owner, a $2,500 SEO retainer needs about 1 new owner every 4 months to break even. Use the [property management fee calculator](/tools/property-management-fee-calculator) to get your own fee per door.

The split then follows a simple rule: put the next dollar where the cost per signed owner is lowest. Re-check it each quarter.

## What changes for under 100 doors?

Under 100 doors, the budget is small, so the cheap base comes first and ads stay small. At $144 a month per door (the example above), 15 doors bring $2,160 a month. That is less than a $2,500 SEO retainer. Our working rule for small managers (hypothesis, not a tested result):

| Order | Item | Typical cost | Helps |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Google Business Profile, set up to the rules | Your time | Local SEO, AI answers |
| 2 | Correct facts on Yelp, BBB and other listings | Your time | AI answers, local SEO |
| 3 | Honest review requests to every client | Your time | All channels |
| 4 | One fee page and one page per real service city | Your time or a writer | SEO, AI answers |
| 5 | Small Google Ads campaign on owner searches | Your choice of daily cap | Leads now |
| 6 | SEO or GEO retainer | From the price table above | Scale, once 1 to 5 are done |

Watch for: Google Business Profile has rules. A virtual office is not eligible. A home-based manager lists a service area and hides the address.

## How often should you change the split?

Track every month, and change the split each quarter. SEO and AI search need months to show results. Ads show results in days. If you judge both after one month, ads will win every time.

Monthly checklist:

- **Leads by source,** from your CRM and from a "How did you hear about us?" field.
- **Signed owners by source,** not only leads.
- **Ad spend and cost per signed owner.**
- **AI answers:** run the same 10 owner prompts in ChatGPT, Gemini and Claude. Note if you are named and which sources appear.
- **Listings:** any new directory that AI cites and you are not in.

Each quarter, move 10% to 20% of the budget toward the channel with the lowest cost per signed owner. Keep the base (listings, reviews, fee page) funded in every quarter. That share is our own rule of thumb, not a benchmark.
