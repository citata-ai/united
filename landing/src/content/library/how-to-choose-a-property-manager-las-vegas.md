## Which license must a Las Vegas property manager hold, and how do I look it up?

A Las Vegas property manager needs two credentials: a Nevada real estate license and a property management permit. NRS 645.6052 says only a licensed real estate broker, broker-salesperson or salesperson may apply for the permit. The applicant must show at least 24 classroom hours of property management study.

The permit expires and renews with the license. Each renewal needs at least 3 hours of property management continuing education.

The office also needs a qualified lead. Under NRS 645.6055, a broker without a permit who runs property management at an office must appoint a designated property manager. That person must hold a broker or broker-salesperson license, hold a permit, and have 2 years of active property management experience in the last 4 years.

How to check, step by step:

1. **Ask for the license number** of the person who will manage your home, and the broker's name.
2. **Open NRED's License Lookup** on red.nv.gov. It lets you search by name or license number.
3. **Confirm the status** is active.
4. **Ask to see the property management permit**, not only the real estate license.
5. **Ask who the broker or designated property manager is** for the office.

Watch for: a person who says "I'm a licensed agent" may not hold the permit. Ask for both in writing.

## Which local rules must a Las Vegas manager know?

A Las Vegas manager must know Nevada's landlord-tenant limits, because state law sets most of them. Three rules affect your money every month.

| Rule | Nevada law | Example at $1,686 rent |
|---|---|---|
| Deposit cap | Security, including last month's rent, may not exceed 3 months' rent (NRS 118A.242) | Up to $5,058 |
| Deposit return | Return the remainder within 30 days, or owe the full deposit plus up to the same amount (NRS 118A.242) | Risk up to $10,116 on a full deposit |
| Late fee cap | No more than 5% of the periodic rent, and no increase based on a prior late fee (NRS 118A.210) | Up to $84.30 |

The example rent is $1,686, the HUD FY2027 fair market rent for a 2-bedroom in the Las Vegas-Henderson-North Las Vegas area. See the [Las Vegas fair market rent 2027 page](/guides/section-8-rent-limits/las-vegas-fair-market-rent-2027).

Many Las Vegas Valley homes sit in HOA communities. Ask how the manager handles HOA rules, violation letters and tenant registration with the HOA. A missed HOA notice can turn into a fine on your account.

This is general information, not legal advice. Check the current law or ask a local attorney.

## What should a Las Vegas management agreement say?

A Las Vegas management agreement must be in writing and signed before the broker acts as your manager. NRS 645.6056 lists what it must include, at a minimum.

| Required by NRS 645.6056 | What to look for |
|---|---|
| The term, and renewal terms if it renews | A fixed term, and how auto-renewal works |
| How tenant deposits are held and handled | The account and who returns deposits |
| The fee or compensation | Every fee in dollars or percent |
| The extent of the broker's authority as your agent | Repair limit and lease signing power |
| Cancellation terms, if it can be cancelled | Notice period, with or without cause, and any fee |
| Asset management services, if offered | What extra services you pay for |

Add these to your own checklist:

- [ ] Fee basis: rent collected or rent due.
- [ ] Who keeps the tenant's late fee.
- [ ] Maintenance markup, if any.
- [ ] Monthly statements and a year-end 1099.
- [ ] HOA tasks the manager covers.

## Which questions should I ask a Las Vegas manager before I sign?

Ask questions that test Nevada knowledge, not only the price. Good answers are exact.

1. **What is your license number, and do you hold a property management permit?**
2. **Who is the broker or designated property manager for your office?**
3. **Where do tenant deposits sit, and how fast do you return them?** Expect "within 30 days."
4. **What late fee do you charge tenants, and who keeps it?** It may not exceed 5% of rent.
5. **What does your agreement say about cancellation?** Ask for the clause by number.
6. **How do you handle HOA rules and violation notices?**
7. **How many homes does each manager handle?**
8. **What does the monthly fee not cover?** Get the full fee schedule.
9. **How do you screen tenants?** Expect one written standard for all applicants, in line with Fair Housing rules.
10. **Can I call 2 owners you manage for now?**

## Where do Las Vegas owners find manager reviews?

Las Vegas owners can check a manager in three places. Each has a gap, so use all three.

| Source | What it tells you | Watch for |
|---|---|---|
| NRED License Lookup | License status | Not service quality |
| Google Business Profile and Yelp | Owner and tenant reviews | Tenant complaints often dominate. Read the manager's replies. |
| Owner references | Results on homes like yours | The manager picks them. Ask for an HOA home if you own one. |

What to do: shortlist 3 managers. Check each license and permit, ask the 10 questions, and compare the agreements against the NRS 645.6056 table above.
