## Which payment options should the portal offer?

A tenant portal should offer payment routes that fit your renters and show fees before submission. Test the full experience rather than only the pay button.

Buildium's Resident Center page describes payments, scheduled payments, and history. AppFolio describes resident payments through its portal. Confirm current options and charges for the proposed account.

| Check | Renter task | Team verification |
|---|---|---|
| Payment methods | See options and fees | Confirm supported methods and who pays |
| Status | Distinguish pending, completed, and failed | Find the underlying transaction |
| Schedule | Review and change autopay | Check what happens after a balance change |
| Receipt | Find proof of submission | Match the payment to the lease |
| Help | Find an alternate contact route | Resolve the exception |

Ask how the portal handles roommates and partial payments. Check the actual setting rather than assuming the default matches your policy.

Try a failed payment in a safe test environment. The renter should receive a clear next step. Staff should see a task they can resolve.

## How should maintenance requests work?

A maintenance portal should capture enough detail to route work and keep the renter informed. A submitted form should become a visible record with an owner.

Buildium describes photo uploads and repair-status updates. AppFolio describes maintenance submission through its portal. Ask each vendor to show the staff handoff.

Test a request with a location, description, and approved sample photo. Confirm the record reaches the correct property and the renter can find it later.

Keep emergency instructions separate from ordinary requests. Define the urgent response route before launch. Do not imply that every portal submission receives immediate attention.

Ask how residents add details after submission. Keep the new information with the original request. Staff should not need to merge a separate email manually.

Check the status labels. Assigned, awaiting approval, and complete should have clear meanings. Completing a vendor task should not imply that every resident concern is resolved.

Test reopening and follow-up. Preserve the original history so the next staff member can understand the issue without another call.

## Should renters see their ledger?

Give renters clear access to approved charge and payment history so they can understand the balance. Test corrections and pending items too.

Buildium's Resident Center page explicitly describes rent, fees, and payment history. Inspect the actual labels before assuming the renter can follow the ledger.

A submitted payment can have a different status from cleared funds. Ask the vendor to explain the display. Use plain words in your help instructions.

Test a credit, refund, and corrected charge. Confirm the balance and history remain consistent. A hidden adjustment can create questions even when the total is correct.

Check what each roommate can see. Decide which shared records are appropriate and keep private details restricted. Ask the vendor to demonstrate those permissions.

Give renters a clear route to question a charge. Staff should find the record and respond with its source. Keep the discussion linked to the relevant account.

Ask how the portal shows old leases and closed balances. A move should not leave a person unsure which property or period a record covers.

## Do renters need a mobile app?

Renters need a reliable phone workflow, whether they use a browser, an app, or both. Requiring installation can add a step without helping every task.

Buildium and AppFolio describe mobile resident access. A mobile claim does not prove the experience works well on every device.

Test signup, password recovery, payment, upload, and download on a phone. Use a small screen and an ordinary connection. Note tasks that require a desktop.

Check interruptions. Close a form, reopen it, and inspect the saved state. Make sure failed uploads and incomplete payments remain clear.

Ask how renters receive updates. A push alert, email, and portal message may carry different details. Confirm where a reply goes and which record staff review.

Use instructions with the actual screen labels. Keep help short and task-specific. Update it when the vendor changes the interface.

Retain an assisted route for people who cannot complete the task. Track the exception so staff do not lose its related record.

Check how an invited user changes a phone or email address. Account recovery should restore the correct access without exposing another household's records.

## Which software has the best tenant app?

Choose the portal that lets renters complete required tasks with the fewest unclear steps. Ratings and feature lists cannot establish a universal winner.

Use Buildium Resident Center and AppFolio's portal as candidates for a matched test. This guide reviews documented scope and does not claim firsthand performance results.

Have a person unfamiliar with the product follow the proposed instructions. Use sample records without private tenant data. Note where the person stops or needs help.

Use this acceptance checklist:

- **Join:** Accept an invitation and confirm the correct unit and contact details.
- **Pay:** Review fees, submit a sample payment, and locate its status.
- **Read:** Find the balance, history, and a lease document.
- **Request:** Upload a maintenance detail and find the request afterward.
- **Reply:** Add information without starting an unrelated thread.
- **Recover:** Reset access without exposing another user's records.
- **Leave:** Confirm access changes when a lease or relationship ends.
- **Get help:** Reach a person when the portal cannot resolve the issue.

Record failures as tasks with owners before rollout. Train staff on the same workflow renters will use. Keep the support process clear when the app fails.
