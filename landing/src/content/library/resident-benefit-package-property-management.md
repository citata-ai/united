## What is in a typical resident benefit package?

A resident benefit package (RBP) holds a set of services that save the resident time or money. The manager adds the package to the lease and charges one monthly fee for it. Some managers build the package themselves. Others buy it from a vendor.

Second Nature is one of the largest RBP vendors. It says 2,500+ property management companies use it. Its site lists these services on 2026-10-19:

| Service | What the resident gets | What the owner gets |
|---|---|---|
| Air filter delivery | New HVAC filters by mail | Less HVAC wear |
| Renters insurance program | Coverage that meets the lease rule | Fewer uninsured damage claims |
| Credit building | Rent payments reported to credit bureaus | A reason for residents to pay on time |
| On-demand pest control | Pest service when needed | Fewer pest disputes at move-out |
| Identity protection | Identity theft monitoring | Nothing direct |
| Group rate internet | Internet at a bulk price | A selling point for the listing |
| Move-in concierge | Help to set up utilities | Fewer move-in calls to staff |
| Resident rewards | Points or perks for on-time rent | A reason for residents to pay on time |

A manager can build a smaller package too, for example filter delivery, a renters insurance program and rent reporting. Each item should have a cost you can show.

## How much revenue per door can it add?

An RBP adds the gap between the fee and the cost of the services, per door, per month. We found no neutral survey of RBP revenue. Vendor numbers come from the vendor that sells it. So test your own numbers.

A worked example (example input, not market data):

| Line | Per door per month | Per year, 200 doors |
|---|---|---|
| RBP fee to resident | $40 | $96,000 |
| Vendor and service cost | $25 | $60,000 |
| Staff time to manage it | $3 | $7,200 |
| Net to the company | $12 | $28,800 |

At these inputs, the package adds $144 a door a year. That can matter for a small company. It also shows the risk: if 20% of residents call the fee unfair and move out at renewal, the turnover cost can erase the gain.

Run the test with your real vendor quote. Then compare the net to what you earn from management fees. The [property management fee calculator](/tools/property-management-fee-calculator) helps you see your per-door revenue.

## What do junk-fee rules and state laws say?

Federal and state fee rules treat rental housing in different ways. Check both before you charge an RBP.

| Rule | What it covers | What it means for an RBP |
|---|---|---|
| FTC Rule on Unfair or Deceptive Fees, 16 CFR Part 464 (in effect 2025-05-12) | Live-event tickets and short-term lodging | Does not cover long-term rentals with an ongoing landlord-tenant relationship, by the FTC FAQ |
| Colorado HB25-1090 (signed 2025-04-21, in effect 2026-01-01) | All pricing offers and ads in Colorado, incl. rental housing | Ads must show the total price of all amounts a person may pay; landlords may not require some fees |
| C.R.S. 6-1-737(4)(a), part of HB25-1090 | Utility charges to tenants | No utility charge above the provider's amount, except the limit in C.R.S. 38-12-801(3)(a)(VI) |

The Colorado Department of Law issued a memo on 2025-11-24 about HB25-1090. It says the law applies to leases amended, renewed or signed on or after January 1, 2026. Holland & Knight, a law firm, reads the law to mean rental ads must show mandatory non-government fees in one total price. It also advises leases to separate optional services from required charges.

Other states and cities have their own fee laws. Rules change often in this area. Before you add an RBP in a new state, check the state law and the local code.

This is general information, not legal advice. Check the current law or ask a local attorney.

## How do I disclose the fee to applicants?

Disclose the RBP fee before the applicant pays anything. A fee a resident first sees at lease signing feels like a trick, even when it is legal. Use this checklist:

- [ ] **Listing:** show the RBP fee next to the rent, or show a total monthly price that includes it.
- [ ] **Application page:** list the fee and each service in it before the application fee is paid.
- [ ] **Lease or addendum:** name each service, the monthly price and how the resident uses each one.
- [ ] **Move-in cost sheet:** show the first month's RBP fee as its own line.
- [ ] **Opt-out terms:** say if a resident can opt out of an item, for example renters insurance they already have.
- [ ] **Same terms for all:** charge the same package and fee to every resident in a property. A fee that changes by applicant creates a Fair Housing risk.
- [ ] **Owner agreement:** tell the owner who keeps the RBP revenue.

Store the signed addendum in the tenant file. If a resident complains, you can show what they saw and when.

## What do residents get in return?

Residents get value only when they use the services. A filter that arrives and sits in a closet is a cost with no benefit. Track use, not only sign-ups.

Three checks each quarter:

1. **Use rate per service.** How many residents changed a filter, filed a pest request or saw a credit report update?
2. **Complaints about the fee.** Count them and read them.
3. **Renewal rate.** Compare renewal rates before and after you added the package.

Drop any service that fewer residents use than you expected. A smaller, used package is easier to defend than a large, unused one.

A clear fee page also helps with owners. Owners now ask AI tools what managers charge. A manager with clear, published fees is easier for an AI answer to describe. See [how owners choose a property manager with AI](/guides/how-owners-choose-a-property-manager-with-ai).
