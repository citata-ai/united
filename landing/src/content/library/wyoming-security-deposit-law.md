## Wyoming security deposit rules at a glance

Wyoming's deposit rules sit in the Residential Rental Property Act, Wyo. Stat. 1-21-1201 to 1-21-1211. We read the full text in the Wyoming Legislature's official Title 1 PDF on 2026-10-19.

| Rule | Limit or deadline | Statute |
|---|---|---|
| Maximum deposit | No cap | Wyo. Stat. 1-21-1201 to -1211 |
| Nonrefundable part | Must be stated in the lease and in a written notice at deposit time | Wyo. Stat. 1-21-1207 |
| Allowed uses | Accrued rent, damage beyond reasonable wear and tear, cleaning, other contract costs | Wyo. Stat. 1-21-1208(a) |
| Return deadline | 30 days after the lease ends, or 15 days after you get the new address, whichever is later | Wyo. Stat. 1-21-1208(a) |
| Damage to the unit | Deadline extended by 30 days | Wyo. Stat. 1-21-1208(a) |
| Itemization | Written, with reasons | Wyo. Stat. 1-21-1208(a) |
| Interest | None owed | Wyo. Stat. 1-21-1208(a) |
| Renter's address | Renter tells you within 30 days where to send payment | Wyo. Stat. 1-21-1208(a) |
| Utility deposit | 10 days after proof that utilities are paid | Wyo. Stat. 1-21-1208(b) |
| Unreasonable failure | Renter may recover the full deposit and court costs | Wyo. Stat. 1-21-1208(c) |
| New owner | Bound by the deposit rules | Wyo. Stat. 1-21-1209 |

This is general information, not legal advice. Check the current law or ask a local attorney.

## What is the maximum security deposit a landlord can charge in Wyoming?

Wyoming sets no maximum security deposit. The Residential Rental Property Act names no dollar or month limit.

The Act does control nonrefundable money. Under Wyo. Stat. 1-21-1207, any rental agreement must state whether any part of a deposit is nonrefundable. You must also give the renter written notice of that fact when you take the deposit. Do both. Without them, you have no written basis to keep that part.

**What to write.** "Of the $1,500 deposit, $250 is a nonrefundable cleaning fee. The other $1,250 is refundable under Wyo. Stat. 1-21-1208." Put the same line in the lease and on the deposit receipt.

## How many days does a Wyoming landlord have to return a security deposit?

A Wyoming landlord has 30 days after the rental agreement ends, or 15 days after receiving the renter's new mailing address, whichever is later (Wyo. Stat. 1-21-1208(a)). If the unit is damaged, the period is extended by 30 days.

The renter has a duty too. Within 30 days after the agreement ends, the renter must tell you or your agent where to send the payment and notice.

**Worked example.** The lease ends on June 30. Day 30 is July 30.

| Case | Address received | Deadline |
|---|---|---|
| No damage, address at move-out | June 30 | July 30 (30 days is later than 15) |
| No damage, address on July 25 | July 25 | August 9 (15 days after the address is later) |
| Damage, address at move-out | June 30 | August 29 (July 30 plus 30 days) |

"Termination" has a broad meaning. Wyo. Stat. 1-21-1201(a)(v) includes the end of the rental period, a mutual agreement, an owner's termination under 1-21-1203(d), abandonment and a court order.

## What can a landlord deduct from a security deposit in Wyoming?

A Wyoming landlord may apply the deposit to four kinds of costs (Wyo. Stat. 1-21-1208(a)):

1. **Accrued rent.** Rent owed up to the end of the agreement.
2. **Damage.** Damage to the unit beyond reasonable wear and tear.
3. **Cleaning.** The cost to clean the unit to its condition at the start of the agreement.
4. **Other contract costs.** Other costs provided by any contract, such as the lease.

The renter's duties set the cleaning standard. Wyo. Stat. 1-21-1204(a)(viii) requires the renter to remove all property and garbage before the agreement ends. The renter must also clean the unit to its condition at the start. A move-in condition report with photos is the best proof of that starting condition.

If the damage is more than the deposit, the renter stays liable for the rest. The renter also owes interest at 10% a year on any unpaid amount (Wyo. Stat. 1-21-1211(b)). You may take any legal action to recover the damage.

## Does a Wyoming landlord have to give an itemized list of deductions?

Yes. Wyo. Stat. 1-21-1208(a) requires "a written itemization of any deductions from the deposit together with reasons therefor." Deliver or mail it with the balance and any prepaid rent.

A clear itemization lists:

- Each deduction by item and room.
- The reason: rent, damage beyond reasonable wear and tear, cleaning, or a lease clause.
- The amount, with a receipt or bid where you have one.
- The balance refunded, or the amount the renter still owes.

**Utility deposits follow a separate clock.** Under Wyo. Stat. 1-21-1208(b), refund a separately named utility deposit within 10 days after the renter shows that all utility charges are paid. If the renter shows nothing within 45 days after the agreement ends, apply the deposit to the unpaid utility debt within the next 15 days. Then refund any balance within 7 days, or within 15 days after you get the new address, whichever is later.

## Must a Wyoming landlord pay interest or keep the deposit in a separate account?

A Wyoming landlord does not pay interest. Wyo. Stat. 1-21-1208(a) says the balance is delivered or mailed "without interest."

The Act also has no rule that requires a separate or trust bank account. We read sections 1-21-1201 to 1-21-1211 on 2026-10-19 and found none. A separate account is still good practice. It keeps the money traceable if the building is sold.

A sale does not end your duty. Under Wyo. Stat. 1-21-1209, whoever holds the owner's interest when the agreement ends is bound by the deposit rules in 1-21-1207 and 1-21-1208. If you buy a building, get the deposit ledger and the funds at closing.

## What is the penalty for a Wyoming landlord who keeps a deposit wrongly?

The penalty is the full deposit plus court costs. Under Wyo. Stat. 1-21-1208(c), if the owner or agent unreasonably fails to comply with the return rules, the renter may recover the full deposit and court costs.

The rule also protects owners from weak claims. If the owner wins and the court finds the renter acted unreasonably in suing, the court may award the owner court costs.

**Worked example.** You hold a $1,400 deposit. You send nothing for 75 days, with no damage and the address in hand. The renter sues. A court may find the failure unreasonable and award the full $1,400 plus court costs, even if you had a valid $300 cleaning charge.

| Mistake | Risk | Statute |
|---|---|---|
| No itemization or late return | Full deposit plus court costs, if unreasonable | Wyo. Stat. 1-21-1208(c) |
| Nonrefundable part not stated in lease and notice | No written basis to keep it | Wyo. Stat. 1-21-1207 |
| Utility deposit kept past the deadlines | Full deposit plus court costs, if unreasonable | Wyo. Stat. 1-21-1208(b)-(c) |

## Wyoming deposit checklist for property managers

- [ ] Lease and deposit receipt state any nonrefundable part.
- [ ] Move-in condition is recorded with photos.
- [ ] Utility deposit, if any, is named separately.
- [ ] Move-out date and the renter's new address date are recorded.
- [ ] Deadline: the later of 30 days or 15 days after the address, plus 30 days if damaged.
- [ ] Written itemization with reasons goes with the balance.
- [ ] On a purchase, deposits and records move with the building.
