## Does AI quote bad reviews?

Yes. AI assistants read review sites and summarize the pattern they find. Google says Gemini in Google Maps lets people "find out what people like about a place by reading the helpful review summarized with Gemini" (Google, 2024-10-31). The same post says Google Search would get "AI-powered review summaries".

People read these summaries. BrightLocal's 2026 Local Consumer Review Survey asked 1,002 US adults. 82% read AI-generated review summaries, and 23% would rely on them alone (BrightLocal, 2026). The same survey found that 45% now use ChatGPT or other AI tools for local recommendations, up from 6% in 2025.

AI assistants also read the review sites themselves. In our October 2026 test, we asked ChatGPT, Claude and Gemini for the best property manager in 50 US cities. Yelp was a source in all 50 Claude answers. Expertise.com was a source in 93 of 150 answers, and BBB in 14 (our data, 2026-10).

A summary does not quote one angry tenant. A summary repeats a pattern. Five reviews about slow repairs become "reviews mention slow maintenance". That line reaches every owner who asks about you.

## Which complaints shape the AI summary most?

The complaints that repeat shape the summary most. For property managers, tenant reviews tend to cluster around a few themes. Map your own reviews against this table.

| Theme in reviews | What the AI may say | Real fix |
|---|---|---|
| Repairs take weeks | "Some reviews mention slow maintenance" | Response time target, work order updates to tenants |
| Deposit deductions | "Some tenants report disputes over deposits" | Move-out photo report, itemized letter on time |
| Nobody answers the phone | "Reviewers say communication is hard" | One shared inbox, a callback rule, after-hours line |
| Fees not clear | "Some owners mention hidden fees" | A full fee table on your site |
| Rude staff | "Mixed reviews about staff attitude" | Training, call review, one escalation contact |

Count your last 50 reviews on Google and Yelp. Tag each negative one with a theme. The theme with the most tags is the one AI will repeat. Fix that one first.

## Should you reply to every review?

Yes, reply to every review, good and bad. BrightLocal's 2026 survey found that 89% of consumers expect owners to reply. 80% are likely to use a business that replies to all its reviews, and 42% are unlikely to use one that never replies (BrightLocal, 2026). 50% are put off by templated replies.

A reply to a negative review is not for the tenant who wrote it. A reply is for the next owner who reads it, and for the AI that reads both.

Reply in 5 steps:

1. **Thank the person.** One short line.
2. **Name the issue in neutral words.** "You waited 3 weeks for the dishwasher repair."
3. **State one fact or one change.** "We now send a status text within 24 hours of each request."
4. **Move the detail offline.** Give a name and a direct phone or email.
5. **Keep private data out.** Do not post rent owed, lease terms, the unit number or any detail of the tenant's life.

Watch for: an angry reply. An AI can quote your reply as easily as the review. Write the reply, wait an hour, then read it as an owner would.

Google notes that a reply may take "up to 10 minutes" to appear, and sometimes "up to 30 days" (Google Business Profile Help).

## Can you remove unfair reviews?

You can remove a review only when it breaks the platform's policy. Disagreement is not enough. Google says you should not report a review "just because you disagree with it or dislike it". Google also says it "doesn't get involved in conflict between businesses and customers" (Google Business Profile Help).

Google's content policy does ban some reviews (Google Maps policy):

- Content "not based on a real experience".
- Content "posted from multiple accounts by or at the request of one person".
- Content "based on a conflict of interest", such as a former employee.
- "General, political, or social commentary or personal rants" not about the location.

How to report on Google: open your Business Profile, select Read reviews, click the report icon and pick a reason. Or use the Reviews Management Tool, which shows the report status. If the status says "no policy violation", you get one appeal. Google says evaluation "typically takes several days".

Do not threaten the reviewer. The FTC rule on consumer reviews, in effect since October 21, 2024, bans review suppression. Section 465.7 covers an unfounded legal threat, intimidation or a false public accusation used to stop or remove a review (16 CFR 465.7). The same section bans a claim that your review page shows all reviews when you hide the negative ones.

This is general information, not legal advice. Check the current law or ask a local attorney.

## How do you balance the review mix?

You balance the mix with more honest reviews, not with fewer bad ones. Most happy owners and tenants never write a review unless you ask. A fair request to all of them brings the average closer to the truth.

Rules for a fair request:

- **Ask everyone, not only happy clients.** Google bans requests that "selectively solicit positive reviews".
- **Offer nothing in return.** Google bans payment, discounts and free services for reviews.
- **No reviews from staff or family.** Google treats them as a conflict of interest. The FTC rule (16 CFR 465.5) bans undisclosed insider reviews.
- **Ask at a natural moment.** A smooth move-in, a fast repair, a year-end owner statement.

A simple check: count reviews from the last 3 months. BrightLocal found 74% of consumers look for reviews from the last 3 months (BrightLocal, 2026). If your newest review is a year old, the old complaints carry more weight.

| Week | Action | Done when |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Tag the last 50 reviews by theme. Reply to every unanswered one. | 0 reviews without a reply |
| 1 | Report the reviews that break policy. Log each report status. | Each report has a status |
| 2 | Fix the top theme in operations. Write the change down. | A written rule exists |
| 3 | Send an honest review request to all owners and tenants with a recent closed job. | Request sent to the full list |
