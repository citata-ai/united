## Which AI tools should property managers compare?

Compare the task, price basis and approval controls before comparing AI brands. AI means software that generates or infers an output from data.

| Tool | Vendor-described work | Public price status |
|---|---|---|
| AppFolio Realm-X | Messages, assistant and plan-based workflows | Core from $1.49 per unit monthly; minimums apply |
| Rentvine AI | Assistant, invoices and Fixie maintenance | Platform as low as $1.50 per active unit; full quote |
| Entrata Leasing AI | Prospect replies, tour scheduling and follow-up | Quote only on the product page |
| LetHub | Leasing responses, scheduling and integrations | $199 monthly platform minimum |
| DoorLoop | Assistant and plan-based agents | One displays $199 monthly minimum |

Sources: [AppFolio](https://www.appfolio.com/pricing), [Rentvine](https://www.rentvine.com/pricing), [Entrata](https://www.entrata.com/products-new/leasing-ai), [LetHub](https://www.lethub.co/pricing) and [DoorLoop](https://www.doorloop.com/pricing), read 2026-10-19.

Platform prices do not establish a stand-alone AI fee. Ask what usage, service and billing terms apply. Do not count a feature marked coming soon as a current tool.

## Which AI is built into AppFolio, Rentvine and Entrata?

AppFolio, Rentvine and Entrata describe AI attached to work inside their own systems. That can reduce copying, but access and approval still need testing.

AppFolio lists Realm-X Assistant and Messages in Core. Plus adds Realm-X Flows. Ask which actions the offered plan can complete, and which require another service.

Rentvine's [AI page](https://www.rentvine.com/ai-assistant) describes invoice extraction, assistant actions, listing writing and Fixie maintenance intake. The page describes Fixie asking follow-up questions and preparing a diagnostic summary.

Entrata's Leasing AI page describes responses across communication channels, tour scheduling and lead activity tracking. It places these functions within Entrata's prospect workflow.

Test with incomplete and conflicting information. A correct answer to a clean example says little about an unusual case. Ask where the tool gets its facts and whether staff can inspect that source.

Do not give a tool broad write access just because it is built in. Confirm permissions and review the action history.

## Which standalone tools handle leasing and maintenance?

LetHub is a leasing-focused option to test beside your property system. Maintenance buyers should distinguish intake, advice, dispatch and full job completion.

LetHub's pricing page describes responses, scheduling, lead follow-up and connections to other systems. It also separates optional voice and lockbox services. Ask which connection works with your exact system.

For maintenance, Rentvine describes Fixie within its platform. DoorLoop describes maintenance agents within its current plan structure. These are not evidence that either product works as an independent replacement for your existing maintenance service.

Use a task boundary table:

| Work | Trial boundary |
|---|---|
| Leasing question | Answer from approved listing facts |
| Tour request | Offer only verified calendar availability |
| Repair intake | Collect symptoms, photos and access details |
| Vendor dispatch | Follow approved vendor and spending rules |
| Invoice entry | Extract fields for review before posting |

If you need an outside maintenance product, request a current integration demo and quote. Do not assume a product connects because it appears in a broad category list.

## Which tasks are safe to hand to AI?

Begin with drafts and structured intake that a person can inspect before use. Safety depends on the task and controls, not the AI label.

A message draft should cite the approved policy or record. A bill extraction should show the source document beside the suggested amount and account. A report answer should link to its underlying data.

Keep human approval on payment changes, charges, applicant decisions and unusual maintenance cases during the trial. These are recommended operating controls, not a statement of legal requirements.

Test an urgent repair message deliberately. Confirm that the system follows your escalation process and reaches a person. Do not ask residents to rely on a trial tool for emergency help.

Use the same approved housing criteria for every applicant. Do not ask AI to infer a protected trait or steer people toward particular homes.

Limit private data in general-purpose prompts. Use only a tool and account approved for the records involved. Ask the vendor how deletion and access work.

## What do AI tools cost?

AI cost can sit inside the platform, in a separate plan or in usage fees. Request a quote that separates those items.

LetHub publishes a $199 monthly platform minimum and onboarding from $199. Its page states a 12-month contract billed monthly. These terms were read on 2026-10-19.

AppFolio and Rentvine publish platform rates, but those figures do not answer every AI usage question. Entrata's Leasing AI page publishes no price. Treat unlisted costs as quote only.

DoorLoop now uses One, Connect and Multiply on its live pricing page. Confirm the offered agents, approval controls and monthly or annual term.

Ask what happens when usage rises. Include messages, voice, active listings, documents and any required partner service. Check whether a new tool duplicates something already in your system.

Avoid treating claimed staff savings as a discount from the invoice. Savings require your own trial evidence.

## How do you measure whether an AI tool pays off?

Measure completed work after review and corrections, then compare the result with the full cost. An automated reply count does not show whether a task finished correctly.

Choose a repeatable workflow and record the starting process. Keep the same definition of completion during the trial. Track cases that staff must reopen.

Use this trial record:

- **Volume:** tasks received and tasks completed.
- **Time:** preparation, review, correction and follow-up.
- **Quality:** missing facts, wrong actions and duplicate entries.
- **Escalation:** cases that reached the right person.
- **Cost:** subscription, usage, setup and support.
- **Outcome:** the work that stayed complete after review.

Calculate labor value with your own loaded staff cost. Label that value as your planning assumption. Do not borrow a vendor's savings claim as your result.

AI inside operations differs from visibility in public AI answers. Read [how owners choose a manager with AI](/guides/how-owners-choose-a-property-manager-with-ai) and the [AI property manager index](/research/ai-property-manager-index-2026) for that separate topic.

Keep the tool only if the measured result meets your chosen quality and cost checks.
