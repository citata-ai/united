An Oklahoma eviction is a "forcible entry and detainer" case in district court. The rules sit in two titles: Title 41 for notices and Title 12 for the court case. I read both on the Oklahoma Legislature site on 2026-10-19. The table below holds every limit in this guide.

| Rule | Limit or deadline | Statute |
|---|---|---|
| Notice for unpaid rent | 5 days after written demand | 41 O.S. §131(B) |
| Notice for a lease breach you can fix | Cure in 10 days, lease ends 15+ days after receipt | 41 O.S. §132(B) |
| Breach that causes imminent harm | File at once, no cure period | 41 O.S. §132(C) |
| Criminal or drug activity | Immediate termination | 41 O.S. §132(D) |
| End a month-to-month tenancy | 30 days' written notice | 41 O.S. §111(A) |
| End a tenancy shorter than a month | 7 days' written notice | 41 O.S. §111(B) |
| Hearing date | 5 to 10 days after the summons issues | 12 O.S. §1148.4, §1148.16 |
| Service of summons | At least 3 days before trial (posting: 5 days) | 12 O.S. §1148.5, §1148.5A |
| Motion for new trial | Within 3 days, does not stop the writ | 12 O.S. §1148.10 |
| Notice before writ | 48 hours | 12 O.S. §1148.10A |
| Base filing fees | $85 flat fee plus $43 add-ons | 28 O.S. §152 |
| Lockout | Up to 2x monthly rent or 2x damages | 41 O.S. §123 |

This is general information, not legal advice. Check the current law or ask a local attorney.

## What Notice Must an Oklahoma Landlord Give Before Filing an Eviction for Unpaid Rent?

An Oklahoma landlord gives a written 5-day demand for the past-due rent. If the tenant does not pay within 5 days, you may end the rental agreement (41 O.S. §131(B)). The demand for rent also counts as a demand for possession, so you need no second notice to quit.

The timing rule is flexible. Section 131(B) says you may give the notice before or after you file the case.

Serve the notice the way 41 O.S. §111(E) says:

1. **Hand it to the tenant.** Personal service comes first.
2. **Hand it to a family member.** If you cannot find the tenant, give it to a household member over 12.
3. **Post and mail.** If both fail, post it on the unit and mail a copy by certified mail.

Keep a copy and a note of the date, time and method. The judge may ask for it.

## How Long Does an Eviction Take in Oklahoma?

An Oklahoma eviction for unpaid rent can finish in about 2 to 3 weeks if nothing goes wrong. Here is a worked timeline from the statutory minimums and maximums:

| Day | Step | Statute |
|---|---|---|
| 0 | Serve the 5-day demand | 41 O.S. §131(B) |
| 5 | Rent still unpaid. Rental agreement ends | 41 O.S. §131(B) |
| 6 | File the affidavit. Clerk issues the summons | 12 O.S. §1148.15 |
| 11 to 16 | Hearing, 5 to 10 days after the summons issues | 12 O.S. §1148.4 |
| Hearing day | Judgment for the landlord. Writ issues on request | 12 O.S. §1148.10 |
| Hearing day | Serve or post the 48-hour notice | 12 O.S. §1148.10A |
| About 13 to 19 | Writ executed, landlord has possession | 12 O.S. §1148.10A |

Real cases often take longer. A court calendar, a failed service or a tenant appeal can add days or weeks.

Watch for: the tenant may post a supersedeas bond (a bond that pauses the judgment during appeal) within 2 days of judgment. A judge can extend that to 7 days. During the appeal, the tenant must pay current rent into the court clerk's office (12 O.S. §1148.10A(F)).

## Where Does a Landlord File an Eviction Case in Oklahoma, and What Does It Cost?

An Oklahoma landlord files the case in district court (12 O.S. §1148.1). Ask the court clerk in the county where the unit sits. You start the case with an eviction affidavit. The Oklahoma Bar Association writes that form in plain language, and OSCN links to it (12 O.S. §1148.15).

If the money you claim is within the small claims limit, the case goes on the small claims docket (12 O.S. §1148.14). The clerk must help a landlord without a lawyer prepare the affidavit and summons.

The fee statute lists these amounts:

| Fee | Amount | Statute |
|---|---|---|
| Flat fee, forcible entry and detainer | $85 | 28 O.S. §152(A)(14) |
| Law library fund | $6 | 28 O.S. §152(B) |
| Court information system fund | $25 | 28 O.S. §152(C) |
| Court-appointed special advocates | $10 | 28 O.S. §152(D) |
| Judicial complaints and interpreters | $2 | 28 O.S. §152(E) |
| Base total | $128 | |
| County sheriff fund (if adopted) | Up to $10 | 28 O.S. §152(F) |
| Summons, per person | $10 | 28 O.S. §152.1(A)(8) |
| Service, per person | $50 | 28 O.S. §152.1(A)(5) |

A worked case: one tenant, county add-on of $10, one summons and one service. That is $128 + $10 + $10 + $50 = $198 before postage. Ask the clerk for the exact total. If you join a rent claim, confirm which fee line applies.

The prevailing party gets a reasonable attorney fee (12 O.S. §1148.9). Since 2024-11-01, a jury trial is not an option for the possession claim itself. It is an option only for money claims, which the court splits off (12 O.S. §1148.7).

## Can an Oklahoma Landlord Evict a Tenant for a Lease Violation or With No Cause?

Yes, Oklahoma allows both. The notice depends on the reason.

**Lease violation you can fix.** For a material breach of the lease or the tenant duties in 41 O.S. §127, give written notice. The tenant has 10 days to fix it. If not, the lease ends on a date at least 15 days after the tenant got the notice (41 O.S. §132(B)). A second breach later allows immediate termination on written notice.

**Breach that causes imminent harm.** Some breaches threaten imminent and irremediable harm to people or the property. For those, you may file the case at once (41 O.S. §132(C)).

**Criminal activity.** Criminal activity that threatens other tenants, and drug-related criminal activity on or near the premises, are grounds for immediate termination (41 O.S. §132(D)).

**No cause.** A month-to-month tenancy ends on 30 days' written notice (41 O.S. §111(A)). A shorter tenancy needs 7 days (§111(B)). A fixed-term lease ends on its end date with no notice (§111(C)).

A tenant who stays after the end without your consent is a holdover. If the holdover is willful and not in good faith, you may recover up to 2x the average monthly rent, prorated daily (41 O.S. §111(D)).

## Who Removes the Tenant After a Judgment in Oklahoma?

In Oklahoma, the landlord, the landlord's agent or an officer may execute the writ (12 O.S. §1148.10A). This differs from many states, where only a sheriff may act.

The steps after judgment:

1. **Ask for the writ.** The court issues it at your request (12 O.S. §1148.10).
2. **Give 48 hours' notice.** Serve the tenant in person or post the notice on the unit.
3. **Return and execute.** Come back after 48 hours to take possession.
4. **Call for help if needed.** You may summon the county sheriff or city police.

A person who refuses to leave after service of the writ commits trespass. The penalty is a fine up to $500, up to 30 days in county jail, or both (12 O.S. §1148.10A(D)).

For property left behind, 41 O.S. §130 applies. Items of no clear value may be discarded. For items with value, mail a certified notice. Property left 30 days or longer is deemed abandoned.

## Is a Self-Help Eviction (Lockout, Utility Shutoff) Legal in Oklahoma?

No. A self-help eviction is illegal in Oklahoma. If a landlord wrongfully removes or excludes a tenant, the tenant may recover possession in court or end the lease. In both cases, the tenant recovers up to 2x the monthly rent or 2x actual damages, whichever is greater (41 O.S. §123).

A utility shutoff carries its own risk. Say you willfully or negligently fail to supply heat, water, electric, gas or another essential service. The tenant may then end the lease or buy the service and deduct it from rent (41 O.S. §121(C)). The tenant may also move to substitute housing and pay no rent for that time.
