## Why does a reply to a bad review matter?

A reply to a bad review is a public sales page. The reviewer may never change the stars. But each owner who reads the review also reads your answer.

The BrightLocal Local Consumer Review Survey 2026 asked 1,002 US adults about replies:

| Finding | Share |
|---|---|
| Expect business owners to respond to reviews | 89% |
| Likely to use a business that responds to all reviews | 80% |
| Unlikely to use a business that never replies | 42% |
| Unlikely to choose a business with templated replies | 50% |

So reply to every review, and write each reply by hand. A copy-paste "Sorry for your experience" fails half of readers.

## What should the first reply say?

The first reply has 4 parts and fits in 60 to 100 words. Google's own tips match this shape: be polite, keep it short, apologize when right, explain limits and take complex cases private.

1. **Thank and name.** Use the first name shown on the review.
2. **Own the part you got wrong.** "We took 6 days to answer your repair request. That is too slow."
3. **State one fact or fix.** What changed, with no private details.
4. **Take it offline.** A named person and a direct phone or email.

Example, for a slow repair:

```text
Hi Sam, thank you for telling us. A 6-day wait for a reply on a heating repair is too slow, and we are sorry. We have since added an after-hours line for no-heat calls. I would like to hear the rest of the story. Please call me directly at [number]. — [Name], Maintenance Manager, [Company]
```

Do not argue the details in public. Do not list what the reviewer did wrong. Google also asks you not to take responsibility for things outside your control. Say what you can and cannot do.

## How do I reply without breaking tenant privacy?

Never post anything a reader could not already see. Google bans sharing "a reviewer's private information" in a reply. For a property manager, that list is long.

| Never write | Write instead |
|---|---|
| "You owe $2,400 in back rent." | "We have a record of this account and want to talk it through." |
| "We filed an eviction in March." | "We followed the lease and state law. Please call me." |
| "Your unit at 412 Oak St had damage." | "We would like to review the move-out report with you." |
| "You had 3 noise complaints." | "We take every concern from neighbors seriously." |
| "Your security deposit claim is wrong." | "Our deposit statement lists each charge. I can go over it by phone." |

Also do not confirm the reviewer is a tenant if the review does not say so. A reply that says "As our tenant at..." gives away where the person lives.

Fair Housing: a reply about a rental is a public statement. The Fair Housing Act (42 U.S.C. 3604(c)) bans any statement about a rental that shows a preference or limit based on race, color, religion, sex, disability, familial status or national origin. Do not mention a reviewer's family, children, faith, origin or disability. Talk only about the service.

This is general information, not legal advice. Check the current law or ask a local attorney.

## When should I take it offline?

Take it offline whenever the facts involve money, a legal step or a named person. Google's tip says the same: "If a situation is complex, politely request that the reviewer contact you via phone or email to resolve the issue privately."

Take it offline when the review involves:

- Rent, fees, late charges or a deposit.
- An eviction, a notice or a lease breach.
- A dispute with a named staff member.
- A safety or habitability issue that needs an inspection.
- An owner's money: statements, owner draws or a contract end.

Reply once in public, then call. If you solve the problem, you may tell the person they can update the review. Do not ask them to remove it, and never offer money, credit or a waived fee for a change. Google bans incentives to revise or remove a negative review.

## Which reviews can I report to Google?

Report a review only when it breaks Google policy. Google says "Do not report a review just because you disagree with it or dislike it." Valid reasons include:

| Reason | Example |
|---|---|
| Fake engagement | A review from a person who never dealt with you |
| Conflict of interest | A former employee or a rival company |
| Harassment or hate speech | Attacks on a staff member's identity |
| Personal information | A staff member's home address or phone number |
| Off-topic or spam | An ad, a link or text about another business |
| Extortion | A demand for money to remove the review |

How to report: open your Business Profile, select **Read reviews**, select **Report** next to the review and pick a reason. Google says evaluation "typically takes several days." Use the Reviews Management Tool to check the status. If Google finds no violation, you can submit a one-time appeal.

## What must I never do?

Some replies break federal law, not only Google policy.

- **No threats.** The FTC rule at 16 CFR 465.7 bans groundless legal threats, physical threats and intimidation against a reviewer. It also bans false public accusations used to stop or remove a review.
- **No gag clauses.** The Consumer Review Fairness Act (15 U.S.C. 45b) voids form contract terms that bar reviews, charge a fee for a review or take the rights to the review content. A standard lease or management agreement is a form contract when the other party cannot negotiate it.
- **No fake counter-reviews.** 16 CFR 465.2 bans fake reviews, and Google bans posts from many accounts by one person.
- **No hiding bad reviews on your own site** while you present the rest as all reviews (16 CFR 465.7(b)).

## Can a good reply win owners?

A good reply can show an owner how you work under stress. An owner hires you to deal with tenants, repairs and disputes. A calm, specific reply is a sample of that work.

Read each reply as a new owner would:

- Does it show we answer fast?
- Does it show we fix systems, not only one case?
- Does it protect the tenant's privacy?
- Would I trust this company with my property?

Reviews also feed AI answers. We tested 150 AI answers about the best property manager in 50 US cities. Yelp was a source in 50 of them (our data, 2026-10-19). Read [how owners choose a property manager with AI](/guides/how-owners-choose-a-property-manager-with-ai) for what they pull.

## Bad review reply checklist

- Reply within a few days.
- 60 to 100 words, written by hand.
- Thank, own, fix, take offline.
- No balance, address, eviction, deposit or complaint details.
- No mention of family, children, faith, origin or disability.
- A named person and a direct contact.
- No offer of money or credit for a change.
- Report only real policy breaks, with one appeal if needed.
- No threats, no gag clauses, no fake reviews.
