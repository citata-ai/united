Oklahoma gives landlords wide room to set rent. The limits are in notice and service, not in the amount. I read Title 41 (Landlord and Tenant) and Title 11 (Cities and Towns) on the Oklahoma Legislature site on 2026-10-19. The table below holds every rule in this guide.

| Rule | Limit or deadline | Statute |
|---|---|---|
| Statewide rent cap | None | No statute sets one |
| City rent control | Banned, with 3 narrow exceptions | 11 O.S. §14-101.1 |
| Notice of a rent increase | No specific section; tie it to the 30-day rule | 41 O.S. §111(A) |
| End a month-to-month tenancy | 30 days' written notice | 41 O.S. §111(A) |
| End a tenancy shorter than a month | 7 days' written notice | 41 O.S. §111(B) |
| Fixed-term lease | Ends on its end date, no notice | 41 O.S. §111(C) |
| Default term with no written term | Month-to-month (week-to-week for weekly roomers) | 41 O.S. §110 |
| How to serve notice | In person, then family member over 12, then post and certified mail | 41 O.S. §111(E) |
| Willful holdover | Up to 2x average monthly rent, prorated | 41 O.S. §111(D) |

## Does Oklahoma Have a Cap on Rent Increases or Rent Control?

Oklahoma has no cap on rent increases and no rent control. No statute in Title 41 limits how much you may raise the rent.

Cities cannot add a cap either. Under 11 O.S. §14-101.1(A), no city or town may enact or enforce an ordinance that regulates the amount of rent. The ban covers privately owned single-family, multi-unit and commercial rentals. The ban dates from 1988.

The statute keeps 3 exceptions (11 O.S. §14-101.1(B)):

- A city may regulate property that the city or its authority owns.
- A city may sign agreements with private owners that limit rent on subsidized units.
- A city may restrict rent on properties assisted with federal Community Development Block Grant funds.

Watch for: a subsidy contract can still limit your rent. If you rent to a Housing Choice Voucher holder, the housing authority checks the rent. See [Section 8 rent limits](/guides/section-8-rent-limits) for how that works.

## How Many Days' Notice Does an Oklahoma Landlord Need to Raise the Rent?

Oklahoma law sets no specific notice period for a rent increase. Title 41 has no section on rent increases.

The 30-day termination rule still shapes your notice. Under 41 O.S. §111(A), either side may end a month-to-month tenancy with at least 30 days' written notice. A rent increase changes the terms of that tenancy. Give at least 30 days' written notice, so the new rent starts no earlier than a lawful end date. The tenant can then choose to stay at the new rent or leave.

Here is a worked example for a month-to-month tenant who pays on the 1st:

| Step | Date | Note |
|---|---|---|
| Serve written notice of new rent | 2026-10-30 | Hand delivery to the tenant |
| 30 days run | 2026-10-30 to 2026-11-29 | Count from the date of service, §111(E) |
| First due date after day 30 | 2026-12-01 | New rent starts |
| Old rent $1,050, new rent $1,120 | | Increase $70, or 6.7% |

If you serve on 2026-11-05 instead, 30 days run to 2026-12-05. Start the new rent on 2027-01-01, the next due date. You may give 60 days to leave a margin. That is a choice, not an Oklahoma rule.

Use these steps for each increase:

1. **Check the lease.** It may require a longer notice or a set method.
2. **Write the notice.** State the old rent, the new rent and the start date.
3. **Serve it by the §111(E) method.** In person first. Then a household member over 12. Then post it and mail a copy by certified mail.
4. **Keep proof.** Note the date, time and method of service.

## Can an Oklahoma Landlord Raise the Rent During a Fixed-Term Lease?

Not unless the lease allows it. Rent is payable at the time and place the parties agree (41 O.S. §109). A lease for a definite term runs to its end date (41 O.S. §111(C)).

So the rent in a 12-month lease stays fixed for 12 months. An exception exists only if the lease has a clause that allows a set increase, such as an escalation on a stated date.

A lease clause cannot remove the tenant's rights under the Act. 41 O.S. §113 makes such a waiver unenforceable. Keep any increase clause clear and specific.

The best time to change rent is at renewal. A fixed-term lease ends on its end date without notice (§111(C)). Offer the new lease, with the new rent, before that date.

If the tenant stays after the end date with your consent, the tenancy becomes month-to-month, unless you agree otherwise (41 O.S. §111(D)). From then on, the 30-day rule applies.

## How Much Notice Is Needed to End a Month-to-Month Tenancy in Oklahoma?

An Oklahoma month-to-month tenancy ends on at least 30 days' written notice from either side (41 O.S. §111(A)). The 30 days start on the date of service.

| Tenancy type | Notice to end | Statute |
|---|---|---|
| Month-to-month or at will | 30 days, written | 41 O.S. §111(A) |
| Shorter than month-to-month (for example weekly) | 7 days, written | 41 O.S. §111(B) |
| Fixed term | None, it ends on the end date | 41 O.S. §111(C) |
| Victim of domestic violence, sexual violence or stalking | Tenant may end without penalty on written notice and a protective order, within 30 days of the incident | 41 O.S. §111(F) |

If no written lease sets a term, the tenancy is month-to-month. A roomer or boarder who pays weekly has a week-to-week tenancy (41 O.S. §110).

A tenant who stays after the end date without your consent is a holdover. You may file for possession at once. If the holdover is willful and not in good faith, you may also recover up to 2x the average monthly rent, prorated daily (41 O.S. §111(D)).

Watch for: 41 O.S. §113.3 bars a landlord from refusing to renew or ending a tenancy because a tenant is a victim of domestic violence, sexual violence or stalking. Do not give a 30-day notice for that reason.

## Do Cities in Oklahoma Add Their Own Rent or Notice Rules?

Oklahoma cities cannot limit the amount of rent (11 O.S. §14-101.1). That covers Oklahoma City, Tulsa, Norman and every other city or town.

The state statute does not bar every local housing rule. Cities still adopt building and housing codes, and Title 41 refers to them (41 O.S. §102). Check your city code for rental registration, inspection or code rules before you raise rent on an older unit.

Notice periods come from state law. Title 41 sets the 30-day and 7-day rules, and no city may change the rent amount.
