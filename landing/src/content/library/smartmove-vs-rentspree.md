## What does each report include?

SmartMove and RentSpree sell packages with different report scopes. Match the included reports before you compare prices. A cheaper package may omit the information your policy calls for.

SmartMove lists ResidentScore, credit, criminal, and eviction-related reports across its packages. Premium adds Income Insights and an identity check. ResidentScore is a screening score, not a substitute for reading the records behind a decision.

RentSpree's pricing page separates basic credit, background, and eviction reporting from enhanced income and reference checks. Bank-linked income and an income estimate are different forms of evidence. Ask what happens when an applicant cannot link an account.

Request an example report from each vendor. Check the source, record dates, identity match, dispute route, and missing-data labels. A blank field should not look like a verified absence of a record.

## What does each cost and who pays?

SmartMove prices by package, while RentSpree lists report charges apart from its rental management plans. Compare the screening order itself. Do not confuse a free landlord account with free applicant screening.

SmartMove's page lists Plus at $40 between Basic and Premium. RentSpree lists a free Basic management plan. These figures come from the vendor pages read 2026-10-19.

Decide who pays before you send the invitation. Ask how refunds, repeat requests, and failed identity checks work. Avoid making an applicant pay again because your office chose the wrong report.

Record the requested package in your application file. Staff should be able to explain why they ordered it. Check local rules with counsel before setting fees or choosing which records to request.

## Which has the better applicant experience?

The better applicant experience is the one your applicants can complete and correct without confusion. Test the invitation, phone view, identity step, support path, and report release.

Ask a colleague to complete a permitted sample workflow. Check whether the applicant can see the price before payment. Find the help link without using the landlord account.

Then test an incomplete application. Staff need to distinguish a person who stopped from a report still in progress. Automated reminders should help, rather than create pressure to pay twice.

Do not rank either vendor on ease without a direct test. Their product pages describe intended workflows, not your applicants' experience. Ask support to show the process for a name mismatch or an income source outside payroll.

## Which syndicates listings?

RentSpree offers syndication, while SmartMove's reviewed page focuses on screening. RentSpree's listing page names Zillow, Redfin, Apartments.com, Zumper, and Realtor.com as distribution partners.

That makes RentSpree worth testing if you also need a place to collect applications. It does not prove that every listing will appear on every partner. Verify your property address and account settings.

A landlord with an existing leasing system may prefer to keep advertising there. In that case, compare screening links and report handling instead of moving the entire listing process.

Check who owns the application record when an agent posts the rental. The owner, agent, and manager need clear access rules. Do not share a report through an open email chain just to make the handoff easier.

## Which integrates with property management software?

Integration fit depends on your exact property management system and workflow. The reviewed pages do not establish universal, bidirectional connections for either vendor. Ask your PMS provider to confirm the supported route.

Test whether you send a screening invitation from the tenant record. Check whether completed reports, status, and decision notes return. An application link alone is not a full integration.

Keep final decisions with trained staff. The FTC says adverse action based partly on a consumer report requires a notice. This can include a denial or a report-based change in rental terms.

Ask who generates the notice and stores proof of delivery. Review the template with counsel. This is general information, not legal advice. Check the current law or ask a local attorney.

## How should you test the choice before you pay?

Test the full workflow with sample records before you move live data. Use the same case in each product. Ask the person who will do the daily work to run the test. A sales demo alone does not show where your team will need help.

Keep a decision sheet with the task, result, missing step, and person who checked it. Mark each result as shown, promised, or still open. Ask for written terms when a feature needs an upgrade or another service. Include staff setup time in your cost review.

Check the exit path while you still control the choice. Export the sample records and open them outside the platform. Confirm that notes, files, dates, and links remain useful. Ask how you will get records after you close the account.

Choose a tool when the test proves a clear gain for your workflow. Keep your current process until the new process passes. Use the [property management fee calculator](/tools/property-management-fee-calculator) to see how software costs affect your service margin.
