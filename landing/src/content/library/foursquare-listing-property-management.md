## How much does ChatGPT use Foursquare?

Nobody outside OpenAI knows how much ChatGPT uses Foursquare. The popular figure says 60–70% of ChatGPT local results come "straight from Foursquare". BrightLocal repeats it in a July 22, 2025 blog post, but it credits unnamed "reports" and gives no method.

BrightLocal's own study gives a different picture. The team ran 800 manual local searches in ChatGPT in November 2024 and recorded the first 10 sources of each. Business websites made up 58% of sources, mentions on other sites 27%, and directories 15%. Foursquare was only in the long tail of directories.

Our own data points the same way. In our [AI Property Manager Index](/research/ai-property-manager-index-2026), we asked ChatGPT, Claude and Gemini for the best property manager in the 50 largest US cities, with web search on. That gave 150 answers on 2026-10-19. Not one answer cited foursquare.com as a source (our data, 2026-10).

A missing link does not prove that Foursquare data is unused. A model can use a data feed without a visible link. But a property manager has limited hours, and the visible sources are a better guide to where those hours go.

## Why do Foursquare and Yelp studies conflict?

Foursquare and Yelp studies conflict because they measure different things at different times. Some count visible links. Some guess at hidden data feeds. Some test restaurants, others test dentists, and few test property managers.

| Study | Date | What it measured | Foursquare result | Yelp result |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| BrightLocal research, 800 ChatGPT searches | Data from November 2024 | First 10 visible sources per answer | Minor directory | Not a source |
| BrightLocal blog | 2025-07-22 | Quotes unnamed reports | "60% and 70%" of ChatGPT local results | Yelp in 33% of BrightLocal's searches across AI tools |
| citata.ai, 50 city prompts | 2026-10-19 | Visible sources, property managers only | 0 of 150 answers | 50 of 150 answers, all from Claude |

Three reasons explain most of the gap:

1. **Visible vs hidden.** A link count cannot see a data feed. A data-feed claim cannot be checked from outside.
2. **Industry.** Restaurants and shops have rich Foursquare data from check-ins. Property management offices get few check-ins.
3. **Engine and date.** Each engine picks sources in its own way, and each one changes often. In our data, Yelp showed up in all 50 Claude answers and in 0 ChatGPT and 0 Gemini answers.

There is one more fact to know. Foursquare closed its consumer City Guide app on December 15, 2024, and the web version on April 28, 2025. Listings stay visible in the Swarm app, and Foursquare says it sends listings to its data partners. So any claim about "Foursquare city guide listings" in AI answers now refers to the data feed, not a consumer app.

## How do you claim a Foursquare place?

You claim a Foursquare place from the Foursquare business listings portal. The steps below follow the flow that Synup describes (page updated September 10, 2026). Foursquare can change the flow, so check each screen.

1. **Make a Foursquare account.** Use a company email that your team controls, not a personal one.
2. **Search for your business.** Use the name and city. Check for old names and old addresses too.
3. **Claim the match.** If the place exists, select it and start the claim.
4. **Verify by phone.** Foursquare calls the listed number. Make sure the listed number reaches a person who can answer.
5. **Create a listing if none exists.** Use the "Create a listing" link in the search results and fill in the form.
6. **Edit the fields.** Use the table in the next section.
7. **Record the claim.** Write the login, the date and the listing URL in your listings sheet.

If another person already claimed the place, you cannot claim it again from the form. Contact Foursquare support and show proof that you run the business.

## Which Foursquare fields matter for a property manager?

The fields that matter most are the ones AI tools repeat: name, address, phone, website and category. They must match your Google Business Profile word for word. A small mismatch, such as "Suite 200" on one site and "#200" on another, is fine. A different phone number is not.

| Field | What to enter | Common mistake |
|---|---|---|
| Name | The brand name on your sign and website | Adding city or keywords to the name |
| Address | The same office address as Google Business Profile | An old office after a move |
| Phone | Your main local number | A call-tracking number that changes |
| Website | Your home page or the city page | A dead link from an old site |
| Category | Property management company | "Real estate agency" only |
| Hours | Office hours | Blank hours |
| Description | 1–2 sentences: service, areas, rental types | Sales slogans with no facts |

Foursquare's Places product page says its data covers 100M+ places in 200+ countries and lists Yahoo, Redfin and Nextdoor among its brands. That reach is the main reason to keep the record correct. A wrong phone in a data feed can spread to apps you never see.

## Where should Foursquare sit in your listing work?

Foursquare should sit near the end of your listing work. Spend your first hours where AI answers already look. In our 150 answers about property managers, the most cited listing sites were these (our data, 2026-10):

| Listing site | Answers that cited it (of 150) | Priority |
|---|---|---|
| Expertise.com | 93 | 1 |
| iPropertyManagement | 50 | 2 |
| Yelp | 50 | 2 |
| Three Best Rated | 21 | 3 |
| BBB | 14 | 3 |
| Thumbtack | 7 | 4 |
| Foursquare | 0 | 5 |

Our run tested one prompt type in one week, so treat the order as a hypothesis, not a rule. ## What should you do this week?

Claim Foursquare in one sitting after your main listings are correct. Use this short checklist:

- [ ] Google Business Profile, Yelp, Expertise.com and iPropertyManagement facts are correct first.
- [ ] Foursquare place found or created.
- [ ] Phone verification done with a number a person answers.
- [ ] Name, address, phone and website match Google Business Profile.
- [ ] Category set to property management.
- [ ] Login and listing URL saved in your listings sheet.
- [ ] One check every 6 months, or after any move or phone change.

The whole job is short. It will not move AI answers on its own. It removes one more place where a wrong fact can live.
