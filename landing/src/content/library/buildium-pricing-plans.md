## What are Buildium's plan names and prices?

Buildium lists Essential, Growth and Premium with starting monthly prices. Compare the stated feature access before comparing the bill.

| Plan | Published starting price | Highlighted work | Important cost condition |
|---|---|---|---|
| Essential | $62 monthly | Basic accounting, maintenance, leasing and AI help | Usage fees still apply |
| Growth | $192 monthly | More reporting, communications and analytics | New customers need onboarding |
| Premium | $400 monthly | Open API and broader automation | New customers need onboarding |

Source: [Buildium pricing](https://www.buildium.com/pricing/), read 2026-10-19. Buildium presents each amount as a starting price.

The page's plan highlights help define a short list. They do not establish a size-specific quote. Ask sales to attach your unit count and billing term to the chosen plan.

The right plan supports your actual tasks. Start with accounting and payments, then leasing and staff access. A broader plan can cost less overall if it removes enough paid work elsewhere.

## How does Buildium price above the entry unit range?

Buildium's public pricing page does not provide a complete static unit-by-unit rate schedule. Ask for quotes at the sizes you need.

Do not assume the entry price continues unchanged when your portfolio grows. Also do not assume a third-party table has the current unit bands.

Give sales your current managed units and expected changes. Ask how vacant homes, inactive records and association portfolios count. Separate your present bill from a growth forecast.

Request a written answer to these questions:

- **Count:** which records create billable units?
- **Timing:** when do added or removed units change the bill?
- **Plan:** which features require an upgrade?
- **Term:** what commitment supports the quoted price?
- **Renewal:** how can the rate or minimum change?

Keep the quote and a sample invoice together. Your bookkeeper should be able to recreate the calculation.

If you acquire another portfolio, ask about setup and data work as well as subscription growth. More doors can create conversion costs outside the recurring plan.

## Are there onboarding or setup fees?

Buildium requires customized onboarding for all new Growth and Premium customers, but does not publish its price on the pricing page. Treat that amount as quote only.

Buildium's [onboarding page](https://www.buildium.com/features/onboarding/) describes importing property, unit, lease, owner, resident and vendor data. It also describes a tailored training plan.

Ask what the import excludes. Signed documents, detailed transaction history and message records need separate confirmation. A list of entity types does not prove that every related record transfers.

Bank setup also matters. The pricing page lists $99 per business bank account on Essential. Growth includes 10 free accounts, while Premium includes 100; additional accounts cost $99.

Those figures come from Buildium's pricing page, read 2026-10-19. Confirm which bank accounts your actual setup needs. Do not budget only for the management company's operating account.

Ask for an itemized first invoice before you sign. Keep your internal cleanup and training time separate from vendor fees.

## Which features need a higher tier?

Buildium highlights more communications, reporting and analytics in Growth, with Open API and the broader automation suite in Premium. Match the plan to a required task.

An API is a software connection. If another tool needs to read or write Buildium data, test that action before choosing Premium. The plan label does not prove that every connection is included.

Support also differs. Buildium says ticket support is available to all customers. Live phone support comes with Growth and Premium. Ask what response process applies to an urgent payment problem.

Signatures differ in cost. Buildium lists $5 per document on Essential, $1 on Growth and included signatures on Premium. Count the documents your team sends.

Do not choose a plan from a single feature. An included inspection tool may replace another subscription, but only if your staff can use it successfully.

Run the same leasing and accounting case in the candidate tiers. Record where the cheaper plan requires added work.

## How do payment fees change the plan comparison?

Payment activity can change the total bill enough to matter more than the base-price gap. Price the methods your residents actually use.

| Incoming bank transfer | Published fee |
|---|---|
| Essential | $2.35 per transaction |
| Growth | $1.35 per transaction |
| Premium | Free for the first 12 months, then $0.60 |

Source: Buildium pricing, read 2026-10-19. Outgoing bank transfers are listed at $0.99 and cards at 2.99% across plans.

A worked example assumes 100 incoming transfers in a month. The published rates produce $235 on Essential and $135 on Growth. Premium produces $60 after its introductory period. These are fee calculations, not total subscriptions.

Do not assume your company pays every fee. Ask who can pay each charge and how the resident sees it. Keep company costs separate from resident costs.

Screening also adds usage costs. Buildium lists standard screening at $17 and enhanced screening at $20 on Growth and Premium. Applicant-paid screening is listed at $35.
