## What fee range do short-term rental managers charge?

Short-term rental management fees vary by service. A published plan is evidence of that provider's price, not a market average.

Evolve lists Core at 10%, Plus at 15%, and Pro at a custom rate on its owner page. We read the page on October 20, 2026. Those plans provide a useful public comparison. They do not prove what a full local service should cost in your town.

Vacasa's fee page, read October 20, 2026, describes a tailored management rate and invites owners to request a quote. It does not publish one fixed rate for every property. Compare that quote with Evolve only after matching the included local work.

Ask each manager to define the amount that the fee applies to. A percentage of nightly rent differs from a percentage of all guest charges. Refunds, pet charges, cleaning fees, and taxes can change the result. Evolve's owner FAQ says its fee includes base rates and additional fees, excluding taxes.

| Cost item | What to check in the offer |
|---|---|
| Management fee | Rate, fee base, and payment date |
| Local support | Who visits the home and who pays |
| Cleaning | Guest charge, cleaner payment, and manager fee |
| Supplies | Purchase cost, delivery, and any added charge |
| Setup | Photos, listing work, locks, and owner training |
| Exit | Notice, future bookings, and record transfer |

Do not compare a remote booking service with full local management as if they do the same work. Write the missing tasks beside each offer. Price those tasks before you judge the headline fee.

## What is included in a vacation rental management fee?

The agreement should list each included service and each task you must arrange yourself.

Evolve's plans page lists photos, listing distribution, pricing tools, guest support, and access to local support contacts. Access to a contact network does not answer who pays a cleaner. Read the agreement and the owner's duties together.

Ask who handles a failed lock, an unsafe stair, and an upset guest. Each problem needs an actual person with clear authority. A guest message service cannot repair a broken pipe. A local cleaner may not have authority to approve a refund.

Use a responsibility list during your sales call. Put the manager, owner, cleaner, and repair vendor in separate columns. Assign each task to a named role. Ask the manager to include the agreed list in the contract.

Check limits on owner decisions as well. Ask whether you can block dates or reject a proposed price. Find out who owns the photos and listing records after termination. Request a sample owner statement that shows charges at the booking level.

Insurance benefits also need their own review. A plan's protection offer is not a reason to cancel your landlord policy. Send the actual terms to your insurance agent. Describe the guest use and local services in plain terms.

## How do cleaning and supplies change the real cost?

Cleaning and supplies change your net income even when guests pay a separate cleaning charge.

Evolve's FAQ explains that its percentage applies to additional fees, including cleaning fees. That detail matters when you set the guest charge. A cleaning fee can fail to cover the cleaner after management charges apply.

Use an illustrative booking, not a forecast. Suppose rent is $1,000 and the cleaning charge is $200. At an assumed 15% fee on both amounts, management costs $180. If the cleaner receives $200, the owner keeps $820 before other costs. These are planning assumptions, not quoted local prices.

Now list the costs outside that example. Include booking platform charges, laundry, restocked goods, minor damage, utilities, and repair visits. Check whether the manager adds a charge to vendor invoices. Ask for the rule in writing.

Set a supply standard before the first stay. List the items, brands where needed, and reorder authority. Ask for receipts and a clear record of stock purchases. This reduces disputes about goods that never reached the house.

Compare the guest cleaning charge with the actual cleaning scope. A quick surface clean differs from linen service and damage checks. Ask who records defects after departure. Request photos that link the defect to the relevant stay, where practical and lawful.

## How does short-term management compare with long-term management?

Compare annual net cash flow and owner work, because the rental models have different costs and tasks.

A short-term home may need guest messages, repeated cleaning, utility service, and furnished rooms. A long-term lease may shift some daily household tasks to the tenant. The exact duties still depend on the lease and management agreement.

Build separate budgets with the same property costs. Keep debt payments, property taxes, insurance, and major repair reserves visible in both. Do not count collected lodging taxes as owner earnings. Do not treat guest cleaning payments as profit before you pay related costs.

Use the [property management fee calculator](/tools/property-management-fee-calculator) to test fee assumptions. Add vacation rental costs outside the calculator if the model does not include them. A tool helps compare inputs. It does not replace actual manager quotes or local booking records.

IRS Publication 527 lists management fees among common rental expenses. That tax treatment does not make an unnecessary service free. Keep invoices and ask your tax professional how each charge applies to your rental activity.

Request a full sample year, including a weak booking season. Ask what work remains with you during empty periods. A lower commission can still leave you with more calls and vendor work.

Before signing, send each finalist the same property facts and service list. Ask for a written annual cost estimate with exclusions. Choose the offer whose work, records, and total cost you can explain without sales terms.

This is general information, not tax advice. Check the current law or ask a tax professional.
