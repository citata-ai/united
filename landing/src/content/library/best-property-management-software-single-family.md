## Which software should single-family managers compare?

Compare the price conditions and work model first. Single-family support is a starting point, not proof of a complete fit.

| Product | Public price basis | Minimum or setup condition | Single-family test |
|---|---|---|---|
| Propertyware Basic | $1 per unit monthly | $250 monthly minimum; implementation extra | Scattered-site operations and reporting |
| Rentvine | As low as $1.50 per active unit | Exact floor and setup quote only | Property records and trust accounting |
| AppFolio Core | From $1.49 per unit monthly | 50 units plus minimum spend | Leasing, work orders and inspections |

Sources: [Propertyware](https://www.propertyware.com/pricing/), [Rentvine](https://www.rentvine.com/pricing) and [AppFolio](https://www.appfolio.com/pricing), read 2026-10-19.

These are published price conditions. They exclude a complete payment and service budget. Ask each vendor to quote the same door count and work.

A home portfolio can have many owner agreements and vendor arrangements. Bring that variety to the demo. A clean sample building does not show how scattered homes will work.

## Which tools were built for scattered-site homes?

Propertyware explicitly positions its platform for single-family management companies. Rentvine and AppFolio also describe support for single-family rentals, but do not assume identical design.

[Propertyware's home page](https://www.propertyware.com/) describes customization, reporting, open data access and multi-location management. Use those claims as tasks to demonstrate.

[Rentvine's properties page](https://www.rentvine.com/properties) describes property fields, appliance records, owner statements and grouping. Its supported classes include long-term single-family rentals.

AppFolio's pricing page names single-family within its residential portfolio description. Core lists inspections, work orders and leasing.

Ask how each product connects a person, address, lease and owner. Then test an owner with several homes and a home that changes owners. Check that historical records keep their correct context.

Avoid choosing solely on a vendor's stated target market. The useful evidence is your staff completing a representative home-management case.

## How do Propertyware, Rentvine and AppFolio compare?

Propertyware emphasizes customization, Rentvine emphasizes accounting and property detail, and AppFolio offers plan-based residential operations. Those are vendor descriptions, not measured quality rankings.

For Propertyware, test the required custom field and report. Ask which work needs configuration help or an additional service. A configurable system still needs someone to define the process.

For Rentvine, test separate property and combined owner reporting. Check how maintenance and financial records link. Ask whether the owner can understand a statement without staff explanation.

For AppFolio, ask which plan contains the required connection and reporting function. Its pricing page distinguishes integration and API access by tier. An API is a software connection.

Give every vendor the same acceptance case. Include a rent payment, repair bill, management fee, owner reserve and distribution. Follow the records through a statement and export.

The best fit is the product whose result your team can explain and repeat. A longer feature list is not enough.

## Which tools handle maintenance across many addresses?

Each candidate describes maintenance functions, but your test should focus on address accuracy and ownership of the next action. A generic work-order checkbox is insufficient.

Use a repair request from a tenant with several attachments. Ask staff to locate the property, appliance record and access instructions. Then assign the approved vendor.

Check what the vendor sees. Private tenant or owner information should not appear without a work reason. Confirm that staff can track acknowledgement and progress.

Follow the job through completion:

- **Receive:** request links to the correct tenant and address.
- **Clarify:** staff can ask for photos or details.
- **Approve:** spending follows your internal approval process.
- **Assign:** vendor receives the right scope and access details.
- **Complete:** evidence and invoice remain attached.
- **Report:** the charge appears on the right owner record.

Test a vendor who works at several properties. Make sure a reply cannot attach to the wrong home.

Ask how urgent cases reach a person. Do not let an automation demo replace your actual emergency response plan.

## Which has the best leasing and showing integrations?

The best integration moves the records your team needs without duplicate entry. Verify the specific partner, plan and transfer direction before buying.

Ask each vendor to show a real listing moving to the showing tool. Change the availability and check whether the other system updates. Then follow a prospect through scheduling and application.

Confirm what remains outside the property system. A connection may transfer a contact but omit messages, tour results or documents. Record those gaps.

Use this connection test:

| Action | Evidence |
|---|---|
| Publish listing | Correct address, rent and availability |
| Receive inquiry | Matching prospect without duplication |
| Book showing | Correct calendar and access rules |
| Update status | Change reaches the required system |
| Recover failure | Staff see and can retry the failed action |

A partner logo does not show that all of these actions work. Ask who supports the connection when records stop syncing.

Choose only the integrations you need now. Extra subscriptions can add cost without reducing work.

## What does each cost per home?

Per-home cost equals the complete bill divided by managed homes, not just the displayed unit rate. Minimums matter most before your portfolio grows.

Propertyware Basic's $250 monthly floor produces $2.50 per home in a worked 100-home example. Its implementation fee is twice the monthly subscription, according to Propertyware's pricing page.

That calculation does not include transactions or add-ons. Propertyware also says billable units adjust in 50-unit increments. Ask how that affects your count.

AppFolio's starting rate remains subject to its unpublished spending floor. Rentvine's as-low-as rate also needs confirmation for your account. Request complete offers rather than estimating missing minimums.
