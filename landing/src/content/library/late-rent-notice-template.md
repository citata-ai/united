## When is rent legally late?

Rent is late when the due date in the lease passes and the tenant has not paid. A late fee, though, often waits for a grace period set by the lease or the law. Your notice must not charge a fee before that period ends.

Two examples of state grace rules:

| State | When a late fee can apply | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Texas | Rent unpaid 2 full days after the due date, and the fee is in a written lease | Property Code 92.019 |
| New York | Rent unpaid within 5 days of the due date | Real Property Law 238-A |

So in Texas, rent due on the 1st can carry a late fee on the 4th. In New York, rent due on the 1st can carry a fee on the 7th. Many leases give more days than the law. Follow the longer of the two.

This is general information, not legal advice. Check the current law or ask a local attorney.

## How much can you charge as a late fee?

You can charge the fee in the lease, up to the state limit. Some states set a hard cap. Some set a test of "reasonable".

| State | Limit | Penalty if you break it |
|---|---|---|
| New York | $50 or 5% of monthly rent, whichever is less | A lease clause that waives it is void (RPL 238-A) |
| Texas | Presumed reasonable up to 12% of monthly rent (4 or fewer units) or 10% (more than 4 units) | $100 + 3 times the fee + attorney's fees (PC 92.019) |

Worked example for a $1,600 rent:

| Place | Calculation | Highest fee |
|---|---|---|
| New York | Lesser of $50 or 5% × $1,600 = $80 | $50 |
| Texas, duplex | 12% × $1,600 | $192 |
| Texas, 20-unit building | 10% × $1,600 | $160 |

Texas also lets a higher fee stand if it does not exceed the landlord's real damages from the late payment. Do not rely on that without legal advice. Stay under the presumed amount.

## Copy-ready late rent notice template

Copy the notice below and fill in each bracket. Send it the day after the grace period ends.

> [Date]
>
> [Tenant full name(s)]
> [Property address, unit]
>
> **Notice of late rent**
>
> Dear [Tenant name],
>
> Our records show that rent for [month] is unpaid. Rent was due on [due date].
>
> - Rent due: $[amount]
> - Late fee under your lease, section [number]: $[amount]
> - Other unpaid charges: $[amount, or "none"]
> - Total now due: $[amount]
>
> Please pay the total by [date] through [portal link / payment method].
>
> If you already paid, please reply with the date and method, and we will check our records.
>
> If you cannot pay in full, please call us at [phone] by [date]. We can discuss your options.
>
> This notice is a reminder. It is not a notice to quit.
>
> Sincerely,
> [Name, title]
> [Company name]
> [Phone, email]
>
> Delivered by: [portal message / email / mail / in person] on [date].

This is a generic template, not a state legal form. Check your lease and local law before you use it.

Send the same notice, on the same day count, to every tenant who pays late. The Fair Housing Act, 42 U.S.C. 3604(b), forbids different rental terms based on a protected class. A fixed routine is the simplest way to treat each tenant the same.

## Late rent notice vs pay or quit notice?

A late rent notice is a reminder. A pay or quit notice is a legal notice. The second starts the eviction path, so its format and timing are set by state law.

| | Late rent notice | Pay or quit notice |
|---|---|---|
| Purpose | Remind and collect | Formal demand before eviction |
| Required by law? | Usually no | Yes, before an eviction for unpaid rent |
| Format | Your template | Strict state rules |
| Can include late fees? | Yes, if the lease allows | Check the state rule on what it may list |
| Next step if unpaid | Call, then pay or quit notice | Court filing |

California is a clear example of strict rules. Code of Civil Procedure 1161 requires a written 3-day notice that does not count Saturdays, Sundays or judicial holidays. The notice must state the amount due. It must give the name, phone number and address of the person to pay. It must also give the payment method.

Do not mix the two notices. A late notice that says "pay or leave" can confuse the tenant and may not meet the legal format. If rent stays unpaid, send the state's formal notice next, or ask an attorney to send it.

This is general information, not legal advice. Check the current law or ask a local attorney.

## How do you record the notice on the tenant ledger?

Record each step on the tenant ledger and in the tenant file on the day it happens. A clean ledger is your best evidence if the case reaches court.

5-step ledger routine:

1. **Post the rent charge** on the due date for the full monthly rent.
2. **Post the late fee** as its own line on the first day the law and the lease allow it. Never fold it into the rent line.
3. **Attach the notice** to the tenant file with the date and delivery method.
4. **Post each payment** on the date received. Note what it pays: rent or fee. Follow the payment order in your lease.
5. **Note each contact** (call, text, payment plan) with the date and the result.

Example ledger for a Texas duplex, rent $1,600 due on the 1st:

| Date | Entry | Charge | Payment | Balance |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Oct 1 | October rent | $1,600 | | $1,600 |
| Oct 4 | Late fee (lease section 6) | $75 | | $1,675 |
| Oct 4 | Late notice sent by portal | | | $1,675 |
| Oct 8 | Payment, ACH | | $1,675 | $0 |
