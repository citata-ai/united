## How long is the software contract term?

The contract term is the commitment period, which can differ from the billing schedule. LeadSimple's pricing page, for example, describes an annual commitment that still bills monthly. Do not assume a monthly invoice means you can leave without further cost.

Read the order form beside every linked agreement. Rentvine's Master Subscription Terms say the agreement includes the order form and incorporated terms. They also explain how conflicts between documents are handled. That makes the linked terms part of the review.

Create a short record before you sign:

| Item | What to record |
|---|---|
| Start | Date service and billing begin |
| Term | Initial commitment and minimum spend |
| Renewal | Renewal date and notice process |
| Change | Who can change price or service scope |
| Exit | Last access date and export conditions |

Use the actual signed documents as the source. A verbal description of “flexible billing” is not enough.

## Can the vendor raise prices mid-term?

Price-change rights depend on the agreement, so ask for the exact rule rather than a general sales assurance. Separate base subscription changes from payment, messaging and third-party service charges.

Ask which rates stay fixed during the initial term. Confirm how the vendor gives notice, when a change takes effect and whether your company can decline it. Request the same answers for renewal.

Read service-change rights too. A stable dollar price can still buy a different scope if a required feature moves or disappears. Rentvine's terms discuss changes to services and terms. Use that as a prompt to review the specific protection in your own agreement.

Save the fee schedule with the order form. Ask how a later online version affects the signed account. Have counsel resolve unclear priority between documents before payment or migration makes leaving harder.

## Who owns your data and how do you get it out?

Data ownership does not automatically guarantee continuing access, useful export formats or free assistance. Review those questions separately. Test an export before signing and repeat the test before cancellation.

Rentvine's reviewed terms describe possible account deactivation at termination and conditional retrieval access. They also allow charges for retrieval assistance. That is a vendor contract example, not a rule for every platform.

Ask for structured ledgers, original documents, photos, messages and audit records. Confirm which items export directly and which need a service request. An owner list is not a full business archive.

Ask how payment details and authorizations are handled. Some records may require a new process rather than direct transfer. Do not assume bank credentials will move with a contact file.

Assign an internal person to approve the archive. Store records under company control before access ends, and verify that staff can open the files without the old subscription.

## Are there payment-processing fee markups?

Payment charges need an itemized schedule that names the payer and the basis. A subscription quote alone does not show the whole cost of collecting rent or paying owners and vendors.

List bank transfers, card payments, returned payments, chargebacks, outgoing payments and account verification. Ask which charges come from the vendor and which come from another provider. Do not claim a markup exists without evidence.

Review optional fee settings separately. DoorLoop's pricing page describes convenience-fee features and payment charges. A feature that allows a fee does not establish that your lease, management agreement or local law permits it.

Ask for a sample renter receipt and business statement. Trace a payment from charge through deposit. Check whether the total matches the quoted schedule. Confirm who handles disputes and how a reversed payment affects the ledger.

## What are the cancellation terms?

Cancellation terms should state the notice method, effective date, remaining charges and last access date. Do not assume deleting a user, stopping payment or removing units cancels the contract.

Ask where cancellation must be sent and what counts as receipt. Keep written confirmation from the vendor. Record whether prepaid amounts receive any refund and whether a minimum commitment remains due.

Coordinate the exit with exports and pending money. Rent collection, returned payments, owner payouts and vendor invoices may continue around the change. Ask how those records remain available after cancellation.

Do not schedule the last export after the last access date. Leave time to fix missing documents or mismatched balances. Ask whether read-only access is available and whether it has a separate charge. Treat an undocumented retrieval promise as unresolved.

## What should be resolved before signing?

Resolve essential promises in the documents that govern the purchase. Do not label a contract unacceptable merely because it is standard. Focus on the terms that affect your company's work and exit.

- **Scope:** name the modules and current features you require.
- **Cost:** attach a full fee schedule and discount conditions.
- **Support:** state contact paths and any agreed service levels.
- **Migration:** list imported data, documents and review duties.
- **Access:** define staff roles and connected-service permissions.
- **Exit:** confirm notice, retrieval formats and assistance charges.
- **Risk:** review liability, dispute rules and data use with counsel.

Have the accountant review payment and record terms. Have the operating lead review service boundaries. Keep the approved version of every linked document with the signed order form.

This is general information, not legal advice. Check the current law or ask a local attorney.
