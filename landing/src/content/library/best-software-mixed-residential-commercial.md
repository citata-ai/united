## Which tools support commercial leases and CAM?

Start with platforms that document commercial workflows, then test the residential work beside them. Mixed-property branding alone is not enough.

| Platform | Vendor-described fit | Price treatment | Main test |
|---|---|---|---|
| Yardi Breeze | Residential and commercial operations; commercial recovery tools | Commercial page: $2 per unit monthly, $200 minimum, annual agreement | Get a quote for the exact mix |
| Yardi Voyager | Commercial lease administration and mixed portfolio tools | Quote only | Entity structure and complex leases |
| AppFolio | Commercial operations with CAM tracking | Commercial quote only | Commercial charges beside rental records |
| Buildium | Markets rental, commercial, and association work | Mixed-portfolio quote needed | Required commercial lease details |

Sources: the vendors' product pages, read on 2026-10-19. Ask each vendor to confirm mixed-portfolio pricing in writing.

CAM means common area maintenance. A commercial lease can set rules for shared expense recovery that differ from ordinary residential rent. Test those rules as part of the required workflow.

Use a commercial tenant with an approved lease exception. A product should show how the exception changes the result, not merely store the lease as an attachment.

## Which handle both in one ledger?

Ask each vendor to demonstrate its proposed accounting structure rather than relying on the phrase one ledger. Shared access can coexist with separate property and entity records.

Yardi's site describes mixed portfolios in a shared database. Buildium markets property-specific accounting across property types. These claims justify a closer review, not an assumption that your chart of accounts will fit unchanged.

Map your ownership entities, banks, properties, and reporting groups before the demo. Ask the accountant which separations the records need. Then inspect the proposed setup.

A management dashboard and a financial ledger serve different purposes. Staff may need a combined view of open work while the accountant needs distinct entity reports.

Test a vendor bill shared across property types. Check the allocation, approval, posting, and report output. Confirm that a correction leaves a clear trail.

Ask how users move between portfolios. A commercial manager may need commercial records without access to unrelated residential data. The combined system should preserve that boundary.

Keep the accepted account map with the migration plan so setup staff do not guess later.

## What are the limits of residential tools?

A residential-focused tool may not represent every commercial lease rule or reporting requirement. Verify the exact gap before assuming a different platform is necessary.

Check scheduled increases, expense recovery, retail sales, critical dates, and billing contacts. Some tools may store these details without calculating or tracking the resulting task.

Ask the vendor to distinguish stored information from automated behavior. A note field saying review rent does not prove the system will post the correct future charge.

Use an exception that your staff currently tracks outside the software. Have the vendor show the starting record, the calculation, the approval, and the final report.

Review both normal and corrected transactions. A product that can create a charge should also support a clear correction process. Ask what remains manual after the change.

Do not reject a tool because it serves residential managers too. Reject it if the required commercial workflow fails or depends on an unsupported workaround.

Likewise, do not buy enterprise software for a simple commercial unit without checking the training and operating burden. Fit comes from the work, not the label.

## How do prices compare?

Compare written quotes for the same mix of properties and required modules. Separate residential and commercial list prices do not establish your combined contract cost.

Yardi Breeze publishes a commercial rate on its commercial page. The table records that reference with its annual agreement. Ask how the vendor bills your actual mixed account.

The reviewed AppFolio Commercial and Voyager Commercial pages use quote-based selling. Treat those prices as quote only until you receive a proposal. Do not multiply a residential rate by every door.

Give vendors the same inventory. Include units, properties, entities, staff, payment volume, required reports, and integrations. State which leases require special setup.

Ask for migration, training, support, processing, signing, and partner charges. Separate recurring subscription from one-time project work.

Compare the staff effort left after deployment. A cheaper platform may still need a manual commercial recovery process. Another may add overhead your team does not need.

## Should you run separate systems?

Use separate systems only when a material workflow gap outweighs the cost of maintaining the connection. A shared platform is useful when it preserves correct records for both property types.

Separate tools can serve a specialized commercial process while another system handles residential operations. The design needs a clear source for leases, charges, contacts, and reports.

Do not let each system become authoritative for the same financial field. Name the controlling record and define how a change reaches the other tool.

Use this decision checklist:

- **List the required work:** Include daily rental tasks and the hardest commercial lease.
- **Test shared records:** Check owners, entities, properties, and restricted access.
- **Review calculations:** Have the accountant inspect commercial charge and recovery output.
- **Name manual work:** Document tasks the product does not support directly.
- **Test a handoff:** If tools remain separate, move a sample charge and correction between them.
- **Check totals:** Confirm that exported and posted amounts match for the same period.
- **Compare full cost:** Include training, support, and continuing review work.

Plan for staff changes too. A process that only its designer can explain is hard to maintain. Keep a short field map and a correction guide.
