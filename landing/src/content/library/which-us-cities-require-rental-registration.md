## Which cities require a yearly rental license?

Most of the large cities we checked require rental owners to register or license each rental. We read each rule at the city's own site or code between 2026-10-19 and 2026-10-20. The table covers the cities we could verify.

| City | Who must register | Cost for one rental home | Term | Source |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| New York (HPD) | Buildings of 3+ units, and 1-2 unit homes the owner's family does not live in | $13 a year | Yearly, by September 1 | NYC HPD |
| Boston | Every rental unit, even vacant or family-occupied | $25 first time, $15 to renew, per unit; cap $2,500 per building | Yearly, by July 1; inspection every 5 years | Boston ISD |
| Baltimore | Every non-owner-occupied property; rentals also need a license | $30 per unit (1-2 units), $35 per unit (multifamily), plus a private inspection | Registration yearly; license 2 years | Baltimore DHCD, City Code Art. 13 |
| Cleveland | Every non-owner-occupied unit | $70 per unit; cap $30,000 per owner | Yearly; Lead Safe Certificate every 2 years for pre-1978 units | Cleveland B&H, Ch. 365 |
| Detroit | Every rental, except owner-occupied homes with rooms or one duplex unit rented | Registration free; inspection $195 (1 unit) or $225 (duplex), plus $20 | Certificate 3 years, then 5 years | City of Detroit |
| Dallas | Every rental: single-family, duplex, condo and 3+ unit properties | $74 per single unit; $13 per unit at 3+ unit properties | Yearly | Dallas City Code 27-31 |
| Kansas City, MO | Every rental property | $25 per unit, plus a one-time $25 application | Yearly, January to December | KCMO Health Department |
| Denver | Anyone renting for 30 days or more | $50 application + $50 license (1 unit), plus a private inspection | 4 years | Denver Excise and Licenses |
| Minneapolis | Every rental property | Tiered fees, set by the city | Per city rules | City of Minneapolis |
| Los Angeles | Units under the RSO or Just Cause Ordinance | Yearly registration fee, plus the Rent Registry | Yearly; rents due by the last day of February | LAHD |
| Oakland | Units under the Rent Adjustment or Just Cause ordinances | $137 per unit RAP fee; half may be charged to the tenant | Yearly | City of Oakland |
| Cook County (unincorporated) | Each unit in buildings of 4+ units | Set by county fee ordinance | Per county rules | Cook County Building and Zoning |

We could not verify current rules for Philadelphia, Washington DC, Seattle or Pittsburgh at an official source during this check, so they are not in the table.

## Which states let counties register rentals (Arizona, Ohio)?

Two states put registration at the county level for every rental owner.

**Ohio.** Ohio Revised Code 5323.02 says an owner of residential rental property must file with the county auditor: the owner's name, address and phone; a contact person for an entity owner; and the street address and parcel number. You must update it within 60 days of a change. In counties over 200,000 people, the auditor sends a notice, and you have 60 days to comply. Cities like Cleveland add their own registration on top.

**Arizona.** Arizona Revised Statutes 33-1902 requires the owner to keep name, contact data, parcel number and the year built on file with the county assessor. Out-of-state owners must name an Arizona statutory agent. The penalties have teeth:

- **Newly bought rentals:** a city or town must assess $1,000, plus $100 for each month until you comply.
- **Other rentals:** a city may impose up to $150 a day.
- **Tenants:** if the owner is not registered, a tenant may give a 10-day notice and then end the lease, with prepaid rent returned.

The statute lets the court dismiss the case if you comply within 10 days of the notice.

## Which states limit registration (North Carolina)?

North Carolina limits city registration by statute. N.C. G.S. 160D-1207(c) says a local government may not require an owner or manager to get a permit or register a rental, with 2 exceptions:

1. A property with more than 4 verified violations in a rolling 12 months, or 2 or more in 30 days.
2. A property in the top 10% for crime or disorder problems under a local ordinance.

Even then, the city may not charge a special rental fee over $500 in any 12 months. It may not make a registration violation a crime. And it may not require an inspection before giving city utility service.

So in Charlotte or Raleigh, most owners pay no rental registration fee at all.

## What does registration cost per unit?

Rental registration costs from nothing to $137 per unit a year in the cities we checked. To compare multi-year terms, we turned each into a yearly cost for one single-family rental:

| City | Cost per year, one home | How we got it |
|---|---|---|
| New York | $13 | $13 yearly fee |
| Boston | $15 | Renewal fee; first year $25 |
| Denver | $25 | $100 for a 4-year license; inspection extra |
| Kansas City, MO | $25 | Plus $25 once in year one |
| Baltimore | $30 | Plus a private inspection every 2 years |
| Cleveland | $70 | Plus a lead clearance every 2 years for pre-1978 units |
| Detroit | $71.67 | $215 for a 3-year certificate |
| Dallas | $74 | Since October 1, 2025 |
| Oakland | $137 ($68.50 net) | If paid on time, half may be charged to the tenant |

Put it next to rent. A $74 Dallas fee is 4.1% of one month at HUD's FY2027 Dallas two-bedroom Fair Market Rent of $1,807. The fees are small; the penalties and the lost court access are what cost money. Find any metro's rent on our [Section 8 rent limits page](/guides/section-8-rent-limits).

## What happens if I rent without a registration?

Renting without a registration brings fines, and in some cities it blocks your eviction case. From the official sources above:

| City or state | Penalty for no registration or license |
|---|---|
| New York | $500 to $1,500 (5 units or less) or $1,000 to $5,000 (over 5); no court case for nonpayment until registered |
| Boston | Up to $300 a month; fees owed back to 2013 |
| Baltimore | Misdemeanor, up to $1,000 per offense, each day separate; the rent court form asks for the license number |
| Cleveland | First-degree misdemeanor, each day a separate offense |
| Detroit | Tickets from $400, liens for unpaid tickets, tenant rent escrow |
| Dallas | Fine up to $2,000 per offense, each day separate; civil penalty up to $1,000 a day |
| Denver | $250 on the first citation with a housing code violation; fines from $150 to $5,000 |
| Arizona | $1,000 plus $100 a month (new owners) or up to $150 a day; tenant may end the lease |

A checklist before you buy or rent out a home in a new city:

- **Find the program.** Search the city site for "rental registration" or "rental license."
- **Check the county and state.** Ohio and Arizona add county filings; North Carolina limits city ones.
- **Note the deadline** and the term: yearly, 2, 3 or 4 years.
- **Book the inspection** if one is required, and check who may do it.
- **Calendar the renewal** and any lead certificate dates.
- **Ask your manager** who files and who pays. See our [property management page](/property-management) for help.
