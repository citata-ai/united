## How do PMW, Fourandhalf, Goodjuju and ClearLead compare on price?

The 4 vendors price in 4 different ways, so a straight price match is not possible. PMW sells monthly bundles tied to Rentvine. Fourandhalf sells monthly bundles plus a setup fee. Goodjuju sells a one-time build and separate monthly plans. ClearLead sells SEO and adds a site to its top plan.

All prices below come from each vendor's own page, read on 2026-10-19. They are list prices, not quotes.

| Vendor | Website | Monthly plans | Setup or build fee | Term on the page |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Property Manager Websites (PMW) | Smartsite included with Rentvine; custom site by quote | Grow $145, Premier $795, Elite $1,995; à la carte from $195 | Paid upfront, or split, or waived on a 3-year term | 1, 2 or 3 years by payment option |
| Fourandhalf | QuickSite from $1,499; custom site from $2,999 | Launch $349, Grow $699, Scale $1,199; à la carte from $199 | Launch $600, Grow $1,799, Scale $2,399 | Not stated on the page |
| Goodjuju Marketing | One-time WordPress build $1,686, $2,760, $9,270; Empire $14,800 by application | Hosting $126 or $340; SEO $1,185, $2,292, $4,460; Empire $7,930 | The build is the one-time fee | Month to month, 15 days' notice |
| ClearLead Digital | Up to 25 custom pages, only as an add-on to Market Authority | SEO $1,199, $1,699, $2,299 | No setup fee; website add-on +$300/mo in year 1 | Month to month; website add-on 12 months |

Read the fine print on each one:

- **PMW:** the pricing page says "Rentvine subscription required" for the bundles. Rentvine is a separate bill.
- **Fourandhalf:** the Grow and Scale bundles include a website with hosting. Launch does not.
- **Goodjuju:** active SEO clients get a discount on every website tier.
- **ClearLead:** the $300 add-on brings Market Authority to $2,599 a month in year 1, then back to $2,299.

## What does each vendor include?

Each vendor packs a different mix of website, content, reviews and ads into its plans. Here is what the entry and middle tiers list.

| Vendor | Entry tier includes | Middle tier adds |
|---|---|---|
| PMW Grow ($145) | Website and owner tools from a shared feature grid; ask PMW which items each tier gets | Premier ($795): 1 blog post a month (1,000 words); Elite ($1,995) lists 2 |
| Fourandhalf Launch ($349) | 1 blog post a month (1,000 words), review request platform, account manager | Grow ($699): a website with hosting, 1,500-word posts, review requests sent for you |
| Goodjuju Emerge SEO ($1,185) | On-site SEO, Google Business Profile work, review system, 50+ listings, CRM, 3 blogs a month | Takeover ($2,292): 6 blogs a month, PR links, Google Ads management ($750+ ad spend) |
| ClearLead Foundation ($1,199) | Technical SEO audit, service page work, basic local SEO, 25 keywords tracked | Growth Engine ($1,699): 2 articles and 2–3 backlinks a month, 75 keywords |

Count blog posts with care. PMW Elite lists 2 posts a month at $1,995. Goodjuju Emerge lists 3 at $1,185. But post length, research and local depth differ, and the pages do not show quality. Ask each vendor for 3 recent client posts and read them.

## Who owns the site if I leave?

Ownership differs, and only 3 of the 4 vendors state it on the pages we read.

| Vendor | What the page says | Watch for |
|---|---|---|
| Goodjuju | "You own your website and all assets outright once they're paid for" | Build is paid up front, so "paid" is clear |
| PMW | You own the HTML once live and paid; HTML download on request | Licensed stock images and graphics stay with PMW |
| ClearLead | Website add-on listed as "New PM website (owned)" | Built over a 12-month term; ask about early exit |
| Fourandhalf | Not stated on the pricing or website pages | Ask in writing before you sign |

Also check that your domain, Google Analytics and Search Console sit in your own company accounts. A vendor can promise the site and still hold the domain login.

## Which vendors integrate with my PM software?

All 4 vendors aim at property managers, but only 3 list software links on the pages we read.

| Vendor | Software named on the page |
|---|---|
| PMW | Rentvine, Propertyware, AppFolio, Buildium, Rent Manager, Tenant Turner, ShowMojo |
| Goodjuju | Buildium, Propertyware, AppFolio ("you name it") |
| Fourandhalf | "Compatible with major PM software platforms"; CRM and most major PM software |
| ClearLead | Not listed on the pricing page |

PMW's site also sells Rentvine software, and its bundles need a Rentvine subscription. If you run AppFolio or Buildium, ask PMW what you pay for a site without Rentvine. The PMW AppFolio page says its sites show AppFolio listings and send users to AppFolio portals, but the bundle prices still carry the Rentvine note.

## Which vendor fits which company?

Pick by what you already run and what you lack. This is a starting point, not a ranking.

1. **You run Rentvine and want a low monthly bill.** PMW Grow at $145 is the lowest listed entry price.
2. **You want a site you own outright, paid once.** Goodjuju sells one-time builds from $1,686 and says you own them once paid.
3. **You want reviews and content but keep your current site.** Fourandhalf Launch at $349 plus $600 setup fits that brief.
4. **You want SEO with no long contract.** ClearLead and Goodjuju both list month-to-month SEO from about $1,200.

Before you sign, ask each vendor 3 questions in writing. Who owns the domain? Which assets stay with the vendor? What do you owe if you leave in month 6?
