Hawaii changed its rent eviction rules on February 5, 2026. Act 278 of 2025 replaced the 5-business-day notice in HRS § 521-68 with a 10-calendar-day notice and a mediation step. Many guides online still show 5 days. I read the current text on the Hawaii State Legislature site on 2026-10-20.

| Rule | Limit or deadline | Statute |
|---|---|---|
| Notice for unpaid rent | At least 10 calendar days after receipt | § 521-68(a) |
| Copy of the rent notice | To a state-funded mediation center | § 521-68(c) |
| Earliest filing, no mediation scheduled | After the 10 days | § 521-68(b)(6) |
| Earliest filing, mediation scheduled in the 10 days | After 20 calendar days from receipt | § 521-68(c), (i) |
| Mediation date | Within 30 days after the center reaches both sides | § 521-68(c) |
| Notice for a rule breach | At least 10 days to cure; file within 30 days of a repeat | § 521-72 |
| Notice for a breach of tenant duties | At least 10 days to cure | § 521-69(a) |
| End a month-to-month tenancy (landlord) | At least 45 days, in writing | § 521-71(a) |
| Where to file | District court of the circuit where the unit is | § 666-6 |
| Who executes the writ | Sheriff, deputy, police officer or listed process server | § 666-11 |
| Penalty for an overnight lockout | 2 months' rent plus attorney fees | § 521-63(c) |
| Penalty for a utility shutoff to evict | At least 3 times monthly rent or $1,000 | § 521-74.5 |

## What notice must a Hawaii landlord give before filing an eviction for unpaid rent?

A Hawaii landlord must give a written notice that allows at least 10 calendar days after receipt to pay, or the rental agreement ends. That is HRS § 521-68(a) as in force from February 5, 2026.

The notice must include 8 items under § 521-68(b):

1. **Landlord or agent name and contact details.** Phone, e-mail and mailing address if possible.
2. **The unit address.**
3. **All tenants on the lease**, with contact details.
4. **The rent due** as of the notice date, after all payments from all sources.
5. **A statement** that a copy goes to a state-funded mediation center.
6. **A statement** that the landlord may file if rent is unpaid and mediation is not scheduled within 10 days.
7. **The bold warning** in the form the statute gives.
8. **A statement** that the landlord will mediate if mediation is scheduled.

The judiciary must publish a form for this notice on its website. Use it.

Delivery and receipt: posting in a conspicuous place counts as receipt on the posting date. A USPS letter counts as received 2 business days after the postmark, unless it comes back undeliverable.

Watch for: the law sunsets. The note to § 521-68 says the 2025 changes are repealed and reenacted on February 4, 2028, except the change to subsection (a). Check the text again before that date.

## How long does an eviction take in Hawaii?

Hawaii law does not set a total eviction time. For a rent case, the pre-filing stage alone now runs 10 to 20 calendar days or more:

| Path | Earliest filing date | Source |
|---|---|---|
| No mediation scheduled within 10 days | Day 11 after receipt | § 521-68(b)(6) |
| Mediation scheduled, tenant takes part, no agreement | After day 20 from receipt | § 521-68(i) |
| Mediation scheduled, tenant fails to attend or cancels | The 20-day wait does not apply | § 521-68(c) |
| Breach of a mediated agreement | No new 10-day notice needed | § 521-68(a) |

After filing, court time depends on the circuit's calendar. The complaint must show proof that the notice went to the mediation center, and the mediation date if one is set (§ 521-68(d)). If the notice has a defect that the court finds unintentional or immaterial, the court may allow a cure without dismissal (§ 521-68(e)).

The tenant can stop the writ in a rent case. Under HRS § 666-14, the writ is stayed if the tenant pays, before it issues, the rent due with 8% yearly interest, plus costs, expenses and a reasonable attorney fee.

## Where does a landlord file an eviction case in Hawaii, and what does it cost?

A Hawaii landlord files a summary possession case in the district court of the circuit where the unit is (HRS § 666-6). The landlord may join claims for rent and damages in the same case, whatever the amount (§ 666-7).

If rent is in dispute, either side may ask the court to order the tenant to pay the disputed rent into a court trust fund as it falls due (§ 666-21). If the tenant cannot pay as ordered, the landlord gets judgment for possession.

Costs: I could not open the current district court fee schedule, so this article gives no fee number. Ask the clerk of the district court. Mediation costs each side its own expenses, unless the tenant defaults on the mediated agreement or misses the session (§ 521-68(h)).

## Can a Hawaii landlord evict a tenant for a lease violation or with no cause?

A Hawaii landlord can evict for a lease violation after a cure notice of at least 10 days. Two sections apply:

| Breach | Notice | Statute |
|---|---|---|
| Material breach of tenant duties in § 521-51 | At least 10 days after receipt to fix it | § 521-69(a) |
| Breach of a house rule under § 521-52 | At least 10 days, in the statutory form; file within 30 days if it continues | § 521-72 |
| Breach that causes or threatens irremediable damage | No cure time required | § 521-69(a), § 521-72(a) |

Hawaii has no just-cause rule for periodic tenancies. A landlord can end a month-to-month tenancy with written notice at least 45 days before the termination date (§ 521-71(a)). For demolition, condo conversion or a change to vacation rentals, the notice is 120 days (§ 521-71(c)).

Limits apply. Under § 521-74, a landlord may not evict after a tenant's good-faith complaint to a health or building agency, or a good-faith repair request, while the tenant keeps paying rent. The section lists exceptions, such as waste, nuisance, or a good-faith move-in by the landlord's family.

## Who removes the tenant after a judgment in Hawaii?

A sheriff, deputy sheriff, police officer, or independent civil process server from the state list removes the tenant in Hawaii. HRS § 666-11 says the writ of possession commands that officer to remove all persons and put the landlord in full possession. Once a writ issues, the rental agreement and the landlord-tenant relationship are cancelled (§ 666-13).

## Is a self-help eviction (lockout, utility shutoff) legal in Hawaii?

Self-help eviction is illegal in Hawaii and carries set penalties:

- **Lockout.** If a landlord removes or excludes the tenant overnight without cause or court order, the tenant may recover possession or end the lease. In either case the tenant gets 2 months' rent or 2 months' free occupancy, plus the cost of suit and attorney fees (HRS § 521-63(c)).
- **Utility shutoff.** A landlord may not take possession by wilfully cutting water, hot water, power, gas or other essential service, except after abandonment or surrender. The penalty includes minimum damages of 3 times the monthly rent or $1,000, whichever is greater, on top of penalties under § 480-3.1 (§ 521-74.5).

## Hawaii eviction checklist

1. **Use the judiciary's 10-day notice form.**
2. **Send a copy** to a state-funded mediation center and keep proof.
3. **Count calendar days** from posting, or 2 business days after the postmark.
4. **Mediate** if the center schedules it.
5. **File in district court** with proof of the mediation notice.
6. **Get the writ** and coordinate with the officer who serves it.
