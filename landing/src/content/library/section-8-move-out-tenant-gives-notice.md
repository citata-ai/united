## Does the HAP stop on the move-out date?

The HAP stops at the end of the move-out month, not on the move-out date. 24 CFR 982.311(d)(1) says the PHA "may not make any housing assistance payment to the owner for any month after the month when the family moves out". In the same rule: "The owner may keep the housing assistance payment for the month when the family moves out."

So the date in the month matters less than the month itself. Here is how the rule plays out:

| Family moves out on | HAP for that month | HAP for next month |
|---|---|---|
| September 3 | You keep it | None |
| September 30 | You keep it | None |
| October 1 | October HAP, if paid, you keep it | None for November |

The HAP contract itself ends when the lease ends (24 CFR 982.309(b)). If the family moves to a new voucher unit, the new lease can start in the same month. The rule says that overlap is not a duplicate subsidy (982.311(d)(2)).

## Can I keep the HAP for the vacant month?

You can keep the HAP for the move-out month only. For the month after, the base voucher rule pays nothing. Two exceptions can help:

- **Local vacancy loss incentives.** Some PHAs pay part of the vacant time as a landlord incentive. Ask your PHA.
- **Project-based vouchers.** A PBV HAP contract may allow up to 2 more months of vacancy payments (24 CFR 983.352). That does not apply to a regular tenant-based voucher.

Watch for: the tenant's share for the move-out month is still owed under the lease. If the tenant leaves early without proper notice, the lease and state law decide what the tenant owes. The PHA does not cover the tenant's share.

## Must I tell the PHA?

The family must tell the PHA, and you should too. Under 24 CFR 982.309(c), the family must give the PHA a copy of any lease termination notice "at the same time" it gives it to you. It must also notify the PHA and you before it moves out. If it does not, that breaks the family's program obligations.

Send the PHA your own short notice as well. Include:

1. **Unit address and tenant name.**
2. **The move-out date** and how you confirmed it (keys returned, walk-through).
3. **A copy of the tenant's notice**, if you have one.
4. **Whether you want to re-rent to a new voucher holder.**

This stops HAP payments on time. Under 24 CFR 982.451(b)(3), you must return any excess payment to the PHA at once. A clear notice avoids an overpayment you later have to pay back.

## How do I handle the deposit?

Handle the deposit under the lease and state law, the same way as for any tenant. 24 CFR 982.313 sets the voucher rules:

- You may use the deposit, with any interest, for unpaid rent owed by the tenant, damage to the unit, or other amounts owed under the lease.
- You must give the tenant "a written list of all items charged against the security deposit, and the amount of each item".
- You must "refund promptly" the unused balance.
- If the deposit is not enough, you may seek the balance from the tenant.

Note the word "tenant". The deposit covers the tenant's share and tenant damage. It does not cover HAP the PHA did not pay.

Tacoma Housing Authority says it "does not perform move-out inspections" for units it does not own. Other PHAs may be the same. So do your own move-out inspection.

Worked example:

| Line | Amount |
|---|---|
| Security deposit held | $1,200 |
| Unpaid tenant share, final month | −$250 |
| Damage beyond normal wear: broken door | −$180 |
| Refund due to tenant | $770 |

Send the itemized list and the $770 within your state's deadline.

## Can a new voucher holder rent the unit fast?

Yes, but each new voucher tenancy starts from zero. A new family means a new Request for Tenancy Approval (RFTA), a new inspection, a new lease and a new HAP contract. The old HAP contract does not carry over.

Federal rules set some clocks under 24 CFR 982.305:

- **Inspection.** A PHA with up to 1,250 budgeted vouchers must inspect and notify you within 15 days after the RFTA. Larger PHAs must do so within a reasonable time, and within 15 days "to the extent practicable". The clock stops while the unit is not available.
- **HAP contract.** The PHA must use best efforts to sign it before the lease starts, and no later than 60 days after. It then pays back to the lease start, up to 60 days.

Some PHAs move faster. Tacoma Housing Authority says it tries to schedule the inspection within 5 business days of the RFTA.

Turnover checklist:

- [ ] Get the tenant's written notice and send a copy to the PHA.
- [ ] Book a pre-move-out walk-through with the tenant.
- [ ] Prep for inspection: alarms, windows, outlets, heat, hot water, paint.
- [ ] List the unit with "Housing Choice Vouchers accepted".
- [ ] Have your RFTA packet, W-9 and ownership proof ready.
- [ ] Do the move-out inspection with dated photos.
- [ ] Send the deposit itemization and refund on time.
- [ ] Confirm the final HAP and that no HAP arrives for later months.

Before you set the new rent, check it against the local payment standard in our [Section 8 rent limits guide](/guides/section-8-rent-limits). A rent the family cannot afford slows the next lease-up.
