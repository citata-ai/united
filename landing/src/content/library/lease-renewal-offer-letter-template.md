## How early should you send a renewal offer?

Send a lease renewal offer 90 to 120 days before the lease ends. Then check that date against the notice rule for any rent change in your state. The legal notice period sets the latest possible date. Your own timeline should start earlier.

Notice rules that change the send date:

| State | Rule for a rent change at renewal | Source |
|---|---|---|
| New York | Increase of 5% or more: 30 days (under 1 year), 60 days (1–2 years), 90 days (2+ years) | Real Property Law 226-C |
| Washington | Any increase: at least 90 days, in writing | Washington Attorney General |
| California (month-to-month) | 30 days if 10% or less over 12 months; 90 days if more | Civil Code 827 |

New York's rule also covers a decision not to renew. Real Property Law 226-C requires the same 30, 60 or 90 days of written notice if you will not renew.

A 120-day renewal timeline:

| Days before lease end | Step |
|---|---|
| 120 | Check market rent and the unit's condition. Ask the owner for a renewal decision. |
| 100 | Get the owner's written approval of the rent and terms. |
| 90–95 | Send the renewal offer. |
| 75 | First follow-up: call and message. |
| 60 | Second follow-up in writing. Note the reply deadline. |
| 45 | If no reply: act under the lease end clause. Prepare to list the unit. |
| 30 | Sign the renewal, or confirm the move-out date and schedule the inspection. |

This is general information, not legal advice. Check the current law or ask a local attorney.

## Should the renewal offer include a rent change?

Yes, if the rent will change. Show the current rent, the new rent and the dollar change for each option. A clear number gets a faster yes than a vague "rent will adjust".

Price the change with the cost of a move-out in mind. Here is a worked example for a $2,000 rent. The leasing fee uses the 70.6% average from iPropertyManagement's sample. The other numbers are an example.

| Option | Calculation | Owner result over the next year |
|---|---|---|
| Tenant renews at +3% | $2,060 × 12 | $24,720 rent |
| Tenant renews at +6% | $2,120 × 12 | $25,440 rent |
| Tenant leaves; 1 month empty, new tenant at +6% | $2,120 × 11 − 70.6% × $2,120 leasing fee | $23,320 − $1,497 = $21,823 |

In this example, a 3% renewal beats a 6% ask that drives the tenant out by $2,897. Run the numbers for your own unit before you choose the rent.

## What renewal options can you offer?

Offer 2 or 3 options so the tenant picks one instead of saying no. Common options:

1. **12-month renewal** at the base renewal rent.
2. **Longer term (18 or 24 months)** at the same rent or a small step-up in year 2.
3. **Month-to-month** at a higher rent, if local law allows month-to-month.

Keep the same options and pricing rules for every tenant in similar units. The Fair Housing Act, 42 U.S.C. 3604(b), forbids different rental terms based on a protected class.

## Copy-ready lease renewal offer letter template

Copy the letter and fill in each bracket. Delete options you do not offer.

> [Date]
>
> [Tenant full name(s)]
> [Property address, unit]
>
> **Lease renewal offer**
>
> Dear [Tenant name],
>
> Your lease ends on [lease end date]. We would like you to stay, and we are happy to offer a renewal.
>
> Your current rent is $[amount] per month. Please choose one option:
>
> | Option | Term | Monthly rent | Starts |
> |---|---|---|---|
> | A | 12 months | $[amount] | [date] |
> | B | [18 / 24] months | $[amount] | [date] |
> | C | Month-to-month | $[amount] | [date] |
>
> All other lease terms stay the same, except: [list changes, or "none"].
>
> To renew, sign the renewal in the portal or reply to this letter by [reply date].
>
> If you plan to move, please tell us in writing by [date], as your lease requires.
>
> Questions? Call [manager name] at [phone] or email [email].
>
> Thank you for being our resident.
>
> Sincerely,
> [Name, title]
> [Company name]
>
> Delivered by: [method] on [date].

This is a generic template, not a state legal form. Check your lease and local law before you use it.

## What if the tenant does not reply?

Follow up twice, then act on the lease. Silence is not a yes. Do not assume the tenant accepted a new rent they never signed.

Steps for a silent tenant:

- [ ] Call and send a short message 15 days after the offer.
- [ ] Send a written reminder 30 days after the offer, with the reply date.
- [ ] Read the lease end-of-term clause. Does the lease turn into month-to-month, or end?
- [ ] Check the state notice rules again before the end date. New York and Washington set fixed day counts.
- [ ] Tell the owner the status and the plan.
- [ ] If the tenant will leave, schedule the move-out inspection and start marketing.

This is general information, not legal advice. Check the current law or ask a local attorney.
