## What leasing and renewal fees do Denver managers charge?

Denver leasing fees are the most uniform of any market we checked: 9 of 11 managers charge 50% of one month's rent. The median is $1,084.50 at a $2,169 rent.

Minimums matter on cheaper homes. Keyrenter Denver sets a $980 floor, Rent Me Denver $850, Stars and Stripes $750 and Evernest $500. Northpoint charges 75%, and Denver Property Management Inc. a flat $1,300.

Renewal fees vary the most. Across the 8 firms that list one, the median is $324.50. Flat renewals run from $195 to $350. Percent renewals cost more at Denver rents: 25% of $2,169 is $542.25, and Apex's 50% is $1,084.50.

Watch for extras:

- **Setup:** Rent Me Denver charges a $200 onboarding fee.
- **Maintenance:** Grace and Northpoint list a 10% coordination fee on repair work.
- **Marketing:** Denver Property Management Inc. lists a $325 marketing fee and a $295 move-out inspection on its Elevated plan.
- **City license:** Colorado Realty and Property Management lists $95 for rental license inspection and coordination.

## Which local rules add cost for Denver owners?

The Denver residential rental license is the main local cost. Denver Excise and Licenses says a license is required for anyone offering or operating a residential rental for 30 days or more. You need a passing inspection from a certified private home inspector and the license fee.

| Item | Amount |
|---|---|
| Application fee | $50 |
| License fee, single unit | $50 |
| License fee, 2-10 units | $100 |
| License fee, 11-50 units | $250 |
| License term | 4 years, unless ownership changes |
| Inspection | Private inspector; at least 10% of units at a multiunit property |
| Unlicensed with a housing code violation | $250 fine on the first citation; fines from $150 to $5,000 |

You must also give each tenant the city's "Denver Tenant Rights and Resources" notice at lease signing and when you serve a rent demand.

Colorado's HB23-1099 also shapes leasing. Most landlords must accept a portable tenant screening report made in the last 30 days. If the applicant provides one, you may not charge an application fee. A landlord who takes only one application fee at a time per unit, and refunds it within 20 days after a decline, is exempt.

## Must a Denver property manager hold a CO license?

Yes. The Colorado Division of Real Estate's 2026 Colorado Real Estate Manual prints C.R.S. 12-10-201(6)(a). It defines a real estate broker as any person or firm that, for compensation, engages in "selling, exchanging, buying, renting, or leasing real estate" or offers to rent or lease it. A manager who leases your home for a fee needs a Colorado broker license.

How to check:

- **Use "Search for a Licensee"** on the Colorado Division of Real Estate site.
- **Check the firm's responsible broker**, not only the agent you meet.
- **Ask for the management agreement** in writing before you hand over keys.

## Flat fee or percent: which model is common in Denver?

Percent pricing dominates in Denver. Ten of our 11 managers charge a percent of rent. Mynd is the only flat-fee manager in our count, at $159 a month for one home. Berg and Co. also offers a flat plan at $250 a month, with a full month's rent to lease.

| Rent | 8% fee | 10% fee | $159 flat | $250 flat |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| $1,600 | $128 | $160 | $159 | $250 |
| $2,169 | $173.52 | $216.90 | $159 | $250 |
| $2,900 | $232 | $290 | $159 | $250 |
| $3,500 | $280 | $350 | $159 | $250 |

At 9%, a $159 flat fee is cheaper once rent passes $1,766.67. At 10%, it is cheaper above $1,590. Denver rents are high, so flat fees save more here than in lower-rent metros.

Minimums change the math at the low end. Rent Me Denver and Denver Property Management Inc. charge at least $175 a month, which equals 9.9% only at a $1,768 rent.
