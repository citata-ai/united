## Which has better receipt scanning?

The better receipt tool records your expense correctly with little repair. No side-by-side scan test supports an accuracy winner here.

Stessa places unlimited scanning in Pro. Check the allowance on your intended plan. A trial can make a workflow look available even when the long-term plan limits it.

Test both apps with the same receipts. Include a clear invoice, a faded receipt and a bill split across properties. Check amount, date, vendor and category. Confirm the source image stays linked to the entry.

Correct a scan on your phone, then inspect the desktop record. Export it and check the image or reference. Fast capture helps only when the record still makes sense during review. Ask how to handle a duplicate upload and confirm the app preserves the correct expense.

## Which makes useful tax reports?

A useful report matches the records your tax professional needs. A report label alone does not prove correct treatment of every expense.

Stessa lists a basic tax package, with a Schedule E report on Manage. Advanced reports vary by tier. Ask your tax professional which files they need before selecting a plan.

Use sample income, repairs and a larger improvement expense in each app. Check how to change categories and preserve source documents. Do not let automatic categorization decide tax treatment without review.

Check detailed exports as well as formatted reports. Your bookkeeper may need entries, property totals and receipts. Confirm the files remain usable after cancellation. This is general information, not tax advice. Check the current law or ask a tax professional.

## How do plan prices compare?

Compare the full cost at your unit count and report tier. The table shows entry points, not complete offers for every landlord.

Stessa uses tiers with unlimited properties, according to its page. Landlord Studio lists included units and paid additions. That difference can change the cost as you add rentals.

Keep monthly and annual billing separate. Record the annual amount due, not only its monthly equivalent. Check the selected currency on Landlord Studio's page before using any figure.

List features that cause an upgrade. They might include receipts, reports, users or support. Price the tier that finishes your work. A free plan fits when its limits work for you. It loses value if you need a separate expense log to finish the books. Include that extra work in your decision.

## Which has the better mobile app?

The better app fits the tasks you do away from a desk. Test your own device and account instead of choosing from unverified ratings.

Capture an expense at a property. Add its category and a short note. Locate the entry later without searching a separate photo folder. Check how easily you can correct a wrong amount.

Try a weak connection and inspect what the app saves. Confirm a failed upload is visible. Ask support how to recover a missing image.

Use this mobile test sheet:

- Capture and attach a receipt.
- Choose the correct property.
- Split an expense when needed.
- Correct a category or date.
- Find the source image later.
- Export the required report.
- Check access after signing out.

Do not pass a task because the desktop version can do it. Record what the phone finishes and what still needs a computer.

## Which handles more units?

Stessa lists unlimited properties across plans. Landlord Studio lists unit allowances and additions. Neither establishes how quickly you can close a complex portfolio.

Distinguish properties from units. A building may contain several units with shared expenses. Test allocation and inspect the report. Ask how ownership changes affect historical records.

If you manage for outside owners, test client accounting carefully. Investment bookkeeping and professional management have different approval and reporting needs. More property capacity does not prove a fit for client money.

Ask your bookkeeper to review the workflow before importing everything. A larger portfolio needs clear categories and consistent records. Fix those rules first. Otherwise, the software can repeat old errors across more transactions. Keep a sample with shared costs and ownership changes in your acceptance test.

## How should you move your books?

Choose after both apps produce a useful report from the same sample. A clean dashboard is not enough.

Export current records and preserve receipts. Decide which system holds the final version during testing. Avoid duplicate live entry without a clear review process.

Check opening balances, date ranges and category mapping. Save an old report and reproduce it in the new app. Resolve differences before relying on the totals.

Test the export again after a correction. Confirm the report and source image still agree. Record who reviews categories and when they close a period. Keep access limited to people who need it.
