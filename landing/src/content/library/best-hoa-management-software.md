## How do Vantaca, CINC and AppFolio compare?

Vantaca and CINC position their platforms around association management. AppFolio serves a broader property mix. That difference makes a useful shortlist, but it does not prove which works best for your communities.

Vantaca's platform page describes configurable processes, assigned tasks and billing linked to actions. CINC describes accounting, receivables, violations, architectural requests and board packet creation. AppFolio's pricing page lists community associations among supported property types.

Give each vendor the same community example. Include regular assessments, unpaid balances, a vendor invoice and an architectural request. Ask how staff move between communities without mixing records.

Use the demonstration to test the daily work, not the length of the feature list. A system may support a function yet require a separate module or staff workaround. Record those exceptions before choosing the final plan.

## Which tools handle assessments and violations?

Assessment and violation tools must preserve clear charges, dates, documents and review steps. CINC explicitly lists receivables and violations. Vantaca describes configured workflows and action-based billing. AppFolio needs a specific association demonstration to establish the detail.

Ask staff to create an assessment charge and correct an error. Check whether the original entry and correction remain understandable. Then test a payment that covers several charges.

For a violation, record the observation, photo, notice and response. Ask how the system prevents a repeated notice after the issue closes. Test a disputed case that needs manual review.

Do not treat automation as authority to impose charges or deadlines. Configure the process from the association's approved rules and local advice. This guide does not state a legal timetable. Ask local counsel to check any notice or enforcement template before use.

## Which have a board portal?

A board portal should give board members the records and approvals their roles require. CINC describes homeowner portals and board packet creation. The reviewed Vantaca overview does not establish every board function, so require a live demonstration.

Create a board account during the demo. Find the meeting packet, budget and invoice awaiting approval. Then restrict the account and confirm that private records remain hidden.

Test a board turnover. Remove the former member's access and give the new member the correct role. Check whether the record shows who approved earlier work without requiring the former account to stay active.

Ask how packet versions work. A board should know which file staff intend members to review. Avoid a process where several email attachments compete with the portal copy. Set a clear source for current records and keep prior versions available when needed.

## How is HOA software priced?

HOA software needs a written quote that reflects communities, homes, users, banking and service scope. Vantaca and CINC's reviewed overview pages provide no dollar price. AppFolio's residential starting rates should not become an assumed HOA rate.

Ask whether the charge depends on homes, associations, users or a minimum account fee. Include bank connections, mail, payment processing, document storage and resident communication. Ask which services require another vendor.

Separate implementation from recurring fees. Community imports can include owner records, balances, governing documents and open enforcement files. Have the vendor list what the quoted migration includes.

Compare the bill after promotional terms end. Ask how the price changes when your company adds or loses an association. A low base fee can become less useful if essential board or accounting functions need extra modules.

## Can rental PM software handle HOAs?

Rental property software can fit association work only when it supports the actual association process. A resident portal and rent ledger alone do not establish assessment accounting, board access or architectural review.

Build a list of work that differs from your rental portfolio. Include owner changes, association budgets, board approvals and records shared with homeowners. Ask the vendor to complete those tasks without renaming rental fields as a workaround.

Test accounting at the association level. Your accountant should be able to explain each community's reports independently. Check whether shared vendors and company staff can work across communities without merging financial records.

A mixed-management company may prefer fewer systems. That preference is reasonable only if association work still passes the required tests. Do not force a fit that leaves boards dependent on side spreadsheets.

## What should your final demo include?

The final demo should show how office staff, board members and residents complete connected work. Give each role a separate test account.

- **Billing:** create, correct and explain an assessment.
- **Payments:** trace a payment to the intended account.
- **Violations:** record evidence, response and closure.
- **Requests:** route an architectural application for review.
- **Board work:** assemble a packet and record an approval.
- **Access:** remove a former board member safely.
- **Export:** download usable ledgers and documents.

Have the association accountant and a manager review the result. Ask a board member to test the portal without coaching. Their questions can reveal missing labels or unclear report dates.

Record every remaining manual step and its responsible person. Ask the vendor to include essential configuration in the implementation scope.
