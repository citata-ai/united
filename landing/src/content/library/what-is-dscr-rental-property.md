## What is DSCR?

DSCR, the debt service coverage ratio, shows how many times a property's income covers its loan payments. Lenders use it to answer one question: if the owner had no other income, could the rent pay the mortgage?

Read the ratio this way:

| DSCR | What it means |
|------|---------------|
| Below 1.00 | Income does not cover the loan. The owner pays the gap. |
| 1.00 | Income exactly covers the loan. No margin. |
| 1.25 | Income is 25% more than the loan payments. |
| 1.50 | Income is 50% more than the loan payments. |

Debt service means the total loan payments for a period: principal plus interest. A higher DSCR means more room for a vacancy or a large repair before the owner misses a payment.

## How do you calculate DSCR from NOI?

You calculate DSCR by dividing net operating income (NOI) by annual debt service. NOI is rent and other income, minus vacancy and operating expenses. NOI does not subtract the mortgage.

DSCR = NOI / annual debt service

Worked example 1, with made-up numbers for a 4-unit building:

| Line | Amount per year |
|------|-----------------|
| Gross rent (4 units x $1,500 x 12) | $72,000 |
| Vacancy and credit loss (5%) | −$3,600 |
| Effective gross income | $68,400 |
| Operating expenses (taxes, insurance, repairs, management, utilities) | −$28,000 |
| NOI | $40,400 |
| Annual debt service (principal and interest) | $31,000 |
| DSCR | $40,400 / $31,000 = 1.30 |

This building passes a 1.25x test with a small margin.

Watch for: the costs you include. A lender will add costs the owner left out, such as a management fee even when the owner self-manages, or a replacement reserve. Each one lowers NOI and the DSCR.

## The rent-based DSCR used by many DSCR loans

Many DSCR loans for 1 to 4 unit rentals use a simpler formula: rent divided by the full housing payment. Baselane gives the formula as gross rental income divided by debt obligations, where the debt side includes principal, interest, taxes, insurance and HOA fees.

Worked example 2, a single-family rental with made-up numbers:

| Line | Amount per month |
|------|------------------|
| Market rent | $2,100 |
| Principal and interest | $1,350 |
| Property taxes | $250 |
| Insurance | $120 |
| HOA | $0 |
| Total payment | $1,720 |
| DSCR | $2,100 / $1,720 = 1.22 |

The 2 formulas give different numbers for the same property. The classic NOI version subtracts repairs, management and vacancy. The rent-based version does not, but it adds taxes and insurance to the debt side. Ask each lender which formula it uses before you compare quotes.

## What DSCR do lenders want?

Lenders want a DSCR of at least 1.0, and many want 1.25 or more. The exact line depends on the loan type.

| Loan type | Typical DSCR rule | Source |
|-----------|-------------------|--------|
| Fannie Mae Small Mortgage Loans (5+ units, $1 million to $9 million) | 1.25x minimum | Greystone term sheet, 2025 |
| Same program, reserves waived | 65% LTV and 1.35x DSCR | Greystone term sheet, 2025 |
| DSCR loans for 1 to 4 unit rentals | 1.0 to 1.25 preferred; some accept 0.75 | Baselane guide |

Baselane also reports a typical minimum credit score near 660, with some lenders at 620, and down payments of 20% to 25%. Some lenders ask for up to 40% when the DSCR or credit is weak.

Lender terms change often. Treat these numbers as a starting point and get written quotes.

## What is a DSCR loan?

A DSCR loan is a mortgage for investors that qualifies the borrower mainly on the property's rent, not on personal income. Baselane describes it as "a type of Non-Qualified Mortgage (Non-QM)". Non-QM means the loan sits outside the standard federal rules for qualified home loans.

Who uses DSCR loans:

- **Self-employed investors** whose tax returns show low income after write-offs.
- **Owners with many properties** who hit the limit on conventional loans.
- **Buyers who close in an LLC**, where the lender allows it.

Trade-offs to check:

- **Rate.** DSCR loans often cost more than conventional investor loans.
- **Down payment.** Usually 20% or more.
- **Prepayment penalty.** Many DSCR loans charge a fee for early payoff. Read the schedule.
- **Rent proof.** The lender may use a signed lease or an appraiser's market rent estimate.

This is general information, not financial or legal advice. Ask a lender or a licensed advisor about your case.

## How a property manager affects DSCR

A property manager affects DSCR through NOI, the top of the formula. Better management can raise income and cut costs; poor management does the opposite.

Ways a manager moves NOI:

1. **Rent at market.** Each $50 per month on 4 units adds $2,400 a year to NOI.
2. **Shorter vacancy.** Each month of vacancy saved on a $1,500 unit adds $1,500.
3. **Lower turnover.** Fewer move-outs mean fewer make-ready bills.
4. **Planned repairs.** A planned repair often costs less than an emergency call.

The management fee lowers NOI. Lenders often include a fee in their NOI even when you self-manage. ## DSCR checklist before you apply

- **Get 12 months of rent and expense records**, or a rent roll and leases.
- **Calculate NOI with a vacancy line and a management fee**, even if you self-manage.
- **Ask the lender for its formula**: NOI version or rent-based version.
- **Run the DSCR at your quoted rate and at a rate 1 point higher.**
- **Check the minimum DSCR, the down payment and any prepayment penalty** in writing.
