## How do I calculate cost per acquired door?

Cost per acquired door is total channel spend divided by new doors signed from that channel. Use the same period for both, such as one quarter.

The formula has 3 parts:

1. **Spend.** Ad spend, agency or tool fees, lead fees, and the hours your team put in, at their hourly cost.
2. **Doors signed.** Doors under a signed management agreement, not leads and not owners.
3. **Period.** Pick 90 days or more. Owner sales take weeks, so one month is too short.

Property Manager Websites (PMW) gives a simple example. Spend $1,000 and sign 2 paying clients, and your acquisition cost is $500 per client. PMW also says that, across channels, its clients see a cost closer to $1,000 per client.

Count doors, not clients. One owner with 3 doors cuts your cost per door to a third. PMW's worksheet uses 1.2 doors per owner as an example.

## How does cost per door differ by channel?

Cost per door differs by channel because each channel has a different cost per lead and close rate. We did not find a public study that measures cost per signed door by channel. So the table below is a worked example. Replace each input with your own.

| Channel | Spend in 90 days | Leads | Close rate | Owners | Doors (×1.2) | Cost per door |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Google Ads | $7,500 | 30 (at $250) | 20% | 6 | 7.2 | $1,042 |
| SEO retainer | $4,500 | 15 | 25% | 3.75 | 4.5 | $1,000 |
| Agent referrals | $1,500 (lunches, time) | 6 | 50% | 3 | 3.6 | $417 |
| Buy a portfolio | $150,000 | n/a | n/a | n/a | 50 units | $3,000 |

Where the inputs come from:

- **Google Ads at $250 a lead:** PMW's worksheet says its PPC (pay per click) clients see $250 to $350 per qualified lead.
- **Portfolio at $3,000 a unit:** PMW puts buying units at $2,000 to $4,000 per unit. It adds that some units leave during the change, so the true cost per kept door is higher.
- **SEO and referral inputs:** example numbers, not benchmarks. SEO costs also land months before the doors do.

Watch for: an SEO retainer keeps paying off after you stop. Ads stop when the money stops. Compare channels over 12 months, not 90 days, before you cut one.

## What does a new door earn each month?

A new door earns its monthly management fee, plus fees for leasing and other services. As an example, the U.S. Census Bureau puts the national median gross rent at $1,413 a month (QuickFacts, 2020–2024). All Property Management says typical fees run 8 to 12% of rent. At 10%, one door earns $141.30 a month, or about $1,696 a year.

Your fee may be flat or higher. Check yours in the [property management fee calculator](/tools/property-management-fee-calculator).

## What is a healthy payback period for a new door?

Payback period is cost per door divided by monthly revenue per door. It tells you how many months of fees it takes to earn back what you spent.

| Cost per door | Revenue per door per month | Payback (revenue) |
|---|---|---|
| $417 | $141.30 | 3 months |
| $1,000 | $141.30 | 7 months |
| $1,042 | $141.30 | 7.4 months |
| $3,000 | $141.30 | 21 months |

That is payback on revenue. Payback on profit takes longer. If your margin is 25%, divide monthly revenue by 4 first. Then a $1,000 door takes about 28 months to pay back from profit.

Compare payback with tenure. If owners stay about 4 years, or 48 months, a 7-month revenue payback leaves a wide margin. A 28-month profit payback leaves less. A door that leaves in year 1 never pays back.

PMW suggests a lifetime value to acquisition cost ratio of 4:1 as a target. At $1,696 a year, 1.2 doors and 4 years, one owner is worth about $8,139. So the cap is about $2,035 per owner, or about $1,696 per door.

## How do I lower my cost per door?

Lower cost per door by fixing the steps after the lead arrives. Those cost nothing extra. Use this checklist:

- [ ] **Reply fast.** Call every owner lead back the same business hour. A lead you pay for and lose is pure cost.
- [ ] **Track close rate by channel.** Tag the source on every lead in your CRM.
- [ ] **Show your fees.** A public fee table screens out owners who will not pay your price.
- [ ] **Ask for referrals.** Real estate agents and current owners send leads at a low cash cost.
- [ ] **Target multi-door owners.** Each sale brings more doors for the same effort.
- [ ] **Keep owners longer.** Every extra year of tenure spreads the cost over more fees.
- [ ] **Fix the website first.** In the Goodjuju 2026 report, Google search and Maps brought about half of all owner leads to 35 companies. Every paid click also lands on your site.

Goodjuju also found that AI tools brought 3.4% of owner leads in fall 2026, with the highest visit-to-lead rate of any channel at 3.9%. That share is small and based on 43 leads. Still, a lead from an AI answer costs no ad spend. Read [how owners choose a property manager with AI](/guides/how-owners-choose-a-property-manager-with-ai) to see how that channel works.

## Cost per door, step by step

1. **Pick a period** of at least 90 days.
2. **List spend by channel**, including staff time.
3. **Count signed doors by channel** from your CRM.
4. **Divide** spend by doors for each channel.
5. **Divide** each result by monthly revenue per door to get payback.
6. **Move budget** toward the channel with the lowest cost per door that still scales.
