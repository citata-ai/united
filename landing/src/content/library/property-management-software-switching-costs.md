## What does each vendor say about switching costs and timelines?

Only one of six vendors puts a clear "no fee" on its pricing page. The others give a fee "by quote" or say nothing. We read each vendor's own pages on 2026-11-09.

| Vendor | Onboarding or setup fee | Stated timeline | What the vendor moves | Data after you leave |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Yardi Breeze** | "No onboarding fees", "No training fees" on the pricing page | "Set up properties, units and residents in minutes" (no go-live figure) | Not stated | Not stated |
| **Rentvine** | One-time setup fee, amount by quote | 45 days, signed agreement to fully live | "We handle 80% of the migration" | Full access for 12 months after cancellation |
| **AppFolio** | Not published | 4 to 6 weeks (FAQ); 1.6 months average go-live (G2, cited by AppFolio) | Dedicated team helps move accounting data | Not stated |
| **Buildium** | Not published; onboarding required on Growth and Premium | Not published | Property, unit, lease, owner, resident and vendor data | Not stated |
| **DoorLoop** | Not published | 3 to 5 business days | Current rent roll or ledger only | "You can export it at any time" |
| **Rent Manager** | Not published (site returned HTTP 503) | Not published | Not checked | Not checked |

"Not published" means the page we read gives no number. It does not mean the cost is zero. Ask each vendor for the fee, the scope and the date in writing.

## Which software has no onboarding fee?

Yardi Breeze is the only vendor in this table that states "No onboarding fees" on its pricing page. The same page says "No training fees" and "No support fees" for residential, commercial, affordable and association portfolios.

Breeze residential costs $1 per unit per month with a $100 monthly minimum, on an annual agreement. Breeze Premier residential has a $400 monthly minimum, and "terms & minimums apply" (Yardi Breeze, read 2026-11-09).

The page does not say who converts your old data or how long a full move takes. "Minutes" refers to setting up properties, units and residents. Ask how opening balances and history come across.

## How long does a switch take?

Stated timelines run from 3 business days to about 2 months. They measure different things, so do not compare them one to one.

- **DoorLoop: 3 to 5 business days.** The DoorLoop help center gives this for its import. The import covers properties, units, leases and tenants from your current rent roll or ledger.
- **AppFolio: 4 to 6 weeks.** The AppFolio FAQ says onboarding "typically" lasts 4 to 6 weeks. An AppFolio post (2026-10-19) says a few weeks to about two months under 250 units. It also cites the G2 Grid Report for Property Management, Fall 2026: an average go-live of 1.6 months, and 72% of users ran implementation with their own team.
- **Rentvine: 45 days.** Rentvine's pricing page counts from signed agreement to fully operational.
- **Buildium and Rent Manager.** No timeline on the pages we read.

A fast import is not a finished switch. Your team still checks balances, trains staff and moves owners and renters to new payment routes. Our [switching checklist](/guides/how-to-switch-property-management-software) lists those steps.

## What does the vendor migrate, and what is left to you?

Scope differs more than price. Read the scope line before you compare quotes.

DoorLoop is the clearest. Its help center says migration "solely includes the import of your current rent roll or ledger". The chart of accounts, transactions and past ledgers are not included. Charges, payments, expenses and bills are not part of the automatic import.

Buildium's onboarding page lists property, unit, lease, owner, resident and vendor data. Buildium standardizes your files and loads them. The page does not mention transaction history.

Rentvine says it handles 80% of the migration. The page does not say what the other 20% is. Ask for the split as a list.

AppFolio's FAQ says a dedicated team walks you through the move. The steps depend on your data and your old system.

Plan to keep history outside the new system. Export old ledgers, owner statements and documents to storage you control before you cancel.

## How do you get your data out later?

Two vendors state an exit position. The other four did not on the pages we read.

- **Rentvine:** "full access to their data for 12 months post-cancellation" (Rentvine blog, updated 2026-07-29). The same post says the open API is in the standard rate.
- **DoorLoop:** "Your data is yours, and you can export it at any time" (DoorLoop pricing page).

A blog post or FAQ line is not a contract. Ask for the exit terms in the order form or the subscription agreement. Ask how many days you have, which formats you get and whether retrieval costs extra.

## What should you ask before you sign?

Ask each finalist the same 6 questions and compare the answers in one sheet:

1. **Fee.** What is the one-time setup or onboarding fee for my unit count?
2. **Scope.** Which records do you import: open balances only, or transaction history too?
3. **Timeline.** What date do you commit to, and what must I deliver before the clock starts?
4. **Minimums.** What is the monthly minimum? AppFolio Core states a 50-unit minimum; DoorLoop states "Minimum spend and units apply".
5. **Payments.** When do owner and renter payments move, and who sets up the bank accounts?
6. **Exit.** How long can I export after cancellation, in which formats, and at what cost?
