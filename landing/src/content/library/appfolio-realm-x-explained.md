## What are Realm-X Assistant and Realm-X Messages?

Realm-X Assistant supports questions and tasks within AppFolio. Realm-X Messages supports the resident inbox and replies. AppFolio's product overview describes them as related parts of its embedded AI tools.

| Component | Role to evaluate | Staff check |
|---|---|---|
| Assistant | Ask questions and request actions | Verify source and intended record |
| Messages | Read, organize, and draft replies | Check facts, recipient, and tone |
| Flows | Run configured processes | Review triggers and exceptions |
| Performers | Handle ongoing operational tasks | Define authority and escalation |

A question answer and a completed action are different results. Ask the demo agent to show how staff tell them apart. A useful reply should not leave you guessing whether a charge or message changed.

Test record access under a normal staff role. A manager should see only the data and actions their role permits. Ask what happens when a prompt requests something outside that role.

## What do the Leasing and Maintenance Performers do?

AppFolio describes the Performers as tools for ongoing leasing, maintenance, and resident work. Confirm the current scope of the specific Performer you want.

For leasing, ask the vendor to demonstrate an inquiry, a follow-up question, tour scheduling, and a staff handoff. Check where the conversation lands and how a person takes over.

For maintenance, test intake, clarification, urgency review, and assignment. Introduce an unavailable vendor and an unclear issue. The exception path matters as much as a normal request.

Do not assume the product can complete work outside your approved vendor roster or property rules. Ask which facts you must configure and who keeps them current.

Inspect the saved record after the demo. It should show what the system did, what failed, and what staff still need to review. A short conversation summary alone may omit important steps.

## Does Realm-X cost extra?

Realm-X access depends on the component and plan. AppFolio's pricing page lists Assistant and Messages in Core, with Flows in Plus. Confirm Performer pricing separately in a written offer.

The page read on 2026-10-19 lists Core from $1.49 per unit monthly, with a 50-unit minimum and minimum spend. Plus starts at $3.20 per unit monthly. These are base-plan figures, not complete Performer prices.

Ask for a line item for each AI component. Include setup, included volume, support, and any usage or service charges. Do not treat a feature listed beside a tier as proof that every related service has no extra cost.

Compare the quote against the tasks you tested. A broad AI bundle can be poor value if the team needs only a narrow workflow. Ask what happens to records and active processes if you stop an add-on.

## What are its limits?

Realm-X depends on the records, permissions, and rules available to it. A missing lease detail or outdated vendor instruction can affect the next action.

Test known gaps in sample data. Ask a question whose answer is absent and inspect the response. Staff need to see uncertainty rather than a plausible guess.

Review outgoing messages before allowing broad automation. Check rent amounts, dates, access details, and promises to residents. A well-written message can still contain a wrong fact.

Keep high-impact actions behind suitable controls. Decide which changes require review and who can approve them. Use logs and exports to check the result.

Do not build your staffing plan from a vendor's broad time-savings claim. Measure your own work before and during the pilot. Include the time spent checking and correcting AI output.

## How does Realm-X compare with Rentvine AI?

Realm-X and Rentvine AI should be compared within the software your team uses. Both vendors describe AI tied to operational data, but the exact tasks and approval models need direct testing.

Rentvine's pricing page lists an assistant and maintenance AI. Its Cru article describes suggestions that staff confirm, edit, or dismiss. The current pricing page also uses the Fixie name, so confirm product names and behavior in your account.

Use the same task in both demos. Ask for a work-order update, a resident reply, and a record lookup. Inspect the source, authority, and final saved result.

## How should you run a Realm-X pilot?

Run a narrow pilot with clear staff ownership and measurable tasks. Start where the team can quickly verify the result.

Keep a list of correct outputs, corrections, failed actions, and staff review time. Inspect cases that look successful as well as visible failures. A quiet error may matter more than a failed prompt.

Expand only when the team understands the record trail and the fallback process. Keep training and configuration current as the vendor changes features. Before the next expansion, repeat the sample case that caused the most trouble.

## How should you test the choice before you pay?

Test the full workflow with sample records before you move live data. Use the same case in each product. Ask the person who will do the daily work to run the test. A sales demo alone does not show where your team will need help.

Keep a decision sheet with the task, result, missing step, and person who checked it. Mark each result as shown, promised, or still open. Ask for written terms when a feature needs an upgrade or another service. Include staff setup time in your cost review.

Check the exit path while you still control the choice. Export the sample records and open them outside the platform. Confirm that notes, files, dates, and links remain useful. Ask how you will get records after you close the account.
