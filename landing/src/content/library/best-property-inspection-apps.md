## Which apps make photo reports with timestamps?

RentCheck's pricing page lists time-stamped images, video, and side-by-side inspection comparison. HappyCo describes configurable inspections and mobile capture. Test the output before choosing either.

Create a sample report for a room with normal wear and a separate repair issue. Include wide views and close details. Confirm that the final report ties each image to the right room and date.

A timestamp helps organize evidence, but it does not explain condition by itself. Staff should write plain notes about the item and observed issue. Avoid vague descriptions that later readers cannot interpret.

Keep the original images where possible. Test the export and check whether resolution, labels, and dates survive. A portal thumbnail is not the same as a usable evidence file.

Ask who can change a completed inspection. You need a clear way to correct an error while preserving the earlier record and reviewer notes.

## Which integrate with your PMS?

The best integration moves the records your team actually needs in the correct direction. A vendor's integration badge does not show every supported field or action.

RentCheck lists PMS integration and work-order options across its plans. HappyCo describes PMS connections on its pricing page. Confirm your exact system and package with both vendors.

Test a unit import, resident change, completed inspection, and maintenance issue. Check whether photos and notes move together. A link to an outside report may be useful, but it differs from a native work order.

Ask what happens when a property transfers to a new owner or manager. Duplicate unit records can separate the move-in and move-out history. Keep stable identifiers during setup.

If your PMS already has inspection tools, test them first. AppFolio lists mobile inspections within its maintenance offer. An existing tool may meet the need without another account or integration.

## Which work offline?

HappyCo's pricing page states that its Maintenance and Optigo Happy apps support offline work after login. The same page says Call Management needs a connection. Do not apply an offline claim to every product in a suite.

For every shortlisted app, test the actual phone and account you will use. Load the property, disconnect, take images, add notes, and restore the connection.

Check for missing files and duplicate records after sync. Then test an interrupted upload. Staff need a clear indicator that the report is fully stored.

Do not assume RentCheck or your PMS works offline from a mobile-app label alone. Ask the vendor to confirm the exact behavior, including login and file limits.

A property with weak reception may make offline capture a deciding feature. Plan a backup process for device failure as well as a weak connection.

## How do reports help in deposit disputes?

Inspection reports help staff compare recorded conditions and explain a proposed charge. They do not automatically prove liability or decide which deductions local law permits.

Use consistent room labels and item names at move-in and move-out. Keep resident comments, repair history, invoices, and the signed condition record together where appropriate.

Separate observed damage from the decision to charge. The report should state what staff saw. A later review can consider wear, responsibility, repair cost, and applicable rules.

Do not treat AI image flags as a final charge. Have a person inspect the underlying photos and context. A flag can help staff find an issue without establishing who caused it.

Ask counsel to review your inspection, notice, and deduction process. This is general information, not legal advice. Check the current law or ask a local attorney.

## What does each cost?

Inspection cost depends on the plan, door count, users, integrations, and report tools. Request the full quote for your actual scope.

RentCheck uses a per-door pricing calculator, but the fetched page did not expose a stable base figure. Get the selected plan total in writing. Do not repeat a search snippet as the price.

HappyCo's pricing page describes modular packages without a universal public amount. Treat the offer as quote only. Ask which inspection, maintenance, and service components it includes.

Buildium's pricing page lists inspection charges that vary by plan. Test what you already have and confirm future charges in the quote. AppFolio also needs a plan-specific review.

Include staff training, template setup, and data retention in the cost check. The cheapest subscription can still require extra field visits or manual file sorting.

Pick after you export a complete move-in and move-out sample. Have the staff member who handles repairs and the person who reviews charges both inspect it.

## How should you test the choice before you pay?

Test the full workflow with sample records before you move live data. Use the same case in each product. Ask the person who will do the daily work to run the test. A sales demo alone does not show where your team will need help.

Keep a decision sheet with the task, result, missing step, and person who checked it. Mark each result as shown, promised, or still open. Ask for written terms when a feature needs an upgrade or another service. Include staff setup time in your cost review.

Check the exit path while you still control the choice. Export the sample records and open them outside the platform. Confirm that notes, files, dates, and links remain useful. Ask how you will get records after you close the account.
