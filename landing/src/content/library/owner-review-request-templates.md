## Why do owner reviews matter for AI search?

Owner reviews matter because AI assistants read review sites when they pick property managers. In October 2026 we asked ChatGPT, Claude and Gemini for the best property manager in 50 US cities. Listing and review sites were a source in 132 of the 150 answers (our data, 2026-10). Expertise.com was a source in 93 answers and Yelp in 50.

Most reviews of a property manager come from tenants. Tenants have more contact with you, and more reasons to complain. Owners are your clients, and their voice is often missing. A fair request to every owner fixes that gap.

Recency counts too. BrightLocal's 2026 survey found that 74% of consumers look for reviews written in the last 3 months (BrightLocal, 2026). A steady flow of owner reviews keeps your profile current.

## When is the best time to ask an owner?

Ask within a few days of a clear win. The owner still remembers the result, and the result is concrete.

| Moment | Why it works | Template |
|---|---|---|
| New lease signed after a vacancy | The owner sees rent coming in | A |
| Large repair closed on time and on budget | The owner saw you protect the asset | B |
| First year-end statement or 1099 sent | The owner sees a full year of work | C |
| 90 days after onboarding | The owner has seen the system work | A |
| Owner says thanks in an email | The owner already said it in words | D |

Do not ask after a dispute, an eviction or a large unplanned cost. That is not a ban: it is just a bad moment. Also never skip an owner on purpose because you expect a bad review. Google bans requests that "selectively solicit positive reviews".

Send one request and one reminder at most. More feels like pressure.

## What should the email and text say?

The request should be short, personal and honest. Name the owner and the event. Ask for an honest review. Give one direct link. Fill in the brackets and send from a real person's address.

Template A, email after a lease or onboarding:

```text
Subject: [Property address]: your new lease, and one small request

Hi [Owner first name],

The new lease at [property address] starts on [date].
Rent is [amount] per month. Your first payout goes out on [date].

Could you share an honest review of your experience with us?
Good or bad, it helps other owners choose, and it helps us improve.
It takes about 2 minutes: [Google review link]

Thank you,
[Your name], [Role], [Company]
[Direct phone]
```

Template B, email after a repair:

```text
Subject: [Property address]: [repair] is done

Hi [Owner first name],

The [repair] at [property address] is complete.
Final cost: [amount], against the estimate of [amount].
Photos and the invoice are in your owner portal.

If you have 2 minutes, an honest review of how we handled it
would help other owners: [Google review link]

Thanks,
[Your name], [Company]
```

Template C, email with the year-end statement:

```text
Subject: Your [year] statement for [property address]

Hi [Owner first name],

Your year-end statement and 1099 are in the owner portal.
Quick view of [year]: [months occupied] months occupied,
[number] work orders closed, [amount] total rent collected.

Once a year we ask every owner for an honest review.
You can write one here: [Google review link]
If something did not go well, reply to this email. I read every reply.

[Your name], [Role], [Company]
```

Template D, text message:

```text
Hi [First name], this is [Your name] at [Company]. Thanks for
your kind note about [event]. Would you share an honest review
on Google? [Short link] No pressure at all.
```

What the templates avoid: no gift, no discount, no "5 stars", no "only if you are happy". Each one asks for an honest review.

## Which site should you send owners to?

Send owners to your Google Business Profile. Google allows requests and gives you a review link or a scannable code to share (Google Business Profile Help). Note that the reviewer needs a Google Account. Google says a non-Gmail email address works for that account.

Do not ask for Yelp reviews. Yelp's support page says: "Don't ask anyone to review your business, be it customers, mailing list subscribers, friends, family, etc." Yelp also says its software "may not recommend reviews that seem to be prompted or encouraged by the business". A Yelp request can lower your Yelp rating instead of raising it.

| Site | Ask owners? | Note |
|---|---|---|
| Google Business Profile | Yes | Use the review link or QR code |
| Yelp | No | Yelp policy forbids requests |
| Other directories | Check each site's terms | Read the rules before you send a link |
| Your website testimonials | Yes, with written consent | Use only real words from real owners |

## What does Google forbid?

Google's content policy lists what a business may not do with reviews. A business may not:

- **Pay for reviews.** "Reviews or ratings that have been paid for, directly or in kind" are fake engagement.
- **Offer incentives.** No "payment, discounts, free goods and/or services" for a review, a change or a removal.
- **Gate reviews.** No requests that "discourage or prohibit negative reviews, or selectively solicit positive reviews".
- **Use insiders.** Content "based on a conflict of interest", such as "current or former employment", is not allowed.
- **Post for others.** Content "posted from multiple accounts by or at the request of one person" is fake.

The FTC rule on consumer reviews has applied since October 21, 2024. Section 465.4 bans an incentive tied to a positive or negative review (16 CFR 465.4). Section 465.5 bans undisclosed reviews by officers and managers. It also bans asking employees or relatives to post reviews without disclosure (16 CFR 465.5). FTC staff note that the rule has no specific ban on asking only happy customers. But the practice can still break the FTC Act (FTC Q&A).

This is general information, not legal advice. Check the current law or ask a local attorney.

## Pre-send checklist

- [ ] The request goes to every owner who had the event, not only to happy ones.
- [ ] The text asks for an "honest review". It does not ask for 5 stars.
- [ ] No gift, discount, fee credit or contest is in the message.
- [ ] The link goes to Google, not to Yelp.
- [ ] No staff member, relative or vendor gets the request.
- [ ] One reminder at most, 7 days later.
- [ ] Every new review gets a reply within a week. BrightLocal found 81% of consumers expect a reply within a week (BrightLocal, 2026).
