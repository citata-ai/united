## What Does Property Management Coaching Cost?

Get a written quote for the exact program and term because public pages may omit the full coaching price. Separate the base program from add-ons.

DoorGrow's pricing page, read on 2026-10-19, lists its main coaching investment as talk to our team. Treat that as quote only. The page also describes different levels of access. Do not use an older quoted joining or monthly price as the current total.

Ask each provider to itemize initial charges, recurring fees, required tools, travel and optional events. Include staff time needed to prepare data and carry out changes. A low monthly fee can still demand more capacity than your company has.

Use this comparison table before requesting proposals. It lists categories to assess, not verified prices for an unnamed provider.

| Option | What you buy | Cost evidence to request |
|---|---|---|
| Private coaching | Advice focused on your business | Written scope, access and full quote |
| Mastermind group | Facilitated peer discussion and shared learning | Program fee, group format and extras |
| Project consulting | Work on a defined deliverable | Scope, milestones and acceptance terms |
| Franchise | Brand and operating relationship under contract | Current disclosure and agreement review |

Compare the total commitment over the term you can actually leave. A monthly billing schedule does not necessarily mean monthly cancellation. Ask for renewal, refund and ownership terms before paying.

## What Should a Coach Actually Do?

A coach should help you make and carry out better decisions within a defined scope. Agree what useful work will look like before joining.

Write the problem in concrete terms. Examples include weak owner intake, delayed repair updates or a sales process no one follows. Avoid a brief that says only grow the company. The provider cannot be judged fairly against an undefined result.

Ask how the coach diagnoses the problem. They should explain the records they need, how they form advice and how progress is reviewed. Ask whether the work includes documents, calls, training or hands-on implementation. Those are different services.

Request references with similar problems and company needs. Ask what work the client performed and what remained difficult. A success story without its context cannot show what you should expect. Do not treat a testimonial as a guarantee.

Check boundaries for legal, tax and licensed advice. A business coach may help you prepare questions but should not replace the qualified adviser your situation needs. Keep final authority with the people responsible for the company and its regulated work.

## Should You Choose a Mastermind or Private Coaching?

Choose a peer group when shared discussion fits the problem, and private work when you need focused review or confidentiality. Check the actual format before deciding.

Ask how the group is formed and who leads it. Find out how much attention each member receives and how questions are handled between meetings. A large library of lessons is different from review of your own process.

Consider whether you can safely discuss the information involved. Owner records, resident details and employee concerns need careful handling. Use anonymized examples where possible and ask about confidentiality and data practices. Avoid sharing sensitive records through a casual group chat.

Private coaching can allow deeper work on your own decisions. It can still fail if the advice stays generic or you lack time to act. Ask to see the proposed work sequence and the way decisions become assigned tasks.

Assess your own readiness. Name the person who will collect data and implement changes. If every action depends on an already overloaded founder, a smaller project may be more useful than an open-ended program. Buy a format your team can use.

## How Does Coaching Compare With a Franchise?

Coaching and franchising create different relationships, so compare the actual rights, costs and restrictions. Neither is automatically the better growth route.

The FTC's franchise guide explains that the disclosure document covers fees, obligations and other important terms. It says the document must arrive at least 14 days before signing or paying the franchisor or an affiliate. Have qualified counsel review the current documents before committing.

A franchise can involve brand rights, operating requirements and continuing payments. Coaching may leave more choices with your company, depending on its contract. Check ownership of websites, accounts and work products in either arrangement. Do not assume you can take everything with you at exit.

Ask franchise contacts and coaching references the same practical questions. What did the provider deliver? What did the operator still have to build? Which costs were unexpected? What happened when support was needed?

Compare independent operation with the full alternative, not only the joining fee. Include systems, staff training, marketing and your time. Avoid taking a provider's claim about another model as independent evidence. Review each offer through its own current terms.

## How Can You Test Whether the Program Is Worth It?

Set a starting record and a defined review before the work begins. Treat the result as a business test, not a promised return.

- Record the problem, current process and supporting data.
- Agree deliverables and who carries out each task.
- Calculate the full cash and staff-time commitment.
- Define evidence that the work was completed correctly.
- Review changes in service, cost and sales with context.
- Use the contract's exit terms if the fit is poor.

Keep other changes visible. New pricing, staff turnover or different advertising can affect results during coaching. Do not credit the program for every improvement or blame it for unrelated losses.
