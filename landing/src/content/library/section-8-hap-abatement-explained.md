## When does the PHA start abatement?

The PHA starts abatement when the owner misses the repair deadline after an inspection. The deadlines in 24 CFR 982.404(a)(3) are:

- **Life-threatening deficiency:** fixed within 24 hours of notice.
- **Other deficiency:** fixed within 30 calendar days of notice, or a PHA-approved extension.

The rule has two stages. The first is withholding, which is optional. The second is abatement, which is required.

| Stage | When | What happens to the HAP | Rule |
|---|---|---|---|
| Withholding (optional) | After written notice of deficiencies, during the cure period | The PHA may hold payments | 982.404(d)(1) |
| Repaired in time | Within 24 hours or 30 days | The PHA must resume and pay back the withheld amount | 982.404(d)(1)(i)-(ii) |
| Abatement (required) | Repairs not done in the cure period | The PHA must abate, including amounts withheld | 982.404(d)(2)(i) |
| Repaired during abatement | Within 60 days of the abatement notice | Payments restart; no pay for the abated period | 982.404(d)(4) |
| Not repaired | After 60 days, or a longer PHA period | The PHA must terminate the HAP contract | 982.404(d)(5) |

The current version of 24 CFR 982.404 applies to HAP contracts signed or renewed after June 6, 2024. Older contracts follow the version in effect on that date (982.404(f)). Ask your PHA which version applies to your unit.

Macon Housing Authority describes the same flow in plain terms. Its inspector mails a report with an "HQS Due Date." A reinspection follows on or soon after that date. If owner repairs are not done, the PHA stops the HAP. That is the abatement.

## Can I collect the abated amount from the tenant?

No. The abated amount is a penalty on the owner, not a debt of the tenant. Macon Housing Authority says: "you cannot collect any of the penalty from the tenant."

This matches the federal rules. Under 24 CFR 982.451(b)(4), the tenant's part of the rent may not be more than the rent minus the PHA payment. Under 24 CFR 982.310(b), the family does not owe the PHA's share. The HAP contract form also bars any payment for the unit beyond the rent to owner.

## Does the tenant still pay their share during abatement?

Yes. The tenant keeps paying the tenant share during abatement. Macon Housing Authority: "the tenant is only responsible for continuing to pay you their share of the rent."

Here is what abatement costs in a simple example. The numbers are for illustration only.

| Item | Amount |
|---|---|
| Rent to owner | $1,600 |
| HAP (PHA share) | $1,150 |
| Tenant share | $450 |
| Abatement period | 45 days (1.5 months) |
| Lost HAP | 1.5 × $1,150 = $1,725 |
| Income you still receive | Tenant share only: $675 |

A repair that would have cost a few hundred dollars can cost more than $1,700 in lost HAP.

## Are payments backdated after the unit passes?

No, not for the abated period. 24 CFR 982.404(d)(4) says that if the unit passes within the 60-day window, "the PHA must recommence payments." It also says: "The PHA does not make any payments to the owner for the period of time that the payments were abated."

Withheld money is different. If you fix the items within the cure period, the PHA "must provide assistance payments to cover the time period" it withheld (982.404(d)(1)(ii)). That is why the cure deadline matters more than the reinspection date.

Macon Housing Authority says payments resume "pro-rated from the date the unit passes the final re-inspection."

## When does abatement turn into HAP contract termination?

Abatement turns into termination after 60 days. When the PHA abates, it must send a notice to the owner and the family (982.404(d)(2)(ii)). The notice says the HAP contract will end if the unit does not pass within 60 days, or a longer PHA period. The PHA must give the family its voucher to move at least 30 days before the contract ends.

Two more rules apply during abatement:

1. **No eviction for abatement.** The owner may not terminate the tenancy because of withholding or abatement (982.404(d)(3)).
2. **The family may leave.** The family may end the tenancy by notice to you and the PHA. The HAP contract then ends on the tenancy end date or the move-out date, whichever is earlier.

Separately, any HAP contract ends 180 calendar days after the last payment to the owner (24 CFR 982.455).

## How do I avoid abatement?

Fix every owner item before the due date, and get proof to the PHA. Use this checklist the day the report arrives:

- [ ] Read the report the same day. Call the inspector if an item is unclear. Macon says the time to ask is when you get the report, not after the due date.
- [ ] Sort items into 24-hour and 30-day lists.
- [ ] Book the trades for each item.
- [ ] Ask the PHA if it accepts photos. Under 24 CFR 982.405(i), it may accept photos or other reliable proof.
- [ ] Ask for an extension in writing if a valid reason exists, such as severe weather. Macon wants the form at least 10 days before the due date.
- [ ] Ask the PHA to waive owner duty for tenant-caused damage (982.404(a)(4)).
- [ ] Check every item again yourself. Macon says a missed item on the list "is the most common source of abatement."
