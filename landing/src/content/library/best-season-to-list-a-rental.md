## Which months have the most rental demand?

The summer months have the most moves, so demand for rentals peaks from May through August. The best national data on move timing comes from the US Census Bureau. Its 2010 report "Seasonality of Moves and the Duration and Tenure of Residence: 2004" uses the Survey of Income and Program Participation (SIPP).

Here is what the Census report found:

| Month or season | Share of moves (2004 SIPP) |
|---|---|
| June | 12% |
| August | About 10% |
| July | About 10% |
| May | About 9% |
| May to August combined | 41% |
| Winter (December to February) | Lowest season for most groups |

The report gives a reason: "presumably because the weather is good and children are less likely to be in school." After August, the share of moves each month goes down.

The Census data is from 2004. The pattern still shows up in 2026 market data. Apartment List says fewer renters move in the fall and winter. Owners then "offer modest discounts to attract demand and fill vacant units at this time of year."

## How long does a rental take to lease by season?

A rental takes longer to lease in the off-season. Apartment List tracks "list-to-lease" time: the days from first listing to a signed lease on its platform.

| Month (2026) | Days from listing to lease | Note |
|---|---|---|
| January | 41 | Record high since tracking began in 2019 |
| September | 34 | Longest September since 2019 |

Apartment List also reported that the national median rent fell 0.1% in September 2026. That was the first monthly decline since January. The company calls this normal for the start of the off-season.

Each vacant day costs you rent. A simple way to see the cost:

- Monthly rent: $1,388 (the Apartment List national median, September 2026).
- Daily rent: $1,388 x 12 / 365 = about $45.63.
- 7 more vacant days in January than in September: 7 x $45.63 = about $319.

Your local numbers will differ. Run the same math with your own rent and your own past vacancy days.

## How do I move a lease end date to spring or summer?

You move a lease end date by choosing the length of the next lease. Pick a term that ends between May and August. Then each future 12-month renewal also ends in the peak season.

Use these steps at the next renewal:

1. **List each lease end date** by unit.
2. **Mark the leases** that end from September to March.
3. **Pick a target month,** for example June.
4. **Count the months** from the current end date to the target month.
5. **Offer a renewal** of that length, or that length plus 12 months.
6. **Send the offer early,** at least 60 days before the end date, so the tenant has time to decide.
7. **Put the new end date in writing** in the renewal.

This table shows the term that lands a lease in June:

| Current lease ends | Short renewal to June | Long renewal to June |
|---|---|---|
| September | 9 months | 21 months |
| October | 8 months | 20 months |
| November | 7 months | 19 months |
| December | 6 months | 18 months |
| January | 5 months | 17 months |
| February | 4 months | 16 months |

If you manage many units, do not move them all to June. Spread the end dates from May to August. Then you do not have 20 turnovers in one month.

## Should I offer a 15-month lease to shift the end date?

A 15-month lease is a good tool when a new lease starts in the off-season. A lease that starts in March and runs 15 months ends in June. You fill the unit now and move the next turnover into peak season.

Compare the options for a tenant who moves in on March 1:

| Lease length | Ends | Next turnover season |
|---|---|---|
| 12 months | End of February | Winter, slowest |
| 15 months | End of May | Late spring, strong |
| 16 months | End of June | Summer, peak |

Some tenants like a longer lease because the rent is set for longer. Some do not. Offer 2 options, for example 12 months and 15 months, and let the tenant choose.

For a month-to-month tenant, state notice rules control the end date. Florida Statute 83.57 requires at least 30 days' notice before the end of a monthly period. In California, Civil Code 827 lets a landlord change the terms of a month-to-month tenancy with at least 30 days' written notice. A rent increase over 10% needs at least 90 days' notice there. Check your own state before you send any notice.

This is general information, not legal advice. Check the current law or ask a local attorney.

## Is winter leasing always slow?

Winter leasing is slower on average, but it is not always slow. The Census report shows that some groups move more in winter. People who moved from outside the United States moved more often in the winter. Students, job transfers and people whose own lease ends in winter also need a home in January.

What helps in the off-season:

- **Price to the market.** Apartment List says owners give "modest discounts" in fall and winter. Check 3 to 5 comparable listings first.
- **Show the unit well.** Good light and a warm, clean unit matter more on a cold day.
- **Answer fast.** Fewer leads means each lead counts more.
- **Offer a term that ends in summer.** This fixes the problem for next year.
