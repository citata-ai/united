## What minimums and prices does AppFolio publish?

AppFolio publishes starting unit rates and a Core unit minimum, but not its minimum spending amounts. Read the conditions beside each price.

| Plan | Starting monthly unit rate | Public minimum information | Features shown |
|---|---|---|---|
| Core | $1.49 | 50 units; minimum spend applies | Accounting, leasing, work orders and Realm-X Assistant |
| Plus | $3.20 | Unit and spend minimums apply; values not shown | Advanced accounting, custom fields and read-only API |
| Max | $5.00 | Unit and spend minimums apply; values not shown | Leasing CRM, read/write API and dedicated success management |

Source: [AppFolio pricing](https://www.appfolio.com/pricing), read 2026-10-19. These are starting rates, not complete account quotes.

Do not substitute a remembered minimum fee for the missing amount. The exact spend condition affects whether the software fits your budget. Ask sales to state it in the offer.

The plan table is a feature and price summary. It does not establish that a feature meets every use case. Ask for a demonstration using your portfolio type.

## What is the minimum monthly fee?

AppFolio's public page says a minimum spend applies but does not state the amount. The minimum monthly fee is therefore quote only.

A unit minimum answers whether a portfolio meets an eligibility threshold. A spend minimum answers the lowest subscription amount. They are different questions.

Ask for the recurring base, required services and billing term. Confirm whether the quoted minimum includes only software or other charges. Also ask what happens if your managed door count falls.

Use this request with sales:

- **State the threshold.** Confirm the eligible unit count for the offered plan.
- **State the floor.** Show the minimum spending amount.
- **Define units.** Explain vacant, inactive and nonresidential records.
- **List extras.** Separate payment, screening, website and connection costs.
- **Show renewal terms.** State how the price can change.

A quote should let your accountant reproduce the subscription calculation. If the minimum is unclear, the budget is incomplete.

## How many units do you need to sign up?

AppFolio Core states at least 50 units on the pricing page. Plus and Max state unit minimums without showing their values.

Do not infer a Plus or Max threshold from another company's article. Ask AppFolio to confirm eligibility for your exact business and portfolio mix.

The way a vendor counts units matters. A vacant managed home may remain part of your operating portfolio. Ask how AppFolio treats that record for eligibility and billing. Get the answer in writing rather than guessing.

If your signed portfolio sits below the Core threshold, compare products that can serve you now. [Yardi Breeze's FAQ](https://www.yardibreeze.com/faq/) states no unit minimum. A monthly spending minimum still applies there.

[Buildium's pricing page](https://www.buildium.com/pricing/) shows entry plans without stating a unit minimum. Confirm eligibility directly. Do not buy based only on doors you hope to add.

Use signed management agreements to define today's portfolio. Keep forecast doors separate from the purchase case.

## What do the AppFolio plans include?

AppFolio's plans add more advanced work and connections as you move upward. Start with the tasks your staff must complete.

Core lists property accounting, leasing, work orders, inspections, communication and Realm-X Assistant and Messages. Test those functions against your common cases.

Plus adds affordable and student housing capabilities, advanced accounting, custom fields and Realm-X Flows. It also lists read-only API access. An API is a software connection; read-only access retrieves data without changing it.

Max adds a leasing CRM and read/write API access. A CRM tracks leads and contact work. Ask which records and actions the connection supports.

Do not upgrade just because a feature name sounds useful. Write the required action first. Then ask which plan and service make it possible.

Give your accountant a separate test from your leasing team. Accounting depth and leasing ease can point to different tradeoffs. Keep the results with the quote.

## Which add-ons cost extra?

AppFolio invites buyers to add value-added services, but the public pricing page does not provide a complete fee schedule. Treat unlisted amounts as quote only.

Ask about the services your business actually needs. Include online payments, screening, websites, integrations and any specialist support. Confirm whether a partner also charges a subscription.

Make a service register:

| Service | What the written offer should show |
|---|---|
| Payments | Who pays, transaction types and return fees |
| Screening | Package, payer and per-report price |
| Connections | AppFolio plan and partner charges |
| Setup | Import scope, cleanup and training |
| Support | Included channels and paid options |

Mark every unresolved amount. A blank line should mean unknown, not free. Ask for a normal invoice and the first invoice.

Separate required services from optional ones. You need a complete usable system cost, then a list of upgrades you can add later.

## What does AppFolio cost at 100 doors?

At the public Core starting rate, 100 units produce a $149 monthly rate calculation before the minimum spend. That is arithmetic, not the actual account price.

The calculation uses AppFolio's $1.49 rate, read 2026-10-19. Plus produces $320 and Max $500 from their published rates at the same assumed door count. Their unpublished eligibility and spending conditions still apply.

Do not present those figures as available offers. Ask AppFolio to confirm the plan, minimum and service bundle for your business.

Then compare the full bill with another product using the same payment volume and features. A low rate calculation is not a reason to change systems by itself.
