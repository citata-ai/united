## What gets done in month 1?

Month 1 of a property management marketing program is for setup: tracking, listings, the owner offer and follow-up. None of this is exciting. But every later decision depends on it.

Start with tracking, because you need a baseline. If you do not know how many owner leads came in last month, you cannot say if the new work helped.

Month 1 checklist:

- [ ] **Track owner forms** as a separate goal in GA4, apart from tenant forms.
- [ ] **Track calls** with a call-tracking number on the website, or ask each caller how they found you.
- [ ] **Log lead source** in your CRM for every owner lead.
- [ ] **Write down the baseline:** owner leads, signed owners and doors for the last 3 months.
- [ ] **Fix the Google Business Profile:** name, category, hours, service area, phone and website link.
- [ ] **Check your listings** on Yelp, BBB and the directories that show up for "[city] property management".
- [ ] **Make the owner offer clear** on the home page: what you manage, where, and the next step.
- [ ] **Start review requests** to current owners, in a way any happy or unhappy owner can answer.
- [ ] **Set a response rule:** every owner lead gets a call within minutes in business hours.

Why the Google Business Profile comes early: Google's own help page lists relevance, distance and prominence as the main local ranking factors. It says, "More reviews and positive ratings can help your business's local ranking." Complete profile facts help with relevance.

Why the response rule comes early: the 2007 InsideSales.com and MIT study looked at over 15,000 web leads. The odds of contact dropped 100 times from a 5-minute call to a 30-minute call. The study was not about property management. But a fast call costs nothing to start.

## What does a 12-month plan look like?

A 12-month plan moves from setup, to more owner content, to budget choices based on signed owners. Property Manager Websites (PMW) publishes a three-phase framework with the same shape. Here is our version, month by month.

| When | Focus | Main tasks | What to measure |
|---|---|---|---|
| Month 1 | Setup | Tracking, Google Business Profile, listings, owner offer, response rule | Baseline leads and response time |
| Month 2 | Owner pages | Fee page, owner services page, rental analysis form | Owner form rate |
| Month 3 | First content | 2 to 4 owner articles on fees, switching managers, local rules | Pages indexed, first search impressions |
| Month 4 to 6 | City pages and tests | One owner page per real service city; test headlines and forms; cut weak paid keywords | Owner leads by source |
| Month 7 to 9 | Depth | Answer more owner questions; refresh pages from month 3 | Leads and signed owners by source |
| Month 10 to 12 | Budget | Move budget to channels with the lowest cost per signed owner; set next year's targets | Cost per signed owner |

Only add a city page for a city you really serve. A page for a place you do not cover brings leads you must turn away.

## When should owner leads start to come?

No public study says when owner leads start after new marketing begins. We looked. PMW's framework says "most companies see early traction within 90 days," with growth by month 6 and compound results by month 12. Those are the agency's framework dates, not measured results.

Google gives a range, not a date. Its SEO Starter Guide says, "Some changes might take effect in a few hours, others could take several months." It suggests you "wait a few weeks" before you judge whether a change helped.

So expect the fast fixes to show first. A clearer owner form, a complete Google profile and a fast callback can change lead counts within weeks. Content and city pages take longer to rank. That is a reasonable order, not a promise.

## What results are fair to expect at 6 and 12 months?

A fair goal at 6 months is a clear trend against your own baseline, with each lead tagged by source. A fair goal at 12 months is cost per signed owner, by channel. We cannot give you a target number, because no public data set links months of marketing to signed owners.

What the data does show is where owner leads come from. Goodjuju's 2026 lead source report tracked 35 property management companies. Its fall update covered 2,525 owner leads.

| Channel | Share of owner leads, spring 2026 | Share of owner leads, fall 2026 |
|---|---|---|
| Google Search and Map Pack | 51.8% | 50.6% |
| Direct / Maps | 33.6% | 35.6% |
| Other search | 5.9% | 4.3% |
| Referral | 4.0% | 3.7% |
| AI tools | 2.1% | 3.4% |
| Social media | 1.6% | 0.8% |
| Paid search | 0.9% | 1.2% |

Read this with care. Goodjuju counts form submissions, not signed owners. The companies are its own clients. Only 6 of 35 ran paid search in fall, so the paid number is not a benchmark. Still, the table says one useful thing: Google Search and Maps bring most owner form leads in this sample. Month 1 work on the Google profile is not optional.

AI answers are a small share now, but they grew from 2.1% to 3.4% between spring and fall in the same report. Our guide on [how owners choose a property manager with AI](/guides/how-owners-choose-a-property-manager-with-ai) covers that channel.

## Which tasks repeat each month?

Five tasks repeat each month after setup. They keep the profile fresh, add content and keep the numbers honest.

1. **Ask for reviews.** BrightLocal's 2026 survey found 74% of consumers look for reviews from the last three months. A steady flow beats a one-time push.
2. **Reply to every review.** Google's help page says helpful replies can help your business stand out.
3. **Publish owner content.** Answer one real owner question each time. Check every fact.
4. **Report leads by source.** Owner leads, calls, signed owners and doors, each tagged by source.
5. **Check response time.** Pull 5 recent leads and check how fast someone called back.

Each quarter, refresh the oldest owner pages, check that fees and facts are still true, and compare the quarter with your month 1 baseline. If owner leads fell in spite of the work, read [why owner leads are dropping](/guides/why-owner-leads-are-dropping) before you change the plan.
