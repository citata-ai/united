## Does Dallas require single-family rental registration?

Yes. Dallas requires every rental to register each year, including single-family homes, duplexes and condo units. Dallas City Code Section 27-30 says an owner commits an offense if the owner leases a unit without a registration or renewal that complies with Section 27-31. The same rule applies to a landlord or property manager who allows the unit to be leased.

The code splits rentals into 2 types:

| Type | Definition (Dallas City Code 27-3) | Inspection rule |
|---|---|---|
| Single dwelling unit rental property | A single-family home, a duplex or a condo unit | At least once every 5 years, not more than once a year (Code Compliance) |
| Multitenant property | 3 or more single dwelling units on the same premises under common ownership, plus boarding houses, group residences and extended stay hotels | Graded inspection, not more than once a year |

For a single-family rental, Dallas Code Compliance also asks for a yearly Owner's Self Inspection Checklist with an affidavit for each unit. A new rental must register before you lease it.

Section 27-30(g) lists 4 defenses to a charge:

- **Not offered for lease.** No unit is leased or offered, and you filed an exemption affidavit.
- **Family only.** The only tenants are related to you by blood or marriage.
- **Homestead.** You had a homestead exemption on file for the single-unit property.
- **Registered short-term rental.** The property is registered under Chapter 42B and hotel taxes are paid.

## What does the Dallas registration fee cost?

The Dallas rental registration fee depends on the property type. Section 27-31 sets the amounts:

| Fee | Amount |
|---|---|
| Single dwelling unit, October 1, 2023 to September 30, 2024 | $53 |
| Single dwelling unit, October 1, 2024 to September 30, 2025 | $64 |
| Single dwelling unit, on or after October 1, 2025 | $74 |
| Multitenant property, per year | $13 × total units, occupied or not, first inspection included |
| Re-inspection, single dwelling unit | $43 |
| Re-inspection, multitenant interior | $96 per unit re-inspected |
| Re-inspection, multitenant exterior and common areas | $114 per structure |
| Re-inspection, pool | $74 per pool |
| Missing documents at a multitenant property | $146 × total units |

Source: Dallas City Code Sections 27-31 and 27-42. No fee is refunded or prorated.

The single-family fee rose 40% in 2 years, from $53 to $74. Plan for it in your yearly budget.

Worked example: you own 5 single-family homes and a 24-unit complex in Dallas. The homes cost 5 × $74 = $370. The complex costs 24 × $13 = $312. Your yearly total is $682. If one home fails its inspection, add $43 for each re-inspection.

## What does a Dallas rental inspection check?

A Dallas rental inspection checks the property against the city's minimum property standards in Chapter 27. Dallas Code Compliance says a city Code Officer inspects the interior, the exterior and the premises. The city does not limit complaint-based inspections.

The rules differ by type:

1. **Single dwelling units.** Code Compliance inspects at least once every 5 years. Section 27-42(b) also lets the director inspect at any time for public health and safety.
2. **Multitenant properties.** The director does a graded inspection, at most once a year unless public safety calls for more. You must post the certificate of inspection score in a common area. Properties with a certificate of occupancy from the past 5 years skip the graded inspection.
3. **Self-certification.** Since December 2021, high-scoring, compliant properties may self-inspect. You inspect each unit before move-in, the tenant signs the city form, and you keep the forms for 5 years after the tenant leaves.

Other duties for registered owners, from Article VII:

- **Emergency contact.** Name a person reachable 24 hours a day. You or an agent must arrive within 1 hour of an emergency call.
- **Taxes.** Do not let property taxes become delinquent.
- **Full-time manager.** Multitenant properties with 12 or more units need one.
- **Records.** Keep leases, the crime prevention addendum for multitenant leases, and a log of tenant complaints.

## What are the penalties for skipping registration in Dallas?

The penalty for a Chapter 27 offense is a fine of up to $2,000, under Dallas City Code Section 27-4. Each day a violation continues is a separate offense. The city may also bring a civil action for up to $1,000 per day under Texas Local Government Code Chapter 54.

Section 27-4(g) makes the owner liable even when an agent or employee allows the violation. So a manager's missed renewal is still your risk. Ask your manager for proof of each year's registration.

## Can Texas cities cap rent?

No, not in normal times. Texas Local Government Code Section 214.902 lets a city adopt rent control only if 2 things happen:

1. The city council finds a housing emergency due to a disaster, as defined in Government Code 418.004.
2. The governor approves the ordinance.

The city must end rent control when the governor ends the state of disaster. So Dallas can register and inspect rentals, but it cannot set your rent.
