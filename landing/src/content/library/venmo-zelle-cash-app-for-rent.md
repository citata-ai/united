## Do peer-to-peer apps allow business payments?

Each app treats business payments in its own way, and rent is a business payment for most landlords. Here is what each app's terms say, as read on 2026-10-19.

| Question | Venmo | Zelle | Cash App |
|---|---|---|---|
| Personal account for business? | Not allowed between personal accounts, unless marked goods and services | Built for people "you trust"; small business use depends on your bank | Peer-to-peer is for personal, non-commercial use |
| Business option | Business profile | Small business account at your bank | Cash App Business Account, or Earn in P2P for sole proprietors |
| Fee to receive | Personal: $0. Goods and services: 2.99%. Business profile: 1.9% + $0.10 | Typically no fee; check your bank | Business: fee shown when you open the account |
| Can the sender reverse it? | Venmo can reverse payments after a review or a purchase protection claim | No, once the recipient is enrolled | Cash App can void business payments it expects to be charged back |
| Monthly recurring rent | Not allowed as recurring goods-and-services payments through Pay and Request | Depends on your bank | Not stated in the parts quoted here |

**Venmo.** The Venmo User Agreement limits personal accounts. They "may not be used to conduct business, commercial or merchant transactions with other personal accounts", except for transactions Venmo authorizes. It also says you may not send "regularly recurring payments for goods and services" through Pay and Request, for example every month. You can receive payments for goods and services only through a business profile or when the sender marks the payment that way.

**Zelle.** The Zelle FAQ calls Zelle a way to send money "with friends, family and others you trust". It runs inside more than 2,400 bank and credit union apps. Use with a business account depends on your bank.

**Cash App.** The Cash App Terms of Service say the peer-to-peer service is for "personal, non-commercial purposes". A seller of goods or services can open a Cash App Business Account. Eligible sole proprietors can use "Earn in P2P" instead. People who do business through an LLC or another entity must use a business account.

## Can a tenant reverse a payment?

A Zelle payment cannot be reversed, while Venmo and Cash App can reverse or void some payments. That matters both for you and for your tenant.

- **Zelle.** The FAQ says payments to an enrolled user "cannot be reversed". A sender can cancel only if the recipient has not enrolled yet.
- **Venmo.** A review can lead to "money or payments you previously received being reversed". Venmo names a goods-and-services deal between personal accounts as one reason. A buyer can also file a purchase protection claim on payments marked as goods and services.
- **Cash App.** For a business account, Cash App may void a transaction it believes is likely to be charged back.

The practical risk: if you take rent on a personal Venmo account, you break the terms. If Venmo then reverses the payment, you may lose the rent.

## How do I keep records for taxes?

Record each rent payment in your own ledger, whatever app you use. Do not rely on the app's history alone.

IRS Publication 527 says rental income is "any payment you receive for the use or occupation of property". The IRS Form 1099-K page sets the app reporting line. A payment app must file Form 1099-K when goods-and-services payments exceed $20,000 in more than 200 transactions. The app may send one for less. The same page says you must report all income on your return, with or without a 1099-K.

Log these fields for every payment:

- [ ] Date received.
- [ ] Tenant name and unit.
- [ ] Rent period, for example "November 2026".
- [ ] Amount, and any fee the app took.
- [ ] App and transaction ID.
- [ ] Type: rent, late fee, deposit or other.

Security deposits need their own line. Publication 527 says you do not include a deposit in income when you plan to return it. If you keep part of it, that part is income in the year you keep it.

A worked example of app fees on 1 unit:

| Item | Amount |
|---|---|
| Monthly rent | $1,500 |
| Venmo business profile fee: 1.9% × $1,500 + $0.10 | $28.60 |
| Fees for 12 months | $343.20 |
| Venmo personal payment marked goods and services: 2.99% × $1,500 | $44.85 per month |

Fee rates come from Venmo's fee page. The rent amount is an example.

This is general information, not tax advice. Check the current law or ask a tax professional.

## What happens if the app freezes funds?

A freeze can hold rent for days or longer, and you cannot force a release. Venmo's user agreement lists what a review can lead to:

- Delayed, blocked or canceled withdrawals.
- Money held by Venmo.
- Account limits, suspension or closure.
- Payments you already received sent back to the sender.

Venmo may also ask for documents to verify your identity, and may limit access to the money until you send them. Cash App's terms say it may delay transfers to your bank while it screens for fraud or risk.

Steps to lower the risk:

1. **Use the business option.** A Venmo business profile or a Cash App Business Account matches the app's terms.
2. **Withdraw often.** Move rent to your bank account each week. Do not keep a large balance in the app.
3. **Keep a backup channel.** Name a second payment method in the lease, such as ACH or a portal.
4. **Keep your records outside the app.** Export statements each month.

## Which payment method should I offer instead?

For most rentals, a bank transfer (ACH) through a rent portal or your bank is the safer default. It gives a clear record and fits a business account.

| Method | Fits a business? | Sender can reverse? | Record quality |
|---|---|---|---|
| Rent portal with ACH | Yes | Check the portal and bank rules | Strong, tied to the lease |
| Zelle via business bank account | Depends on your bank | No | Good bank record |
| Venmo business profile | Yes, per Venmo's terms | Venmo can reverse after review | Good if exported |
| Venmo personal account | No, per Venmo's terms | Venmo can reverse after review | Weak |
| Cash App Business Account | Yes | Cash App may void likely chargebacks | Good if exported |

Offer the same payment options to every tenant. A fair process is also a Fair Housing safeguard.
