## Why can the first payment take up to 60 days?

The first payment can take up to 60 days because of one federal rule. Under 24 CFR 982.305(c), the PHA must use "best efforts" to sign the HAP contract before the lease starts. But it has until 60 calendar days after the lease start to sign it. The PHA "may not pay any housing assistance payment" until the contract is signed.

After the contract is signed, the PHA still has to process it. Each PHA runs its own payment cycle. Three PHAs show the range:

| Housing authority | What it tells owners about the first payment |
|---|---|
| Tacoma Housing Authority (WA) | "It may take 60 days to receive your first payment," but payments "are usually made sooner" |
| Housing Authority of Maricopa County (AZ) | "It may take up to 60 days for the HAP Contract to be issued and the 1st HAP payment to be released" |
| Housing Authority of the County of Santa Cruz (CA) | "Please allow two to eight weeks for your first payment" |

Tacoma also explains its cycle. It pays in the next check run after it receives the signed HAP contract. Its pay dates are on or the first business day after the 1st, 10th and 20th of each month.

If the PHA misses the 60-day window, the contract is void unless HUD approves an extension. The PHA must ask HUD within two weeks after the deadline (24 CFR 982.305(c)(4)). That case is rare, but it is a reason to follow up if you hear nothing.

## Is the first payment backdated to the move-in date?

Yes, within limits. If the PHA signs the HAP contract within the 60-day window, it pays for the time before the signing. The limit is 60 days (24 CFR 982.305(c)(3)).

PHAs start the clock on the later of two dates:

- **Tacoma:** the first payment "will include all money owed from the pass inspection or move-in date, whichever is later."
- **Maricopa County:** payments start from "the move in date OR the passed inspection date, whichever is later."

The first month is prorated. Section 7(c)(3) of the HAP contract form says the payment for the first month "shall be pro-rated for a partial month." Tacoma prorates on a 30-day month.

Here is a worked example. The numbers are for illustration only.

| Item | Value |
|---|---|
| Lease starts and family moves in | March 18 (after the unit passed on March 15) |
| Monthly HAP | $1,500 |
| March proration (Tacoma method) | 13 of 30 days × $1,500 = $650 |
| HAP contract signed | April 20 |
| First payment covers | March 18 to April 30: $650 + $1,500 = $2,150 |
| Next payment | May, on the PHA's normal date |

In this example you carry about 6 weeks of the PHA share. Budget for it before you sign.

## Can I charge a late penalty if the PHA pays late?

Not for the first payment. The HAP contract form (HUD-52641, section 7(a)(3)) allows late penalties only "after the first two calendar months of the HAP contract term." Three conditions must also be true:

1. **Local practice.** The penalty matches accepted local practice and law for tenant late fees.
2. **Same for all tenants.** You charge such penalties to assisted and unassisted tenants alike.
3. **Same for this tenant.** You also charge the tenant a late fee on the tenant share.

The PHA does not owe a penalty if HUD finds the delay was outside the PHA's control (24 CFR 982.451(b)(5)). It also does not owe one when it holds the payment as a remedy for an owner breach, such as a failed inspection.

The tenant does not owe the PHA share either. Under 24 CFR 982.310(b), a PHA's failure to pay "is not a violation of the lease," and you may not evict for it.

## What paperwork delays the first payment?

Missing owner papers delay the first payment more than anything else. Use this checklist when you return the RFTA:

- [ ] IRS Form W-9 with the TIN that matches the ownership records (Tacoma).
- [ ] Proof of ownership, such as a deed or a settlement statement (Tacoma, Maricopa).
- [ ] Direct deposit form with a voided check or bank letter (Maricopa).
- [ ] Property management agreement, if a manager receives the money (Tacoma, Maricopa).
- [ ] Signed lease with the HUD tenancy addendum, sent to the PHA (Maricopa).
- [ ] Signed HAP contract, returned the same day you get it.

Tacoma says to follow up if you sent the papers and hear nothing within 5 business days. Email the address on its FAQ and contact the housing specialist named on the RFTA.

## Does the PHA pay by direct deposit?

Most PHAs pay by direct deposit, and some require it. Maricopa County says payments are issued "once a month via Direct Deposit only." Tacoma says it prefers direct deposit for all landlords. It also calls to verify bank details to stop fraud, which "may cause a slight delay." Santa Cruz County offers direct deposit but will mail checks if you prefer. It pays by the third business day of each month.
