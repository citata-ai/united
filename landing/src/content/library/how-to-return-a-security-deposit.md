## What are the deadlines to return a security deposit?

The deadline to return a security deposit is set by state law, and it is short. Count from the day the tenant moves out or surrenders the unit, not from your inspection.

| State | Deadline | Key rule | Penalty for getting it wrong |
|---|---|---|---|
| New York | 14 days | Itemized "receipt" for any deduction | No receipt in 14 days: return the whole deposit |
| California | 21 calendar days | Itemized statement; documents and photos for repair or cleaning deductions | Bad faith: up to 2 times the deposit, plus actual damages |
| Texas | 30 days | Written description and itemized list of deductions | Bad faith: $100 + 3 times the amount wrongly withheld + attorney's fees |
| Florida | 15 days (no claim) or 30 days (notice of claim) | Notice of claim by certified mail or email; tenant has 15 days to object | No timely notice: return the deposit; landlord may sue later |

Sources: California Civil Code 1950.5(h) and (m), Texas Property Code 92.103 and 92.109, Florida Statutes 83.49(3), and the New York Attorney General's tenant guide. Other states use other deadlines. Check yours.

This is general information, not legal advice. Check the current law or ask a local attorney.

## How do I return a deposit step by step?

Follow the same steps for every move-out. Put the deadline on your calendar the day the tenant gives notice.

1. **Offer a pre-move-out inspection.** California requires you to tell the tenant in writing of the right to an initial inspection, no earlier than 2 weeks before move-out. New York lets tenants ask for one too.
2. **Inspect on move-out day.** Use your move-in checklist and photos.
3. **Take photos before work starts.** California requires photos after the tenant leaves and before repairs or cleaning, and again after the work.
4. **Get bids or receipts.** Each deduction needs a cost you can show.
5. **Remove wear and tear.** California and Texas bar deductions for normal wear and tear.
6. **Write the itemized statement.** See the sample below.
7. **Pay the balance.** Use the method the law or the lease requires.
8. **Send and keep proof.** Certified mail, email with a read receipt, or a delivery record.

## What goes in an itemized statement?

An itemized statement lists each deduction, its cost and the reason. It ends with the balance due to the tenant.

Sample, deposit $1,800:

| Item | Reason | Cost |
|---|---|---|
| Deposit received | | $1,800.00 |
| Unpaid rent, June 1–5 | Tenant held over 5 days | −$300.00 |
| Living room wall | Hole, patch and paint one wall | −$185.00 |
| Bedroom blinds | 2 broken blinds, replace | −$64.00 |
| Cleaning | Oven and fridge left dirty; invoice attached | −$120.00 |
| **Balance returned** | | **$1,131.00** |

California adds document rules in 1950.5(h)(2). Include them when repair and cleaning deductions together exceed $125:

- [ ] **Your own work:** describe it, with the time spent and a reasonable hourly rate.
- [ ] **A vendor's work:** a copy of the bill, invoice or receipt, with the vendor's name, address and phone.
- [ ] **Materials:** a copy of the bill or receipt, or a vendor price list for items you buy often.
- [ ] **Photos:** the before and after photos from 1950.5(g), by mail, email, flash drive or a link.

If a repair cannot be done within 21 days, California lets you send a good-faith estimate first. You then send the invoice within 14 days of the work.

Texas Property Code 92.104 has one exception. You do not need an itemized list if the tenant owes rent and there is no dispute about the amount.

## Where do I send it if the tenant left no address?

Each state handles a missing address in its own way:

- **California:** Send mail to the address the tenant gave. If there is none, send it to the vacated unit (1950.5(h)(6)).
- **Texas:** You do not have to refund or itemize until the tenant gives a written forwarding address (92.107). The tenant does not lose the right to the refund by failing to give one.
- **Florida:** Send the notice of claim to the tenant's last known mailing address, or by email if the tenant agreed to email notices (83.49(3)).

Ask for a forwarding address in writing at move-out in every state. Put a line for it on your move-out form.

## How do I pay it out?

Pay the balance by a method you can prove. A check by first-class mail is the default in many states.

California has a newer rule in 1950.5(h)(1)(A). If the tenant paid the deposit or rent electronically, you must return the deposit electronically to an account the tenant names in writing. You may use another method if you both agree in writing. If several adults signed the lease, follow the written agreement on how to split the refund.

Keep a copy of the check or the transfer record with the statement.

## What happens if I miss the deadline?

You can lose the right to keep any of the deposit, and you may owe a penalty. The rules differ by state:

- **New York:** If you do not provide the itemized receipt within 14 days, you "must return the entire security deposit, whether there is damage or not" (NY Attorney General).
- **Texas:** If you miss the 30 days, the law presumes bad faith (92.109(d)). Bad faith retention costs $100 plus three times the wrongly withheld amount, plus fees. Bad faith failure to itemize also forfeits your right to withhold any of the deposit.
- **California:** Bad faith retention may bring statutory damages up to twice the deposit, plus actual damages (1950.5(m)).
- **Florida:** If you do not send a timely notice of claim, you must return the deposit, though you may sue later for damages (83.49(3)).
