## What does NARPM membership include?

NARPM membership gives you education at member rates, a national and a state conference, a member magazine and a code of ethics you can show owners. NARPM is the National Association of Residential Property Managers. It was founded in October 1988 and has more than 6,000 members, by its own About page.

NARPM calls itself the only national group that focuses mainly on residential property management. That focus is the main reason to join. Most real estate groups serve sales agents first.

What the join page lists:

- **Education:** classes at discounted member rates.
- **Residential Resource:** the member news magazine, included in dues.
- **Legislative alerts:** updates on laws that affect managers.
- **Events:** access to the national convention and to state conferences that local chapters run.
- **Ethics:** every new member must finish the NARPM Code of Ethics course within 90 days. The course is free for new members.

NARPM offers four kinds of membership:

| Type | Who it is for | Dues (NARPM, 2026-10-19) |
|---|---|---|
| NARPM member | People who manage residential property for others, licensed where required | $320 a year, $270 in year 1 |
| International member | Professional members who work outside the US | $320 a year, $270 in year 1 |
| Company membership | Companies with 4 or more members | $760 for 4 members, $125 per extra member |
| National affiliate | Vendors who sell to the industry | Not shown on the pages we read |

A full year of dues is due when you join. NARPM prorates the second year to match the partial first year. Dues are due on January 1 after you join, and they are not refundable.

## What does NARPM cost for a team?

For a team of 4 or more, company membership costs less than single dues. Here is the math from NARPM's own prices.

| Team size | Single dues at $320 | Company membership | You save |
|---|---|---|---|
| 4 people | $1,280 | $760 | $520 |
| 6 people | $1,920 | $1,010 | $910 |
| 10 people | $3,200 | $1,510 | $1,690 |

Company membership for 6 people is $760 plus 2 × $125, or $1,010. For 10 people it is $760 plus 6 × $125, or $1,510. Local chapter dues may come on top. The pages we read do not list them, so ask your chapter.

## What do local chapters offer?

Local chapters are where most of the value of NARPM sits. A chapter holds regular meetings for managers in one city or region. You meet peers who face the same local laws, the same courts and the same vendors.

Chapters also run state conferences, by NARPM's join page. In practice, three things come from a chapter:

- **Referrals.** A manager who does not take a property type, or works in a different area, can send that owner to you.
- **Local answers.** A peer can tell you how the local court handles evictions this year or which vendor is reliable.
- **Vendor contacts.** Affiliate members sell services to managers. You see many of them in one room.

NARPM has a chapter finder on its site. Go to one meeting as a guest before you pay, if the chapter allows it.

## Which NARPM designations exist?

NARPM has four designations. You must be a national member to apply for each. Anyone, member or not, may take the RMP and MPM classes.

| Designation | For | Key requirement (NARPM pages) |
|---|---|---|
| RMP (Residential Management Professional) | A person | Manage 100 units over a 2-year period |
| MPM (Master Property Manager) | A person | Hold the RMP; manage 500 units over a 5-year period |
| CRMC (Certified Residential Management Company) | A company | An MPM on staff; 500 unit-years of experience; pass an audit |
| CSS (Certified Support Specialist) | Support staff | Work for a firm with an RMP or MPM; options in maintenance and bookkeeping |

A unit-year is one residential unit managed for one year. So a firm that manages 100 units for 5 years has 500 unit-years.

The CRMC audit must finish within 3 years of the application. The company renews each year and recertifies every 3 years. If you ask for an on-site audit, you pay an application fee and the auditor's costs. A virtual audit is also an option.

Designations matter more now that owners ask AI tools to compare local managers. A designation is a fact that a third party checks. Third-party facts are the kind of proof an AI answer can repeat. ## What is NARPM membership worth?

NARPM membership is worth it if one referral or one avoided mistake a year pays for the dues. We found no NARPM survey on member value, so test it with your own numbers.

A break-even test (example input, not NARPM data):

1. **Dues:** $270 in year 1, $320 after.
2. **Value of one new door:** say you earn $150 a month per door. One door for 12 months is $1,800.
3. **Break-even:** one referred door covers dues more than 5 times over.
4. **Time cost:** count the hours for chapter meetings. At 2 hours a month, that is 24 hours a year.
5. **Decide:** if the chapter is active and you will attend, join. If you will not go to meetings, the dues buy you little more than the magazine.

Watch for one risk. Membership alone does not prove skill. Owners care about results: days vacant, repair speed, clear statements. Show those on your site next to the NARPM logo. For how AI search reads a manager's site, see [how we help property managers in AI search](/property-management).

NARPM's application says dues may be deductible as a business expense. This is general information, not tax advice. Check the current law or ask a tax professional.
