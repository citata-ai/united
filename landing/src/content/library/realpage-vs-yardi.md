## What modules does each vendor sell?

Compare specific products and modules, not RealPage and Yardi as undivided brands. The proposal must name which capabilities your team will actually receive.

| Platform | Vendor-described core | Price on reviewed enterprise pages | Main evaluation task |
|---|---|---|---|
| RealPage OneSite | Leasing, maintenance, accounting, and operations | Quote only | Trace work across the proposed modules |
| Yardi Voyager Residential | Leasing, accounting, and property operations | Quote only | Check the ledger and multi-entity reports |

Sources: RealPage OneSite and Yardi Voyager Residential pages, read on 2026-10-19. The table does not claim every adjacent service is included in the base contract.

List required resident, accounting, maintenance, procurement, and reporting work before asking for a proposal. Mark each item as included, separately licensed, integrated, or unsupported.

Ask for a demonstration using the proposed package. A presentation of a broader suite can hide a module that is absent from your quote.

Keep a record of product names and versions. Enterprise vendors can offer different paths for different markets, and a reference customer may use another setup.

## How is enterprise pricing set?

Enterprise pricing needs a written scope covering software, services, and the deployment project. The reviewed pages do not support a reliable all-in public price comparison.

Give both vendors the same inventory. Include units, properties, entities, staff roles, banks, required modules, interfaces, and reporting needs. Explain the work that currently happens outside your main system.

Separate recurring subscription from migration, training, consulting, and custom reports. Ask how the price changes as properties enter or leave the portfolio.

Confirm transaction and partner costs. A subscription proposal can look complete while payment processing, screening, document services, or another vendor adds separate charges.

Ask which project assumptions control the price. Poor source data, custom fields, or an added integration may change the work. Put the change process and approval owner in the proposal.

Do not compare discounts before scope matches. A lower total can reflect fewer modules or less support rather than better value.

Have finance review the full contract term and expected ongoing work. Keep the quote date and accepted requirements together.

## Which has better accounting?

The better accounting platform is the one your team can close, explain, and control under your operating model. Vendor feature claims alone cannot decide that.

Yardi's Voyager Residential page describes a general ledger with receivables, payables, and reporting across entities. RealPage's OneSite page presents accounting within a broader operating platform. Test the required outputs in each.

Give both vendors the same closed-period sample. Include receipts, bills, transfers, corrections, opening balances, and ownership structure. Ask your accountants to define the expected result in advance.

Check who can post, approve, reverse, and reopen records. Inspect the change history and the process for investigating a difference. A correct total in a demo is only a starting point.

Review the report path from summary to source. Staff should be able to explain an amount without sending a series of private spreadsheets between departments.

Test the interface with your other financial systems. Decide which ledger owns each record and who checks the handoff. A connected suite still needs a clear accounting design.

## How long does implementation take?

Implementation takes a project-specific schedule, with data review and staff time built in. Do not plan go-live from a general sales promise alone.

Yardi's full-service implementation material says core functionality averages 3 to 6 months. That is a vendor planning reference, not a promise for every contract or portfolio.

The same material describes client participation in data mapping, validation, and issue resolution. That work needs assigned people in your business, not just a vendor project manager.

Ask RealPage and Yardi for a schedule tied to your proposed scope. Name dependencies, required exports, conversion checks, training, and acceptance points. Keep later modules distinct from the initial go-live.

Run a sample conversion early. Compare record counts, balances, lease dates, documents, and permissions with the source. Resolve the gaps before repeating the process across the portfolio.

Do not choose a cutover date before the required close and user tests pass. Keep a fallback process for critical work and a named person who can delay go-live.

## Which suits a large multifamily portfolio?

Choose by complexity and operating structure as well as unit count. A large centralized operator and a company managing separate owners can need different controls.

Review the hardest tasks first. These may include multi-entity reports, acquisitions, specialized housing records, procurement approvals, or cross-property staffing. Require each vendor to show the proposed solution.

Ask reference customers about the exact modules you plan to buy. A positive reference from another market or configuration may not answer your key question.

Use a shared scorecard:

- **Confirm scope:** Link each required task to a quoted module or documented interface.
- **Check the close:** Have accounting approve reports, corrections, and access controls.
- **Test operations:** Let leasing and maintenance staff run their own sample tasks.
- **Test conversion:** Compare imported records with source evidence and known totals.
- **Assign staff:** Name internal owners for data, decisions, training, and support.
- **Price the project:** Include ongoing software and deployment work under the same assumptions.
- **Set acceptance:** Record the evidence required before live processing starts.
- **Plan the exit:** Confirm usable exports and access to historical records.

Keep unresolved items visible when the sales process ends. The team that will operate the platform should approve the final task results.
