On November 10, 2026, OpenAI opened Business Hub in beta. It is a free place to claim your company in ChatGPT. OpenAI says ChatGPT has 1.2 billion weekly users (OpenAI, 2026-11-10). Some of them are rental owners who ask it to name a property manager.

This guide gives the steps, the limits for a local company, and what the dashboard can and cannot tell you. Every rule below comes from OpenAI's announcement and help page, read on 2026-11-12. Business Hub is a beta, so check the help page again before you start.

## What is Business Hub in ChatGPT?

Business Hub is OpenAI's free tool to claim a business profile in ChatGPT. After you claim and verify your company, you can do 3 things (OpenAI Help Center):

- **Edit your details.** Website, description, service category, logo, cover photo and policies.
- **See insights.** Mentions, website clicks and discovery topics for the past 28 days.
- **Add people.** Give teammates or your agency access with their own ChatGPT accounts.

People who see your company in an answer can click through to its ChatGPT profile. A verified profile gets a blue check.

Business Hub also offers Business Agents, a chat that answers questions for a business. For now, OpenAI offers them only to eligible ecommerce and online service businesses in the US. Most property management companies sell a local service, so check the Business Agents Policy before you plan on one.

## Can a property management company claim its listing?

A property management company can claim its listing if it owns or represents the company and the listing is in Business Hub. The local rule is strict. OpenAI's help page says: "Local stores and restaurants can currently claim only an existing U.S. listing." The option to add a new business shows only for eligible online businesses. A property manager with a local office is closer to a local store than to an online business, so plan for this limit.

So search first. If ChatGPT has no listing for you, Business Hub cannot fix that today. Fix the sources that ChatGPT reads instead (see the last section).

Each office is a separate claim. OpenAI says claiming the main brand does not claim its locations. Central management across brands and locations is not available yet.

## How to claim your company in ChatGPT: 6 steps

1. **Open Business Hub.** Sign in to ChatGPT and open Business Hub from the sidebar. Use an account your company controls, not a staff member's private account.
2. **Find your listing.** Search for your company by name. Pick the listing with your address and confirm the account that will manage it.
3. **Verify your claim.** Pick one method: a code by SMS or call to the business phone, a code to the business email, or a TXT record in your domain's DNS. Copy the DNS type, host and value exactly.
4. **Wait for approval.** OpenAI reviews the claim and sends its decision by email.
5. **Pass Persona.** Persona is the identity check OpenAI uses. You may need a government ID, business registration data and proof that you can act for the company. Make the name, address and tax number match your official records.
6. **Fix your details.** Open your profile and update the website, description and service category. OpenAI reviews each change before it shows.

Watch for: "Contact support" in step 2 means the listing is locked. If another person already claimed it, open the profile, select the ••• menu and pick "Dispute ownership".

What to do: give step 3 to the person who runs your DNS or your phone line. Ask them before you start, so the code does not expire.

## What do mentions and clicks mean in Business Hub?

Business Hub counts a mention differently for a local business. For a local business, a mention counts when ChatGPT shows a result for that location. It counts even if the person does not open the listing or visit your site. For an online business, only a reference to its website counts (OpenAI Help Center).

The 3 insights are:

| Insight | What it shows | Limit |
|---|---|---|
| Mentions | Times ChatGPT showed your location in 28 days | Aggregate only, no prompts |
| Website clicks | Clicks from ChatGPT to your site | Can be incomplete |
| Discovery topics | Themes in answers that named or cited you | Not single questions, not intent |

A blank number does not mean zero. OpenAI says a blank metric can mean the activity is under a reporting threshold, or the data is not ready. Small offices will see blanks at first.

These numbers are new and free. Before 2026-11-10, a property manager could only guess how often ChatGPT named it. Now you can compare the mention count with the leads your team writes down as "found us on ChatGPT". ## What claiming does not fix

Claiming does not make ChatGPT recommend you. OpenAI's help page says a claim "doesn't guarantee increased visibility or give you direct control over ChatGPT's independent recommendations." It also says ads do not change those answers.

ChatGPT also reads other sources. OpenAI says "ChatGPT may also use other sources when answering questions about your business." For property managers, those sources are mostly directories.

Our own data shows this. On 2026-10-19 we asked "What is the best property management company in <City, ST>? Give names." for the 50 largest US cities. The ChatGPT engine (OpenAI API with web search) linked a listing or review site in 48 of 50 answers. It linked a property manager's own site in only 15 of 50 (our data, [AI Property Manager Index 2026](/research/ai-property-manager-index-2026)). The test used the API, not the ChatGPT app, so treat it as a direction.

So a claimed profile is one source among many. If Expertise.com or Yelp shows an old phone number, ChatGPT can still repeat it.

## What to do after you claim

Do these 4 things in the same week:

1. **Match every listing.** Use the same name, address, phone and website on your site, Google Business Profile, Bing Places, Yelp and the BBB. Our list of the [directories AI cites for property managers](/guides/directories-ai-cites-property-management) shows which ones to fix first.
2. **Write a plain description.** Say what you manage, where and for whom: "We manage single-family homes and small multifamily in Cook County." Use the words owners use.
3. **Record a baseline.** Note the first mention and click counts with the date. Check again after 28 days.
4. **Ask the owner question yourself.** Ask ChatGPT "Who is the best property management company in <your city>?" and write down the names and links.
