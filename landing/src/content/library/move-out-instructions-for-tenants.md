## When should I send move-out instructions?

Send move-out instructions as soon as either side gives notice, or about 30 days before the lease ends. Thirty days gives the tenant time to clean, patch small holes and book movers. A letter sent 2 days before move-out arrives too late to change anything.

The timing also fits state deadlines. In California, Civil Code 1950.5(f) says the landlord must offer an initial inspection after either side gives notice. That inspection can happen no earlier than 2 weeks before the tenancy ends. The move-out letter is a good place to make the offer.

Send the letter in writing, by the method your lease names. Keep a copy with the date you sent it.

## What should the move-out letter include?

A move-out letter should answer 8 questions before the tenant asks them. Use this list as your template:

1. **The end date and time.** For example: "Return possession by 12:00 p.m. on November 30."
2. **The cleaning standard.** State the standard in one line, then give the room-by-room list below.
3. **Repairs.** Ask the tenant to fix damage they caused, or to tell you about it now.
4. **Trash and items left behind.** Say where trash goes and that the unit must be empty.
5. **Utilities.** Say which utilities the tenant must transfer or close, and on what date.
6. **Keys.** List every key, fob, remote and card, and how to return them.
7. **The final walk-through.** Offer a time and say the tenant may attend.
8. **The deposit.** Ask for a written forwarding address and state your state's return deadline.

Keep the letter to 1 or 2 pages. Add the room checklist as a second page.

## What cleaning standard do I ask for?

Ask for the unit to be as clean as it was at move-in, minus ordinary wear and tear. California writes this into law. Civil Code 1950.5(b) allows cleaning charges only to return the unit to "the same level of cleanliness it was in at the inception of the tenancy."

That is why your move-in record matters. Photos and a signed move-in form show what "clean" meant on day 1. Arizona requires a move-in form: A.R.S. 33-1321 says the landlord must give the tenant a form to list existing damage. California now requires photos for tenancies that start on or after July 1, 2025.

Give the tenant a room-by-room checklist:

- [ ] **Kitchen.** Fridge empty, cleaned and wiped. Oven and stovetop free of grease. Cabinets and drawers empty.
- [ ] **Bathrooms.** Toilet, tub, shower and sink clean. Mirror wiped. No hair in drains.
- [ ] **Floors.** Carpets vacuumed. Hard floors swept and mopped.
- [ ] **Walls.** Tape, hooks and nails removed. Marks wiped where possible.
- [ ] **Windows.** Sills and tracks wiped. Blinds dusted.
- [ ] **Closets and storage.** Empty, including garage, shed and storage units.
- [ ] **Outside.** Trash out to the right place. No items left in the yard.
- [ ] **Small items.** Working light bulbs and smoke alarm batteries, if the lease makes these the tenant's job.

Do not ask for more than the move-in level. A demand for a "professional deep clean" of a unit that was not deep-cleaned at move-in can lead to a dispute.

## How do tenants return keys?

Tenants return keys by the one method you name in writing. Pick one of these 3 and say it clearly:

| Method | Good for | Watch for |
|---|---|---|
| Hand over at the walk-through | Local tenants who attend | You must both be there at the same time |
| Drop box or locked mailbox at the office | Tenants who leave early | Ask for a text or email when they drop the keys |
| Mail to the office | Tenants who already left town | Ask for tracking, and treat the delivery date as the return date |

List every item on the key list: door keys, mailbox keys, garage remotes, gate fobs and parking cards. Ask the tenant to count them against the move-in list.

Change or rekey the locks after move-out, before the next tenant gets keys. Check your state and city rules on locks.

## How do I get a forwarding address?

You get a forwarding address by asking for it in writing, in the move-out letter, with a reason. Tell the tenant that the address is where the deposit and the list of deductions will go.

State law shows why this matters:

| State | Deadline to return the deposit or give notice | Address rule | Statute |
|---|---|---|---|
| California | 21 calendar days after the tenant vacates, with an itemized statement | Mail goes to the address the tenant provides, or to the vacated unit if none | Civil Code 1950.5 |
| Texas | On or before the 30th day after the tenant surrenders the premises | Landlord need not refund until the tenant gives a written forwarding address | Property Code 92.103, 92.107 |
| Florida | 15 days with no claim; 30 days to give written notice of a claim | Notice goes by certified mail to the last known mailing address, or by email under 83.505 | Fla. Stat. 83.49(3) |
| Arizona | 14 days, excluding weekends and legal holidays, with an itemized list | Starts after possession returns and the tenant demands the deposit | A.R.S. 33-1321 |

Florida has a sharp penalty. If the landlord does not send timely notice of a claim, the landlord loses the right to claim against the deposit. A good address on file protects you as much as the tenant.

Texas adds a rule on the last month's rent. Property Code 92.108 says a tenant may not withhold the last month's rent because the deposit covers it. You can say so in the letter if you rent in Texas.

This is general information, not legal advice. Check the current law or ask a local attorney.

## What do I do after the tenant leaves?

After the tenant leaves, inspect, document and send the deposit result before your state's deadline. Use these steps:

1. **Walk the unit** with the move-in form and photos in hand.
2. **Take dated photos** of every room. California now requires photos after move-out and again after repairs.
3. **Price each deduction** with a bill or quote. California requires copies of receipts with the itemized statement.
4. **Send the deposit or the itemized list** to the forwarding address.
5. **Keep the file** with the letter, photos, receipts and proof of mailing.
