## What leasing and renewal fees do Phoenix managers charge?

Half a month's rent is the most common Phoenix leasing fee: 5 of the 13 pages list it. The rest spread widely:

- **A flat fee:** $499 plus a $199 marketing fee (Zen Rent), $599 (Stratton Vantage), $400 or $600 by plan (RPM Phoenix Valley), $800 (Real Estate Brokers of Arizona), $1,500 (Ziprent).
- **A share of the lease:** Bullseye charges 4% of the annual lease, with a $500 minimum. On a $1,750 rent, that is $840, about 48% of a month.
- **One month:** RPM East Valley's placement-only service.

Renewal fees on the pages that list one run from $125 to $350. PURE includes renewals in its management fee, and so does Zen Rent's Premium plan.

## Which local rules add cost for Phoenix owners?

Two Arizona rules changed the cost picture for Phoenix owners:

- **No more city rental tax.** From 2025-01-01, owners no longer collect or pay city transaction privilege tax on residential rentals of 30 days or more (Arizona Department of Revenue). That line on a long-term lease budget is now $0.
- **County registration still applies.** ARS 33-1902 requires owners to keep owner and property data on file with the Maricopa County Assessor. Skipping it can cost $150 a day, or $1,000 plus $100 a month for a newly bought rental, unless you comply within 10 days of notice. RPM Phoenix Valley lists this registration among its included services.

Rents are the other side of the budget. HUD's FY2027 fair market rent for a Phoenix-area 2-bedroom fell 5.7%, from $1,839 to $1,734. A percent fee falls with rent, a flat fee does not. See [Phoenix fair market rent 2027](/guides/section-8-rent-limits/phoenix-fair-market-rent-2027).

This is general information, not legal or tax advice. Check the current law, or ask a local attorney or tax professional.

## Must a Phoenix property manager hold an Arizona license?

Yes. ARS 32-2101 defines a real estate broker as a person who, for another and for pay, rents or leases real estate, offers to, or collects rent for its use. ARS 32-2122 makes it unlawful for a person or company to do that work without a license from the Arizona Department of Real Estate (ADRE).

Arizona also sets what your management agreement must say. Under ARS 32-2173, it must:

- [ ] State all material terms of the firm's services and duties.
- [ ] Be signed by you and the firm's designated broker or an authorized licensee.
- [ ] Give a start date and an end date.
- [ ] Contain cancellation terms both sides agree to.
- [ ] Say how the firm handles all money it collects, including tenant deposits.
- [ ] Set the type and frequency of reports to you.
- [ ] State the amount and purpose of any operating reserve.
- [ ] Say who gets interest earned on trust account money.
- [ ] State your fees.
- [ ] Bar assignment to another firm without your written consent.

An auto-renewal clause is allowed only if the firm sends a reminder at least 30 days before the renewal date. Use this list when you compare the fee pages above: a firm with low fees and a weak contract can cost you more.
