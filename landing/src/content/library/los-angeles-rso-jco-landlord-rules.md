## How do I know if my Los Angeles rental is under the RSO?

Your Los Angeles rental is likely under the RSO if the building was first built on or before October 1, 1978 and it is inside the City of Los Angeles. LAHD lists these covered types (LAHD, RSO overview):

- Apartments and duplexes.
- 2 or more single-family homes on the same parcel.
- Condos and townhomes. But the rent amount is not regulated for tenancies that started after December 31, 1995.
- Hotel, motel or rooming house rooms with the same tenant for more than 30 days in a row.
- Homes attached to a commercial building.
- ADUs and junior ADUs, and replacement units under LAMC 151.28.

A single-family home alone on its lot is not on this list. It may still fall under the JCO and state law, as the next sections show.

How to check fast:

1. **Find the year built** on your county property record.
2. **Confirm the address is in the City of LA,** not an unincorporated area or another city like Santa Monica.
3. **Look at your yearly LAHD bill.** If LAHD bills you an RSO fee, LAHD treats the unit as RSO.

Every RSO landlord must serve each tenant with the yearly registration statement, also called the certificate (LAHD, RSO overview).

## What is the yearly RSO rent increase?

The yearly RSO rent increase for July 1, 2026 through June 30, 2027 is 3% (LAHD Allowable Rent Increase Bulletin). The rate now runs from a floor of 1% to a ceiling of 4%, based on the CPI average for the 12 months ending September 30. You can apply it once every 12 months. It is not cumulative: you cannot bank a skipped year.

Two changes started on February 2, 2026 (LAHD bulletin):

- **No utility add-on.** The yearly increase can no longer include an extra percent for utilities.
- **No dependent add-on.** You cannot raise the rent when a dependent joins the household. For a new tenant who is not a dependent, you may still add 10%, if you give notice within 60 days of learning about that tenant.

Worked example for one RSO unit:

| Item | Amount |
|---|---|
| Current rent | $2,000 |
| 2026–27 increase at 3% | $60 |
| New rent after notice | $2,060 |
| Deposit may also rise by 3% | on a $2,000 deposit, $60 |

The notice rule: LAHD's bulletin says to give 30 days' written notice for an increase of less than 10% in 12 months, and 90 days for more than 10%.

## What does the LA Just Cause Ordinance cover?

The LA Just Cause Ordinance (JCO) covers most residential rentals in the City of Los Angeles that are not under the RSO (LAHD, JCO page). It bans ending a tenancy without one of the listed reasons. It also requires relocation pay for no-fault evictions.

Key points from LAHD:

- **When it starts:** once the tenant has lived in the unit 6 months, or the first lease has expired, whichever comes first.
- **Newer buildings:** the JCO can apply to buildings built after October 1, 1978.
- **Single-family homes:** the JCO can apply to a property with only one single-family home.
- **Exceptions:** transient hotels, licensed care homes, fraternity and sorority houses, an owner's roommate, some co-ops, some non-profit and government housing.
- **Rent:** the JCO does not cap rent. State law AB 1482 may cap it for buildings older than 15 years, at 5% plus regional CPI, or 10%, whichever is lower.

Two rules apply to both RSO and JCO units:

1. **The FMR threshold for nonpayment.** Since March 27, 2023, you cannot evict for unpaid rent unless the tenant owes more than one month of HUD's fair market rent for that unit size. For FY2027, a Los Angeles-area 2-bedroom FMR is $2,964. See [Los Angeles fair market rent 2027](/guides/section-8-rent-limits/los-angeles-fair-market-rent-2027) for each size.
2. **File every eviction notice.** You must file a copy with LAHD within 3 business days of serving it.

## What is the LAHD Rent Registry and who must register?

The LAHD Rent Registry is the yearly report of the rent and tenancy data for each unit. Landlords must file it by the last day of February each year, along with the yearly registration fee (LAHD, Rent Registry). Registration is complete only when you pay all fees and give all rent, tenancy and emergency contact data.

You can file on the paper form that comes with the yearly RSO, JCO and SCEP bill. Or file online at HousingBill.LAcity.org, which also offers a CSV upload for large portfolios. An owner who lives in one unit still files, and can claim a yearly temporary exemption for that unit.

The 3 yearly fees (LAHD bulletin):

| Fee | Applies to | Per unit | Tenant share |
|---|---|---|---|
| RSO registration | RSO units | $38.75 | 50%, as $1.61 a month |
| JCO registration | Non-RSO units | $31.05 | Not stated in the bulletin |
| SCEP (code enforcement) | Rental units | $67.94 | 50%, as $2.83 a month |

Worked example, a 4-unit RSO building: 4 × ($38.75 + $67.94) = $426.76 a year. If you collect both surcharges from all 4 tenants for 12 months, you get back 4 × ($1.61 + $2.83) × 12 = $213.12. Give 30 days' notice before you start the $1.61 surcharge (LAHD, RSO overview).

## When does an LA owner pay relocation assistance?

An LA owner pays relocation assistance for every no-fault eviction from an RSO or JCO unit (LAHD Relocation Assistance Bulletin A). No-fault reasons include move-in by the owner or close family, Ellis Act withdrawal, demolition, a government order to vacate, and condo conversion. You must file a Declaration of Intent to Evict with LAHD before you serve the notice.

Chart A, effective July 1, 2026 through June 30, 2027:

| Household | Under 3 years | 3+ years, or low income (80% AMI or less) | Mom and Pop, owner or family move-in only |
|---|---|---|---|
| Eligible | $11,000 | $14,400 | $10,550 |
| Qualified (62+, disabled, or with minor children) | $23,150 | $27,400 | $21,250 |

For a single-family home under the JCO, owned by a natural person with no more than 4 units and one home in the city, the amount is one month's rent (LAMC 165.06.A.6). LAHD pays these amounts per unit, not per tenant. When 2 tenants share a unit, each gets an equal share.

Payment timing: make the money available within 15 days of serving the notice. You may use an escrow account instead, but you must fund it within those 15 days.

Checklist before a no-fault move-out:

- [ ] Confirm the reason is on the LAHD no-fault list.
- [ ] File the Declaration of Intent to Evict with LAHD.
- [ ] Check the tenant's age, disability, children and income to pick the right column.
- [ ] Serve the notice, then file a copy with LAHD within 3 business days.
- [ ] Pay or escrow the relocation amount within 15 days.
