Louisiana's deposit law is 4 short sections: La. R.S. 9:3251 to 9:3254. I read them on the Louisiana State Legislature site on 2026-10-19. The history line of R.S. 9:3251 now lists Acts 2026, No. 63. That act changed the deadline for the itemized statement.

| Rule | Limit or deadline | Statute |
|---|---|---|
| Maximum deposit | None set | R.S. 9:3251 |
| Return deadline | Within 1 month after the lease ends | R.S. 9:3251(A) |
| Lawful deductions | Cure a tenant default or unreasonable wear | R.S. 9:3251(A) |
| Itemized statement | Within 1 month, or within 15 more days (Acts 2026, No. 63) | R.S. 9:3251(A) |
| Forwarding address | Tenant must give one at lease end | R.S. 9:3251(A) |
| Sale of the property | Transfer deposits to the buyer | R.S. 9:3251(B) |
| Tenant abandons | Return rule does not apply | R.S. 9:3251(C) |
| Willful failure | Amount kept + $300 or 2x, whichever is greater | R.S. 9:3252(A) |
| No refund 30 days after written demand | Counts as willful | R.S. 9:3252(A) |
| Attorney fees | Court may award to the prevailing party | R.S. 9:3253 |
| Waiver | Void | R.S. 9:3254 |

## What is the maximum security deposit a landlord can charge in Louisiana?

Louisiana sets no maximum security deposit. R.S. 9:3251 to 9:3254 have no cap, and we found no other Louisiana statute with one.

The law covers "any advance or deposit of money" given to secure "any part of a written or oral lease" (R.S. 9:3251(A)). So the rules apply whatever you call the payment.

## How many days does a Louisiana landlord have to return a security deposit?

A Louisiana landlord returns the deposit "within one month after the lease shall terminate" (R.S. 9:3251(A)).

If the landlord keeps any part, the itemized statement may go out "within one month after the date the tenancy terminates or within fifteen days after the date that is one month after the tenancy terminates." This wording came with Acts 2026, No. 63 (HB 292), effective August 1, 2026, per the official digest.

The statute ties the extra 15 days to the itemized statement. It does not say in plain words that the refund of the balance moves too. To be safe, pay any balance within 1 month, and use the extra 15 days only for the final statement.

Worked timeline:

| Event | Date |
|---|---|
| Lease ends | June 30 |
| 1 month after | July 30 |
| Return balance by | July 30 |
| Latest date for itemized statement | August 14 |

The tenant must give a forwarding address at the end of the lease (R.S. 9:3251(A)). Ask for it in writing at move-out.

## What can a landlord deduct from a security deposit in Louisiana?

A Louisiana landlord may keep "all or any portion" of the deposit "which is reasonably necessary to remedy a default of the tenant or to remedy unreasonable wear to the premises" (R.S. 9:3251(A)).

That gives 2 kinds of deduction:

- **Tenant default:** unpaid rent and other unpaid sums due under the lease.
- **Unreasonable wear:** damage beyond what normal use causes.

Normal wear, such as light carpet wear or faded paint from living there, is not a deduction. A burned counter or broken door is.

## Does a Louisiana landlord have to give an itemized list of deductions?

Yes. If the landlord keeps any part of the deposit, the landlord "shall forward to the tenant" an "itemized statement accounting for the proceeds which are retained and giving the reasons therefor" (R.S. 9:3251(A)).

A sound statement lists:

| Item | Reason | Amount |
|---|---|---|
| Unpaid rent, June | Tenant default | $950 |
| Replace broken bedroom door | Unreasonable wear | $240 |
| Total kept | | $1,190 |
| Balance returned | | $310 |

Send it to the forwarding address the tenant gave. Keep a copy and proof of mailing.

## Must a Louisiana landlord pay interest or keep the deposit in a separate account?

R.S. 9:3251 to 9:3254 do not require interest or a separate account. We found no such rule in those sections.

When the property is sold during a lease, the seller "shall also transfer to his successor in interest the sum deposited," and is then relieved of further liability (R.S. 9:3251(B)). The buyer returns the deposit at the end of the lease.

## What is the penalty for a Louisiana landlord who keeps a deposit wrongly?

The penalty applies to "willful failure to comply" with R.S. 9:3251. The tenant may recover the part of the deposit wrongly kept "and three hundred dollars or twice the amount of the portion of the security deposit wrongfully retained, whichever is greater" (R.S. 9:3252(A)).

"Failure to remit within thirty days after written demand for a refund shall constitute willful failure." So a tenant's written demand starts a 30-day clock.

Worked example: deposit $1,000, landlord keeps $400 with no lawful reason.

| Item | Amount |
|---|---|
| Amount wrongly kept | $400 |
| Penalty: greater of $300 or 2 x $400 | $800 |
| Total | $1,200 |

The tenant may sue in the parish of the landlord's domicile or where the property is (R.S. 9:3252(B)). The court may award costs and attorney fees to the prevailing party (R.S. 9:3253). A tenant cannot waive these rights in the lease (R.S. 9:3254).

## Louisiana deposit checklist

- Write the deposit amount and its purpose in the lease.
- Photograph the unit at move-in and move-out.
- Ask for a forwarding address in writing at move-out.
- Return the balance within 1 month after the lease ends.
- Send the itemized statement within 1 month, or 15 days after that.
- Answer any written demand within 30 days.
