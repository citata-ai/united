## ChatGPT vs Google: the numbers side by side

Google gives property managers far more owner leads than ChatGPT, but ChatGPT leads convert better. The best public data comes from Goodjuju Marketing. Its fall 2026 update tracks the same 35 property management companies over two 90-day windows.

| Measure (fall 2026, July 8 to October 5) | Google Search and Map Pack | All AI tools | ChatGPT only |
|---|---|---|---|
| Owner leads | 639 | 43 | 37 (client data) |
| Share of owner leads | 50.6% | 3.4% | not split |
| Share of site traffic | 37.9% | 0.8% | not split |
| Visitor-to-lead rate | 1.2% | 3.9% | not split |
| Change since spring 2026 | lead share 51.8% to 50.6% | lead share 2.1% to 3.4% | not split |

Source: Goodjuju Marketing lead source study, read 2026-10-19. "Direct and Maps" is a separate line in the study and gave another 35.6% of fall leads.

What the table says:

- **Volume:** Google gave about 15 times more owner leads than all AI tools together (639 vs 43).
- **Quality:** an AI visitor was about 3 times more likely to become a lead.
- **Direction:** the Google share held steady. The AI share grew.

## What share of owner leads comes from AI search?

AI search gave 3.4% of owner leads in fall 2026, in a sample of 35 companies that all use one SEO agency. The study says these companies may do better than the industry average. Treat 3.4% as one data point, not a market rate.

Inside AI, ChatGPT dominates. Of 43 AI leads in client data, ChatGPT gave 37, Gemini 5 and Perplexity 1.

## Do AI leads convert better than Google leads?

AI leads convert better in the data we have. Fall 2026 AI visitors became owner leads at 3.9%. Google Search and Map Pack visitors did so at 1.2%.

There is a plain reason. A landlord who asks ChatGPT "who is the best property manager for a duplex in my city" gets a short list. When that landlord clicks, the first filter already happened. A Google visitor often still compares 10 tabs.

Watch for: a high rate on a small base moves a lot. 43 leads is a small number. One busy month can shift the rate by a full point.

## Why does AI under-report in analytics?

AI under-reports in analytics because the path from AI answer to lead often hides the AI step. Three things cause it.

1. **No referrer.** Some AI apps open links without a referrer. GA4 files the visit as direct.
2. **Brand search after AI.** An owner reads ChatGPT, then types your name into Google. GA4 credits Google.
3. **Phone calls.** The owner calls the number from the AI answer. No web visit happens at all.

Goodjuju measured the gap on its own site. From May to October 7, 2026, GA4 credited 7 leads to AI. Form records showed at least 13. GA4 missed about 4 in 10 traced AI leads.

What to do: add "ChatGPT or another AI tool" to your "How did you hear about us?" field. Ask the same on each owner call. Our guide on [how to track AI traffic](/guides/track-ai-traffic-property-management) covers GA4 setup.

## How does each channel pick which company to show?

Google and ChatGPT pick companies from different sources, so a good Google rank does not carry over by itself.

| Question | Google Search, Maps, AI Overviews | ChatGPT search |
|---|---|---|
| Whose crawler? | Googlebot | OAI-SearchBot |
| What decides the local list? | Relevance, distance and prominence (reviews and links count) | Its search index, listing sites, reviews, company sites |
| Special markup needed? | No, Google says no extra schema is needed for AI features | No public rule |
| Can you block it? | Yes, but you leave Search | Yes; OpenAI says a block takes about 24 hours to apply |
| Where to see results? | Search Console Performance report (AI features count as "Web") | No console; test by hand |

Sources: Google Business Profile Help "Tips to improve your local ranking", Google Search Central "AI features and your website" and OpenAI crawler docs, read 2026-10-19.

Our own data shows how much ChatGPT leans on third-party sites. On 2026-10-19 we asked "What is the best property management company in <city>?" for 50 US cities. ChatGPT cited a listing site in 96% of its answers and a company website in 30%. Gemini cited a company website in 96% (our data, 2026-10-19). Across all 3 engines, expertise.com was a source in 93 of 150 answers.

So for ChatGPT, your profile on Expertise.com, Yelp and similar sites can matter as much as your own site.

## Where should a small company spend first?

A small company should spend first on the work that helps both channels. Most of it is the same work. Here is a simple order for a company with 50 to 500 doors.

1. **Fix your Google Business Profile.** Right name, address, phone, hours, services and service area. This feeds Google Maps, and it is the base for every other listing.
2. **Fix your top listing sites.** Claim and correct your Yelp, Expertise.com, BBB and iPropertyManagement pages. In our 50-city run, these sites showed up most in AI answers.
3. **Publish your owner fees.** A clear fee table answers the exact question owners ask both Google and ChatGPT. Use our [fee calculator](/tools/property-management-fee-calculator) to check your numbers against the market.
4. **Ask for reviews.** Honest requests to real owners, on Google first.
5. **Test ChatGPT once a month.** Ask 5 to 10 owner questions for your city. Write down who it names.

Rough budget split for a small team: keep most of the spend on Google work. Steps 1, 3 and 4 serve both channels. Give about one hour a month to the ChatGPT test. Move more to AI work only when your own form data shows AI leads growing.

## The bottom line on ChatGPT vs Google

Google is the volume channel today, and ChatGPT is the fast-growing, high-intent one. In the fall 2026 data, Google gave 15 times more owner leads. But AI leads converted about 3 times better. Their share grew from 2.1% to 3.4% in six months.

You do not have to choose. Clear facts on your site and on the listing sites help both. Then measure each channel on its own, with form answers next to GA4.
