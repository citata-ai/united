## What features does MagicDoor have?

MagicDoor markets rental workflows and AI within a shared property management platform. Evaluate the accounting and daily records before deciding whether its automation helps your team.

The pricing page lists leasing, rent collection, maintenance, communication, documents, and vendor tools. It also distinguishes plans by AI capacity. Confirm the current scope in your quote.

This is a review of the vendor's public material read on 2026-10-19. We have not run a live portfolio on the product. The test steps below are recommendations, not measured results.

Use the same sample tasks you use to assess an established suite. Open a lease, receive a payment, assign work, approve a bill, and produce an owner report. Require record links and a clear review trail.

Test an AI suggestion separately from the action it may trigger. A useful draft does not prove that automatic sending or posting is safe.

Ask staff to complete the task without the sales presenter. That can reveal missing instructions, unclear permissions, or work that still needs another tool.

## How does MagicDoor handle trust accounting?

MagicDoor says it includes double-entry accounting, separate trust and operating records, and reconciliation. Those are important claims for a management company, so ask your accountant to test them.

The accounting page describes tracking by owner and property, distributions, and audited transfers. It does not remove your responsibility to check the setup or approve accounting work.

Bring a sample close with known balances. Include rent, a vendor bill, an owner reserve, a returned payment, and a transfer. Ask how the system keeps each record linked.

Have the accountant compare the bank record, ledger, and client balances. Inspect how staff investigate a difference. Do not accept a balanced total without understanding the underlying entries.

Ask who can change a posted record and whether the history shows the original value. Test restricted roles and period controls. A helpful dashboard is not a substitute for an inspectable ledger.

Check the exported reports and transaction detail. The accountant should be able to review the result outside a sales session and identify any required workarounds.

## How does MagicDoor handle Section 8 subsidies?

MagicDoor describes subsidy payers as linked organizations and separates tenant and subsidy amounts within a lease. That data structure can help staff review who owes what.

The vendor's subsidy page says owner statements show the split. Treat that as a claim to inspect in a sample report. Do not infer that the feature handles every agency's payment format.

Ask for a demonstration with a changed tenant portion and an agency payment received later. Confirm which balance appears to the renter and which remains assigned to the subsidy payer.

Test a payment that covers different leases or periods. Ask how staff use the remittance record and correct a wrong allocation. Check the owner statement after the correction.

Review any automated fee or reminder rule. Your team should know which balance triggers the action and who can stop it. Keep program and lease decisions under human review.

This section describes software records, not voucher law. For program context, read our [Section 8 rent guide](/guides/section-8-rent-limits). Use the agency's actual documents when testing your ledger setup.

The page also names transaction, ACH, messaging registration, application, and optional service fees. Ask for the fee schedule rather than assuming the unit rate is the whole invoice.

Have the vendor explain how extra AI usage is priced and approved. Your team needs a clear limit and a way to inspect charges.

Keep the contract term and billed unit count in the comparison. A discounted rate with a commitment is not equivalent to a cancellable monthly offer.

## Who is MagicDoor for?

MagicDoor belongs on a shortlist for managers willing to test a combined accounting and automation platform. Choose it only after your required work and data migration pass review.

Do not use product age as proof of quality or weakness. Ask for references that match your portfolio, accounting process, and team structure. Use their answers to form test questions.

Compare MagicDoor with your current software on the work that causes delays today. A new assistant does not help if staff still need to rebuild owner reports elsewhere.

Use this final review checklist:

- **Check records:** Match sample leases, owners, properties, and bank accounts.
- **Close the books:** Have your accountant approve the reports and corrections.
- **Test subsidies:** Inspect payer balances and the owner statement.
- **Restrict access:** Confirm a limited user cannot reach unrelated private records.
- **Review AI actions:** Require clear approvals before external or financial changes.
- **Test migration:** Compare sample imports with source totals and attached documents.
- **Confirm cost:** Include billing floors, contract terms, and service fees.
- **Plan departure:** Export records in a form your team can use.

Keep unresolved items in writing before go-live. For an established-suite reference point, read [AppFolio vs Buildium](/guides/appfolio-vs-buildium). Choose the tool whose required workflow you can verify and support.
