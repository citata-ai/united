## Which sources does ChatGPT search use for local businesses?

ChatGPT search uses outside search providers, then reads and cites the pages they return. The OpenAI Help Center says ChatGPT "sometimes partners with other search providers" and rewrites your question into shorter search queries. Its example: "good restaurants near me" becomes "top restaurants San Francisco". The same page links the privacy policies of Microsoft and Shopify as search providers.

For a property manager, the question "best property manager in Tampa" becomes a search query. ChatGPT then picks names from the pages that come back.

We tested this on 2026-10-19 (our data). We asked "What is the best property management company in <City, ST>? Give names." for the 50 largest US cities. We used the OpenAI API with web search, the closest match to ChatGPT search. Here is what ChatGPT cited:

| Source type | ChatGPT answers that cited it (of 50) |
|---|---|
| Any directory or review site | 48 |
| Expertise.com | 30 |
| reviews.birdeye.com | 17 |
| bestinhood.com | 12 |
| bbb.org | 11 |
| propertymanagement.com | 8 |
| threebestrated.com | 7 |
| A property manager's own site | 15 |

ChatGPT named 5.3 companies per answer on average. The full city list is in our [AI Property Manager Index 2026](/research/ai-property-manager-index-2026).

What this means: in ChatGPT, your own site is the second step. The first step is to be on the lists that ChatGPT reads for your city.

## Does ChatGPT use Google reviews?

ChatGPT does not show signs that it reads Google reviews directly. OpenAI's help page names Microsoft and Shopify as search providers, not Google. In our 50-city run, ChatGPT cited google.com in 0 of 50 answers.

Reviews still count. ChatGPT cited Birdeye review pages in 17 of 50 answers. Many best-of lists also rank companies by review count and stars.

What to do:

1. **Keep Google reviews coming.** Google AI Overviews and Gemini use them, and other sites copy them.
2. **Claim your Birdeye, Yelp and BBB pages.** In our test, ChatGPT cited Birdeye in 17 answers and BBB in 11. Claude cited Yelp in all 50.
3. **Reply to reviews in plain words.** A reply that names the service and the city adds facts a search can find.

## How do you get on the pages ChatGPT reads?

You get on those pages by fixing the listings first, then your own site. This is the order we use.

1. **Search your city like ChatGPT does.** Type "best property management company <city>" in Bing. Write down the top 10 list pages.
2. **Check each list.** Are you on it? Are your name, address, phone and website right?
3. **Apply where you can.** Some lists take an application. Others pick by review count and stars. Read each site's rules before you apply.
4. **Fix BBB and Birdeye.** Claim the page, fix the facts, and add your service area.
5. **Let OAI-SearchBot crawl your site.** OpenAI says sites that block OAI-SearchBot "will not be shown in ChatGPT search answers". Check your robots.txt and your firewall rules.
6. **Submit new pages through IndexNow.** IndexNow shares one submission with Bing and the other member engines. Microsoft is one of the search providers OpenAI names.
7. **Put the facts on your own pages.** Each city you serve, your fees, your contract terms, and the property types you manage. Our [AI search checklist](/guides/ai-search-checklist-property-management-website) lists each item.

Watch for: a list site that asks for money to place you. Some lists sell spots. Ask how they pick companies before you pay.

## How long until ChatGPT mentions a new company?

No one can give you a date. Each step has its own delay:

| Step | Time | Source |
|---|---|---|
| robots.txt change reaches OpenAI | about 24 hours | OpenAI crawler docs |
| IndexNow tells Bing about a new page | at submit time | IndexNow FAQ |
| A list site adds you | days to months | the list site |
| ChatGPT search finds and uses the list | not published | none |

OpenAI says it can take "~24 hours from a site's robots.txt update for our systems to adjust". That is the only time OpenAI publishes. The rest depends on other sites and on the search provider.

I plan in 30-day steps. Fix the listings in week 1. Run the same prompts after 30 days and after 90 days. Compare who ChatGPT names and which sources it links.

## Can you pay ChatGPT to recommend you?

No. You cannot buy a place in a ChatGPT answer. OpenAI lists "Answer independence" as an ad principle: "Ads do not influence the answers ChatGPT gives you." OpenAI says ads show "at the bottom of answers", "clearly labeled and separated from the organic answer".

So a sales call that offers a paid spot inside ChatGPT answers is not true. A paid ad below the answer is a different product. It is labeled as an ad.

You can pay for work that helps: listing cleanup, better landlord pages, review requests. Nobody can promise the result. AI answers change from run to run.

## How do you test what ChatGPT says about you?

Run a fixed set of owner questions in ChatGPT each month and log the answers. Keep the words the same each time, so you can compare.

1. **Write 10 to 20 owner questions.** Use the words owners use: "property manager for a duplex in <city>", "property management fees <city>".
2. **Add your city to each question.** OpenAI says ChatGPT guesses your area from your IP address. A city in the question gives the same answer from any office.
3. **Use a logged-out window.** Memory can change the search query, says OpenAI.
4. **Run each question 2 times.** Answers change between runs.
5. **Log 4 things.** Named or not, rank in the list, your site linked or not, and the sources linked.
6. **Check the sources.** OpenAI says results "can be incomplete, outdated, or incorrect". A wrong fact usually comes from a wrong listing.

What to expect: in our own 20-prompt test for citata.ai (2026-10-19), ChatGPT named us in 0 of 36 non-brand answers. We report our zero because a baseline starts there. You start the same way: measure, fix the sources, measure again.
