## What does an eviction cost in total?

An eviction costs a landlord 5 kinds of money. Court fees are the only part with a fixed price. The rest depends on your rent, your state and your tenant.

| Cost | What drives it | Who sets it |
|---|---|---|
| Court and filing fees | State and county fee schedules | The court |
| Service and sheriff fees | Number of tenants, number of trips | Sheriff or process server |
| Attorney fees | Contested or not, local market | Your lawyer |
| Lost rent | Rent amount, case length, re-rent time | Your market and the court calendar |
| Damage and turnover | Unit condition at move-out | The tenant, then your vendors |

Most landlords plan for the first two and forget the last two. Lost rent is usually the largest line.

## How much are court and filing fees?

Court and filing fees for an eviction are a few hundred dollars in the places we checked. The amount depends on the state, the county and how much money you ask for.

| Place | Fee | Amount | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| California | Filing, claim up to $10,000 | Usually $240 | California Courts Self-Help Guide |
| California | Filing, claim over $10,000 up to $35,000 | Usually $385 | California Courts Self-Help Guide |
| California | Filing, claim over $35,000 | Usually $435 | California Courts Self-Help Guide |
| California | Jury trial | $150 | California Courts Self-Help Guide |
| Hendry County, FL | Clerk filing fee | $185 | Hendry County Clerk |
| Hendry County, FL | Issue each summons | $10 | Hendry County Clerk |
| Hendry County, FL | Sheriff serves summons, per defendant | $40 | Hendry County Clerk |
| Hendry County, FL | Sheriff serves the writ of possession | $90 | Hendry County Clerk |

The California courts say San Bernardino, San Francisco and Riverside counties may charge slightly more. In Florida, each county clerk sets its own packet and fees, so check your county.

Florida example for one tenant: $185 + $10 + $40 + $90 = $325. Two adult tenants on the lease add a second summons and a second service fee: $375.

## How much are attorney fees?

Attorney fees for an eviction vary too much for one national number. We did not find a public, reliable survey of eviction attorney prices, so we do not quote one.

What to do:

1. **Ask for a flat fee.** Many eviction lawyers quote one price for an uncontested case.
2. **Ask what it includes.** Does the quote cover the notice, filing, the hearing and the writ?
3. **Ask the price if the tenant fights.** A contested case or a motion costs more.
4. **Check your lease.** Some states let the court shift fees to the loser.

Arizona is one example of fee shifting. A.R.S. 12-1178(A) lets the court award the winning landlord attorney fees and court costs. Under A.R.S. 33-1368(B), a tenant who wants to stay after you file must pay past-due rent, late fees, attorney fees and court costs. A judgment is not cash, though. Collecting it is a separate step, and a tenant who could not pay rent may not pay the judgment.

## How much rent is lost during the case?

Lost rent runs from the first missed payment until a new tenant pays. That span has 3 parts: the notice period, the court case and the turnover.

The court part alone is not short. The California courts say an eviction "can take 30 to 45 days or more". In California, the tenant has 10 court days to answer after personal service. If the tenant files a response, you wait for a trial date. A demurrer can add a few weeks.

Then the unit is empty. You clean, repair, list, show and screen before the next tenant pays.

## What do damage and turnover cost?

Damage and turnover cost whatever the unit needs after the tenant leaves. The security deposit covers part of it, under strict state deadlines.

| State | Deposit cap | Deadline to account for the deposit | Law |
|---|---|---|---|
| Arizona | 1.5 months' rent | Itemized list within 14 days, excluding weekends and holidays | A.R.S. 33-1321 |
| Florida | No cap in 83.49 | Return in 15 days, or notice of a claim in 30 days | Fla. Stat. 83.49(3)(a) |

Watch for: in Arizona, a landlord who misses the deadline can owe twice the amount wrongfully withheld (A.R.S. 33-1321(E)). Send the itemized list on time, even after an eviction.

## Worked example: what does one eviction cost?

Here is one worked total. The rent and the time are example inputs, not market data. Replace them with your own.

Inputs:

- **Rent:** $1,800 per month (example).
- **Fees:** $325, the Hendry County, Florida total for one tenant.
- **Lost rent:** 3 months (example: 1 month of notice and case, 1 month of writ and move-out, 1 month to turn and re-rent).
- **Attorney fee, repairs and deposit:** your numbers.

| Line | Math | Amount |
|---|---|---|
| Court and sheriff fees | $185 + $10 + $40 + $90 | $325 |
| Lost rent | 3 × $1,800 | $5,400 |
| Subtotal before attorney and repairs | $325 + $5,400 | $5,725 |
| Attorney fee | Your flat-fee quote | + A |
| Repairs and cleaning | Your vendor quotes | + R |
| Security deposit applied | Your deposit | − D |
| Total | $5,725 + A + R − D | |

The fees are 6% of the subtotal. Lost rent is 94%. So the fastest way to cut the cost is to cut the time. Serve the notice on the first day the law allows. File on the first day you can. Use a correct notice so the court does not throw out the case.

Sometimes a written move-out agreement costs less than a case. If the tenant leaves 4 weeks sooner, you save about one month's rent, $1,800 in this example. The California courts say you can dismiss the case once the tenant moves out or you reach an agreement.

If you own many units, a manager may cost less than one bad eviction. Use the [property management fee calculator](/tools/property-management-fee-calculator) to compare.
