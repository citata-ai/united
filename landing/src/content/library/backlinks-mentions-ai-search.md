## Do backlinks still help a property manager in AI search?

Backlinks still help, but they are not the strongest signal. Ahrefs studied 75,000 brands and compared each factor with how often Google AI Overviews named the brand (Ahrefs, 2025-05-26).

| Factor | Correlation with AI Overview mentions |
|---|---|
| Branded web mentions (linked or not) | 0.664 |
| Branded anchor text | 0.527 |
| Branded search volume | 0.392 |
| Domain Rating | 0.326 |
| Referring domains | 0.295 |
| Backlinks | 0.218 |

Read the table with care. Ahrefs says "correlation ≠ causation". The study used only domains above DR 40, so most local managers sit outside its sample. Still, the order is clear: people who write your name matter more than the raw count of links.

Backlinks keep one job. AI answers pull from pages that search engines index and rank. A site with no links may not reach the index or the first page that AI reads.

## Is a mention without a link worth anything?

A mention without a link is worth something in AI search. Ahrefs counted "branded web mentions, linked or unlinked", and that factor scored highest. AI tools read the text of a page. A line like "Acme Property Management charges 8% in Tampa" teaches the tool a fact, with or without a link.

Our own data shows the same pattern for property managers. We asked ChatGPT, Gemini and Claude for the best property manager in each of the 50 largest US cities. Of the 150 answers, Expertise.com was a source in 93 (our data, 2026-10-19). Many companies got into the answer through a directory page, not their own site.

In a smaller test, we looked at 10 clients of Property Manager Websites. Six of them were named in 11 answers, with only 1 citation of a client's own site (our data, 2026-10-19). The mention came first; the link was rare.

## Which sites give the best mentions?

The best mentions come from the sites AI already cites for your prompts. In our 50-city test, these sources showed up most often (our data, 2026-10-19):

| Source | Answers that cited it (of 150) | Type of site |
|---|---|---|
| expertise.com | 93 | Curated "best of" lists by city |
| ipropertymanagement.com | 50 | Property management directory |
| yelp.com | 50 | Review site |
| reddit.com | 22 | Forum |
| threebestrated.com | 21 | Curated "best of" lists by city |
| reviews.birdeye.com | 19 | Review pages |
| bbb.org | 14 | Business profiles and ratings |

Expertise.com says it picks providers in 5 steps: find candidates, check licenses, score review data, assess services, and call them as mystery shoppers. It researches more than 60,000 businesses a month. Its page says providers may advertise only after they pass selection.

Local mentions also count: a chamber of commerce member page, a local news story, a NARPM chapter page. These are hard to fake, and they name your city next to your company.

## Are guest posts still safe?

Guest posts are safe when they help readers and do not exist to pass links. Google's spam policies list "links with optimized anchor text in articles, guest posts, or press releases distributed on other sites" as link spam.

Use this checklist before you write or accept a guest post:

- **Real audience:** the host site has readers who are landlords or investors.
- **Real content:** the post answers one question with your own numbers or local facts.
- **Brand anchor only:** the link, if any, says your company name, not "best property management Tampa".
- **No payment for links:** if you pay, the link carries rel="sponsored" (Google's outbound link guide).
- **No mass campaign:** one post on a relevant site beats 20 posts on unrelated blogs.
- **Name in the text:** your company name appears in the body, so the mention counts even without a link.

Google's AI guide adds a direct warning: "seeking inauthentic 'mentions' across the web isn't as helpful as it might seem."

## How do you find mention chances?

You find mention chances by asking AI tools your landlord prompts and listing the sources they cite. Then you work down the list. Use these steps:

1. **Write 20 prompts** a landlord asks, for example "best property manager in Tampa for a condo".
2. **Run each prompt** in ChatGPT, Gemini and Claude, with search on.
3. **List every cited domain** and count how many answers use it.
4. **Mark each domain:** listed, listed but wrong, or not listed.
5. **Fix the wrong ones first.** A wrong fee or address on Yelp spreads into answers.
6. **Request or earn a spot** on the domains where you are missing.
7. **Re-run the same prompts** after 4 to 6 weeks and compare.

For the full prompt method, see [how landlords use AI to choose a property manager](/guides/how-owners-choose-a-property-manager-with-ai).

## What should a 50 to 500 door manager do first?

Start with the directories, not with link building. In our competitor study, all 3 leading agencies fix public listings for their clients (our data, 2026-10-19). That step also had the most direct link to AI answers in our data.

A simple order:

1. **Week 1:** fix Expertise.com, Yelp, iPropertyManagement, BBB and Google Business Profile facts.
2. **Weeks 2 to 4:** ask happy owners for honest reviews on the sites AI cites.
3. **Month 2:** earn 1 or 2 local mentions: a news story, a chamber page, a NARPM chapter page.
4. **Ongoing:** write guest posts only where landlords read them.

We do not promise a citation from any of these steps. AI answers change. We report what each step moved, with the same prompts before and after.
