## Does AI cite BBB pages?

AI cites BBB pages for property manager questions, but mostly ChatGPT. We asked ChatGPT, Gemini and Claude "What is the best property management company in [City, ST]? Give names." for 50 US cities on 2026-10-19. That gave 150 answers with live web search (our data).

| Engine | Answers | Answers that cited bbb.org |
|---|---|---|
| ChatGPT (gpt-6-luna) | 50 | 11 |
| Claude (claude-sonnet-5-5) | 50 | 3 |
| Gemini (gemini-3-flash) | 50 | 0 |
| Total | 150 | 14 |

The 14 answers came from 14 different cities, from Boston to Fresno. So BBB is not a local quirk. It is a source ChatGPT uses across the country.

BBB is not the top source. Here is where it sits next to the other directories in the same 150 answers:

| Source | Answers that cited it |
|---|---|
| expertise.com | 93 |
| ipropertymanagement.com | 50 |
| yelp.com | 50 |
| threebestrated.com | 21 |
| reviews.birdeye.com | 19 |
| bbb.org | 14 |

So a BBB profile is worth one check and a few fixes. It is not worth more than your Google Business Profile or your Expertise.com and Yelp listings. This is a single run. The share can change from week to week.

## How does a BBB rating work?

A BBB rating is a letter grade from A+ to F, based on a 100-point score. A score of 97 to 100 is A+. A score below 60 is F. BBB gives "NR" (No Rating) when it does not have enough information or the file is under review.

BBB lists the elements and their points:

| Element | Points (best to worst) |
|---|---|
| Unanswered complaints | 40 to 0 |
| Unresolved complaints | 30 to 0 |
| Complaint volume, weighted by age | 15 to 0 |
| Time in business | 10 to 0 |
| Complaint resolution delayed | 5 to 0 |
| Transparent business practices | 0 to -5 |
| Failure to address a complaint pattern | 0 to -31 |
| Type of business | 0 to -41 |
| Competency licensing | 0 to -41 |
| Failure to honor mediation or arbitration | 0 to -41 |
| Advertising review, per incident | 0 to -41 |

Two facts matter for property managers:

- **Reviews do not count.** BBB says customer reviews are not used in the letter grade. Only complaints and the other elements count.
- **Licensing counts.** If your state requires a license to manage rentals for others, a missing license can take off up to 41 points. Check your state's rule.

BBB also says "A rating is not a guarantee of a business's reliability or performance."

## Do BBB complaints show in AI answers?

BBB complaints are public, so AI engines can read them. BBB's FAQ says a filed complaint "becomes part of the business's public profile."

We have not yet measured how often AI repeats BBB complaint text. Our test asked for the "best" company, not "is [company] legit". An owner who checks one company by name is more likely to see complaint details. That is a hypothesis.

What to do now:

1. **Answer every complaint.** Unanswered complaints carry the most points, up to 40.
2. **Resolve what you can.** Unresolved complaints carry up to 30 points.
3. **Write a calm, factual reply.** Your reply sits on a public page that AI can read. Do not share tenant details.
4. **Fix the pattern.** If 3 complaints say "deposit returned late", fix the deposit process, not only the replies.

## Is BBB accreditation needed?

BBB accreditation is not needed to have a BBB profile or a rating. BBB says it publishes profiles "for Accredited and non-accredited businesses alike." It also says not being accredited is, "on its own, not a mark against the business."

Accreditation is a paid program. BBB says dues fund its work and do not buy a better rating or remove a complaint. To apply, a business must meet the 8 BBB Standards for Trust, including:

- **At least 6 months in business** before you apply.
- **All required licenses** in place.
- **At least a B rating.**
- **Honest advertising and clear terms**, such as total costs and refund policies.
- **Answers to BBB complaints**, and mediation when asked.

Should you pay for it? Accreditation adds the BBB seal and BBB dispute resolution. We found no data that accreditation changes AI answers. Decide on its value to owners in your market, not on AI.

## How do you fix BBB facts?

You fix BBB facts by checking your profile against your website and your Google Business Profile, then asking BBB to update what is wrong. BBB's FAQ links to a business login. Your local BBB can also help.

Checklist for your BBB profile:

- **Business name** matches your website and Google Business Profile exactly.
- **Address and phone** match, with no old office.
- **Website** points to your own domain, not an old domain.
- **Category** says property management, not a general real estate category.
- **License number** is listed, if your state requires one.
- **Years in business** and owner names are current.
- **Complaints** all have a reply.

Same facts everywhere makes your company easier for AI to identify. Our guide on [how owners choose a property manager with AI](/guides/how-owners-choose-a-property-manager-with-ai) shows which sources owners see in AI answers.
