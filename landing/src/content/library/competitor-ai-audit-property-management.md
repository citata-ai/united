## Why run a competitor AI audit?

A competitor AI audit shows which property managers AI recommends in your city and which sources put them there. Owners now ask ChatGPT and Gemini for a short list before they call anyone. If three competitors are on that list and you are not, you lose the call before it starts.

We ran one prompt, "What is the best property management company in [City, ST]? Give names.", in 50 US cities (our data, 2026-10-19). ChatGPT, Gemini and Claude gave 150 answers. Those answers named 821 different companies. Each answer listed sources, and directories showed up in 134 of the 150 answers.

The lesson: the names change by city, but the path to the list is the same. A competitor audit finds that path for your city.

## Which prompts should you run?

Run the questions a rental owner asks, not the keywords you bid on. Use 10 prompts and keep the words fixed, so you can repeat the test next month.

| # | Prompt | What it tests |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | What is the best property management company in [city, ST]? | The main short list |
| 2 | Best property manager for a single-family home in [city] | Fit for small owners |
| 3 | Property management companies in [city] with low fees | Fee pages |
| 4 | Who manages Section 8 rentals in [city]? | Niche services |
| 5 | Property manager for a small apartment building in [city] | Multifamily fit |
| 6 | Is [competitor name] a good property manager? | What AI says about a rival |
| 7 | [Competitor] vs [your company] | Head-to-head facts |
| 8 | Property manager in [suburb near you] | Service area reach |
| 9 | How much does property management cost in [city]? | Who AI quotes on price |
| 10 | Which property managers in [city] have the best reviews? | Review sources |

Run each prompt 2 times in ChatGPT, Gemini and Claude. Use a logged-out or new chat, so past chats do not shape the answer. Also check Google for each prompt. Google says AI Overviews show only when its systems judge them useful, so many queries will not show one.

## How do you record the names and the sources?

Record each answer as one row in a sheet. The sources matter more than the names, because the sources are what you can change.

Use these 9 columns:

1. **Date.** The day you ran the prompt.
2. **Engine.** ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude or Google AI Overview.
3. **Prompt number.** 1 to 10 from the table above.
4. **Run.** 1 or 2.
5. **Companies named, in order.** Copy them as written.
6. **Your company named?** Yes or no, and the position.
7. **Sources listed.** Every domain the answer links.
8. **Source type.** Directory, company site, forum, news or other.
9. **Notes.** Wrong facts, old prices, closed companies.

Then count. For each competitor, count the answers that name it. For each domain, count the answers that cite it. Sort both lists from high to low.

Expect each engine to behave in its own way. In our 50-city test, Claude named 15.5 companies per answer on average, ChatGPT 5.3 and Gemini 5.7 (our data, 2026-10-19). ChatGPT linked a directory in 96% of its answers, and a company site in 30%. Gemini linked a company site in 96% of its answers and Reddit in 22 of its 50 answers.

Here is one city from our test, to show what a row-level result looks like. These are AI outputs, not our ranking of these companies (our data, 2026-10-19).

| Engine | Names in answer | First 3 names | Sources linked |
|---|---|---|---|
| ChatGPT | 5 | Landmark Property Management, GC Realty & Development, Daniel Management Group | bestinhood.com, biggerpockets.com |
| Claude | 23 | Hales Property Management, Landmark Property Management, HomeRiver Group Chicago | Expertise.com, Yelp, iPropertyManagement, Clutch and 5 more |
| Gemini | 6 | GC Realty & Development, Landmark Property Management, Chicago Style Management | 3 sites, including gcrealtyinc.com and chicagostylemanagement.com |

Two companies, Landmark Property Management and GC Realty & Development, appeared in all three engines. Those are the names to study first. Open their Expertise.com, Yelp and Google Business Profile pages, and read their owner pages.

## How do you compare listings and reviews?

Compare your profile with the top 3 competitors on each source that AI cited. Make one table per source.

| Same name, address, phone on each site? | | | | |
| Owner fee page on own site? | | | | |

Expertise.com explains how it picks companies. Its selection page lists five steps: find providers, check licenses and awards, analyze review data, assess services, and call each company as a mystery shopper. It looks at the number of review sites, the average score and how much the ratings change. So a directory gap is often a review gap.

## What gaps should you fix first?

Fix the gaps with the most answers behind them first. A source that 30 answers cite is worth more than one that 2 answers cite.

1. **Missing from a top directory.** Get listed, or ask for a review of your company, on the directory that AI cites most for your city.
2. **Wrong facts on a listing.** Fix your name, address, phone, cities and services so each site matches. See the [AI search checklist](/guides/ai-search-checklist-property-management-website) for the site side.
3. **Fewer reviews than rivals.** Ask real owners for honest reviews on the sites AI cites. Do not pay for reviews.
4. **No owner page for a city.** Write one page per city you serve, with your fees and services.
5. **No fee page.** Publish a full owner fee table, so prompt 9 has a number from you to read.

Google says no special work is needed to appear in AI Overviews beyond normal SEO. It does list keeping your Business Profile current as a best practice. That matches what our data shows: listings first.

Repeat the audit each month with the same 10 prompts. AI answers change between runs, so judge the trend over 2 to 3 months. To see how owners use these answers, read [how landlords use AI to choose a property manager](/guides/how-owners-choose-a-property-manager-with-ai).
