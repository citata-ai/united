## What does each free or base plan include?

TurboTenant has a free plan, while RentRedi sells the same core feature set under different billing terms. Start by comparing the tasks you need today.

TurboTenant's pricing page lists rental marketing, applicant screening, and online rent collection at entry level. Paid tiers add tools such as lease creation and accounting. Confirm the exact tier in your account.

RentRedi's pricing page describes a subscription for unlimited properties, tenants, and teammates. That can simplify budget planning as you add rentals. It does not prove the software fits a third-party manager's accounting duties.

Build a task list before signing up. Include rent, maintenance, leasing, files, and your accountant's export. Mark which tasks you can complete in the base plan without another product.

## How do rent payment fees compare?

Rent payment fees should be compared by payment method, payer, and plan. TurboTenant's pricing page distinguishes its standard bank-transfer fee from free ACH for Pro landlords.

RentRedi's subscription price does not establish the complete payment cost. Request its current payment fee schedule and check the renter confirmation screen. Do not substitute an old third-party review for that schedule.

Test a bank payment, a card payment, and a returned payment in the vendor's permitted demo process. Find where the charge appears and whether the tenant balance changes correctly.

Ask how staff record a payment received elsewhere. Your books need the receipt even when a tenant uses an approved offline route. Check that recording the receipt does not start a duplicate withdrawal.

Include processing time in the workflow review. A payment started, a payment settled, and cash available to use are different states. Train staff to explain the state shown.

## Which has a better mobile app?

The better mobile app is the one that handles your tasks from request to record. Do not rank either app without testing it on your own phone and connection.

TurboTenant links landlord and renter apps on its pricing page. RentRedi presents a mobile rental workflow in its product offer. That confirms a mobile option, not equal function on every device.

Run the same field test. Find a tenant, open the lease, view a balance, send a message, and attach a repair photo. Check whether any action sends you back to a desktop.

Then test permissions. A helper who handles repairs may not need financial access. Confirm what each role can see before adding staff or vendors to a live account.

Test the tenant side too. A simple manager view is of limited use if renters cannot find their charges or submit a clear request.

## How many sites does each syndicate listings to?

Listing reach should be checked against named destinations, rather than assumed from a headline count. Ask both vendors for the current partner list and any restrictions that apply to your rental.

Check Zillow, Apartments.com, and the sites that produce your local inquiries. Confirm whether each is included in the selected plan and whether separate advertising fees apply.

Run a sample publication with accurate, approved details. Inspect the address, rent, availability, photos, and application link on each destination. Save the result in your decision sheet.

Also test removal. A feed that posts correctly but leaves a leased unit active creates extra work. Ask how failed updates appear and who contacts the listing partner.

Record inquiry source in a consistent field. Compare completed applications and signed leases, not only total messages. Broad distribution does not establish better leads for every property.

## Which has better screening?

The better screening workflow supplies the reports your policy needs and a clear path for applicants to get help. Compare report scope before comparing screening price.

TurboTenant's pricing page separates screening from landlord subscriptions and advertises income and fraud tools in Pro. RentRedi also offers screening within its rental workflow. Ask which checks require separate payment or consent.

Request a sample report and inspect source dates, record matching, missing-data labels, and dispute support. Do not treat a score as a complete decision.

Ask how applicants with income outside standard payroll complete verification. Staff need an alternate review route for supported evidence. A failed connection should not become an unsupported claim of fraud.

Choose after the same sample case works in each system. Keep final criteria, report review, and applicant communication in your own documented process.

## How should you test the choice before you pay?

Test the full workflow with sample records before you move live data. Use the same case in each product. Ask the person who will do the daily work to run the test. A sales demo alone does not show where your team will need help.

Keep a decision sheet with the task, result, missing step, and person who checked it. Mark each result as shown, promised, or still open. Ask for written terms when a feature needs an upgrade or another service. Include staff setup time in your cost review.

Check the exit path while you still control the choice. Export the sample records and open them outside the platform. Confirm that notes, files, dates, and links remain useful. Ask how you will get records after you close the account.

Choose a tool when the test proves a clear gain for your workflow. Keep your current process until the new process passes. Use the [property management fee calculator](/tools/property-management-fee-calculator) to see how software costs affect your service margin.
