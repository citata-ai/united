## What is a rent concession?

A rent concession is a discount you give a renter to sign a lease. Zillow's June 2026 rental report defines it as "a move-in discount, commonly a free month's rent, waived fees or free parking." The lease rent stays the same. The renter pays less for a set period or skips a fee.

Concessions are common now. Zillow found that 39.7% of rentals on its site offered one in June 2026, up from 35.2% a year earlier. The national typical asking rent was $1,965, up 2.2% year over year.

The share swings by metro. Zillow says the highest rates are where the most new apartments were built.

| Metro | Share of rentals with a concession | Typical rent | Rent change, 1 year |
|---|---|---|---|
| Charlotte, NC | 67.1% | $1,750 | +0.5% |
| Denver, CO | 65.9% | $1,930 | -1.3% |
| Dallas, TX | 64.6% | $1,673 | 0% |
| Austin, TX | 64.3% | $1,653 | -1.7% |
| San Francisco, CA | 24.9% | $3,301 | +8.2% |
| Chicago, IL | 23.3% | $2,275 | +5.2% |

Source: Zillow, June 2026 rental report.

## When should you offer a concession?

Offer a concession when 2 things are true: your unit is still empty past your target date, and the listings that compete with yours already offer deals. If neither is true, a concession gives away money you did not need to spend.

Use these steps before you offer a deal:

1. **Set a vacancy target.** Pick a date, for example 21 days after the listing goes live. Write it down before you list.
2. **Check the competition.** Search your zip code on 2 or 3 listing sites. Count how many similar units show a special.
3. **Fix the listing first.** Weak photos, a missing floor plan or a slow reply cost you leads. A concession does not fix those.
4. **Check the price.** If your rent is far above similar units, a price cut may be the honest fix.
5. **Price the empty days.** Each empty day costs about 1/30 of the monthly rent. At $1,965, that is about $65.50 a day.
6. **Pick the smallest deal that works.** Start with a waived fee or 2 weeks free. Go to a full month only if leads stay low.

The math in step 5 matters. A unit at $1,965 that sits 30 extra days loses $1,965. A month free that fills it 30 days sooner costs the same, and you also skip the extra turnover risk.

## One month free vs a lower monthly rent: which is better?

One month free is usually better for the landlord than a lower monthly rent. Both can cost the same over the lease. But the lease rent sets the base for the renewal, and a free month keeps that base at full price.

Here is a worked example with Zillow's June 2026 national typical rent of $1,965 and a 12-month lease.

| Option | Lease rent | Months paid | Year 1 total | Net effective rent | Renewal base |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| No concession | $1,965 | 12 | $23,580 | $1,965 | $1,965 |
| 1 month free | $1,965 | 11 | $21,615 | $1,801.25 | $1,965 |
| Lower rent | $1,801 | 12 | $21,612 | $1,801 | $1,801 |

The year 1 cost is almost the same. The difference shows up at renewal. With the free month, a renewal raise (say 3%, as an example) starts from $1,965. With the lower rent, it starts from $1,801. That gap is $164 a month before any raise.

A lower rent has one benefit. The renter has no "rent cliff" in month 2, and that can lower the risk of a late payment early in the lease. A free last month also has a benefit: the renter has a reason to stay to the end.

Other low-cost concessions:

- **Waived application or admin fee.** Small cost, and it helps in a slow week.
- **Free parking or storage.** Low cost if the space is empty anyway.
- **Flexible move-in date.** Free for you if the unit is ready.
- **Prorated first month.** Not a discount, but it can close a renter who is on the fence.

## How do you write a concession into the lease?

Write the concession into the lease or a signed addendum. A verbal promise leads to disputes. State the full lease rent, then the concession as a separate line.

A concession clause should hold 6 facts:

1. The full monthly rent.
2. The type of concession, for example "rent for month 1 is waived."
3. The dollar value.
4. Which month or months it applies to.
5. What happens on early move-out, for example repayment of the unearned part.
6. Whether the concession carries into a renewal. Most say no.

Keep the full rent in your ledger and post the concession as a credit. That keeps your rent roll and your renewal math clean.

Check the repayment clause against your state law before you use it. State rules on early move-out charges differ.

This is general information, not legal advice. Check the current law or ask a local attorney.

## Do concessions attract weaker applicants?

A concession does not change who qualifies. Your screening rules decide that. Use the same written criteria for income, rental history and credit for every applicant, with or without a deal.

Two rules keep you safe:

- **Same deal for all.** The Fair Housing Act, 42 U.S.C. 3604(b), bans different "terms, conditions, or privileges" of rental because of race, color, religion, sex, familial status or national origin. Offer one written concession to every applicant for that unit.
- **Same screening for all.** If you use a credit report, follow the FTC rules for landlords. If you reject someone or ask for more because of the report, you must send an adverse action notice.

Watch for one real risk: a free month can pull in renters who plan to move again at the end of the lease. A free last month, or a repayment clause for early move-out, helps with that.
