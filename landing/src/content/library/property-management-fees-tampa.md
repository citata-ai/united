## What leasing and renewal fees do Tampa managers charge?

Tampa leasing fees are higher than in many metros. Five of the 10 managers charge up to 100% of one month's rent. Half of one month is the floor on most pages. Ziprent uses a flat $1,500.

Tampa renewal fees vary more than any other line:

- **None:** Rent Solutions says it adds no renewal fee.
- **Flat:** $250 (Evernest, Ziprent, Vintage), $299 (Mynd), $295 to $1,000 (PMI JCM, by plan).
- **Percentage:** 25% of one month's rent (HomeRiver).

On a $1,917 rent, 25% is $479. That is almost twice a $250 flat fee.

## Which local rules add cost for Tampa owners?

Florida law, not the City of Tampa, sets most landlord rules. Florida Statutes 83.425 preempts the regulation of residential tenancies to the state. It supersedes local rules on screening, security deposits, application fees and lease terms. So a Tampa manager works mostly to one state rulebook.

The deposit rule is where mistakes cost money. Under Florida Statutes 83.49:

1. **No claim:** return the deposit within 15 days after the lease ends.
2. **A claim:** send written notice within 30 days, by certified mail or by e-mail as section 83.505 allows.
3. **Use the form:** the notice must follow the wording the statute gives.

Ask each manager who sends this notice and how. A missed notice can cost you the right to keep part of the deposit.

Rents matter for fees too. HUD's FY2027 fair market rent for a 2-bedroom in the Tampa metro fell 3.0%, from $1,977 to $1,917. A lower rent shrinks a percentage fee in dollars. A flat fee stays the same. See all bedroom sizes on the [Tampa fair market rent 2027 page](/guides/section-8-rent-limits/tampa-fair-market-rent-2027).

This is general information, not legal advice. Check the current law or ask a local attorney.

## Must a Tampa property manager hold a Florida license?

Yes, in most cases a Tampa property manager needs a Florida real estate license. Florida Statutes 475.01 defines a broker as a person who, for another and for pay, rents or negotiates the rental of real property. Section 475.42 says a person may not operate as a broker or sales associate without a valid, active license. A violation is a third-degree felony.

Section 475.011 exempts some roles. One example is a salaried employee of an apartment community owner who works in an onsite rental office.

Checklist before you sign:

- Get the broker's name and license number.
- Check the license status with the Florida Department of Business and Professional Regulation (DBPR).
- Match the licensed broker to the company on the agreement.

## Flat fee or percent: which model is common in Tampa?

A percentage of rent is the common model in Tampa. Eight of the 10 managers in our table charge a percentage on their main plan. Mynd and Ziprent charge only flat fees. Hoffman and Vintage offer both.

To find the break-even rent, divide the flat fee by the percentage:

- Mynd $129 vs 8%: $129 / 0.08 = $1,613.
- Ziprent $150 vs 8%: $150 / 0.08 = $1,875.
- LHT $200 cap vs 9%: $200 / 0.09 = $2,222. Above that rent, LHT's fee stops at $200.

At the FY2027 2-bedroom fair market rent of $1,917, both Mynd and Ziprent cost less per month than 8%. But Ziprent's $1,500 leasing fee erases that gain in year 1.

What to do: ask three managers for the full fee schedule. Put each one into the two-year table above. Pick the lowest two-year total for the service level you need.
