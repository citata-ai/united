## What does Hemlane software do?

Hemlane combines rental records and tenant workflows with optional help from people. Start by choosing which work you want the software to track and which work you want a service to handle.

Hemlane's pricing page lists advertising, screening, accounting, lease tools, documents, and rent collection across its plans. Not every item belongs to the free tier. Check the current plan comparison before building your process.

This review uses Hemlane's own pages read on 2026-10-19. It is a buying guide, not a claim from a hands-on trial or a current customer.

For a software test, use a sample lease and a maintenance request. Confirm how the tenant sees each item and how you find the resulting record. Ask where the task becomes your responsibility.

A useful demo should include an error. Try a wrong lease date or a payment posted outside the system. Ask how staff correct it and what history remains.

Do not decide from the feature count alone. Choose the plan that completes your actual rental workflow with a clear review process.

## What does leasing and local agent help add?

Hemlane's service add-ons can support tenant placement and other local work, but you need a property-specific scope. A software subscription alone does not establish local coverage.

Hemlane lists tenant placement and inspections separately on its pricing page. Ask whether those services are available at your property and whether a partner provides the work.

Define the handoff in writing. Name who schedules showings, handles inquiries, checks applications, prepares documents, and communicates the approved decision. Keep the owner approval step clear.

Ask what happens when the local provider cannot attend. You need a contact and an alternate process for missed appointments or urgent issues. Do not wait until a vacancy to learn the limit.

For repairs, distinguish coordination from paying the repair bill. Confirm who approves estimates, how spending limits work, and who follows up when the work remains incomplete.

Ask whether the service uses your vendors, its network, or both. Review insurance and access instructions through your own vendor process.

The right fit depends on the tasks you want to retain. A service bundle can help without replacing every duty of an owner or manager.

Do not assume the example total on the page covers your portfolio. Compare the formula and the service scope at your own unit count.

Request a separate list of add-on and usage costs. Ask who pays screening, card, placement, inspection, and repair charges. Keep optional services distinct from ongoing subscription fees.

Read the cancellation and service terms before committing. A low software charge and a broad service promise need different checks.

## How does Hemlane record Section 8 payments?

Hemlane support recommends separate requests for the tenant and subsidy portions so staff can track the payments independently. This is a software recording method, not a statement of voucher law.

The support guide describes a tracking-only co-tenant without an email address for the subsidy payer. Staff then record the subsidy as an offline transaction. Confirm the current interface during your demo.

Ask the vendor to show the tenant's view. The renter should see the correct request and clear payment status. Check whether a tracking record could trigger an unwanted message.

Test a changed subsidy amount, a late agency payment, and a payment covering different periods. Match the entries to the remittance information. Do not use a generic rent total as a substitute for payer detail.

Review late-fee settings with the responsible manager. Ask the vendor to show which request receives any configured fee and how staff correct a wrong result.

Keep the agency records and bank evidence with the reconciliation process. The software setup should make those records easier to compare.

## What are the alternatives, and who should choose Hemlane?

Choose Hemlane when its mix of software and defined service help matches the work you want to keep. Compare it with software-only products if you mainly need records and portals.

For a wider management suite, compare your required workflow with the platforms in our [AppFolio vs Buildium guide](/guides/appfolio-vs-buildium). Compare service help separately rather than treating every subscription as the same product.

An owner with reliable local support may need less coordination. An owner who lives far away may value a clear local service route. Neither case proves that a bundle covers every need.

Use this acceptance checklist:

- **Define the work:** List the tasks you retain and the tasks Hemlane will handle.
- **Confirm coverage:** Get a written property and location scope.
- **Test records:** Complete a lease, request, payment, and correction in a sample account.
- **Test approvals:** Confirm spending limits and the route for urgent decisions.
- **Check handoffs:** Name the person responsible when a provider or connection fails.
- **Price the full service:** Include the platform, units, selected services, and likely usage.
- **Plan an exit:** Export the records and identify any service transition steps.

Ask the vendor to resolve the open items before the subscription starts. Choose on the evidence of your test and written scope, not the breadth of a plan name.
