## Which sites does each tool reach?

Compare named sites, network rules, and actual delivery for your listing. A large headline count can include several related destinations and does not measure unique renters.

RentSpree's listing page names its direct partners and explains that each partner controls further distribution. Zillow's direct listing page names Zillow, Trulia, and HotPads. Apartments.com describes its own network.

For TurboTenant and Avail, ask for the current destination list tied to your plan. Avoid repeating older site counts from competitor reviews. Product pages and partner feeds can change.

Create a delivery sheet with the source listing, destination URL, publication status, and last checked date. Use it when you change rent, replace photos, or lease the unit.

Do not mark a feed successful merely because it accepted the listing. Open the public destination and check the result. A missing application link or wrong unit number can make delivery unusable.

## Which tools reach both Zillow and Apartments.com?

RentSpree explicitly names both Zillow and Apartments.com in its free listing offer. Its listing page says each partner decides which network sites display a listing.

Confirm your property and source account before relying on that route. Ask which source controls an existing listing and how you should handle a feed conflict. Get support help before adding another source.

Do not create duplicate source records to force delivery. That can leave different rents or contact details on the same unit. Choose the supported controlling source and keep its links.

When another landlord tool promises broad distribution, ask for confirmation of both networks in writing. A logo on a marketing page may not describe your account's current access.

Test a price change and off-market update on both destinations. Correct first publication is only part of the job. You also need accurate listings through the end of the vacancy.

## Is free syndication enough?

Free syndication can be enough when the listing reaches the right renters and your team responds well. Paid promotion should solve a measured problem, rather than replace basic listing work.

Start with correct rent, strong approved photos, clear fees, and an easy inquiry route. Check the mobile listing and application link. Broad reach cannot repair confusing property facts.

Track inquiries, showings, completed applications, and signed leases. Compare the outcome and staff work across channels. Do not call every inquiry a useful lead.

If the free listing gets little response, inspect the facts and local demand before buying more exposure. A rent or availability issue may matter more than placement.

Request the exact paid offer for your property. Keep promotion cost separate from screening, lease, or payment services. Free entry pricing does not mean every later action is free.

## How do PMS tools syndicate?

A PMS can send listing data through supported partner feeds, but the scope depends on its connections and contract. Ask your vendor to show the live destination settings for your account.

Identify which system is the source of truth for rent, photos, availability, and contact details. If leasing software sits above the PMS, define which direction each field moves.

Test a newly vacant unit, a change of rent, and a leased unit. Confirm that status reaches each important destination. Ask how staff see rejected or delayed updates.

Also test an integration outage. Find who owns support between the PMS and listing partner. A vague instruction to contact the other vendor can leave a vacancy poorly advertised.

Keep a manual correction route documented. Use it only through the supported process so the next feed does not overwrite the fix.

## How do you track which site brought the lead?

Keep the original inquiry source and follow the applicant through the leasing process. A website visit alone does not establish which listing produced a lease.

Use source tags or tracking contacts where the platform supports them. Ask staff to preserve the original field when they merge duplicates. Do not replace it with the last message channel.

Add a simple optional source question when a renter contacts you directly. Treat that answer as reported information, since a person may have seen the rental on several sites.

Build a report by listing source with completed applications, showings, leases, and staff follow-up work. Keep periods and status definitions consistent. Record major changes to rent or photos.

Use the results to decide the next channel test. For your own website's AI traffic, use the live [AI traffic tracking guide](/guides/track-ai-traffic-property-management). Listing attribution and AI website referrals are separate records.

## How should you test the choice before you pay?

Test the full workflow with sample records before you move live data. Use the same case in each product. Ask the person who will do the daily work to run the test. A sales demo alone does not show where your team will need help.

Keep a decision sheet with the task, result, missing step, and person who checked it. Mark each result as shown, promised, or still open. Ask for written terms when a feature needs an upgrade or another service. Include staff setup time in your cost review.

Check the exit path while you still control the choice. Export the sample records and open them outside the platform. Confirm that notes, files, dates, and links remain useful. Ask how you will get records after you close the account.
