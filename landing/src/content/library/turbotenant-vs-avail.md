## What does each free plan include?

TurboTenant and Avail both provide a useful entry point for a self-managing landlord. Their pricing pages list rental marketing, applications, screening, and rent collection among core tools.

Free refers to the landlord subscription. Applicants and renters may still pay fees. Review the current report and payment charges before you invite anyone.

Use the free account to test a full rental cycle. Create a sample listing, review an application, record a charge, and inspect a receipt. Check what happens when a tenant asks for help.

A free plan can suit a simple portfolio without meeting every manager's accounting needs. If you hold owner funds, run separate checks for client ledgers and reconciliation. Do not infer trust-account fit from an income and expense screen.

## What do the paid plans add?

Paid plans add different kinds of convenience, so buy the feature you will use. Avail's page links its upgrade to faster payments, customization, and waived bank-transfer fees. TurboTenant lists lease tools in Essentials and accounting in Pro.

Write down the task that causes your current delay. If you rebuild leases, test document editing and signing. If you spend time on bookkeeping, test categories, exports, and supporting receipts.

Ask whether the upgrade covers your whole account or selected units. Check what happens to documents and reports if you later downgrade. Do not assume a paid feature remains editable after cancellation.

Compare the cost over the term you plan to keep the tool. Include units, optional services, and staff setup. A simple monthly headline does not show every charge.

## How many listing sites does each use?

The current vendor pages describe broad listing distribution, but site counts need a defined network list. Avail's pricing page names partners such as Zumper, PadMapper, and Realtor.com. TurboTenant advertises rental distribution through its marketing tools.

Do not choose solely on a large network count. A group may include several sites with overlapping audiences. What matters is whether your rental appears correctly where local renters search.

Ask each vendor to confirm Zillow and Apartments.com for your exact listing. Then inspect the published results. Record the rent, fees, availability, photos, and application link.

Test a change after publication. If the rent updates in the source account but stays old on a partner site, the feed needs review. Ask who owns that fix and how staff see failed delivery.

## Which has state-specific leases?

Both vendors advertise state-specific lease tools, but plan access and editing features differ. Avail lists state-specific leases in its free tier and further customization in Unlimited Plus. TurboTenant's pricing page places online lease creation and signing in Essentials.

A state label does not establish that a lease fits every local rule or property. Review the exact template with local counsel before use. Keep approved clauses and required attachments together.

Test a renewal, a co-signer, and a change to an existing draft. Ask which steps require a paid upgrade. Also check the signed file export and how the system records each signer's action.

Do not use software suggestions as a substitute for document review. This is general information, not legal advice. Check the current law or ask a local attorney.

## How do ACH fees compare?

ACH means bank-to-bank electronic payments, and charges depend on the plan. Avail lists a tenant bank-transfer fee of $2.50 on Unlimited and waived ACH charges on Unlimited Plus.

TurboTenant's pricing page shows a $2 bank-transfer fee and free ACH for Pro landlords. Those figures come from the pages read 2026-10-19. Confirm the selected plan before you tell tenants what they will pay.

Review payment fees separately from the landlord subscription. Card charges, failed payments, and faster payout services can follow different rules. Ask who pays each charge and where the receipt shows it.

Then compare convenience with the cost your renters face. A tenant should see the payment method and fee before confirming. Test a failed payment and a refund so your staff can explain both.

Keep the final choice narrow. Pick the platform whose lease workflow and payment records meet your needs with the fewest unresolved steps. Use the trial evidence, rather than a competitor's comparison chart, to make that choice.

## How should you test the choice before you pay?

Test the full workflow with sample records before you move live data. Use the same case in each product. Ask the person who will do the daily work to run the test. A sales demo alone does not show where your team will need help.

Keep a decision sheet with the task, result, missing step, and person who checked it. Mark each result as shown, promised, or still open. Ask for written terms when a feature needs an upgrade or another service. Include staff setup time in your cost review.

Check the exit path while you still control the choice. Export the sample records and open them outside the platform. Confirm that notes, files, dates, and links remain useful. Ask how you will get records after you close the account.
