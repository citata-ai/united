In Goodjuju's fall 2026 study of 35 property management companies, Google Search and the Map Pack produced 50.6% of owner form leads. With Direct / Maps added, Google's local results were tied to 86.2% of owner leads (Goodjuju, 2026). The Map Pack is the map with 3 local businesses at the top of Google results. You cannot appear there without a verified Google Business Profile.

## Which verification methods does Google use now?

Google decides which verification methods you get, and you cannot change them. The options depend on your business type, public information, region and business hours. Sometimes Google asks for more than one method (Google Business Profile Help).

| Method | How it works | Watch for |
|---|---|---|
| Phone or text | Google calls or texts a code | An IVR phone menu blocks the code; answer the line yourself |
| Email | Google sends steps to an email address | Use an address you can open now |
| Video recording | You record and upload a video from a phone | Live, unedited, at least 30 seconds |
| Live video call | A Google support person watches you on camera | Only during your business hours, at your business |
| Postcard | Google mails a 5-digit code | Most arrive within 14 days; codes expire after 30 days |

Google recommends the video recording option when it is offered. Some profiles verify instantly, for example when you already verified the website in Google Search Console (Google Business Profile Help).

## What video proof does Google want?

Google wants a live, unedited video of at least 30 seconds. It must prove three things: where you are, that the business exists and that you manage it. You must record it through the Business Profile on a mobile device. Pre-recorded videos are not allowed (Google Business Profile Help).

The rules depend on your profile type.

**Storefront or hybrid office.** Most property management offices that meet owners and tenants on site are here. Your video must show:

1. **Location.** Street signs, the building number, nearby businesses.
2. **The business exists.** Your business name on a permanent sign, wall or window. The name must match your profile.
3. **You manage it.** Open the office door with your key, open a key cabinet, log in to your property management software.

**Service-area business.** A manager with no public office belongs here. Google says do not add your address to the profile if you do not serve customers there. Your video must show:

1. **Where you operate.** Street signs or landmarks near your business address. From a home office, record signs in your neighborhood.
2. **The business exists.** Tools, business cards, branded shirts, lockboxes, signs you put in yards.
3. **You manage it.** Open a branded vehicle with your key, or show a business permit, invoice or utility bill with the profile name.

Do not show bank account, tax or ID numbers, private information or other people's faces (Google Business Profile Help). For a property manager, that also means no tenant files, owner statements or keys tagged with addresses.

A 45-second shot plan for a storefront office:

| Seconds | Shot |
|---|---|
| 0–10 | Street sign at the corner, then pan to your building number |
| 10–20 | Walk to the door; show the permanent sign with your business name |
| 20–35 | Open the door with your key; show the front desk and branded material |
| 35–45 | Open the key cabinet or log in to your software (no tenant data on screen) |

## What if verification fails?

If verification fails, Google shows "Get verified" again or a "Review issues" notice on your profile. Follow the fix for each (Google Business Profile Help):

- **"Get verified" appears again.** Google could not fully verify you. Try again, or try another method if more than one is shown.
- **"Review issues" after a video.** Your video was not accepted. Read the reasons, then record a new video. Include everything from the first video plus the missing items.
- **Asked for more information.** Google often asks after you change business details. Select Get verified and finish the steps.

Common video reasons Google lists: business name not visible on the storefront, no nearby area shown, and missing proof that you may operate the business.

Two postcard mistakes to avoid. Do not edit your name, address or category while you wait, or the code will not work. Do not request a new code, because Google cancels the first one (Google Business Profile Help).

## Can a virtual office be verified?

No. Google says a rented mailing address where you do not operate, a virtual office, is not eligible for a Business Profile. P.O. boxes and remote mailboxes are not acceptable addresses (Google Business Profile guidelines).

The rules that matter for property managers:

- A co-working office counts only with clear signage, customers received during business hours and your own staff on site during those hours.
- A service-area business cannot list a "virtual" office unless it is staffed during business hours.
- Businesses that show an address should keep permanent fixed signage with the business name at that address.

If you manage from home, set up a service-area profile and hide the address. Do not rent a mailbox to get a pin in a better part of town. It breaks Google's guidelines.

## After you are verified

After verification, finish the profile and protect access:

1. **Add a second owner or manager** in your company, so one person leaving does not lock you out.
2. **Tag the website link** with UTM tracking codes. Goodjuju suggests `?utm_source=google&utm_medium=organic&utm_campaign=gbp` so Map Pack visits show as their own line in GA4 (Goodjuju, 2026).
3. **Fill every field** that applies: categories, service areas, hours, services, photos.
4. **Start a review routine.** Ask every happy owner and tenant, and reply to every review.
