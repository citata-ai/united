## What does a living trust do for a rental?

A living trust can set who manages the rental and who benefits from it. It can also plan for a successor when the current trustee cannot act. The document and the ownership records must work together.

Santa Clara Superior Court explains that a funded living trust can let a successor trustee manage or distribute trust assets without probate. Its guidance also stresses funding the trust through title transfers. A signed trust document alone does not show that a particular rental is in the trust.

Ask your estate attorney to check the deed and any entity interest. Identify whether the trust should hold the rental directly or hold an interest in an existing company. Do not use a new deed if the approved plan calls for a different transfer.

Use this planning checklist before signing:

| Issue | Question for your adviser | Record to confirm |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Ownership | What asset enters the trust? | Deed or ownership-interest record |
| Authority | Who can act after incapacity? | Trust and succession terms |
| Rental operations | Who instructs the manager? | Contact and authority record |
| Creditors | Which claims remain possible? | Local legal review |
| Taxes | How will basis and income be tracked? | Tax schedules and written advice |
| Insurance | Which parties need coverage? | Agent's written confirmation |

Choose a successor who can handle the rental's work or supervise qualified help. Discuss the role before naming that person. Explain where records sit and how urgent repair decisions will be made.

## Does a living trust protect against lawsuits?

Do not use a revocable living trust as your main liability plan. A trust you can revoke can leave property available to your creditors under state law. Separate estate transfer goals from protection against rental claims.

California Probate Code section 18200 allows settlor creditor claims against property subject to the settlor's power to revoke during life. Florida section 736.0505 similarly addresses revocable trust property, while preserving applicable exemptions. These are state examples, not a complete national analysis.

Santa Clara Superior Court's living trust guidance makes the same practical distinction. A living trust does not shelter the settlor from creditors. The settlor is the person who creates and funds the trust.

Ask counsel to review liability, insurance and ownership together. List who signs the lease, who directs repairs and who provides any guarantee. A title plan does not answer every question about those acts and contracts.

Give your insurance agent the proposed trust name and ownership map before making changes. Ask how the policy should identify the relevant interests. Confirm that the insurer understands the property remains a rental. Keep the reply with your estate documents.

If an asset-protection plan requires a different trust or entity structure, obtain advice specific to that plan. Do not treat revocable and irrevocable trusts as interchangeable. Ask what control you would give up and which duties you would gain.

## How does a trust affect inherited tax basis?

A trust's name alone does not determine inherited basis. IRS Publication 551 generally ties inherited-property basis to date-of-death fair market value, subject to exceptions and other valuation rules. It also addresses property received through a trust.

Publication 551 directs readers to inherited-property rules for a trust's basis in inherited property included in the settlor's gross estate. Ask your tax professional whether those conditions apply to the proposed plan. Do not promise beneficiaries a tax-free sale.

Owners often call the adjustment a step-up in basis. The value can also move down. The tax result depends on the property, ownership, estate inclusion and applicable exceptions. Review those facts rather than relying on the phrase.

Keep the rental's purchase records, improvements and depreciation schedules while you own it. Ask your successor to obtain a qualified valuation when needed. A later tax review needs evidence of value and ownership, not only a trust document.

If the rental remains in service after death, ask the tax professional how to report income and future depreciation. Do not keep using an old schedule without review. Separate the estate or trust's reporting duties from the beneficiary's own return.

For jointly owned or entity-held rentals, request a specific basis analysis. Do not assume the full property receives the same adjustment. Have the preparer identify the asset whose basis changes and the records needed to support that result.

## How can a living trust work with an LLC?

A trust and an LLC may address different parts of an ownership plan. Ask counsel whether the trust should hold an LLC interest while the LLC keeps the deed. The correct arrangement depends on the documents and state law.

Check the LLC's transfer and management provisions before assigning an interest. Ask who becomes the authorized manager after incapacity or death. A beneficiary's future economic interest does not by itself explain who can approve a repair today.

Review federal tax classification as a separate step. The IRS generally treats a single-member LLC as disregarded for income tax unless it elects corporate treatment. Its guidance also identifies separate rules for certain taxes. Adding a trust requires a review of the actual owner and tax facts.

Make a practical handover file for the successor. Include the lease, manager agreement, tenant deposit record, bank details and current repair list. Add lender and insurance contacts. Store sensitive access details securely and explain how the successor can obtain them lawfully.

Ask the manager what proof of authority it needs before accepting instructions from a successor trustee. Resolve that process with counsel while you can answer questions. Do not leave the manager to interpret competing family requests during an urgent repair.

Review the ongoing cost of the ownership plan with your advisers. Include legal work, accounting and property service costs. The [property management fee calculator](/tools/property-management-fee-calculator) can help with the rental management part. Then confirm that the approved transfer is complete and the records match.
