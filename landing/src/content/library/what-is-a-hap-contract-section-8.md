## What is a HAP contract?

A HAP contract is the agreement between a Section 8 owner and the public housing authority (PHA). HAP means Housing Assistance Payments. The contract says the PHA pays part of the rent to the owner each month, and the owner keeps the unit in good condition.

A Section 8 tenancy uses 3 documents:

| Document | Parties | What it does |
|----------|---------|--------------|
| HAP contract | Owner and PHA | PHA pays its share; owner meets HUD rules |
| Lease | Owner and tenant | Normal lease terms, plus the HUD tenancy addendum |
| Tenancy addendum | Part of the lease | HUD terms that override conflicting lease terms |

Under 24 CFR 982.451, the HAP contract must use the form HUD requires. Its term is the same as the lease term.

## Who signs the HAP contract?

The owner and the PHA sign the HAP contract. The tenant does not. Under 24 CFR 982.305(e), after the PHA approves the tenancy, "the owner and the PHA execute the HAP contract."

Timing matters. Under 24 CFR 982.305(c):

- The PHA must use best efforts to sign before the lease starts.
- The contract must be signed no later than 60 calendar days after the lease term begins.
- The PHA may not pay any HAP until the contract is signed.
- If it is signed within 60 days, the PHA pays for the time before signing.
- A HAP contract signed after the 60-day window is void.

What to do: if day 30 of the lease passes with no signed contract, call the PHA. Do not wait for day 59.

## When does the housing authority pay?

The housing authority pays its share each month, and federal rules require it to pay on time. Under 24 CFR 982.451, the PHA "must pay the housing assistance payment promptly when due." The PHA's share counts toward the monthly rent to owner. The tenant pays the rest.

Late payments: the HAP contract may require the PHA to pay a late penalty, but only if 3 conditions are true (24 CFR 982.451):

1. The penalty matches local practice and law for late rent.
2. The owner charges late penalties to both assisted and unassisted tenants.
3. The owner also charges the tenant for late family rent.

HUD can excuse the penalty if the delay was "due to factors beyond the PHA's control."

Worked example, with made-up numbers. Contract rent to owner is $1,600. The PHA sets its HAP at $1,150. The tenant's share is $450.

| Line | Amount |
|------|--------|
| Rent to owner (contract rent) | $1,600 |
| HAP from the PHA | $1,150 |
| Tenant's share | $450 |
| Most the owner may collect from the tenant | $450 |

Under 24 CFR 982.451, the owner "may not demand or accept any rent payment from the tenant in excess of this maximum." Any extra must be refunded to the tenant at once. Side payments are a serious program violation. To estimate the split for a real unit, use our [Section 8 rent calculator](/tools/section-8-rent-calculator).

## What happens to the HAP contract when the unit fails inspection?

When a unit fails inspection, the owner gets a deadline to fix it, and the PHA can withhold, then abate, the HAP. These rules are in 24 CFR 982.404 and apply to HAP contracts signed or renewed on or after June 6, 2024.

| Step | Rule | Deadline |
|------|------|----------|
| Life-threatening defect | Owner must fix it | 24 hours from notice |
| Other defect | Owner must fix it | 30 calendar days, or a PHA-approved extension |
| Withholding | PHA may hold payments after written notice | Paid back if fixed in time |
| Abatement | PHA must abate HAP if repairs miss the deadline | From the missed deadline |
| Termination | PHA must end the HAP contract | If not fixed within 60 days of the abatement notice |

Key points for owners:

- **Abated money is gone.** If you finish repairs within the 60 days, payments start again, but the PHA does not pay for the abated period.
- **No eviction for abatement.** The owner may not end the tenancy because the PHA withheld or abated the HAP.
- **Tenant damage is different.** If the PHA finds the tenant caused damage beyond normal use, it may waive the owner's repair duty. Then the HAP cannot be withheld or abated.

## Can rent change under a HAP contract?

Rent can change under a HAP contract, but only with notice to the PHA and a rent reasonableness check. Three rules apply:

1. **Initial term.** Under 24 CFR 982.309, the owner may not raise rent during the initial lease term.
2. **Notice.** Under 24 CFR 982.308(g)(4), the owner must tell the PHA about any rent change at least 60 days before it takes effect.
3. **Reasonableness.** Under 24 CFR 982.507, the PHA must redetermine the reasonable rent before any increase. The rent to owner may not exceed that reasonable rent.

The PHA compares your unit with similar unassisted units. It looks at location, quality, size, type, age, amenities, services, maintenance and utilities. Bring comps to support your ask. Payment standards also limit what a voucher covers. See our guide to [Section 8 rent limits](/guides/section-8-rent-limits).

## When does a HAP contract end?

A HAP contract ends when the lease ends or when the PHA ends the contract or the family's assistance (24 CFR 982.309). It also ends by itself. Under 24 CFR 982.455, "The HAP contract terminates automatically 180 calendar days after the last housing assistance payment to the owner."

Common end points:

- The tenant moves out at the end of the lease.
- The owner or the tenant ends the lease under its terms and local law.
- The PHA ends the contract after failed repairs.
- The family's income rises, the HAP drops to $0, and 180 days pass.

This is general information, not legal advice. Check the current law or ask a local attorney.

## HAP contract checklist for owners

Use this checklist for each new voucher tenancy:

- **Before move-in:** unit passes the initial inspection.
- **Day 1 of lease:** lease includes the HUD tenancy addendum.
- **By day 60:** HAP contract signed by you and the PHA.
- **Each month:** HAP received; tenant share collected, never more.
- **60+ days before a rent change:** written notice to the PHA, with comps.
- **Within 24 hours or 30 days of a failed item:** repair done and proof sent.
