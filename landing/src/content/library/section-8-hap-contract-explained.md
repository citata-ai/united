## Who are the parties to the HAP contract?

The parties to the HAP contract are the owner and the PHA. Part B of the HUD form (HUD-52641) opens with: "This is a HAP contract between the PHA and the owner." The tenant is not a party. 24 CFR 982.456(b) says the family "is not a party to or third party beneficiary of the HAP contract."

The tenant still has rights against you. Those rights come through the lease and its tenancy addendum, not through the HAP contract.

The HUD form has three parts:

| Part | What it holds |
|---|---|
| Part A: Contract information | Tenant, unit address, household members, lease dates, initial rent, initial HAP, utilities and appliances, deposit, signatures |
| Part B: Body of contract | 17 sections: maintenance, term, rent, payment, owner certifications, breach, access, assignment |
| Part C: Tenancy addendum | The HUD lease terms that must be in your lease word for word |

The PHA you sign with uses the current HUD edition. The sample we read is the 8/2009 edition posted by the Santa Cruz County housing authority. Ask your PHA for the current form before you sign.

## What does the owner promise?

The owner promises to follow the contract and the lease. Section 8 of Part B lists the owner's certifications for the whole term:

1. **Standards.** You maintain the unit and premises to HUD's standards (HQS now, NSPIRE by 2027).
2. **Lease.** The lease includes the tenancy addendum, and you gave the PHA a copy.
3. **Rent.** The rent is not more than you charge for comparable unassisted units in the premises.
4. **No side payments.** You receive no payment for the unit other than the rent to owner, from anyone.
5. **No tenant ownership.** The family has no ownership interest in the unit.
6. **Residence.** To your knowledge, the family lives in the unit as its only home.
7. **No close relatives.** You are not the parent, child, grandparent, grandchild, sister or brother of a family member. The PHA can approve an exception as a reasonable accommodation.

The PHA promises to pay its share "at the beginning of each month" and "promptly when due" (Part B, section 7). The first month is prorated.

24 CFR 982.452 adds the owner's day-to-day duties. You screen and select the tenant and keep up the unit. You collect the tenant share and any deposit, enforce the lease, and pay the utilities the lease assigns to you.

## How does the HAP contract relate to my lease and the tenancy addendum?

The HAP contract and the lease are linked, and each depends on the other. The rules:

- **Same term.** The HAP contract term is the same as the lease term (24 CFR 982.451(a)(2)).
- **Addendum in both.** The tenancy addendum is Part C of the HAP contract. It must be in your lease word for word (24 CFR 982.308(f)).
- **Addendum wins.** If the lease conflicts with the addendum, the addendum controls.
- **One ends, both end.** If the HAP contract ends for any reason, the lease ends automatically (tenancy addendum, section 9). If the lease ends, the HAP contract ends (24 CFR 982.309(b)).

The Santa Cruz County housing authority sums it up: the landlord and tenant have "a direct relationship that the Housing Authority is not party to."

## Can the PHA end the HAP contract?

Yes. Section 4(b) of Part B lists when the contract ends. Some reasons are automatic. Others are PHA choices.

| Reason the HAP contract ends | Automatic or PHA choice |
|---|---|
| The owner or tenant ends the lease | Automatic |
| The PHA ends assistance for the family | Automatic |
| The family moves out | Automatic |
| 180 days pass after the last HAP payment | Automatic (24 CFR 982.455) |
| A single-member household dies | Automatic |
| Program funding is not enough | PHA choice (24 CFR 982.454) |
| The unit is too small after a family change | PHA choice |
| The family breaks up | PHA choice |
| The unit fails standards or the owner breaches the contract | PHA choice |

An owner breach includes any violation of the HAP contract and fraud or bribery in a federal housing program. Drug-related or violent criminal activity by the owner also counts (24 CFR 982.453). PHA remedies include recovery of overpayments, abatement, termination of payments and termination of the contract.

## What happens to the HAP contract when the tenant moves out?

The HAP contract ends automatically when the family moves out. 24 CFR 982.311(d) sets the money rule:

- The PHA "may not make any housing assistance payment to the owner for any month after the month when the family moves out."
- "The owner may keep the housing assistance payment for the month when the family moves out."

The family must tell you and the PHA before it moves out (24 CFR 982.309(c)). If you receive a payment for a later month, return it. The Santa Cruz County housing authority says the last month is not prorated. Depending on the date, you may keep the full payment or have to pay it back.

A sale works differently. The Santa Cruz County housing authority says the contract cannot be assigned without its consent. The new owner must agree in writing to be bound by it. Tacoma Housing Authority says the new owner must honor the current lease. The new owner may ask the tenant to leave only after it ends, with proper notice.

## Can I add my own lease terms?

Yes, if they follow the rules. Under 24 CFR 982.308(b), if you use a standard lease for unassisted tenants, you use the same form for the voucher tenant. You add the addendum. Your own terms are allowed if they are consistent with state and local law and the addendum.

Watch for these limits:

- [ ] No term that conflicts with the addendum. The addendum controls.
- [ ] No extra charges for items that unassisted tenants get at no cost (24 CFR 982.510).
- [ ] Any lease change in writing, with a copy to the PHA at once (24 CFR 982.308(g)).
- [ ] A new HAP contract if you change who pays utilities or the lease term.
