## Which Oakland rentals are under the RAP?

Oakland's Rent Adjustment Program (RAP) runs 2 ordinances in Oakland Municipal Code Chapter 8.22: the Rent Adjustment Ordinance, which caps rent, and the Just Cause for Eviction Ordinance (Measure EE), which limits evictions. They cover different sets of units.

| Rule | Units covered | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Rent cap (Rent Adjustment Ordinance) | Units with a certificate of occupancy issued before January 1, 1983 | California Attorney General chart of OMC 8.22 |
| Just cause for eviction | Most rental units, except an owner who shares a kitchen or bath with the tenant, and units with a certificate of occupancy from the past 10 years | RAP Just Cause info sheet |
| RAP fee | Units covered by either ordinance | City of Oakland fee page |

So a 1990 fourplex is outside the Oakland rent cap, but it is under Oakland just cause and the RAP fee. State law (AB 1482) may then cap its rent instead.

The City of Oakland publishes exemption flyers, including a single-family home exemption certificate. If you think a unit is exempt, petition the RAP for the exemption in writing before you rely on it.

## What is the Oakland yearly CPI rent increase?

The Oakland yearly CPI rent increase is 2.3% from August 1, 2026 to July 31, 2027. Since a June 2022 amendment, the formula is 60% of the change in CPI, or 3%, whichever is lower.

| Effective date | Allowable increase |
|---|---|
| August 1, 2026 | 2.3% |
| August 1, 2025 | 0.8% |
| August 1, 2024 | 2.3% |
| August 1, 2023 | 2.5% |
| August 1, 2022 | 3% |

Source: City of Oakland, allowable rent increases page, read 2026-10-20.

The rules for a CPI increase, per RAP:

1. **Once in 12 months.** And not earlier than 12 months after the tenant moved in.
2. **RAP Notice first.** No increase until at least 6 months after the tenant first got the RAP Notice. Give it at move-in and with every rent increase notice.
3. **Registration first.** You cannot raise rent on a covered unit that is not registered with RAP.
4. **30 days' written notice.** In a fixed-term lease, wait until the term ends unless the lease allows the increase.
5. **Business tax.** Since April 15, 2025, you may not raise rent if you are delinquent on Oakland business taxes. Attach a current Business Tax Certificate, or for a CPI-only increase, a signed payment plan.

Banking is allowed, with limits. If you skip a year, you may "bank" the increase. One banked increase may not exceed 3 times the current CPI increase. Since January 1, 2026, banked increases expire after 5 years, not 10. Since December 24, 2024, banked increases expire when the property changes owners, except for some family inheritances.

Worked example: a covered two-bedroom at $2,805 a month, HUD's FY2027 Fair Market Rent for the Oakland metro. The 2.3% CPI increase allows $64.52 more a month, for a new rent of $2,869.52. If you banked last year's 0.8% too, the total is 3.1%, or $86.96, within the 3 × 2.3% = 6.9% limit. See the [Oakland Fair Market Rent 2027 page](/guides/section-8-rent-limits/oakland-fair-market-rent-2027) for other sizes.

New tenants reset the base. Under Costa-Hawkins, you may set the first rent for a tenancy that began on or after January 1, 1996. The RAP adds an exception: no vacancy increase for a unit cited for serious code violations that stayed uncorrected more than 60 days before the vacancy.

## What is the RAP program fee and who pays it?

The RAP fee is $137 per unit as of July 1, 2025, per the City of Oakland. You pay it each year for every unit covered by the Rent Adjustment Ordinance or the Just Cause Ordinance.

| Item | Amount or rule |
|---|---|
| RAP fee | $137 per covered unit per year |
| Tenant share | Half, $68.50, if you paid the fee on time |
| Business tax | Required for all Oakland landlords, due March 1, based on gross rental income |
| Owner-occupied | May qualify for a business tax exemption or refund |

Worked example: a 6-unit pre-1983 building. The RAP fee is 6 × $137 = $822. If you pay on time, you may collect 6 × $68.50 = $411 back from tenants, so your net cost is $411.

Late fees and late business taxes cost more than money. A delinquent business tax account blocks rent increases and no-fault evictions.

## What does Oakland Measure EE require for evictions?

Oakland Measure EE, the Just Cause for Eviction Ordinance in OMC 8.22.300, lets an owner end a tenancy only for a listed cause. The RAP info sheet lists 10:

1. **Failure to pay rent**, of at least one month of HUD Fair Market Rent for a unit of the same size in the Oakland metro.
2. **Material lease violation** after written notice to stop.
3. **Substantial damage** to the unit.
4. **Continued disturbance** of other tenants after written notice.
5. **Unlawful activity** on the premises.
6. **Refusing access** for repairs after proper written notice.
7. **Owner returns** to their principal residence under a written agreement with the tenants.
8. **Owner or family move-in** for at least 36 months.
9. **Ellis Act** withdrawal from the rental market.
10. **Substantial repairs** that need the unit empty, for health and safety.

The first cause sets a real threshold. Using the $2,805 two-bedroom FMR, a tenant must owe at least that amount before nonpayment is a just cause.

Not valid causes, per RAP: lease expiration, sale of the property (including foreclosure), condo conversion, or a change in a unit's Section 8 status.

Process rules:

- **State the ground** in the notice and say that advice is available from RAP.
- **File a copy** of every eviction notice with RAP within 10 days of service. Failure is a defense in the eviction case.
- **No-fault notices** (owner move-in or substantial repairs) must include a current Business Tax Certificate and a statement about the business-tax limit, since December 24, 2024.

Tenants also have more time to fight increases. Since December 24, 2024, a tenant may petition against an unlawful rent increase within 180 days of the notice, up from 90.
