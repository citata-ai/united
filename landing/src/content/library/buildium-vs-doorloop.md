## How do the entry plans compare?

Buildium Essential and DoorLoop One differ in price basis and included work. Start with the current feature and price table.

| Item | Buildium | DoorLoop |
|---|---|---|
| Entry plan | Essential | One |
| Published price | From $62 monthly | $2.50 per unit monthly displayed |
| Minimum | Unit minimum not stated | $199 monthly minimum displayed |
| Higher plans | Growth and Premium | Connect and Multiply |
| Entry focus | Basic accounting, maintenance and leasing | Core platform with AI Assistant |
| Connection question | Open API listed in Premium | Full API access described in One |

Sources: [Buildium pricing](https://www.buildium.com/pricing/) and [DoorLoop pricing](https://www.doorloop.com/pricing), read 2026-10-19. DoorLoop offers monthly and annual billing; confirm the term behind displayed rates.

DoorLoop's current page differs from older tables that list Starter, Pro and Premium. It says existing plans and prices remain in place. Ask whether your offer is a new plan or an existing-account arrangement.

The lowest starting amount does not establish the lowest total bill. Compare the full work you need at your actual unit count.

## Which has more features at the base tier?

DoorLoop describes a broad core platform in One, while Buildium places some advanced work in higher tiers. More named features do not prove a better fit.

Buildium Essential highlights basic accounting, maintenance, leasing, communications and reporting. Premium highlights Open API access and the broader automation suite. Growth adds reporting, communications and analytics capabilities.

DoorLoop describes accounting, leasing, maintenance, owner access and API access in One. Its higher plans add more agent work. Ask the vendor to mark any feature-table exceptions in your quote.

Create a required-action list before counting features. A single missing approval or export can matter more than several optional tools.

Have each vendor demonstrate the same owner statement, vendor payment and lease renewal. Ask the operator to complete the task from the offered base plan.

If an action needs an add-on or upgrade, record the price beside it. That produces a useful comparison instead of a long list of checkmarks.

## How do their accounting tools compare?

Both products describe property accounting, but your accountant should test the exact controls and reports. Neither feature page proves an accounting-quality winner.

Buildium lists bank reconciliation, financial reporting and payment approvals. DoorLoop describes accounting, reporting, bank matching and owner distributions.

Use a sample with an owner reserve and an unpaid vendor bill. Ask the vendor to explain available cash before distributing funds. Then return a tenant payment and inspect the revised records.

Check these points:

- **Detail:** can you trace a report total to source entries?
- **Corrections:** does a reversal preserve the change history?
- **Approvals:** can staff prepare a payment without authorizing it?
- **Access:** can owners see only their own property records?
- **Close:** can your accountant explain outstanding items?
- **Export:** do downloaded records remain usable outside the product?

Do not accept a balancing entry that nobody can explain. The test should finish with a clear account of the money.

## How do payment and leasing fees change cost?

Payment and leasing activity can change the price comparison, so add usage to the subscription. Use company-paid and resident-paid columns.

Buildium's pricing page lists incoming EFT at $2.35 on Essential and $1.35 on Growth. EFT means a bank transfer. Premium lists an introductory free period followed by a fee.

DoorLoop's current table lists incoming ACH at $1.50 on One and free on Connect and Multiply. ACH is a bank-transfer network. Confirm who pays each charge and which payment types qualify.

Buildium lists signatures at $5 per document on Essential and $1 on Growth, with signatures included in Premium. DoorLoop's page contains both broad included-signature wording and per-document fee rows.

Treat that DoorLoop inconsistency as a quote question. Ask sales to state the actual signature fee for the offered plan. Do not resolve unclear terms by assuming the cheapest reading.

Build a first-year and renewal-year budget. Include onboarding, bank setup, screening and required outside tools. Keep staff training estimates separate from vendor fees.

## Which is easier for a small team?

The easier system is the one your team can use correctly without constant help. Test real work with the people who will perform it.

Have a leasing user find an applicant, send a document and record a renewal. Have a maintenance user assign a repair and attach the invoice. Have an accountant close the sample period.

Observe rather than guide every click. Record where users need help or repeat entry. Ask the vendor to explain the training available for those points.

Buildium says live phone support comes with Growth and Premium. DoorLoop describes support channels and priority differences on its pricing page. Confirm the channel for your plan and urgent issue type.

For setup, Buildium requires onboarding for new Growth and Premium customers. Its [onboarding page](https://www.buildium.com/features/onboarding/) names imported entity types. DoorLoop says onboarding cost depends on portfolio size.

Get import scope and acceptance checks in writing before planning a move.

## Which has better reviews on Capterra and G2?

Vendor pages cannot establish an independent review-score winner, so this guide does not publish one. Current ratings would need separate review-site verification.

Use reviews as a source of questions, not a replacement for your demo. Look for the same portfolio type, plan and task. Old reviews may describe plans no longer offered to new buyers.

Ask each vendor for a reference with a similar operating model. Prepare questions about the last close, recent support case and data move. A general satisfaction statement gives little detail.

Use the [AppFolio and Buildium guide](/guides/appfolio-vs-buildium) if your short list needs another option. Check affordability with the [fee calculator](/tools/property-management-fee-calculator).

Choose only after staff tasks, accounting results and written terms pass review. Keep the evidence with the contract so future plan changes are easier to assess.
