## What does each free plan include?

Both free plans support basic rental financial work. Stessa Essentials lists bank feeds, basic reports, and rent collection. Baselane Core lists property-level income and expense tracking, banking tools, and rent collection.

Use the free tier to test how each product handles your records. Import a clean sample of rent receipts, bills, transfers, and refunds. Check whether the categories remain correct after you edit a record.

A dashboard can look useful while hiding missing transactions. Compare the total with your bank statement. Find a transaction from its receipt, then find the receipt from the transaction.

Decide whether you need an investor view or a daily money process. That choice can shape the trial better than a long feature list. Neither free plan automatically proves fit for client trust accounting.

## Which plan gives Schedule E reports?

Baselane Core lists Schedule E reports, while Stessa lists the report in Manage. This is a direct plan difference on the vendor pricing pages.

The report's presence does not prove that every transaction belongs in its suggested category. Ask your tax professional to review the mapping. Keep a note for expenses that need separate treatment.

Test a report with your actual property structure. Check ownership shares, property names, and the period covered. Confirm that an exported file carries enough detail to trace a total back to its records.

Do not buy a subscription solely for a form label. First show the sample export to the person who prepares your return. This is general information, not tax advice. Check the current law or ask a tax professional.

## How do their banking features differ?

Stessa pairs rental records with Cash Management, while Baselane centers property banking and bookkeeping. Compare the account structure and daily controls before you compare promotional rates.

Baselane's pricing page names Thread Bank as the banking provider and states that Baselane is a financial technology company. Read the account terms alongside the software terms.

List the accounts you need by property and entity. Check who can view an account, approve a transfer, and change bank details. A convenient dashboard should not make it easy to send money from the wrong entity.

Avoid choosing on yield figures alone. Rates and conditions change, and the reviewed Stessa page shows different rate displays within the page. Confirm current account terms directly before you move funds.

Keep an exit plan for banking as well as software. Ask how you download statements and close an account without losing your records.

## Which has better rent collection?

The better rent collection tool is the one that clearly connects a tenant charge, receipt, payout, and bank entry. Both vendors list rent collection on their pricing pages.

Test a normal payment and a failed payment. Find where the fee appears and who receives the notice. Then check how the ledger changes after a return.

Also test a payment received outside the platform. A tenant may pay through another approved route. Your books still need the receipt without a duplicate charge or false late balance.

Ask about partial payments, credits, and account changes before launch. Check whether a tenant can understand the balance from the portal. Do not assume faster payout claims apply to every account or payment method.

Review rent reminders with your own staff. Make sure the notice uses the posted balance and does not treat a pending payment as settled cash.

## What do the paid plans cost?

Stessa offers Manage and Pro, while Baselane lists Smart above Core. The table gives annual-plan monthly equivalents from the pricing pages read 2026-10-19.

Stessa also lists Manage at $15 and Pro at $35 when paid monthly. Compare the full term you must pay for, rather than only the monthly equivalent.

Stessa Pro adds advanced reports and transaction tools. Baselane Smart adds automation, receipt matching, shared access, and further reports. Test the paid feature that solves your current problem before upgrading.

Create a cost sheet with subscription, payment costs, outside accounting help, and setup time. Keep bank service charges in a separate line. Your total financial workflow may involve more than a software subscription.

A self-managing owner may choose on personal bookkeeping needs. A third-party manager should also test owner ledgers, fund controls, and reporting duties. Ask an accountant to confirm the required setup before replacing an established accounting process.

## How should you test the choice before you pay?

Test the full workflow with sample records before you move live data. Use the same case in each product. Ask the person who will do the daily work to run the test. A sales demo alone does not show where your team will need help.

Keep a decision sheet with the task, result, missing step, and person who checked it. Mark each result as shown, promised, or still open. Ask for written terms when a feature needs an upgrade or another service. Include staff setup time in your cost review.

Check the exit path while you still control the choice. Export the sample records and open them outside the platform. Confirm that notes, files, dates, and links remain useful. Ask how you will get records after you close the account.

Choose a tool when the test proves a clear gain for your workflow. Keep your current process until the new process passes. Use the [property management fee calculator](/tools/property-management-fee-calculator) to see how software costs affect your service margin.
