## Which PMS tools include two-way texting?

AppFolio and Buildium document resident messaging, so start by testing the tools already tied to your property records. Confirm replies and history in the plan you intend to buy.

| AppFolio | Central inbox; individual and mass texts | Residential Core starts at $1.49 per unit monthly; minimums apply | Message fees and plan access |

Sources: AppFolio communication and pricing pages, and Buildium Resident Center and pricing, read on 2026-10-19. These are platform starting prices, not all-in texting quotes.

Test a conversation with approved sample content. Send a message, reply from the recipient side, assign the thread, and hand it to another staff member.

Ask where messages live after a tenant moves or a staff member leaves. Keep the record attached to the correct relationship without depending on a private phone.

## What are the consent rules for text messages?

Build a documented consent and opt-out process for your actual messaging service and use. Do not assume a phone number authorizes every message.

Twilio's Messaging Policy requires prior express consent for messages through its services and a way to withdraw consent. It distinguishes informational and promotional messaging. These are provider requirements, not a complete explanation of all applicable law.

Ask your software vendor which messaging provider it uses and which policies apply. Have a local attorney review the collection language and planned message types.

Keep the consent source, scope, date, and withdrawal status in an approved record. Staff should check whether a contact can receive the intended message before sending.

Test an opt-out from the recipient side. Confirm that later messages stop through every connected workflow. A stopped campaign should not continue through another reminder rule.

Plan another contact route when a person opts out. Keep service communication available through appropriate channels without overriding the withdrawal.

This is general information, not legal advice. Check the current law or ask a local attorney.

## How do you send mass notices?

Send mass texts through a reviewed audience and message, with a named person responsible for replies. A broad send needs more checks than a private conversation.

AppFolio describes mass text sending. Buildium describes property-wide announcements and SMS. Confirm audience filters, exclusions, and limits in your account.

Choose the audience from current records. Remove former residents and contacts whose consent does not cover the message. Check duplicate or shared phone numbers.

Preview the text as the renter will see it. Identify the sender, state the practical point, and give a clear contact route. Keep private balances out of broad notices.

Use a sample audience first. Check that recipients cannot see each other's phone numbers or replies. Confirm where failed delivery appears.

Assign the reply queue before sending. A notice can prompt repair reports or wrong-number responses. Someone must review replies and update the records.

Do not assume a mass text satisfies formal notice requirements. Have the approved process checked for the notice type and location.

## How do you keep a record of messages?

Keep content, recipients, timestamps, reply history, and staff ownership in a company-controlled system. Delivery status and human response should remain distinct.

A sent label does not show that a person read the message. Ask the vendor to explain each status and the evidence available for it.

Test an export before choosing a tool. Confirm whether attachments, failed sends, opt-outs, and staff notes appear. Search records by tenant, property, and date.

Keep internal notes separate from resident messages. Test the difference explicitly. A mistaken note should not become an automatic external reply.

Review access. Staff should reach assigned work without browsing unrelated private records. Remove a sample employee and confirm the company retains the conversation history.

Set a retention rule with your adviser and operating lead. This guide does not prescribe a universal period. The system should support your adopted policy.

Ask how corrections work. A deleted or edited message needs a clear record when your process requires review of what originally happened.

Keep a company owner for the sending number too. A staff change should not leave the business unable to receive replies or recover access.

## Can AI answer tenant texts?

AI can help prepare replies, but your team needs limits before it sends without review. AppFolio markets AI suggestions and automated communication workflows.

Start with draft-only help for routine office information. Check the source and wording before sending. Do not let an assistant invent a repair date or payment agreement.

Route urgent safety reports to the approved human response process. A reply should not imply that a technician has been assigned when no assignment exists.

Use a test set before enabling more actions:

- **Routine question:** Check that the reply uses current approved office information.
- **Missing detail:** Confirm the assistant asks for the needed fact rather than guessing.
- **Private record:** Check whether a reply exposes unrelated tenant information.
- **Opt-out:** Confirm automation stops after consent is withdrawn.
- **Sensitive request:** Require a human handoff for accommodation or legal issues.
- **Wrong recipient:** Confirm staff can stop the send and correct the record.
- **No reply owner:** Keep unanswered threads visible to the team.

Review the results with staff who handle resident messages. Keep failed examples and update the rules before widening access.
