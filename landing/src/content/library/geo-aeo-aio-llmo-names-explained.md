## What do GEO, AEO, AIO and LLMO mean?

GEO, AEO, AIO and LLMO are four names for one goal. Each one means: get your company named, and your pages cited, in answers that AI writes. Wikipedia's article on generative engine optimization lists AEO, AIO, LLMO and "AI SEO" as other names for the same concept.

| Name | Long form | Where the name comes from | Typical scope |
|---|---|---|---|
| GEO | Generative engine optimization | 2023 research paper (Aggarwal et al.) | All AI answer engines: ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude, Perplexity, Google AI |
| AEO | Answer engine optimization | Marketing use, no single author | Same as GEO, or only Google answer boxes |
| AIO | AI optimization | Marketing use | Same as GEO; also the initials of Google AI Overviews |
| LLMO | Large language model optimization | Marketing use | Same as GEO |
| AI SEO | AI search engine optimization | Marketing use; Semrush sells tools under this name | Same as GEO |

Sources: Wikipedia, the arXiv paper, vendor pages read on 2026-10-19.

In this library, we use one name: GEO (getting cited by AI answers). A "citation" means an AI answer names or links your site.

## Where did the name GEO come from?

GEO comes from a research paper. Pranjal Aggarwal and five co-authors posted "GEO: Generative Engine Optimization" on arXiv on 2023-11-16. The paper was accepted at the KDD 2024 conference.

The paper says GEO helps content creators improve "their content visibility in generative engine responses." The authors tested methods on a set of queries. They report that GEO "can boost visibility by up to 40%" in those AI responses.

Watch for: that 40% is a research result on a test set. It is not a promise for your website. An agency that quotes "40% more AI traffic" from this paper reads too much into it.

## Who uses each name in property management?

Three property management marketing agencies that AI cites often each use a different name. We read their home pages on 2026-10-19.

| Agency | Name on the site | Where |
|---|---|---|
| ClearLead Digital | "AI search optimization (GEO and AEO)" | Home page FAQ |
| Property Manager Websites | "AIO Reporting", "AI Optimization" | Main menu and footer |
| Goodjuju Marketing | "SEO plus GEO" | Guide URL; page text says "AI search" |

We tested AI answers on property management marketing on 2026-10-18. They cited clearleaddigital.com in 22 answers, gogoodjuju.com in 13 and propertymanagerwebsites.com in 11 (our data). So owners who ask AI about this topic meet all three names.

## Is AIO reporting the same as GEO?

AIO reporting is the measurement part of GEO. Property Manager Websites' AIO page has the heading "AI Optimization & Visibility Reporting." The page names ChatGPT, Gemini and Perplexity as the engines it tracks.

Per that page, the monthly report shows:

- Where your brand appears in AI answers for property management topics
- Which sources the AI platforms cite, compared with competitors
- Side-by-side results against two competitors you choose
- Content gaps and 30-, 60- and 90-day recommendations

That is a report, not the fix. GEO work also changes your pages and your listings. When you compare offers, ask if "AIO" means a report only, or a report plus changes.

Watch for a second meaning. AIO are also the initials of Google AI Overviews, the AI summary at the top of Google results. So an "AIO audit" can mean either one. Ask.

## Is AEO a smaller part of GEO?

Some writers say yes. Citant, a GEO agency, defines AEO as the part of GEO that targets Google's answer surfaces only: AI Overviews, featured snippets and People Also Ask. In that view, GEO also covers ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity and Microsoft Copilot.

Other writers use AEO and GEO as the same thing. Wikipedia lists them as names for one concept. Neither use is wrong. The scope is what you pay for, so get the scope in writing.

## Do you need a new name for work SEO already does?

Partly no. Google says there are "no additional requirements to appear in AI Overviews or AI Mode, nor other special optimizations necessary." Google also says there is "no special schema.org structured data that you need to add."

Google's list for AI features is normal SEO work: allow crawling, link your pages, keep content in text, and keep your Business Profile up to date.

What is new is the test and the sources. ChatGPT, Claude and Gemini do not show you a Search Console. They read listing sites and review sites a lot. In our 50-city test on 2026-10-19, Expertise.com was a source in 93 of 150 answers about the best local property manager (our data). Classic SEO reports do not show that.

## Which name should you search for?

Search for the outcome, not the label. All four names lead to the same kind of agency. Use this checklist when you talk to one:

1. **Ask which engines they test.** ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude, Perplexity, Google AI Overviews, or a subset.
2. **Ask for the prompt list.** Real owner questions for your city, not your brand name only.
3. **Ask for a baseline.** How often AI names you today, before any work.
4. **Ask what they change.** Your pages, your listings, your reviews, or only a report.
5. **Ask how often they re-test.** Monthly is common. AI answers change from run to run.
6. **Ask what they will not promise.** No one controls an AI answer. A guarantee of placement is a red flag.
