## Which license must a Phoenix property manager hold, and how do I look it up?

A Phoenix property manager must hold an Arizona real estate license from the Arizona Department of Real Estate (ADRE). ARS 32-2101 defines a real estate broker as a person who, for another and for compensation, "rents or leases real estate" or "collects or offers, attempts or agrees to collect rent." ARS 32-2122 makes it unlawful to do that work without a license.

One narrow exception exists. A clerical worker of a licensed broker who collects rent in person, as part of clerical duties, and gives a receipt does not need a license (ARS 32-2101).

How to check a manager in 4 steps:

1. **Ask for the firm's designated broker.** ARS 32-2173 says the designated broker, or the broker's authorized licensee, signs your agreement.
2. **Look up the person and the firm** on the ADRE website's public license database.
3. **Match the names.** The firm on the contract must match the licensed entity.
4. **Ask about any complaint** or discipline you find on the record.

## Which local rules must a Phoenix manager know?

A good Phoenix manager knows the Arizona statutes that set your duties as an owner. Use these as test questions.

| Rule | What the law says | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Assessor registration | Keep owner name, address, phone, parcel number and year built on file with the county assessor. Update within 10 days of a change. | ARS 33-1902(A) |
| Out-of-state owners | Name an Arizona statutory agent with the assessor | ARS 33-1902(B) |
| Unregistered rental | The tenant may give a 10-day notice and then end the lease, with prepaid rent returned | ARS 33-1902(C) |
| City penalty, newly bought rental | $1,000 plus $100 a month until you comply | ARS 33-1902(E) |
| City penalty, other rentals | Up to $150 a day | ARS 33-1902(G) |
| Deposit cap | 1.5 months' rent, prepaid rent included | ARS 33-1321(A) |
| Deposit return | Itemized list and refund within 14 days, not counting weekends and holidays, after move-out and the tenant's demand | ARS 33-1321(D) |
| Move-in papers | Signed lease, a move-in damage form, and notice that the tenant may attend the move-out inspection | ARS 33-1321(C) |

ARS 33-1902(F) gives some relief: if you comply within 10 days of the complaint, the court must dismiss it. In Maricopa County, the Assessor runs the registration online.

## What should a Phoenix management agreement say?

A Phoenix management agreement must follow ARS 32-2173. It must be in "clear, unambiguous language" and must:

1. **State all material terms** of the manager's duties to you.
2. **Be signed** by you and the firm's designated broker or the broker's authorized licensee.
3. **Give a start date and an end date.**
4. **Hold cancellation terms** both sides agree to.
5. **Say how all money is handled**, including tenant deposits.
6. **Name the type and frequency** of reports to you.
7. **State the reserve**: the amount and purpose of money held for emergencies.
8. **Say who gets interest** on trust account money.
9. **State the fees** you pay.
10. **Ban assignment** to another licensee without your written consent.

The law also allows some terms. An automatic renewal is allowed only if the firm sends a reminder at least 30 days before the renewal date. A reasonable early cancellation fee is allowed.

On exit, ARS 32-2173 sets a timeline:

| When | What the firm must give you |
|---|---|
| At once | All leases, applications, inventories, notices, keys, plans, warranties and reports |
| Within 5 days | A list of all tenant security obligations |
| Within 35 days | The money left in your accounts, less unpaid bills from the term |
| Within 75 days | A final receivables and payables list and a bank reconciliation |

Bring this table to the signing. If the draft contract does not match it, ask why.

## Which questions should I ask a Phoenix manager before I sign?

Ask questions that need a document or a number. Twelve to start with:

1. Who is your designated broker, and what is the firm's license number?
2. How many doors do you manage in Phoenix, and how many near my address?
3. Show me your standard agreement. Where is each ARS 32-2173 term?
4. What reserve do you hold, and where is it written?
5. Who gets the interest on the trust account?
6. How do you keep my rental registered with the Maricopa County Assessor?
7. Show me a sample monthly owner statement.
8. What is your repair approval limit, and do you mark up vendor bills?
9. How do you handle a dead air conditioner in July, and how fast?
10. How do you screen applicants with one written standard for all?
11. Does the contract renew on its own, and when do I get the 30-day reminder?
12. Can I call 2 owners who have used you for more than 2 years?

The screening question matters for Fair Housing. One written standard for every applicant protects you.
