## What comparable units does the PHA use?

The PHA compares your unit with comparable unassisted units. 24 CFR 982.507(b) says the PHA "must determine whether the rent to owner is a reasonable rent in comparison to rent for other comparable unassisted units." It must consider seven factors:

| Factor (982.507(b)) | What helps your case |
|---|---|
| Location | Comps in the same neighborhood or ZIP code |
| Quality | Comps with similar finishes and condition |
| Size | Comps with the same bedroom count and close square footage |
| Unit type | Same type: house, duplex, townhouse, low-rise or high-rise |
| Age | Comps built in a similar period |
| Amenities and housing services | Parking, laundry, dishwasher, garage, storage |
| Maintenance and utilities the owner provides | Note which utilities you pay; comps must match |

The Santa Cruz County housing authority says its maximum rent "is based on market rents collected by the Housing Authority." The RFTA form (HUD-52517) asks for the same facts: bedrooms, year built, structure type and who pays each utility. Maricopa County's packet also asks about square footage, appliances, parking, pest control and services within 2 miles.

Your own rents count as well. In the HAP contract and the tenancy addendum, the rent may not exceed what you charge for comparable unassisted units in the premises. Owners of buildings with more than 4 units must list recent unassisted rents on the RFTA. By taking each HAP payment, you certify the rent is not higher than those rents (982.507(d)).

## Can my rent pass the payment standard but fail rent reasonableness?

Yes. The payment standard and rent reasonableness are two different tests.

| Test | What it limits | Rule |
|---|---|---|
| Payment standard | The maximum subsidy for a unit size, set at 90% to 110% of the FMR | 24 CFR 982.503 |
| Rent reasonableness | The rent itself, against the local market for similar units | 24 CFR 982.507 |
| 40% rule at move-in | The family share, only if the gross rent is above the payment standard | 24 CFR 982.508 |

The payment standard is set for a whole area and unit size. Rent reasonableness looks at your unit. A plain unit in a lower-rent block can fail rent reasonableness even below the payment standard. A renovated unit in a higher-rent block can pass rent reasonableness above the payment standard. The family then pays the difference.

Worked example, for illustration only:

| Line | Amount |
|---|---|
| Payment standard, 2 bedrooms | $1,900 |
| Asking rent | $1,850 |
| Comparable unassisted rents (3 comps) | $1,650, $1,700, $1,725 |
| PHA's reasonable rent | About $1,700 |
| Result | Fails at $1,850; passes at $1,700 or below |

Under the payment standard, but over the market. The PHA cannot approve $1,850 (982.305(a)(4)).

## How do I give the PHA my own comps?

Send comps that match the seven factors, before the PHA decides if you can. The Santa Cruz County housing authority says: "Landlords can submit their own 'comparable rents' to be considered in determining the reasonable rent."

Use this checklist for each comp:

- [ ] Address or block, and distance from your unit.
- [ ] Bedrooms, bathrooms and square footage.
- [ ] Unit type and year built.
- [ ] Current rent and the date it was leased or listed.
- [ ] Utilities included in the rent.
- [ ] Amenities: parking, laundry, appliances, outdoor space.
- [ ] Proof: a listing printout, a lease with names removed, or a rent roll.
- [ ] Confirm the comp is unassisted.

Pick 3 to 5 comps. Use recent leases over old ones, and skip units in clearly different condition.

## Is rent reasonableness checked again at a rent increase?

Yes. 24 CFR 982.507(a)(2) says the PHA "must redetermine the reasonable rent" in three cases:

1. **Before any increase in the rent to owner.**
2. **After a 10% FMR drop.** If the FMR in effect 60 days before the contract anniversary is 10% lower than the FMR one year before.
3. **When HUD directs it.**

The PHA "may also redetermine the reasonable rent at any other time" (982.507(a)(3)). At all times, the rent "may not exceed the reasonable rent as most recently determined or redetermined" (982.507(a)(4)). The Santa Cruz County housing authority says an increase that goes above current market comps "would have to be lowered."

Some units have a simpler test. Tax credit (LIHTC) and HOME units can skip the market comparison (982.507(c)). That works if the voucher rent is not more than the rent for the project's other program units without vouchers.

## Can I appeal a rent reasonableness decision?

Federal rules do not set an owner appeal, but many PHAs have one. The Santa Cruz County housing authority says that if you disagree with its comparable rent, you "may appeal the determination by completing the Owner's Submittal of Comparable Rents form for review."

If your PHA has no form, follow these steps:

1. **Ask for the basis.** Request the comps or method the PHA used.
2. **Answer factor by factor.** Show where its comps differ in location, size, age or amenities.
3. **Send better comps.** Use the checklist above.
4. **Offer a middle rent.** The owner and family negotiate the rent, and the PHA must help the family if asked (24 CFR 982.506).
5. **Decide.** Accept the reasonable rent, or decline the tenancy.
