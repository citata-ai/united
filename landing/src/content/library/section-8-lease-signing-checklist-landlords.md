## What order do the Section 8 move-in steps follow?

The Section 8 move-in steps follow the order in 24 CFR 982.305. The PHA may not approve the tenancy or sign the HAP contract until it finds five things: the unit is eligible, the unit passed inspection, the lease has the tenancy addendum, the rent is reasonable, and the family share passes the 40% test at move-in.

The timeline at a glance:

| Step | Who | Federal rule |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Screen and select the family | You | 24 CFR 982.307 |
| 2. Submit the Request for Tenancy Approval | Family and you | 24 CFR 982.305 |
| 3. Inspect the unit | PHA | Within 15 days for PHAs with up to 1,250 budgeted units (982.305(b)(2)) |
| 4. Rent reasonableness and 40% test | PHA | 24 CFR 982.507, 982.508 |
| 5. Sign the lease with the tenancy addendum | You and tenant | 24 CFR 982.308 |
| 6. PHA approves the tenancy | PHA | 24 CFR 982.305(b)(1)(iii) |
| 7. Sign the HAP contract | You and PHA | By lease start if possible; within 60 days at most (982.305(c)) |
| 8. Move-in and first HAP | Family, PHA | HAP only after the contract is signed |

## Has the unit passed inspection?

The unit must pass the PHA's inspection before the lease term starts. 24 CFR 982.305(b)(1)(i) lists it as the first thing that must be done. A PHA with up to 1,250 budgeted units must inspect and tell you the result within 15 days after the family and owner submit the request. The 15-day clock stops when the unit is not available for inspection.

Before the inspector comes:

- [ ] Utilities on: water, power, gas, heat.
- [ ] Every smoke alarm and carbon monoxide alarm works.
- [ ] Appliances you supply are in and working.
- [ ] Windows open, lock and have no broken glass.
- [ ] Someone can let the inspector in at the set time.

Some PHAs use other inspection options under 24 CFR 982.305(f), such as allowing move-in when only non-life-threatening items fail. Ask your PHA which one it uses.

## Is the lease signed with the addendum attached?

The lease must be signed, with the HUD tenancy addendum, before the lease term starts. Under 24 CFR 982.308:

1. **Use your normal lease.** If you use a standard form for unassisted tenants, use the same form for the voucher tenant (982.308(b)(2)).
2. **Add the tenancy addendum word for word.** It overrides any other lease term, and the tenant can enforce it (982.308(f)(2)).
3. **Include the required facts.** Names of owner and tenant, the unit address, the lease term, the monthly rent to owner, and who supplies each utility and appliance (982.308(d)).
4. **Make the first term at least 1 year,** unless the PHA approves a shorter one (982.309(a)).
5. **Add the lead paint disclosure** if the home is target housing built before 1978 (24 CFR 35.92(b), cited in 982.305(b)(1)(ii)).

Watch for: you may not raise the rent during the initial lease term (982.309(a)(3)). Set the rent right at the start.

## Is the HAP contract signed?

The HAP contract should be signed before the lease starts, and must be signed within 60 calendar days after. 24 CFR 982.305(c) says the PHA "must use best efforts" to sign before the lease term begins. If it signs within the 60 days, the PHA pays HAP back to the lease start. A HAP contract signed after day 60 is void, unless HUD approves an extension.

A worked example, with made-up dates and rent:

| Date | Event |
|---|---|
| 2026-11-01 | Lease term starts |
| 2026-11-20 | HAP contract signed (day 20) |
| 2026-12-05 | First HAP arrives: covers November and December |
| HAP amount | $1,200 a month |
| Paid in that first payment | $2,400 |

Until the contract is signed, the PHA "may not pay any housing assistance payment" (982.305(c)(2)). Plan your cash flow for that gap.

## Is direct deposit set up with the PHA?

Set up your payment method as soon as the PHA approves the tenancy. The federal rule only says the PHA must pay HAP "promptly when due" under the HAP contract (24 CFR 982.451(b)(5)). Each PHA sets its own payment process.

- [ ] Ask the PHA for its owner payment or direct deposit form.
- [ ] Send the tax form the PHA asks for.
- [ ] Make sure the payee name matches the owner on the HAP contract.
- [ ] Ask for access to the PHA's owner portal, if it has one.
- [ ] Ask on which day of the month HAP is paid.

## Did I document the unit condition?

Record the unit's condition with the tenant on move-in day. The PHA inspection report is not a full move-in record. Your own record is what you use if you deduct damage from the deposit later. Under 24 CFR 982.313(d), you must give the tenant a written list of each charge at move-out.

- [ ] A room-by-room checklist, signed and dated by you and the tenant.
- [ ] Dated photos of every room, wall, floor and appliance.
- [ ] Meter readings for each utility in the tenant's name.
- [ ] Keys given, with a count.
- [ ] Security deposit received and recorded, in line with state law.

## The full checklist

- [ ] Screened the family with your written criteria (982.307).
- [ ] Not related to the family as parent, child, grandparent, grandchild, sister or brother (982.306(d)), unless the PHA approves a reasonable accommodation.
- [ ] Request for Tenancy Approval submitted.
- [ ] Rent checked against the payment standard and the 40% cap (982.508).
- [ ] Unit passed inspection.
- [ ] Lease signed, with the tenancy addendum and lead disclosure if needed.
- [ ] PHA approved the tenancy.
- [ ] HAP contract signed.
- [ ] Payment setup done.
- [ ] Move-in condition recorded and signed.
