## Do ebooks and owner guides still work as lead magnets?

Long generic ebooks are weak lead magnets for property managers. An owner does not want "10 tips for landlords"; search and AI answers give that for free. Short local guides still work, because they save the owner hours of research about one city.

We read the client sites of three marketing agencies that serve property managers: ClearLead Digital, Property Manager Websites and Goodjuju Marketing (our data, 2026-10). All three push a rental analysis that gives value before the call, and owner tools such as rent-vs-sell, vacancy loss and rental ROI. On Goodjuju's 11 named client sites, 8 offered a free rental analysis or consultation. One offered all three owner calculators.

| Lead magnet | Owner question it answers | Gate it? | Effort to build |
|---|---|---|---|
| Free rental analysis | "What will my home rent for?" | Yes, it needs the address | Low; a person does it |
| Rent-vs-sell calculator | "Should I sell or rent?" | Result open; report gated | Medium |
| Vacancy cost calculator | "What does an empty month cost?" | Result open | Low |
| Rental ROI calculator | "What will I earn?" | Result open; report gated | Medium |
| Local rules guide (PDF) | "What do I need to rent here legally?" | Yes | Medium; facts must be checked |
| Fee comparison sheet | "What do managers charge here?" | Optional | Low |
| Owner checklist (make-ready, move-out) | "How do I get the home ready?" | Optional | Low |

The common thread is the owner's own property. The more the result depends on the owner's address, the stronger the offer.

## Which calculators convert best for property managers?

We do not have public data that ranks calculators by conversion, so we will not claim one wins. What we can show is what top competitor clients chose. Traverse Property Management in Fort Worth runs three: a rent-versus-sell calculator, an ROI calculator and a vacancy-loss calculator (our audit, 2026-10). Goodjuju's Traverse case study reports owner leads rose from 3 to 7 a month to more than 20, with a 38% closing ratio. That is a client account in a vendor case study. It does not prove which tool caused the change.

Build the calculator that matches the decision your owners face most:

- **Owners who inherited or moved:** rent-vs-sell.
- **Owners who self-manage now:** vacancy cost and management fee comparison.
- **Investors buying more doors:** rental ROI.

Show every formula and every default. An owner trusts a number when they can see where it came from. You can point owners to a working example like the [property management fee calculator](/tools/property-management-fee-calculator).

## How do I gate a property management lead magnet?

Gate the part that needs your work, and leave the part that builds trust open. A calculator result on screen costs you nothing to show. A detailed PDF, a local rent comparison or a call with a manager costs you time, so ask for contact details there.

Keep the form short:

1. Name.
2. Email.
3. Property address or ZIP code (needed for a rental analysis).
4. Number of doors (a dropdown: 1, 2 to 4, 5 to 20, 21+).
5. "How did you first hear about us?" with each AI tool named.

Goodjuju's 2026 study recommends that last question. On its own site, GA4 credited 7 leads to AI tools from May to early October. Lead answers and form records showed at least 13. Ask "first heard", not "found": an owner who heard of you in ChatGPT and then searched your name will answer "Google" to "found".

Add hidden fields for the page, the referrer and UTM codes. Google Analytics Help says to always use utm_source, utm_medium and utm_campaign when you tag a link.

## How do I follow up on lead magnet downloads?

Follow up fast and in person. A download is a small signal, not a request for a sales call. Send a short personal note within one business day, from a named manager, that adds one fact about the owner's property.

A simple plan:

| Day | Message | Goal |
|---|---|---|
| 0 | Auto email with the download link | Deliver what you promised |
| 1 | Personal note: one fact about their ZIP code or home type | Start a real conversation |
| 4 | A short owner story from your book, with consent | Show proof |
| 10 | Your full fee table and what is included | Remove the price question |
| 21 | Offer a 15-minute call or a rental analysis | Ask for a next step |
| 45 | Check-in; offer to stop emails | Respect their time |

The FTC's CAN-SPAM guide sets rules for these emails. Each marketing email needs a clear way to opt out, your valid postal address, and you must honor an opt-out within 10 business days. This is general information, not legal advice. Check the current law or ask a local attorney.

Measure each lead magnet by signed doors, not by downloads. A tool with 200 downloads and 0 signed owners is worse than a rental analysis form with 10 requests and 3 signed owners.
