## Which lead sources bring the most owner clients?

Google search and Google Maps bring most owner leads to property management companies. The best public data is the Goodjuju 2026 lead source report. Goodjuju, a property management marketing agency, tracked 35 companies and 2,525 owner leads in 2 windows: spring (2026-02-26 to 2026-05-26) and fall (2026-07-08 to 2026-10-05).

| Source | Fall share of visits | Fall share of owner leads | Fall visit-to-lead rate | Spring share of leads |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Google search and Map Pack | 37.9% | 50.6% | 1.2% | 51.8% |
| Direct and Maps | 45.0% | 35.6% | 0.7% | 33.6% |
| Other search engines | 3.2% | 4.3% | 1.2% | 5.9% |
| Referral sites | 3.0% | 3.7% | 1.1% | 4.0% |
| AI tools | 0.8% | 3.4% | 3.9% | 2.1% |
| Paid search | 5.3% | 1.2% | 0.2% | 0.9% |
| Social media | 4.3% | 0.8% | 0.2% | 1.6% |

Source: Goodjuju report, fall 2026 update. "Leads" are unique people who sent an owner form. Phone calls and tenant forms are not in the count.

## What share of owner leads comes from Google search and Maps?

Google search, the Map Pack and Maps visits together brought 86.2% of owner leads in fall 2026, and 85.4% in spring. No other source came close.

The split between "Google search" and "direct" is not clean. Goodjuju notes that Map Pack clicks without tracking tags can show up as organic search. Many "direct" visits also start on Google Maps. So treat the two rows as one Google block.

What to do: your website and your Google Business Profile carry most of your owner leads. Fix both before you spend on a new channel.

## How do referrals compare on close rate?

Referral sites brought 3.7% of fall owner leads in the Goodjuju data. That row counts clicks from other websites, such as directories and partner links. It does not count an agent who gives an owner your phone number.

Close rate is a different question. Close rate means signed owners divided by leads. We found no public study that measures close rate by source with a clear method. Goodjuju does not report it. Property Manager Websites (PMW) rates each channel in words:

| Channel | Cost per lead (PMW) | Close rate (PMW) |
|---|---|---|
| Website, inbound | Low | Medium |
| Paid ads | Medium to high | Higher |
| Referrals | Low | Very high |
| Buying portfolios | High | High |
| Outbound (cold calls, mail) | Low | Very low |

These are ratings, not measured numbers. To get real ones, tag the source on every owner lead in your CRM. Then count signed owners by source each quarter.

## Do social media and paid ads bring owner leads?

Social media and paid search brought few owner leads in the Goodjuju study. Social media brought 4.3% of fall visits but only 0.8% of leads. Paid search brought 5.3% of visits and 1.2% of leads.

Watch for 2 limits on the paid number:

- **Few companies ran ads.** Only 6 of the 35 companies ran paid search in fall, and 4 in spring.
- **Ad leads were missed.** Ad landing pages outside the main site and calls from ads were not tracked. Goodjuju says the paid figures are "not a benchmark for paid search".

So the study does not prove that ads fail. It shows that, for these companies, ads were a small part of the mix.

## How big is AI as an owner lead source?

AI tools brought 3.4% of owner leads in fall 2026, up from 2.1% in spring. They brought only 0.8% of visits. So AI visitors turned into leads at 3.9%, about 3.2 times the rate of Google organic visitors.

| AI tool | Fall 2026 owner leads |
|---|---|
| ChatGPT | 37 |
| Gemini | 5 |
| Perplexity | 1 |
| Total | 43 |

Goodjuju calls the AI rate "directional", because it rests on 43 leads. It also found that analytics missed about 4 in 10 AI leads it could trace on its own site. So the real AI share is likely higher than the report shows.

Google still sent about 15 times more owner leads than all AI tools together (639 against 43). AI is small, but it grows, and it converts well. To measure it on your own site, read [how to track AI traffic](/guides/track-ai-traffic-property-management).

## Which sites do AI answers use to pick a property manager?

AI answers name managers from directories and review sites as well as from manager websites. Those sites matter even when they send few clicks.

Our own data:

- **50-city test (2026-10-19).** We asked 3 engines for the best property management company in each of the 50 largest US cities. Expertise.com was a source in 93 of 150 answers.
- **Chicago baseline (2026-10-16).** In 144 answers about a Chicago manager, the most cited sources were manager sites, then Yelp (35 answers), Expertise.com (22) and Thumbtack (17). The manager is Fridman Properties, a related party: its owner is CEO of MyRemotely, our partner. See the [case study](/case-studies/fridman-properties).

So a correct listing on Expertise.com, Yelp and Google Business Profile helps twice. It can send a few direct leads, and it feeds the AI answers that send more.

## How to compare lead sources in your own company

1. **Tag every owner lead** with its first source: form field, call tracking number or UTM tag.
2. **Split owner leads from tenant leads.** Tenant forms inflate every channel.
3. **Count signed doors by source** each quarter, not only leads.
4. **Compare cost per signed door**, not cost per lead.
5. **Check AI answers** for your city each month, and note which sites they cite.

Watch for: all 35 companies in the Goodjuju study work with an SEO agency. Their Google share may be higher than for a company with a weak website.
