## Which tenant screening services should you compare?

Compare report contents and price before choosing a provider. A package name does not tell you whether it includes every report you need.

| Service | Published price | Reports or features listed | Workflow |
|---|---|---|---|
| SmartMove Basic | $25 plus applicable tax | ResidentScore and criminal background | Pay as used |
| SmartMove Plus | $40 plus applicable tax | Credit, score, criminal and eviction-related reports | Pay as used |
| SmartMove Premium | $49 plus applicable tax | Plus reports, Income Insights and identity check | Pay as used |
| RentSpree Standard | $39.99 per report | Credit, background and eviction | Applicant or landlord pays |
| RentSpree Comprehensive | $49.99 per report | Standard reports, bank-verified income and references | Applicant or landlord pays |
| Buildium standard screening | $17 per screening | TransUnion-powered screening | Inside Buildium subscription |

Sources: [SmartMove pricing](https://www.mysmartmove.com/pricing), [RentSpree pricing](https://www.rentspree.com/pricing) and [Buildium pricing](https://www.buildium.com/pricing/), read 2026-10-19.

Report availability and package limits need confirmation for the property. The table is not a legal approval checklist or a recommendation to use every report.

## Which services include credit, criminal and eviction reports?

SmartMove Plus and Premium list those report categories; RentSpree's Standard and Comprehensive packages also describe them. Check the actual package rather than the brand.

SmartMove Basic lists ResidentScore and a criminal background report. ResidentScore is not the same item as a full credit report. The pricing page places the full credit report in higher packages.

RentSpree describes its Comprehensive package as adding bank-verified income and reference checks to the Standard scope. Do not confuse a bank-based income check with a score-derived income signal.

Ask for a redacted sample report. Look at how names, addresses and record dates appear. Check whether unresolved identity questions are visible.

No label proves complete nationwide coverage. Ask the provider about limitations for your property location and report type. Do not assume a missing record establishes that no record exists.

Have a person review unclear or conflicting information. Do not turn an ambiguous result into an automatic applicant decision.

## Who pays the screening fee?

SmartMove and RentSpree let the landlord choose who pays, subject to the applicable conditions. A provider's payer switch does not establish what your local rules allow.

SmartMove describes a choice between landlord payment and passing the report cost to the prospective tenant where permitted. RentSpree also describes applicant or landlord payment.

Buildium's pricing page distinguishes standard screening, enhanced screening and applicant-paid screening. It lists applicant-paid screening at $35. Do not compare that price with landlord-paid screening without explaining the difference.

Before ordering, show the applicant the actual package and payer process. Confirm whether a failed identity step can still trigger a charge. Ask about duplicate orders and refunds.

Keep a record of the requested report and amount. Do not collect a separate fee without reviewing the full process.

This is general information, not legal advice. Check the current law or ask a local attorney.

The useful cost comparison includes both your business's bill and the applicant's payment experience.

## Which credit bureaus do the services use?

The providers reviewed here describe TransUnion-based screening, but the report package still determines what you receive.

SmartMove is a TransUnion service. RentSpree's pricing page says credit, background and eviction history come from TransUnion. Buildium describes its screening as powered by TransUnion.

A shared bureau does not make the services identical. Applications, identity checks, income evidence, delivery and record storage can differ.

Ask which fields come from the bureau and which come from another service. Check who handles a dispute or correction. Staff should know where to send an applicant with a report question.

Do not assume a score from different packages is directly interchangeable. Ask what the named score measures and how it fits your approved criteria.

Compare the report you will actually order, not a general provider brochure. Save a redacted sample with your buying notes.

## Which services integrate with your property system?

Screening inside your existing property system can reduce copying, but a standalone tool may fit another workflow. Verify the exact connection before purchasing.

Buildium offers screening inside its platform. That does not establish that standalone SmartMove or RentSpree writes every report field into your chosen system.

Ask the software vendor to demonstrate these actions:

- **Invite:** send the correct application to the correct person.
- **Collect:** record consent and the selected package.
- **Deliver:** show completed reports to authorized staff.
- **Track:** keep status visible without duplicate invitations.
- **Store:** retain the approved records with access controls.
- **Correct:** route a report problem to the right provider.

Check whether documents move automatically or staff must attach them. Ask who supports a failed transfer.

Use sample data in the demo. Do not expose real applicant records just to test a sales claim.

## How should you choose a service?

Choose the package that supports your approved screening process and gives staff a clear way to handle questions. The cheapest report is not always the complete report you need.

Write the criteria before reviewing applicants. Use the same process consistently. Do not ask a screening tool or AI to infer protected traits or steer renters.

Test the invitation and report view from both sides. Check the instructions when someone cannot verify identity or has a thin credit file. Keep a person available for support.

Build a selection record:

| Decision | Evidence |
|---|---|
| Package | Current report list and redacted sample |
| Cost | Payer, amount and refund process |
| Coverage | Provider limitations for location |
| Access | Staff permissions and storage process |
| Connection | Demonstrated transfer to your system |
