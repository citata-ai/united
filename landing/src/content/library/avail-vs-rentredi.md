## Which has a free plan?

Avail offers a free landlord plan, while RentRedi's reviewed pricing page shows paid billing terms. This makes Avail easier to test without a subscription commitment.

A landlord with a small portfolio should compare total useful work. A free plan that handles rent and lease records may be enough. A paid tool may be worth the cost if it removes repeated manual steps.

Write down your current process before starting either trial. Note how you send leases, confirm rent, store files, and handle repairs. This gives the test a clear purpose.

Ask whether you can export everything created during the trial. A free entry point should not make your records hard to retrieve later. Check signed documents and tenant transactions, not just a contact list.

## How do their mobile apps compare?

Mobile fit depends on whether you can complete the work you do away from a desk. Test both landlord and tenant access rather than judging screenshots.

RentRedi centers much of its offer on rental work across devices. Avail's pricing page describes a tenant portal and online rental tasks. Check current app or mobile-browser support directly for your device.

During a demo, open a repair request and add a photo. Find a lease while standing at a property. Check a tenant balance without exposing another resident's record.

Ask a renter tester to view a charge and find help. They should understand what they owe and whether a payment is pending. Confirm that messages stay linked to the right property.

Do not treat a dedicated app as proof of better usability. A mobile website may meet the task well, while an app may omit a report. Choose through completed work.

## Which has better lease templates?

Avail expressly lists state-specific, lawyer-reviewed lease templates and digital signatures. Its paid tier adds further template customization. RentRedi's document flow needs a direct test against your own approved lease.

Ask each vendor to show a lease for the property's state and local area. Check required attachments, signer order, and the way staff add approved terms.

Try a renewal and a correction before anyone signs. Confirm that the final file includes every attachment and that staff can identify the signed version. Avoid sending different drafts from separate systems.

Template claims are not a promise that your lease fits every property or local rule. Have counsel review the document you will actually use. This is general information, not legal advice. Check the current law or ask a local attorney.

## How do rent payments work in each?

Compare rent payments from charge creation through bank settlement and any later return. Both platforms offer online rent collection, but the plan and payment method affect the experience.

Avail's pricing page lists tenant bank-transfer fees on Unlimited and waived ACH fees on Unlimited Plus. Ask RentRedi for its current transaction fee schedule. A subscription quote alone does not answer who pays for processing.

Check whether each tenant can see a ledger with charges, credits, and payments. Then test a partial receipt, a refund, and an outside payment. Ask how staff correct an entry without losing its history.

Confirm payout account details before inviting tenants. A shared owner login and a wrong bank account can create a difficult cleanup. Assign bank changes to an authorized person and verify them through a known contact.

Review notices before enabling automatic late fees. The posted ledger must match your lease and approved payment policy. Do not let an unclear setup decide who receives a notice.

## What is the state of Avail after the Realtor.com purchase?

Avail presents itself as part of Realtor.com in its current company material. Treat that ownership context as background, rather than proof of service quality or future product support.

Check the terms that govern your account today. Find the support route, data export instructions, billing entity, and cancellation process. Ask for written confirmation when a sales statement affects your choice.

A parent brand does not establish that every lead source or product connection is included. Test the specific listing and application links you expect to use.

For a small portfolio, the final choice can remain simple. Use Avail if its free workflow meets the need. Pay for RentRedi when the trial demonstrates a clear advantage worth the flat bill.

## How should you test the choice before you pay?

Test the full workflow with sample records before you move live data. Use the same case in each product. Ask the person who will do the daily work to run the test. A sales demo alone does not show where your team will need help.

Keep a decision sheet with the task, result, missing step, and person who checked it. Mark each result as shown, promised, or still open. Ask for written terms when a feature needs an upgrade or another service. Include staff setup time in your cost review.

Check the exit path while you still control the choice. Export the sample records and open them outside the platform. Confirm that notes, files, dates, and links remain useful. Ask how you will get records after you close the account.
