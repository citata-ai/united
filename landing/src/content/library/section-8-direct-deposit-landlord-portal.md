## What does the PHA need for direct deposit?

The PHA needs its direct deposit form, a voided check and your tax ID. Two examples:

- **Norfolk Redevelopment and Housing Authority (NRHA)** asks you to submit "the ACH Direct Deposit form along with a voided check".
- **Tacoma Housing Authority** says "we prefer that all landlords get paid via direct deposit" and asks for the form with a voided check. It may call to verify your bank details, "which may cause a slight delay".

Direct deposit and portals matter to PHAs too. In a 2021 HUD Inspector General review of Moving to Work agencies, PHAs rated direct deposit and landlord portals as the most effective non-cash landlord incentives (NLIHC summary).

Setup checklist:

1. **Get the PHA's form.** Use the current version from the landlord page.
2. **Match the names.** The account name should match the HAP payee and the W-9 name.
3. **Attach a voided check** or a bank letter, if the PHA accepts one.
4. **Send a W-9** with your SSN or EIN. The PHA uses it for the year-end 1099.
5. **Keep a copy** and the date you sent it.
6. **Watch the first deposit.** Tacoma says the first payment can take up to 60 days under the HAP contract, but is usually sooner.

## When does the PHA pay?

Each PHA sets its own pay dates. NRHA deposits "on the first of the month", or the next business day if the first is a weekend or holiday. Tacoma pays "on or the first business day after the 1st, 10th, and 20th".

Under 24 CFR 982.451(b)(5), the PHA must pay HAP "promptly when due". The HAP contract must include late payment penalties against the PHA only if 3 things are true: penalties are normal in your local market, you charge them to all tenants, and you charge them to the voucher tenant for late tenant rent.

| PHA | Pay date | Bank change cutoff | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| Norfolk (NRHA) | 1st, or next business day | By the 16th for the next deposit | NRHA landlord portal page |
| Tacoma (THA) | On or after the 1st, 10th and 20th | Not stated | THA landlord FAQ |

## What does a landlord portal show?

A landlord portal shows your HAP payments and your units' status. The contents vary by PHA:

| Feature | Norfolk (NRHA) portal | NYC HPD DTR Owner Portal |
|---|---|---|
| HAP or payment history | Yes | Yes |
| Abatement history | Yes | Yes |
| Inspection schedules and results | Yes | Yes |
| Tenant information | Yes | Not stated |
| Check registers and data download | Not stated | Yes |
| Forms and Administrative Plan | Yes | Not stated |

Source: NRHA landlord portal page and NYC HPD DTR Owner Portal page, read 2026-10-19. NYC HPD says that to register, "all you need is an email address and a recent HAP register".

Register early. You will need the portal most when a payment changes.

## How do I read a HAP payment statement?

A HAP payment statement lists each unit and the HAP amount paid for it. It may be a paper stub, a PDF or a portal screen. Read it line by line against your rent roll.

Check these fields for each unit:

- **Tenant and unit.** Match each line to your lease.
- **Period.** The month the payment covers.
- **HAP amount.** Compare with last month.
- **Adjustments.** Back pay, prorated amounts or a repayment.
- **Abatement.** A $0 or a note can mean the PHA stopped HAP after a failed inspection.

Worked example: you expected $3,600 for 3 units at $1,200 each. The deposit is $2,880.

| Unit | Expected HAP | Paid | Difference | Likely reason to check |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1A | $1,200 | $1,200 | $0 | — |
| 1B | $1,200 | $1,080 | −$120 | Tenant income review; tenant share went up |
| 2A | $1,200 | $600 | −$600 | Abatement or move-out mid-month |
| Total | $3,600 | $2,880 | −$720 | |

For unit 1B, bill the tenant the new tenant share from the PHA's notice. Our [Section 8 rent calculator](/tools/section-8-rent-calculator) shows how the split moves when income changes.

## What if a payment is missing?

If a payment is missing, find the cause before you call. Work through these steps:

1. **Check the portal** for an abatement, a hold or a change notice.
2. **Check your mail and email** for a failed inspection notice. Under 24 CFR 982.404, the PHA may withhold HAP after it tells you of deficiencies, and must abate if you miss the repair deadline.
3. **Check the bank details.** A changed account or a name mismatch can stop a deposit.
4. **Check the lease status.** HAP stops after the month a family moves out.
5. **Contact the PHA** with the unit, tenant name, month and amount.
6. **Bill only the tenant share.** The family is not responsible for the HAP part (24 CFR 982.451(b)(4)).

Watch for: a late or missing HAP is not grounds to evict the tenant. 24 CFR 982.310(b) says PHA failure to pay the HAP "is not a violation of the lease", and you may not end the tenancy for it during the lease term.

## How do I change bank details?

To change bank details, send a new direct deposit form and a voided check before the PHA's cutoff. NRHA says changes "must be reported no later than the 16th of the month in order to be effective by the next deposit".

Do not close the old account until the first deposit lands in the new one. If you sell a unit, the buyer sets up their own direct deposit. NYC HPD, for example, pays a buyer only after the HAP contract is assigned.

This is general information, not legal advice. Check the current law or ask a local attorney.
