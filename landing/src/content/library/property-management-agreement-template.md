## Copy-ready property management agreement template

Copy the template below and fill in each bracket. The sections after the template explain what to check in the key clauses.

> **Property Management Agreement**
>
> **1. Parties and property.** This agreement is between [owner legal name] ("Owner") and [company legal name], [license type and number] ("Manager"), for the property at [address, units] ("Property").
>
> **2. Term.** This agreement starts on [start date] and ends on [end date]. It renews for [12] months at a time unless either party gives written notice at least [30] days before the end date.
>
> **3. Manager duties.** Manager will: market vacant units; screen applicants under a written, uniform policy that follows fair housing law; sign leases for Owner within the rent and terms Owner approves; collect rent and other charges; enforce leases; arrange repairs; pay approved Property bills from Owner funds; and keep full records.
>
> **4. Authority.** Owner gives Manager authority to act for Owner on the duties in clause 3. Manager will not sell, refinance or put a lien on the Property.
>
> **5. Fees.** Owner will pay Manager:
>
> - Management fee: [percent]% of [rent collected / rent due], or $[amount] per unit per month.
> - Leasing fee: [percent]% of one month's rent for each new lease.
> - Renewal fee: $[amount] per renewal.
> - Setup fee: $[amount], one time.
> - Other fees: [list each fee and amount, or "none"].
>
> Manager will not charge any fee that is not in this clause. Any markup on vendor invoices is [percent]% / not charged.
>
> **6. Repair limit.** Manager may approve repairs up to $[amount] per job without Owner approval. Above that amount, Manager will get Owner's approval first, except in an emergency that threatens life, safety or serious damage to the Property. In an emergency, Manager may act and will tell Owner within [24] hours.
>
> **7. Reserve.** Manager will hold a reserve of $[amount] in the trust account for [repairs and bills]. Manager will top up the reserve from rent before each payout.
>
> **8. Trust funds.** Manager will hold rent, security deposits and the reserve in a trust account at [bank], separate from Manager's own money. Interest on trust funds, if any, goes to [Owner / Manager / tenant as the law requires].
>
> **9. Reports and payouts.** Manager will send Owner a monthly statement by the [day] of each month and pay the balance due to Owner by [ACH / check] by the [day]. Manager will send a year-end summary and Form 1099-MISC as the IRS requires.
>
> **10. Insurance.** Owner will keep landlord liability insurance of at least $[amount] and name Manager as an additional insured. Manager will keep [general liability and errors and omissions] insurance of at least $[amount].
>
> **11. Indemnity.** Owner will defend and pay Manager for claims that arise from the Property, except claims caused by Manager's negligence, willful misconduct or breach of this agreement.
>
> **12. Termination.** Either party may end this agreement with [30 / 60] days' written notice. [No fee / A fee of $amount] applies. On termination, Manager will, within [30] days, give Owner or the next manager: security deposits and a list by tenant, Owner's balance, leases, tenant ledgers, keys, and open work orders.
>
> **13. Assignment.** Manager may not assign this agreement to another person or company without Owner's written consent.
>
> **14. Disclosures and law.** Owner will give Manager all known information on lead-based paint for housing built before 1978, and any other required disclosures. This agreement follows the law of [state].
>
> Signed: [Owner name, date] / [Manager's broker or authorized signer, date]

This is a generic template, not a state legal form. Licensing laws differ. Have a local attorney check it before you use it.

## What fee and term clauses are standard?

Standard fee clauses list each fee, its amount and its base. Standard term clauses give a start date, an end date and a renewal rule. Arizona's ARS 32-2173 requires a start and end date and compensation terms in every agreement.

Use market data to check that your fees are in a normal range. iPropertyManagement's 722-branch sample (updated 2026-09-23) shows these averages:

| Fee | Average | Range in the sample |
|---|---|---|
| Management fee (% of rent) | 8.49% | 3.75% to 14% |
| Flat management fee | $101.04 per unit per month | $49 to $250 |
| Leasing fee (with management) | 70.6% of one month's rent | 6% to 125% |
| Renewal fee (flat) | $231.77 | $32 to $1,000 |
| Setup fee | $185.24 | $45 to $850 |

All Property Management gives a range of 8% to 12% of monthly rent for the management fee. It also notes that contracts may charge on rental value, rent due or rent collected. Write the base in clause 5.

To test the full fee list, put it in the [property management fee calculator](/tools/property-management-fee-calculator).

## How should the termination clause read?

The termination clause should say how either side ends the agreement, what it costs, and what the manager hands over. Arizona requires cancellation terms that both parties accept. Clause 12 above covers all 3 parts.

Check these 4 points:

1. **Notice period and method.** For example, 30 days, in writing, by email or mail.
2. **Fee.** A flat fee, or none. Avoid a fee equal to many months of management.
3. **Handover list.** Deposits, balance, leases, ledgers, keys and open work orders.
4. **Handover deadline.** A date, such as 30 days after the agreement ends.

## What authority limit for repairs should the owner set?

Set a per-job limit high enough for routine repairs and low enough to protect cash. Then add a separate emergency rule. Clause 6 does both.

| Owner profile | Example limit | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Single home, new owner | $300 | Owner wants to see most jobs |
| Small portfolio, trusted manager | $500 | Fewer approval delays |
| Large portfolio, strong reserve | $1,000 | Fast repairs, fewer calls |

These are example amounts, not market data. Match the limit to the reserve in clause 7: a limit above the reserve means the manager may lack cash to pay the bill.

## What must be checked against state licensing law?

Check each clause against your state's real estate or property management license law before you sign. Some states set required contents and money rules.

| Check | Example rule |
|---|---|
| Required contents | Arizona's ARS 32-2173: material terms, signatures, start and end date, cancellation, handling of funds, reports, reserve, trust interest, compensation |
| Assignment | Arizona: no assignment without the owner's express written consent |
| Deposit timing | California 10 CCR 2832: trust funds deposited within 3 business days after receipt |
| Commingling | California 10 CCR 2835: commingling prohibited, except up to $200 of broker money for bank fees and a few other cases |
| Lead paint | EPA: landlords and property managers must disclose known lead hazards in pre-1978 housing |

This is general information, not legal advice. Check the current law or ask a local attorney.
