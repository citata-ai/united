## Why do directories win "best property manager" searches?

Directories win "best property manager" searches because the searcher asks for a list. Yelp, Expertise.com and Thumbtack each show a list of local managers with ratings. Your website shows one company: yours. For a list query, a list is the better match.

Directories also have strong prominence signals. Google's Business Profile help says prominence is how well known a business is. It uses facts such as how many websites link to the business and how many reviews it has. Yelp has hundreds of millions of reviews, per its recommendation software page. A local manager site cannot match that scale.

So the honest answer is: on broad "best" searches, you will often sit below a directory. That is not a sign your site is broken.

| Search type | Example | Who usually wins | Can your site win? |
|---|---|---|---|
| Best / top list | "best property management Denver" | Directories, list posts | Rarely |
| Map search | "property management near me" | Google map pack | Yes, through your Google profile |
| City + service | "single-family property management Aurora" | Manager sites | Yes |
| Fee question | "property management fees Denver" | Manager sites, guides | Yes |
| Brand | "Smith Property Management reviews" | Your site, your profiles | Yes |

The table is our reading of how search works, a hypothesis. Run each search in your own city and fill in what you see.

## Should I claim and fix my directory profiles?

You should claim and fix every directory profile, because AI engines read them. On 2026-10-16 we tested 24 owner prompts for Fridman Properties, a Chicago manager, in ChatGPT, Claude and Gemini. We got 144 answers. The most cited sources after manager sites were:

| Source | Answers that cited it |
|---|---|
| Yelp | 35 |
| Expertise.com | 22 |
| Thumbtack | 17 |

Source: our data, 2026-10-16. Fridman Properties' owner is CEO of MyRemotely, our partner, so this is a related party. Details are in the [Fridman Properties case study](/case-studies/fridman-properties).

In a wider test across 150 answers (H03, our data), Expertise.com was a source in 93. We also studied clients of 3 PM marketing agencies. For one agency, 6 clients were named in 11 AI answers, and only 1 answer cited the client's own site. The rest came through directories.

Goodjuju Marketing saw the same thing. Its fall 2026 study says ChatGPT's local answers lean on Yelp and Bing listings, and it tells PM companies to keep both current.

Use this checklist for each profile:

- **Claim it.** Yelp, Bing Places, Expertise.com, Thumbtack, BBB, Nextdoor and your local chamber.
- **Match the core facts.** Same name, address, phone and website as your Google Business Profile.
- **Write the owner story.** Say who you serve: property types, door count range, service area.
- **Add the fee basics.** If the profile allows it, state your management fee and leasing fee.
- **Answer reviews.** Reply in a calm, factual way. Never share private details.
- **Log it.** Keep a sheet with the URL, login owner and last check date.

## Can my property management site outrank Yelp?

Your site can outrank Yelp on narrow searches, not broad ones. A directory page lists 10 managers in one city. It cannot explain your Section 8 process, your fee table or your owner portal. Those are the searches where your site has the edge.

Build pages that a directory cannot copy:

1. **City pages with real local facts.** Name the neighborhoods you serve, typical rent ranges and local rules. Do not clone one page across 20 cities.
2. **A full fee table.** Management fee, leasing fee, renewal fee, and what each one covers.
3. **Owner question pages.** "How long does it take to rent a house in Aurora?" with your own data.
4. **Proof.** Door count, years in business, review count, licensed broker name.
5. **Links from local sites.** Google counts links toward prominence. Earn them from your chamber, local news and partners.

Watch for: anyone who promises to "beat Yelp" for "best property manager in [city]". That promise is hard to keep and easy to sell.

## How do directories affect AI answers about property managers?

Directories shape AI answers about property managers more than most manager sites do. An AI engine that answers "best property manager in Chicago" needs a list it trusts. A directory gives it one, with ratings. Our numbers above show the effect: directories were sources far more often than the managers' own pages.

That changes the goal. Ranking first on Google matters less if the owner reads an AI answer first. What matters is that every source the AI reads says the same true things about you. The [AI search checklist for property management websites](/guides/ai-search-checklist-property-management-website) covers your own site.

## Step list: what to do this month

Use this order. Each step fits in a week.

1. **Search 5 owner phrases** in your city on Google and ChatGPT. Note each directory that shows.
2. **Claim each directory profile** from that list.
3. **Fix the facts** so name, phone and site match on every profile.
4. **Add a lead-source question** to your owner form: "How did you first hear about us?" with each directory as an option.
5. **Build one page** a directory cannot copy: your fee table or one real city page.
6. **Recheck in 30 days.** Run the same 5 phrases and compare.

Goodjuju found referral traffic gave 3.7% of owner leads from 3.0% of traffic in fall 2026. That is small but real. The bigger reason to fix directories is the AI answer that quotes them.
