## What does AI triage do with a work order?

AI triage can organize a request, ask for missing facts, suggest urgency, and route the next task. These are separate actions, and a product may support only some.

Define the output you need before comparing tools. A maintenance note should preserve the resident's words, property, issue, images, and contact route. Staff also need to see what the system inferred.

Do not treat a confident summary as a diagnosis. A report about water near an appliance may need further questions or a qualified person. Keep uncertain information labeled.

Ask how the tool detects a repeat request. Linking a new call to an old work order can help, but combining unrelated issues can hide work. Test both situations.

The final record should identify the action taken, its owner, and what still needs review. A good category without a next step does not complete triage.

## How do Cru, Maintenance Performer, and Latchel compare?

Rentvine's Cru launch page describes suggestions for staff to confirm, edit, or dismiss. AppFolio describes resident responses, image analysis, urgency review, and dispatch. Latchel describes a front-office and maintenance service with human support.

These are different operating models. Cru's suggestion model needs staff to act. A broader service needs clear authority for actions taken while staff are away.

Rentvine's current pricing page also uses the Fixie name for AI maintenance. Confirm the present product name, feature availability, and approval behavior in your account. Do not assume older Cru material describes every current function.

Ask the same questions in each demo. Who sends the resident reply? Who chooses the vendor? Who can change urgency? Who closes the work order?

Then inspect the written record. Check whether your staff can tell an AI suggestion from a completed action. The difference affects both workload and accountability.

## Can AI handle after-hours emergencies?

AI can support after-hours intake, but you need people and vendors who can respond. The software's availability does not guarantee a contractor's availability.

AppFolio and Latchel describe emergency prioritization or dispatch in their own offers. Test those claims through safe, controlled scenarios, rather than using a real emergency as a pilot.

Use an unclear request, a dropped call, a wrong address, and an unavailable preferred vendor. Check the fallback process in each case. A retry loop without escalation is not useful coverage.

Do not let troubleshooting delay contact with emergency services when there may be immediate danger. Use approved instructions and local guidance. Give residents a clear way to ask for a person.

Ask how the service handles a system outage. The fallback should work even when the PMS integration is unavailable.

## How do you keep a human in the loop?

Name a staff owner for each action the tool can take. Define which actions need approval and which can run under clear rules.

Use a control checklist:

- **Limit authority.** Set vendor, spend, and communication boundaries.
- **Show evidence.** Keep the original request beside the AI summary.
- **Route uncertainty.** Send unclear or high-risk cases to trained staff.
- **Test overrides.** Confirm that a person can change or stop an action.
- **Review completion.** Check that vendor acceptance and repair completion are distinct states.

Review both successful and failed cases. A service can route a request correctly while sending a poor resident message. Check the whole exchange.

Keep a correction log. Use it to improve rules and training, without quietly rewriting the original record. Ask the vendor what activity history staff can export.

## What does each cost?

The cost depends on the base software, AI access, service scope, and transaction volume. Request a written quote for the actual workflow shown in the demo.

Rentvine's pricing page publishes an entry rate for its broader platform, but that is not a standalone Cru quote. Confirm the included AI service and any limits.

For AppFolio, separate the PMS subscription from the exact Maintenance Performer package. For Latchel, separate front-office response from full maintenance coordination. Do not compare those offers as if they cover the same work.

Include setup, property rules, roster cleanup, and staff review time. A tool may reduce intake work while increasing exception review during launch.

Expand only after the pilot produces correct records and reliable escalation. Use the saved cases to decide which actions can safely move from staff approval to configured automation.

## How should you test the choice before you pay?

Test the full workflow with sample records before you move live data. Use the same case in each product. Ask the person who will do the daily work to run the test. A sales demo alone does not show where your team will need help.

Keep a decision sheet with the task, result, missing step, and person who checked it. Mark each result as shown, promised, or still open. Ask for written terms when a feature needs an upgrade or another service. Include staff setup time in your cost review.

Check the exit path while you still control the choice. Export the sample records and open them outside the platform. Confirm that notes, files, dates, and links remain useful. Ask how you will get records after you close the account.
