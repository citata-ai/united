## Who needs a Detroit rental registration?

Every rental property in Detroit needs a rental registration and a Certificate of Compliance before you rent it legally. The BSEED Landlord Quick Reference Guide defines rental property as any dwelling unit rented, leased or occupied by tenants. That includes verbal leases and rentals to family members.

The guide lists 2 exclusions:

- **Owner-occupied single-family home** with rooms rented out.
- **Owner-occupied duplex** with one unit rented out.

Registration is free and does not expire unless the owner or the use changes, per BSEED. A buyer must register again and apply for a new certificate. A sale does not erase fees and fines from the time you owned the property.

The need is large. The City of Detroit says only about 10% of its estimated 82,000 rental properties were in compliance before the new law.

## What is the Detroit Certificate of Compliance?

The Detroit Certificate of Compliance shows that BSEED found the rental meets the city's safety standards. City Council passed a new rental ordinance on October 29, 2024, in effect January 1, 2025. The city calls the new system "One Inspection, One Fee."

What changed, per the City of Detroit August 2025 update:

| Item | Old process | New process |
|---|---|---|
| Inspections | Two inspections, 4 to 8 hours | One inspection, 1 to 2 hours |
| Fees | Over $1,000 | $195 for 1 unit, $225 for a duplex, plus a $20 processing fee |
| Checklist | 37 inspection parts | 15 points |
| First certificate | Varied | 3 years |
| Renewal before expiry | Varied | 5 years |

The 4 steps to a certificate, from BSEED:

1. **Register the property** as a rental in the Code Enforcement module of your eLAPS account. Upload the required forms.
2. **Pay and schedule.** For 1-2 units, pay the fee online, and the city assigns an inspection firm by zip code. For 3 or more units, call BSEED at 313-628-2451.
3. **Pass the 15-point inspection.** If you fail, fix the items. The first re-inspection costs nothing extra.
4. **Receive the certificate** from BSEED.

A faster path exists for subsidized units. If the property passed a Section 8, other HUD, MSHDA or Detroit Housing and Revitalization Department inspection in the last 12 months, upload that report instead.

Worked example: you own 2 single-family homes and 1 duplex. Inspections cost 2 × $195 + $225 = $615, plus 3 × $20 = $60 in processing fees, so $675 in total. That covers 3 years. Renew on time and the next certificates last 5 years.

## What does the 15-point Detroit inspection check?

The Detroit rental inspection checks 15 points, set out in the city's checklist:

| # | Point |
|---|---|
| 1 | Walls, floors, stairs, porches and decks free of major structural defects |
| 2 | Handrails on stairs with 4 or more risers, guardrails on porches |
| 3 | Roof, walls and gutters keep out rain and snow |
| 4 | Gas and electric on and working |
| 5 | Heat can reach at least 68°F |
| 6 | No exposed wires, cover plates on all switches and plugs |
| 7 | Smoke and carbon monoxide detectors installed and working |
| 8 | Hot and cold water, hot water between 110°F and 140°F |
| 9 | Sinks, toilets, tubs and water heater work, drain and do not leak |
| 10 | Proper egress from all rooms, including attics or basements used as living space |
| 11 | Windows that should open do open and lock |
| 12 | Unit entry doors have a working lock |
| 13 | No rodent or insect infestation |
| 14 | Sanitary floors, walls and ceilings, no mold or sewage |
| 15 | No deteriorated paint or bare soil |

The checklist details matter. Point 10 means at least one window in each bedroom has no fixed bars. Point 13 counts any bedbugs or roaches in a unit as an infestation. Point 15 allows small amounts of peeling paint: under 20 square feet outside or 2 square feet per room inside.

## What lead clearance does Detroit require?

Detroit treats lead through the paint point of the inspection and through extra rules when a child is harmed. The ordinance presumes that all paint on a rental built before January 1, 1978 is lead-based, along with original windows and doors.

What the city documents say:

- **At inspection.** Point 15 fails a unit for deteriorated paint or bare soil within the roofline. If you fail on that point, the city says to consult a lead-safe certified professional.
- **If a child is lead poisoned.** When a tenant under 6 has an elevated blood lead level, the city requires a lead inspection and risk assessment at the owner's cost (August 2025 update; City Code 8-15-91).
- **Reports.** A lead inspection must come from a certified lead inspector or risk assessor, and the report must list the build date, the address and the inspector's certification number (City Code 8-15-92).

Watch for: the ordinance as passed still includes a lead clearance subdivision. Ask BSEED if your pre-1978 property needs a separate lead clearance report before you lease it.

## Can a Detroit tenant escrow rent if the owner has no certificate?

Yes. Detroit City Code Section 8-15-86 creates a tenant escrow program. A tenant may pay rent into it if the property lacks a Certificate of Compliance or has documented code violations that pose a safety risk. Paying into escrow on time does not break the lease.

How escrow money moves:

1. The tenant tells the owner that rent went into escrow.
2. The Housing and Revitalization Department reviews the account at least every 120 days.
3. If you got the certificate or fixed the violations, the money goes to you.
4. If not, the money goes back to the tenant.

The certificate also matters in court. Under City Code 8-15-88 and MCL 600.5720(f), failure to get a certificate counts as a breach of lease. A court decides if that breach excuses rent in an eviction for nonpayment.

## What are the penalties in Detroit for renting without a certificate?

Penalties start at $400. The BSEED guide lists tickets from $400, liens on the property for unpaid tickets, and tenant escrow. The city's update adds that tickets rose by $150 each and can go to management companies too. If you come into compliance, a hearing officer can cut the ticket by up to 50%.

Repeat problems bring more visits. Properties with repeat violations face more frequent inspections.

Rents in the Detroit metro rose slightly this year. HUD's FY2027 two-bedroom Fair Market Rent is $1,459, up 3.4% from $1,411.
