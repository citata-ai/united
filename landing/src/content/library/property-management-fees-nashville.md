## What is the average monthly management fee in Nashville?

The average monthly management fee in Nashville is about 8% of collected rent, based on the price pages we read. On 2026-10-19 we read the published prices of 11 managers in the Nashville metro. We did not use survey averages from past years. Each number below is on the manager's own page.

| Manager | Area | Monthly fee | Leasing fee | Renewal fee | Setup fee |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| [Evernest](https://www.nashville-property.management/pricing) | Nashville | 8% of rent (single-family) | 50% of 1 month, $500 min | $250 | None |
| [PURE HomeRiver](https://purehomeriver.com/locations/tennessee/nashville) | Nashville | 8% | 50% of first month | Not shown | Not shown |
| [Dyad PM](https://www.dyadpm.com/pm/franklin) | Franklin, Nashville | 8% of collected rent | 50% of first month | Not shown | Not shown |
| [Baseline TN](https://baselinetn.com/services/) | Nashville | 8% to 10% | 50% to 75% of first month, $1,500 min | Not shown | $250 per unit |
| [Canden Properties](https://www.candenpm.com/pricing) | Murfreesboro | 8.5% | $750 new clients ($950 one-time placement) | $60 | $150 to take on a current tenant |
| [RENEW Nashville](https://www.renewtn.com/pricing) | Nashville | 10%, no monthly minimum | Half a month (portfolio owners) | $150 | $250, waived for multi-unit owners |
| [Stevens Realty](https://stevensrentals.com/property-management/nashville/) | Nashville | 7.5% to 11.9% (1–5 units, by plan) | $175 to $275 | $255 | $150 (1–5 units) |
| [GOLDfeather](https://www.goldfeatherpropertymanagement.com/pricing/) | Nashville | 7.9%, 8.9% or 11.9% | Not shown | Not shown | $299 one time |
| [Redbird](https://www.redbird-pm.com/pricing) | Franklin | 7%, 10% or 12% | 50%, 25% or none (by plan) | 10% of 1 month, or none | $250 or $125 |
| [Mynd](https://www.mynd.co/locations/tennessee/nashville-area-property-management) | Nashville | $129 flat (1 home) | 50% of 1 month | $299 | Not shown |
| [Ziprent](https://ziprent.com/property-management/nashville/) | Nashville | $150 flat ($250 with ZipGuarantee) | $1,500 | $250 (included in ZipGuarantee) | Not shown |

What the table shows:

- **Percent fees.** 9 of 11 managers charge a percent of rent. The range is 7% to 12%. Evernest, PURE HomeRiver and Dyad charge exactly 8%. Baseline starts at 8%.
- **Flat fees.** Mynd charges $129 a month for 1 home, $119 each for 2 homes and $109 each for 3 to 10 homes. Ziprent charges $150 for the first home and $100 for each more home.
- **Bronze-type plans.** Stevens Realty also sells a 4% Bronze plan. It leaves out tenant maintenance oversight, so we left it out of the range.

Watch for: a "percent of rent" can mean rent due or rent collected. Evernest and Dyad say collected. Ziprent says it does not charge the monthly fee when it does not collect rent. Ask each manager which one applies.

## What leasing and renewal fees do Nashville managers charge?

Nashville leasing fees center on 50% of one month's rent. 7 of the 11 pages use that number for at least one plan. Two managers set a floor: Evernest at $500 and Baseline TN at $1,500. Ziprent charges a flat $1,500. Canden Properties charges $750 to new clients.

## Which local rules add cost for Nashville owners?

Short-term rental rules add the most local cost in Nashville. Metro Nashville requires a short-term rental property permit from the Metro Codes Department before you list a home. Airbnb's Nashville page cites Metro Code Sec. 6.28.030 and lists two permit types: owner-occupied and non-owner-occupied. It says new non-owner-occupied permits are only allowed in certain non-residential zones. Each permit is valid for 365 days and you must renew it each year.

So a long-term rental and a short-term rental are two different jobs in Nashville. Short-term work adds permits, hotel occupancy tax filings and guest turnover. Ask a manager for a separate quote if you want both.

## Must a Nashville property manager hold a Tennessee license?

A Nashville property manager who works for a fee must hold a Tennessee real estate license in most cases. Tenn. Code Ann. 62-13-102(4)(A) defines a broker as a person who, for a fee, negotiates a lease or rental, or "collects rents or attempts to collect rents." The Tennessee Real Estate Commission (TREC) issues and enforces these licenses.

Checklist before you sign:

- [ ] Ask for the principal broker's name and license number.
- [ ] Look up the license on the TREC site.
- [ ] Ask where the manager holds your rent and the tenant's deposit.
- [ ] Get every fee in the management agreement, not only on the web page.

This is general information, not legal advice. Check the current law or ask a local attorney.

## Flat fee or percent: which model is common in Nashville?

The percent model is more common in Nashville. 9 of the 11 managers we checked charge a percent of rent. Only Mynd and Ziprent sell a flat monthly fee.

Use rent to choose. At 8%, the fee equals $150 when rent is $1,875 a month. Below that rent, 8% costs less than Ziprent's $150 flat plan. Above it, the flat plan costs less. Mynd's $129 equals 8% of $1,612.50. Compare the full year, with leasing and renewal fees, before you choose.
