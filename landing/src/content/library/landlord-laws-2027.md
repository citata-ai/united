## Which landlord laws start in 2027?

Four state changes start in 2027 for owners and property managers. Two are rent caps that reset each year. Two are new rules from 2026 bills.

| State | What changes | Starts | Who it covers | Law |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Washington | Rent increase cap: **10%** in any 12 months (2026: 9.683%) | 2027-01-01 | Most residential units. Exempt: units 12 years old or newer, some owner-occupied and nonprofit housing | RCW 59.18 (EHB 1217, 2025) |
| Oregon | Rent increase cap: **10%** in any 12 months (2026: 9.5%) | 2027-01-01 | Most residential units. Exempt: units less than 15 years old, some regulated affordable housing | ORS 90.323, 90.324 |
| Illinois | Application and screening fee cap **$50**; every required fee on page 1 of the lease; a list of banned fees | Leases signed on or after 2027-01-01 | All residential leases, except owner-occupied buildings with 6 units or fewer | HB 3564 (765 ILCS 705/35) |
| Virginia | Written notice of a renewal rent increase **90 days** before the lease ends (now 60) | 2027-07-01 | Landlords with more than 4 rental units, or more than 10% interest in more than 4 | HB 678, Chapter 1066 (Va. Code § 55.1-1204(K)) |

We read each law or agency page on 2026-11-04. The Illinois row uses two summaries (a law firm and Chicago REALTORS), because the ilga.gov bill page did not load in our run. Check the enrolled text before you change a lease.

## What is the Washington rent cap for 2027?

The Washington rent cap for 2027 is 10%. The Washington Department of Commerce publishes the number each year, and it applies to increases from 2027-01-01 to 2027-12-31.

The law (EHB 1217, 2025) sets the cap at 7% plus the June 12-month change in the Seattle-area CPI, or 10%, whichever is less. For 2026 the cap was 9.683%. For 2027 the formula gives more than 10%, so the 10% limit applies.

Other Washington rules from the same law:

- **No increase in year 1.** A landlord may not raise rent in the first 12 months of a tenancy.
- **90 days' notice.** A landlord must give at least 90 days' written notice of any rent increase. Income-based subsidized units need 30 days.
- **Exemptions.** The cap does not apply to a unit whose first certificate of occupancy is 12 years old or less on the notice date. It also does not apply to some public, nonprofit and tax-credit housing, and to some owner-occupied homes and 2-4 unit buildings. The owner-occupied exemptions do not apply to a REIT or a corporation.
- **Claim the exemption in writing.** A landlord who goes above the cap must put the facts for the exemption in the notice.
- **Penalties.** A court can award the excess rent, up to 3 months of the unlawful rent and attorney fees. The attorney general can ask for up to $7,500 per violation.
- **Lease parity.** Rent for the same unit may not differ by more than 5% between a month-to-month and a fixed-term lease.

The cap rules in EHB 1217 expire on 2040-07-01.

## What is the Oregon rent cap for 2027?

The Oregon rent cap for 2027 is 10%, per the Oregon Office of Economic Analysis. The 2026 cap was 9.5%. The formula is 7% plus CPI, with a maximum of 10%.

ORS 90.323 sets the other rules:

- **No increase in year 1.** A landlord may not raise rent in the first year of a tenancy.
- **90 days' notice.** After year 1, the landlord gives written notice at least 90 days before the increase. A week-to-week tenancy needs 7 days.
- **Once a year.** Rent may go up only once in any 12 months.
- **What the notice says.** The amount of the increase, the new rent, the start date, and the facts for any exemption.
- **Exemptions.** The cap does not apply when the first certificate of occupancy is less than 15 years old on the notice date. It also does not apply to some regulated affordable housing.

Manufactured dwelling parks and floating home facilities with more than 30 spaces have a separate cap of 6% under ORS 90.600.

## What changes for Illinois rental fees in 2027?

Illinois HB 3564 changes rental fees for leases signed on or after 2027-01-01. A trailer bill, HB 5234, moved the start from 2026-07-01 to 2027-01-01 (Chicago REALTORS, 2026-07-10). Governor Pritzker signed HB 3564 at the end of June 2026.

The main rules, per Gould & Ratner (2026-08-31):

- **$50 application fee cap.** The cap includes the background check. A landlord may charge more only when the real third-party screening cost is higher. The landlord pays the provider first and bills the applicant with a receipt within 14 days. If not, the extra charge is waived.
- **No duplicate charges.** "Processing" or "administrative" fees that repeat the screening cost are not allowed under a new name.
- **Page 1 of the lease.** Every non-optional fee, one-time or recurring, must be on the first page. If a required fee is not on page 1, the tenant does not have to pay it.
- **Listings.** An ad that shows rent must show the non-optional fees, on the ad or by a link. Both the ad and the lease must say if utilities are in the rent.
- **Banned fees.** No fee to renew or change a lease. No eviction notice fee before a court order. No fees for after-hours maintenance, a maintenance hotline, repair travel or routine upkeep. No fee to contact the manager. No pest control fee when the tenant did not cause the pests. No move-in or move-out walkthrough fee.
- **Not changed.** Security deposits and move-in fees are not banned. Late fees get no new statewide cap.
- **Exemption.** Owner-occupied buildings with 6 units or fewer.
- **Remedies.** A tenant can sue for injunctive and money relief, plus attorney fees and costs.

For property managers, the work is in the lease template and the listing template. Put the fee list on page 1 before the first 2027 lease.

## What changes for Virginia rent notices in 2027?

Virginia HB 678 (Chapter 1066, approved 2026-04-22) changes the rent increase notice at renewal. The act starts on 2027-07-01.

Today, Va. Code § 55.1-1204(K) asks for 60 days' notice of a renewal rent increase. From 2027-07-01:

- **90 days for a rent increase.** A landlord with more than 4 rental units gives written notice at least 90 days before the lease term ends. This applies when the tenant can renew or the lease renews by itself.
- **A reply deadline.** The notice must give a date for the tenant to say if they will renew. That date is at least 30 days after the notice arrives.
- **60 days for nonrenewal.** If the landlord does not renew, the written notice of nonrenewal still comes at least 60 days before the lease ends.
- **Small landlords.** The rule covers landlords with more than 4 units, or more than a 10% interest in more than 4 units, in Virginia.

Some trackers say HB 678 changes the security deposit cap. It does not. The deposit cap stays at 2 months of rent (Va. Code § 55.1-1226). SB 349, a bill to cut it to 1 month, was continued to the 2027 session.

## What should a property manager do before 2027?

Each change needs a template update before the start date. Use this list:

1. **Washington and Oregon.** Check every increase notice dated for 2027 against the 10% cap. Keep the 90-day notice. Write the exemption facts in the notice if you claim one.
2. **Illinois.** Move every required fee to page 1 of the lease. Set the application fee to $50 or the real screening cost. Remove banned fees from the fee schedule and from listings.
3. **Virginia.** The new timing applies to leases that end 90 days or more after 2027-07-01 (our reading: from late September 2027). Send renewal increase notices 90 days out. Give a reply date at least 30 days later. Put the new dates in your renewal calendar now.
4. **All states.** Tell owners what changes and when. A short owner letter in December prevents surprise questions in January.
