## Who can buy ChatGPT ads now?

Businesses in the countries on OpenAI's availability list can buy ChatGPT ads, and the United States is on that list. OpenAI says advertisers can buy through agency partners or through a beta self-serve Ads Manager. In Ads Manager, a business can register, add payment details, set budgets and bids, upload ads and view results.

One rule matters for small firms. OpenAI says the legal entity that advertises and gets the bill must be based in an available country. On 2026-10-19, OpenAI's help center listed the United States, Canada, the United Kingdom and more than 50 other countries as "Available".

Buying options as OpenAI describes them:

| Option | What OpenAI says |
|---|---|
| Self-serve Ads Manager | Beta. Sign up at ads.openai.com. "Gradually opening" to more businesses. |
| Agency partners | Dentsu, Omnicom, Publicis and WPP, plus technology partners such as Adobe and Criteo. |
| CPM bidding | Pay per 1,000 impressions. The first pricing model in the pilot. |
| CPC bidding | Pay only for a click. Added later in the pilot. |

OpenAI says "we're still early in building advertising in ChatGPT". Expect the rules and the tools to change.

## Where do ChatGPT ads show?

ChatGPT ads show below a ChatGPT response, in one labeled ad unit. OpenAI's help center says ads are "clearly labeled and shown separately" from the answer. OpenAI is also testing an image ad format during image generation.

Not everyone sees ads. Per OpenAI's help center:

- **Free and Go users** may see ads.
- **Plus, Pro, Business, Enterprise and Edu** accounts have no ads.
- **Users under 18** do not see ads, based on account age data and age prediction.
- **Temporary Chats** show no ads.
- **Sensitive chats** about personal health, mental health or politics are not eligible for ads.

Think about who your buyer is. A landlord with 3 homes may use the free plan. A busy investor may pay for Plus and never see your ad.

## Can a property manager advertise rentals in ChatGPT?

No. OpenAI's ad policies say ads for individual housing rentals or sales are prohibited. OpenAI's changelog says it clarified its stance on housing and job listings in August 2026.

What you can do:

- **Advertise your management service** to owners. That is not a rental listing.
- **Link to a platform with listings,** if the ad and the landing page do not name a specific listing. The policy allows this.

What you cannot do:

- Run an ad for "3-bed home for rent at [address]".
- Send an ad to a page for one unit.

Read the full policy before you launch. OpenAI also keeps the right to decline ads, per its September 2026 policy update.

## Do ChatGPT ads affect the recommendations?

No, according to OpenAI. Its help center says "ads do not influence ChatGPT's answers". It says ads run on separate systems from the chat model, and advertisers cannot shape, rank or alter responses. It also says an ad does not mean OpenAI endorses the advertiser.

This is the key point for a property manager. When an owner asks "Who is the best property manager in Denver?", the answer lists companies from ChatGPT's own sources. Your ad may show below that answer. It does not move you into the list.

| Question | Organic AI answer | ChatGPT ad |
|---|---|---|
| Can you pay for it? | No | Yes, CPM or CPC |
| Where does it show? | Inside the answer | Below the answer, labeled |
| Who sees it? | All plans | Free and Go users only |
| What drives it? | Sources ChatGPT finds: sites, directories, reviews | Bid, relevance, ad text, landing page |
| Does it last? | Changes run to run | Stops when the budget stops |

To show up inside the answer, work on the sources ChatGPT reads. We ran 150 AI answers for 50 cities. Expertise.com was a source in 93 of them, and Yelp and iPropertyManagement in 50 each (our data, 2026-10). ## How do you measure ChatGPT ad leads?

Measure ChatGPT ad leads with OpenAI's tools and your own owner form. OpenAI says it launched a Conversions API and pixel-based measurement for actions such as a purchase, a lead or a sign-up. Advertisers get aggregated results, not the user's chat.

Set up 4 things before you spend:

1. **A separate landing page** for ChatGPT ad clicks. Then every form fill on it is an ad lead.
2. **The OpenAI pixel or Conversions API** on the form's thank-you step.
3. **A "How did you hear about us?" field** with "ChatGPT ad" and "ChatGPT answer" as two separate options.
4. **Crawler access for OAI-AdsBot.** OpenAI says this bot visits pages submitted as ads to check them against its policies. Do not block it in robots.txt.

Then use the break-even math from your other channels. Divide your monthly ad spend by the margin of one signed owner. That gives the number of owners the ads must bring each month. ## Should a property manager buy ChatGPT ads now?

Test ChatGPT ads only with a small, fixed budget and a clear stop date. The product is a beta, the audience excludes paid-plan users, and no public data shows owner-lead results for property managers yet.

Put your first hours into the organic answer. It reaches every user, costs no ad spend and keeps working when your budget stops. Fix your directory profiles, your fee page and your city pages first. Then try ads as an extra, and judge them on signed owners, not clicks.
