## Which loan documents hold the occupancy rule?

Your signed security instrument and related loan documents hold the terms you agreed to follow. Read them before relying on a general article about renting. Find the note, mortgage or deed of trust, riders, and any separate occupancy promises.

Look for occupancy, use, notices, transfers, leases, and insurance provisions. Also identify exceptions or a consent process. A lender's current product page does not replace the documents signed at closing.

Fannie Mae's servicing guidance illustrates why occupancy still matters. Its LLC transfer rule says a permitted change to investment use must not violate the security instrument. The guidance gives a 12-month principal-residence occupancy requirement as an example.

That example does not create a single rule for every mortgage. Your loan may have different program conditions or facts. Ask the servicer to identify the provision that controls your situation.

| Document or record | What to check |
|---|---|
| Security instrument | Occupancy, use, notice, and transfer terms |
| Note | Payment and default provisions |
| Riders | Extra terms for the property or program |
| Closing statements | What you represented about occupancy |
| Servicer response | Required consent, documents, and conditions |

## What should you say when you contact the servicer?

Describe the actual plan and ask for a written response. Use the servicer contact shown on your loan statement. Keep a dated record of the request and any reference number.

Explain when you bought the home, when you occupied it, and when you expect to move. State whether you will rent the entire home or only a room. Include the proposed lease term and whether it has a purchase option.

Ask these questions in plain language:

- **Identify the rule.** Which signed provision or loan program condition applies to this use?
- **Confirm consent.** Do you require written permission before the lease begins?
- **List documents.** Do you need a lease, explanation, insurance evidence, or updated contact information?
- **Explain consequences.** Does this change any current loan term or reporting duty?
- **Confirm completion.** How will you record that I met the required steps?

Treat this as a request outline, not a legal form. Do not omit facts to fit a preferred answer. If the response conflicts with the documents, ask a qualified attorney to review both.

Do not sign a lease that depends on permission you have not obtained. Explain any necessary conditions to your local adviser before drafting the rental agreement.

## Can the lender call the loan due?

A due-on-sale clause can allow acceleration after a covered transfer, but federal law includes important limits. It is separate from every other promise in the mortgage. Keep those questions distinct.

Under 12 USC Section 1701j-3(d), covered residential property with fewer than 5 dwelling units has specified transfer protections. The listed protections include granting a lease of 3 years or less without an option to purchase.

Ask counsel how the statute applies to your loan and lease. Do not assume a longer lease or a purchase option receives the same treatment. Fannie Mae's servicing guidance also addresses lease terms and renewal options.

The federal lease protection does not automatically cure a separate occupancy breach. The loan documents and the facts still need review. Keep paying the loan while you resolve questions rather than treating a dispute as permission to stop.

An LLC transfer is also different from granting a lease. Do not record a deed because a short lease may receive federal protection. A title change needs its own lender, legal, tax, and insurance review.

## Is renting without notice mortgage fraud?

A later decision to rent does not supply enough facts to label the conduct. Review what you represented when borrowing and what you actually did. Ask counsel about any inaccurate statement or possible breach.

Do not use a fixed waiting period as proof that all earlier statements were accurate. Likewise, do not assume a genuine later job move creates a criminal violation. The signed promises and facts require a proper review.

Give the servicer accurate information now. If earlier records contain an error, ask an attorney how to correct it. Do not create a false occupancy history or backdate an explanation.

Keep the distinction between a contract issue and an allegation of fraud clear. A general guide cannot decide either for a particular borrower. Obtain advice before taking an action based on a feared or assumed consequence.

## Is renting a room different from renting the whole home?

Renting a room can leave you in residence, which changes the facts you must describe. It does not create universal permission. Check the actual loan terms and tell the servicer who will occupy each part.

Also review association and local requirements. A permitted loan use does not settle zoning or licensing. Ask the relevant office about the arrangement you intend to offer.

NAIC warns that rental use can affect homeowners coverage. Tell the insurer whether you remain in the home, the stay pattern, and any services you provide. Obtain suitable coverage before accepting a renter.
