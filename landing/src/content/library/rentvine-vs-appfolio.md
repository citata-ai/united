## How do Rentvine and AppFolio prices compare?

The advertised starting rates are close, but they cannot settle the price comparison. Rentvine says “as low as,” which does not promise that rate to every account. AppFolio states that a minimum spend applies to Core. Its higher tiers also have unit and spend minimums.

Ask both vendors for the same scope. Include occupied and vacant units, bank accounts, payment volume and staff needs. State which extra tools you want to remove after the move.

Separate recurring charges from setup costs. Rentvine explicitly describes a one-time setup fee for migration and onboarding. Its public page also says transaction and screening fees may apply. Ask AppFolio for a matching schedule of charges.

Compare the steady monthly bill and moving cost separately. An introductory discount can hide the later bill. Save a dated quote with your buying notes and have the vendor confirm the renewal basis.

## Which has stronger trust accounting?

Rentvine provides a clear public description of three-way reconciliation, but that does not prove a better result for your company. Rentvine describes matching the bank statement, trust ledger and property ledgers. AppFolio lists property accounting and reporting, with advanced accounting in Plus.

Ask your accountant to define the test before the demo. Start with a reconciled period and known balances. Add a prepayment, returned payment, owner contribution and unpaid vendor invoice.

Require a report that explains every difference. Check whether staff can trace each owner balance to transactions. Ask who can reopen a closed period and how that action appears in the record.

Do not select software based on the phrase “trust accounting” alone. The right tool must support your account structure and review process. Have your accountant approve the reports before you approve the migration.

## How do their AI features compare?

Both vendors place AI tools inside their property systems. Rentvine lists an AI Assistant, Fixie maintenance agent and MCP connection. MCP is a connection standard for AI tools. AppFolio lists Realm-X Assistant and Messages in Core, with more automation in higher plans.

Test access before testing speed. Give a limited staff account a question about another owner's records. Confirm that the tool follows the account's access rules. Ask how an administrator reviews actions and reverses errors.

Use a maintenance request with missing information. Check whether the assistant asks for facts or invents them. Then try a rent question that requires a current lease record.

Ask which AI functions are included, optional or usage-based. Treat a demonstration of a separate module as a separate purchase. Neither vendor's feature list proves how much time your staff will save.

## Which has better owner and tenant portals?

Portal quality depends on the tasks your owners and renters need to finish. Rentvine lists portals among its core features. AppFolio's owner help page describes owner access and account help. Public pages alone cannot establish which portal users find easier.

Create test accounts for an owner and a renter. Ask each person to find a statement without staff help. Have the renter submit a repair request with a photo. Check whether the office receives the right property and unit details.

For owners, test report dates, distribution records and access to older documents. Check what happens when ownership changes. A former owner should not retain access to future records.

Avoid sharing staff-only notes by default. Decide which work-order details owners need and which details require review. The best portal gives users clear records without exposing unrelated people or properties.

## How hard is a switch between them?

A switch depends on record quality, export access and the scope of the import. A vendor's migration service does not necessarily include every attachment or historical transaction. Ask both sides for a written list of what moves.

Map old accounts to new accounts before importing balances. Assign a person to unresolved ledger differences. Keep signed leases and original statements in a separate archive your company controls.

Use a trial import to test the move:

- **Contacts:** match owners, renters and vendors to the right properties.
- **Balances:** compare deposits, prepayments, unpaid bills and owner funds.
- **Documents:** open actual leases and invoice attachments.
- **Payments:** confirm which bank authorizations need fresh setup.
- **Reports:** compare the final old statement with the opening new statement.

Do not shut down the old account after a successful contact import alone. Accounting and documents need their own checks.

## How should you choose the final system?

Choose the system that passes your tests with fewer unresolved exceptions. Record each exception, its workaround and who owns that work. A lower subscription price can still lead to more staff work.

Ask for references with the same rental mix. Focus the call on bank reconciliation, support and owner reporting. Ask which jobs remain outside the system after setup.

Keep the buying choice separate from owner marketing. Software can manage a client record after contact. It does not ensure owners will find your company. See [how owners choose a manager with AI](/guides/how-owners-choose-a-property-manager-with-ai) for that separate task.
