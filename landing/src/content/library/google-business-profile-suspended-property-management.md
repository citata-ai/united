## Why do property management Google Business Profiles get suspended?

Google suspends a Business Profile when it decides the profile breaks its guidelines. Google's suspension help page says it may suspend or disable profiles that do not follow the guidelines. The page does not list every trigger, so check your profile against the guideline rules one by one.

A suspension costs a property manager more than most businesses. Goodjuju Marketing's 2026 lead source report studied 35 property management companies. In its fall window, Google Search and the Map Pack brought 50.6% of owner leads. A suspended profile drops out of the Map Pack.

These are the rules in Google's guidelines that property managers break most often:

| Rule break | What the guideline says | Fix before you appeal |
|---|---|---|
| Virtual office or mailbox | A virtual office is not eligible; P.O. boxes are not acceptable | Use your real office, or hide the address as a service-area business |
| Coworking desk | Allowed only with signage, staff and customers received during hours | Hide the address if you do not meet all three |
| Keywords in the name | Extra words in the name are not allowed and can lead to suspension | Use the exact name on your license and signage |
| Two profiles for one place | One profile per business per location | Ask Google to merge or remove the duplicate |
| Service area too large | About 2 hours of driving time from your base | Cut the list to cities you serve |
| Paid or fake reviews | Incentives for reviews are fake and misleading content | Stop the program; Google can block new reviews for a set time |

A name like "Smith Property Management Chicago Best Rentals" is a common case. Google's guidelines say unnecessary information in the name is not allowed and could result in suspension. Use "Smith Property Management" if that is the name on your sign and license.

## What proof does Google ask for in a suspension appeal?

Google's appeal page lists evidence that can make an appeal stronger:

- an official business registration
- a business license
- tax certificates
- utility bills for the business, such as electricity, phone, water or internet

Google adds one tip: the business name and address on each document must match the profile you appeal. A lease in the owner's personal name, or a bill at a home address when the profile shows an office, weakens the case.

For a property management company, add documents that prove the business is real at that place:

1. Your state real estate or property management license, if your state requires one.
2. A photo of permanent signage at the office, with the street visible.
3. The office lease or deed in the company name.
4. A recent invoice or management agreement header that shows the same name and address (remove owner data).

### Appeal steps

1. Fix every rule break first. Google says to make sure the profile follows all guidelines.
2. Open the Google Business Profile appeals tool while signed in to the Google account that owns the profile.
3. Select the profile and the decision you want to appeal.
4. Attach your files. You can attach more than one.
5. Write a short, factual note: what you fixed and what each file proves.
6. Check the box and submit. Google says appeals can be submitted only once.

If the account itself is restricted, Google's page says to appeal the account restriction first on the My accounts page, then appeal the profile.

## How long does Google Business Profile reinstatement take?

Google's help pages do not give a fixed time for an appeal decision. Google says it reviews the appeal and sends an email with the decision. You can check the status in the appeals tool.

Plan for lost leads while you wait. During the suspension:

- **Answer the phone from other sources.** Your website, Yelp, directories and referral partners still send owners.
- **Tell current owners.** If they search your name and see nothing, a short email stops worry.
- **Do not edit the suspended profile.** More edits add noise to the review.

If Google denies the appeal, its page says you may be able to request an additional review and add new evidence that was not in the first appeal. Use that chance for the document you missed, not for the same files again.

## How do I avoid a second Google Business Profile suspension?

The best defense is a profile that matches the real world on every field. Make a written rule set for your team and follow it on each edit.

1. **One owner account.** Keep the profile owner as a company Google account, not a past employee or agency login. Add managers as needed.
2. **One change per week.** Do not edit name, address, phone and category together.
3. **Real name only.** Match the license and sign. No city or service words added.
4. **Honest reviews.** Google says incentives for reviews are prohibited. The FTC's 2024 final rule also bans compensation conditioned on reviews with a set sentiment. Ask all owners and tenants for reviews the same way, with no reward.
5. **Agency access in writing.** If an agency manages the profile, list what it may change.
6. **Yearly audit.** Each January, read the profile against Google's guidelines and log what you checked.
