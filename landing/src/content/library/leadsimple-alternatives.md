## What does LeadSimple do?

LeadSimple's pricing page combines CRM, operations and communications in One and Max. Start by listing which jobs your company uses.

CRM means a system for tracking customer relationships. A property manager may track prospective owners, current clients and residents. Their follow-up steps differ.

An owner lead may need a call, proposal and agreement. A resident lead may need availability, a showing and an application. Workflows can coordinate the steps after either relationship begins.

Before replacing LeadSimple, write the current process as a list. Name the responsible person, trigger and output for each step. Mark what the software handles. The replacement must cover that chain, or your team must accept and price the remaining work. A missing handoff can matter more than a missing dashboard.

## Why do managers consider alternatives?

A manager may want fewer tools, different workflows or a lower complete bill. These are decision questions, not verified claims about every LeadSimple user.

Inspect your own task history. Find where work waits, messages repeat or staff copy data. Ask whether the cause is software, setup or an unclear process.

Check your current plan before comparing old prices. LeadSimple now uses One and Max on its pricing page. It says existing customers can keep current plans. Your bill may differ from a new offer.

Try a process repair first. Remove unused steps, assign clear owners and fix missing fields. Then test whether delays remain. New software cannot decide who should call an owner if your team has not assigned responsibility. Keep the repair option beside any replacement quote.

## Which PMS tools include CRM features?

Rentvine includes CRM in its platform offer. Buildium lists prospect management. EliseAI presents EliseCRM. Each requires a closer scope check.

Ask whether the record represents an owner prospect, rental applicant or current resident. Leasing follow-up may not cover management proposals or owner agreements.

Give each vendor the same owner-sales case. Record the source, schedule follow-up, attach a proposal and mark the agreement signed. Ask how the approved owner record reaches operations.

Test the handoff after the sale. Staff should not rebuild data without a clear reason. Ask who controls duplicate records and updates. Confirm the quoted plan includes the workflow. Keep leasing and owner acquisition as separate tests. If the product handles only one, decide whether it replaces a part of LeadSimple rather than the whole service.

## How do prices compare?

LeadSimple pricing depends on commitment, doors and usage. The current One price is not an unlimited flat offer.

Its pricing page lists One at $199 monthly or $169 with an annual commitment. Extra doors and communications can add charges. Use your actual activity to request a full estimate.

Ask alternatives for the same work. Include owner pipelines, users, calls, messages, automation and connections. If the vendor publishes no price for that scope, treat the offer as quote only.

Compare the extra cost needed to finish the workflow. A PMS subscription you already need is not directly comparable with a separate CRM bill. Keep setup and training visible. Use the [management fee calculator](/tools/property-management-fee-calculator) to check confirmed costs against your service income.

## How does LeadSimple integrate with Rentvine?

LeadSimple documents a direct API connection with Rentvine. An API lets software exchange structured records. Its help page lists owners, tenants, properties, units, applicants, vendors and work orders.

Some information moves only from Rentvine to LeadSimple; other information can move both ways. Do not turn that detail into a blanket claim of two-way sync.

Use this connection checklist:

- Confirm access under your plans.
- Name the system that owns each record.
- Check field direction.
- Create and update a sample owner.
- Inspect linked property records.
- Review errors and retries.
- Test duplicate handling.
- Confirm how to stop the connection.

Document missing fields and delayed updates. A connection can exist while leaving important work manual. Ask the vendor to show the actual update and the destination record. Save that map with your operating instructions.

## How should you choose a replacement?

Choose after owner-sales and operations handoff tests pass. Use the same records for every option.

Save current workflows and exports before changing the live account. Decide where the final customer record belongs. Test permissions and message history before staff start using the replacement.

Record the full bill and remaining manual steps. Ask a reference to explain a failed handoff and recovery. Avoid ranking products by unverified review scores.

Owner acquisition also depends on how people find your company. Read [how owners use AI to choose a manager](/guides/how-owners-choose-a-property-manager-with-ai) for that separate task. Bring your lead-to-agreement process to the demo and require the vendor to complete it.

Keep a copy of the tested workflow with your contract. Staff should know which product owns each handoff after the change.
