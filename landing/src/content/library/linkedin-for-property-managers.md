A listing agent meets a seller who cannot get the price they want. The seller asks, "Should I just rent it out?" The agent needs a property manager to call, today. NAR reported existing-home sales of 3.99 million in September 2026. Each of those deals has an agent, and some sellers choose to rent instead. LinkedIn is a good place to become the manager that agents call.

## What should a property manager's LinkedIn profile show?

A property manager's LinkedIn profile should show who you serve, where and how you charge, in the first lines. Agents and owners scan the headline and About section.

| Profile part | Weak | Strong |
|---|---|---|
| Headline | "CEO" | "Property manager, 240 single-family homes in Columbus, OH" |
| About | Company history | Who you help, areas, fee model, response time, one proof number |
| Featured | Empty | Owner page, rent-vs-sell page, a market report |
| Experience | Job title only | Doors, years, license, services |
| Photo | Logo | A real face |

Profile checklist:

- [ ] City and ZIP areas in the headline or About
- [ ] Door count and property types
- [ ] Fee model in plain words
- [ ] License number, if your state requires a broker license for property management
- [ ] Link to your owner page with a UTM tag (a tracking label in a link)
- [ ] Contact details that match your Google Business Profile

## How do I connect with local agents?

Connect with local agents in small, personal batches. Fourandhalf, a property management marketing agency, suggests searching for "Realtor" or "Real Estate Agent," then using "All Filters" to narrow by city or region.

A 4-week plan:

1. **Week 1: list 40 agents.** Pick agents with recent listings in your service area.
2. **Weeks 1–4: send 10 invites a week.** Add a 2-line note: who you are and one useful offer.
3. **After they accept: send one helpful item.** For example, a rent estimate for one of their current listings.
4. **Each month: share one agent's listing** or win, with their consent.

Sample note: "Hi Jordan, I manage 240 rentals in Columbus. If a seller of yours asks about renting instead, I can send a free rent estimate the same day. No strings."

What agents want from you:

- A fast rent estimate when a seller wavers.
- A promise to send the home back to them when the owner sells later.
- A clean handoff and updates on the owner they referred.

That second point builds the relationship. Write it into your process: when a managed owner decides to sell, call the agent who referred them.

| Item | Value |
|---|---|
| Agents connected | 40 |

## Which posts work on LinkedIn?

Posts with local facts and a clear point work best. LinkedIn's Pages best practices page gives these numbers from its own data:

- "Images typically result in a 2x higher comment rate."
- "Video gets 5x more engagement on LinkedIn, Live Video gets 24x more."
- "Companies that post weekly see a 2x lift in engagement with their content."

Post ideas for a property manager:

1. **Rent vs sell:** a real (anonymous) home, the sale net vs a year of rent net, with the numbers.
2. **Market fact:** your median days on market last month.
3. **New rule:** a city or state rental rule, with the official source linked.
4. **Behind the scenes:** a turnover in 4 photos, before and after, with owner consent.
5. **Agent shout-out:** thank an agent who referred an owner, with their consent.

Keep posts short: a 1-line hook, 3 to 5 lines of facts, one question. Do not post tenant details or photos of tenants.

## Should the company page or the founder post?

The founder should post, and the company page should back it up. People connect with people. An agent remembers "Dana who manages homes in Columbus," not a logo.

The company page still matters. LinkedIn says "pages with complete information get 30% more weekly views." Fill every field: description with your city and services, website, industry, size and location. Then share the founder's best posts from the page.

| Task | Founder profile | Company page |
|---|---|---|
| Weekly post | Yes | Share the best one |
| Agent invites | Yes | No |
| Complete info | Yes | Yes, every field |
| Job posts | No | Yes |

Keep it in scale. In the Goodjuju Marketing fall 2026 study of 35 property management companies, all social media gave 0.8% of owner leads. LinkedIn is a referral tool, not a lead machine. Give it 1 to 2 hours a week and track every referral by source. For the bigger picture on owner leads, read [why owner leads are dropping](/guides/why-owner-leads-are-dropping).
