## What is Apple Business Connect now?

Apple Business Connect is now part of Apple Business. Apple combined Apple Business Manager, Apple Business Essentials and Apple Business Connect into one product. You sign in at business.apple.com. Apple's guides published on 2026-04-14 use the new name.

The part a property manager needs is the same: the place card. A place card is the panel that opens when someone taps your business in Apple Maps. Apple says the place card appears "from Maps to Siri to Calendar, Messages, and more."

Apple said in October 2024 that businesses can register and customize their brand for free. Listing tools like Yext, SOCi, Uberall, Rio SEO and DAC can also manage Apple listings for you.

## How do you claim your Apple Maps listing?

You claim your Apple Maps listing in three steps: sign up, verify your organization, then add or claim the location. Here are the steps from Apple's guides, as read on 2026-10-19.

1. **Go to business.apple.com** and select Sign up now.
2. **Enter your company name, website and address.** Apple says the website is optional but can speed up verification.
3. **Enter a real person's name.** Apple returns names like "IT Coordinator" for correction.
4. **Confirm the codes** sent to your email and phone.
5. **Verify your organization** within 60 days. If Apple does not approve it in that window, it deletes the organization and its data.
6. **Choose Brands, then Locations, then Add Location.** Search for your office if it already shows on Maps.
7. **Move the pin** if it sits on the wrong building.
8. **Verify the location.** For an existing place, Apple may offer a phone call to the number it has on record. You type the 4-digit code from the call.

If another account already manages your location, select Request Transfer. Add a business license or utility bill. Apple says the review can take up to 5 business days.

Tip: allow email from all apple.com domains. Apple sends verification and change notices by email.

## What fields does Apple show on a place card?

Apple shows your name, category, address, hours, phone, website, photos, an About text and optional extras. Here are the fields and Apple's rules for each.

| Field | Apple's rule | What to enter as a property manager |
|---|---|---|
| Display name | Must match your storefront or website; no extra words | Your legal brand name, no "Best PM in Denver" |
| Primary category | Exactly 1, as specific as possible | The closest property management category |
| Other categories | Up to 19 more | Related services you really offer |
| Address | Where customers get services during open hours | Your office address |
| Pin | On top of the building, centered | Your office building |
| Hours | Normal, special and temporary hours | Office hours, or appointment hours |
| Phone | Must include the country code; no extensions | Your main line |
| Website | Your own site; a location page is fine; no third-party sites | Your city or office page |
| About | Expertise, specialties, history, achievements | Door count, cities, services, years in business |
| Photos | Logo and cover photo; Apple reviews them | Office front, team, logo |
| Good to Know | Amenities and facts | Accessible parking, card payment, if true |
| Showcase | Time-limited promotion with a call to action | An owner offer, if you have one |

Write the About text with facts AI and people can reuse. Example: "Full-service residential property management in Denver and Aurora since 2014. 420 doors. Single-family homes, condos and small multifamily." Use your real numbers.

Keep the same name, address, phone and website as on your Google Business Profile and your website. One set of facts across platforms makes your company easier to identify.

## Where does Apple take reviews from?

Apple takes ratings from Apple Maps users. A user with an Apple Account taps Rate on a place card and rates the place in the categories shown. Users can also add photos. Apple says the Ratings and Photos feature is not available in all countries or regions.

Apple also says some place card areas show data "provided by the Apple ecosystem and related partners." Apple's guides do not list which review partners appear for your category. Check your own place card on an iPhone to see what shows.

Apple says Maps may suggest a rating after a person visits a place or takes a photo there. So Apple ratings grow slowly for an office that few people visit.

## How long does verification take?

Organization verification can take multiple business days, based on how many applications Apple has. Here are the times Apple states:

| Step | Time Apple states |
|---|---|
| Create the organization | Minutes |
| Window to verify the organization | 60 days |
| Organization verification | Multiple business days |
| Location verification by phone | During the call, when offered |
| Location transfer review | Up to 5 business days |
| Display name change review | Up to 5 business days |

Apple can also change your place card. If a user reports that your office was closed when the card said open, Apple reviews it. Apple then emails your admins about changes to your name, status or hours. You can accept or contest each change.

## Is it worth it for a service-area business?

Apple Business is worth about an hour if you have an office address, even one open by appointment only. Apple says that if customers can visit only by appointment, you list the hours when appointments can be made.

If you have no office at all, a place card is a poor fit. Apple's address rule is a place where customers get services during open hours. A home address is not a good public address for a property manager.
