## How do Rentec Direct and Buildium prices compare?

Rentec Direct has lower published entry prices, but the correct manager comparison needs matching quotes. Compare Rentec PM with the Buildium plan your workflow requires.

| Product | Published starting price | Vendor-listed focus | Check before buying |
|---|---|---|---|
| Rentec Starter | $25 monthly | Smaller owner setup | Edition limits |
| Rentec Pro | $50 monthly | Landlords and investors | Owner-workflow requirements |
| Rentec PM | $50 monthly | Trust accounting and owner management | Price at your unit count |
| Buildium Essential | $62 monthly | Basic management suite | Add-on and transaction costs |
| Buildium Growth | $192 monthly | Added support and tools | Required onboarding scope |
| Buildium Premium | $400 monthly | Broader tools and API access | Required integrations |

Source: Rentec Direct and Buildium pricing pages, read on 2026-10-19. Starting prices are not fixed quotes for every portfolio.

Request the same unit count, user needs, payment volume, and document volume from each vendor. Put setup and ongoing charges in separate lines.

Avoid the low prices repeated on older comparison sites. The current Rentec Direct page does not support a token monthly price for a full manager account.

## Which supports three-way reconciliation?

Rentec Direct documents three-way reconciliation and the reports used to support it. Ask Buildium to demonstrate the equivalent close and evidence in the package you plan to buy.

Rentec's guide explains that adjusted bank, book, and client-ledger balances should agree. That definition is more useful than a generic bank-sync label.

A bank feed can bring transactions into a system without proving that client balances are correct. Test how your staff finds and resolves a difference.

Use a sample with an outstanding check, a returned receipt, and an unposted bank fee. Ask the vendor to show the source record, correction, and resulting reconciliation.

Have your bookkeeper review the owner balances as well as the bank total. A matching bank account does not by itself show that each owner's funds are correct.

Ask who can change a closed period and how the software records the change. Keep the accepted reports from the demo. Your team should be able to reproduce them without sales staff.

This is an accounting workflow comparison. Confirm your reporting process with the person responsible for your books and applicable local requirements.

## Which has better support?

Choose support by entitlement, availability, and the quality of a test response. This guide does not claim that either vendor resolves issues faster in practice.

Rentec Direct's pricing page says support and personalized onboarding are included. Buildium's pricing page distinguishes ticket support from phone support on higher plans. Get the actual entitlement in writing.

Ask a realistic question during evaluation. Describe a reconciliation issue or a portal-access problem without sharing private data. Note whether the answer explains the next action clearly.

Check who supports partner features. The main vendor may not control a screening provider, payment processor, or document service. Staff need to know where an issue starts and who owns the handoff.

Ask what happens when your trained admin is away. A system that only one person understands creates an operating risk even when vendor support is available.

Review training materials with a future user. Have that person complete a sample task and record the points that need help. Use those gaps when comparing onboarding scope.

## Which suits management companies versus landlords?

Compare manager and owner needs before you compare feature counts. A landlord tracking personal rentals has a different job from a company holding records for separate owners.

Rentec Direct labels Pro for landlords and investors and PM for property managers. That distinction should guide the demo. Do not assume the owner edition includes the manager controls you need.

A management company should test owner distributions, reserves, fees, access roles, and separate property reporting. Have staff explain each output to a sample owner using the source records.

A self-managing owner may put more weight on simple rent records, expenses, and tenant tasks. Extra workflow options can add training without solving a current problem.

Build a required-task list before watching either demo. Name the task, its reviewer, the report it produces, and the failure you need to detect.

Ask about your next likely change too. Adding an employee, a new owner, or another bank account can expose limits that a simple starting account does not show.

## Which has better portals?

The better portal is the one that lets your tenants and owners complete their required tasks with clear records. Do not rank portal quality from a vendor screenshot alone.

Rentec Direct's pricing page lists tenant and owner tools across its editions. Buildium describes payments, requests, documents, and communication through Resident Center. Verify the package and user type for each feature.

Test as a tenant and as an owner. The tenant should find payment status and submit a request. The owner should find the correct report without seeing another owner's records.

Try an incorrect email invite, a forgotten password, and a user leaving a property. Check who can fix access and whether the change takes effect as expected.

Use the same acceptance list for both vendors:

- **Pay:** Review fees, submit a sample payment, and locate its status.
- **Request:** Add an approved attachment and track a maintenance update.
- **Read:** Find a lease or report on a phone.
- **Correct:** Resolve a wrong record link without creating duplicates.
- **Restrict:** Confirm each user sees only permitted records.
- **Export:** Download records needed for service changes or review.

Choose after your accounting and portal tests pass. For another suite comparison, read [AppFolio vs Buildium](/guides/appfolio-vs-buildium). Keep the final quote and unresolved tasks beside the test results.
