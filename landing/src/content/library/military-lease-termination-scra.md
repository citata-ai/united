## Who qualifies for SCRA lease termination?

Qualification depends on the covered lease and the military event, not simply the tenant's occupation. Read the request as a protected termination review before quoting an ordinary lease break fee.

Under 50 USC 3955, covered events include entering military service after signing, a permanent change of station and qualifying deployment. The deployment route uses a period of at least 90 days. The section also covers specific stop movement, death and catastrophic injury circumstances. [Read the federal statute](https://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/50/3955).

Do not try to compress every route into the same form. A request tied to deployment needs different facts from a request after death. Escalate an unfamiliar case rather than rejecting it because the standard checklist does not fit.

Ask only for the information needed to verify the route. Keep sensitive orders in a restricted file. Leasing staff need the approved outcome and account dates, not broad access to the service record.

## What notice must the tenant give?

The usual process calls for written termination notice and a copy of qualifying military orders. The Justice Department explains that a commanding officer's letter can also establish the required orders information. [Read the DOJ guidance](https://www.justice.gov/servicemembers/financial-and-housing-rights-0).

Acknowledge receipt promptly and preserve the original delivery. The statute allows several delivery methods, including specified electronic means. Do not tell tenants that an in-person office visit is the only way to submit the request.

Check the named tenant, property, notice date and claimed qualifying event. If something is missing, explain what you need in plain words. Avoid asking the tenant to restart the whole request when a targeted clarification will do.

Use a review record like this:

| Review item | Record to retain | Staff question |
|---|---|---|
| Covered lease | Lease and named parties | Who holds the lease? |
| Qualifying event | Orders or accepted verification | Which termination route applies? |
| Delivery | Original notice and receipt record | When did delivery occur? |
| Effective date | Written date calculation | Which rule sets the end date? |
| Account closure | Rent adjustment and refund record | What remains lawfully due? |

The table is a recommended workflow. The statute supplies the legal conditions.

## When does the lease end after notice?

For the usual covered lease with monthly rent, termination takes effect 30 days after the next rent payment is due following proper notice. That rule comes from 50 USC 3955(d), as the DOJ guide explains. It is not simply a month after the tenant sends an email.

Confirm which statutory route applies before calculating. The stop movement provision has a separate effective-date rule. Other payment schedules also need their own review.

Put the calculation in writing. State the date you received the complete materials, the next rent due date and the resulting effective date. Have another staff member review the account setup.

Avoid using a departure date as a substitute for the legal end date. The tenant may need to move before termination takes effect. Ask counsel how to handle a disputed calculation or an additional state protection.

Do not promise that your portal will calculate the result correctly. Review its late fee, notice and concession settings. An approved request can still produce a wrong demand if automation keeps running.

## Can I charge a fee or reclaim concessions?

Do not charge an early termination fee for a qualifying SCRA termination. The Justice Department also treats required repayment of rent discounts or concessions as an unlawful termination charge in this context.

The rule does not mean every account entry disappears. The federal statute distinguishes rent before termination and other lawful obligations from prohibited exit charges. Review damage and other charges on their actual basis, with supporting records.

Do not rename an exit charge as processing, reletting or lost rent to get the same result. Give the tenant an itemized explanation that separates the effective date from unrelated account issues.

Use the North Carolina State Bar's property manager guide as a practical review aid. It discusses the federal rule, covered requests and documentation. It does not replace advice on your property's state law. [Read the State Bar guide](https://www.ncbar.gov/for-lawyers/bar-programs/legal-assistance-for-military-personnel-lamp/legal-research-other-resources/property-managers-guide-to-the-scra/).

Check any collection referral already in progress. Send the corrected balance to the vendor and retain the update. A corrected portal account alone may not stop an outside demand.

## How do I close the military tenant's account?

Close the account with a written explanation, a reviewed rent adjustment and the required refund process. Under 50 USC 3955(f), prepaid rent for after termination must be refunded within 30 days of the effective date.

Keep the security deposit review separate. Do not use the deposit as a way to collect barred future rent. Document inspection findings and refer uncertain deductions for review.

Give staff a named contact for questions. Avoid making the tenant repeat a sensitive account of deployment or family loss to each department. Share the decision internally through an access-controlled record.

Use this recommended closeout checklist:

- **Confirm eligibility.** Record the provision and materials supporting the decision.
- **Set the date.** Review the calculation before changing the ledger.
- **Remove exit charges.** Check both lease fees and concession reversals.
- **Review rent.** Separate periods before and after termination.
- **Process refunds.** Keep proof of payment and the recipient details.
- **Update vendors.** Correct any account already sent outside the office.
- **Preserve the file.** Keep orders restricted and the decision easy to audit.

Review your lease process before another request arrives. Add a clear way for tenants to reach a person when an automated notice is wrong. The [guide library](/guides) offers related rental operations material.
