## Which software should a new company compare?

Compare current affordability and owner accounting before buying for expected growth. A new firm needs a working process, not the longest feature list.

| Product | Published entry price | Minimum condition | Startup test |
|---|---|---|---|
| Yardi Breeze residential | $1 per unit monthly | $100 monthly; annual agreement | Owner reports and accounting |
| Buildium Essential | From $62 monthly | Unit minimum not stated | Core work and total usage fees |
| Rentvine | As low as $1.50 per active unit | Exact minimum quote only | Trust accounting and connections |
| DoorLoop One | $2.50 per unit monthly displayed | $199 monthly minimum displayed | Core platform and staff tasks |
| AppFolio Core | From $1.49 per unit monthly | 50 units plus minimum spend | Eligible portfolio and total quote |

Sources: [Yardi](https://www.yardibreeze.com/pricing/), [Buildium](https://www.buildium.com/pricing/), [Rentvine](https://www.rentvine.com/pricing), [DoorLoop](https://www.doorloop.com/pricing) and [AppFolio](https://www.appfolio.com/pricing), read 2026-10-19. Confirm DoorLoop's displayed billing basis.

Treat forecast doors as a planning assumption. Your first subscription should remain affordable if growth takes longer than expected.

## Which tools have no unit minimum?

Yardi Breeze explicitly says no minimum unit count applies. Its monthly spending floor still applies, so eligibility does not make the software free.

Buildium's pricing page does not state a unit minimum. Ask sales to confirm that the offered plan accepts your current portfolio and business type.

Rentvine's minimum-unit FAQ does not publish a threshold. Its as-low-as rate also leaves your actual offer unresolved. Treat both questions as quote only.

AppFolio Core states a 50-unit minimum. Do not treat leads or hoped-for management agreements as signed doors. Ask about eligibility when your portfolio reaches the stated threshold.

For every offer, confirm how vacant and inactive records count. Also ask how the bill changes if your first owner leaves.

Keep a cash plan based on the doors you already manage. A product can fit a large company well and still be an expensive start for yours.

## Which tools can grow past the startup stage?

A tool grows with your firm when it handles more owners, roles and work without losing controls. A high maximum door count alone does not prove that.

Test the next operating model, not just more records. Add a staff member who prepares bills but cannot release payments. Add an owner with several properties. Check whether reports remain clear.

Yardi's FAQ describes an upgrade path to Premier or Voyager. Buildium offers broader tiers, including Premium's Open API. Rentvine and DoorLoop describe connections and broader operating tools.

An API is a software connection. Ask what your future partner tool needs to read or change. Confirm which plan permits that work.

Do not buy every future feature now. Record the upgrade trigger and price. A good startup plan supports today's work and has a clear, affordable next step.

Keep exports in that plan. Growth can include an acquisition, a new portfolio type or a later system change.

## What is the cheapest complete tech stack?

The cheapest useful stack covers your required work without duplicate services or missing controls. Price categories before selecting brands.

Start with this worksheet:

| Category | Initial requirement |
|---|---|
| Property system | Owners, tenants, properties and books |
| Payments | Approved collection and payout process |
| Communication | Shared business email and known contact |
| Documents | Signed agreements and controlled storage |
| Work tracking | Assigned tasks and open maintenance |
| Sales tracking | Owner lead status and next action |

Check what the property system already provides. Avoid buying a separate task or messaging tool until you test included work.

Add outside services only for a documented gap. Record the task owner, expected benefit and total bill. A subscription without a responsible user often becomes neglected work.

Use current quotes for all paid products. Do not call a stack cheap by omitting setup, screening or payment charges.

Your staff time also matters. A lower software bill can leave more manual work, but any time-savings estimate should come from your own trial.

## Do you need trust accounting from the start?

If your firm will hold or track money for others, evaluate trust accounting before handling that money. Do not defer the controls until the portfolio grows.

Rentvine's [accounting page](https://www.rentvine.com/features/trust-accounting) describes client-fund tracking, native three-way reconciliation and account diagnostics. Use that description to form demo questions for every candidate.

Ask a qualified local adviser to define the required account structure and records. This guide does not set a universal legal rule. This is general information, not legal advice. Check the current law or ask a local attorney.

Have the accountant test a payment, owner reserve, vendor bill and distribution. Ask how the system prevents or flags a negative owner position. Verify who can change bank details.

Keep the operating business's money distinct in your process. Software labels alone do not prove that staff have set up and used accounts correctly.

Approve the accounting design before taking live payments.

## Which tool makes a later move easier?

The tool with usable exports, clear records and tested conversion support makes a later move easier. An open API is useful, but does not replace a complete archive.

Ask for sample owner, tenant, property and financial exports. Open the files outside the product. Check whether attachments remain readable without a login.

Buildium's [onboarding page](https://www.buildium.com/features/onboarding/) names incoming property, lease and people data. That does not prove every system can export the same detail. Test the direction you need.

Use an exit checklist:

- **Records:** confirm types, fields and date ranges.
- **Documents:** download readable signed files.
- **Money:** retain balances and supporting transactions.
- **Access:** record cancellation and archive terms.
- **Move:** agree how a destination will check the data.

Read the [AppFolio versus Buildium guide](/guides/appfolio-vs-buildium) for more buying questions. Use the [fee calculator](/tools/property-management-fee-calculator) to check your current margin.

Select after your accountant and daily users approve a sample workflow. Keep the launch stack small enough that every part has a clear owner.
