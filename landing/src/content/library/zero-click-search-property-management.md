## How many searches end without a click?

More than half of US Google searches end without a click to the open web. The Datos and SparkToro study found that 58.5% of US Google searches in 2024 were zero-click. Only 360 of every 1,000 US searches sent a click to a site that Google does not own.

A zero-click search is a search where the user stops on the results page. The user reads the answer, clicks a Google property such as Maps, or searches again.

| Measure | Number | Source |
|---|---|---|
| US Google searches with zero clicks | 58.5% | Datos and SparkToro, 2024 |
| Clicks to the open web per 1,000 US searches | 360 | Datos and SparkToro, 2024 |
| Searches in the Pew sample that showed an AI summary | 18% | Pew Research, March 2025 |
| Pages with an AI summary where the user ended the session | 26% | Pew Research, 2025 |
| Pages with no AI summary where the user ended the session | 16% | Pew Research, 2025 |

No public study splits out property management searches. The numbers above are for all searches. We use them as the best general signal, not as an exact count for your market.

## How much do AI Overviews cut clicks?

AI Overviews cut clicks to the top result by about 58%. Ahrefs compared 150,000 keywords with an AI Overview and 150,000 keywords without one. In December 2025, the top result on AI Overview keywords got a 1.6% click rate. Ahrefs expected 3.7% without the AI Overview.

Pew Research tracked the real browsing of 900 US adults in March 2025. Users clicked a normal result on 8% of visits when an AI summary showed. They clicked on 15% of visits when no summary showed. They clicked a link inside the summary on only 1% of visits.

The loss is smaller at lower positions. Here is the Ahrefs click loss by position when an AI Overview shows:

| Position | Click rate change with an AI Overview |
|---|---|
| 1 | -58.0% |
| 2 | -50.8% |
| 3 | -46.4% |
| 5 | -32.6% |
| 10 | -19.4% |

So the page that ranks first loses the most. A first-page ranking still matters, but the click is no longer the main prize.

## Which property management searches lose the most clicks?

Long question searches lose the most clicks, because they trigger AI answers most often. Pew found that searches with 10 or more words showed an AI summary 53% of the time. One- or two-word searches showed one only 8% of the time. Question searches showed a summary 60% of the time.

Owners type both kinds. Here is how common owner searches map to that data. The risk column is our reading of the Pew data, not a measured number.

| Owner search | Words | Type | Zero-click risk |
|---|---|---|---|
| property management Denver | 3 | Local, short | Lower |
| how much does a property manager charge in Denver | 9 | Question | High |
| can a landlord in Colorado keep a security deposit for cleaning | 11 | Question, law | High |
| is it worth hiring a property manager for one house | 9 | Question | High |
| best property management company near me | 6 | Local, ranked | Medium |

The Semrush data shows the mix is changing. In January 2025, 91.3% of AI Overview keywords were informational. By October 2025, the share fell to 57.1%, and commercial keywords rose from 8.15% to 18.57%. AI Overviews now reach more buying searches than before.

Search demand also fell. In our own sample, 7 generic property management terms fell 43% in the US year over year (Keyword Planner, our data, 2026-10). The cause is a hypothesis. Our guide on [why owner leads are dropping](/guides/why-owner-leads-are-dropping) has the full numbers.

## What still gets clicks?

Short local searches, lower-position results and your Google Business Profile still get clicks. Pew found that short searches rarely show an AI summary. Ahrefs found that position 10 loses only 19.4% of clicks, not 58%.

Your Business Profile is a click path that Google keeps on its own page. Google Business Profile performance counts:

- **Calls:** taps on the call button on your profile.
- **Website clicks:** taps on the website link on your profile.
- **Directions:** requests for directions to your office.
- **Messages and bookings:** if you turned them on.
- **Searches:** the terms people used to find your profile.

These actions count as zero-click in a study like SparkToro's, because the user did not visit a website. For you, a call from Maps is a lead. Count it.

Google also says that clicks from pages with AI Overviews are "higher quality", because users spend more time on the site. Google gives no data for this claim, so treat it as Google's view.

## How do you measure zero-click loss on your own site?

You measure the loss with impressions and clicks per query in Google Search Console. Google counts AI Overview and AI Mode traffic inside the normal Web search report. There is no separate AI filter.

Do this once a month:

1. **Export the last 3 months** of queries from Search Console, Performance, Web.
2. **Compare the same 3 months** one year earlier.
3. **Mark queries with more impressions and fewer clicks.** Those are your likely zero-click queries.
4. **Check the live result** for each one. Note if an AI Overview shows and if it names you.
5. **Add Business Profile calls and website clicks** for the same months.

## How do you win a zero-click search?

You win a zero-click search when the answer names your company. The owner then calls you, opens your profile, or searches your name. The click moves from a generic search to a branded one.

Checklist:

- **Put one direct answer per question** at the top of a page or section. Write your real fee, for example "8% of collected rent", not "competitive pricing".
- **Fill your Google Business Profile.** Add your categories, service area, hours, phone and a short description with your door count and cities.
- **Fix your listings.** In our test of 150 AI answers for "best property management company" in 50 US cities, Expertise.com was a source in 93 answers, and Yelp and iPropertyManagement in 50 each (our data, 2026-10-19).
- **Ask for honest reviews** from owners and tenants after real events, such as a lease signed or a repair done.
- **Track branded searches** in Search Console. More searches for your name can be a sign that AI answers name you.

Watch for: an agency that promises to bring back the old click volume. The searches that end on Google do not come back. The goal is to be the name in the answer.
