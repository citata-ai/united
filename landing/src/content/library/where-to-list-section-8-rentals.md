## Which channels reach voucher holders?

Three kinds of channel reach voucher holders: the housing authority's own list, voucher-focused listing sites, and general rental sites. The PHA itself points families to landlords. Under 24 CFR 982.301(b)(11), the PHA's briefing packet must include "a list of landlords known to the PHA who may be willing to lease a unit to the family", or other resources such as online search tools.

Here is how the channels compare, from pages we read on 2026-10-19.

| Channel | Cost to list | What we verified | Limits |
|---|---|---|---|
| PHA vacancy list (San Mateo County) | Free | Available-unit form; posting stays 30 days | PHA "does not match or screen voucher holders" |
| PHA Housing Search List (Sacramento SHRA) | Free | For managers with multiple units; given to voucher holders | Individual vacancies go to AffordableHousing.com |
| State search site (GeorgiaHousingSearch.org) | Free per Macon Housing Authority | Listed by Macon HA with a toll-free number | Georgia only |
| AffordableHousing.com Free | $0 | "Free unlimited listings (with ads)", applications, messaging | Ads on listing, basic renter data |
| AffordableHousing.com Premium | Price not shown publicly | Boosts, renter scores, Listing Multiplier cross-posting | Paid |
| AffordableHousing.com Enterprise | Quote | API bulk upload, account manager | For multi-unit communities |
| General rental sites | Varies | Reach renters who do not search voucher sites | We found no documented voucher filter |

## Is the PHA landlord list free?

Yes, at the housing authorities we checked, the landlord list is free. San Mateo County's Housing Authority takes an available-unit form. It removes the posting after 30 days, and it says it "does not match or screen voucher holders for landlords". It also tells owners to post elsewhere, since "there is no guarantee a voucher holder will be interested".

Sacramento's SHRA runs a Housing Search List for property management companies with multiple units. It says to "market your rentals for free to voucher holders". For single vacancies, SHRA sends owners to AffordableHousing.com.

What to do: search your PHA's site for "list a unit" or "landlord list". Ask how long a posting stays up, and put a reminder to repost.

## What does GoSection8 cost?

GoSection8 is now AffordableHousing.com, and its basic listing is free. On 2026-10-19 we requested gosection8.com and got a 301 redirect to affordablehousing.com. A Kingsport Housing flyer for GoSection8 and the AffordableHousing.com pages both use the same support line, (866) 466-7328.

The AffordableHousing.com pricing page shows 3 plans:

1. **Free.** $0. "Free unlimited listings (with ads)", rental applications with basic renter data, and secure messaging.
2. **Premium.** The page says "Starting at ... per month", but the price did not load without an account. It adds boosts, renter scores, text messaging, QuikMatch prospects and lease templates.
3. **Enterprise.** By quote, for owners with multi-unit communities. It adds the largest ad size, an API for bulk listing uploads and a dedicated account manager.

The Kingsport flyer also says GoSection8 syndicates listings to local participating housing authorities, so vacancies can appear on PHA "printed lists".

## How does AffordableHousing.com compare?

AffordableHousing.com is the voucher-focused site that many PHAs name. Of the PHA pages we read, San Mateo County, SHRA and Macon all point owners to it. Its owners page says it is "trusted by 700+ government agency partners". That is the company's own claim.

The free plan is enough to test the channel. The Premium "Listing Multiplier" says it cross-posts listings to Trulia, Zillow, HotPads and other partner sites. If you already post on those sites yourself, that feature adds less.

Watch for: the free plan shows ads on your listing. Premium listings appear above free ones in search, per the site. If your area has many voucher listings, a free listing may sit low.

## Do Zillow and Apartments.com filter for vouchers?

We could not confirm a voucher filter on Zillow or Apartments.com. We searched for an official help page on 2026-10-19 and found none. Third-party blogs disagree on this point, so we do not rely on them.

So write for keyword search. Put "Housing Choice Vouchers accepted" in the description, not only in a checkbox. Renters who type "Section 8" or "voucher" into a search box can then find the unit. For safe wording, see the fair housing rules in 24 CFR 100.75 and keep every line about the unit, not the renter.

## Which channel leases fastest?

We found no public study that compares lease-up speed by channel for voucher units. So we will not guess. The answer depends on your city, your rent and the local payment standard.

You can measure it yourself in 4 steps:

1. **Tag each lead** with its source when it arrives: PHA list, AffordableHousing.com, general site, sign, referral.
2. **Track 3 numbers per channel:** leads, showings and signed leases.
3. **Track days on market** from listing to signed RFTA (Request for Tenancy Approval).
4. **Review after 5 to 10 vacancies.** Drop channels that bring leads but no leases.
