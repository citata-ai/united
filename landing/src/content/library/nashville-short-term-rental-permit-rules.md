## Who needs a Nashville STRP permit?

Do you rent a Nashville or Davidson County property through a short-term rental website? Then you need a Short Term Rental Property (STRP) permit from Metro Codes before listing. The rule is in Metro Code section 6.28.030.

Two time limits define an STRP stay, per Metro Codes:

- **At least 24 hours.** The owner may not take pay for occupancy of less than 24 hours.
- **At most 30 consecutive days** for any guest.

Nashville has two permit types:

| Item | Owner-occupied (OO) | Not owner-occupied (NOO) |
|---|---|---|
| Who | A natural person who lives on the property permanently | Any owner, including entities |
| Entities | LLCs, corporations, trusts and partnerships are not eligible | Allowed; applicant must be linked to the entity |
| What you can rent | Up to 4 sleeping rooms to one party, or at least 1 sleeping room and a bathroom | Up to 4 sleeping rooms, one party |
| Proof | 4 documents of owner occupation (2 from Group A, 2 from Group B), matching the deed | Ownership on the application must match the deed |
| Limit | One permit per lot in single-family and two-family zones | Zoning limits below |

Source: Metro Codes, "Short Term Rental Property Permit Types." The new online application process started on March 11, 2026.

## Which zones allow non-owner-occupied short-term rentals?

New non-owner-occupied (NOO) permits are allowed only in mixed-use, office, commercial and downtown zones. Metro Codes issues them as a use permitted with conditions (PC) in these districts:

| Zone family | Districts where new NOO permits can be issued |
|---|---|
| Mixed-use | MUN, MUN-A, MUL, MUL-A, MUG, MUG-A, MUI, MUI-A |
| Office | OG, OR20 through OR40-A, ORI, ORI-A |
| Commercial | CN, CN-A, CL, CL-A, CS, CS-A, CA, CF |
| Downtown | DTC North, DTC South, DTC-West, DTC Central |
| Shopping center | SCN, SCC, SCR |
| Not allowed for new NOO permits | AR2A, R, RS, RM |

In Specific Plan (SP) or Planned Unit Development (PUD) zones, a permit is issued only if the SP or PUD allows it. The Metro Planning Commission answers those questions.

Existing NOO permit holders in AR2A, R, RS or RM zones may be able to renew. But those permits do not transfer when the property is sold.

What this means for you: if you plan to buy a house in an RS zone to run as a non-owner-occupied short-term rental, a new permit is not available. Check the zone with the Metro Codes property search before you buy.

## What does Nashville require for the permit each year?

Nashville asks for a set of documents to get the permit and an annual renewal to keep it. The application page lists these steps.

1. **Send sensitive documents by email.** Proof of residency for OO permits, or proof that links the applicant to the owning entity for NOO permits.
2. **Prepare forms.** A notarized "True and Accurate" affidavit (OO or NOO) and, if someone else applies for you, an Owner Authorization Form.
3. **Show taxes paid.** Proof that all taxes are paid, including property taxes. For renewals, also room, occupancy and sales taxes.
4. **Prove insurance.** Homeowner's fire, hazard and liability insurance, with liability of at least $1 million per occurrence. A listing company's coverage may count.
5. **Notify neighbors.** Written notice to the owner of each adjacent property: front, back, sides, and above and below if they apply.
6. **Get a safety certification.** For single and two-family homes, an inspection by a licensed architect, engineer or home inspector. Multifamily buildings with a common hallway need a Fire Marshal inspection.
7. **Apply online** in ePermits and upload the documents as PDFs.

Renewal, per Metro Codes:

- **Term:** a permit expires 365 days after it is issued.
- **Reminders:** emails go out 60 and 45 days before expiry, with a link to the renewal affidavit.
- **Owner's duty:** Metro is not responsible for your renewal. The permit is not renewed until you pay.

Operating rules also apply all year. The principal renter must be at least 21. Occupancy may not exceed twice the number of permitted sleeping rooms plus four, with a maximum of 12. You may not rent to more than one party at a time. Post the name and phone number of a local responsible party who answers 24 hours a day. Metro can revoke a permit after three violations of any ordinance or law of general application.

This is general information, not legal advice. Check the current law or ask a local attorney.

## Is a mid-term rental a better choice for a Nashville owner?

A mid-term rental can be a better choice for an owner who cannot get an NOO permit. The STRP rules set a 30-day maximum stay for any guest. A furnished rental with stays longer than that is not an STRP stay under the Metro Codes definition. Confirm with Metro Codes before you change your model.

Compare the two models with a simple check:

| Factor | Short-term (STRP) | Mid-term (more than 30 days) |
|---|---|---|
| Permit | STRP permit, renewed every year | No STRP permit for stays over 30 days, per the 30-day limit |
| Zoning (NOO) | New permits barred in AR2A, R, RS, RM | Not limited by STRP zoning |
| Taxes | Permit holder collects and remits room, occupancy and sales taxes | Ask a tax professional |
| Turnover | High | Lower |
| Ownership change | Cancels the permit | No STRP permit to lose |
