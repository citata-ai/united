## Which owner prompts matter most?

The prompts that matter most are the ones an owner asks right before they pick a manager. "Best property manager in <city>" is the core one. Fee questions and property-type questions come next, because they decide who makes the short list.

A prompt is the question a person types into an AI assistant. Here is a 30-prompt list in 5 groups. Replace `<city>` with your city and `<company>` with your name. Copy it as is, then cut the lines that do not fit your business.

```text
[Group 1: who to hire (10)]
What is the best property management company in <city>? Give names.
Best property manager for a single-family home in <city>
Best property manager for a duplex or small multifamily in <city>
Property management companies near <neighborhood>, <city>
Top-rated property management company in <city> with good reviews
Who manages rental homes in <city> for out-of-state owners?
Small local property management company in <city>, not a franchise
Property manager in <city> that accepts Section 8 tenants
Property management for a condo in <city>
I inherited a house in <city>. Should I rent it out and who can manage it?

[Group 2: fees (6)]
How much do property managers charge in <city>?
Property management company in <city> with no leasing fee
Flat-fee property management in <city>
What fees should I expect from a property manager in <city>?
Is 10% a fair property management fee in <city>?
Property manager in <city> with no long-term contract

[Group 3: owner problems (6)]
My tenant stopped paying rent in <city>. Who can help me manage the property?
How do I find a good property manager in <city>?
What questions should I ask a property manager in <city> before I hire them?
Property manager in <city> that handles rental registration and inspections
How long does it take to rent a house in <city> with a property manager?
Should I manage my rental in <city> myself or hire a property manager?

[Group 4: comparisons (4)]
<company> vs <competitor 1> property management in <city>
<competitor 1> alternatives in <city>
Is a national property management company or a local one better in <city>?
<competitor 2> reviews: is there a better property manager in <city>?

[Group 5: brand (4)]
What is <company>?
<company> reviews and fees
Is <company> a good property manager in <city>?
Where is <company> located and what areas does it serve?
```

Group 1 is the base. In our 50-city test on 2026-10-19, we used the first prompt in that group for the 50 largest US cities (our data, [AI Property Manager Index 2026](/research/ai-property-manager-index-2026)). It gave 150 answers that named 821 different companies.

Fair Housing: track prompts about property types, fees and services. Do not track prompts that sort renters by race, religion, family status, disability or any other protected class.

## How many prompts are enough?

20 to 30 prompts are enough for a company that serves 1 to 3 cities. Fewer than 10 gives you noise. More than 50 costs time and money and rarely changes the decision.

Count the work before you pick a number:

| Prompts | Engines | Runs per month | Answers to log per month |
|---|---|---|---|
| 10 | 3 | 2 | 60 |
| 20 | 3 | 2 | 120 |
| 30 | 3 | 2 | 180 |
| 50 | 3 | 2 | 300 |

By hand, each answer means one run and one log row. Time yourself on the first 10, then plan the month.

## How do you add your city?

Put the city and state in the prompt text, every time. The OpenAI Help Center says ChatGPT "may use an approximate location based on your IP address". It also says ChatGPT may rewrite "restaurants near me" into a search for "top restaurants San Francisco". So "near me" gives one answer from your office and another from your tool's server.

Rules for the city slot:

1. **Use "City, ST".** "Mesa, AZ", not "Mesa". Many US towns share a name.
2. **Use the city owners use.** If owners say "Chicago" for a suburb, track both.
3. **Track each market you serve.** 3 cities at 10 prompts each is better than 1 city at 30.
4. **Add 1 or 2 neighborhood prompts.** Some owners search by area, such as "South Side Chicago".
5. **Never change the words later.** Add a new prompt instead. Old and new results must stay comparable.

## Should you track brand prompts?

Yes. Track 2 to 4 brand prompts, because they show if AI has your facts right. A wrong address, wrong fees or a mix-up with another company hurts you even when you are named.

We found this on our own name. On 2026-10-19, we ran 4 brand answers per engine about citata.ai (our data). Claude described a different company, Citate.ai or Citant.ai, in 4 of 4 answers. ChatGPT got us right in 2 of 4. We now track brand prompts every week.

Keep brand prompts apart in your report. A brand prompt names you by design, so it inflates your mention rate. Report two numbers: non-brand mentions and brand accuracy.

## How do you log the results?

Log the same 6 fields for every answer, in a sheet or a TOML file:

| Field | Example |
|---|---|
| Date | 2026-10-19 |
| Engine and model | ChatGPT, logged out |
| Prompt id | fees-no-leasing |
| Named you? | yes / no |
| Your rank in the list | 3 |
| Sources linked | expertise.com; yelp.com; your site |

Then add a short note when an answer has a wrong fact. The sources column tells you where to fix it.
