Iowa puts all of its deposit rules in one section: Iowa Code § 562A.12. I read the Iowa Code 2026 version on the Iowa Legislature site on 2026-10-19. The text is short, and every limit has a number.

| Rule | Limit or deadline | Statute |
|---|---|---|
| Maximum deposit | 2 months' rent | § 562A.12(1) |
| Where to hold it | Federally insured bank, S&L or credit union | § 562A.12(2) |
| Commingling | Not with the landlord's personal funds | § 562A.12(2) |
| Interest | Landlord keeps interest for the first 5 years | § 562A.12(2) |
| Return deadline | 30 days after tenancy ends and address received | § 562A.12(3) |
| Written statement | Specific reasons; nature of damages | § 562A.12(3) |
| Missed deadline | Landlord forfeits right to withhold | § 562A.12(4) |
| No address for 1 year | Deposit reverts to landlord | § 562A.12(4) |
| Bad-faith retention | Up to 2x monthly rent punitive, plus actual damages | § 562A.12(7) |
| Burden of proof | On the landlord | § 562A.12(3)(b) |

## What is the maximum security deposit a landlord can charge in Iowa?

The maximum Iowa security deposit is 2 months' rent. Section 562A.12(1) says "a landlord shall not demand or receive as a security deposit an amount or value in excess of two months' rent."

| Monthly rent | Maximum deposit |
|---|---|
| $800 | $1,600 |
| $1,100 | $2,200 |
| $1,500 | $3,000 |
| $2,200 | $4,400 |

The Code defines a rental deposit as money that secures performance of the rental agreement (§ 562A.6). Count any refundable "fee" that does this job toward the 2-month cap.

## How many days does an Iowa landlord have to return a security deposit?

An Iowa landlord has 30 days. The clock starts on the later of 2 events: the end of the tenancy, and receipt of the tenant's mailing address or delivery instructions (§ 562A.12(3)(a)).

Within those 30 days, the landlord does one of these:

1. Returns the full deposit.
2. Sends a written statement of the specific reason for keeping all or part of it, with any balance.

If the tenant gives no address or instructions within 1 year after the tenancy ends, the deposit reverts to the landlord. The tenant then forfeits all rights to it (§ 562A.12(4)).

Worked timeline:

| Event | Date |
|---|---|
| Lease ends, tenant moves out | June 30 |
| Tenant emails forwarding address | July 5 |
| Last day to return deposit or send statement | August 4 |

## What can a landlord deduct from a security deposit in Iowa?

An Iowa landlord may withhold only amounts "reasonably necessary" for 3 reasons (§ 562A.12(3)(a)):

1. **Unpaid rent or other funds** due under the rental agreement.
2. **Restoring the unit** to its condition at the start of the tenancy, ordinary wear and tear excepted.
3. **Costs to get possession** from a tenant who does not act in good faith in failing to leave after notice of noncompliance.

Ordinary wear and tear is never a lawful deduction. Faded paint and light carpet wear from normal living stay with the landlord. A hole in a wall or a broken window can be a deduction.

## Does an Iowa landlord have to give an itemized list of deductions?

Yes. The statement must show "the specific reason for withholding" any part of the deposit (§ 562A.12(3)(a)). If the landlord keeps money to restore the unit, the statement must "specify the nature of the damages."

A good statement lists:

- each item, such as "replace broken bedroom blind";
- the cost of each item;
- unpaid rent or charges, by month;
- the total withheld and the balance paid.

In a court case about the deposit, the landlord must prove the reason for each deduction by a preponderance of the evidence (§ 562A.12(3)(b)). Move-in and move-out photos help you carry that burden.

## Must an Iowa landlord pay interest or keep the deposit in a separate account?

Iowa requires a separate, insured account but no interest to the tenant for the first 5 years.

**Account.** All rental deposits must be held "in a bank or savings and loan association or credit union which is insured by an agency of the federal government" (§ 562A.12(2)). They may not be commingled with the landlord's personal funds. The landlord may use one common trust account for all deposits, and that account may earn interest.

**Interest.** "Any interest earned on a rental deposit during the first five years of a tenancy shall be the property of the landlord" (§ 562A.12(2)). The statute does not say who owns interest after year 5. Ask an attorney if you hold a deposit that long.

**Sale of the building.** When you sell, transfer the deposits to the buyer and tell each tenant the buyer's name and address, or return the deposits (§ 562A.12(5)). The buyer then takes on the duty to return them (§ 562A.12(6)).

## What is the penalty for an Iowa landlord who keeps a deposit wrongly?

Iowa has 2 levels of penalty.

**Missed statement.** A landlord who does not send the written statement within 30 days "shall forfeit all rights to withhold any portion of the rental deposit" (§ 562A.12(4)). The full deposit is then due, even if the tenant caused damage.

**Bad faith.** "The bad-faith retention of a deposit" subjects the landlord to punitive damages "not to exceed twice the monthly rental payment in addition to actual damages" (§ 562A.12(7)).

Worked example: rent $1,000, deposit $1,000, landlord keeps all of it in bad faith.

| Item | Amount |
|---|---|
| Deposit owed (actual damages) | $1,000 |
| Punitive damages, maximum | $2,000 |
| Total exposure before fees | $3,000 |

The court may also award reasonable attorney fees to the prevailing party (§ 562A.12(8)).

## Iowa deposit checklist

- Cap the deposit at 2 months' rent.
- Hold it in a federally insured account, apart from your own money.
- Take dated photos at move-in and move-out.
- Ask for a forwarding address in writing.
- Send the refund or a specific, written statement within 30 days.
- Never deduct for ordinary wear and tear.
