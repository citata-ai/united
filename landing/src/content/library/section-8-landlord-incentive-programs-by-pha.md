## Which PHAs pay landlord incentives, and how much?

Landlord incentives are set by each PHA, city or state, so you must check locally. The federal voucher rules in 24 CFR part 982 do not create a signing bonus or a vacancy payment. Programs come from local funds, state laws or special grants.

We read the program pages below on 2026-10-19. Where a page is old, we give its date.

| Program | Signing bonus | Vacancy or holding pay | Damage help | Other |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Atlanta Housing (HCV) | Leasing Incentive Fee for some hardship cases; amount not shown | None shown | Up to $2,000 per claim, after a $250 deductible | $250 first-time pass bonus (single-family); $100 per referred new owner |
| Philadelphia Housing Authority (post of 2022-03-16) | $300 per voucher holder; $500 for Emergency Housing Vouchers; $1,000 in opportunity areas | None shown | Up to $2,500, after the security deposit | Deposit help up to 1 month of contract rent (EHV) |
| Hawaii Public Housing Authority (Act 287, summary of 2022-08-02) | Up to 1 month of rent | Up to 1 month of rent, at first lease-up or between tenants | Damage above the security deposit; amount not shown | Inspection within 15 days of a lease-up request |
| Housing Authority of the City of Los Angeles (release of 2023-12-04) | None shown | Holding fee up to 1 month of rent per vacant unit held | Damage mitigation, based on bedrooms and FMR | Move-in help, based on bedrooms and FMR |
| Washington State Commerce (statewide, any subsidy) | None | Up to 14 days of rent loss, with up to $1,000 for required move-in upgrades | Up to $5,000 for tenant-caused damage | Not for properties owned by housing authorities |
| City of Boston, Mayor's Office of Housing | Signing bonuses; amount not shown | None shown | Cleaning fees or repair costs | For households leaving homelessness |

Two cautions. First, a program that does not show an amount may still pay; ask. Second, the Philadelphia post said its $300 bonus ran "through July 31st," and the post is from 2022. Confirm that any program is still open before you rely on it.

## Which PHAs pay a signing bonus?

On the pages we read, Hawaii's HPHA and Philadelphia's PHA show a signing bonus amount. The Hawaii summary of Act 287 (2022) lists "a signing bonus of up to one month of rent." The Philadelphia post (2022) listed $300 per voucher holder, $500 for Emergency Housing Voucher holders, and $1,000 for a lease in a qualified opportunity area.

Atlanta Housing pays a Leasing Incentive Fee, but only for applicants with a hardship, such as credit issues or a reasonable accommodation. A second "Automatic" fee applies to families moving from another housing authority who submit a Request for Tenancy Approval within 30 days. The page does not show the amount.

## Which PHAs pay vacancy loss between tenants?

Hawaii's HPHA pays vacancy loss under Act 287: up to one month's rent "when the rental unit sits vacant during initial lease up or between Section 8 tenants." HACLA pays a holding fee instead: up to one month's rent per vacant unit held for a voucher family, per its 2023 release.

Worked example, with a made-up rent:

| Item | Amount |
|---|---|
| Contract rent | $1,800 |
| Days vacant between voucher tenants | 25 |
| Rent lost (25 ÷ 30 × $1,800) | $1,500 |
| Vacancy payment, if capped at 1 month | Up to $1,800 |
| Net loss after payment | $0 |

Check the cap rule. Some programs pay the actual days vacant, not a flat month.

## What damage claim limits apply?

Damage claim limits on the pages we read range from $2,000 to $5,000 per claim:

1. **Atlanta Housing.** Property Protection Program: "After a $250 deductible per claim, AHA pays up to $2,000 per claim" for tenant damage beyond normal wear and tear. Owners are eligible by default.
2. **Philadelphia PHA (2022).** Owner Assurance Fund: up to $2,500 for damage beyond normal wear and tear, after subtracting the security deposit.
3. **Washington State.** Landlord Damage Relief Program: up to $5,000 for qualifying tenant-caused damage, under RCW 43.31.605. Watch for: a post-move-in claim bars you from suing the tenant for more money later.
4. **Hawaii HPHA.** Covers damage costs above the security deposit. The summary does not give a cap.

A worked Atlanta example, with made-up damage:

| Item | Amount |
|---|---|
| Tenant damage beyond wear and tear | $2,900 |
| Deductible | −$250 |
| Remaining | $2,650 |
| Atlanta Housing pays (cap $2,000) | $2,000 |
| Your cost before deposit or tenant claim | $900 |

## How do I apply for a landlord incentive?

You apply through the PHA or agency that runs the program. Each one sets its own form and timing. Steps that apply to most programs:

- [ ] **Find the program page.** Search your PHA's site for "landlord incentive" or "owner incentive."
- [ ] **Read the conditions.** New owners only? Opportunity areas only? Certain voucher types only?
- [ ] **Keep move-in proof.** Washington asks for a signed move-in condition report, the lease, a tenant ledger and repair invoices.
- [ ] **Note the deadline.** Claim windows can be short.
- [ ] **Ask about funds.** Programs often pay "subject to available funding."
- [ ] **Keep copies** of every claim and payment.

## Do incentives count as taxable income?

Incentive payments are likely income for tax purposes. Federal law, 26 U.S.C. 61, defines gross income as "all income from whatever source derived," unless the tax code makes an exception. We found no exception for PHA landlord incentives. Ask a tax professional how to report one.
