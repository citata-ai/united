## What do property management SEO agencies charge each month?

Property management SEO agencies that publish prices charge about $1,000 to $2,500 a month for one market. That range comes from Goodjuju's 2026 cost guide, and the public price pages of two specialist agencies fit inside it or just above it. We read each page on 2026-10-19.

| Agency and plan | Monthly price | Setup | Contract |
|---|---|---|---|
| ClearLead Digital Foundation | $1,199 | No setup fee | Month to month |
| ClearLead Digital Growth Engine | $1,699 | No setup fee | Month to month |
| ClearLead Digital Market Authority | $2,299 | Not stated | No long-term contract |
| ClearLead Market Authority + website | $2,599 in year 1, then $2,299 | Not stated | 12 months for the website add-on |
| ClearLead Market Ownership License | $3,449 to $4,598 | Not stated | One client per metro |
| Goodjuju standard SEO plans | $1,185 to $4,460 | Included | Month to month, 15 days' notice |
| Goodjuju Empire 2.0 (multi-location) | $7,930 | Not stated | Not stated |
| Property Manager Websites Grow / Premier / Elite | $145 / $795 / $1,995 | Not stated | Not stated |

Sources: ClearLead SEO pricing page, Goodjuju cost guide (2026-06-15), PMW pricing page. PMW plans bundle the website, tools and some SEO work. The PMW page does not show which features come with which plan, so compare it with care.

## What is included at each price level?

Each price level buys a different amount of monthly work. Goodjuju's guide splits the market into 3 levels.

| Level | Price per month | What you usually get |
|---|---|---|
| Foundational | $300 to $800 | Google Business Profile setup, basic citations (directory listings), light reporting. Often a freelancer or web shop. |
| Specialist | $1,000 to $2,500 | Full GBP work, citations, owner keywords, on-page SEO, city pages, a review system, some blog posts and local links, monthly lead reports |
| Competitive or multi-market | $2,500 to $5,000+ | All of the above at more volume, plus digital PR, competitor tracking and AI search work |

Source: Goodjuju, "What Property Management SEO Actually Costs".

ClearLead's page shows the same steps in units you can count. Foundation tracks 25 keywords and has no content articles. Growth Engine adds 2 articles and 2 to 3 backlinks a month and tracks 75 keywords. Market Authority adds 4 articles, 4 to 6 backlinks and 150 keywords, plus schema and AI answer work.

Use those counts when you read any quote. "Content and links" means nothing until it says how many, each month.

## Is cheap SEO a risk?

Cheap SEO is a risk when the price cannot pay for the work it promises. Goodjuju's guide calls retainers under $300 a month a red flag. At that price, an agency can keep your listings tidy. It cannot also write owner pages, earn local links and report leads.

Watch for these signs in a low quote:

- **A ranking guarantee.** Google's local ranking help page says "there's no way to request or pay for a better local ranking on Google."
- **Hundreds of pages fast.** Google's spam policies name "scaled content abuse": many pages made mainly to manipulate rankings.
- **Reports with traffic only.** No owner leads, no signed doors.
- **The agency owns your site or domain.** You lose the work when you leave.

Cheap is not always bad. A 40-door manager in one small town may need only the foundational level. The risk is to pay for the specialist level and get the foundational work.

## How many signed doors does SEO need to pay back?

SEO pays back when the new doors' fees cover the plan cost. Goodjuju's guide uses an example of $180 per door per month in fees, kept for 36 months. That is about $6,480 per door over the owner's tenure.

Here is the math for a 2-year view. We use the same $6,480 value per door.

| Plan price | Cost per year | Cost over 2 years | Doors to break even in 2 years |
|---|---|---|---|
| $1,199 | $14,388 | $28,776 | 5 |
| $1,699 | $20,388 | $40,776 | 7 |
| $2,299 | $27,588 | $55,176 | 9 |
| $4,460 | $53,520 | $107,040 | 17 |

Doors to break even = cost over 2 years / $6,480, rounded up. Your own fee and owner tenure change the answer. Put your real management fee into our [property management fee calculator](/tools/property-management-fee-calculator) to get your value per door. Note that SEO often takes months to bring the first leads, so year 1 carries most of the cost.
