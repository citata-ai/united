The 2021 Rental Housing Finance Survey looked at rental properties with 1 to 4 units. Individual investors owned 70% of them, or 15.9 million properties. Only about 22% of these small properties had professional management (HUD and Census, 2022). That is the owner a postcard can reach: a person with one or two rentals who has never typed "property manager near me".

Direct mail does not replace search. Goodjuju's fall 2026 study of 35 property management companies found that Google Search and the Map Pack produced 50.6% of web form leads (Goodjuju, 2026). Mail is a second channel for owners who do not search yet.

## Which mail pieces get responses?

Mail pieces that get responses make one clear offer to one type of owner. There is no public response study for property management mail, so test offers against each other. These four formats fit most managers:

| Piece | Best for | Offer on the piece |
|---|---|---|
| Oversized postcard | Addressed owner list | Free rental analysis for the street address |
| Letter in an envelope | Out-of-state owners, larger portfolios | A short note from the owner of the company, with fees |
| Market report postcard | Owners in one neighborhood | Median rent and days on market for their area |
| EDDM flat | Dense rental routes, brand test | Your name, service area and one link |

Rules that apply to every piece:

1. **One offer.** A rental analysis, a fee sheet or a market report. Not all three.
2. **One action.** A phone number or a short link. Both should be tracked.
3. **Local proof.** A street they know, a local review, a real number of doors you manage.
4. **No pressure.** Do not use fear about vacancy or eviction. Owners keep the card that helps them.

## How many times should I mail one list?

Mail the same list at least 3 times before you judge it. That number is a planning rule, not a study result. One postcard reaches an owner on the wrong day. Three cards over 3 months give the owner three chances to need you.

A sample plan for a list of 1,000 owners:

| Week | Touch | Message |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Postcard 1 | Free rental analysis for your address |
| 5 | Postcard 2 | Local rent and vacancy numbers |
| 9 | Letter | Note from the owner, fee summary, one review |
| 13 | Postcard 3 | Same offer as card 1, new design |

Stop mailing an owner who asks you to stop. Remove sold properties before each drop. A county record update each quarter keeps the list clean.

## What does a campaign cost?

A campaign costs postage, printing, design and the list. USPS publishes the postage. On 2026-10-19 its EDDM page listed these facts:

- EDDM Retail postage: $0.26 per piece for USPS Marketing Mail flats.
- EDDM BMEU (bulk entry): as low as $0.259 per piece.
- EDDM Retail needs at least 200 pieces and allows up to 5,000 pieces per day per ZIP Code.
- EDDM Retail needs no special mailing permit.
- Pieces go to "Postal Customer" at every address on the routes you select.

Worked example for one EDDM test:

| Line | Calculation | Cost |
|---|---|---|
| Postage | 2,000 pieces × $0.26 | $520 |
| Printing | your printer's quote | $ |
| Design | one-time | $ |
| Total per drop | postage + printing + design | $ |

Then compare the cost to the value of one door. If a door pays you $150 a month for 36 months, it earns $5,400 in gross fees. One signed door can pay for several drops. Use the [property management fee calculator](/tools/property-management-fee-calculator) to put your own fee in.

EDDM has one limit. It reaches every door on a route, so most pieces land with tenants and owner-occupants. Use it for a cheap test in a dense rental area. For owner leads, an addressed list of rental owners is more precise. Our guide on finding rental owners covers how to build that list from county records.

## How do I track mail leads?

Track mail leads with a unique phone number and a unique link on every mailing. Without them, a mail lead looks like a Google lead. The owner reads the card, searches your name and calls from your Google listing.

The tracking setup:

1. **Static call tracking number.** One per mailing, forwarded to your office.
2. **Short link with UTM tags.** For example `yoursite.com/owners?utm_source=postcard&utm_medium=mail&utm_campaign=2026-q4`.
3. **QR code.** Point it at the same tagged link.
4. **"How did you first hear about us?" on every form and call.** Goodjuju's study recommends this question, with each source named as an option (Goodjuju, 2026).
5. **One row per drop in a sheet.** Pieces sent, calls, link visits, owner leads, signed doors.

Report results per 1,000 pieces. That makes a 2,000-piece drop and a 500-piece drop easy to compare.

## Is direct mail legal to send to owners?

Postal mail to owners is legal, and the federal CAN-SPAM rules cover email, not paper mail (FTC). Two rules still apply. Do not use data you got under a restriction. For example, the Driver's Privacy Protection Act lets states release motor vehicle records for bulk marketing only with the person's express consent (18 U.S.C. 2721). Honor every request to stop. If you add owners' emails from a mail response, follow CAN-SPAM: a valid postal address, a clear way to opt out, and opt-outs processed within 10 business days (FTC).

This is general information, not legal advice. Check the current law or ask a local attorney.

## Direct mail checklist

- List of real rental owners, cleaned each quarter.
- One offer and one action per piece.
- At least 3 touches to the same list.
- Static phone number and tagged link per drop.
- Results per 1,000 pieces, by signed doors.
- Stop requests honored at once.
