## Wyoming eviction rules at a glance

Wyoming calls an eviction "forcible entry and detainer" (FED). The rules sit in Wyo. Stat. 1-21-1001 to 1-21-1017. The Residential Rental Property Act, 1-21-1201 to 1-21-1211, adds renter duties and the sheriff's role. We read the official Title 1, 5 and 34 PDFs from the Wyoming Legislature on 2026-10-19.

| Step or rule | Limit or deadline | Statute |
|---|---|---|
| Court | Circuit court in the judicial district | Wyo. Stat. 1-21-1001 |
| Unpaid rent | Case allowed after rent is 3 days past due | Wyo. Stat. 1-21-1002(a)(i) |
| Holdover | Case allowed against a tenant holding over the term | Wyo. Stat. 1-21-1002(a)(i) |
| Renter breach | Case allowed for violations of 1-21-1204 or 1-21-1205 | Wyo. Stat. 1-21-1002(a)(vi) |
| Notice to quit | Written, served at least 3 days before filing | Wyo. Stat. 1-21-1003 |
| Summons | Served 3 to 12 days before trial | Wyo. Stat. 1-21-1004 |
| Written answer | Not required | Wyo. Stat. 1-21-1004, 1-21-1006 |
| Continuance over 2 days | Tenant must post a bond | Wyo. Stat. 1-21-1007 |
| Filing fee | $20 + $40 automation + $10 legal services = $70 | Wyo. Stat. 5-9-135 |
| Writ of restitution | Issued on request after judgment | Wyo. Stat. 1-21-1012 |
| Execution | Within 2 days after the officer receives it, Sundays excepted | Wyo. Stat. 1-21-1013 |
| Stay on appeal | Bond within 48 hours of judgment, Sundays excepted | Wyo. Stat. 1-21-1014(b) |

This is general information, not legal advice. Check the current law or ask a local attorney.

## What notice must a Wyoming landlord give before filing an eviction for unpaid rent?

A Wyoming landlord must give a written notice to quit at least 3 days before filing. Wyo. Stat. 1-21-1003 says the party who wants to bring an FED action "must notify the adverse party to leave the premises." Serve it by leaving a written copy with the tenant. If you cannot find the tenant, leave it at the tenant's usual home or place of business.

For unpaid rent, there is also a timing rule. Wyo. Stat. 1-21-1002(a)(i) allows the case against tenants "after a failure to pay rent for three (3) days after it is due."

**Worked example.** Rent is due on the 1st. It is unpaid at the end of the 4th, which is 3 days after it was due. You serve the notice to quit on the 5th. Count at least 3 days. File on the 8th or later.

| Day | Event |
|---|---|
| 1st | Rent due |
| 4th | Rent unpaid for 3 days |
| 5th | Serve written notice to quit |
| 8th or later | File the FED complaint |

The statute does not require a "pay or quit" option. It requires a notice to leave. Your lease may promise more, such as a grace period. If it does, follow the lease.

## How long does an eviction take in Wyoming?

A Wyoming eviction moves fast on paper. The statutes set short windows at every step:

| Phase | Statutory time | Statute |
|---|---|---|
| Rent unpaid | 3 days after due | 1-21-1002(a)(i) |
| Notice to quit | At least 3 days before filing | 1-21-1003 |
| Summons service | 3 to 12 days before trial | 1-21-1004 |
| Continuance | Over 2 days only with the tenant's bond | 1-21-1007 |
| Judgment | On the return day, if not continued | 1-21-1008(a) |
| Stay by appeal | Bond within 48 hours, Sundays excepted | 1-21-1014(b) |
| Writ executed | Within 2 days of receipt, Sundays excepted | 1-21-1013 |

Add the minimums: 3 days of late rent, 3 days of notice, and at least 3 days from service to trial. That puts the earliest trial about 10 days after the due date, if you file and serve at once. The court's calendar decides the real date.

At trial, the court sits as judge unless a party demands a jury (Wyo. Stat. 1-21-1008, 1-21-1009). In a rent case, the court also finds the rent due. It enters judgment for that rent, plus costs and attorney's fees as the lease provides (1-21-1008(b)).

If the tenant does not appear, the court still tries the case. You must file a complaint and prove it, or the case is dismissed (Wyo. Stat. 1-21-1005).

## Where does a landlord file an eviction case in Wyoming, and what does it cost?

A Wyoming landlord files an FED case in circuit court. Wyo. Stat. 1-21-1001 gives any circuit court within the judicial district the power to hear it. Wyo. Stat. 5-9-128 lists FED actions under 1-21-1001 to 1-21-1016 as part of circuit court jurisdiction.

The filing cost comes from Wyo. Stat. 5-9-135:

| Fee | Amount |
|---|---|
| Original filing fee | $20 |
| Court automation fee | $40 |
| Indigent civil legal services fee | $10 |
| Total | $70 |

Service and sheriff costs are extra. Ask the clerk for the current total.

A business owner may file without a lawyer in some cases. Wyo. Stat. 1-21-1017 lets a business entity appear through an owner, shareholder, member or partner. The entity must own a majority interest in the property. Every owner must sign a written notice to the court and the parties. The rule does not apply if the entity seeks a judgment above the small claims limit in Wyo. Stat. 1-21-201.

## Can a Wyoming landlord evict a tenant for a lease violation or with no cause?

Yes. A Wyoming landlord may bring an FED case against renters who violate the duties in Wyo. Stat. 1-21-1204 or the bans in 1-21-1205 (1-21-1002(a)(vi)). Examples from those sections:

- **Not paying.** Not current on all payments the lease requires (1-21-1204(a)(vi)).
- **Unsafe unit.** Not keeping the unit clean and safe, or not disposing of garbage safely.
- **Extra occupants.** Adding occupants above the lease limit without written permission.
- **Damage.** Intentional or negligent damage to the unit.
- **Interference.** Interfering with another person's peaceful enjoyment.
- **Blocking access.** Unreasonably refusing entry for repairs, inspection or showings.

For no cause, the end of the lease term is the key. Wyo. Stat. 34-2-128 says no tenancy arises by implication, except a tenancy by sufferance. When a lease term ends and the tenant holds over, accepting rent creates only a tenancy by sufferance. Wyo. Stat. 1-21-1002(a)(i) allows an FED case against tenants "holding over their terms." Serve the 3-day notice to quit first.

## Who removes the tenant after a judgment in Wyoming?

The sheriff or other officer removes the tenant. When the court enters a judgment of restitution, it issues a writ of restitution at your request (Wyo. Stat. 1-21-1012). Unless the tenant appeals, the officer must execute it within 2 days after receiving it, Sundays excepted (Wyo. Stat. 1-21-1013).

Wyo. Stat. 1-21-1211(a) adds that, if the renter does not leave as the court ordered, the sheriff may remove the renter's things. The sheriff may also prevent the renter from returning, without further court action.

After you regain lawful possession, Wyo. Stat. 1-21-1210 controls property left behind:

1. **Trash.** Dispose at once of property you reasonably believe is hazardous, perishable or valueless and abandoned.
2. **Valuable property.** Send written notice that you will dispose of it after 7 days.
3. **Renter responds.** If the renter answers in writing within 7 days, hold it 7 more days.
4. **Storage costs.** You may charge reasonable storage and removal costs before release.

## Is a self-help eviction (lockout, utility shutoff) legal in Wyoming?

Wyoming's statutes give the removal job to the sheriff under a court order (Wyo. Stat. 1-21-1013, 1-21-1211(a)). The FED and Residential Rental Property Act sections we read do not authorize a landlord to change locks or cut utilities to force a tenant out. Use the court process.

The Act also requires the owner to keep up essential services. Wyo. Stat. 1-21-1203(a)(iii) requires the owner to maintain electrical systems, plumbing, heating, and hot and cold water. A shutoff can break that duty. The renter may then seek costs, damages and other relief in circuit court under Wyo. Stat. 1-21-1206.

## Wyoming eviction checklist for landlords

- [ ] Rent is at least 3 days past due, or the tenant holds over, or a 1-21-1204/1205 breach is documented.
- [ ] Written notice to quit served at least 3 days before filing; keep proof.
- [ ] FED complaint filed in circuit court; budget $70 plus service.
- [ ] Summons served 3 to 12 days before trial.
- [ ] Lease, ledger and photos ready to prove the complaint.
- [ ] Writ delivered to the sheriff; never change locks yourself.
- [ ] Property left behind handled under the 7-day notice rule.
