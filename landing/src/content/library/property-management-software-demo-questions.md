## How do you test trust accounting in a demo?

Ask the vendor to finish a complete money cycle and explain the balances. A dashboard tour does not prove your accounting process.

Start with a tenant charge and payment. Add a vendor bill, management fee and owner reserve. Ask what cash is available for distribution and why.

Then introduce a returned payment. Check the reversal, tenant balance and effect on the owner. Have the accountant trace every change.

Rentvine's [trust accounting page](https://www.rentvine.com/features/trust-accounting) describes native three-way reconciliation. Use that claim to ask for the bank, trust ledger and property-ledger comparison. Give other vendors the same test.

Use an acceptance sheet:

| Question | Evidence to request |
|---|---|
| Whose money is held? | Client or property detail |
| What has cleared? | Bank reconciliation and open items |
| What can be paid? | Available funds after required holds |
| Who approved it? | Role permissions and action record |
| What changed? | Correction and reversal history |

Your accountant should explain the result without an unexplained adjustment. Record a failed test as unresolved, even if the salesperson promises a later fix.

## What questions reveal hidden fees?

Ask for complete invoice examples at your exact size and activity. A starting price cannot show every cost.

The vendors' own [AppFolio](https://www.appfolio.com/pricing), [Buildium](https://www.buildium.com/pricing/), [Rentvine](https://www.rentvine.com/pricing) and [DoorLoop](https://www.doorloop.com/pricing) pages show different pricing structures. Read 2026-10-19, they leave several account-specific amounts for a quote.

Use these questions:

- **Subscription:** what is the actual bill at our current count?
- **Minimum:** what unit and spending floors apply?
- **Payments:** who pays incoming, outgoing and return charges?
- **Leasing:** what do screening and signatures cost?
- **Connections:** which plan and partner fees apply?
- **Setup:** what import, training and bank fees appear?
- **Renewal:** what changes when the offer ends?
- **Exit:** what notice and archive costs apply?

Ask the vendor to mark unknown amounts. A blank should mean unresolved, not free.

Buildium requires onboarding for new Growth and Premium customers. Rentvine describes a one-time setup fee. Ask what those services include rather than comparing the subscription alone.

## How do you check support quality?

Ask how the offered plan handles a real blocked task, then test the available help. Marketing claims cannot establish a service-quality winner.

Use a payment or close problem as the scenario. Ask who responds, what information staff provide and how an unresolved case escalates. Confirm the hours for your team.

Buildium's pricing page distinguishes ticket support from live phone support in higher tiers. AppFolio lists standard and enhanced support by plan. DoorLoop also describes plan-based support differences.

Ask for a support article covering the scenario. Have a daily user follow the instructions. Record any missing step or unclear term.

Use this support record:

| Area | Written answer needed |
|---|---|
| Access | Channels included in the offer |
| Hours | Staffed periods and urgent route |
| Ownership | Vendor or partner handles the problem |
| Escalation | How a stuck case moves forward |
| Training | Included sessions and later learning |

If a response target matters, ask whether it is contractual. Do not turn a broad average-response claim into a promise for your account.

## What should you ask about data exports?

Ask to export sample records during the demo and open them outside the product. Data ownership language is useful, but usable files are the practical test.

Request people, properties, leases, balances, transactions, attachments and open work. Ask which date ranges and identifiers the files retain.

Check documents separately from spreadsheets. A link that needs an active account may not be a durable archive. Ask what happens after cancellation.

DoorLoop's pricing page says users can export their data. Ask exactly which records and formats that covers. Do not infer every attachment or historical event is included.

Buildium's [onboarding page](https://www.buildium.com/features/onboarding/) names import categories. Import help is not evidence of a complete outgoing archive.

Ask the vendor to answer:

- **Scope:** which records cannot be exported?
- **Format:** can another system read the files?
- **Links:** do attachments remain accessible?
- **Timing:** how long does a full export take?
- **Cost:** who pays for assistance?
- **Access:** when does cancellation remove login?

Save a sample export before buying. It gives your team something concrete to inspect.

## Which workflows should vendors show live?

Ask vendors to show your repeated work and your difficult exceptions. A prepared success case does not reveal recovery steps.

For leasing, move an inquiry into an application and signed agreement. Change a rent amount and inspect the final document. Test an applicant who asks a question staff must answer.

For maintenance, receive a tenant request with photos. Assign an approved vendor, collect an invoice and show the owner charge. Confirm who can approve spending.

For owners, produce a statement and answer a question about a repair. Test separate access for owners with different properties.

For automation or AI, give an incomplete request. Ask where the facts come from and when the system stops for approval. Keep financial changes under review during evaluation.

Run the offered plan, not a broader demonstration account without a feature map. Record every upgrade, outside tool and manual transfer needed to finish.

## How should you score the demo?

Score the required results before comparing optional features. Keep failures visible rather than averaging them into a high overall score.

Use a simple result set: pass, unresolved or fail. Your accounting and access requirements should act as acceptance checks. An attractive interface cannot compensate for a failed money-control test.

Have daily users and the accountant sign different parts of the record. Name who will resolve each open question. Ask the vendor to attach answers to the quote.

End the demo by reviewing the missing evidence. Book the next session around those gaps. Sign only after the required tasks and written terms pass review.
