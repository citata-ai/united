## What counts as a side payment on a Section 8 lease?

A side payment is any money the voucher tenant pays you for rent above the approved tenant share. The rule is in 24 CFR 982.451(b)(4). The tenant's part of the rent may not be more than the rent to owner minus the PHA's housing assistance payment (HAP). The owner "may not demand or accept any rent payment from the tenant in excess of this maximum."

The rule does not care what you call the money. These all count:

- **Cash on top of rent.** "$150 a month, off the books."
- **A second agreement.** A side lease or a note that says the tenant pays more than the HAP contract shows.
- **A fee for what others get free.** A charge for an item that unsubsidized tenants in the building get at no cost (24 CFR 982.510(c)).
- **A hidden rent increase.** A higher rent that you did not send to the PHA. 24 CFR 982.308(g)(4) says you must notify the PHA at least 60 days before a rent change.

Here is how the approved split works, with made-up numbers:

| Line | Amount |
|---|---|
| Rent to owner in the HAP contract | $1,500 |
| HAP paid by the PHA | $1,100 |
| Maximum tenant rent (982.451(b)(4)) | $400 |
| Extra "side" payment you ask for | $150 |
| Amount you must return to the tenant | $150 each month |

The tenant can also say no. 24 CFR 982.451(b)(4)(iii) says the family does not owe the part of the rent the HAP covers.

## What happens to a landlord caught taking side payments?

A landlord caught taking side payments risks the HAP contract and future voucher business. The PHA's tools are in the federal rules:

1. **Breach of contract.** 24 CFR 982.453(a) lists breaches, including any violation of HAP contract obligations and "fraud, bribery or any other corrupt or criminal act in connection with any federal housing program."
2. **Money back.** 982.453(b) lets the PHA recover overpayments, abate or reduce HAP, stop HAP, and end the HAP contract.
3. **No future tenancies.** Under 24 CFR 982.306(c), the PHA may refuse to approve tenancies with an owner who violated a HAP contract, or who committed fraud in a federal housing program.
4. **Every unit at risk.** 982.453(a)(2) also counts a violation of any other Section 8 HAP contract as a breach. One bad lease can affect your other voucher units.

The tenant faces risk too. 24 CFR 982.551(k) says family members must not commit fraud or any corrupt act in connection with the program. A side deal can put the family's voucher in danger, so a tenant may report it to protect their assistance.

This is general information, not legal advice. Check the current law or ask a local attorney.

## Can I charge for parking, pets or storage?

You can charge for an extra only when the charge is lawful for every tenant and the PHA knows about it. The test is in 24 CFR 982.510(c). You may not charge a voucher tenant extra for items that are:

- customarily included in rent in your area, or
- given at no extra cost to unsubsidized tenants in the same property.

| Charge | Allowed? |
|---|---|
| Parking that other tenants get free | No (982.510(c)) |
| Parking that every tenant pays for, written in the lease | Ask the PHA how it treats the charge |
| Pet fee that you charge all tenants | Ask the PHA; follow fair housing rules for assistance animals |
| Meals or supportive services | No, the lease may not require them (982.510(b)) |
| Late fee on the tenant share | Only if the lease allows it and state law permits it |

The safe path: write every charge in the lease. Send the lease to the PHA. 24 CFR 982.308(g)(1) says any lease change must be in writing, and you must give the PHA a copy at once.

## Can the tenant pay more if the PHA approves it?

A voucher tenant can pay more rent when the PHA approves a higher contract rent in the lease. That is legal. A private deal is not.

Two rules limit the higher rent:

1. **Rent must be reasonable.** Under 24 CFR 982.507, the PHA compares your rent with similar unassisted units. The rent to owner may never exceed the reasonable rent.
2. **The 40% cap at move-in.** Under 24 CFR 982.508, when gross rent is above the payment standard, the family share at first occupancy may not exceed 40% of adjusted monthly income.

So if you think the rent is too low, ask the PHA for a rent review. Bring comparable listings. ## How do I return a side payment?

Return every excess payment to the tenant at once. 24 CFR 982.451(b)(4)(ii) says the owner "must immediately return any excess rent payment to the tenant." The same section, 982.451(b)(3), says excess HAP goes back to the PHA.

Steps:

1. **Stop taking the extra money today.** Do not wait for the next lease term.
2. **Add up every extra payment.** Use bank records and receipts.
3. **Pay it back in a traceable way.** A check or bank transfer, with a short letter that lists the months and amounts.
4. **Keep copies.** Keep the letter and proof of payment with the lease file.
5. **Talk to a local attorney about the PHA.** An attorney can help you decide how to report the past months.

## Checklist: keep your Section 8 rent clean

- [ ] The tenant pays only the tenant rent on the PHA's notice.
- [ ] No side lease, side note or cash deal exists.
- [ ] Every fee is in the lease and the PHA has a copy.
- [ ] No fee covers an item other tenants get free.
- [ ] You give the PHA 60 days' notice before a rent change.
- [ ] You return any overpayment at once and keep proof.
