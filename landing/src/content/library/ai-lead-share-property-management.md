## What do lead source studies show for property managers?

Lead source studies show AI tools give a small but growing share of owner leads. The largest public study we found comes from Goodjuju Marketing, a marketing agency for property managers. Its fall 2026 update tracks 35 companies with clean lead tracking over two 90-day windows.

Here are its numbers for owner leads, by channel.

| Channel | Lead share, spring 2026 | Lead share, fall 2026 | Conversion rate, fall |
|---|---|---|---|
| Google Search and Map Pack | 51.8% | 50.6% | 1.2% |
| Direct and Maps | 33.6% | 35.6% | 0.7% |
| Other search | 5.9% | 4.3% | 1.2% |
| Referral | 4.0% | 3.7% | 1.1% |
| AI tools | 2.1% | 3.4% | 3.9% |
| Paid search | 0.9% | 1.2% | 0.2% |
| Social media | 1.6% | 0.8% | 0.2% |

Source: Goodjuju Marketing, read 2026-10-19. Spring window: February 26 to May 26, 2026. Fall window: July 8 to October 5, 2026.

Three facts stand out:

1. **Google still leads.** Google Search and Map Pack gave 639 of 1,262 fall owner leads. AI tools gave 43. That is about 15 times fewer.
2. **AI converts well.** AI visitors were only 0.8% of traffic but 3.4% of leads.
3. **ChatGPT is most of AI.** Of the 43 AI leads in client data, ChatGPT gave 37, Gemini 5 and Perplexity 1.

The study counts unique people, not form events. It leaves out phone calls and tenant forms.

## Why do AI lead share numbers differ between studies?

AI lead share numbers differ because each study measures a different set of sites, windows and channels. Four things change the result.

- **Who is in the sample.** All 35 Goodjuju companies are clients of an SEO agency. The study says their results may be stronger than the industry average.
- **What counts as AI.** Goodjuju counts visits from chatgpt.com, claude.ai, gemini.google.com, perplexity.ai, Copilot, grok.com, meta.ai and you.com. A study that counts only ChatGPT will show less.
- **First touch or last touch.** The spring study used GA4 first-user source. An owner who reads ChatGPT, then searches the brand name on Google, can count as Google.
- **Lost referrers.** Some AI apps do not pass a referrer. GA4 then files the visit as direct.

The Goodjuju spring study first counted "(data not available)" as AI traffic. The fall update fixed that: it gave zero owner leads in both windows. So the spring AI share in the first report (1.6%) and the re-pulled spring share (2.1%) are not the same number. Compare like with like.

## How much AI traffic does GA4 miss?

GA4 misses a large part of AI leads, so read every AI share as a floor. On its own site, from May to October 7, 2026, Goodjuju found that GA4 credited 7 leads to AI tools. Form answers and records showed at least 13. That means GA4 missed about 4 in 10 AI leads that the team could trace.

You can close part of that gap in three steps:

1. **Ask on the form.** Add a "How did you hear about us?" field with "ChatGPT or another AI" as a choice.
2. **Ask on the call.** Ask each new owner which tool they used before they called.
3. **Set an AI channel in GA4.** Our guide on [how to track AI traffic](/guides/track-ai-traffic-property-management) shows the setup.

## Is the share of AI leads growing?

Yes, the share of AI leads grew in the one study that compares the same companies over time. For the same 35 companies, AI tools went from 27 owner leads in spring to 43 in fall 2026. Lead share rose from 2.1% to 3.4%.

The AI conversion rate also rose, from 3.0% to 3.9%. The study puts the fall AI rate at 3.2 times Google organic, up from 2.6 times in spring.

Watch for: spring and fall are different seasons. The study itself warns to read small shifts with care. Two windows are a start, not a trend line.

Outside property management, the Ahrefs team saw the same shape on its own site. In a 30-day window in 2025, AI search gave 0.5% of Ahrefs traffic but 12.1% of signups (Ahrefs, 2025-06-16). That is one software company, not a property manager. It shows the pattern: small traffic, high intent.

## What should you expect for your size?

Expect AI to give a few percent of owner leads today, not a large share. No public study splits AI lead share by door count. So we use a simple model from the fall 2026 average share (3.4%).

| Owner leads per month from your site | Expected AI leads per month at 3.4% | Expected AI leads per year |
|---|---|---|
| 5 | 0.17 | about 2 |
| 10 | 0.34 | about 4 |
| 20 | 0.68 | about 8 |
| 50 | 1.7 | about 20 |

We did the math: leads per month times 0.034, then times 12. This is a planning number, not a promise. Your share can be 0 if AI does not name you at all.

That is the real risk. In our own run on 2026-10-19, we asked ChatGPT, Claude and Gemini "What is the best property management company in <city>?" for 50 US cities. The 150 answers named 821 different companies (our data, 2026-10-19). The average answer named 8.8 companies. If your company is not on that short list, your AI lead share stays at zero, whatever the market average is.

## What should you do with this data?

Use the data to set a budget split and a test, not to move all spend to AI. Google Search and Maps still give about half of owner leads. Keep that work first.

Then do three small things for AI:

1. **Check if AI names you.** Ask ChatGPT and Gemini the owner questions for your city once a month. Write down who they name.
2. **Fix the sources AI reads.** In our 50-city run, expertise.com appeared as a source in 93 of 150 answers, yelp.com in 50 and ipropertymanagement.com in 50 (our data, 2026-10-19).
3. **Count AI leads by hand.** The form field and the call question catch what GA4 misses.
