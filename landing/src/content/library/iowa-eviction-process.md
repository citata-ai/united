Iowa law sets short, fixed clocks for each eviction step. I read the Iowa Code 2026 sections on the Iowa Legislature site on 2026-10-19. Two chapters do the work: chapter 562A (the residential landlord and tenant law) sets the notices, and chapter 648 (forcible entry and detainer) sets the court steps.

| Rule | Limit or deadline | Statute |
|---|---|---|
| Notice for unpaid rent | 3 days to pay after written notice | § 562A.27(2) |
| Notice for a lease breach | 7 days to cure, then the lease ends | § 562A.27(1) |
| Same breach again within 6 months | 7 days' notice, no cure right | § 562A.27(1) |
| Clear and present danger | 1 notice of 3 days, then file | § 562A.27A |
| Month-to-month tenancy | 30 days' written notice | § 562A.34(2) |
| Hearing date | No later than 8 days after filing (15 if landlord agrees) | § 648.5(1) |
| Service of original notice | At least 3 days before the hearing | § 648.5(2) |
| Removal after judgment | Execution for removal within 3 days | § 648.22 |
| Small claims filing fee | $95, plus $20 postage for mailed notice | § 631.6 |

## What notice must an Iowa landlord give before filing an eviction for unpaid rent?

An Iowa landlord gives a written 3-day notice for unpaid rent. Iowa Code § 562A.27(2) says the landlord may terminate if "the tenant fails to pay rent within three days after written notice" of nonpayment and of the intent to terminate.

Write both points in the notice: the rent is unpaid, and the lease will end if the tenant does not pay in 3 days.

Under § 648.3(1), a landlord who gave this 3-day pay notice and ended the tenancy may file without a separate 3-day notice to quit. For other grounds, such as a holdover, § 648.3 requires a written 3-day notice to quit first.

How to serve the notice (§ 648.3(2)):

1. **Hand delivery** with a signed and dated receipt from a resident aged 18 or more.
2. **Personal service** under Iowa rule of civil procedure 1.305.
3. **Post and mail.** Post it on the main entry door and mail it by both regular and certified mail. Write the posting date on the notice.

Mailed notice counts as served 4 days after it is mailed (§ 648.3(3)). Add those 4 days when you plan your dates.

## How long does an eviction take in Iowa?

An Iowa eviction for unpaid rent can finish in about 2 to 3 weeks when the tenant does not contest it. The statutes set each clock:

| Step | Days | Running total |
|---|---|---|
| 3-day notice to pay (served by post and mail) | 4 days for mail + 3 days | 7 |
| File the petition; court sets hearing | Up to 8 days | 15 |
| Judgment; execution for removal | Within 3 days | 18 |

This is a worked example, not a promise. Courts get busy. If the tenant raises a real dispute of fact, the court can set an evidentiary hearing later (§ 648.5(7)). The tenant can also ask for a continuance if the hearing is less than 3 days after service (§ 648.5(5)).

## Where does a landlord file an eviction case in Iowa, and what does it cost?

A landlord files in the district court of the county where the unit is (§ 648.5(1)). The district court sitting in small claims can hear evictions for nonpayment, holdover and lease breach (§ 631.1(2)). Most residential cases go there.

Costs in the Code:

- **Filing fee:** $95 for a small claims action (§ 631.6(1)(a)).
- **Postage:** $20 when the clerk mails the original notice (§ 631.6(1)(c)).
- **Service fee:** the sheriff's fee is set by other law (§ 631.6(1)(d)).

The court must have proof of service. Serve the original notice by a signed receipt, by personal service, or, after 2 failed tries, by posting and mailing (§ 648.5(2)). Posting must be at least 3 days before the hearing. If you post and mail, file an affidavit with copies of what you posted (§ 648.5(4)).

## Can an Iowa landlord evict a tenant for a lease violation or with no cause?

Yes to both, with the right notice.

**Lease violation.** Send a written notice that names the breach and says the lease ends on a date at least 7 days after receipt if the tenant does not fix it in 7 days (§ 562A.27(1)). If the tenant fixes it in time, the lease goes on. If the same breach comes back within 6 months, a 7-day notice ends the lease with no second chance.

**Danger to others.** For a clear and present danger, such as assault or illegal weapon use, § 562A.27A allows one 3-day notice of termination and notice to quit. The notice must quote the tenant exemptions in subsection 3.

**No cause.** For a month-to-month tenancy, give written notice at least 30 days before the periodic rental date named in the notice (§ 562A.34(2)). For a fixed term, give 30 days' notice before the end of the term (§ 562A.34(3)).

**Watch for retaliation.** Section 562A.36 bars an eviction because the tenant complained to a code agency, complained to you about repairs, or joined a tenant union. A good-faith complaint within 1 year creates a presumption of retaliation. The bar does not apply if the tenant is in default in rent.

## Who removes the tenant after a judgment in Iowa?

The sheriff removes the tenant. If the court finds for the landlord, § 648.22 says judgment is entered that the tenant be removed and "an execution for the defendant's removal within three days from the judgment shall issue." The execution goes to the officer, who also collects costs.

Do not move the tenant's things yourself. Wait for the officer to carry out the execution.

## Is a self-help eviction (lockout, utility shutoff) legal in Iowa?

No. Iowa Code § 562A.33 says a landlord "may not recover or take possession of the dwelling unit by action or otherwise," including by cutting electric, gas, water or other essential service. The only exceptions are abandonment, surrender, or a process the chapter allows.

The cost of a lockout is high. Under § 562A.26, the tenant can get back in or end the lease. In either case, the tenant can recover:

- actual damages,
- punitive damages up to twice the monthly rent, and
- reasonable attorney fees.

On $1,200 rent, the punitive part alone can reach $2,400.

## Iowa eviction checklist

- Serve a 3-day pay notice or a 7-day cure notice, in writing.
- Use one of the 3 service methods in § 648.3 and keep proof.
- Add 4 days if you serve by mail.
- File in the county where the unit is. Budget $95 plus service.
- Serve the original notice at least 3 days before the hearing.
- Attend the hearing, which should fall within 8 days of filing.
- Let the sheriff carry out the removal. Never lock out or cut utilities.
