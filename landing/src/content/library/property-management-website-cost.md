## How much does a property management website cost overall?

A property management website costs from about $150 a year to over $20,000 up front, by build type. Property Manager Websites (PMW) says in its cost post that property management sites "generally range from $2,000 to $20,000". The same post says enterprise builds "can even exceed $100,000".

Here are the published prices we found, read on 2026-10-19.

| Build type | Vendor | Setup | Monthly |
|---|---|---|---|
| DIY website builder | Squarespace Basic / Core / Advanced | $0 | $12 / $17 / $25 on annual billing; $19 / $27 / $36 monthly |
| Software-bundled site | PMW Smartsite with Rentvine | Not listed | "Smartsite included with Rentvine" |
| Template site (PM-specific) | PMW Smartsite package | From $1,795 | Hosting from $195 |
| Custom site (PM-specific) | PMW Custom package | From $3,895 | Hosting from $195 |
| Site plus marketing bundle | PMW Grow / Premier / Elite | Not listed | $145 / $795 / $1,995 |
| New site inside an SEO plan | ClearLead Market Authority + website | Not stated | $2,599 in year 1 ($2,299 + $300), 12-month term |

Sources: PMW cost post (updated 2025-11-03), PMW pricing page, ClearLead SEO pricing page, Squarespace pricing page. Squarespace says annual billing saves up to 36%. We read the lower prices as the annual rate.

## What do template sites cost per month?

Template sites for property managers cost about $145 to $195 a month at PMW, plus setup. A template site uses a ready design with your logo, colors and text. PMW's cost post lists its Smartsite package from $1,795 setup and $195 a month for hosting. It says the package includes hosting, SEO setup and mobile design.

A general builder costs less but gives you no property management features. Squarespace runs $12 to $36 a month. You build the owner pages, the rental analysis form and the listings feed yourself. For a 30-door owner-operator, that can be enough. For a 300-door company, your time costs more than the vendor.

## What does a custom site cost?

A custom property management site costs from $3,895 setup at PMW, plus $195 a month hosting. "Custom" means a design made for your brand, not a template with your logo.

ClearLead Digital sells a site a different way. It does not list a one-time build price. Its Market Authority SEO plan ($2,299 a month) has a website add-on of $300 a month in year 1. The add-on covers a new site with up to 25 custom pages and needs a 12-month agreement. After year 1, the price steps down to $2,299 a month.

So the "website cost" there is $3,600 over 12 months, but only on top of $27,588 in SEO fees for the year.

## Which add-ons raise the price?

SEO, call tracking, content, chat and listing feeds raise the monthly price. PMW's pricing page lists these features across its plans: automated rental analysis, rent vs sell, ROI and vacancy loss calculators, call tracking, live chat, blog posts, Yext with AI review responses, PPC management, SEO consulting and video. The page does not show which plan includes which feature, so ask for a plan sheet.

## What does a property management website cost over 3 years?

The 3-year total is the fair way to compare a website, because hosting runs every month. Here is the math with the published starting prices.

| Option | Setup | Monthly | 36 months | 3-year total |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Squarespace Core (annual rate) | $0 | $17 | $612 | $612 |
| PMW Smartsite (template) | $1,795 | $195 | $7,020 | $8,815 |
| PMW Custom | $3,895 | $195 | $7,020 | $10,915 |
| PMW Smartsite + CallRail Lead Tracking | $1,795 | $245 | $8,820 | $10,615 |

These are floors. Each vendor says "starts at". Your quote can be higher.

Put the 3-year total next to what one owner is worth. Use our [property management fee calculator](/tools/property-management-fee-calculator) with your fee. If one new door is worth a few thousand dollars in fees over the owner's tenure, a site that brings 1 or 2 extra owners a year can pay for itself. That is a hypothesis to test with your own lead data.

## What hidden fees should I watch for?

The fees that hurt most show up when you leave, not when you sign. PMW's cost post says its packages have no hidden fees. Check every vendor's contract for these lines anyway.

- [ ] **Domain ownership.** Register the domain in your own name, with your own login.
- [ ] **Content and design ownership.** Can you take the pages, images and blog posts with you?
- [ ] **Minimum term.** ClearLead's website add-on needs 12 months. Ask each vendor for its term in writing.
- [ ] **Cancel fee or buyout.** Ask if the contract charges a fee to release the site or the domain.
- [ ] **Software lock-in.** PMW's Smartsite comes with a Rentvine subscription. If you change software, ask what happens to the site.
- [ ] **Per-page or per-change fees.** Ask what a new city page or a text edit costs after launch.
- [ ] **Call tracking overage.** CallRail charges $3 per extra local number and $0.05 per extra local minute.
- [ ] **Hosting increase.** Ask if the monthly price can rise during the term.
