## Can I charge a deposit?

Yes. These five questions cover money at move-in.

**1. Can I charge a security deposit?** Yes. 24 CFR 982.313(a) says "the owner may collect a security deposit from the tenant." The PHA may bar a deposit above private market practice or above what you charge unassisted tenants.

**2. Does the housing authority pay the deposit?** Usually not. The Maricopa County housing authority states: "HAMC DOES NOT PAY FOR SECURITY DEPOSITS." The Santa Cruz County housing authority says the tenant pays it, and points tenants to a deposit help program.

**3. Can I keep the deposit for damage?** Yes, subject to state law. Under 982.313(c)-(d), you may use it for unpaid rent, damage or other amounts owed under the lease. You must give the tenant a written, itemized list and refund the rest promptly.

**4. Can I charge fees that other tenants do not pay?** No. 24 CFR 982.510(c) bars extra charges for items included in rent locally or free to unsubsidized tenants.

**5. Can the tenant pay me extra on the side?** No. The HAP contract bars any payment for the unit beyond the approved rent to owner.

## Who screens the tenant?

You screen the tenant. These five questions cover finding and approving a family.

**6. Who screens the tenant?** The owner. 24 CFR 982.307(a)(2) says "the owner is responsible for screening and selection." The PHA checks eligibility only.

**7. Can I run credit and background checks?** Yes. The Santa Cruz County housing authority says: "You can perform any background check within the law that you find necessary." Use the same criteria for all applicants.

**8. Can I refuse a voucher holder?** It depends on where the unit is. PRRAC's March 2026 list shows 19 states plus DC and Guam with source-of-income laws that protect voucher holders.

**9. How do I find voucher tenants?** List the unit where you list any rental. The Santa Cruz County housing authority suggests a free listing on AffordableHousing.com.

**10. What does the PHA check before it approves?** Four things under 24 CFR 982.305: the unit is eligible, it passes inspection, the lease has the HUD tenancy addendum, and the rent is reasonable. At move-in, the family share also cannot be over 40% of adjusted income if the rent is above the payment standard. Our [Section 8 rent calculator](/tools/section-8-rent-calculator) runs that check.

## How long until the first payment?

Plan for up to 60 days. These five questions cover payments.

**11. How long until the first payment?** Tacoma says "it may take 60 days." Maricopa says "up to 60 days." Santa Cruz County says "two to eight weeks."

**12. Is the first payment backdated?** Yes. Tacoma and Maricopa pay from the move-in date or the passed inspection date, whichever is later.

**13. How do I get paid each month?** Most PHAs pay by direct deposit. Maricopa pays by direct deposit only. Santa Cruz County pays by the third business day of each month.

**14. What if the PHA pays late?** You cannot evict for it (24 CFR 982.310(b)). The HAP contract allows a late fee only after the first two months, and only if you charge late fees to all tenants.

**15. Will I get a 1099?** Yes, at most PHAs. Tacoma says it reports payments to the IRS on a 1099 under the TIN on your W-9.

|---|---|---|---|
| First payment | Up to 60 days | Up to 60 days | 2 to 8 weeks |
| Monthly method | Direct deposit preferred | Direct deposit only | Direct deposit or check |
| Backdated to | Later of move-in or pass | Later of move-in or pass | Passed inspection date |

## Can I raise rent?

Yes, after the first lease term. These five questions cover rent and repairs.

**16. When can I raise the rent?** Not during the initial lease term (24 CFR 982.309(a)(3)). After it, give the PHA at least 60 days' notice (982.308(g)(4)).

**17. Does the PHA check the new rent?** Yes. It must redetermine the reasonable rent before any increase (982.507(a)(2)).

**18. How often is the unit inspected?** At least every two years, or every three for small rural PHAs (24 CFR 982.405(b)).

**19. How long do I have to fix inspection items?** 24 hours for life-threatening items and 30 days for others (24 CFR 982.404(a)(3)).

**20. Is HQS being replaced?** Yes, by NSPIRE. HUD's September 2025 notice (90 FR 46911) gives PHAs until February 1, 2027 to switch.

## Can I leave the program?

Yes, at the end of a lease term. These five questions cover ending a tenancy.

**21. Can I leave the program?** The Santa Cruz County housing authority says "the landlord can leave the program at the end of any lease term." State notice rules still apply.

**22. Can I evict a Section 8 tenant?** Yes, by court action, for a serious or repeated lease violation, a violation of law or other good cause (24 CFR 982.310). Give the PHA a copy of any eviction notice.

**23. Can I sell the property?** Yes. Tacoma says you must tell the buyer about the tenant, and the new owner must honor the current lease.

**24. What if the tenant moves out early?** The HAP contract ends. You keep the payment for the move-out month, but no later month (24 CFR 982.311(d)).

**25. Does the PHA do move-out inspections?** Not always. Tacoma says it does not perform move-out inspections for units it does not own. Do your own, with photos, for the deposit.
