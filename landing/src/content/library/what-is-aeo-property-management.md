## What is AEO for a property management company?

AEO (answer engine optimization) is the work that gets your pages quoted in AI answers. An answer engine gives one written answer, not a list of 10 links. Google AI Overviews, Google AI Mode, ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude and Perplexity are answer engines.

A landlord asks: "What does a property manager charge in Austin?" The engine reads some pages, writes an answer, and names a few sources. AEO is the work that makes your page one of those sources.

For a property manager, there are 2 wins:

1. **Citation.** The answer links your page as a source.
2. **Recommendation.** The answer names your company as a manager to call.

Both wins matter. A citation sends a few visits. A recommendation puts your name on a short list for an owner who wants to hire.

The stakes are clear in one study. Pew Research Center tracked 900 US adults in March 2025. When a Google search showed an AI summary, people clicked a normal result in 8% of visits. Without a summary, they clicked in 15% of visits. Links inside the summary got about 1% of visits. So the answer itself now does much of the work your page did before.

## Is AEO the same as GEO?

AEO and GEO name mostly the same work. GEO means generative engine optimization: getting cited by AI answers. Google's own guide defines both terms. Then Google says that "optimizing for generative AI search is optimizing for the search experience, and thus still SEO."

Some agencies draw a line. They use AEO for Google answer features, such as AI Overviews and featured snippets. They use GEO for all AI assistants, including ChatGPT and Claude. The line is a label, not a different method.

| Term | Short meaning | Main targets | Main result to measure |
|---|---|---|---|
| SEO | Rank in the list of links | Google and Bing results | Rank, clicks |
| AEO | Get quoted in one written answer | AI Overviews, AI Mode, featured snippets | Citations in answers |
| GEO | Get cited and named by AI engines | ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude, Perplexity, Google AI | Citations and mentions |

The term GEO comes from a 2023 research paper by Aggarwal and others. The authors report that some page changes can "boost visibility by up to 40%" in generative engine answers. The test used a research benchmark, not live property management searches. Treat the number as a sign that page format matters, not as a forecast.

## What makes a property management page an answer?

A page is an answer when an engine can lift one passage and use it with no edits. Google says AI features use its core ranking systems. So the basics come first: the page must be indexed and able to show a snippet. Google says there are no other technical requirements.

After the basics, the page itself decides. Use this checklist on each owner page:

- **One question per page.** "How much does property management cost in Denver?" is one page. Do not mix it with tenant topics.
- **Answer in the first sentence.** Put the number or the yes or no first. Explain after.
- **Real numbers.** Show your fee as a number or a range. Owners ask AI for numbers, and a page with no number gives the engine nothing to quote.
- **Named facts.** Name the city, the service, the license and the company. Write "We manage 240 homes in Denver" (with your real count), not "we manage many homes".
- **One topic per section.** Each H2 must stand alone. AI engines cut pages into parts, so a section that starts with "it" loses its subject.
- **A unique view.** Google asks for "a unique point of view" and content based on what you know. Your own vacancy days or your own owner questions beat a copy of a national article.
- **Tables for comparisons.** A fee table with plan, price and what it includes is easy to quote.

Google also says you do not need llms.txt files, AI text files or special schema to appear in Google AI features. ## Which pages should a property manager fix first for AEO?

Fix the pages that answer the questions owners ask before they hire: cost, trust and fit for their city. We suggest this order:

1. **Fee page.** One table: management fee, leasing fee, renewal fee, other fees. A number in each row.
2. **Service city pages.** One page per real service city. Name the city, the areas, the home types and the team.
3. **Services page.** A plain list of what you do and do not do.
4. **About page.** The company facts AI repeats: name, founding year, owner, license, address, phone.
5. **Public listings.** Google Business Profile, Yelp, BBB, Expertise.com and other directories.

Why listings in a list about pages? Because AI answers about local managers lean on directories. On 2026-10-19 we asked ChatGPT, Claude and Gemini "What is the best property management company in [City, ST]?" for 50 US cities (our data, 2026-10). A listing site was a source in 132 of 150 answers. Some manager's own site was a source in 106. Expertise.com alone was a source in 93 answers, and Yelp and iPropertyManagement in 50 each. So wrong facts on a listing can reach the answer as often as your page does.

## How do you measure AEO for a property management company?

Measure AEO with 3 checks each month. A rank tracker alone does not show it, because AI answers are not a list of positions.

| Check | Tool | What you see |
|---|---|---|
| Prompt run | A fixed list of 20 owner prompts in each engine | Is your company named? Is your page cited? |
| Google AI impressions | Search Console, Generative AI performance report | How often your pages show in Google AI features |
| AI visits | GA4 AI Assistant channel | Visits from ChatGPT, Gemini, Copilot and others |

Google's guide points site owners to the Generative AI performance report in Search Console. Google Analytics defines an AI Assistant channel for sources like ChatGPT, Gemini, DeepSeek, Copilot and Grok. That channel excludes Google AI Overviews and AI Mode, which count as organic search. The setup steps are in our guide to [track AI traffic and AI mentions](/guides/track-ai-traffic-property-management).

Start the prompt run before you change a page. Without a baseline, you cannot tell what your work did. Our own first run on 2026-10-18 named Citata.ai in 0 of 108 non-brand answers (our data, 2026-10). That zero is our point A. We report each change against it.

## What AEO cannot do

AEO cannot promise a place in an answer. Ahrefs tracked 43,000 keywords for one month in 2025. An AI Overview had a 70% chance to change from one check to the next. No one controls that. AEO also cannot fix a weak service. If owners leave bad reviews, AI engines read those too.

AEO can remove the reasons an engine skips you. Common ones are no number on the fee page, no page for the city and wrong facts on a listing. Fix those 3 things first. Then run the same prompts again in 30 days and compare.
