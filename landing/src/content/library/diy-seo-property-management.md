Google has a page called "Do you need an SEO?" Its answer for small firms is plain. If you run a small local business, you can probably do much of the work yourself (Google Search Central). A property management company with one office is that kind of business.

The stakes are real. In Goodjuju's fall 2026 study of 35 property management companies, Google Search and the Map Pack produced 50.6% of owner form leads (Goodjuju, 2026). The Map Pack is the map with 3 local companies at the top of Google. Most of the work that gets you there is work an owner can do.

## Which SEO tasks can an owner do in a few hours a week?

An owner can do the local tasks: the Google Business Profile, reviews, listings and owner answer pages. These tasks need knowledge of your business more than technical skill. You already have that knowledge.

Google says local results depend on relevance, distance and prominence. Prominence includes how many reviews you have and how many sites link to you (Google Business Profile Help). You control relevance and reviews directly.

A weekly plan of 2 to 4 hours:

| Day | Task | Time |
|---|---|---|
| Monday | Reply to every new Google review | 15 min |
| Monday | Send review requests to owners and tenants from the last week | 15 min |
| Tuesday | Post one update on your Google Business Profile | 15 min |
| Wednesday | Write or update one owner answer page (every 2 weeks) | 60–90 min |
| Thursday | Check one directory listing: Yelp, Bing, BBB, Expertise.com | 15 min |
| Monthly | Search Console check: clicks, top queries, errors | 30 min |

The owner answer page is the core task. Write one page per real owner question: your fees, your leasing timeline, the local deposit rule, the cost of a vacancy. Answer in the first sentence. Add your own numbers. Our [AI search checklist](/guides/ai-search-checklist-property-management-website) lists the page details that help AI tools quote you too.

## Which tasks need an expert?

Tasks that can break the whole site need an expert. A wrong review reply hurts one review. A wrong redirect plan can drop every page from Google at once.

| Task | DIY or expert | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Google Business Profile | DIY | You know your hours, services and photos |
| Review requests and replies | DIY | Needs your voice and your client list |
| Owner answer pages | DIY, with a style guide | Needs local facts you have |
| Directory listings | DIY | Simple, but needs care |
| Site redesign or platform move | Expert | Redirects and URL changes can lose rankings |
| Technical errors in Search Console | Expert if you cannot read them | Crawl and index problems need code access |
| Schema markup | Expert or a good website vendor | Wrong markup can be ignored or flagged |
| Link building | Careful DIY or expert | Paid or spam links break Google's spam policies |

Google also says when to hire. A good time is before a site redesign or a new site launch, so the site is built for search from the start (Google Search Central).

## Which free tools help?

Four free tools cover most of what a small property manager needs:

1. **Google Search Console.** Shows the queries that bring clicks, the pages Google indexed and errors. Google's starter guide points site owners to it first (Google Search Central).
2. **Google Business Profile.** Your Map Pack listing, with calls, direction requests and website clicks.
3. **Google Analytics 4.** Shows which pages owners read before they fill a form.
4. **Bing Webmaster Tools.** Shows Bing search data. Goodjuju's 2026 study found that ChatGPT's local answers lean on Bing and Yelp listings, so keep both current.

Add one habit that costs nothing. Ask every new owner, "How did you first hear about us?" Goodjuju found that GA4 missed about 4 in 10 of the AI-referred leads it could trace on its own site (Goodjuju, 2026). The owner's own answer fills that gap.

## What should I skip?

Skip the tasks that Google says do not matter. Google's starter guide lists several:

- Google Search does not use the keywords meta tag.
- Keywords in the domain name have hardly any effect.
- There is no magic word count for a page.
- Keyword stuffing is against Google's spam policies.

Source for all four: Google Search Central, SEO Starter Guide. Time you save here goes to reviews and owner pages.

## When should I hire help?

Hire help when the weekly plan stops for more than a month, or when you need work you cannot check. Three signs:

- You plan a new site, a new platform or a new domain.
- Search Console shows index or crawl errors you cannot explain.
- You manage several offices or cities and cannot write pages for each.

Agency SEO is a real cost. On 2026-10-19 Goodjuju listed plans from $1,185 to $4,460 per month, plus a $7,930 plan for multi-location firms (Goodjuju pricing). Compare that cost to the value of one door. At $150 per month in fees for 36 months, one door earns $5,400. Run your own numbers in the [property management fee calculator](/tools/property-management-fee-calculator).

Before you sign, ask the agency for owner leads by source, not traffic. Ask how they will report AI answers. Our guide to [GEO agencies for property management](/guides/geo-agencies-for-property-management) lists more questions to ask.

## How long until DIY SEO shows results?

Google says some changes show in a few hours and others take several months. It suggests you wait a few weeks before you judge a change (Google Search Central). For local SEO, judge the plan after 3 months:

- Review count and average rating, against month 0.
- Google Business Profile calls and website clicks.
- Search Console clicks for owner queries with your city name.
- Owner leads by first-heard source.

Write the month-0 numbers down today. Without a baseline, you cannot tell if the hours paid off.
