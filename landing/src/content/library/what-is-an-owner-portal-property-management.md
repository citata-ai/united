## What is an owner portal?

An owner portal is the secure website or app where a rental owner sees their property's money, documents and repairs. The property manager runs it, usually as part of their property management software. The owner logs in at any time, without a phone call or an email to the manager.

The owner portal answers the 4 questions most owners ask each month:

1. **Did the rent come in?**
2. **What did you spend, and why?**
3. **How much did you send me?**
4. **What is broken, and what will it cost?**

A portal that answers these 4 questions cuts routine calls. It also builds trust, because the owner can check each number.

## What reports are in an owner portal?

An owner portal should hold the owner statement, an income report and a transaction list for each property. The Buildium owner portal page (read 2026-10-19) says owners can "view and download profit and loss statements, income statements, contributions, draws, and property-specific transactions". It also says managers control which reports owners see and when.

Use this checklist to judge an owner portal:

| Item | What it shows | Must have? |
|---|---|---|
| Monthly owner statement | Rent in, fees and bills out, net paid to owner | Yes |
| Profit and loss (income statement) | Income and expenses by category for a period | Yes |
| Year-to-date summary | Totals for the tax year so far | Yes |
| Transaction list by property | Each charge and payment with date | Yes |
| Rent roll | Each unit, tenant, rent and balance | Yes, for multi-unit |
| Bills and vendor invoices | The original invoice behind each repair charge | Yes |
| Leases and addenda | Signed copies for each unit | Yes |
| Inspection reports | Move-in, move-out and periodic photos | Strongly advised |
| Year-end tax documents | Form 1099-MISC and a year-end summary | Yes |
| Owner contributions | Requests to fund a reserve or a large repair | Yes |
| Messages | A written record of owner and manager talks | Helpful |

The bills line matters more than it looks. The Propertyware owner portal guide, from RealPage (January 2020), lets owners view the bills for an owner statement and open the invoice. With the original invoice, an owner can check any markup on a repair.

## Can owners approve repairs in the portal?

Owners can approve repairs in some owner portals, if the manager turns that feature on. The Propertyware owner portal guide lists "Approve or reject work orders" as an owner task. The guide says the buttons "are only available if your property management company requires approvals for work orders". They show only for open, unapproved work orders.

Other portals handle repairs as requests and history. The Buildium owner portal page says owners "can submit maintenance requests as tasks" and view "the full history of maintenance and repair tasks". The page does not describe an owner approval button, so ask your manager how approvals work in theirs.

Portal approval works best when it follows the management agreement. Here is a sample flow:

1. **The manager gets a quote.** The job is $1,200. The owner's spend limit in the agreement is $500.
2. **The portal flags it.** The work order shows as "pending approval" in the owner's alerts.
3. **The owner reviews.** The owner reads the scope, photos and quote.
4. **The owner decides.** Approve or reject, with a date stamp.
5. **The manager acts.** The vendor gets the go-ahead, or the manager calls the owner to talk options.

A date-stamped click in the portal is better proof than a text that says "ok". It protects both sides if a dispute starts later.

Watch for: approvals that sit for days. What to do: agree in writing what happens if the owner does not answer in a set time, and what counts as an emergency.

## Owner portal vs tenant portal?

The owner portal shows the owner's money and property records. The tenant portal serves the tenant: pay rent, see their own balance and ask for repairs. Both come from the same software, but each user sees only their own data.

| Feature | Owner portal | Tenant portal |
|---|---|---|
| Main user | Rental owner | Tenant |
| Money in | Owner contributions to the property | Rent and fees |
| Money out | Owner draws and payouts | Refunds |
| Reports | Statements, P&L, rent roll, 1099 | Tenant ledger, receipts |
| Repairs | See history; approve in some software | Submit requests with photos |
| Documents | Leases, invoices, inspections, tax forms | Own lease, notices |
| Buildium name | Owners Portal | Resident Portal |

The Buildium pricing page lists both an "Owners Portal" and a "Resident Portal" (read 2026-10-19). Other software uses other names, such as resident portal or tenant app.

## What should an owner ask about the portal before signing?

An owner should ask to see a sample of the owner portal before signing a management agreement. A short screen share shows more than any sales sheet.

Ask these questions:

- [ ] Which reports will I see, and on what date each month?
- [ ] Can I see the original vendor invoice behind every repair charge?
- [ ] How do repair approvals work, and what is my spend limit?
- [ ] Will my year-end 1099-MISC and summary be in the portal?
- [ ] Can I see all my properties in one login?
- [ ] Is there a mobile app?
- [ ] Can I download my data if I leave?

The last question matters at the end of the relationship. When an owner moves to a new manager, the old records must go with them.

## How does an owner portal help a property manager?

An owner portal helps a property manager by turning routine owner calls into self-serve checks. It also gives the manager a clear, dated record of what the owner saw and approved.

For a manager, 3 habits make the portal work:

1. **Post on a fixed day.** Owners learn when to look.
2. **Attach the source.** Each repair line links to its invoice and photos.
3. **Write one short note.** A 2-line summary each month ("Unit 2 renewed at $1,650; water heater replaced, invoice attached") answers most questions before they come.
