## What does each cost at a hundred doors?

A hundred-door portfolio needs a quote because the public figures are starting prices. Rentvine's advertised rate gives an illustrative $150 monthly multiplication. The page does not state a numeric minimum or promise that rate for this portfolio.

Buildium's starting plan prices do not establish its bill at that door count. Ask for the selected tier, unit allowance and total. Include the payment, screening and document charges that apply to your account.

Give both vendors the same payment volume and bank-account needs. Separate business-paid costs from charges renters or applicants pay. Both affect the service you offer, even when only some reach your books.

Ask for the bill after promotions end. Record setup charges separately. Use the [property management fee calculator](/tools/property-management-fee-calculator) to check whether your management fee covers the full cost.

## Which handles three-way reconciliation natively?

Rentvine explicitly describes three-way reconciliation on its trust-accounting page. Buildium's pricing page lists bank reconciliation, but that does not establish an identical report or workflow. Missing detail on a public page does not prove a feature is absent.

Three-way reconciliation compares the bank statement, trust ledger and underlying property balances. Ask each sales team to show actual reports and the steps staff take. Your accountant should approve those steps.

Use the same closed period in both demonstrations. Include outstanding checks, a returned payment and a prepayment. Ask the presenter to explain differences without changing balances simply to make totals match.

Check the correction record. Staff should be able to find the original entry, correction and reason. Ask who can reopen a closed period. Choose the process your accountant can review without rebuilding the records elsewhere.

## Which has better maintenance tools?

Maintenance fit depends on how requests reach a completed invoice. Buildium lists work-order management, projects, reporting and billing. Rentvine lists maintenance among its core tools. Neither description establishes which creates less staff work.

Run a request from intake to close. Begin with a renter message missing a clear location. Ask staff to request details, assign a vendor and obtain approval.

Then introduce a missed visit or a higher estimate. Check whether the new plan stays linked to the original issue. Staff need to see who owns the next action.

Decide where the bill enters the books. If you keep a separate maintenance tool, test invoice transfer and duplicate prevention. Do not assume a connection logo proves the entire process works. Give the maintenance lead a chance to operate each system.

## Which integrates with LeadSimple?

LeadSimple names both Rentvine and Buildium as property accounting systems it can work alongside. That supports shortlisting both, but it does not define the data exchange. Ask for a current field list and a demonstration.

Test an owner lead through onboarding. Check whether the owner, property and unit records reach the property system correctly. Change an email address and inspect where the correction appears.

Decide which system controls each field. Agree how to handle duplicate owners, shared contacts and owners with several properties. The connection should not create a new record for every spelling correction.

Confirm required plans and support ownership on both sides. Ask how failed updates appear and who fixes them. A visible error queue is more useful than an assurance that the connection usually works.

## Which suits single-family portfolios?

Either can suit single-family work when owner accounting and field tasks fit. Scattered homes need clear property balances, vendor routing and owner reports. Test those functions instead of relying on a vendor's market label.

Create an owner with several homes and another owner with a single home. Post income and repairs separately. Ask for a combined owner view and separate property reports.

Test a vacant home too. Check how inspections, leasing tasks and reserves appear before rent starts. Confirm whether vacant units remain billable and how a removed property affects the subscription.

Ask someone outside the buying team to find a statement. Can they explain the distribution from the report alone? Owner clarity is a useful test, but no feature list proves a retention gain.

## What should you check before moving?

Approve the move only after staff complete the required tasks and the accountant approves opening balances. A contact import alone does not prove a successful migration.

Use this acceptance list:

- **Books:** explain deposits, prepayments, unpaid bills and owner funds.
- **Repairs:** route a request, approve spending and close an invoice.
- **Owners:** find statements and contribution records in the portal.
- **Connections:** transfer the intended LeadSimple records without duplicates.
- **Documents:** open signed leases and original invoice attachments.
- **Exit:** export usable records into company-controlled storage.

Assign a person to each failed item. Keep the old account open until the essential checks pass. Ask how late changes between the first export and launch will move.
