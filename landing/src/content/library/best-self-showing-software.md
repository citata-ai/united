## How do ShowMojo, Tenant Turner, Rently and Showdigs compare?

The products differ in leasing workflow, access hardware and human service options. ShowMojo and Tenant Turner describe automated scheduling and self-showing access. Rently offers a custom package. Showdigs combines a leasing platform with self-showings and optional agent-led services.

Start with the type of visit you need. Vacant homes, occupied homes and shared-building access can require different controls. Ask the vendor to demonstrate each relevant case.

Follow a prospect from inquiry to completed tour. Check messages, calendar rules, identity checks and access delivery. Then inspect the record the office receives after the visit.

Do not rank products solely by the lowest starting rate. A platform that needs replacement devices or extra field work can cost more. Compare the proposed setup for your actual properties and current staff process.

## How do they verify a prospect's identity?

Identity checks should match the person seeking access to the approved tour request. ShowMojo and Tenant Turner describe ID verification and fraud controls. Showdigs describes identity screening and facial and ID recognition. Confirm Rently's exact controls in the proposed package.

Ask which checks are mandatory and which are configurable. Test an unreadable document, a mismatched name and an applicant who cannot complete the automated route. Staff need a clear review path.

Ask where identity data remains and who can view it. Avoid copying identity documents into general staff messages. Confirm the retention and deletion process with the provider.

A successful check is not proof of safe behavior inside a property. Keep access windows, cancellation, audit records and field checks in the plan. Review every control as part of the same system.

## Which lockboxes and smart locks do they support?

Hardware support must match the exact device and configuration you intend to deploy. Tenant Turner names several connected access options on its pricing page. ShowMojo lists its access devices and charges for third-party devices. Rently's package and Showdigs compatibility need confirmation for your chosen hardware.

Inventory current devices before requesting quotes. Record model, connection needs and who owns each device. Ask whether a software switch requires replacement or a new recurring service.

Test installation and routine access changes at a pilot property. Confirm how staff remove access, replace a battery and recover from a device failure. Keep a secure backup method.

Do not assume a supported brand means every model works. Ask the vendor to list the exact device in the order form. Assign someone to check the home after tours and manage hardware condition.

## What do they cost?

Compare software, devices and human services as separate costs. The opening table shows published starting software terms. It does not quote every portfolio or device setup.

ShowMojo and Tenant Turner also offer listing-based pricing. Ask which basis fits your vacancy pattern. A portfolio charge and an active-listing charge behave differently when leasing demand changes.

Showdigs lists optional agent-led tours at $49 per tour on the reviewed page. That is additional service work, not a substitute price for the platform subscription. Check availability and scope for your location.

Request a complete quote with billing term, minimum, device count and recurring access charges. Include installation, replacement and staff travel where relevant. Record the bill after any promotion ends before deciding which package costs less.

## What are the security risks?

Self-showings create access risks that require a clear operating process. These include shared access information, failed cancellation, device faults and uncertainty about whether a visitor left. A software feature alone cannot close every gap.

Use short, approved access windows and inspect the record of issued access. Test whether cancellation stops the intended access route. Ask what happens when the property changes status while a tour remains booked.

Keep occupied-property access under a separate reviewed process. Confirm permissions and local requirements before enabling it. Do not reuse vacant-home settings without checking the difference.

Set a response path for a locked-out prospect or a door that will not secure. Staff should know who handles the call and who visits the property. This is general information, not legal advice. Check the current law or ask a local attorney.

## What should you test before launch?

Run the failure cases as carefully as a successful tour. Use test accounts and a pilot property.

- **Calendar:** block an unavailable time and attempt a booking.
- **Identity:** submit an incomplete verification attempt.
- **Access:** try before and after the approved window.
- **Cancellation:** cancel a scheduled visit and recheck access.
- **Hardware:** simulate a connection or battery problem.
- **Handoff:** request staff help and inspect the alert.
- **Closeout:** confirm the office can see the visit record.

Ask a staff member outside the project to follow the recovery steps. Rewrite any step that depends on unstated knowledge. Keep a manual showing route for unresolved cases.
