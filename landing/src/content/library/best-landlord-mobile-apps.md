## Which landlord apps belong on your shortlist?

Shortlist apps by task, then test each task on your phone. The table uses the vendors' pages read on 2026-10-19. It is a buying shortlist, not a hands-on performance ranking.

| App | Vendor-listed strength | Price reference | Check before choosing |
|---|---|---|---|
| Landlord Studio | Receipt scanning and mileage tracker | GO free; paid price varies by plan and units | Receipt limits, export, and trip workflow |
| Stessa | Financial tracking and leasing tools | Essentials free; Manage $15 monthly; Pro $35 monthly | Paid signing and scanning limits |
| Buildium | Broader management suite and resident tools | Essential starts at $62 monthly | Which staff tasks work in the mobile app |

Source: Landlord Studio and Stessa pricing pages, Buildium pricing, and Buildium Resident Center. Stessa also lists annual-billing prices; the table uses monthly billing.

Do not compare a resident app with a manager app as if they serve the same user. Confirm who installs what and which account receives the records.

A useful phone app should reduce later data entry. If staff must rebuild every field at a desk, the phone workflow remains incomplete.

## Which full property management apps work best on mobile?

The best full management app is the one that completes your required field tasks without a desktop rescue. Check the exact manager workflow rather than a screenshot of the resident portal.

Buildium describes Resident Center as a place for payments, requests, documents, and communication on desktop and mobile. That supports a resident-facing shortlist. It does not prove that every accounting or admin task works from a phone.

Use a practical test. Open a resident record, find the correct lease, review an open request, and send an approved reply. Then check the result from another staff account.

Try poor reception and an interrupted upload. Ask whether the app saves a draft and whether it clearly reports failure. Do not assume that a button press means the server received the work.

Check role limits and account recovery. A field employee should see the assigned work without gaining access to unrelated financial records. Remove a test user's access and confirm the session ends.

## Which apps scan receipts?

Landlord Studio and Stessa both list receipt scanning, but plan limits and export needs can change the fit. The useful result is a checked expense linked to its source document.

Take a photo of a faded receipt with invented or approved test details. Inspect the date, payee, amount, and category. Correct an error and confirm the original image remains available.

Check how the app handles a receipt that covers different properties. Ask whether staff can split the expense and keep the shared source image. Do not accept the scan's suggested category without review.

Test duplicate capture. Upload the same image again and see whether the app warns you or creates another expense. Then compare the expense list with a bank-imported transaction.

Ask your accountant to open the export. A clear photo inside an app is less useful if the year-end file loses the document link.

Choose a plan based on your expected capture and review work. Keep the receipt limit in the same buying note as the subscription price.

## Which apps track mileage?

Landlord Studio lists a mileage tracker, and Stessa lists mileage expense tracking. Those labels do not establish that both offer the same automatic trip capture.

Test how a trip starts, ends, and links to a property. Record the business purpose in the app. Check whether you can correct a missed route without hiding the original record.

Ask about background location access. Decide who can view trip details and how staff separate private travel from company records. A mileage feature should not create unclear monitoring expectations.

Compare the phone log with the export. Confirm that dates, distance, purpose, and property details remain readable. Have your accountant state which supporting records your business needs.

Do not choose an app because it promises a tax saving. This guide compares capture workflows and does not claim a deduction or rate.

Use a sample trip that includes a stop. The test can show whether the app records a continuous journey, separate segments, or a manual expense entry.

## Which apps sign leases, and which are free?

Stessa's paid plans list eSignature allowances, while its free plan has a different feature set. Check the allowance and billing period on the current pricing page before choosing a lease workflow.

A signature feature needs more than a drawing box. Test the invite, signer identity steps, completed document, and record of completion. Confirm how staff correct a wrong recipient before sending.

Check the renter experience on a small screen. Read the full lease, open attachments, complete required fields, and download the final copy. A manager's successful test does not prove the renter's task is clear.

Free software can still involve payment processing, screening, or document charges. Record who pays each fee. Compare the services you will actually use rather than treating free access as free operation.

- **Choose a primary task:** Receipt capture, field work, or leasing should drive the first test.
- **Check the free plan:** Confirm units, users, exports, and document limits.
- **Use your phone:** Test the operating system and device staff already use.
- **Inspect the record:** Confirm that the result reaches the correct property and account.
- **Check the exit:** Export sample records before making the app your only store.
