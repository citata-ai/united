## How do prices compare?

TenantCloud has the lower published entry price, but entry plans may cover different work. TenantCloud Pro lists bank reconciliation and a separate owner portal. An owner choosing Starter should not assume those features come with it.

Read capacity separately from price. TenantCloud lists unlimited properties and units, but also gives active lease allowances. A large unit list does not remove a lease limit. Ask how inactive leases and renewals affect the allowance.

Buildium's entry figure is a starting price. Request a quote for the portfolio and services you need. Record the support, onboarding and connected services included.

Use your recent activity to compare total cost. Count payments, screenings, signatures and work done outside the software. Separate annual commitments from monthly billing. Save the quote and pricing date so your team can check what changes at renewal.

## Which has better accounting?

Accounting fit depends on the records you must control. No hands-on test of your books supports an absolute accounting winner here.

Bring sample bank activity, an owner contribution, an unpaid bill and a tenant credit. Ask each vendor to produce your close reports. Then correct a transaction and inspect the audit history.

Check the difference between cash in the bank and money available for a payout. Reserves, deposits and outstanding payments need clear treatment. Ask your bookkeeper to explain the report without a separate spreadsheet.

Test your reporting basis. Ask how the system handles prior-period corrections and opening balances. Confirm what happens when a bank feed stops or a payment returns. Matching ordinary deposits is not enough. Use exceptions as the main accounting test and keep the sample reports with your decision notes.

## Which suits a landlord or management company?

Personal rentals and outside clients create different reporting needs. Portfolio size matters, but ownership and client obligations matter too.

For personal rentals, test expense entry, receipts, rent balances and bookkeeper exports. Keep the workflow small. More features help only when they remove work you actually do.

For outside owners, test separate balances, management fees, statements and payout approval. Include shared ownership and owners with several properties. Ask how access changes when staff join or leave.

Do not choose by labels alone. TenantCloud offers Business, while Buildium has several service levels. Ask each vendor to complete your tasks in the proposed plan. Record what still needs another tool or a custom report. A product may handle more units without meeting a specific client reporting need. Keep those questions separate.

## How do their portals compare?

Judge portals from resident and owner accounts, not from the staff dashboard. A useful portal gives the right person an answer without exposing another client's records.

Start with a resident payment. Inspect the balance, fees, status and receipt. Ask how a returned payment appears. Submit a maintenance request and follow the update.

Then open an owner account. Find the latest statement, invoice and payout. Check which dates the report covers. Ask how a revised statement appears after a correction.

Use this portal checklist:

- Verify access to the correct properties.
- Check payment status before and after settlement.
- Open a document from a phone.
- Follow a maintenance request to completion.
- Locate the owner statement and payout detail.
- Confirm restricted records remain hidden.

Ask someone outside accounting to explain the payout. If they cannot, change the report or add a clear explanation before inviting owners.

## When do you outgrow TenantCloud?

You outgrow a plan when it cannot meet a required task or capacity at an acceptable cost. That does not automatically mean leaving TenantCloud.

List unmet needs with examples. They might involve lease capacity, roles, reporting or a connected service. Ask whether another plan resolves them. Request a demonstration rather than a general sales answer.

Compare an upgrade with a move. Moving includes cleanup, training and temporary duplicate work. A lower subscription may still leave more manual reporting.

If Buildium passes a task that your current setup cannot handle, document the result. Confirm that the quoted plan includes it. A future feature remains an open question. Choose through the tested result and written scope. Keep the cost of staying visible so the decision does not become a comparison of new tools alone.

## How should you test before choosing?

Use a controlled sample and written pass rules. Keep the same records for both vendors so the comparison stays fair.

Export core data and sample documents from your current process. Check links between owners, properties, tenants and leases. Approve opening balances before testing payouts or collection cutover.

Ask staff to complete normal work without the presenter taking control. Record where help remains necessary. Include the bookkeeper, maintenance lead and person who answers owner questions.
