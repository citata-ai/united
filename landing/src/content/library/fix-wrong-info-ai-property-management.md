## Where does the wrong fact come from?

A wrong fact in an AI answer almost always comes from a page or profile the engine read. There are four common sources:

| Source | Example of a wrong fact | Who can fix it |
|---|---|---|
| Your own website | An old fee page still live at a second URL | You |
| Google Business Profile | Old hours, old address, a "closed" flag | You, after you verify |
| Directories and list sites | Old phone, old fee, wrong owner name | You, by claim or by request |
| Model training data | A mix-up with a company with a similar name | No one directly. Fix the web sources and wait. |

Google confirms the mix for its own data. It builds each Business Profile from public web content, licensed third-party data, facts from users and owners, and its own contact with the business. So one stale directory can push a wrong fact into your Google profile too.

ChatGPT works from live pages when it searches. OpenAI says ChatGPT search "sometimes partners with other search providers" and rewrites the prompt into search queries. It also warns: "Search results and citations can be incomplete, outdated, or incorrect."

We saw a name mix-up on our own brand. On 2026-10-19 we asked 3 engines what citata.ai is. Claude answered about Citate.ai or Citant.ai in 4 of 4 brand answers. Gemini merged us with Citant.ai. ChatGPT was right in 2 of 4 answers (our data, 2026-10-19). Property managers with common names, such as "Premier" or "Elite", can hit the same problem.

## How do you find the source of a wrong fact?

Open the answer and read its sources. Then follow these steps:

1. **Run the exact prompt.** Use the words the owner used, such as "Does Acme Property Management charge a leasing fee?"
2. **Open the Sources panel.** In ChatGPT, select Sources. In Google AI Overviews and AI Mode, open the link icons or the side panel.
3. **Find the sentence.** Search each source page for the wrong fact.
4. **Check your own site.** Search Google for `site:yourdomain.com "leasing fee"`. Old pages often stay indexed.
5. **Check your Google profile.** Open Edit profile, then "Collected info". Google shows facts it gathered, with the source and the date.
6. **Write it down.** Log the prompt, engine, date, wrong fact and source URL.

If no source shows the wrong fact, it may come from training data. Then the fix is slower: put the right fact on many trusted pages and wait for new models.

## Which listings do you fix first?

Fix the listings that AI engines cite most for your city. In our test of 150 answers about the best property manager in 50 US cities, these domains showed up most (our data, 2026-10-19):

| Priority | Source | Answers that cited it (of 150) | Fix |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Your website | Company sites in 106 | Delete or redirect old pages. One fee page, one address. |
| 2 | Google Business Profile | Not counted (API test) | Edit name, hours, category, services, status |
| 3 | expertise.com | 93 | Contact Expertise.com to update facts |
| 4 | yelp.com | 50 | Claim your Yelp business page and edit it |
| 5 | ipropertymanagement.com | 50 | Contact the site to update the listing |
| 6 | reviews.birdeye.com | 19 | Find which tool feeds the page, then fix it there |
| 7 | bbb.org | 14 | Update the BBB business profile |

Engines differ. Claude cited Yelp in all 50 of its answers. ChatGPT cited Birdeye pages in 17 and the BBB in 11. So check the sources for each engine you care about.

## How do you send feedback to Google?

Google gives three ways, one for each surface.

**AI Overviews.** At the bottom of each overview, click the thumbs-down icon. Then click Report a problem, choose a category and add details. Google says the report includes your recent search and its results. You can also use the three-dot menu at the top right of the overview.

**AI Mode.** Google's help page asks users to give feedback with thumbs up or thumbs down when AI Mode "doesn't always get it right".

**Places and profiles.** This is the strongest tool, because it changes the data itself.

- If you own the profile, edit it directly. Google says edits usually show in about 10 minutes and can take up to 30 days.
- If you do not own the profile yet, search the business on Google and click Suggest an edit. You can fix the name, category, address, hours, phone and website.
- If Google shows you as closed, use Suggest an edit, then Operating status changed, then Reopen, in Google Maps. Google warns: do not mark a place open or closed based on its hours.

Google also lets owners report a wrong Maps summary. It removes only keywords linked to an unrelated place and editorial summaries that name services you do not offer. It does not remove a summary for being "unclear or negative".

## How do you send feedback to OpenAI?

OpenAI's help page on ChatGPT web search does not list a form to correct business facts. It tells users to open cited sources, check dates, and "ask ChatGPT to search again using a specific source, date, or location".

So for ChatGPT, the fix is the source:

- **Let OAI-SearchBot in.** OpenAI says sites that block OAI-SearchBot "will not be shown in ChatGPT search answers", except as navigation links. It says a robots.txt change takes about 24 hours to apply.
- **Publish the right fact in text.** Put your fees, address and service cities on your own site, in plain HTML.
- **Fix the directories ChatGPT cites.** Start with the ones in your own prompt log.

## How long until AI shows the fix?

No engine publishes a fixed time. Here are the waits each company does publish:

| Step | Wait | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Google Business Profile edit | About 10 minutes, up to 30 days | Google Business Profile Help |
| Google page recrawl | Several days to several months | Google Search Central |
| Google place edit by a user | Reviewed first, no time given | Google Maps Help |
| OpenAI robots.txt change | About 24 hours | OpenAI crawler docs |
| Model training data | Unknown | No public timeline |

You can speed up the Google crawl. In Search Console, use URL Inspection and ask Google to recrawl the corrected page.

Then check again. Re-run the same prompt in each engine 1 week, 4 weeks and 8 weeks after the fix. Log the answer and the source. Our guide on [how to track AI traffic and AI mentions](/guides/track-ai-traffic-property-management) shows a simple log.

Watch for: a wrong fact can come back. If one directory still shows the old fee, it can feed a new answer next month. Keep your log open until 2 checks in a row are clean.
