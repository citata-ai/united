## Which businesses can send owner referrals to a property manager?

Any business that meets a rental owner at a decision point can send you referrals. The owner inherits a house, moves for a job, buys a rental or gets tired of tenant calls. At each of these moments, a professional is in the room before you are.

Use this checklist to map your partners:

| Partner | When they meet the owner | What their client needs |
|---|---|---|
| Real estate agent | The owner buys a rental, or cannot sell and decides to rent | A manager who will not steal the listing later |
| Estate or probate attorney | Heirs receive a house | Someone to secure, rent or prepare the house |
| Real estate attorney | The owner has a tenant dispute | A manager to run the next lease well |
| CPA or tax preparer | The owner files Schedule E for a rental | Clean books and year-end statements |
| Mortgage lender or broker | The owner buys or refinances a rental | A rent estimate and a plan to fill the unit |
| Title or escrow company | Closing on an investment home | A contact for the new owner |
| Relocation team at an employer | An employee moves and keeps the old home | A manager in the old city |
| Contractor, painter, flooring installer | Turnover repairs or tenant damage | Fewer emergency calls to the owner |
| Cleaner or carpet cleaner | Move-out cleaning | A steady flow of turnovers |
| Insurance agent | The owner insures a landlord policy | Lower claim risk |

Fourandhalf, a property management marketing agency, named carpet cleaners, painters, CPAs, contractors, flooring installers and estate attorneys as good vendor partners in 2016. Their reason still holds: these people have direct contact with owners who rent out homes.

## Do lenders, title companies and attorneys refer owners?

Lenders, title companies and attorneys do refer owners, but each one has a different reason and a different rule set. Lenders and title companies meet investors at purchase. Attorneys meet owners in a crisis or at an estate.

What each one wants from you:

- **Lenders.** A fast rent estimate helps their buyer qualify or plan. Offer a written rental analysis within 2 business days.
- **Title and escrow.** They want to give the new owner a useful contact at closing. Offer a one-page "new rental owner" sheet.
- **Estate attorneys.** Heirs often need a house secured and rented or sold. Offer a fixed checklist for vacant inherited homes.
- **Real estate attorneys.** They see owners after a bad tenancy. Offer to explain your screening and lease process.

A legal flag for lenders and title companies: RESPA Section 8 limits referral fees. Under 12 CFR 1024.14(b), no person may give or accept "any fee, kickback or other thing of value" for such referrals. The rule covers settlement service business tied to a federally related mortgage loan. If your partnership includes referrals of mortgage or title business, get legal advice before any money or gift changes hands.

This is general information, not legal advice. Check the current law or ask a local attorney.

## Can vendors and contractors refer owners?

Vendors and contractors can refer owners, and they meet owners at the worst moments of self-management. A flooring installer sees a trashed unit. A cleaner sees a rushed move-out. Those are the times an owner thinks "I should hire a manager."

Fourandhalf quoted a property management coach who built "a substantial portion of her business" on vendor referrals. That is one story, not data. But it shows the path.

How to make vendor referrals work:

1. **Pick vendors who work for owners, not only for you.** A painter who serves 50 self-managing landlords is a better partner than one who serves only your portfolio.
2. **Give them a reason.** Send them your work first. Pay on time. Recommend them to your owners.
3. **Give them a card or a link.** One page that explains what you do for small landlords.
4. **Thank them every time.** A note, a public thank-you or a small gift.
5. **Check the fee rules.** Fourandhalf wrote that referral fees are legal in most places but not all. Rules on fees to people without a real estate license differ by state. Check your state's real estate license law first.

## Do relocation companies send leads?

Relocation teams can send leads, but the volume depends on large local employers. An employee who moves for a job may keep the old home as a rental. The relocation team or the employer's HR team often keeps a list of local contacts.

Steps to start:

- **List the top employers** in your service area that hire from out of town.
- **Ask HR who handles relocation.** Some use an outside relocation company.
- **Offer a simple service.** "We manage the home you leave behind" is clear.
- **Send a one-page sheet** with your fee table and your owner reporting.

We have no data on relocation referral volume. Treat this channel as a test. Give it 6 months and count the leads.

## How much is one referral worth?

One referral is worth the fees that owner pays over the time they stay. Fourandhalf put the lifetime value of a management contract at $3,000 to $20,000 in 2016, by home and area. Use your own numbers instead.

Worked example (hypothetical numbers; use yours):

| Item | Value |
|---|---|
| Monthly management fee | $120 |
| Months an average owner stays | 48 |
| Fee revenue per owner | $5,760 |
| Leasing fees over the same period | $1,000 |
| Lifetime fee revenue per owner | $6,760 |

If a partner sends you 4 owners a year, that is about $27,000 of lifetime fee revenue. Spend real time on that partner. Try the [property management fee calculator](/tools/property-management-fee-calculator) to test your fee numbers.

## How do I start each partnership?

Start each partnership with a meeting, a clear offer and a feedback loop. Most partnerships fail because nobody follows up.

Checklist for each new partner:

- [ ] Meet in person or by video for 20 minutes.
- [ ] Ask what their client asks them about rentals.
- [ ] Give them one page that answers those questions.
- [ ] Agree on how they will hand off an owner: email intro, form or phone.
- [ ] Add a "Referred by" field to your owner lead form and CRM.
- [ ] Tell the partner what happened to each referral within 7 days.
- [ ] Review all partners every quarter. Keep the ones who send owners.

Track the source by name. Goodjuju's fall 2026 study of 35 companies found that referral traffic brought 3.7% of form leads. But person-to-person referrals often arrive as a phone call or a typed URL. GA4 then files them as Direct. So a "Referred by" field is the only way to know which partner works.
