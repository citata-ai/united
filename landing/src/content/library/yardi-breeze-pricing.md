## What is the price per unit?

Yardi Breeze residential lists $1 per unit monthly under an annual agreement. Yardi's page shows residential Premier at the same per-unit rate with different terms and minimums. Read the property type beside the price.

| Residential plan | Displayed rate | Monthly minimum | Contract signal |
|---|---|---|---|
| Breeze | $1 per unit monthly | $100 | Annual agreement |
| Breeze Premier | $1 per unit monthly | $400 | Annual agreement; terms apply |

Source: Yardi Breeze's residential features and pricing page, read October 19, 2026. Other property types have separate schedules. Do not carry residential figures into a commercial quote.

Ask what counts as a billable unit. Confirm the treatment of vacant homes, inactive records and recent additions. Request the answer with your contract. A rate without a unit definition leaves your budget incomplete.

## What does the monthly minimum mean?

The minimum is the lowest base charge under the displayed schedule. It can matter more than the per-unit rate for a smaller portfolio.

Use the greater of the unit-based charge and the plan minimum for a planning estimate. Add confirmed services afterward. A minimum is not a cap or proof that every service is included.

Our calculations below use hypothetical portfolios and Yardi's published residential rates:

| Hypothetical units | Breeze base | Premier base |
|---|---|---|
| 50 | Greater of $50 and $100: $100 | Greater of $50 and $400: $400 |
| 100 | Greater of $100 and $100: $100 | Greater of $100 and $400: $400 |
| 500 | Greater of $500 and $100: $500 | Greater of $500 and $400: $500 |

These are calculated estimates, not quotes. Ask whether a contract term changes the result. Include property mix and selected services before comparing totals.

## What does Breeze Premier add?

Premier adds workflow and reporting functions that may fit a more complex operation. Yardi labels invoice approval workflows and customized owner financials as Premier features.

Translate each feature into a task. An approval workflow should show who approves, what happens after a refusal and where the record stays. A custom report should answer a real owner question.

Ask the presenter to use a sample invoice and statement. Include an expense correction. Check whether the report changes clearly and the original entry remains traceable.

Do not upgrade because Premier has a longer list. Record the required task Breeze cannot finish and how Premier handles it. If your team will not use the control, a higher minimum adds little practical value. If it removes manual work, use your own staff records to estimate that gain.

## Are there setup or migration fees?

Get setup and migration terms in writing. The public base rate does not establish the full cost of moving data.

Ask the vendor to separate account setup, import, balance review and training. Confirm which records arrive as structured data and which stay in an archive. Assign a person to resolve missing fields.

Use this quote checklist:

- Rate and minimum by property type.
- Contract term and cancellation rules.
- Billable-unit definition.
- Setup and import work included.
- Training scope and support.
- Payment and screening charges.
- Optional services and connected tools.
- Archive access after cancellation.

Request a sample import before the final transfer. Include owners, residents, open bills and held funds. Compare opening balances with source reports. A correct contact import does not prove that the financial transfer is complete. Keep unresolved differences on a written list until someone approves the fix.

## What does it cost at 100 doors?

For a hypothetical residential portfolio of 100 units, the displayed formula gives Breeze a $100 monthly base. Premier's minimum makes its base $400 monthly. Those amounts come from Yardi's schedule and our arithmetic.

Build the remaining budget around your activity. Add setup, payments, screening and selected services from the quote. Separate recurring charges from costs that apply only during the move.

Ask how invoices change when units enter or leave. Confirm taxes and partial-period billing. A monthly rate does not mean a month-to-month contract.

Use the [management fee calculator](/tools/property-management-fee-calculator) to assess confirmed costs against your service income. A minimum can be a large share of a small portfolio's fee income. For a larger one, payment volume and staff work may matter more. Keep both in the decision sheet.

## Which plan should you test first?

Test Breeze first if it appears to cover required work. Test Premier when a specific approval, reporting or operational need remains unresolved.

Give both plans the same task. Produce a statement, process an invoice and record a correction. Ask staff to complete the work without the presenter taking control. Save the sample reports beside the quote.

Review owner access separately from staff access. Owners should find statements and understand payouts. Staff should see only the records needed for their roles. Check permissions before sending invitations.
