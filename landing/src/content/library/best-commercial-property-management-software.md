## Which commercial tools belong on your shortlist?

Choose commercial software by the lease rules it can reproduce and explain. A rent collection feature does not establish full commercial accounting support.

| Platform | Vendor-listed commercial scope | Price reference | Required demo |
|---|---|---|---|
| Yardi Breeze | CAM recovery, retail sales, percentage rent | $2 per unit monthly; $200 monthly minimum; annual agreement | Your recovery pools and lease exceptions |
| AppFolio Commercial | Accounting, reports, CAM tracking | Quote only for commercial page reviewed | Charge setup and reconciliation output |
| Yardi Voyager Commercial | Office, retail, and industrial lease administration | Quote only | Complex lease and reporting structure |

Sources: the vendors' pages, read on 2026-10-19. These are shortlist criteria based on published capabilities, not a hands-on ranking.

Start with a lease that causes manual work today. Give the vendor an approved, redacted copy of the relevant terms. Ask the presenter to show where each term lives in the system.

Keep a written gap list. A clear supported workflow is more useful than a promise that an implementation team can make anything work later.

## Which tools handle CAM reconciliation?

Yardi Breeze documents common area maintenance recovery, while AppFolio lists CAM tracking. CAM means common area maintenance. Test the full recovery process before treating either label as sufficient.

The required workflow starts with the lease's expense rules. Identify which costs enter a pool, which tenants share them, and how the lease changes the allocation. Do not rely on a generic split.

Ask the vendor to show estimated charges, actual expenses, the comparison, and the resulting adjustment. Then inspect the tenant statement and source detail.

Test a lease exception. A tenant may have a cap, a different expense period, or an excluded category under the agreed terms. Use your actual approved lease rules rather than invented legal assumptions.

Check how the system handles a corrected expense after review. Ask whether staff can explain the new adjustment and preserve the prior result.

Have the accountant approve the output before purchase. A spreadsheet can remain a review aid, but it should not become an unexplained second set of billing rules.

## Which handle percentage rent?

Yardi Breeze explicitly lists retail sales tracking and percentage-rent posting. Ask other shortlisted vendors to demonstrate the required method before assuming support.

Percentage rent links a charge to sales under the lease's terms. The software needs the correct reporting period, sales definition, and calculation rule. A field labeled percentage does not establish that process.

Use a retail lease sample with a known expected result. Enter approved test sales and ask the vendor to show the charge, source period, and supporting report.

Check revised sales reports. Ask how staff replace an estimate, record a correction, and explain the difference to the tenant. Keep the calculation evidence with the charge.

Test the review role separately from data entry. The person entering sales should not need unrestricted rights across the whole portfolio.

Do not buy a higher plan just because it advertises more automation. First confirm that the product can represent your lease accurately and show a reviewer how it calculated the bill.

## Which residential tools also do commercial?

AppFolio offers a commercial product page, and Yardi markets both residential and commercial platforms. That makes them candidates for mixed operations, not automatic choices for every commercial lease.

A commercial workflow can require different fields, schedules, parties, and reports from a residential rent cycle. Ask the vendor to show commercial records directly.

Check tenant identity and contacts. A business lease may involve a billing contact, property contact, and other approved parties. Your system should not force staff to overwrite one contact to reach another.

Review changes over the lease term. Ask where scheduled increases, options, and critical dates appear. Confirm which items generate charges and which only create reminders.

Test maintenance and vendor work too. Commercial software still needs clear request routing, access instructions, and invoice review. A strong lease module does not prove an effective service workflow.

## What does commercial software cost?

Use public prices only where the vendor publishes them for the relevant product. Otherwise, record quote only and request a matching proposal.

Yardi Breeze's commercial page provides the price and annual-agreement terms shown in the opening table. AppFolio Commercial and Voyager Commercial require quotes on the reviewed pages. Do not substitute a residential rate.

Ask for a proposal that names units, properties, entities, users, modules, and billing term. Include required integrations and reporting work. Keep optional services separate.

Migration can involve lease review as well as record import. Ask who maps the lease terms and who signs off on the first billing output.

Price the staff work required after setup. If a required reconciliation remains outside the software, document the review and import process. Compare that cost with a more complete platform.

Keep the quote date in your decision record. Public pricing and contract scope can change. A written proposal should state which terms apply to your account.

## When do you need a separate tool?

Add a separate tool when a required lease, accounting, or reporting task cannot work reliably in the main platform. Do not add one only to avoid learning an existing feature.

Use a clear test before splitting the workflow:

- **Define the gap:** Name the unsupported rule and its effect on billing or reporting.
- **Choose the source:** Decide which system owns the lease and calculated charge.
- **Map the handoff:** Specify the fields, period, and supporting documents.
- **Check corrections:** Confirm how a changed result reaches the ledger.
- **Assign review:** Name the person who compares source and posted totals.
- **Price support:** Include both vendors and the integration work.

A separate tool can fit a specialized task when the handoff remains inspectable. It becomes a burden when staff cannot explain which result controls.
