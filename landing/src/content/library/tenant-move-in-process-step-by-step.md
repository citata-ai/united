## What does the tenant move-in process look like?

The tenant move-in process has 3 stages: before keys, move-in day, and the first 2 weeks. Most disputes start with a step that was skipped in stage 1 or 2. Use the checklist below for every move-in, even for a tenant you know.

| Stage | Step | Done when |
|-------|------|-----------|
| Before keys | 1. Sign the lease and all addenda | Every adult occupant signed |
| Before keys | 2. Give required disclosures | Signed copies on file |
| Before keys | 3. Collect move-in funds | Funds cleared, receipt sent |
| Before keys | 4. Confirm utilities transfer | Tenant accounts start on move-in date |
| Before keys | 5. Get renters insurance proof, if the lease requires it | Policy page on file |
| Before keys | 6. Clean, rekey and test the unit | Locks changed, smoke alarms tested |
| Before keys | 7. Prepare the condition checklist | Form ready, photos taken |
| Move-in day | 8. Walk the unit with the tenant | Both see every room |
| Move-in day | 9. Sign the condition checklist | Both signed, tenant has a copy |
| Move-in day | 10. Record meter readings | Photos of each meter |
| Move-in day | 11. Hand over keys against a receipt | Key count signed |
| Move-in day | 12. Give the welcome packet | Tenant has it on paper or by email |
| First 2 weeks | 13. Collect checklist notes, if your form allows them | Notes filed with the checklist |
| First 2 weeks | 14. Check in by phone or message | Early repairs logged |

## What must be paid before keys?

Collect move-in funds before you hand over keys, and only in cleared funds. A personal check can bounce after the tenant is in. Once the tenant has possession, you need a court process to remove them.

A common set of move-in funds:

- **First month's rent.** Prorate it if the lease starts mid-month.
- **Security deposit.** Many states cap the deposit and set rules for where you hold it and what receipt you give. Check your state before you set the amount.
- **Pet deposit or pet fee, if allowed.** Do not charge one for a service or support animal. HUD notice FHEO-2020-01 bars a fee or deposit for them (NLIHC summary).
- **Any other fee the lease names.** Charge only fees your state allows.

Worked example: rent is $1,500 and the lease starts on the 20th of a 30-day month. The tenant pays for 11 days (20th through 30th). $1,500 ÷ 30 = $50 per day. 11 × $50 = $550 prorated rent. Add the deposit, and you have the full move-in total. Put this math in writing so the tenant sees how you got the number.

## Which documents do I collect before move-in?

Collect a signed lease, every addendum, and every required disclosure before keys. Signed means each adult who will live in the unit, not only the person who applied.

A standard document set:

1. **Lease.** Signed and dated by all adult occupants and by you.
2. **Addenda.** Pet, parking, smoke-free, mold, or HOA rules, as they apply.
3. **Lead paint disclosure.** Required for housing built before 1978. The EPA says the landlord must give the pamphlet "Protect Your Family From Lead In Your Home." The landlord must also disclose known lead paint and hazards, share available records, and include a Lead Warning Statement. This must happen before the tenant signs the lease. Keep the signed copy for 3 years after the lease begins.
4. **State and local disclosures.** Many states and cities add their own, such as mold, flood or bed bug notices.
5. **Renters insurance proof**, if the lease requires a policy.
6. **Emergency contact form.**

## How do I run the move-in inspection?

Run the move-in inspection with the tenant present, room by room, on a written form. Take dated photos of every wall, floor, appliance and fixture. Note any existing damage in plain words: "2-inch scratch on oven door," not "minor wear."

Some states write rules for this step. Three examples:

| State | Rule | Statute |
|-------|------|---------|
| Washington | A landlord may not collect a deposit without a written checklist or statement of the unit's condition. Both parties sign and date it, and the tenant gets a copy. Without it, the landlord is liable for the full deposit. | RCW 59.18.260 |
| Georgia | Before taking a deposit, the landlord gives the tenant a list of existing damage. The tenant may inspect before move-in. Both sign. A tenant who disagrees lists the disputed items in writing. | O.C.G.A. 44-7-33 |
| Michigan | The landlord gives the tenant 2 blank copies of a checklist at move-in. The tenant returns one, filled in, within 7 days after getting possession. | MCL 554.608 |

Other states have no checklist law. Use one anyway. A signed checklist with photos is your best proof at move-out.

## How do I hand over keys?

Hand over keys only after funds clear and all documents are signed. Count every key, fob, garage remote and mailbox key. Write the count on a key receipt and have the tenant sign it.

Also on move-in day:

- **Read the meters.** Photograph the electric, gas and water meters with the date visible. These readings settle the first utility bill.
- **Show the shutoffs.** Walk the tenant to the water main, the breaker panel and the gas shutoff.
- **Test the safety devices together.** Press the test button on each smoke and carbon monoxide alarm.

Rekey between tenants. A former tenant or a past contractor may still hold a copy.

## What do I put in a welcome packet?

A welcome packet answers the questions a new tenant asks in the first month. Good packets cut calls and late payments.

Checklist for the packet:

- [ ] Your name, phone and email, plus office hours
- [ ] Emergency number and what counts as an emergency
- [ ] How to pay rent, the due date and the late fee rule from the lease
- [ ] How to send a maintenance request, and what to include
- [ ] Utility company names and phone numbers
- [ ] Trash, recycling and bulk pickup days
- [ ] Parking rules and guest parking
- [ ] Location of the water shutoff, breaker panel and filter sizes
- [ ] Notice rules for entry, from your lease and state law

Keep the packet on one page if you can. Send a PDF copy by email so the tenant can search it later.
