Kansas puts its deposit rules in one section: K.S.A. 58-2550. I read it on the Kansas Revisor of Statutes site on 2026-10-19. The history line shows the last change in 1997. It sets 3 caps and a 1.5x penalty.

| Rule | Limit or deadline | Statute |
|---|---|---|
| Unfurnished unit | Max 1 month's rent | K.S.A. 58-2550(a) |
| Furnished unit | Max 1.5 months' rent | K.S.A. 58-2550(a) |
| Pet deposit | Extra, max 0.5 month's rent | K.S.A. 58-2550(a) |
| Deductions | Accrued rent and damages, itemized in writing | K.S.A. 58-2550(b) |

| Separate account or interest | None required in this section | K.S.A. 58-2550 |

## What is the maximum security deposit a landlord can charge in Kansas?

The maximum Kansas deposit depends on furniture and pets. K.S.A. 58-2550(a) sets 3 limits:

- **Unfurnished:** no more than 1 month's periodic rent.
- **Furnished:** no more than 1.5 months' rent, if the tenant uses the landlord's furniture.
- **Pets:** an extra deposit of no more than half of 1 month's rent, if the lease allows pets.

| Monthly rent | Unfurnished | Furnished | Unfurnished + pet | Furnished + pet |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| $900 | $900 | $1,350 | $1,350 | $1,800 |
| $1,200 | $1,200 | $1,800 | $1,800 | $2,400 |
| $1,600 | $1,600 | $2,400 | $2,400 | $3,200 |

We did the math from the statute. Public housing authorities that set rent by income may use their own deposit schedule by bedroom size, with a payment plan (K.S.A. 58-2550(a)).

## How many days does a Kansas landlord have to return a security deposit?

A Kansas landlord has 2 clocks under K.S.A. 58-2550(b):

1. Return the balance "within 14 days after the determination of the amount" of deductions.
2. In no event later than "30 days after termination of the tenancy, delivery of possession and demand by the tenant."

The 30-day limit starts only when all 3 events have happened: the tenancy ended, the tenant gave back possession, and the tenant asked for the deposit.

If the tenant does not demand the deposit within 30 days after the tenancy ends, the landlord "shall mail that portion of the security deposit due the tenant to the tenant's last known address."

Worked timeline:

| Event | Date |
|---|---|
| Lease ends; tenant returns keys | May 31 |
| Tenant asks for deposit by email | June 2 |
| Landlord gets contractor quote, sets deductions | June 10 |
| 14-day limit from deductions | June 24 |
| 30-day outside limit | July 2 |
| Balance due by | June 24 |

## What can a landlord deduct from a security deposit in Kansas?

A Kansas landlord may apply the deposit to 2 things (K.S.A. 58-2550(b)):

- **Accrued rent** that is unpaid.
- **Damages** the landlord suffered from the tenant's failure to meet K.S.A. 58-2555 (tenant duties to keep the unit clean and safe) and the rental agreement.

The statute also mentions "expenses, damages or other legally allowable charges under the provisions of the rental agreement." Put any such charge, such as cleaning, in the lease in plain words.

K.S.A. 58-2555(f) makes the tenant responsible for "destruction, defacement, damage, impairment or removal" of any part of the unit caused by the tenant, a guest or a pet. Charge for that kind of harm, and keep proof of it.

## Does a Kansas landlord have to give an itemized list of deductions?

Yes. Deductions must be "itemized by the landlord in a written notice delivered to the tenant" (K.S.A. 58-2550(b)).

A sound notice lists:

- each deduction and its cost;
- unpaid rent by period;
- the total deducted;
- the balance returned with the notice.

Keep move-in and move-out photos and invoices. They back up each line if the tenant sues.

## Must a Kansas landlord pay interest or keep the deposit in a separate account?

K.S.A. 58-2550 does not require interest or a separate account. We found no such rule in the section.

Two related rules apply:

- **New owner.** The person who holds the landlord's interest when the tenancy ends is bound by the section (K.S.A. 58-2550(f)). A buyer takes on the deposits.
- **Unlawful lockout.** If a tenant ends the lease after a lockout or utility cutoff, the landlord must return the deposit amount due under 58-2550 (K.S.A. 58-2563).

Many managers still keep deposits in a separate trust account. It makes the 14-day refund easy to prove.

## What is the penalty for a Kansas landlord who keeps a deposit wrongly?

If the landlord fails to comply with subsection (b), the tenant may recover "that portion of the security deposit due together with damages in an amount equal to 1½ the amount wrongfully withheld" (K.S.A. 58-2550(c)).

Worked example: deposit $1,200, landlord keeps $700 with no itemized notice.

| Item | Amount |
|---|---|
| Deposit due back | $700 |
| Damages (1.5 x $700) | $1,050 |
| Total | $1,750 |

The tenant may also seek other damages the act allows (K.S.A. 58-2550(e)).

## Kansas deposit checklist

- Cap the deposit: 1 month unfurnished, 1.5 furnished, plus 0.5 for pets.
- Write any cleaning or fee charges in the lease.
- Take photos at move-in and move-out.
- Set deductions fast; pay the balance within 14 days of that.
- Never pass 30 days after move-out and the tenant's demand.
- Mail the balance to the last known address if the tenant never asks.
