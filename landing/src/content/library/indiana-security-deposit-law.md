## What is the maximum security deposit a landlord can charge in Indiana?

Indiana sets no maximum security deposit. We found no cap in IC 32-31-3.

Cities and counties cannot add one. IC 32-31-1-20(c)(2) bars a unit of local government from regulating "security deposits" unless the General Assembly authorizes it.

What counts as a deposit is broad. IC 32-31-3-9 includes 3 kinds of money. The first is a required prepayment of rent beyond the first full period. The second is rent above the average for the term. The third is any money returned when the unit comes back in good condition. So a prepaid last month's rent is a security deposit under the chapter.

## How many days does an Indiana landlord have to return a security deposit?

An Indiana landlord has 45 days. The landlord returns the deposit, minus lawful deductions, "all as itemized by the landlord with the amount due in a written notice that is delivered to the tenant not more than forty-five (45) days after termination of the rental agreement and delivery of possession" (IC 32-31-3-12(a)).

IC 32-31-3-14 sets a matching rule: the landlord mails the itemized list "not more than forty-five (45) days after the termination of occupancy."

One condition protects the landlord: "The landlord is not liable under this chapter until the tenant supplies the landlord in writing with a mailing address."

Worked timeline:

| Event | Date |
|---|---|
| Lease ends; tenant returns keys | July 31 |
| Tenant gives forwarding address in writing | August 2 |
| Last day to deliver notice and balance | September 14 |

Count 45 days from the later of the lease end and the return of possession. Send it sooner if you can.

## What can a landlord deduct from a security deposit in Indiana?

IC 32-31-3-13 says a deposit "may be used only" for 4 purposes:

1. **Damage:** actual damage to the unit or any ancillary facility "that are not the result of ordinary wear and tear."
2. **Rent:** all rent in arrears, and rent due for early termination by the tenant.
3. **Last period:** the last rent payment, if a written agreement says the deposit will serve as last month's rent.
4. **Utilities:** utility or sewer charges the landlord paid that the tenant owed under the lease and did not pay.

Ordinary wear and tear is not a deduction. A hole in the wall can be. Faded paint from normal use cannot.

Unless the parties agree otherwise, the tenant may not apply the deposit to rent (IC 32-31-3-12(a)).

## Does an Indiana landlord have to give an itemized list of deductions?

Yes. IC 32-31-3-14 requires "an itemized list of damages claimed." The list must set out:

- the estimated cost of repair for each damaged item, and
- the amounts and lease on which the landlord intends to assess the tenant.

Include "a check or money order for the difference between the damages claimed and the amount of the security deposit."

| Item | Estimated cost |
|---|---|
| Replace cracked bathroom sink | $260 |
| Patch and paint 4 wall holes | $180 |
| Unpaid water bill per lease | $64 |
| Total claimed | $504 |
| Check enclosed ($1,200 deposit) | $696 |

## Must an Indiana landlord pay interest or keep the deposit in a separate account?

IC 32-31-3 sets no interest rule and no separate account rule. We found none in the chapter's sections.

Two related rules:

- **The owner at move-out is bound.** "The owner of the dwelling unit at the time of the termination of the rental agreement is bound by this section" (IC 32-31-3-12(d)). A buyer inherits the duty.
- **No waiver.** "A waiver of this chapter by a landlord or tenant is void" (IC 32-31-3-17).

## What is the penalty for an Indiana landlord who keeps a deposit wrongly?

Indiana has 3 linked rules:

1. **No list means no damages.** Failure to send the notice of damages "constitutes agreement by the landlord that no damages are due," and the landlord "must remit to the tenant immediately the full security deposit" (IC 32-31-3-15).
2. **Liability.** A landlord who fails to comply with sections 14 and 15 "is liable to the tenant in an amount equal to the part of the deposit withheld" plus "reasonable attorney's fees and court costs" (IC 32-31-3-16).
3. **Section 12 remedy.** If the landlord fails to comply with section 12(a), the tenant may recover all of the deposit due and reasonable attorney fees (IC 32-31-3-12(b)).

Worked example: deposit $1,500, landlord keeps all of it and sends no list. The tenant recovers $1,500, plus attorney fees and court costs.

## Indiana deposit checklist

- Treat any prepaid last month's rent as part of the deposit.
- Ask for the tenant's forwarding address in writing.
- Deduct only for the 4 uses in IC 32-31-3-13.
- Mail an itemized list with repair costs within 45 days.
- Include a check or money order for the balance.
- Never deduct for ordinary wear and tear.
