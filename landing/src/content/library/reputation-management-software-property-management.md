Podium's top listed plan costs $599 a month. NiceJob's entry plan costs $75. Both send review requests by text. The gap is $6,288 a year, so the choice matters for a property manager with thin margins. This page compares the tools by their published prices and then asks the harder question: do you need one at all?

## What do tools like Birdeye and Podium do?

Review tools like Birdeye, Podium and NiceJob automate the ask, the tracking and the reply. They share a core and differ in add-ons.

| Tool | Listed price (read 2026-10-20) | Core review features | Extras on the page |
|---|---|---|---|
| NiceJob Starter | $75 a month, no contract | SMS and email review requests, website widgets, social sharing, team leaderboard, reviews microsite | Free to start |
| NiceJob Pro | $125 a month | Starter plus AI replies to reviews, referrals, NPS surveys, competitor tracking | Sites add-on $99 a month + $199 setup |
| Podium Core | $399 a month | Reviews, texting and webchat, per the page | Talk to sales |
| Podium Pro | $599 a month | More features than Core | Talk to sales |
| Podium Signature | Custom | Not listed | Talk to sales |
| Birdeye | Quote only, priced by number of locations (1–3, 4–9, 10–39 and up) | Reviews, social, AI tools | "Get pricing" form |

Read the feature lists with care. Much of each plan is not about reviews: messaging, webchat, booking or AI agents. If you already have those in AppFolio, Buildium or Rentvine, you pay twice.

## Do property management software platforms include review tools?

Some property management marketing packages include review tools. We checked two vendors that sell only to property managers:

- **Fourandhalf Launch:** $600 setup and $349 a month. It lists a "Reputation Management Platform" with email review request campaigns, SMS review requests, survey tools, a review monitoring and response dashboard, and a website review widget. It also includes a monthly blog post.
- **Property Manager Websites:** plans at $145, $795 and $1,995 a month list "Reputation management" among the services, with a website and rental analysis tools.

For the main software platforms, check your own contract. Ask your AppFolio, Buildium or Rentvine rep if review requests are part of your plan or an add-on. We did not find a public price page for that.

### Worked example: payback

You charge 8% of rent. The average rent is $1,700. One door earns you $136 a month, or $1,632 a year.

- NiceJob Starter ($900) pays back with 1 new door kept for about 7 months.
- Podium Core ($4,788) needs about 3 doors for a full year.

The tool does not sign owners. Reviews help an owner pick you from the Map Pack. Google's local ranking help page lists review count and ratings under prominence. Our [fee calculator](/tools/property-management-fee-calculator) helps you set the per-door number.

## What review rules apply to property managers?

Two rule sets apply to every tool you buy. A tool that breaks them puts your profile at risk.

1. **Google bans review gating.** Google's fake engagement policy says businesses must not "discourage or prohibit negative reviews, or selectively solicit positive reviews from customers." It also bans incentives such as payment or discounts for reviews.
2. **The FTC bans review suppression.** The FTC Rule on the Use of Consumer Reviews and Testimonials took effect on 2024-10-21. Courts can impose civil penalties for knowing violations. The rule covers fake reviews (section 465.2), incentives tied to a sentiment (465.4) and review suppression (465.7).

So turn off any "smart routing" feature that sends 4–5 star clients to Google and 1–3 star clients to a private form. Ask every owner and tenant the same way.

## Do I need a review tool below 200 doors?

Often not. At 200 doors or fewer, a simple process does the same job for free. Example: 120 doors and 70 owners. You send 15 owner requests and 20 tenant requests a month. That is about 1 hour of work.

Free process checklist:

- [ ] Get your Google review link from your Business Profile
- [ ] Write one owner and one tenant request (2–3 lines, no incentive)
- [ ] Send after real moments: a lease signed, a repair closed, a year-end statement
- [ ] Ask everyone, not only happy clients
- [ ] Reply to every review within 2 business days
- [ ] Count new reviews each month in a spreadsheet

Buy a tool when one of these is true: you send 100+ requests a month, you run 3+ offices, you miss replies, or you need reviews from Yelp and Google in one place. AI answers cite review sites often.
