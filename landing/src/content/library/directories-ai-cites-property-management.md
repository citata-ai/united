## Which directories do AI assistants cite most for property managers?

Expertise.com is the directory that AI engines cite most for property managers in our data. On 2026-10-19 we asked the same question in 50 large US cities: "What is the best property management company in <City, ST>? Give names." We sent it to 3 engines, with live web search on: the OpenAI API (the ChatGPT model family), the Gemini API with Google Search grounding, and the Claude API. That gave 150 answers (our data, 2026-10-19).

Then we counted how many answers linked each site as a source.

| Site | Answers (of 150) | ChatGPT engine | Claude engine | Gemini engine | Cities (of 50) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| expertise.com | 93 | 30 | 47 | 16 | 49 |
| yelp.com | 50 | 0 | 50 | 0 | 50 |
| ipropertymanagement.com | 50 | 1 | 42 | 7 | 42 |
| reddit.com (forum) | 22 | 0 | 0 | 22 | 22 |

Source: our data, 2026-10-19. One prompt, one run, one day. API answers are close to the consumer apps, but not the same.

## How often does each engine cite each directory?

Each engine uses a different set of directories, so one profile does not cover all 3. The pattern in our data:

- **ChatGPT engine:** Expertise.com (30 answers), Birdeye reviews (17), Bestinhood (12) and BBB (11). It linked Yelp in 0 of 50 answers.
- **Claude engine:** Yelp (50 of 50), Expertise.com (47) and iPropertyManagement (42). It linked the most sources per answer, 7.6 on average.
- **Gemini engine:** the Mynd company site and Reddit (22 answers each), then Expertise.com (16) and ThreeBestRated (9). It linked a company site in 48 of 50 answers.

Watch for: the Yelp result is about the API engines. Yelp signed a data deal with OpenAI in 2026, so the ChatGPT app may show Yelp data in ways our API test does not catch.

## Do directories beat company websites in AI answers?

Directories beat company websites in the ChatGPT engine, and company websites lead in the Gemini engine. Across all 150 answers, 88% linked at least one listing or review site, and 71% linked at least one company site (our data, 2026-10-19).

| Engine | Answers with a listing site | Answers with a company site | Answers with listing sites only |
|---|---|---|---|
| ChatGPT | 48 of 50 | 15 of 50 | 34 |
| Claude | 50 of 50 | 43 of 50 | 7 |
| Gemini | 34 of 50 | 48 of 50 | 2 |

A larger study points the same way. Yext looked at 6.8 million AI citations from July 1 to August 31, 2025. Listings were 48.7% of OpenAI citations, and websites were 52.1% of Gemini citations (Yext, 2025). Two different methods, one shape: ChatGPT leans on lists, Gemini leans on your own site.

What this means for you: a strong website alone may not get you into a ChatGPT answer. A good directory profile alone may not get you into a Gemini answer. You need both.

## Which property management directories are free to join?

Yelp and Google Business Profile are free to claim. Expertise.com is not something you join: it picks companies. The table shows what we could confirm on each site's own pages.

| Directory | How you get on it | Cost to be listed |
|---|---|---|
| Google Business Profile | You create or claim it | Free (Google) |
| Yelp | You claim the page | $0 (Yelp) |
| Expertise.com | Editorial pick after research and a manual review | No fee to be picked; ads are separate |
| iPropertyManagement | Editorial lists | Not checked |
| BBB | Business profile; accreditation is a separate program | Not checked |
| ThreeBestRated, Bestinhood, TrustAnalytica | Editorial "best of" lists | Not checked |

Expertise.com says a company that passed its review "may choose to advertise on the page." It also says review requests are open only in its Legal and Home Improvement categories. So for property management, you cannot apply. You can only become a strong candidate.

## Which directory should a property manager fix first?

Fix the 3 sites with the widest reach first: Expertise.com, Yelp and Google Business Profile. Then work down the list for your own city. Here is the order we use.

1. **Run the prompt for your city.** Ask "What is the best property management company in <your city>? Give names." in ChatGPT, Gemini and Claude. Write down every source link.
2. **Check Expertise.com.** Open the property management page for your city. If you are listed, check your name, address and services. If not, see what the listed companies show that you do not.
3. **Claim your Yelp page.** Yelp lets you update your phone, address, website and hours. Use the same name and address as on your site.
4. **Complete your Google Business Profile.** Google says the free profile controls how you show on Google Search and Maps.
5. **Check iPropertyManagement, BBB and ThreeBestRated.** Look for your city page and fix wrong facts.
6. **Match every listing to your website.** Same company name, same address, same phone. AI engines join facts from many sites, and a mismatch can split you into 2 companies.
7. **Run the prompt again in 4 to 6 weeks.** Compare the source list to your first run. Our method for that is in [How to Track AI Traffic and AI Mentions](/guides/track-ai-traffic-property-management).

Watch for: none of these steps promises a citation. AI answers change from run to run. We report what happened in our test, not a rule.

## What our data does not show

Our data shows which sites AI linked, not why it picked them. We ran 1 prompt per city, on 1 day, through APIs. Small cities and other prompts ("Section 8 property manager", "cheapest property manager") may give other sources. The cause of any pattern here is a hypothesis.
