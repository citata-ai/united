## Do I need to register with the housing authority before I list?

No federal rule makes you register before you list. The process starts when a family with a voucher wants your unit. Under 24 CFR 982.302, the family sends the PHA a request for approval of the tenancy and a copy of the lease. It must do so during the term of its voucher. The PHA sets the form and the method.

Some PHAs help you find voucher holders. The Santa Cruz County housing authority suggests a free listing on AffordableHousing.com. It also says you can advertise in the same places you use for other tenants.

Prepare the owner papers before the first RFTA arrives. They are the most common cause of delay.

| Document | Why the PHA asks | Example source |
|---|---|---|
| IRS Form W-9 | The PHA reports payments on a 1099 | Tacoma, Maricopa |
| Proof of ownership | HUD rules require the PHA to verify the owner | Tacoma (deed, loan papers) |
| Direct deposit form and voided check | Payments go by direct deposit | Maricopa (direct deposit only) |
| Management agreement | Needed if a manager collects the rent | Tacoma, Maricopa |
| Blank copy of your lease | The lease must include the HUD tenancy addendum | Maricopa |

Tacoma Housing Authority says to give the SSN or TIN that matches the ownership records "to prevent delay".

## What is the Request for Tenancy Approval (RFTA)?

The RFTA is the form that tells the PHA about your unit. The federal form is HUD-52517. The Maricopa County packet includes the 04/2023 edition. It asks for the address, the bedroom count, the year built, the proposed rent, the deposit and the date the unit is ready for inspection. It also asks which utilities and appliances the owner pays for.

You also certify three things on the form:

1. **Rent.** The rent is not more than you charge for comparable unassisted units.
2. **Family ties.** You are not the parent, child, grandparent, grandchild, sister or brother of a family member. The PHA can approve an exception as a reasonable accommodation.
3. **Lead paint.** You state whether lead-based paint disclosure applies, for units built before 1978.

## Who screens the tenant, me or the PHA?

You screen the tenant. 24 CFR 982.307 makes the owner responsible for screening and selection. The PHA has no liability for the family's behavior. The RFTA form says the same thing in item 13.

The PHA checks program eligibility only. The Santa Cruz County housing authority lists what it checks: income, immigration status, sex offender registration and some violent or drug-related crimes. It tells owners to run "all reference, background and credit checks" they use for other tenants.

The PHA must give you the family's current and prior address (24 CFR 982.307(b)). It must also give the names and addresses of the current and prior landlords, if it has them.

## When do I sign the HAP contract and the lease?

You sign the lease after the PHA approves the tenancy, and the HAP contract (Housing Assistance Payments contract) right after. Under 24 CFR 982.305, the PHA cannot approve the tenancy until four checks pass:

- [ ] The unit is eligible.
- [ ] The unit passes inspection.
- [ ] The lease includes the HUD tenancy addendum.
- [ ] The rent is reasonable compared with similar unassisted units.

There is one more check at first move-in. If the gross rent is above the payment standard, the family share cannot be more than 40% of monthly adjusted income. Our [Section 8 rent calculator](/tools/section-8-rent-calculator) runs this 40% check.

Here is the full sequence, with the timelines two PHAs publish:

| Step | Who acts | Timeline (PHA source) |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Screen and pick the family | Owner | Your own process |
| 2. Fill out and return the RFTA packet | Owner and family | Incomplete packets delay it (Maricopa) |
| 3. RFTA review: rent, 40% check, jurisdiction | PHA | Within 14 days of a complete packet (Maricopa) |
| 4. Inspection | PHA inspector | Scheduling up to 24 days (Maricopa); about 5 business days if ready (Tacoma) |
| 5. Sign the lease with the tenancy addendum | Owner and family | After the unit passes (Tacoma) |
| 6. Sign the HAP contract | Owner and PHA | No later than 60 days from lease start (24 CFR 982.305(c)) |
| 7. First payment | PHA | Up to 60 days (Maricopa, Tacoma) |

The federal rule protects you on timing. If the PHA signs the HAP contract within 60 days of the lease start, it pays back to that start (24 CFR 982.305(c)(3)). The PHA cannot pay anything before the HAP contract is signed.

Do not let the family move in early. The Santa Cruz County housing authority warns that a tenant who moves in before the unit passes pays the entire rent until it passes.

## How do I find the PHA that covers my property?

Find the PHA by the unit address, not by your own address. Some areas have more than one PHA. The Housing Authority of Maricopa County, for example, does not serve Phoenix, Scottsdale, Chandler or Tempe. It shares parts of Mesa and Glendale with those cities' own housing authorities. It publishes an online map so owners can check an address.

Use these steps:

1. **Ask the family.** The voucher names the PHA that issued it.
2. **Check the PHA's map or city list.** Confirm that your address is inside its area.
3. **Read its landlord page.** Look for the RFTA packet, the payment standards and the inspection checklist.
4. **Save the contact.** Note the landlord liaison or the housing specialist named on the RFTA.

Before you set the rent, look at the local limit. Our [Section 8 rent limits guide](/guides/section-8-rent-limits) lists HUD's FY2027 Fair Market Rents for the 50 largest metros. Each PHA sets its payment standard from those numbers.
