## Which rent payment method is best?

ACH autopay through a rent portal is the best default for most landlords. It costs little, it runs on the same day each month, and each payment leaves a record. Here is how the 5 common methods compare.

| Method | Typical cost | Speed to you | Can the payer reverse it? | Records |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| ACH via portal | $0 to $2.50 per payment (see below) | Up to 5 business days (Zillow) | Yes, unauthorized or wrong-terms claims within 60 days (Nacha) | Strong |
| Credit or debit card via portal | 2.95% to 3.5% to payer, or $9.95 for debit (see below) | Same as the portal | Yes, card billing disputes | Strong |
| Zelle | Typically no fee (Zelle) | Minutes, if enrolled | No, once sent to an enrolled user | Weak: bank memo only |
| Paper check | Your time and bank deposit | Days, plus mail time | Yes, stop payment or bounce | Medium |
| Cash | None | Instant | No | Weak, unless you give a receipt |

Sources: the Zillow, Buildium, Avail, Nacha and Zelle pages linked below.

## What are the fees for each method?

Fees depend on the tool and on who pays them. Here is what 3 rent tools list on their own pages.

| Tool | ACH (bank) | Card |
|---|---|---|
| Zillow Rental Manager | Free for landlord and tenant | Tenant pays 2.95% (credit) or $9.95 (debit) |
| Avail, free plan | Tenant pays $2.50 per transfer | 3.5% added to tenant payment |
| Avail, Unlimited Plus ($9 per unit) | No fee for bank payments | 3.5% added to tenant payment |
| Buildium Essential | $2.35 per incoming EFT | 2.99% per transaction |
| Buildium Growth | $1.35 per incoming EFT | 2.99% per transaction |
| Buildium Premium | First 12 months free, then $0.60 | 2.99% per transaction |

**Worked example, 20 doors, ACH only, Buildium Essential:**

- 20 payments a month × $2.35 = $47 a month.
- $47 × 12 = $564 a year in incoming EFT fees.
- The same 20 doors on Buildium Premium after year 1: 20 × $0.60 × 12 = $144 a year.

Watch for: before you pass any payment fee to tenants, check your state law and your lease. A fee the lease does not name is hard to collect.

## How fast does each method settle?

Zelle is fastest, and ACH through a portal is the slowest of the online methods. Zelle says money reaches an enrolled recipient "within minutes." Zillow says rent can take up to 5 business days to reach your account, depending on how the renter pays.

Set your rent due date with this lag in mind. If your mortgage is due on the 1st, ask tenants to start payment a few days before, or set autopay to run early.

## Which methods can a tenant reverse?

ACH debits, cards and checks can all come back. Zelle and cash cannot.

- **ACH:** under Nacha's rules, a consumer can return a debit as unauthorized (code R10) or as not matching the authorization (code R11). Nacha states the R11 return timeframe is 60 days, the same as for R10.
- **Cards:** the federal Fair Credit Billing Act, 15 U.S.C. 1666, lets a cardholder send a written billing error notice within 60 days after the statement.
- **Checks:** a check can bounce or be stopped.
- **Zelle:** Zelle says payments to an enrolled user "cannot be reversed." A sender can cancel only if the recipient has not enrolled yet.

What to do:

1. **Keep a signed autopay authorization** for every ACH tenant. It is your proof against an unauthorized claim.
2. **Set a rule for bounced payments.** Virginia's statute, for example, lets a landlord demand cash, a cashier's check, a certified check or a completed electronic transfer after an insufficient funds payment.
3. **Do not count a payment as final** until the return window is past, for move-out balances.

## Should I accept cash?

Accept cash only if a tenant has no other way to pay, and always give a receipt. Cash leaves no bank trail, and holding it is a safety risk for you and your staff.

Some states make the receipt a legal duty. Minnesota's statute 504B.118 says a landlord who gets cash must give a written receipt "immediately" if paid in person, or within 3 business days if not.

A cash receipt should show:

- Date and amount.
- Unit address.
- Rent period covered.
- Name and signature of the person who took the money.

## A rent collection policy you can copy

Put these rules in the lease and in your move-in letter:

1. **Preferred method:** ACH autopay through our portal, on the 1st.
2. **Other methods:** card through the portal (the card fee is the tenant's), or certified check.
3. **Not accepted:** cash, unless agreed in writing, with a receipt.
4. **Returned payments:** after one returned payment, we may require certified funds.
5. **Due date and grace period:** state them, plus any late fee your state allows.
