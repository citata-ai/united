Goodjuju studied 35 property management websites from July 8 to October 5, 2026. The average site turned 0.9% of visitors into owner form leads. Google Search visitors converted at 1.2%, and visitors from AI tools at 3.9% (Goodjuju, 2026). At 0.9%, a site with 1,000 visits a month brings about 9 owner leads. Each point you add to that rate is real money.

The landing page is where that rate is won or lost.

## What are the parts of a strong owner landing page?

A strong owner landing page has 8 blocks, in this order. Each block answers one question the owner has before they fill in the form.

| # | Block | Owner question |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Headline + offer + form | Is this for me, and what do I get? |
| 2 | Proof bar | Can I trust you? |
| 3 | What we do | What will you handle? |
| 4 | Fee table | What will it cost? |
| 5 | How it works, 3–4 steps | What happens after I submit? |
| 6 | Guarantees and cancel terms | What if it goes wrong? |
| 7 | Local facts | Do you know my area? |
| 8 | FAQ + second form | What about my special case? |

## The template

Copy the outline and fill in each bracket.

```text
[BLOCK 1 — ABOVE THE FOLD]
Headline: Property Management in [City] for [owner goal, e.g. hands-off rental income]
Subhead:  Get a free rent estimate for your [City] rental in [1 business day].
Form:     Property address · Name · Email · Phone · How did you first hear about us?
Button:   Get my free rent estimate

[BLOCK 2 — PROOF BAR]
[Rating] stars from [count] Google reviews · [number] doors managed in [City] · Since [year]

[BLOCK 3 — WHAT WE DO]
Marketing and showings · Tenant screening · Rent collection · Repairs and 24/7 emergencies
Inspections every [x] months · Monthly statements by the [day] · Owner portal

[BLOCK 4 — FEES]
Monthly fee [x]% · Leasing fee [amount] · Renewal fee [amount] · Setup fee [amount or none]
Example: on $[rent] rent, you pay $[amount] a month.

[BLOCK 5 — HOW IT WORKS]
1. Send your address. 2. Get your rent estimate in [1 business day].
3. 15-minute call. 4. We list your home within [x] days of signing.

[BLOCK 6 — GUARANTEES]
[Guarantee]: [promise, number, condition, remedy]
Cancel: [notice], [fee or no fee]

[BLOCK 7 — LOCAL FACTS]
Typical rent for a 3-bed in [City]: $[range] ([source, date])
Average days to lease in our portfolio: [days] ([period])
Local rule owners ask about: [deposit limit, rental license, notice period, with source]

[BLOCK 8 — FAQ + FORM]
5–6 questions from your owner calls, each with a 1–3 sentence answer.
Repeat the form.
```

## How long should the page be?

The page should be long enough to answer the questions that stop an owner from calling. For most owner pages, that is 600 to 1,200 words. That is our working range, not a study result. Google's guidance says page length alone does not matter for ranking. What matters is whether the reader leaves with enough to reach their goal (Google Search Central).

Two rules keep a long page usable:

- **Form near the top.** The owner who is ready should not need to scroll.
- **Second form at the bottom.** The owner who read every block is ready too.

## Which offer should the page lead with?

Lead with a free rent estimate or rental analysis for the owner's address. It gives the owner something useful before they talk to you, and the address tells you if the home fits your service area.

| Offer | Strength | Weakness |
|---|---|---|
| Free rent estimate | Clear value, easy yes, gets the address | Needs fast delivery |
| Free consultation | Personal | Feels like a sales call |
| Fee sheet download | Good for price shoppers | Little info about the property |
| Rent-vs-sell analysis | Strong for accidental landlords | More work per lead |

Deliver the estimate within 1 business day. A slow estimate wastes the offer.

## How do I add proof?

Add proof near the top, in numbers. Owners compare you with 2 or 3 other managers in one sitting.

- **Reviews.** In BrightLocal's 2026 survey, 47% of consumers will not use a business with fewer than 20 reviews. 74% only care about reviews from the last 3 months (BrightLocal, 2026). Show the count, the rating and 2 recent owner reviews.
- **Doors and years.** "312 doors in [City] since 2014" beats "trusted local experts".
- **A named person.** A photo and name of the owner contact.

## How do I test changes?

Test one change at a time and judge it by owner leads, not clicks. Small pages need patience. At a 1% rate, 2,000 visits bring about 20 owner leads. A test with fewer leads than that per version is mostly noise.

Test plan:

| Order | Change to test | Why first |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Offer: rent estimate vs consultation | Biggest effect on form starts |
| 2 | Headline with city and goal | First thing every visitor reads |
| 3 | Fee table visible vs "contact us" | Filters price shoppers, builds trust |
| 4 | Form fields: 4 vs 7 | Each field adds effort |
| 5 | Proof bar placement | Trust before the form |

Track every lead by source. Goodjuju recommends a required "How did you first hear about us?" field. It also suggests hidden fields for the referrer and UTM tags, and a separate owner thank-you page (Goodjuju, 2026). If you also run Google Ads, the landing page affects your Quality Score. Google rates landing page experience as "Above average", "Average" or "Below average", based on how relevant and useful the page is to people who click your ad (Google Ads Help).
