## What do AppFolio and Yardi Breeze cost?

Yardi Breeze gives a clear residential minimum; AppFolio publishes starting rates with extra conditions. Start with the feature and price table.

| Product | Published monthly price | Minimum | Feature or scope |
|---|---|---|---|
| AppFolio Core | From $1.49 per unit | 50 units and undisclosed minimum spend | Accounting, leasing and work orders |
| AppFolio Plus | From $3.20 per unit | Unit and spend thresholds quote only | Advanced accounting and read-only API |
| Yardi Breeze residential | $1 per unit | $100 monthly; annual agreement | Residential operations and accounting |
| Yardi Breeze commercial | $2 per unit | $200 monthly; annual agreement | Commercial portfolio offer |
| Yardi Breeze Premier residential | $1 per unit, terms apply | $400 monthly; annual agreement | Expanded edition |

Sources: [AppFolio pricing](https://www.appfolio.com/pricing) and [Yardi Breeze pricing](https://www.yardibreeze.com/pricing/), read 2026-10-19. Ask Yardi to confirm Premier's offer conditions and feature scope.

A starting rate is not a full account bill. Add payments, conversion work and any required services. Use the same billing term when you compare offers.

## How do minimums affect a midsize company?

Minimums can determine the bill before the advertised unit rate matters. Distinguish eligibility from spending.

AppFolio Core states a 50-unit threshold. It also states a minimum spend without showing the amount. You cannot derive the actual bill from the unit rate alone.

Yardi's [FAQ](https://www.yardibreeze.com/faq/) says no minimum unit count applies. It gives separate monthly spending floors for residential, commercial or mixed portfolios, and Premier.

For a worked residential example, Yardi's published formula produces $100 monthly at 50 units. It also produces $100 at 100 units and $200 at 200 units. These calculations use Yardi's rate and minimum, not transaction fees.

At the same assumed sizes, AppFolio Core's unit-rate arithmetic stays subject to its spending floor. Ask AppFolio for the account bill rather than comparing a conditional calculation with Yardi's stated minimum.

Use your smallest likely signed portfolio as well as your growth case. That reveals the cost when an owner leaves.

## What is the difference between Breeze and Breeze Premier?

Breeze Premier is a separate edition with broader capabilities and property-type coverage. Do not treat Premier as a renamed basic plan.

Yardi's FAQ says Breeze serves residential and commercial portfolios in the United States. It says Premier also covers affordable housing, associations, manufactured housing and self storage.

Those categories do not establish the exact modules your account includes. Ask for a feature sheet tied to the offered edition. Check whether the functions you saw in a demo require Premier.

Make a plan-scope register:

- **Accounting:** required reports, approvals and close process.
- **Leasing:** applications, signatures and renewals.
- **Owners:** statements, payments and portal functions.
- **Properties:** each asset type you manage.
- **Connections:** required outside tools and data transfer.
- **Support:** channels and setup work.

Ask the vendor to mark included, optional and unavailable work. Keep that record with the contract.

A broader edition makes sense only when your team needs the added functions. Test them before accepting the higher minimum.

## Which vendor has better support?

The public pages cannot establish a support-quality winner. Compare the support you can access under the exact plan.

Yardi's pricing page states no training or support fees. That does not prove every difficult conversion task falls within normal support. Ask where included setup ends and specialist work begins.

AppFolio lists standard support in Core and enhanced support in Plus. Max adds dedicated customer success management. Ask which channel handles a blocked payment or close.

Give sales a realistic support scenario. Ask what the user sends, who responds and how an unresolved case moves upward. Request the support hours and any contractual response target.

If possible, have the staff member who will need help review a support article. Can that person follow the steps without outside explanation?

Treat awards and testimonials as marketing evidence, not a substitute for an operating test. Your staff need a clear path when the normal process fails.

## Which handles commercial units?

Yardi explicitly offers Breeze for commercial portfolios; AppFolio also lists commercial property among its solutions. You still need a commercial-specific demo and quote.

The AppFolio rate table above reflects its residential offer. Do not use those rates as a confirmed commercial price. Ask AppFolio to price the actual asset mix.

For either product, bring a commercial lease with your real charge structure. Test recurring increases, recoverable expenses, deposits and reporting. If you need common-area cost reconciliation, request a full example.

For a mixed portfolio, test a residential owner and commercial owner separately. Then check consolidated reporting. A product category label does not prove the report meets your agreement.

Ask whether property type changes the monthly minimum. Yardi's FAQ gives a separate floor for commercial or mixed portfolios.

Have your accountant review the resulting postings. A successful charge entry is not the same as a complete lease-accounting workflow.

## Which system is easier to learn?

The easier system is the one your team can use correctly with the training you can support. The public pages do not establish a universal winner.

Run an observed task session with daily users. Let them enter a bill, find an unpaid balance and answer an owner question. Record where they stop.

Ask the vendor to show a correction as well as a successful entry. Staff need to recover from an error without hiding its history. Test a new user's permissions before payment access begins.
