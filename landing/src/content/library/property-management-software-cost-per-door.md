## Which vendors charge per unit and which use starting tiers?

AppFolio, Yardi Breeze, Propertyware, Rentvine and DoorLoop publish unit-based prices; Buildium publishes starting monthly plan prices. Compare the terms before dividing by doors.

| Product | Published base | Price condition |
|---|---|---|
| AppFolio Core | From $1.49 per unit monthly | Spend minimum and 50-unit minimum |
| Yardi Breeze residential | $1 per unit monthly | $100 monthly minimum; annual agreement |
| Propertyware Basic | $1 per unit monthly | $250 monthly minimum |
| Rentvine | As low as $1.50 per active unit | Full quote required |
| Buildium Essential | From $62 monthly | Exact unit count needs pricing |
| DoorLoop One | $2.50 per unit monthly displayed | $199 monthly minimum displayed |

Sources: the vendors' [AppFolio](https://www.appfolio.com/pricing), [Yardi](https://www.yardibreeze.com/pricing/), [Propertyware](https://www.propertyware.com/pricing/), [Rentvine](https://www.rentvine.com/pricing), [Buildium](https://www.buildium.com/pricing/) and [DoorLoop](https://www.doorloop.com/pricing) pricing pages, read 2026-10-19.

DoorLoop offers monthly and annual billing. Confirm the term behind its displayed offer. Keep every quote on the same basis.

## How do monthly minimums change the effective price?

A monthly minimum raises the effective per-door cost when the rate calculation falls below the minimum. It is a spending floor, not necessarily a unit requirement.

Yardi's residential pricing makes the effect clear:

| Doors in this worked calculation | Unit-rate amount | Base after minimum | Base per door |
|---|---|---|---|
| 50 | $50 | $100 | $2 |
| 100 | $100 | $100 | $1 |
| 200 | $200 | $200 | $1 |

These calculations use Yardi Breeze's published $1 rate and $100 minimum, read 2026-10-19. They exclude payments and other costs.

AppFolio names a minimum spend without showing its amount. Do not calculate its actual bill from the unit rate alone. Rentvine's as-low-as wording also leaves your applicable rate unresolved.

Ask what happens when an owner removes properties. Check whether the bill follows active doors immediately or at renewal. Your lowest expected door count deserves a budget alongside your growth case.

## What setup and onboarding fees apply?

Setup fees depend on the product and plan, so request an itemized first invoice. Separate data conversion from training, account setup and optional help.

Propertyware's pricing page states an implementation fee equal to twice the monthly subscription. Its Basic monthly minimum is $250. At that floor, the stated implementation formula produces $500 before other charges.

Buildium says new Growth and Premium customers must use its onboarding service. The pricing page does not publish the onboarding amount. Treat that price as quote only.

Rentvine describes a one-time setup fee for migration and specialist support. Its pricing page does not state the amount. DoorLoop says onboarding depends on portfolio size.

Yardi Breeze's pricing page states no onboarding, training or support fees. Confirm the scope of included work. A clean contact import and a difficult historical conversion are different requests.

Ask who cleans source data and who checks opening balances. Unpriced staff work can still make a low-cost move expensive.

## Which add-ons raise the bill?

Payments, signatures, screening, integrations and outside services can raise the bill. Count the activities your company actually performs.

Buildium's pricing page lists incoming electronic funds transfer fees of $2.35 on Essential and $1.35 on Growth. EFT means a bank transfer. Those fees matter when payment volume is high.

A worked example assumes 100 incoming transfers monthly. The listed fees produce $235 on Essential and $135 on Growth before subscription costs. These are calculations, not estimates of your payment volume.

Ask who pays each fee. Keep tenant-paid fees in a separate column. Do not count them as your expense unless you absorb them.

Use this review list:

- **Banking:** account setup, transfers, returns and mailed checks.
- **Leasing:** applications, screening, signatures and showing tools.
- **Work orders:** contact centers, inspection tools and vendor payments.
- **Connections:** API access, partner subscriptions and setup.
- **Support:** training, conversion, priority help and outside bookkeeping.

Check renewal terms for promotional waivers. Your normal-year budget should show what happens after the offer ends.

## How do you calculate total yearly cost?

Calculate the recurring bill first, then add setup and any overlap during the move. Keep assumptions visible so another person can change them.

Use this worksheet:

| Budget row | Input |
|---|---|
| Base subscription | Actual quote after minimums |
| Required add-ons | Contracted recurring amount |
| Company-paid usage | Expected activity times fee |
| Recurring yearly cost | Monthly total times months used |
| First-year additions | Setup, migration and training |
| Transition additions | Old-system overlap and staff time |

A calendar-year comparison uses 12 months as a planning assumption. It is not a vendor promise about your contract. Match your calculation to the actual term.

Do not count the same service twice. A higher plan may include a function you currently buy elsewhere. Remove that outside cost only after testing the replacement.

Also record what the budget excludes. Taxes, card choices, added doors and unexpected cleanup can change the final amount. Mark each unknown as a question for the vendor.

## How should you use cost per door in a buying decision?

Use cost per door to compare complete offers, then test whether the cheaper offer supports your work. A low price has little value if staff cannot explain owner balances.

Keep a current-size case and a growth case. Use signed doors for today's bill. Treat added doors as your forecast, with the assumption stated.

Ask the accountant to approve financial functions. Ask the team lead to approve daily tasks. A plan upgrade may solve a problem more cheaply than a system change.

Read the [AppFolio and Buildium comparison](/guides/appfolio-vs-buildium) for more plan questions. Then use the [management fee calculator](/tools/property-management-fee-calculator) to connect software costs with your service margin.

Request the first invoice, normal invoice and renewal terms before signing. That gives you a price you can use, rather than a rate that only looks low.
