## What should a rent receipt show?

A rent receipt shows who paid, how much, for which unit and period, and who took the money. New York's Real Property Law 235-e lists the core fields for a required receipt. It names the date, the amount, the premises, the period the payment covers, and the signature and title of the person who received the rent.

Those fields make a good base in any state. Add the payment method, the tenant's name and the balance due, and the receipt answers most later questions.

| Field | Why it matters |
|-------|----------------|
| Receipt number | Lets you find the record fast |
| Date paid | Proves the payment was on time or late |
| Amount paid | The core fact of the receipt |
| Payment method | Cash, money order, check number, or online ID |
| Property address and unit | Ties the money to one lease |
| Rent period covered | Shows which month the money pays |
| Tenant name | Names the payer under the lease |
| Balance due | Shows if the period is fully paid |
| Received by: name and title | Names the landlord or agent |
| Signature | Required in New York for receipts under 235-e |

## Must a landlord give rent receipts?

Whether a landlord must give rent receipts depends on the state and on how the tenant pays. There is no single federal rule. Two state examples show the range.

**New York.** Real Property Law 235-e requires a written receipt when rent is paid in cash or by any instrument other than the tenant's personal check. A tenant who pays by personal check can ask for receipts in writing. The landlord must then give receipts for the rest of the tenancy, unless the tenant says otherwise. The landlord must keep records of cash receipts for at least 3 years.

**Minnesota.** Statutes 504B.118 requires a written receipt for rent or other payments in cash. The landlord must give the receipt at once if the tenant pays in person. If the cash payment is not in person, the deadline is 3 business days. The Revisor's page notes a 2026 amendment to this section, so read the current text.

Other states and cities have their own rules. This is general information, not legal advice. Check the current law or ask a local attorney.

## What details go on a receipt for cash rent?

A receipt for cash rent needs every field in the template, plus a clear timing record. Cash leaves no bank trail. The receipt is the only proof for both sides.

Checklist for cash rent:

- **Write the exact time and date.** New York requires a receipt at once when rent is paid in person, and within 15 days when it is paid another way.
- **Count the cash with the tenant present.** Write the total in digits and in words, for example "$1,250 (one thousand two hundred fifty dollars)".
- **Sign and print your title.** New York requires the signature and title of the person who took the rent.
- **Give the original to the tenant.** Keep a copy, a carbon, or a photo.
- **Log the payment the same day.** Enter it in your ledger or software.
- **Deposit it fast.** If a property manager holds the cash, it belongs in the trust account, not in an operating account.

## How do partial payment receipts work?

A partial payment receipt records the amount paid and the balance still owed for the same period. It must not say "paid in full". Use the "Partial payment" box in the template and fill in the balance.

Worked example:

| Line | Amount |
|------|--------|
| Rent due for November | $1,400 |
| Late fee charged under the lease | $50 |
| Total due | $1,450 |
| Paid on November 6 (cash) | $900 |
| Balance due | $550 |

The receipt should say: "Received $900 toward November rent and fees. Balance due: $550 by November 20." Write which charge the payment pays first, if your lease sets an order.

Watch for: some states and leases treat a partial payment as a factor in an eviction case. Before you take a partial payment from a tenant who has an eviction notice, ask a local attorney.

## Rent receipts in property management software

Most property management software creates a receipt for each online payment and emails it to the tenant. That covers card and bank payments. Cash and money orders still need a manual entry, so a team needs a written step for them.

What to do:

1. **Pick one method for cash.** A receipt book with numbered pages, or a receipt from your software at the counter.
2. **Match receipts to deposits each week.** Every cash receipt should match a bank deposit.
3. **Keep the records for at least 3 years.** That is New York's minimum for cash receipts. Tax rules can need longer.
