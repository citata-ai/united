On 2026-10-19 we asked ChatGPT, Claude and Gemini 50 questions like "best property manager in [city]". We got 150 answers and counted every source they named. Yelp came up in 50. Facebook did not make the top 30.

## Which review sites do AI answers cite for property managers?

AI answers about property managers cite Yelp most often among classic review sites. Directories that rank local firms come up even more. Here is the count from our 50-city test (our data, 2026-10-19, 150 answers).

| Site | Kind | Answers that cite it (of 150) |
|---|---|---|
| Expertise.com | Directory with ranked lists | 93 |
| Yelp | Review site | 50 |
| iPropertyManagement | Directory and guides | 50 |
| Reddit | Forum | 22 |
| ThreeBestRated | Directory with ranked lists | 21 |
| Birdeye (reviews.birdeye.com) | Review pages | 19 |
| BBB | Review and complaint site | 14 |
| Clutch | Directory with reviews | 10 |
| Thumbtack | Lead marketplace with reviews | 7 |
| Facebook | Social pages and reviews | Not in top 30 |
| Google review pages | Maps reviews | Not in top 30 |

A second test points the same way. For a Chicago manager on 2026-10-16, the most cited sources were manager sites, Yelp (35 answers), Expertise.com (22) and Thumbtack (17). Fridman Properties' owner is CEO of MyRemotely, our partner.

Limits: our samples are small. A source in an answer is a link, not proof that it caused the pick.

## Which sites does ChatGPT link?

ChatGPT leans on Yelp for local business data. Steady Demand studied ChatGPT local answers in one 72-hour window in August 2026, in US English. Its report came out on 2026-08-21.

| Provider | In ChatGPT business cards | Visible citation |
|---|---|---|
| Yelp | 95.83% of runs | 1.04% |
| TripAdvisor | 28.90% | 13.55% |
| Google | 4.62% | 0.07% |
| Foursquare | 0% | 0% |

Source: Steady Demand. The report ties the Yelp result to a Yelp data license reported on 2026-07-23. It calls the result a snapshot that may change. The study did not cover Facebook or BBB.

What this means for you: when ChatGPT shows a business card for your firm, the rating and hours likely come from Yelp. Check that your Yelp page has the right name, phone, hours and service area.

## Which sites does Bing show?

Bing and Copilot data for property managers is thin, and we did not test Copilot in our runs. One tool helps you see your own share. Bing Webmaster Tools has an AI Performance report, a public preview since February 2026, says Search Influence. It shows citations in Copilot, the pages cited and the grounding queries (the searches the AI ran). It shows no clicks yet.

What to do: verify your site in Bing Webmaster Tools. Then look at which of your pages Copilot cites. If Copilot cites your fee page, keep that page current.

## Which sites does Google AI read?

Google AI Overviews and AI Mode read pages from the Google index. Google says a page must be indexed and able to show a snippet to appear as a supporting link. Google also says AI Mode and AI Overviews may run many related searches ("query fan-out") to build one answer.

So any review site that Google indexes can feed Google AI. Your Google Business Profile reviews feed Google Maps, and landlords see them when they search your name. Our test did not show Google review pages as a cited link. That does not mean Google AI ignores them.

## Where should you send owners to leave a review?

Send happy owners to Google first. Google allows you to ask: its Business Profile help says to remind customers and share a review link or QR code. Yelp says the opposite. Its policy says, "Don't ask anyone to review your business." Its software may hide reviews that look requested.

Use this plan:

| Site | Ask for reviews? | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| Google Business Profile | Yes, every owner | Send the review link after a good month or a lease-up |
| Yelp | No | Claim the page, fix the facts, reply to each review |
| BBB | We did not check BBB rules | Keep the profile complete and answer complaints fast |
| Facebook | Optional | Keep name, address and phone the same as on Google |
| Expertise.com, ThreeBestRated | Not a review ask | Check your listing and its facts each quarter |

Watch for: rules on rewards. The FTC rule at 16 CFR 465.4 bans incentives tied to a review with a set sentiment, positive or negative. Google bans free or discounted services in exchange for reviews. Yelp also says do not offer freebies, discounts or payment. This is general information, not legal advice. Check the current law or ask a local attorney.

## What should a property manager do this month?

Start with the sites that showed up most in real answers. Each step takes less than an hour.

1. **Search your firm on Expertise.com.** Check if you are listed for your city and if the facts are right.
2. **Claim your Yelp page.** Fix the name, phone, hours and service area. Reply to the last 10 reviews.
3. **Check BBB.** Make sure the name, license and address match your site.
4. **Set up a Google review ask.** Add the link to your owner statement email.
5. **Run 10 landlord prompts** in ChatGPT, Gemini and Claude. Note which review sites they cite for your city.
6. **Repeat in 30 days.** Compare the source list, not one answer.
