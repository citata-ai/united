## Which property management keywords show owner intent and which show tenant intent?

Owner keywords ask for a manager or the cost of one. Tenant keywords ask for a home, a payment or a login. The word "property management" appears in both, so read the rest of the search.

| Keyword pattern | Intent | Example | Target? |
|---|---|---|---|
| property management + city | Owner (mostly) | property management naperville | Yes, city page |
| property manager + near me | Mixed | property manager near me | Yes, home and city pages |
| rental management company + city | Owner | rental management company aurora | Yes, city page |
| [type] property management + city | Owner | single family property management joliet | Yes, service page |
| how much do property managers charge | Owner | property management fees illinois | Yes, fee page |
| hire / should I hire a property manager | Owner | is a property manager worth it | Yes, article |
| rent my house / rent out my condo | Owner | how to rent out my house | Yes, article |
| homes for rent + city | Tenant | houses for rent schaumburg | Listings only |
| [company] pay rent / portal | Tenant | smith pm tenant portal | Portal page |
| property management jobs | Job seeker | property manager salary | No |

"Near me" searches are mixed. Many tenants search "property management near me" to pay rent or find a unit. Win them on your home page, but do not count them as owner demand.

## How do I find local property management keyword volume?

Use Google Keyword Planner with the location set to your city or metro area. Google Ads Help says Keyword Planner shows estimates of monthly searches for a keyword and the cost to target it. It also says keywords with very low volume are not discoverable or forecastable, so small suburbs may show no data.

### Steps

1. Open Google Ads, Tools, Keyword Planner. You need an account, not a live campaign.
2. Choose "Discover new keywords" and enter: property management [city], property manager [city], rental management [city].
3. Set the location to your metro area or county, not the whole US.
4. Set the date range to the last 12 months.
5. Export the list. Mark each keyword owner, tenant, mixed or job.
6. Add the volume for each owner group.

Our own pull shows the scale. For a Chicago-area property manager, Keyword Planner showed "property management chicago" at 1,900 searches a month in the Chicago area, October 2025 to September 2026. The same data showed 7 generic property management terms fell 46% in the Chicago area and 43% in the US year over year. ## Which long-tail owner questions should I target?

Long-tail owner questions are longer, more specific searches. Each has low volume, but together they bring owners who are close to a decision, and they face fewer competitors.

### Owner question list

- **Cost:** "how much do property managers charge in [city]", "property management fees for a single family home", "is 10% for property management too much"
- **Decision:** "should I hire a property manager or do it myself", "when to hire a property manager"
- **Situation:** "rent out inherited house", "rent my house while I relocate", "accidental landlord [state]"
- **Rules:** "[state] security deposit law for landlords", "[city] rental license", "[city] rental inspection"
- **Switching:** "how to switch property management companies", "how to fire my property manager"
- **Section 8:** "section 8 property management [city]", "how much does section 8 pay in [city]"

Find more in three places: questions owners ask on your sales calls, Search Console queries with 4 or more words, and the follow-up questions in AI answers. Goodjuju Marketing's 2026 study found AI tools sent 3.4% of owner leads in its fall window, up from 2.1% in spring, so the questions owners type into ChatGPT matter too.

## How do I map property management keywords to pages?

Map each group of keywords with the same intent to one page. Two pages for the same intent compete with each other. One page for many intents answers none of them well.

| Keyword group | Page | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| property management [home city], property manager [home city] | Home page or main city page | Pick one, not both |
| property management [suburb] (each) | /property-management-[suburb] | Only where you serve real doors |
| property management fees [city/state] | /fees | Full fee table |
| single family / condo / small multifamily property management | /services/[type] | One per type |
| should I hire a property manager | Article | Link to fee page |
| rent out inherited house | Article | Link to rental analysis |
| houses for rent [city] | /rentals | Tenant page |

Write a city page only where you manage doors and know the area. A page that swaps only the city name is thin; Google's SEO Starter Guide advises content that is helpful, reliable and people-first.

If you run Google Ads on the same terms, block the tenant and job patterns above with negative keywords. Google Ads Help says negative keywords do not match close variants, so add plurals.
