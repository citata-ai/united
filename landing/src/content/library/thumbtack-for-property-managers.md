Thumbtack is a marketplace where people hire local pros. Most pros on it clean houses or fix sinks. But it also runs a property management category, and that category shows up in AI answers. In our Chicago baseline for Fridman Properties (a related party: its owner is CEO of MyRemotely, our partner), AI answers cited Thumbtack as a source in 17 answers (our data, 2026-10-16).

So the question has two parts. Does Thumbtack bring owner leads you can pay for? And does a Thumbtack profile help AI tools name you? This guide covers both.

## How does Thumbtack charge for leads?

Thumbtack charges property managers per lead. There is no membership fee and no contract. The Thumbtack pro page says three things, read on 2026-10-20:

- You pay only for leads from customers who choose you.
- You control your pricing and budget for leads, and that budget sets how many leads you get.
- Sign-up is free, with "limited competition per lead."

Thumbtack does not publish a lead price list by category. In a Thumbtack community thread, a pro reported paying $83.25 for one furniture-assembly lead that also went to 2 other pros. A Thumbtack admin replied that the pro could lower the "max lead price settings." That thread tells you two facts: a lead can go to more than one pro, and you set a price cap.

| Item | What Thumbtack says or shows | What it means for you |
|---|---|---|
| Membership fee | None | You can keep a free profile |
| Lead charge | Pay per lead from customers who choose you | Cost grows with volume |
| Price control | Max lead price and budget settings | Set a cap before day 1 |
| Competition | "Limited competition per lead" | A lead can go to a few pros |
| Pause | Pros can hide the business | Turn it off when the test ends |

### Worked example: cost per signed door

Use your own numbers. These are example inputs, not Thumbtack prices.

| Input | Example |
|---|---|
| Average lead price | $40 |
| Leads in 90 days | 15 |
| Lead spend | $600 |
| Leads that book a call | 6 (40%) |
| Owners who sign | 2 |
| Doors signed | 3 |
| Cost per signed door | $600 ÷ 3 = $200 |

Then compare $200 with what one door earns you in a year. If you charge 8% on $1,800 rent, one door brings $144 a month, or $1,728 a year. Our [fee calculator](/tools/property-management-fee-calculator) does this math for other fee models.

## Do owners search Thumbtack for property managers?

Some owners do, but the volume is small. The Thumbtack property management category asks the owner real questions: start date, how long they need management, monthly rental income, property type, number of properties and occupancy. Those filters fit a single-family owner with 1 to 3 homes.

The hire counts are low. On the Charleston list we read on 2026-10-20, the top profiles showed 7 hires and 6 hires on Thumbtack, with 3 to 44 reviews each. That is a side channel, not a main one.

Wider data agrees. In the Goodjuju Marketing fall 2026 update (35 property management companies, 2,525 owner leads over two 90-day windows), Google Search and the Map Pack gave 50.6% of owner leads. Referral traffic, which includes directories, gave 3.7%. Thumbtack sits inside that small referral slice at best.

## Why do AI tools cite Thumbtack lists?

AI tools cite Thumbtack because a Thumbtack page puts several local managers, star ratings, review counts and short quotes on one page. An AI answer (a reply from ChatGPT, Gemini or Claude) needs exactly that to build a list of "top property managers in a city."

Our own data shows the pattern:

- Fridman Properties baseline, Chicago, 144 answers: AI cited Yelp in 35 answers, Expertise.com in 22 and Thumbtack in 17 (our data, 2026-10-16).
- In that test, the company's own site was not the main source for "best property manager" prompts. Directories were.

So a Thumbtack profile is partly a listing for AI, not only for people. A free, complete profile can help even if you never pay for a lead. That is a hypothesis we are still testing, not a proven result. See how owners use AI to pick a manager in [our guide](/guides/how-owners-choose-a-property-manager-with-ai).

## How do I set up a Thumbtack profile that converts?

Set up the profile like a short sales page. Each item below is something an owner or an AI answer reads.

1. **Use your exact legal business name.** Match your Google Business Profile and your website. AI tools merge facts by name.
2. **Pick only the services you do.** Thumbtack lists on-site, HOA and vacation rental management. Leave out what you do not offer.
3. **Write a 3-line intro.** Who you serve, which areas, and your fee model in plain numbers.
4. **Add 5+ photos.** Your team, your office, a real managed home (with the owner's consent).
5. **Ask past owner clients for reviews.** Ask all clients the same way. Do not pay for reviews or filter who gets asked.
6. **Set a maximum lead price and a monthly budget.** Start low, then raise it if leads are thin.
7. **Reply fast.** Thumbtack ranks pros by response speed in its "Fastest response" sort.
8. **Tag your website link.** Add UTM tags (tracking labels in a link) so Google Analytics shows Thumbtack visits as their own source.

### 90-day test checklist

- [ ] Profile complete, name matches GBP and site
- [ ] Lead price cap and monthly budget set
- [ ] Every lead logged: date, price, outcome
- [ ] Day 30: cost per lead checked
- [ ] Day 60: cost per booked call checked
- [ ] Day 90: cost per signed door compared with your other channels
- [ ] Decision: keep paying, keep a free profile only, or pause

If the paid leads do not pay back, keep the free profile live and stop the lead spend. You keep the listing for AI answers, and you stop the cost.
