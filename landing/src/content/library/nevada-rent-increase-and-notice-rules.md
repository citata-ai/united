Nevada sets a rent increase notice in one sentence: NRS 118A.300. It sets the notice to end a periodic tenancy in NRS 40.251. We read both in the current code on the Nevada Legislature site (Rev. 4/15/2026). Here is the short version.

| Rule | Limit or deadline | Statute |
|---|---|---|
| Rent increase notice, monthly or longer | 60 days before the first increased payment | NRS 118A.300 |
| Rent increase notice, tenancy under 1 month | 30 days before the first increased payment | NRS 118A.300 |
| State rent cap | None in chapter 118A | NRS chapter 118A |
| Rent above the lease figure | Not allowed | NRS 118A.200(7) |
| Retaliatory rent increase | Not allowed, unless uniform for all tenants | NRS 118A.510 |
| End a month-to-month tenancy | At least 30 days' notice | NRS 40.251(1)(b) |
| End a week-to-week tenancy | At least 7 days' notice | NRS 40.251(1)(b) |
| Extra time for tenant 60+ or with a disability | 30 more days on written request | NRS 40.251(2) |
| Late fee | Max 5% of periodic rent, not before day 3 | NRS 118A.210(4) |
| Shorter notice by contract | Void | NRS 40.252 |

## Does Nevada have a cap on rent increases or rent control?

Nevada state law has no cap on rent increases. Chapter 118A of NRS is the state law for residential tenancies. We read its full table of sections, and none of them sets a rent cap or a rent formula.

The law does limit why and how you raise rent:

- **No retaliation.** NRS 118A.510 bars a rent increase in reply to a good-faith code complaint, a complaint under chapter 118A, or a fair housing complaint. Joining a tenant union is also protected.
- **The uniform exception.** An increase that applies in a uniform way to all tenants does not break that rule (NRS 118A.510(3)(d)).
- **One rent figure.** NRS 118A.200(6) requires the lease to state rent as a single figure. That figure includes any mandatory fees on top of base rent. The history note shows the Legislature amended this section in 2025.

## How many days notice does a Nevada landlord need to raise the rent?

A Nevada landlord needs 60 days' written notice to raise the rent. NRS 118A.300 counts the days back from the first rental payment that goes up. For a periodic tenancy of less than 1 month, the notice is 30 days.

A worked example for a monthly lease:

| Step | Date |
|---|---|
| First payment at the new rent | 2027-03-01 |
| Count back 60 days | 2026-12-31 |
| Serve the written notice on or before | 2026-12-31 |

Serve the notice the right way. NRS 118A.190 says written notices to the tenant under chapter 118A must be served in the manner set by NRS 40.280. NRS 40.280 lists personal delivery, delivery to a person of suitable age plus mailing, or posting plus mailing.

The written notice should state:

- the current rent and the new rent;
- the date of the first payment at the new rent;
- the unit address and the tenant names.

## Can a Nevada landlord raise the rent during a fixed-term lease?

A Nevada landlord cannot charge more than the rent in the written lease during its term. NRS 118A.200(7) says a landlord shall not charge periodic rent above the maximum total rent in the written agreement. A clause that breaks section 118A.200 is void (NRS 118A.200(9)).

So, for a 12-month lease at $1,600, the rent stays $1,600 until the term ends. A narrow exception lets a landlord bill some utility costs as a separate monthly fee (NRS 118A.200(8)). For a new rent at renewal, serve the 60-day notice before the first higher payment.

## How much notice is needed to end a month-to-month tenancy in Nevada?

A Nevada landlord needs at least 30 days' notice to end a month-to-month tenancy. NRS 40.251(1)(b) sets 30 days for periodic tenancies other than week to week. A week-to-week tenancy needs at least 7 days.

Under NRS 118A.210(2), a tenancy with no fixed term is month to month. The exception is a tenant who pays weekly rent: that tenancy is week to week.

Two extra rules apply to the notice:

1. **Extra time on request.** A tenant 60 or older, or with a physical or mental disability, may ask in writing for 30 more days. The tenant must give proof of age or disability (NRS 40.251(2)).
2. **Required statement.** The notice must tell the tenant about that right. It must also explain the shutdown rule for federal, state and tribal workers (NRS 40.251(3)–(5)).

You cannot shorten these periods in the lease. NRS 40.252 makes such a clause void.

## Do cities in Nevada add their own rent or notice rules?

Chapter 118A applies to rental agreements for dwellings anywhere in Nevada (NRS 118A.180). Its notice periods are the state rules. We did not review city or county codes for this guide, so check your local code too.

Some housing is outside chapter 118A. NRS 118A.180(2) excludes, among others:

- mobile home lots under chapter 118B;
- public housing authority low-rent programs;
- hotel or motel stays under 30 days, in most cases.

Federal programs such as housing vouchers may add their own notice terms. Read the contract you signed.

## Rent increase checklist for Nevada landlords

- [ ] Check the lease end date. No increase above the lease figure before it.
- [ ] Count back 60 days (30 for weekly) from the first higher payment.
- [ ] Apply the increase in a uniform way, and never as a reply to a complaint.
- [ ] Serve the written notice under NRS 40.280 and keep proof.
- [ ] Check that the late fee in the lease is 5% or less.
