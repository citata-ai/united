## What are Small Area Fair Market Rents?

Small Area Fair Market Rents (SAFMRs) are Fair Market Rents for single ZIP codes. A normal FMR covers a whole metro area or a non-metro county. 24 CFR 888.113(d)(2) says Small Area FMR areas "are the U.S. Postal Service ZIP code areas within a designated metropolitan area."

HUD's FY2027 FMR notice (91 FR 56156, September 1, 2026) explains the method:

1. **Start with ZIP code rents.** HUD uses 40th percentile gross rents for each ZIP Code Tabulation Area, when Census data are reliable.
2. **Average 3 years.** For FY2027, HUD averages the 2022, 2023 and 2024 5-year American Community Survey estimates.
3. **Fill gaps with a ratio.** Where data are thin, HUD uses the ratio of the ZIP code's median rent to the metro median, times the metro FMR.
4. **Cap the result.** Each 2-bedroom SAFMR may not be more than 150% of the metro or county 2-bedroom FMR.
5. **Limit drops.** In mandatory areas, an FY2027 SAFMR may not fall below 90% of the FY2026 SAFMR.

The goal, in HUD's words from its 2023 notice, is to help households "use their vouchers in low poverty opportunity areas."

## Which areas must use SAFMRs?

The metro areas HUD designates must use SAFMRs. 24 CFR 888.113(c) sets the test, and HUD's October 25, 2023 notice (88 FR 73352) applied it again. A metro qualifies if it meets all five values:

| Test | Value |
|---|---|
| Vouchers under lease | At least 2,500 |
| Rental stock in ZIP codes where SAFMR is over 110% of the metro FMR | At least 20% |
| Voucher families in concentrated low-income areas | At least 25% |
| That share, relative to all renters in those areas | Over 155% (1.55) |
| Metro vacancy rate | Over 4% |

The 2023 notice added 41 metro areas, effective October 1, 2024. PHAs there had to align payment standards with SAFMRs by January 1, 2025. Among the 41: Akron, Birmingham, Cincinnati, Cleveland, Columbus, Detroit, Indianapolis, Jersey City, Kansas City, Los Angeles-Long Beach-Glendale, Miami, Nashville, Orlando, Phoenix, Raleigh, San Jose, Seattle, St. Louis and Tucson.

A first group of metros has used SAFMRs since April 1, 2018. Designations are permanent (888.113(c)(4)). HUD makes new designations every 5 years. HUD may suspend a designation after a major disaster or a sudden influx of displaced households.

## How can a ZIP code SAFMR be higher than the metro FMR?

A ZIP code SAFMR is higher than the metro FMR when rents in that ZIP code are higher than in the metro as a whole. The metro FMR is one number for many ZIP codes. The SAFMR splits it. High-rent ZIP codes get more. Low-rent ZIP codes get less.

A worked example. The metro number is HUD's: the FY2027 2-bedroom FMR for the Phoenix-Mesa-Chandler, AZ MSA is $1,734 (HUD User). The two ZIP code SAFMRs are made up to show the math.

| Area | 2-bedroom FMR | Basic range payment standard (90% to 110%) |
|---|---|---|
| Phoenix metro, area-wide | $1,734 | $1,561 to $1,907 |
| ZIP A, higher rent (example SAFMR) | $2,100 | $1,890 to $2,310 |
| ZIP B, lower rent (example SAFMR) | $1,450 | $1,305 to $1,595 |
| Cap on any 2-bedroom SAFMR here (150% of $1,734) | $2,601 | |

In ZIP A, a landlord can get a voucher rent up to about $400 more than under the metro FMR. In ZIP B, the cap is lower than before. The rent must still pass the reasonable rent test either way.

## Can a PHA opt in to SAFMRs?

A PHA can opt in to SAFMRs. 24 CFR 888.113(c)(3) says a PHA in a metro area not subject to SAFMRs, or in a non-metro area where HUD publishes SAFMRs, "may choose to use Small Area FMRs after notification to HUD." The FY2027 FMR notice repeats that PHAs not mandated "must notify HUD of their voluntary use."

A PHA can also use SAFMRs only in part. Under 24 CFR 982.503(d)(2), a PHA that does not use SAFMRs may set an exception payment standard for a ZIP code up to 110% of that ZIP code's SAFMR. The exception must cover the whole ZIP code.

## How do I find the SAFMR for my ZIP code?

You find the SAFMR on HUD User. The FY2027 FMR notice says SAFMRs for all metro and non-metro areas are at huduser.gov/portal/datasets/fmr/smallarea.

Steps:

- [ ] Open the HUD User Small Area FMR page and pick the current fiscal year.
- [ ] Choose your state and metro area, or search by ZIP code.
- [ ] Read the SAFMR for your unit's bedroom count.
- [ ] Check your PHA's payment standard schedule for the same ZIP code.
- [ ] Subtract the PHA's utility allowance if your tenant pays utilities.

The SAFMR is HUD's number. The payment standard is your PHA's number, set at 90% to 110% of the SAFMR in the basic range (24 CFR 982.503(c)). Your PHA's schedule is what decides the HAP.

## Do SAFMRs change my current HAP contract?

SAFMRs do not change your rent to owner or rewrite your HAP contract. They change the payment standard, which caps the subsidy. If a switch to SAFMRs raises your ZIP code's payment standard, the PHA must apply the increase to the family no later than the earliest of a rent increase that raises the family share, the next reexamination, or one year (24 CFR 982.505(c)(4)).

If the switch lowers it, the family is protected for a time. Under 24 CFR 982.505(c)(3), the PHA may keep the old payment standard for as long as the family stays. Any cut may not apply earlier than 2 years after the decrease, and only after 12 months' written notice.
