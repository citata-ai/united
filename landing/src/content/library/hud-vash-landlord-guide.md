## What is HUD-VASH, and who runs it?

HUD-VASH is a Housing Choice Voucher program for homeless veterans, run by a PHA together with the VA. HUD's August 13, 2024 Federal Register notice (89 FR 65769) says the program "combines HUD HCV rental assistance for homeless veterans administered by PHAs with case management and supportive services provided through VA." It is authorized by section 8(o)(19) of the Housing Act, 42 U.S.C. 1437f(o)(19).

The scale is large. VA's HUD-VASH page (updated August 24, 2026) says HUD has awarded more than 116,000 HUD-VASH vouchers since 2008. As of March 2026, more than 95,000 formerly homeless veterans lived in homes through the program.

Who does what, for a landlord:

| Task | PHA | VA |
|---|---|---|
| Screens the veteran for program criteria | No | Yes |
| Issues the voucher | Yes | No |
| Helps the veteran search for a unit | No | Yes |
| Inspects the unit | Yes | No |
| Signs the HAP contract with you | Yes | No |
| Pays the subsidy part of the rent | Yes | No |
| Provides case management | No | Yes |
| Screens the tenant for your lease | No, you do (24 CFR 982.307) | No |

The VA tasks above come from section II.G of the 2024 notice. The notice also says the VA may act through a Designated Service Provider approved by the VA Secretary.

## Do the same inspection and rent rules apply to HUD-VASH?

The same inspection and rent rules apply, with a few HUD-VASH waivers. Section II.N of the 2024 notice says "all regulatory requirements pertaining to HQS found at 24 CFR 5.703 apply to HUD-VASH." Rent must still be reasonable under 24 CFR 982.507.

What changes for HUD-VASH:

1. **Shorter first lease.** The notice waives the 1-year initial lease rule (24 CFR 982.309(a)), so a tenant-based HUD-VASH lease may be under 12 months. The waiver does not apply to project-based vouchers.
2. **Pre-inspection.** A PHA may pre-inspect available units. If the veteran picks a unit that passed inspection within 90 days of the Request for Tenancy Approval (form HUD-52517), with no one living there in between, the PHA may approve it if all other 982.305 rules are met.
3. **Higher payment standard.** The PHA may set a separate HUD-VASH exception payment standard up to 120% of the FMR or Small Area FMR. Above 120% needs HUD approval. For a reasonable accommodation, a PHA may go up to 140%.
4. **Eviction does not always end the voucher.** For most voucher families, an eviction for a serious lease violation ends assistance (24 CFR 982.552(b)(2)). The 2024 notice waives this for HUD-VASH: the PHA "may" end assistance instead of "must."

A worked example of rule 3, with a made-up FMR:

| Item | Regular voucher | HUD-VASH exception standard |
|---|---|---|
| FMR, 1 bedroom (example) | $1,400 | $1,400 |
| Highest payment standard without HUD approval | 110% = $1,540 | 120% = $1,680 |
| Extra room for rent plus utilities | | $140 a month |

The rent still has to match similar unassisted units. A higher payment standard lets a veteran rent a unit that costs more. It does not let you charge more than the market.

## What does the VA case manager do?

The VA case manager supports the veteran before and after move-in. The 2024 notice lists VA duties that include screening veterans, helping with the PHA application, housing search help, and "regular ongoing case management, outpatient health services, hospitalization, and other supportive services."

For you, the case manager is a contact. VA's Landlord FAQs page says case managers help veterans with rent payment timing, lease terms and the landlord relationship. "They are also a resource for you, the landlord, to help resolve issues with the tenancy."

What to do when a problem starts:

1. **Talk to the tenant first.** VA's FAQ says the first step is to try to resolve the issue with the veteran directly.
2. **Call the case manager.** Ask the tenant for the case manager's contact at lease signing.
3. **Follow your lease and the HAP contract.** Lease enforcement rules are the same as any voucher tenancy.

Case management is a condition of the voucher. Under section II.H of the notice, if the VA finds a veteran is not taking part in required case management without good cause, the PHA must end the HUD-VASH assistance. The PHA may offer a regular voucher instead.

## Do HUD-VASH landlord incentives exist?

There is no federal HUD-VASH signing bonus for landlords in the 2024 notice. The VA Landlord FAQs page does not describe a separate landlord incentive or risk fund either. Local PHAs may run their own incentive programs, so ask yours.

The VA page names two benefits instead. A portion of the rent "is automatically paid on time by a PHA or an SSVF grantee." And through the separate Supportive Services for Veteran Families (SSVF) program, help can include "security deposits, rental subsidies, housing case management, and financial assistance."

## How do I list a unit for HUD-VASH?

You list a unit for HUD-VASH through the VA or your local PHA. VA's Landlord FAQs page says: "Fill out our online form to let us know about your available units." VA then connects you with your local VA for referrals.

Steps:

- [ ] Fill out the VA online form on the Landlord FAQs page.
- [ ] Call your PHA and ask if it runs HUD-VASH and pre-inspects units.
- [ ] Ask the PHA for its HUD-VASH payment standard for your unit size.
- [ ] Prepare the unit to pass inspection before you list it.
- [ ] Screen the veteran with the same written criteria you use for all applicants.
- [ ] At lease signing, get the case manager's name and phone number.
