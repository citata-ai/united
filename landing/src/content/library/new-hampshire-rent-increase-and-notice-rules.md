## Does New Hampshire have a cap on rent increases or rent control?

New Hampshire state law sets no cap on rent increases. We read RSA chapter 540 (actions against tenants) and RSA 540-A (prohibited practices and security deposits). Neither chapter limits the amount of rent or the size of an increase. Both chapters control notice, eviction and deposits.

The state does control how you raise the rent. A rent increase needs a written notice with a 30-day lead time. Retaliation rules also apply, as the section on cities below explains.

## How many days notice does a New Hampshire landlord need to raise the rent?

A New Hampshire landlord gives at least 30 days' written notice to raise the rent. The rule sits in RSA 540:2, IV. The notice must state 2 things:

1. **The amount** of the new rent.
2. **The effective date** of the increase.

RSA 540:2, IV ties this notice to eviction. If the tenant refuses the new rent, that refusal is "good cause" to end the tenancy. But the rule works only if you gave the 30-day written notice first.

A worked example. The rent is $1,800 a month. You want $1,900 from December 1.

| Step | Date | Rule |
|---|---|---|
| Serve the rent notice by | October 31 | 31 days before December 1: 30 days plus 1 day of margin |
| New rent starts | December 1 | RSA 540:2, IV |
| Tenant refuses; you serve a 30-day eviction notice | December 2 or later | RSA 540:3, II |
| Earliest end of the tenancy | 30 days after the notice | RSA 540:3, II |

Serve the notice early. A few extra days protect you from a dispute over the count. RSA 540:5 lets any person serve notices in hand or at the tenant's home. Keep a copy and an affidavit of service.

From 2026-01-01, also check how you collect the new rent. RSA 540-A:3, X bans a rule that the tenant pays only by electronic transfer. You must allow at least one non-electronic way to pay.

## Can a New Hampshire landlord raise the rent during a fixed-term lease?

A New Hampshire landlord can raise the rent during a fixed-term lease only if the lease allows it. RSA 540:1 says every tenancy is at will, with rent payable on demand, "unless a different contract is shown". A written lease with a fixed rent is that different contract.

So read your lease first:

- [ ] Does the lease fix the rent for the whole term?
- [ ] Does it have a clause that allows a mid-term increase?
- [ ] Does it say how much notice you must give?
- [ ] Does any clause waive the tenant's rights under RSA 540? RSA 540:28 makes such a clause void.

If the lease is silent, plan the increase for the renewal date. Send the 30-day notice before the new term starts.

## How much notice is needed to end a month-to-month tenancy in New Hampshire?

A New Hampshire landlord gives 30 days' written notice to end a month-to-month residential tenancy. RSA 540:3, II says "30 days' notice shall be sufficient in all cases" for residential tenancies. Shorter 7-day notice applies to some fault reasons, such as unpaid rent.

Whether you need a reason depends on the property type in RSA 540:1-a.

| Property type | Examples | Reason to end the tenancy |
|---|---|---|
| Nonrestricted | Single-family houses of an owner with 3 or fewer; units in an owner-occupied building of 4 units or fewer | No reason needed (RSA 540:2, I) |
| Restricted | All other residential rentals | A reason from RSA 540:2, II |

For restricted property, "other good cause" is one reason. RSA 540:2, V says it includes "any legitimate business or economic reason". It need not be based on the tenant's acts. If the cause is the tenant's conduct, you first give a written warning (RSA 540:2, III).

New since 2026-07-01: the end of a long lease is now a reason. It covers a lease of 12 months or longer. It also covers a shorter lease renewed for 12 months or more in total. You give written notice at least 60 days before the lease ends. The notice says you will not renew and the tenant must leave. You must file the eviction within 6 months of the end date (RSA 540:2, II(i)). RSA 540:2, VIII says courts note such a case as no fault of the tenant.

## Do cities in New Hampshire add their own rent or notice rules?

The rent and notice rules in RSA 540 apply in every New Hampshire city and town. We did not check each city's code, so check your local ordinances too.

Local housing codes still matter. RSA 540:13-a protects a tenant who reports a code problem to a local inspection board. If you raise the rent or end the tenancy in retaliation, the tenant has a defense. The defense does not apply if the tenant owes one week's rent or more. A tenant who wins can get up to 3 months' rent in damages (RSA 540:14, II).

Watch for: a rent increase sent soon after a code complaint looks like retaliation. Write down your business reason and the date you decided on it.

Manufactured housing parks follow a separate chapter, RSA 205-A, as RSA 540-A:5 notes. This page does not cover park rules.
