## West Virginia security deposit rules at a glance

West Virginia puts its deposit rules in article 6A of chapter 37 of the Code. We read each section on the West Virginia Legislature's code site on 2026-10-19.

| Rule | Limit or deadline | Statute |
|---|---|---|
| Maximum deposit | No cap in the article | W. Va. Code 37-6A |
| What counts as a deposit | Any refundable money held to secure the lease or against damage | W. Va. Code 37-6A-1(14) |
| Pet and application fees | Not a deposit only if agreed in writing as nonrefundable | W. Va. Code 37-6A-1(14) |
| Return deadline | 60 days after the tenancy ends, or 45 days after a new tenant moves in, whichever is shorter | W. Va. Code 37-6A-1(7), 37-6A-2(a) |
| Itemized list | Required with the return | W. Va. Code 37-6A-2(a) |
| Damage above the deposit, contractor needed | Notice within the period, then 15 more days to itemize | W. Va. Code 37-6A-2(c) |
| Records of deductions | Keep 1 year; show or copy within 72 hours of a written request | W. Va. Code 37-6A-3 |
| Waiver in the lease | Unenforceable | W. Va. Code 37-6A-4 |
| Willful or bad-faith failure | Deposit plus 1.5 times the amount wrongfully withheld | W. Va. Code 37-6A-5 |
| Court | Magistrate or circuit court of the county | W. Va. Code 37-6A-5(b) |

## What is the maximum security deposit a landlord can charge in West Virginia?

West Virginia sets no maximum security deposit. Article 6A of chapter 37 defines a deposit and controls its return, but it names no dollar or month limit.

The definition matters for fees. W. Va. Code 37-6A-1(14) says a security deposit is any refundable money given to secure the rental agreement or as security for damage. It does not include rent or prepaid rent. A pet fee or an application fee is outside the deposit only if both parties agree in writing that the fee is nonrefundable.

**What to do.** Write each fee into the lease with the word "nonrefundable" and get both signatures. A fee without that writing may count as a refundable deposit.

## How many days does a West Virginia landlord have to return a security deposit?

A West Virginia landlord must return the deposit within the "notice period." W. Va. Code 37-6A-1(7) defines it as the shorter of two windows:

- **60 days** after the tenancy ends, or
- **45 days** after a new tenant moves in.

**Worked example.** The tenant moves out on March 31. Day 60 is May 30. You rent the unit again, and the new tenant moves in on April 10. Day 45 after that is May 25. May 25 comes first, so the deposit and itemization are due by May 25.

| Move-out | New tenant moves in | 60-day date | 45-day date | Deadline |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| March 31 | None | May 30 | n/a | May 30 |
| March 31 | April 10 | May 30 | May 25 | May 25 |
| March 31 | May 1 | May 30 | June 15 | May 30 |

If damage exceeds the deposit and needs a third-party contractor, W. Va. Code 37-6A-2(c) gives more time. Send the tenant written notice of that fact within the notice period. You then have 15 more days to send the itemization and the repair cost.

Delivery may be in person or by mail to the tenant's last known or forwarding address (W. Va. Code 37-6A-2(g)). The tenant must give you an accurate address. If mail comes back as undeliverable, hold the deposit or notice for 6 months. Hand it over at your office within 72 hours of a written request.

## What can a landlord deduct from a security deposit in West Virginia?

A West Virginia landlord may apply the deposit only to five items. W. Va. Code 37-6A-2(b) lists them:

1. **Rent due.** Includes reasonable late charges stated in the rental agreement.
2. **Damages.** Losses from the tenant's noncompliance with the lease, less reasonable wear and tear.
3. **Unpaid utilities.** Utilities billed to and paid by the landlord that the lease made the tenant's duty.
4. **Removal and storage.** Reasonable costs to remove and store the tenant's property.
5. **Other lease charges.** Damages or charges set out in the rental agreement, such as a third-party contractor's repair bill.

Reasonable wear and tear is never a deduction. A tenant may not demand an immediate credit of the deposit against unpaid rent at move-out (W. Va. Code 37-6A-2(d)).

If the building changes hands, the new owner holds the duty. W. Va. Code 37-6A-2(e) binds the landlord at the end of the tenancy to return any deposit owed, even if the old owner never passed the money on.

## Does a West Virginia landlord have to give an itemized list of deductions?

Yes. A West Virginia landlord must deliver a written itemization of damages and other charges with the deposit balance (W. Va. Code 37-6A-2(a)).

Keep the records. W. Va. Code 37-6A-3 requires you to keep itemized records of all deductions for each tenant for 1 year after the tenancy ends. Within 72 hours of a written request during business hours, you must let the tenant or the tenant's agent see them. You may give a copy instead.

A good itemization lists, for each charge:

- What was damaged and where.
- Why it is beyond reasonable wear and tear.
- The cost, with an invoice or contractor bid.
- The lease clause that supports any "other charge."

## Must a West Virginia landlord pay interest or keep the deposit in a separate account?

Article 6A of chapter 37 does not require interest on deposits. It also does not require a separate or trust bank account. We read all six sections of the article on 2026-10-19 and found no such rule.

Holding deposits in a separate account is still a good practice. It makes the yearly records under 37-6A-3 easy to produce. It also shows that the money was never mixed with operating funds.

## What is the penalty for a West Virginia landlord who keeps a deposit wrongly?

The penalty applies when the failure is willful or not in good faith. W. Va. Code 37-6A-5(a) gives the tenant a judgment for:

1. The amount of any unreturned deposit, and
2. Damages for annoyance or inconvenience equal to 1.5 times the amount wrongfully withheld.

If the tenant owes rent, the court credits the award against the rent due.

**Worked example.** You hold a $1,200 deposit and keep $500 with no valid basis, in bad faith. The tenant may get the $500 back plus $750 (1.5 times $500), for $1,250 total. If the tenant owes $300 in rent, the court credits the award against that rent.

Two more rules protect the tenant. A lease clause that waives the tenant's rights under article 6A is unenforceable. If you try to enforce one, the tenant may recover actual damages and attorney's fees (W. Va. Code 37-6A-4). Cases go to magistrate court or circuit court in the county where the rental is (W. Va. Code 37-6A-5(b)).

## West Virginia deposit checklist for property managers

- [ ] Pet and application fees are marked nonrefundable in a signed writing.
- [ ] Move-out date and any new tenant's move-in date are on file.
- [ ] Deadline is the shorter of 60 days after move-out or 45 days after a new move-in.
- [ ] Contractor-level damage above the deposit: written notice sent within the period.
- [ ] Itemized list and balance delivered in person or by mail.
- [ ] Deduction records kept for 1 year and ready within 72 hours of a request.
