## What is in a typical RFTA packet?

A typical RFTA packet has the HUD form plus the PHA's own owner forms. The Housing Authority of Maricopa County (HAMC) packet, revised January 2025, has 10 pages:

| Page | What it is | Who fills it in |
|---|---|---|
| Steps of the RFTA | HAMC's process and timelines | Read and initial |
| Jurisdiction list | Cities HAMC does and does not serve | Check your address |
| Inspection request | Rent, utilities, amenities, unit facts, inspection contact | Owner |
| HUD-52517 (2 pages) | The federal Request for Tenancy Approval | Owner and head of household |
| IRS Form W-9 | Tax ID of the payee | Owner or company |
| Direct deposit authorization | Bank account for payments | Owner |
| Lead-based paint disclosure | Required for pre-1978 housing | Owner and tenant |
| Common fail items | A checklist before the inspection | Owner signs |
| Landlord certification | Program rules you accept | Owner |

The HUD-52517 itself (04/2023 edition in the packet) asks for:

- [ ] PHA name and unit address.
- [ ] Requested lease start date.
- [ ] Number of bedrooms and year built.
- [ ] Proposed rent and security deposit.
- [ ] Date the unit is available for inspection.
- [ ] Structure type, and any other subsidy on the unit.
- [ ] Who pays each utility and who provides the refrigerator and range, marked "O" or "T".
- [ ] For buildings with more than 4 units, rents of the most recently leased comparable unassisted units.
- [ ] Lead-based paint status.
- [ ] Signatures of the owner and the head of household.

## How long does the PHA take to approve an RFTA?

Times differ by PHA, and the federal rule sets only the inspection clock. Under 24 CFR 982.305(b)(2), a PHA with up to 1,250 budgeted units must inspect and give its decision within 15 days after the RFTA. Larger PHAs must act "within a reasonable time," and within 15 days "to the extent practicable." The clock stops while the unit is not available for inspection.

| PHA | RFTA review | Inspection |
|---|---|---|
| Maricopa County (AZ) | Approve or deny within 14 days of a complete RFTA | Scheduling may take up to 24 days from receipt |
| Santa Cruz County (CA) | Typically three to five business days | Scheduled "shortly thereafter" |
| Tacoma (WA) | An inspector contacts you after receipt | "Every effort" to inspect within 5 business days if the unit is ready |

The family's voucher clock stops during the review. 24 CFR 982.303(c) says the PHA must suspend the voucher term from the day the family submits the request until the PHA's written decision.

## What are the most common RFTA mistakes?

The most common mistake is a missing page or signature. HAMC's packet says it twice: "Incomplete packets will only delay the process." Use this list before you hand back the packet:

1. **Fill in every field.** HAMC says all information "MUST be completed" to request an inspection.
2. **Use rent only.** HAMC's form says the requested amount "cannot include additional fees; only rent and taxes."
3. **Match the utilities.** The utility chart must match your lease. A change later needs a new HAP contract.
4. **Mark the unit vacant, if it is.** HAMC says the unit "must be vacant for initial inspection."
5. **Turn on utilities.** HAMC will not inspect without all utilities on and a working refrigerator.
6. **Match the tax ID.** The W-9 name and TIN must match the bank account and the ownership records.
7. **Attach a blank lease.** It must include the HUD tenancy addendum word for word, as item 14 on the HUD form states.
8. **List comparables, if required.** Owners of more than 4 units must list recent unassisted rents.

## Do I need the W-9 and proof of ownership?

Yes, at most PHAs. These are PHA requirements tied to HUD rules and tax reporting.

- **W-9.** Tacoma Housing Authority checks the SSN or TIN with the IRS so it can issue accurate 1099s. HAMC says the name and TIN on the bank account "MUST match" the W-9.
- **Proof of ownership.** Tacoma says: "Per HUD requirements, we must verify ownership of the property." HAMC accepts an assessor record, a settlement statement or a deed.
- **Management agreement.** If a property manager collects the rent, both PHAs want a copy of the management agreement.
- **Bank proof.** HAMC wants a voided check or a bank letter. It pays by direct deposit only.

## What happens if the PHA rejects the proposed rent?

The PHA rejects a rent for one of two reasons. Under 24 CFR 982.305(a), it cannot approve a tenancy unless the rent is reasonable. At first move-in, if the gross rent is above the payment standard, the family share also cannot exceed 40% of the family's monthly adjusted income.

| Reason | What you can do |
|---|---|
| Rent not reasonable vs comparable unassisted units (982.507) | Send your own comparable rents. Santa Cruz County accepts owner comps. |
| Family share over 40% of income | Lower the rent until the family share fits |
| Both | Lower the rent to the amount the PHA says it can approve |

The owner and the family negotiate the rent, and the PHA must help the family if asked (24 CFR 982.506). The Santa Cruz County housing authority says its staff "will review the RTA, discuss the appropriate rent with the landlord," and then schedule the inspection.
