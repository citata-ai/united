## What did Yelp license to OpenAI?

Yelp licensed its local business content to OpenAI, and announced the agreement on February 12, 2026. The Yelp press release for Q4 2025, filed with the SEC, says: "we recently signed an agreement with OpenAI." The shareholder letter says Yelp plans more licensing deals "with leading AI search providers" in 2026.

Trade press filled in the details in July 2026. TechWyse, citing Axios, reports these points:

| Point | What was reported |
|---|---|
| What ChatGPT gets | Yelp reviews, ratings, photos and business details for local answers |
| Size | 330 million cumulative reviews and more than 8 million business listings |
| Credit | Yelp branding and links appear, but OpenAI controls the design |
| Next step | Yelp plans to bring "Request a Quote" into ChatGPT; not live in that report |
| Terms | Not disclosed; the deal is not exclusive |
| Other partners | Yelp already licenses content to Amazon Alexa, Apple Maps, Microsoft Bing, Meta.ai and Yahoo |

Neither company has published how ChatGPT weighs Yelp ratings or review text when it picks a business.

## How often do Yelp links show in AI answers about property managers?

Yelp links show up often in one engine and not at all in the others, in our test. On 2026-10-19 we asked "What is the best property management company in <City, ST>? Give names." for the 50 largest US cities, through 3 APIs with web search on (our data, 2026-10-19).

| Engine | Answers | Yelp linked |
|---|---|---|
| Claude | 50 | 50 |
| ChatGPT (OpenAI API, web search) | 50 | 0 |
| Gemini (Google Search grounding) | 50 | 0 |

Watch for: the ChatGPT app is not the same as the API. The Yelp data in the app can show as business details, ratings and photos, not only as a source link. So 0 in our API test does not mean 0 in the app. Test your own city in the app, logged out, and look for Yelp ratings next to company names.

What to do: treat Yelp as a source for at least 2 large AI systems now, OpenAI by license and Claude by citation in our test. One free page covers both.

## How do you claim and fix a Yelp page?

You claim a Yelp page for free from Yelp for Business. Yelp says there is no charge to create a page, and a claimed page gives you 20+ free features.

1. **Search for your company** on Yelp for Business and claim the page, or create one if none exists.
2. **Match your name, address and phone** to your website and Google Business Profile, letter for letter.
3. **Set the category** to property management, and add your service areas.
4. **Add your website link and hours.**
5. **Write the description** in plain words: doors you manage, home types, cities, and the year you started.
6. **Upload photos** with captions: your team, your office, homes you manage (with the owner's permission).
7. **Turn on messages** so owners can ask for a quote. Yelp says users can request calls, quotes and consultations from a page.
8. **Check the page each month** for edits by others and new reviews.

Yelp also has a rule you must know before step 9: do not ask for reviews.

## Can a property manager ask for Yelp reviews?

No. Yelp's support page says: "Don't ask anyone to review your business, be it customers, mailing list subscribers, friends, family, etc." Yelp's software tries to find requested reviews and may not recommend them. Yelp also says not to offer freebies, discounts or payment for reviews.

The law points the same way. The FTC final rule of August 14, 2024 bans incentives conditioned on a review with a positive or negative sentiment. It also bans unfounded legal threats or intimidation to remove a negative review.

This is general information, not legal advice. Check the current law or ask a local attorney.

So on Yelp, your reviews come from good service and a visible page. Put your review requests on sites that allow them, such as Google.

## What about bad tenant reviews on Yelp?

Bad tenant reviews are normal for property managers, and a calm reply helps more than silence. Owners read them to see how you handle conflict. AI answers may also summarize them.

| Review type | What to do |
|---|---|
| Breaks Yelp's Content Guidelines (read the guidelines first) | Report it from the Reviews section of your business account. Yelp's moderators decide, which may take several days. |
| A fair complaint | Reply in public. Say what you fixed and how to reach a person. |
| A complaint about a denied application or eviction | Reply with the policy in general terms. Do not share the tenant's private details. |
| A false fact | Reply with the correct fact, once, without blame. |

Fair Housing note: keep every reply about the process, never about the person. Do not mention a protected class, and do not discuss one applicant's file in public.

Watch for: a reply is permanent and AI can quote it. Write each one as if an owner reads it next to your name in ChatGPT.
