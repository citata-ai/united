Delaware puts its deposit rules in 25 Del. C. § 5514, part of the Residential Landlord-Tenant Code. I read the text on the Delaware Code Online site on 2026-10-19. The 20-day deadline is short, so put it on your calendar the day a lease ends.

| Rule | Limit or deadline | Statute |
|---|---|---|
| Maximum deposit, lease of 1 year or more | 1 month's rent | § 5514(a)(2) |
| Maximum deposit, month-to-month after 1 year | 1 month's rent; credit any excess at once | § 5514(a)(3) |
| Furnished units | Cap does not apply | § 5514(a)(4) |
| Escrow account | Separate account, federally insured bank with a Delaware office | § 5514(b) |
| Itemized list of damages and refund | Within 20 days after the lease ends | § 5514(e), (f) |
| Tenant objection to the list | Within 10 days after receiving the payment | § 5514(f) |
| Penalty for a late or wrong return | Double the amount wrongfully withheld | § 5514(g)(1) |
| Pet deposit | Up to 1 month's rent, any lease length | § 5514(i)(2) |
| Application fee | Greater of 10% of monthly rent or $50 | § 5514(d) |

## What is the maximum security deposit a landlord can charge in Delaware?

The maximum security deposit in Delaware is 1 month's rent when the rental agreement is for 1 year or more (§ 5514(a)(2)). The same 1-month cap applies to month-to-month or open-term tenancies once they have lasted 1 year (§ 5514(a)(3)). Federally assisted housing rules are an exception to that second cap.

When a month-to-month tenancy reaches 1 year, you must "immediately return, as a credit to the tenant" any amount above 1 month's rent. A surety bond counts toward the cap too.

Three more rules change the total you can collect:

- **Furnished units.** The deposit limits "shall not apply to furnished rental units" (§ 5514(a)(4)).
- **Pet deposits.** A separate pet deposit can be up to 1 month's rent (§ 5514(i)(2)). No pet deposit for a certified and trained support animal for a disabled resident.
- **Surety bonds.** A tenant may buy a surety bond instead of all or part of the deposit (§ 5514A). You may not require one. Bond plus deposit may not exceed 1 month's rent per unit.

Worked example for a 12-month lease at $1,500 per month, unfurnished, with a dog:

| Item | Cap | Maximum |
|---|---|---|
| Security deposit | 1 × rent | $1,500 |
| Pet deposit | 1 × rent | $1,500 |
| Application fee | Greater of 10% of rent or $50 | $150 |

## How many days does a Delaware landlord have to return a security deposit?

A Delaware landlord has 20 days after the rental agreement ends or expires to return the deposit (§ 5514(e)). If you keep part of it, you must send the itemized list and pay the difference within the same 20 days (§ 5514(f)).

The refund goes to the tenant's address in the lease, or to a forwarding address the tenant gave in writing at or before the end of the lease (§ 5514(h)). If the tenant gives no address, you are not liable for the double penalty. You still owe any unused part of the deposit if the tenant claims it in writing within 1 year.

## What can a landlord deduct from a security deposit in Delaware?

Section 5514(c) lists three purposes for the deposit:

1. **Damage.** Actual damage to the premises caused by the tenant that exceeds normal wear and tear, or that painting and ordinary cleaning cannot correct.
2. **Unpaid rent.** All rent owed under the agreement, including late charges and rent due because the tenant ended the lease early or left.
3. **Re-renting costs.** Reasonable costs to renovate and re-rent after the tenant ends the lease early. If the tenant ended it under the early termination rights in § 5314, this part may not exceed 1 month's rent.

Pet damage comes out of the pet deposit first. Only the part the pet deposit does not cover can come out of the security deposit (§ 5514(i)(1)).

## Does a Delaware landlord have to give an itemized list of deductions?

Yes. Within 20 days, you must send "an itemized list of damages to the premises and the estimated costs of repair for each" (§ 5514(f)). Send the payment for the balance with it.

The list has two legal effects:

- **No list, no deduction.** If you do not send it in time, the law treats this as your acknowledgment that no payment for damages is due.
- **Silence means agreement.** If the tenant accepts the payment and does not object in writing within 10 days of receiving it, the tenant agrees to the damages on the list.

Use one line per item: the damage, the room, and the estimated repair cost.

## Must a Delaware landlord pay interest or keep the deposit in a separate account?

Section 5514 does not contain a rule that requires interest on a security deposit. It does require a separate account.

Account rules in § 5514(b):

- Place each deposit in an escrow bank account at a federally insured bank with an office in Delaware that accepts deposits.
- Name it a security deposits account. Do not use it to run any business.
- Tell the tenant where the account is.
- Hold the money for the tenant. The tenant's claim comes before your other creditors, even in bankruptcy.

If the lease says so, you may raise the deposit when you raise the rent (§ 5514(j)). If the increase is more than 10% of monthly rent, the tenant pays it in parts over the lease term, or over 4 months on a month-to-month tenancy.

## What is the penalty for a Delaware landlord who keeps a deposit wrongly?

The tenant is entitled to double the amount wrongfully withheld if you do not return the deposit, or the balance after the list, within 20 days (§ 5514(g)(1)).

A second penalty covers the account rules (§ 5514(g)(2)):

| Failure | Result |
|---|---|
| You do not tell the tenant where the account is within 20 days of a written request | You forfeit the whole deposit to the tenant |
| You do not keep the deposit in a federally insured bank with a Delaware office | You forfeit the whole deposit to the tenant |
| You do not return a forfeited deposit within 20 days | Tenant is entitled to double the deposit |

Worked example: a tenant paid $1,500. You keep $600 for damage but send no list until day 30. The list came late, so the law treats it as your acknowledgment that no damages are due. The $600 is wrongfully withheld, and double that is $1,200.

## A move-out checklist for Delaware

- Confirm the lease length and whether the unit is furnished. Then check the cap.
- Open a security deposits account at a federally insured bank with a Delaware office.
- Tell the tenant where the account is, in writing.
- At move-out, confirm the forwarding address in writing.
- Inspect and photograph. Price each repair.
- Send the itemized list and the balance by day 20.
