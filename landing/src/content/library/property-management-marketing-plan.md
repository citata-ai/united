## What goes into a property management marketing plan?

A property management marketing plan holds seven parts, and each one fits on a page. Long plans sit in a folder. A short plan with numbers gets used every month.

| Part | What it says | Example |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Door goal | Net doors to add this year | +60 net doors |
| 2. Owner profile | Who you want, and who you do not | Single-family and 2 to 4 unit owners in 6 suburbs |
| 3. Lead goals | Leads, meetings and signed owners per month | 12 qualified leads a month |
| 4. Channels | 3 to 4 channels, why each one | GBP and reviews, local SEO, referrals |
| 5. Budget | Spend per channel per month | $2,000 a month total |
| 6. Tracking | How you count leads and doors by source | CRM source field, UTM tags |
| 7. Review | Who checks what, and when | Monthly numbers, quarterly plan |

Write the owner profile with care. "Every owner" is not a profile. Name the property types, cities and sizes you serve best, so your pages and ads speak to them.

## Which channels should a small property management company start with?

A small property management company should start with the channels where owners already look: Google Business Profile and reviews, local SEO, and referrals. Goodjuju Marketing's fall 2026 study of 35 property management companies found Google Search and the Map Pack brought 50.6% of owner leads. With Direct and Maps, Google's local ecosystem brought 86.2%.

| Channel | Owner lead share, Goodjuju fall 2026 | Start now? | First tasks |
|---|---|---|---|
| Google Business Profile and reviews | Part of 50.6% (Search and Map Pack) | Yes | Complete profile, tag link, ask every owner for a review |
| Local SEO (owner pages, fee page) | Part of 50.6% | Yes | Owner page per real city, full fee page |
| Referrals (owners, agents) | Not measured well by GA4 | Yes | Ask after wins, agent partner list |
| Directories and listings | 3.7% (referral sites) | Yes, one-time cleanup | Fix NAP on Yelp, Bing, BBB, AI-cited directories |
| AI visibility | 3.4%, up from 2.1% in spring | Track monthly | Ask AI who it names in your city |
| Paid search | 1.2% (only 6 of 35 ran it) | Only with tracking | Negative keywords, owner-only landing page |
| Social media | 0.8% | For brand, not leads | Post owner stories and hiring news |

Google's local ranking help page says complete and accurate profiles are more likely to show in local results, and more reviews and positive ratings can help local ranking. That makes the profile the cheapest first step.

Add paid search only once you can count signed doors by source. Goodjuju warns its paid data covers 6 companies and misses calls and outside landing pages.

## How do I set monthly owner lead goals from a door goal?

Work backward from the door goal. Every number below should come from your own records. Use these example numbers only to see the method.

### Inputs (example numbers)

- Doors today: 300
- Net growth goal: +60 doors this year
- Yearly door churn: 10%, so 30 doors lost
- Gross doors to sign: 60 + 30 = 90
- Doors per new owner: 1.5
- Lead-to-signed-owner rate (qualified leads): 25%

### Calculation

| Step | Formula | Result |
|---|---|---|
| Owners to sign this year | 90 ÷ 1.5 | 60 owners |
| Qualified owner leads needed | 60 ÷ 0.25 | 240 leads |
| Qualified leads per month | 240 ÷ 12 | 20 a month |
| Raw owner leads per month (70% qualify) | 20 ÷ 0.70 | about 29 a month |

Churn often decides the plan. In this example, one third of all signing work only replaces lost doors. Cutting churn from 10% to 7% would remove 9 doors, or 6 owners, from the goal.

### Split the goal by channel

Use your last 12 months of signed owners by source as the starting split. If 60% of signed owners came from Google, 25% from referrals and 15% from other sources, set channel goals in the same ratio, then adjust as new channels prove themselves.

Set budget from value, not habit. Work out owner lifetime value from your fees, churn and doors per owner, pick a maximum cost per signed door, and check it against cash flow. Our [property management fee calculator](/tools/property-management-fee-calculator) helps with the fee side.

## How often should I review the property management marketing plan?

Review the numbers each month and the plan each quarter. Small firms sign a few owners a month, so one month of data per channel is noise. Change a channel only after 3 months.

### Review calendar

| When | Who | What |
|---|---|---|
| Weekly | Marketing lead | New leads logged with source; follow-up within 1 business day |
| Monthly | Owner of the firm + marketing lead | Leads, meetings, signed owners, signed doors, cost per door, churn |
| Quarterly | Leadership | Keep, change or stop each channel; update budget |
| Yearly | Leadership | New door goal and owner profile |

### First 90 days

1. **Week 1:** Set up tracking. Required "How did you first hear about us?" field, hidden UTM fields, GBP link tagged. Google Analytics Help says to always use utm_source, utm_medium and utm_campaign.
2. **Weeks 1 to 2:** Complete the Google Business Profile. Fix the top 10 listings.
3. **Weeks 2 to 4:** Publish the fee page and the first city owner page.
4. **Weeks 3 to 6:** Start review requests and referral asks.
5. **Weeks 4 to 8:** Use Keyword Planner to pick the next 3 city or service pages. Google Ads Help says it shows monthly search estimates by location.
6. **Weeks 8 to 12:** First quarterly review.
