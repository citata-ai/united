## Why do Google reviews matter to a property manager?

Google reviews help both rank and trust. Google says "More reviews and positive ratings can help your business's local ranking." Owners also read them before they call.

The BrightLocal Local Consumer Review Survey 2026 asked 1,002 US adults:

| Finding | Share |
|---|---|
| Only use a business with 4 stars or more | 68% |
| Only use a business with 4.5 stars or more | 31% |
| Look for reviews from the last 3 months | 74% |
| Were asked to write a review in the last 12 months | 78% |
| Always write a review when asked | 28% |

Two lessons for a property manager. Recent reviews count, so you need a steady flow. And people write when asked, so you must ask.

## What are the rules for asking?

Google lets you ask every customer for an honest review. It bans anything that buys or bends the review. Read these rules before you build a campaign.

| Allowed | Not allowed |
|---|---|
| Ask every customer for an honest review | Give gifts, discounts, rent credit or cash for a review (Google policy; 16 CFR 465.4 when tied to sentiment) |
| Send a link or QR code | Ask only happy customers, or screen with a survey first (Google: "selectively solicit positive reviews") |
| Ask by email, text, WhatsApp or on paper | Pay to remove or change a negative review |
| Remind once | Ask staff or relatives to post reviews (Google conflict of interest; 16 CFR 465.5) |
| Reply to all reviews | Pressure people while you stand with them, or tell them what to write |
| Report reviews that break policy | Write fake reviews or buy them (16 CFR 465.2) |

The FTC published its rule on August 22, 2024 (89 FR 68077). Its definition of "business" covers any person or company that sells products or services, so a small property manager is in scope.

Watch for: "review gating" is the most common trap. A tool asks "How did we do?" and sends only 5-star answers to Google. Google bans this. Send the review link to everyone.

This is general information, not legal advice. Check the current law or ask a local attorney.

## When is the best time to ask for a review?

Ask within 24 hours of a moment that went well. The person still feels the result. This list maps the moments in a property manager's year.

| Moment | Who | Why it works |
|---|---|---|
| Lease signed and keys handed over | New tenant | Relief, fresh memory of the leasing team |
| Repair closed within 48 hours | Tenant | Clear proof of service |
| First owner statement sent | New owner | First proof that money flows |
| Vacancy filled | Owner | The main thing the owner paid for |
| Lease renewal signed | Tenant | They chose to stay |
| One-year anniversary | Owner | Time to reflect on a full year |

Do not ask after a dispute, a rent increase notice or an eviction filing. Do ask every person who hits a moment, even if you think they had a hard time. That keeps you inside Google's rule against selective requests.

## Should I ask owners, tenants or both?

Ask both, at their own moments. Owners and tenants give different proof.

- **Owner reviews** speak to the next owner. They mention statements, vacancy and trust with money. An owner who reads "I get my statement on the 10th every month" learns what you deliver.
- **Tenant reviews** are more numerous. A 200-door manager has more tenants than owners. Tenants judge repairs and communication, which owners also care about.

A new owner who reads only tenant complaints about slow repairs will not call. So answer every tenant review too, in a calm and short way.

## How do review drip campaigns work?

A drip campaign sends review requests on a fixed rule, not in one blast. Your software or a simple task list triggers a message when a moment happens.

A sample drip for a 150-door manager:

1. **Trigger:** work order closed, lease signed, owner statement sent or renewal signed.
2. **Day 0:** send one short text or email with the review link.
3. **Day 4:** send one reminder if no review appears. Then stop.
4. **Monthly:** count requests sent and reviews received.

A worked example. Say you close 60 work orders, sign 8 leases and 6 renewals and onboard 2 owners in a month. That is 76 moments. If 1 in 10 people writes a review, you get about 7 or 8 reviews a month. Your real rate will differ. Track it for 3 months, then set a goal.

Do not send more than one reminder. A third message feels like pressure, and Google bans pressure.

## How do I make the review link easy?

Use Google's own link. Google's help page gives the path:

1. Open your Business Profile.
2. Select **Read reviews**, then **Get more reviews**.
3. Select **Copy** for the link, or save the QR code.

Google says the QR code can be made only in a computer browser. Put the QR code on move-in packets, the office door and the back of a business card. Put the link in email signatures and text messages.

Google also notes that a reviewer must sign in to a Google Account. A person without Gmail can make one with any email address. Tell people this in one line. It removes the most common blocker.

## What should the review request say?

Keep it short, personal and neutral. Do not ask for 5 stars or for words about a staff member. Google bans requests for specific content.

Tenant, after a repair:

```text
Hi [first name], this is [your name] at [company]. Your [repair] is done. If you have a minute, an honest Google review helps other renters and owners know what to expect: [link]. Thank you.
```

Owner, after the first statement:

```text
Hi [first name], your first owner statement for [address] is in the portal. If you have a minute, could you leave an honest Google review of your first month with us? It helps other owners decide: [link]. Reply here with any question about the statement.
```

## What about Yelp and other sites?

Yelp works the other way. Its support page says "Don't ask anyone to review your business." Yelp software may not recommend reviews that look prompted. So do not send Yelp links in your drip.

You can still claim your Yelp page and reply to reviews there. AI answers often cite review sites, so keep the facts on each one correct. For the wider effect of reviews on AI answers, read [how owners choose a property manager with AI](/guides/how-owners-choose-a-property-manager-with-ai).

## Review request checklist

- Google review link and QR code saved.
- 6 moments mapped to a trigger in your software or task list.
- Same request to every person at a moment, with no survey gate.
- One reminder at most.
- No gifts, credits, raffles or discounts tied to reviews.
- No reviews from staff, relatives or vendors.
- Every new review answered within a week.
- Requests sent and reviews received counted each month.
