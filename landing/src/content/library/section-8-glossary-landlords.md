## What are HAP, TTP, RFTA and PHA?

HAP, TTP, RFTA and PHA are the four terms on almost every page a voucher landlord gets. In one line each:

- **PHA (public housing agency).** The local housing authority that runs the voucher program, approves your unit and pays you.
- **HAP (housing assistance payment).** The PHA's monthly payment to you. Under 24 CFR 982.505, it is the lower of the payment standard or the gross rent, minus the TTP.
- **TTP (Total Tenant Payment).** The family's minimum monthly payment toward rent and utilities. Under 24 CFR 5.628 it is the highest of 30% of monthly adjusted income, 10% of monthly income, a welfare rent, or the minimum rent.
- **RFTA (Request for Tenancy Approval).** Form HUD-52517. The family and owner send it to the PHA to start inspection and approval, as HUD's 2024 HUD-VASH notice names it.

## What is the difference between FMR and payment standard?

The FMR is HUD's number and the payment standard is the PHA's number. HUD sets the Fair Market Rent (FMR) at the 40th percentile of recent-mover rents in an area, including utilities (24 CFR 888.113). The PHA then sets its payment standard, the cap on its subsidy, at 90% to 110% of the FMR without HUD approval (24 CFR 982.503(c)).

| | FMR | Payment standard |
|---|---|---|
| Set by | HUD | The PHA |
| Updated | Each federal fiscal year, from October 1 | Within 3 months of a new FMR, if needed to stay in range |
| Includes utilities | Yes | Yes, it caps rent plus utility allowance |
| Changes your lease | No | No, it changes the HAP |

To see FY2027 FMRs for the 50 largest metros, read [Section 8 rent limits by metro](/guides/section-8-rent-limits).

## What is abatement?

Abatement means the PHA stops paying HAP for a unit that failed housing standards and was not fixed in time. Under 24 CFR 982.404, the owner must fix a life-threatening item within 24 hours and other items within 30 days. If the owner misses the deadline, the PHA must abate the HAP. If the unit still fails 60 days after the PHA's finding, the PHA ends the HAP contract. You may not evict the family because the HAP was abated.

## What is portability?

Portability is a family's right to move with its voucher to the area of another PHA. 24 CFR 982.4 defines it as renting a unit "outside the jurisdiction of the initial PHA." The PHA where the family moves is the receiving PHA. It runs the voucher under its own policies and pays you (24 CFR 982.355).

## What is NSPIRE-V?

NSPIRE is HUD's newer inspection standard, set out in 24 CFR 5.703. Landlords and PHAs often call the voucher version NSPIRE-V. A HUD Federal Register notice of September 30, 2025 extended the compliance date for the voucher program: PHAs must use NSPIRE by February 1, 2027. Until then, a PHA may keep using the older Housing Quality Standards. The same notice says the smoke alarm and carbon monoxide rules apply now, because they come from federal law.
