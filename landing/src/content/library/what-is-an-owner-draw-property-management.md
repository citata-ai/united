## How does a property manager calculate the owner draw?

A property manager calculates the owner draw by taking cash collected, then subtracting bills, the fee and the reserve top-up. Buildium's guide to owner distributions gives the order:

1. **Income.** Cash actually deposited to the trust account for the property: rent, late fees, pet rent. Unpaid rent and pending fees do not count.
2. **Expenses.** Operating costs already paid. For approved invoices not yet paid, the manager either holds the amount or deducts it next month.
3. **Management fee.** A percent of collected rent or a flat amount, as the agreement says.
4. **Reserves and minimum balances.** Fund the agreed reserve and keep any minimum balance.
5. **Owner contributions.** Money the owner sent in, tracked apart from rent.
6. **Chargebacks and holds.** Bounced payment fees, damage above the deposit, and money held for a set purpose, such as an insurance claim.

TenantCloud's help page puts the core of it in one formula: distribution = (owner balance − property reserve) × ownership share. The ownership share matters when 2 or more people own the property.

## What does an owner draw look like with real numbers?

Here is a worked example for a duplex in one month. The numbers are an example.

| Line | Calculation | Amount |
|---|---|---|
| Rent collected, unit A | Paid in full | $1,500.00 |
| Rent collected, unit B | Tenant has not paid yet | $0.00 |
| Late fee collected, unit A | Paid last month's fee | $50.00 |
| **Income** | | **$1,550.00** |
| Plumber, unit A | Work order paid | − $320.00 |
| Management fee | 8% × $1,500 collected | − $120.00 |
| **Owner balance** | $1,550 − $320 − $120 | **$1,110.00** |
| Reserve target | $250 × 2 units | $500.00 |
| Reserve before this month | After an earlier repair | $300.00 |
| Reserve top-up | $500 − $300 | − $200.00 |
| **Owner draw** | $1,110 − $200 | **$910.00** |

The owner expected about $3,000 of rent. The draw is $910. Unit B's unpaid $1,500 is the main reason, then the repair, the fee and the reserve top-up. When unit B pays, that rent goes into the next draw, less the fee on it.

## When do owners get paid by their property manager?

Owners get paid on the schedule in the management agreement. Buildium's guide says the schedule can be monthly, quarterly or custom. Buildium adds that payouts go out after the books are reconciled and exceptions are checked.

Buildium gives typical transfer times:

| Method | Time to arrive |
|---|---|
| ACH | 1 to 2 business days |
| Check | 5 to 7 business days |
| Wire | Same or next day |

Ask your manager for the exact payout day. A fixed day, such as the 12th, is easier to plan around than "early in the month".

## Why is my owner draw less than the rent?

Your owner draw is less than the rent because the manager pays costs out of rent before paying you. Check these 6 causes in order:

- [ ] **Unpaid or late rent.** Only cash collected counts as income.
- [ ] **Management fee.** Check the rate and the base (rent collected or rent due).
- [ ] **Repairs and bills.** Match each one to a work order or invoice.
- [ ] **Reserve top-up.** After a repair, the manager refills the reserve before paying you.
- [ ] **Leasing or renewal fee.** These often fall in one month.
- [ ] **Minimum payout.** Buildium mentions minimums such as $50 or $100. A smaller balance can roll to next month.

If the statement does not explain the gap, ask in writing for the line-by-line calculation.

## What is held back before an owner draw?

Three kinds of money stay in the trust account: tenant money, reserves and holds for known bills.

| Held back | Why | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Security deposits | They belong to the tenant | TenantCloud excludes them from the draw |
| Owner reserve | Cash for repairs and bills | iPropertyManagement: typically $250 per unit |
| Approved but unpaid bills | So the bill can be paid | Buildium |
| Money for a set purpose | Insurance claim, tax payment | Buildium |
| Cash cushion (some managers) | About one month of typical expenses | Buildium |

Security deposits are not your income while you hold them to return. The IRS says a deposit you plan to return is not income when you receive it. This is general information, not tax advice. Check the current rules or ask a tax professional.

## How does the owner reserve affect the draw?

The reserve lowers the draw in the month the manager builds or refills it, and has no effect when it is full. Buildium suggests a maintenance reserve as a fixed amount per unit each month, set by the property's age and condition.

Look at the example above. The reserve target is $500. A repair earlier left $300, so this month the manager adds $200 before the draw. Next month, if nothing breaks, the reserve is full and the full balance goes to the owner.

Some states require the reserve in writing. Arizona's ARS 32-2173 says the management agreement must state the amount and purpose of any operating reserve. Check that your agreement has a number, not "an amount the manager decides".
