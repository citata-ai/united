## Normal wear and tear vs damage: what is the line?

Normal wear and tear is the slow decline from ordinary daily living. Damage is harm from an accident, misuse, neglect or a pet. You may deduct for damage. You may not deduct for normal wear and tear.

California Civil Code 1950.5 states the rule. A landlord may use the deposit to repair damage caused by the tenant or guests, but not ordinary wear and tear. The landlord may also deduct cleaning needed to return the unit to its move-in level of cleanliness.

Most statutes do not list examples, so use a consistent test: "Would this happen in a careful household over this many years?" The table below gives typical examples. It is a practical guide, not a legal definition.

| Item | Usually normal wear and tear | Usually damage |
|---|---|---|
| Walls | Light scuffs, small nail holes, faded paint | Large holes, crayon, unapproved paint colors |
| Carpet | Traffic paths, light fading | Burns, pet urine, large stains, tears |
| Floors | Light surface scratches | Deep gouges, water damage from a spill left |
| Doors | Loose handle from use | Hole in a door, broken frame |
| Blinds | Sun fading, light bends | Broken or missing slats |
| Kitchen | Worn finish on counters | Burns, cuts in counters, broken shelves |
| Bathroom | Worn grout, dull fixtures | Cracked sink, mold from a fan never used |
| Yard | Seasonal wear | Dead lawn the lease told the tenant to water, trash left |

Age matters. A 7-year-old carpet with traffic paths is wear. A 1-year-old carpet with a burn is damage. Charge only the share of value the tenant took, not the full cost of a new item.

This is general information, not legal advice. Check the current law or ask a local attorney.

## Should the tenant attend the move-out inspection?

Invite the tenant whenever you can, and follow the state rule where one exists. A joint walk-through turns many disputes into a short talk in the room.

| State | Rule | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Virginia | Landlord gives written notice of the right to attend within 5 days after notice of intent to vacate. The tenant who asks in writing may attend. Inspection within 72 hours of delivery of possession. | Code 55.1-1226 |
| California | Landlord tells the tenant in writing of the right to request an initial inspection, no earlier than 2 weeks before the end. At least 48 hours of written notice, unless both sides waive it. | Civil Code 1950.5(f) |

California's initial inspection is a preview. The landlord gives the tenant a list of problems, and the tenant can fix them before leaving to avoid deductions.

## How do you compare move-in and move-out reports?

Compare the two reports line by line, using the same form and the same order. Each line gets one of 3 results: same, wear, or damage.

Step list:

1. **Open the signed move-in report** and its photo set before you enter the unit.
2. **Walk in the same order** as the move-in inspection.
3. **For each line, write the result:** same / wear / damage.
4. **For each damage line, add** a photo number, a short cause and a repair estimate.
5. **Note cleaning needs** separately from damage.
6. **Record keys returned** and meter readings.
7. **Sign and date** the report. Ask the tenant to sign if present.

Worked example of a deduction line, with age taken into account. The numbers are an example.

| Item | Move-in | Move-out | Result | Calculation | Deduction |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Living room carpet | Good, 2 years old | Pet stain, 1 m² | Damage | Clean and patch quote | $180 |
| Bedroom wall | Good | 3 small nail holes | Wear | None | $0 |
| Kitchen blinds | Good | 4 slats broken | Damage | Replace 1 blind | $45 |
| Oven | Clean | Heavy grease | Cleaning | 1 hour × $40 | $40 |
| **Total** | | | | | **$265** |

## Which photos should you take?

Take photos in the same views as at move-in, before anyone cleans or repairs. Then take photos after each repair you deduct.

California sets this as a rule. Civil Code 1950.5(g) requires photos within a reasonable time after the unit is returned, and before any repair or cleaning you deduct. It also requires photos within a reasonable time after that work is done. Since April 1, 2025, you must send these photos with the itemized statement when you deduct for repairs or cleaning. You can send them by mail, email, flash drive or an online link.

Photo checklist:

- [ ] One wide shot per wall, in the move-in order
- [ ] A close-up with a scale for each damage line
- [ ] Inside each appliance
- [ ] Under each sink
- [ ] Each floor area with stains or marks
- [ ] Keys and remotes returned
- [ ] Meter readings
- [ ] After photos of each repair or cleaning you deduct

## When must you send the deposit statement?

Send the itemized statement and any refund before your state's deadline. California gives the landlord 21 calendar days after the tenant leaves. Mark the date on the day of move-out.

In the example above, the deposit is $1,500 and the deductions are $265. The tenant gets $1,235 back with a statement that lists the 3 items, the photos and the cost of each.
