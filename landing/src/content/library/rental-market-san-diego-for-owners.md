## How much did HUD's fair market rent change in San Diego for FY2027?

HUD's fair market rent for a 2-bedroom in San Diego fell 5.9% for FY2027, from $3,001 to $2,823. The FY2027 figures took effect on 2026-10-01 (Federal Register notice FR-6553-N-03). The area is all of San Diego County.

| Unit size | FY2026 FMR | FY2027 FMR | Change |
|---|---|---|---|
| Studio | $2,288 | $2,192 | -4.2% |
| 1 bedroom | $2,459 | $2,335 | -5.0% |
| 2 bedrooms | $3,001 | $2,823 | -5.9% |
| 3 bedrooms | $3,998 | $3,743 | -6.4% |
| 4 bedrooms | $4,845 | $4,526 | -6.6% |

Source: HUD FY2027 and FY2026 FMR files. Every size fell, and larger units fell more.

What this means for you: if you rent to a voucher holder, the FMR sets the band for the housing authority's payment standard. Under 24 CFR 982.503, the basic range is 90% to 110% of the FMR. A lower FMR can mean a lower ceiling. See all sizes and the housing authority link on the [San Diego fair market rent 2027 page](/guides/section-8-rent-limits/san-diego-fair-market-rent-2027).

## Are San Diego rents rising or falling?

San Diego market rents are rising slowly. Zillow's Observed Rent Index (ZORI, the typical asking rent for all home types) reached a record for the San Diego metro in September 2026.

| Point in time | Zillow rent index | Change |
|---|---|---|
| September 2019 | $2,056 | Before the boom |
| September 2025 | $2,904 | +41.2% from 2019 |
| August 2026 | $2,966 | |
| September 2026 | $2,967 | +2.2% in 1 year, a record |

We did the math from the Zillow file. The US index rose 2.7% in the same year, so San Diego grew a little slower than the country. The month-to-month change in September was close to 0.

Yardi Matrix measures apartments only. Its June 2026 report put the San Diego average asking rent at $2,718. Asking rents slid 0.1% over the 3 months through April.

Why does HUD's number fall while Zillow's rises? The two sources measure different homes over different periods. For your rent price, use current local listings. For a voucher tenant, use the HUD and housing authority numbers.

## What vacancy rate does San Diego have?

San Diego has low vacancy. Yardi Matrix put occupancy in stabilized properties at 95.9% in March 2026, down 0.3 points in a year. That leaves about 4.1% of those units empty.

New supply is moderate. Yardi Matrix counted 11,703 units under construction and 2,204 units delivered in 2026 through April. It expects more than 8,000 units to open in 2026.

Watch for: a vacant week costs more here than in most metros. At $2,967 a month, each empty week costs about $685.

## Which local rules affect San Diego rental returns?

State and city rules cap rent increases, cap deposits and set relocation pay in San Diego. These rules move your return the most.

| Rule | What it says | Effect on your return |
|---|---|---|
| Cal. Civil Code 1947.12 | Rent increase over any 12 months is capped at 5% plus the local cost of living change, max 10% | Limits raises on covered units |
| Cal. Civil Code 1950.5 | Deposit cap of 1 month's rent; 2 months for a small landlord (natural person, up to 2 properties and 4 units) | Less cash held against damage |
| San Diego Mun. Code 98.0706 | No-fault termination pays 2 months of current rent, or 3 months for a senior (62+) or disabled tenant | A sale or remodel costs more |
| San Diego Mun. Code 98.0703 | Exempts housing with a certificate of occupancy from the last 15 years, and some owner-occupied homes | Newer units skip just cause rules |

The rent cap in Section 1947.12 uses the San Diego-Carlsbad CPI-U for San Diego homes. It also exempts housing built in the last 15 years and some single-family homes owned by natural persons, if the tenant got the written exemption notice. Section 1947.12 stays in effect until 2030-01-01.

Worked example: you own a covered unit at $2,967. If the local cost of living rose 3%, your cap is 8%, so the most you can raise in 12 months is $237.36. If it rose 6%, the cap is 10%, or $296.70. Check the actual CPI change before you send a notice.

For the full city ordinance, the relocation rules and the remodel limits, read the San Diego tenant protection ordinance in the City of San Diego Municipal Code, Chapter 9, Article 8, Division 7.

This is general information, not legal advice. Check the current law or ask a local attorney.

## Should I rent or sell my San Diego home now?

Rent or sell in San Diego depends on your loan rate and your plans, because the rent yield is thin. Zillow's home value index (ZHVI) for the San Diego metro is $926,961 for September 2026. That is 0.8% more than a year ago and 2.0% below the July 2024 peak.

| Metro | Typical rent (ZORI) | Typical value (ZHVI) | Gross yield |
|---|---|---|---|
| San Diego | $2,967 | $926,961 | 3.84% |
| United States | $1,932 | $366,157 | 6.33% |

We did the math from the Zillow September 2026 files. A gross yield is before tax, insurance, repairs, vacancy and management.

Questions to answer before you decide:

- [ ] Does the rent cover your mortgage, tax, insurance and HOA? Owners with an old, low-rate loan often can say yes. New buyers often cannot.
- [ ] Do you plan to sell within a few years? A no-fault termination for a covered tenant costs 2 to 3 months of rent.
- [ ] Is your unit exempt from the rent cap (built in the last 15 years, or a qualifying single-family home with the notice)?
- [ ] Can you handle a 21-day deposit return and the written notices, or will you hire a manager?

If you rent, check the cost of help in the [property management fee calculator](/tools/property-management-fee-calculator). Low vacancy works in your favor. Thin yield and strict exit rules work against a short hold.
