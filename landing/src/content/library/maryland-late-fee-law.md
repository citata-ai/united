Maryland caps late fees in its list of banned lease clauses. I read Real Property § 8-208 on the Maryland General Assembly site on 2026-10-19. Subsection (d)(3) sets the cap, and § 8-401 shows how late fees work in a rent case.

| Rule | Limit or deadline | Statute |
|---|---|---|
| Monthly or other rent periods | Max 5% of unpaid rent due for that period | § 8-208(d)(3)(i) |
| Weekly rent | Max $3 a week, $12 a month | § 8-208(d)(3)(ii) |
| Grace period | None set by statute | § 8-208 |
| Written lease | Required for landlords offering 5+ units | § 8-208(a) |
| Rent case complaint | States rent and late fees due | § 8-401(b)(2) |
| Court finding | Rent and late fees due | § 8-401(e)(2) |
| Redemption | Pay past-due amounts found, plus costs | § 8-401(h) |
| Deposit use | Unpaid rent, breach of lease, damage | § 8-203(f) |

## What is the maximum late fee a landlord can charge in Maryland?

The maximum Maryland late fee is 5% of the unpaid rent for the late period. Section 8-208(d)(3)(i) bars a lease that "provides for a penalty for the late payment of rent in excess of 5% of the amount of unpaid rent due for the rental period for which the payment was delinquent."

The base is the unpaid amount, not the full rent. If a tenant pays part of the rent on time, the cap is 5% of what is still unpaid.

| Monthly rent | Unpaid on the due date | Maximum late fee |
|---|---|---|
| $1,500 | $1,500 | $75 |
| $1,500 | $500 | $25 |
| $2,000 | $2,000 | $100 |
| $2,400 | $1,200 | $60 |

We did the math from the statute.

## What is the cap for weekly rent in Maryland?

For a lease with weekly rent, § 8-208(d)(3)(ii) bars a late penalty "of more than $3 per week or a total of no more than $12 per month."

| Weeks late in a month | Maximum at $3 a week | Monthly cap | Allowed |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | $3 | $12 | $3 |
| 3 | $9 | $12 | $9 |
| 5 | $15 | $12 | $12 |

## Does Maryland law require a grace period before a late fee?

No. Real Property § 8-208 sets no grace period. The lease sets the due date and when the late fee starts.

A short grace period of a few days is a business choice. It can cut disputes over mail and bank timing. Check local law where your unit is, too.

## Must the late fee be written in the lease in Maryland?

Yes, in practice. The cap in § 8-208(d)(3) limits what a lease may provide, so the fee comes from the lease.

Section 8-208(a) also requires a written lease for any landlord "who offers 5 or more dwelling units for rent in the State." If such a landlord rents without one, the tenancy is presumed to be for 1 year.

Write these items in the lease:

- **Due date** and how to pay.
- **When the fee applies.**
- **Amount,** at or below 5% of the unpaid rent for the period.
- **Weekly rent:** $3 a week, $12 a month at most.

## Can a Maryland landlord charge a daily late fee?

Section 8-208 does not use the word "daily," but it caps the total "penalty for the late payment of rent" at 5% of the unpaid rent for the period. All daily charges for that period together must stay at or below 5%.

Worked example for $1,600 unpaid rent (cap: $80):

| Fee design | Total after 20 late days | Legal? |
|---|---|---|
| Flat $80 | $80 | Yes |
| $5 a day, capped at $80 | $80 | Yes |
| $5 a day, no cap | $100 | No |

## Can a Maryland landlord evict a tenant for unpaid late fees?

Maryland folds late fees into the failure-to-pay-rent case. The complaint states "the amount of rent and any late fees due and unpaid" (§ 8-401(b)(2)(iii)). The landlord also lists "any late fees for overdue rent payments" (§ 8-401(b)(3)).

At trial, the court determines "the amount of rent and late fees due" (§ 8-401(e)(2)(ii)). If the trial is late, it counts late fees that accrued in or before the month of filing (§ 8-401(e)(2)(iii)).

The tenant can end the case at trial by paying the rent and late fees found due, plus costs (§ 8-401(e)(5)). After judgment, the tenant can redeem by paying "all past due amounts, as determined by the court," plus costs and fees, before the eviction (§ 8-401(h)).

Before filing, send the 10-day notice of intent to file required by § 8-401(c). A case about late fees alone, with rent fully paid, is a different question. Ask a Maryland attorney before you file one.

## Maryland late fee checklist

- Write the late fee in the lease. Use a written lease if you offer 5+ units.
- Cap it at 5% of the unpaid rent for the late period.
- For weekly rent, cap at $3 a week and $12 a month.
- Keep rent and late fees on separate ledger lines.
- In a rent case, list late fees in the complaint.
