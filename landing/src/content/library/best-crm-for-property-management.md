## Do you need a CRM if your PMS has one?

You need a separate CRM only when the built-in tool fails your owner-sales process. CRM means customer relationship management. A property management system, or PMS, may track renters without handling the path from owner inquiry to signed management agreement.

List the owner tasks your staff cannot reliably complete today. Those might include lead-source tracking, missed-call follow-up, agreement reminders and onboarding. Give each task a clear result.

Test the current PMS before buying another subscription. A simple owner pipeline may be enough if staff can find the next action and see who owns it. More software can add duplicate records when responsibilities remain unclear.

Keep owner sales separate from renter leasing in the demo. An owner may have several properties and several decision-makers. The CRM should preserve that relationship without treating every property as an unrelated sale.

## How do LeadSimple, Rentvine CRM and general CRMs compare?

LeadSimple packages sales and operations around property management. Rentvine connects its Owner Lead Portal to onboarding inside the property system. A general CRM requires you to define more of the property-specific record structure.

Rentvine's announcement describes owner lead stages, prequalification forms, management agreement generation and creation of property records at onboarding. It also calls this release the foundation for further owner CRM work. Do not buy future features as if they already exist.

LeadSimple's pricing page includes CRM pipelines and operations workflows. That can suit a team that wants sales work and post-sale tasks in the same workspace. Confirm the exact workflow during a demonstration.

For a general CRM, test an owner with several homes and a shared contact. Ask how the signed agreement becomes an operational property record. Count setup and support work, not only the advertised subscription.

## Which track owner leads through a signed agreement?

The right CRM should show an owner lead's source, responsible staff member, next action and agreement status. Rentvine's Owner Lead Portal explicitly describes the path from lead to onboarding. LeadSimple lists sales pipelines alongside operations workflows.

Ask the vendor to demonstrate the entire path. Enter a lead, record a call and send a follow-up. Change the proposed property count before the agreement goes out.

Then stop the process partway through. Can a colleague see what remains and continue without asking the original salesperson? A lead record is useful only when another person can understand it.

Track why a deal closes or fails. Avoid a pipeline where every lost lead simply disappears. Source and outcome records help you compare channels without relying on memory. Decide who checks those records and keeps stage definitions consistent.

## What does each cost?

Compare the complete bill for your team and workflow, not the smallest plan price. LeadSimple One lists $199 monthly with 50 managed doors included. Its page states extra-door pricing and communication allowances. Annual commitment changes the price and terms.

Rentvine advertises a platform rate as low as $1.50 per active unit, but that is not a separate CRM quote. Ask whether your chosen owner workflow, website connection and setup are included. Get the answer in the order form.

General CRMs can price by seats, modules or usage. Verify the selected plan directly. Do not use a price from a software roundup as your budget.

Include staff setup, imported contacts, phone numbers and ongoing administration. A cheap subscription that needs constant manual repair may be a poor fit. Keep those internal costs separate from the vendor invoice.

## Which integrate with your PMS?

LeadSimple's pricing page names AppFolio, Buildium, Rentvine, Propertyware and Rent Manager. Rentvine's launch page describes a direct PMW website lead connection. Neither statement defines every field or workflow your company needs.

Test the data exchange using scrubbed records. Change an owner email, add a property and correct a duplicate. Check where each update appears and which system controls the final value.

Ask about failed updates. Your staff need a visible alert and a clear support owner. An integration that fails silently can leave a signed client outside the onboarding queue.

Confirm required tiers and extra charges on both sides. Test the connection you plan to buy, rather than a different configuration shown in a sales account.

## What should your buying checklist include?

Choose the system that gives staff a clear next action and preserves the owner history. Use the same checklist for every finalist.

- **Lead source:** preserve the original channel and referral details.
- **Responsibility:** assign each lead and each handoff.
- **Follow-up:** show overdue work without a separate spreadsheet.
- **Agreement:** keep the signed file linked to the owner.
- **Onboarding:** transfer approved facts to the property system.
- **Reporting:** explain lost leads and signed owners by source.
- **Exit:** export contacts, notes and agreement records.

A CRM helps convert inquiries after they reach you. It does not create demand by itself. See [why owner leads are dropping](/guides/why-owner-leads-are-dropping) and [how owners choose a manager with AI](/guides/how-owners-choose-a-property-manager-with-ai).

Bring your sales lead and onboarding lead to the final demo. Have them run a real handoff, then approve the quote together.
