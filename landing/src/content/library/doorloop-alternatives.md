## How does DoorLoop price its plans?

DoorLoop now sells One, Connect and Multiply. The current DoorLoop pricing page replaces the Starter, Pro and Premium labels found in older comparisons. Check your own renewal terms before you assume a new plan applies.

DoorLoop lists core accounting, leasing, maintenance and portals in One. Connect adds its Receptionist Agent. Multiply adds agents for maintenance and renewals. Treat those as vendor descriptions, then ask for a live example using your policies.

A low price on an old review page can distort your shortlist. Save the current quote with its date, billing term and unit definition. Ask whether vacant, archived or inactive units count. Record support, setup and payment fees in the same sheet.

The right comparison starts with the work you need done. If you only need reports, paying for more automated conversations may solve the wrong problem. If calls interrupt your team, test the call flow before judging a higher plan by price alone.

## Which alternatives cost less?

Buildium and Yardi Breeze show lower entry prices in the table. That does not establish the lower total for your portfolio. Features, minimums and transaction charges change the result.

Make a cost sheet with separate lines for the base plan, payment processing, screening, signatures, migration and outside services. Enter your actual activity from recent records. A quote based only on occupied doors can miss the work created by vacancies and turns.

Ask each vendor to price the same task list. Include an owner statement run, a maintenance call and a lease renewal. Ask which steps require another product. Keep taxes and optional services separate from the base subscription.

Use the [property management fee calculator](/tools/property-management-fee-calculator) to see how software costs affect your own service fee. A cheaper application has little value if your team must rebuild reports outside it. Count that work in your decision, using your own staff costs.

## Which alternatives have stronger accounting?

Accounting fit needs a practical test, not a claim that one brand is stronger. Rent Manager lists accounting and custom reports in its Basic bundle. Rentvine makes trust accounting central to its pricing offer. Neither statement proves how well your bookkeeper will work in it.

Bring a clean sample ledger to the demo. Post rent, a vendor bill, a management fee and an owner contribution. Ask the presenter to correct an error without losing the record of the change. Then reconcile the result against a sample bank statement.

Check whether you can see each owner's cash position without mixing funds between properties. Inspect the owner statement and the detailed ledger together. Ask your bookkeeper to explain the result in plain words.

Do not choose based on a polished dashboard alone. Reports need to agree with the underlying entries. Ask where opening balances come from and who approves them after migration. Keep the old reports until you can reproduce the balances in the new system.

## Which alternatives suit commercial units?

Rent Manager lists commercial portfolios among its supported markets. Yardi Breeze publishes a separate commercial rate schedule. Those are useful shortlist signals, but commercial lease terms still need testing.

Bring a lease with the actual charge rules your team uses. Test recurring charges, expense recovery, annual adjustments and a tenant's share of shared costs. Ask the vendor to produce the tenant statement after a correction.

A residential payment portal can look suitable while missing a commercial accounting requirement. Ask how the system handles different lease dates, charge categories and supporting documents. Require the presenter to show the calculation, not just name a feature.

For a mixed portfolio, test reports across both property types. Confirm whether the same owner can see the right records without separate logins or manual exports. Ask how the contract prices each property type. Keep those answers in writing beside the demo notes.

## What should you do with reviews?

Use reviews to form questions, not to declare a winner. This guide does not repeat an unverified score or claim users leave for a single reason. Ratings can reflect a different version, plan or support arrangement.

Ask references what they moved from and what they still do outside the system. A complaint about slow support means more when you know the task, plan and response path. A positive review needs the same detail.

Use this reference checklist:

- Ask which report needs the most manual work.
- Ask how support handled a failed payment or wrong balance.
- Ask which feature required an unexpected plan upgrade.
- Ask what the team would change about its migration.
- Ask whether the owner portal reduced follow-up questions.

Keep each answer tied to a specific task. Avoid turning a single story into a claim about every customer. Your own bookkeeper and operations lead should test the same task before you decide.

## How should you move from a shortlist to a choice?

Pick the unresolved task that matters most and schedule a focused demo. Send the vendor the sample in advance. State what counts as a pass: a correct ledger, a clear statement or a recorded approval.

Ask who will import each record and verify each balance. Clarify whether documents, message history and payment authorizations move with the data. Do not cancel the old system until you have checked the exports and archive access.

Keep the decision small enough to explain to your team. Write down the cost, the task the new tool improves and the work it adds. If no vendor proves a clear gain, fix the process first.
