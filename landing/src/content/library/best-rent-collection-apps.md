## Which apps are free for landlords?

Avail Unlimited and Baselane Core advertise free subscriptions. Free software access does not mean every transaction or optional service is free. Compare the selected payment method and the account where rent lands.

Avail's free tier includes rent collection and basic rental tasks. Baselane Core includes bookkeeping and rent collection alongside its banking-related tools. Ask whether you need a particular deposit account to receive a fee waiver.

Hemlane's reviewed page separates free and paid feature sets. Its Basic rent-collection package lists a platform fee plus a unit charge. Confirm the plan required for your actual collection workflow before comparing it with a free account.

Write down what the subscription replaces. If you still need another ledger or report tool, keep that cost in the comparison. A free collection app can be useful, but it should not leave you without complete rental records.

## Who pays the ACH and card fees?

The payer depends on the product, plan and method. Avail's pricing page says renters on Unlimited pay $2.50 per bank transfer. It lists a 3.5% card processing fee regardless of subscription, while Unlimited Plus waives bank-transfer fees.

Hemlane lists no ACH transaction fee and a 3% card fee on its payment comparison. Confirm who pays each charge and whether your selected account has any extra conditions.

Baselane's page contains conflicting ACH details. Its fee table lists a range with a waiver tied to deposits, while its FAQ describes a flat charge. We do not choose a single universal Baselane ACH price from that conflict.

Ask for a written fee schedule and run a test payment. Inspect both the renter's receipt and the landlord's deposit. Keep fee pass-through authority separate from software settings.

## How fast do funds arrive?

Funds arrive according to the product's payout rules, verification status and payment method. An advertised fast-payment feature is not an unconditional settlement promise. Ask about new accounts, weekends, holidays, holds and returned payments.

Avail advertises FastPay on Unlimited Plus and describes next-day deposits among its payment features. Baselane advertises faster deposits with Smart. Those claims need the product's eligibility terms before you plan owner distributions around them.

Trace a test from submission to available funds. A tenant confirmation screen can show successful submission before the bank deposit is available. Ask the vendor to explain each status in plain terms.

Plan a buffer in your operating process rather than assuming every payment arrives on the best-case date. Review the actual timing before promising a distribution schedule to owners. Keep a clear process for failed or reversed payments.

## Which block partial payments?

A payment app should demonstrate partial-payment controls before you rely on them. The reviewed pricing pages do not establish a complete comparison of these controls. We therefore do not rank the apps on an unverified claim.

Ask the vendor to create an open balance and attempt a smaller payment. Test a new payment, an existing autopay instruction and a shared household payment. Check whether the controls differ by method.

Then ask staff to disable online payment for a test account. Confirm the effect on already scheduled transactions. A switch that blocks new payments may not behave the same way for earlier instructions.

Have local counsel review the process before using it during a dispute. Software settings do not settle the legal effect of accepting or refusing a payment. This is general information, not legal advice. Check the current law or ask a local attorney.

## Which report rent to credit bureaus?

Avail lists CreditBoost, Baselane lists rent reporting and Hemlane describes credit-building services. Those labels do not establish identical coverage, cost or reporting rules. Ask for the current service terms.

Check which bureaus receive the record and who enrolls. Ask how roommates, missed payments, disputes and corrections work. Confirm whether the service reports past payments or only future activity.

Explain the service to renters using the provider's current terms. Do not promise a credit-score increase. A reporting feature is not proof of a particular financial result for a renter.

Keep rent reporting separate from your payment ledger. If a transaction changes, the reporting record may need its own correction. Ask who handles that request and how renters contact support. Include the fee in the renter experience review even when your business does not pay it.

## What should you test before collecting live rent?

Test the money, records and exception path before asking renters to enroll.

- **Enrollment:** verify a renter can see charges and payment options.
- **Fees:** compare the screen, receipt and final deposit.
- **Timing:** record submission, processing and available-funds dates.
- **Controls:** test partial payments and scheduled instructions.
- **Returns:** reverse a test entry and inspect the ledger.
- **Export:** download a usable payment history.
- **Support:** identify who resolves a missing or disputed payment.

Confirm your bookkeeping process can match deposits to individual charges. A collection app should reduce unexplained transactions rather than create another unreviewed account.
