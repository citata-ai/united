## What does an AI leasing assistant do?

An AI leasing assistant handles defined prospect conversations and passes unresolved questions to staff. EliseAI describes answering questions, following up and booking tours across communication channels. Showdigs Plus describes AI coverage for calls and messages. These functions still need correct property data and rules.

Start with the questions your staff answer repeatedly. Availability, rent, parking, application steps and tour times make a useful test set. Give the assistant an approved source for each answer.

Then test questions that lack an answer. The assistant should say what is unknown and route the request. An invented parking policy creates work even when the reply sounds clear.

Separate drafting from action. Writing a proposed reply needs less access than changing a tour or contacting a prospect. Ask which actions need approval, where the record stays and how staff stop an incorrect action.

## How does EliseAI differ from AI built into the PMS?

EliseAI sells dedicated leasing and resident tools, while built-in AI operates within an existing property management system. A property management system, or PMS, holds operational records. AppFolio lists AI tools by plan alongside accounting, leasing and maintenance.

The useful question is where data and work remain authoritative. Ask whether the assistant reads live availability or a delayed copy. Check where staff edit a policy and how quickly the assistant uses the change.

A separate product can add another inbox or connection to support. Built-in tools can still require extra modules. Neither model guarantees better answers or less work.

Ask staff to handle a conversation that starts by phone and continues by text. Check whether the full history stays together. Choose based on the handoff your team can support, rather than whether the product is separate or embedded.

## How do AI assistants handle showings?

A leasing assistant should book only valid times and use an approved access process. EliseAI describes tour booking. Tenant Turner describes scheduling, access control and an optional AI Virtual Agent. Showdigs combines leasing software with self-showings and optional agent services.

Test a changed appointment, an occupied home and a blocked calendar. Ask what happens when the prospect requests a time that does not exist. The assistant should offer valid alternatives without overriding restrictions.

Treat access codes separately from conversational skill. Ask when identity checks occur and when access becomes available. Test cancellation before the scheduled visit and confirm that access ends.

Keep a staff path for a renter who cannot complete the automated process. A successful showing workflow includes a clear exception route. Measure completed visits and staff corrections, rather than counting scheduled tours alone.

## What are the Fair Housing risks?

The main operating risk is an assistant giving different answers or recommendations based on personal traits rather than stated property facts. This guide offers buying controls, not a legal interpretation. Have local counsel review your leasing scripts and screening process.

Use the same approved policy source for every prospect. Ask the assistant about accessibility, an accommodation request and an unclear application requirement. Check whether it routes sensitive questions to trained staff.

Test paired prompts with identical property needs and different personal descriptions. Compare the answers. A tool should not invent neighborhood claims, suggest who belongs in a property or add screening rules.

Log the prompt, answer, source and staff review. Remove unsafe replies from the approved material and repeat the test after changes. Do not treat a vendor's fairness claim as a substitute for your review.

This is general information, not legal advice. Check the current law or ask a local attorney.

## How are AI leasing assistants priced?

Pricing depends on the product, module, usage and portfolio. EliseAI's reviewed page gives no dollar amount, so it is quote only. AppFolio's base plan price does not establish the cost of every leasing function.

Ask whether the bill depends on managed units, active listings, conversations or voice minutes. Include overages, onboarding and the connection to your existing software. Ask for a bill at normal demand and at your busy-season demand.

List which current charges would disappear after launch. Do not count savings from an answering service you still need. Keep staff review time in the comparison, even if it does not appear on the vendor invoice.

Request a sample itemized bill. Have the vendor identify every optional line. A lower base price can cost more when the plan excludes the channels your prospects use.

## What should your pilot prove?

The pilot should prove accurate answers, valid actions and reliable handoff. Use scrubbed examples from your own leasing inbox.

- **Availability:** change a listing and check the assistant's answer.
- **Policies:** ask a question outside the approved source.
- **Tours:** cancel and reschedule a visit.
- **Access:** confirm the showing controls remain in force.
- **Handoff:** request a person and inspect the staff notification.
- **Records:** find the complete conversation after the test.
- **Cost:** compare actual usage with the quoted allowance.

Give each failure an owner and a fix. Launch only the channels that pass. Keep a simple way to pause automation while staff resolve an error.
