## What leasing and renewal fees do Miami managers charge?

Most Miami managers charge one month's rent to place a tenant. Six of the 10 pages say so. HomeRiver charges 50%, and Keyrenter Miami West 90% when you also buy management. Ziprent charges a flat $1,500. Miami Property Management LLC charges 2 months for a 2-year lease.

Renewal fees spread more than any other line:

| Renewal fee | Firms |
|---|---|
| $0 | Miami Property Management LLC, Novel Management |
| $150 | RPM Dade (Starter), AVO |
| $250 | Ziprent, Mastery |
| 15% of a month | Keyrenter Miami West |
| 25% of a month | HomeRiver Miami |
| 50% of a month | Florida Management & Consulting Group |

On a $2,500 rent, those range from $0 to $1,250. If your tenants tend to stay, the renewal fee matters more than the leasing fee over 5 years.

## Which local rules add cost for Miami owners?

Florida's condo milestone inspection law adds cost for many Miami condo owners. Florida Statutes 553.899 requires a milestone structural inspection for any condo or co-op building of 3 or more habitable stories. It is due by December 31 of the year the building turns 30, based on its certificate of occupancy, and every 10 years after. A local agency can move the first one to 25 years for buildings near salt water.

A licensed architect or engineer does the inspection. Repairs it finds fall on the association, and owners usually pay through assessments. So when you budget a Miami condo rental, ask the association 3 questions:

1. **When is the next milestone inspection due?**
2. **Did the last one find substantial structural deterioration?**
3. **Is a special assessment planned?**

Rents set the other side of the budget. HUD's FY2027 fair market rent for a Miami-area 2-bedroom rose 5.3%, from $2,436 to $2,564. A percent fee rises with rent, a flat fee does not. See [Miami fair market rent 2027](/guides/section-8-rent-limits/miami-fair-market-rent-2027).

This is general information, not legal advice. Check the current law or ask a local attorney.

## Must a Miami property manager hold a Florida license?

Yes, in most cases. Florida Statutes 475.01 defines a broker as a person who, for another and for pay, rents or leases real property, or offers to. Section 475.42 says a person may not operate as a broker or sales associate without a valid, current, active license. Doing so is a third-degree felony.

Section 475.011 lists exemptions. The ones that matter most here:

- **Owners** who lease their own property.
- **Salaried on-site leasing staff** of an apartment community, working for the owner or a registered broker.
- **Salaried condo or co-op managers** who arrange rentals of no more than 1 year in their complex.

So an outside company that leases your condo and collects the rent needs a license. Ask for the firm's broker license number and check it with the Florida Department of Business and Professional Regulation (DBPR) before you sign.

## Flat fee or percent: which model is common in Miami?

The percent model is the norm in Miami: 9 of the 10 price pages use it. Ziprent is the one flat-fee firm in our set.

| Rent | 8% fee | Ziprent flat | Cheaper |
|---|---|---|---|
| $1,500 | $120 | $150 | 8% |
| $1,875 | $150 | $150 | Same |
| $2,500 | $200 | $150 | Flat |
| $3,500 | $280 | $150 | Flat |

At Miami rent levels, a flat fee often costs less each month. But look at caps too. Mastery caps its fee at $250, and AVO sets a $200 floor. On a $3,500 rent, Mastery's 8% would be $280, so the cap brings it to $250. Compare the total for the year, including placement and renewal, before you choose.
