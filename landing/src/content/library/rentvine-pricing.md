## What is Rentvine's price per unit?

Rentvine's pricing page advertises a rate as low as $1.50 per unit. Its FAQ says pricing depends on active units managed. The phrase “as low as” describes a starting rate, not a promise that every customer receives it.

Source: [Rentvine pricing](https://www.rentvine.com/pricing), read October 19, 2026. We use the vendor's own page rather than conflicting third-party price listings.

Ask what qualifies as an active unit. Confirm how vacancies, future properties and units leaving management affect the invoice. Get the effective billing date for additions and removals.

Request the rate for your current portfolio, not only a larger growth scenario. A discount tied to a future size may not apply immediately. Save the quote with its date, scope and conditions so your team can check the first invoice.

## What is the monthly minimum?

Rentvine's reviewed public pricing page does not give a numeric monthly minimum. Its minimum-unit FAQ describes the product's professional-management audience without stating a number. That does not prove there is no minimum.

Ask sales to confirm both a unit minimum and a spend minimum. They are different terms. Your company might satisfy a unit threshold while still paying a base monthly charge.

Use this quote checklist:

| Question | Answer required before budgeting |
|---|---|
| Billable units | Definition of active, vacant and removed units |
| Starting rate | Conditions that qualify the account |
| Minimum | Unit threshold and minimum monthly spend |
| Billing change | When additions and removals affect the bill |
| Commitment | Initial term and renewal basis |
| Extra charges | Transactions, screening and selected services |

Do not enter an unknown minimum as zero in your cost sheet. Mark it unresolved until the vendor supplies a written answer.

## What add-ons cost extra?

Rentvine says core tools include trust accounting, leasing, maintenance and portals. Its pricing page also lists an AI suite and open integrations. The same page says transaction and screening fees may apply.

Ask for a complete service schedule. Include incoming and outgoing payments, cards, returned payments, screening, signatures, websites and any selected AI or connection services. A broad feature label does not establish every usage allowance.

Confirm which services are optional and which conditions support the advertised rate. If an offer depends on another service, price that service too. Keep business-paid and applicant- or renter-paid charges separate.

Rentvine's trust-accounting page describes financial tools such as reconciliation and owner payments. Those descriptions explain functions, not a universal fee schedule. Ask the salesperson to match every required function to the quoted scope before you sign.

## Are there setup fees?

Rentvine's pricing page describes a one-time setup fee for full-service migration and onboarding support. The reviewed page does not show a universal dollar amount. Ask for the charge and work scope together.

List the records to import. Include owner and renter contacts, properties, balances, leases and invoice attachments. Ask whether historical transactions and inactive records move or remain in your archive.

Separate data cleanup from import. A setup team may load the supplied file without resolving every duplicate or unexplained balance. Assign an internal person to approve corrections and opening balances.

Ask for training, review and launch steps in writing. Do not treat a general onboarding timeline as a guaranteed completion date for your account. Keep the old system available until accounting and document checks pass.

Ask Rentvine to replace each illustration with a written account quote. Include your expected payment and screening volume. Request a steady-state monthly total after promotions end, plus initial cash due. Do not assume a published rate alone establishes the final annual cost.

## How do you verify the full offer?

Verify the offer by comparing the order form, fee schedule and linked terms before migration starts. Rentvine's Master Subscription Terms explain that the agreement includes incorporated documents. Read the current versions rather than relying on a summary email.

Confirm data access and exit conditions as well as purchase terms. The reviewed terms discuss account termination, conditional retrieval and possible assistance charges. Keep a tested export process while the account remains active.

Ask the vendor to produce a sample invoice using your proposed scope. Check the unit count, minimum, selected services and fees. If a charge remains unclear, resolve it before signup.

Use this final review:

- **Scope:** name the current tools and services included.
- **Rate:** confirm eligibility and billable-unit rules.
- **Minimum:** obtain a written figure or explicit confirmation.
- **Setup:** approve migration and training work.
- **Usage:** assign each extra charge to its payer.
- **Renewal:** record the later pricing basis.
- **Exit:** test exports and confirm notice steps.
