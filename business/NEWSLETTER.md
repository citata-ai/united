# NEWSLETTER.md

Status: v1, 2026-10-18. Owner: Tao. All names, days and cadence are hypotheses until we send 4 issues.

This file is the structure for citata.ai email. Voice rules live in [`/VOICE.md`](../VOICE.md). Read both before you write an email.

Model: the [Every](https://every.to) email program, studied 2026-10-18 (8 emails, 3 posts). We copy the shapes, not the words.

## 1. Email types

| # | Type                  | Every example                     | citata.ai use                                  | Cadence           |
|---|-----------------------|-----------------------------------|------------------------------------------------|-------------------|
| 1 | Weekly letter         | Sunday "Context Window" digest    | What we saw in AI answers this week            | Weekly            |
| 2 | Field report          | "Working Overtime" essays         | Case study: one account, real numbers          | Monthly           |
| 3 | Guide                 | "How to Build an AI Style Guide"  | How to get cited, step by step                 | Monthly           |
| 4 | Offer or launch       | "Introducing Plus One", workshop  | Free audit, new plan, new vertical page        | When needed, max 2 per month |
| 5 | Client Monday report  | (none, ours)                      | One account, private                           | Weekly, Monday    |

Start with types 1 and 5. Add 2 after the first case study has 6 weeks of data. Add 3 and 4 after 4 weekly letters.

## 2. Parts every email has

Every email from Every has the same frame. We keep it.

```
[Column label]                 ← small, above the headline
Headline                       ← Title Case, 4–10 words
Dek                            ← one line, 8–18 words; also the preheader
by Name                        ← a real person
[Image, optional]
Forward line                   ← "Was this forwarded to you? Subscribe here."
Body                           ← per type, below
Close                          ← one next step, one link
Signature                      ← name, role
Bio line (long pieces only)    ← who wrote it, where to read more
Footer                         ← why you got this, unsubscribe, address
```

Rules:
- Subject line = headline. Preheader = dek. Do not write a third text.
- One main link per email. Repeat it at most 2 times (top and bottom).
- Plain layout. One column. No sponsor blocks.

## 3. Type 1: Weekly letter

Shape from Every's Sunday digest: one top story, then short labeled items, then a list of what we published.

```
Headline: the top story's claim
Dek: "Plus: <second item>, <third item>"

Hello, and happy <day>. <one line: what this issue is about>

## Top story
<2–4 paragraphs. One event or finding. Number in the first paragraph.>

## Signal
**<Short title>**
The news: <1–2 sentences. What happened.>
The context: <1–3 sentences. Why it happened.>
What it means for you: <1–3 sentences. What to do or watch.>
—<Name>

## From the field
<One account, one change, one result. 80–150 words.>

## What we published
- "<Title>" — <1 sentence summary>. Read this for <reason>.
- ...

<Close: one question that invites a reply.>
<Signature>
```

Length: 600–1,200 words. Signal: 1–2 items. Field: 1 item.

## 4. Type 2: Field report (case study)

Shape from Every's first-person essays: a scene, the stakes, the process with the mess, what we learned.

```
Headline: a result or a mistake, with a number
Dek: the tension in one line

<Disclosure line, if related party: "Fridman Properties is a client and a partner of the founder.">

## Hook (1–3 short paragraphs)
A real moment. A quote, a screenshot of an AI answer, a date.

## Stakes (2–3 paragraphs)
Who is the client. What buyers ask AI. What AI said before. Baseline numbers.

## What we did (numbered sections, 3–6)
### 1. <Verb-led title>
What we changed. Why. The prompt or page. Before and after.
Watch for: <sign of the problem>
What to do: <the fix>

## What did not work
At least one failed change. What it cost.

## Results
| Prompt set | Engine | Before | After | Weeks |
|------------|--------|--------|-------|-------|
Source and date under the table.

## What you can do
1–3 steps the reader can take this week. One link to the free audit.

<Signature> <Bio line>
```

Length: 1,200–2,500 words. At least 1 table, 2 screenshots.

## 5. Type 3: Guide

Shape from Every's guides: a short history or a problem, why it matters now, then numbered steps.

```
Headline: "How to <outcome>"
Dek: "<N> steps to <result>, with prompts"

## Why this matters (2–4 paragraphs)
## Steps
### 1. <Verb-led step>
2–4 sentences. A prompt or example in a code block.
Watch for: ...
What to do: ...
## Checklist
- [ ] ...
## Next step
```

Length: 1,000–2,500 words. 5–9 steps.

## 6. Type 4: Offer or launch

Shape from Every's workshop and launch emails: TL;DR, a story about one person, what it is, who it is for and not for, the details box, the call to action, a signature.

```
Hi <first name or "there">,

TL;DR: <what, when, price, one link>. 1–3 sentences.

[Button: <verb> <thing>]

<Story: one real person, one result, 2–4 sentences.>

<What it is. 1–2 paragraphs. Plain.>

<Who it is for. Who it is NOT for. Say it plainly.>

<Details box>
What: ...
Price: ...
Start: ...
What you get: 3–5 bullets

[Button, repeat]

<Help line: "Not sure? Reply and I will tell you if it fits.">

<Signature>
```

Length: 300–600 words. Buttons: 2, same link.

## 7. Type 5: Client Monday report

Private. Sent every Monday per account. See `strategy.md` section 8.

```
Subject: <Account> — week <N>: <one-line result>

## This week in 3 lines
## Citations
| Engine | Prompts | Cited | Change vs last week |
## Changes we made (one per day)
- <date> <page> <change> — checked by <name>
## Next week
## Needs from you
```

Length: 150–400 words. Numbers first. No marketing.

## 8. Headline and subject rules

Patterns that work at Every, in our words:

| Pattern                         | Example (ours)                                          |
|---------------------------------|---------------------------------------------------------|
| Result with a number            | ChatGPT Named Our Client in 6 Weeks                     |
| Plain mistake                   | We Wrote 40 Pages. AI Cited 2.                          |
| How to                          | How to Check What AI Says About Your Company            |
| Contrast                        | AI Sends 1% of Visits and 12% of Signups                |
| Question from a real buyer      | Which Property Manager Takes Section 8?                 |

Do not: clickbait, ALL CAPS, emoji, "you won't believe", fake "Re:".
