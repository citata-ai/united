# Property Manager Websites: client delivery audit

**Citata.ai · Seen 2026-10-19 · 10 confirmed clients; 0 likely; 0 weak.**

PMW repeats a complete owner website: city pages, service pages, owner FAQs, fee pages, a rental-analysis offer and three tools. This package appears in firms that H03 names and in firms it does not name. The sample shows a delivery pattern. It does not show that PMW caused AI mentions or owner leads.

## Scope and method

Purposeful sample from PMW portfolio; selected for city overlap and contrast, not random. Live home and portfolio pages returned 200. Fetch public HTML, sitemap, robots, llms and selected child pages; check blog index pages through the six-month cutoff. No forms, accounts, contact or paid tools.

Current door range is unknown for seven firms. LREI and Thirdstone state more than 500 properties; an outside directory places Service Star above 500. All serve residential owners; retain those three as scale references, not proved 50–500-door buyers. Counts are sitemap URLs, not indexed pages. Static samples do not prove the full SEO retainer, content authorship, listing work, lead results or causation. H03 is one saved answer per engine/city, not a repeated trial. Rates and expected effects are hypotheses. No separate AI search was needed for the ten chosen service cities; Buffalo Grove visibility remains unknown.

A confirmed client means PMW names the firm in its portfolio. A footer or code signature alone would be likely. Each portfolio detail and client home returned 200. Live contact details, owner service pages and rental routes support active business sites. No selected domain is a Nesthub preview. Twelve Point was excluded because its live page still showed a placeholder address: `123 Main St, Anywhere, FL`. This does not prove the business is inactive; it fails the clean active-site check for this sample.

The blog window is **2026-04-19 through 2026-10-19**, inclusive. Counts use unique blog URLs and visible post dates, not sitemap lastmod. For larger libraries, we read index pages until older dates appear and assume date order holds. Fridman uses `/blog/page/2`; other paged sites use `?pg=`. Duplicate cards do not count twice. Displayed dates may mark edits or a site move. These counts are apparent dated output, not proved first publication or the writer’s identity.

Sitemap counts include support and success URLs. They do not prove HTTP health for every URL, unique copy or index inclusion. City counts include only URL paths ending in `-property-management`, with service subtypes removed. They can include both cities and neighborhoods. We fetched owner, FAQ, fee, ROI, primary-city and rental pages. Tools were inspected as pages; no inputs or lead forms were submitted. Rental feeds were checked for a live route, not for a complete current vacancy count.

Read with [the competitor rule](../../../competitors.toml) and [the main teardown](../2026-10-19-teardown.md). H03 source: `git show origin/stage:business/marketing/ai-visibility/cities-2026-10/2026-10-19/cities.csv`. This file leaves the main teardown unchanged.
