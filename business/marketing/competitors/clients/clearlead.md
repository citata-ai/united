# ClearLead Client Delivery Audit

Citata.ai checked 10 firms on 2026-10-19. Evidence confirms 6 relationships; 2 are likely and 2 remain weak leads.

## Scope And Method

The buyer rule is US residential property management for owner leads. Resident Inspect is software, so it is excluded. Heave Ho Crane is also excluded. Mixed firms qualify here when residential management is a core offer. Exact 50–500 door fit is not proved for most firms. Oak Street claims 200+ doors. Public directories report 175+ for Smart Move and 220 for Ironclad; these figures can be stale.

Sources include ClearLead's [case page](https://www.clearleaddigital.com/clients-and-case-studies), [home page](https://www.clearleaddigital.com/), client HTML, sitemaps and public review pages. Confirmed means a named case or named work review. Likely means client footer or code evidence. Weak means a logo without named work scope. Weak entries are leads, not proved current paid clients.

Counts measure sitemap URLs, not indexed or useful pages. Old, success and test URLs can inflate them. Out Fast has 100 URLs after its sitemap index is expanded; image sitemap nodes are excluded. Oak Street returns its home HTML at `/sitemap.xml`, so its page total is unknown.

The blog comparison uses 2026-04-01 through 2026-09-30, the last 6 complete months used in round 1. October is separate. Rolling counts use 2026-04-19 through 2026-10-19 where date coverage permits. Visible dates and publication fields show apparent publication, not proof of first release. Unknown and lower bounds stay explicit. We did not complete a full article-date crawl.

Schema findings apply to fetched home HTML. They do not prove all-site coverage or rich-result eligibility. We parsed JSON-LD blocks; type text inside invalid JSON does not count as valid markup. Missing JSON-LD does not rule out other markup methods. HTTP 200 alone does not prove a valid machine file.

H03 comes from `origin/stage:business/marketing/ai-visibility/cities-2026-10/2026-10-19/cities.csv`: 150 saved answers, 3 per city. A name and a source-domain citation are different measures. We match the firm in its city. Cities outside H03 stay unknown; no free fresh AI-answer run was available. We did not use search results as new AI answers.

## Repeated Delivery Pattern

1. **Owner-first page set.** Owner services, fee/pricing links, FAQs and rental analysis recur across the sample. Resident portals remain separate. Evidence: Allegiant; Hendricks; Osto; Nest Finders; Smart Move; Ironclad. Observed site pattern; ClearLead authorship is not proved for every page.

2. **City and neighborhood pages.** Local service URLs recur on Allegiant, Nest Finders, Out Fast, Smart Move, Red Roof and Ironclad. Evidence: Allegiant; Nest Finders; Out Fast; Smart Move; Red Roof; Ironclad. H03 names Nest Finders and Out Fast; their site domains are not directly cited under the audited domains.

3. **Owner decision tools.** ROI, vacancy and rent-versus-sell calculators recur, often beside free rental analysis. Evidence: Allegiant; Hendricks; Osto; Nest Finders; Smart Move; Home Rentals Depot. PMW credits on many sites make this a market pattern, not a unique ClearLead invention.

4. **Local facts and owner questions.** Law, screening, fees, rent setting and local market posts recur. Cadence differs sharply. Evidence: Hendricks; Allegiant; Osto; Nest Finders; Out Fast; Smart Move. No single weekly blog rate fits the sample. Dates are public site claims, not proof of original publication.

5. **Platform-independent SEO work.** Confirmed cases retain PMW sites; other sites use Squarespace, WordPress or LeadConnector. Evidence: Allegiant; Osto; Nest Finders; Hendricks; Out Fast; Smart Move; Ironclad. The offer can improve an existing site. A site rebuild is not shown as a necessary condition.

6. **Direct ClearLead conversion and review code.** Same review-worker host appears on Smart Move and Ironclad. ClearLead-hosted forms appear on Out Fast and Oak Street. Evidence: Smart Move; Ironclad; Out Fast; Oak Street. Shared code supports a delivery pattern. It does not prove a standard CRM package or review-generation results.

7. **Uneven AI files and schema.** 5 sites serve plain llms.txt. Oak Street serves HTML at machine-file paths. Some home schema blocks fail JSON parsing. Evidence: Hendricks; Nest Finders; Out Fast; Smart Move; Ironclad; Oak Street; Allegiant. All 3 H03-named firms have llms.txt, but 2 untested firms do too. This cannot establish an AI benefit.

8. **Directories beside AI names.** H03 answers that name clients cite directories. Nest Finders is first in every Jacksonville answer. Evidence: Nest Finders; Hendricks; Out Fast. Small sample, correlation only. No pre-work control or assignment of a cited directory to each name is available.

## Ten Moves To Copy

Rank reflects Citata.ai judgment, not measured lift. Every expected effect below is a hypothesis. Site patterns are worth testing even where ClearLead authorship is unknown.

1. **Build one owner decision page per core market.** State service area, property types, fee model, proof, FAQs and next step. Link service details and rental analysis. Evidence: Nest Finders; Hendricks; Out Fast. Expected effect, hypothesis: more qualified owner inquiries. Measure: owner inquiries and signed doors per landing page.

2. **Check and fix public directory facts.** Match name, address, phone, site and service scope on Expertise.com, iPropertyManagement, BBB and NARPM where eligible. Keep source links. Evidence: Nest Finders; Hendricks; Out Fast; Allegiant. Expected effect, hypothesis: more correct ai names and fewer entity errors. Measure: repeat the same city prompts; track named firm and cited url separately.

3. **Add useful city and neighborhood pages.** Use local service facts, fees, staff proof and cases. Avoid pages that only swap city names. Evidence: Nest Finders; Out Fast; Allegiant; Smart Move. Expected effect, hypothesis: broader owner search reach. Measure: qualified owner leads by service city and page.

4. **Make fees and guarantees easy to compare.** Publish fee components, exclusions, example totals and written guarantee terms. Use an honest price range on citata.ai too. Evidence: Allegiant; Hendricks; Osto; Nest Finders; Out Fast; Smart Move. Expected effect, hypothesis: better fit before the first call. Measure: owner-call quality and call-to-agreement rate.

5. **Link a decision tool to rental analysis.** Start with one vacancy-cost or rent-versus-sell tool. Show assumptions and results before optional contact. Use a worked ROI tool for citata.ai buyers. Evidence: Hendricks; Osto; Nest Finders; Smart Move; Home Rentals Depot. Expected effect, hypothesis: more owner inquiries with clear intent. Measure: tool completion, analysis requests and signed doors.

6. **Publish local owner answers with named review.** Start with fees, screening, repair duties, rent setting and local market facts. Test a small schedule before raising volume. Evidence: Hendricks; Out Fast; Osto; Allegiant. Expected effect, hypothesis: more useful search entries and answers. Measure: owner queries, assisted inquiries and pages named in ai sources.

7. **Make reviews visible and source-linked.** Show review dates, rating source and a current total. Ask real clients for reviews as part of normal delivery. Never invent or gate reviews. Evidence: Smart Move; Ironclad; Allegiant. Expected effect, hypothesis: more trust and stronger public proof. Measure: verified review growth, owner conversion and complaint trend.

8. **Add a narrow Section 8 owner guide.** Use local housing-authority sources, payment steps, inspections and owner duties. Obtain local expert review. Add a cited citata.ai service example. Evidence: Out Fast; Allegiant; Nest Finders; Ironclad; Red Roof. Expected effect, hypothesis: better fit for underserved owner queries. Measure: section 8 owner leads and repeated niche ai prompt results.

9. **Ship valid machine-readable facts.** Parse JSON-LD, align it with visible facts, check robots and serve plain llms.txt. Test for soft 404s. Do this on citata.ai too. Evidence: Nest Finders; Smart Move; Ironclad; Oak Street; Allegiant; Hendricks. Expected effect, hypothesis: fewer crawl and entity errors; ai impact unproved. Measure: valid files, crawl checks and before/after fixed-query results.

10. **Report from AI answer to signed door.** Keep prompt, engine, full answer, source URL, owner lead and signed agreement as separate fields. Offer work on the existing CMS. Evidence: Out Fast Clutch review; Nest Finders H03; PMW client credits. Expected effect, hypothesis: clearer roi and fewer false success claims.
